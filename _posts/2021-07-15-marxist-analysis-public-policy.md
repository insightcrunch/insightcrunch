A reader arrives holding a policy and a question about it, and the honest service an article can perform is to hand over a procedure rather than a verdict. Public policy, in the pages that follow, is treated as something to be taken apart in a fixed order, by questions that can be asked of a measure sight unseen, in ignorance of its sponsors, its slogans, and its press coverage. The procedure supplied here has seven steps. It assumes no conspiracy, requires no hidden director, and asks for no prior loyalty to the tradition that assembled it. A sceptic can run every step, dispute the weighting at each stage, and still leave with a sharper account than the one carried in. That portability is the point. Methods earn their keep by surviving transfer into unfriendly hands, and a framework that only persuades the converted has confessed its own weakness before the argument begins.

![Ledger pages, a budget table, and a fountain pen resting beside columns of figures, Insight Crunch](/assets/images/blog/blog-01.webp)

Step one, stated at once because a procedure should declare itself early, is the tracing of material flows. Before motives, before rhetoric, before the biography of any minister or the mood of any editorial page, the analyst asks who pays, who receives, in what form the transfer travels, and across what stretch of time its costs and benefits fall due. Money is the obvious flow and rarely the only one. Time moves through policies, as waiting, as queuing, as hours surrendered to forms and offices. Risk moves as well, shifted from balance sheets that can absorb it toward households that cannot, or gathered upward into public hands. Obligations move, quietly, when a service once provided as a right reappears as a purchase each family must finance alone. A policy described only in the language of its announcement is a policy half examined, and the first step exists to prevent that half examination from hardening into a conclusion.

Nothing in the opening step presumes an answer. Some measures, traced honestly, deliver resources toward households with the least, financed by levies on those with the most, and the tracing says so without embarrassment. Other measures announce generosity toward the many while routing advantage, through exemptions, thresholds, and definitions buried in schedules, toward a narrow band of recipients who needed no announcement at all. The tracing cannot tell the two apart in advance, which is precisely why it comes first. An analysis that begins with the beneficiaries it expects to find will find them, in any evidence, under any government, and a method that guarantees its conclusions in advance has stopped being a method and become a loyalty test. The discipline of the first step is the discipline of the whole procedure: follow the flows before naming the interests, and let the naming be answerable to what the following uncovered.

A second commitment belongs in these opening paragraphs, because readers reasonably suspect the tradition behind this article of smuggling its conclusions in through the back of its vocabulary. The suspicion deserves a direct answer. When the analysis speaks of class interest, it means a position within the organisation of production that disposes people toward certain outcomes whether or not they articulate the disposition, and the fuller treatment of that idea belongs to the series guide on the [marxist theory of the state guide](/2012/12/01/marxist-theory-of-the-state-guide/), which this article links rather than repeats. When it speaks of the state, it means the ensemble of institutions that taxes, spends, coerces, and legitimises, not a committee with a membership list. When it speaks of labour power, it means the capacity to work, sold by the day or the month, whose daily and generational renewal is a material process with costs that somebody always bears. Each term does analytical work or it does not belong, and the steps below are arranged so that every term must pay its way in observable questions before it is allowed to appear in a conclusion.

The article also declines, openly and at the start, to advocate or oppose any policy type. Measures of every family pass through the seven steps and emerge with different profiles, and two measures wearing the same label can diverge at step four or step six so sharply that the shared label stops meaning anything. A subsidy can underwrite training that raises the capacities of a workforce, or it can underwrite margins that would have existed without it, and the procedure exists to separate those cases rather than to bless or condemn the category. Readers who came for a ranking of policies by ideological soundness will leave disappointed, and that disappointment is deliberate. What the article offers instead is a ranking of questions by the order in which they discipline judgement, together with one fully worked demonstration, a statement of the framework's limits severe enough to satisfy its critics, and a named condition under which its central argument would collapse. An analysis that specifies its own defeat conditions has earned the right to be taken seriously, and one that refuses to specify them has announced, whatever else it claims, that it intends to survive every possible evidence unchanged.

## Public policy and the seven steps in outline

The seven steps table is the article's principal asset, and it is presented early so that the long exposition that follows can be read as commentary on a map the reader already holds. Each step asks one question, seeks one kind of evidence, and courts one characteristic error, and the table compresses those triples into a worksheet an analyst can keep open beside any policy document. Read down the rows in order, because the sequence is load-bearing. Flows precede interests, interests precede constraints, constraints precede the deeper assessments of reproduction, contradiction, organisation, and ideology, and an analyst who jumps to the seventh step, denouncing the justification before tracing a single payment, has performed criticism without analysis and will persuade nobody who was not persuaded already.

| Step | Question to ask | Evidence to seek | What a finding looks like | Error commonly made |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One | Who bears the cost, who gains, in what form, across what span? | Budgets, schedules, eligibility rules, incidence studies | A map of transfers separating stated from actual beneficiaries | Reading the announcement as the outcome |
| Two | Whose interests were represented, through which mechanisms? | Consultation records, access patterns, agenda histories | Access, agenda control, and anticipated reactions made visible | Hunting for instructions nobody needed to give |
| Three | What constraint did investment conditions impose? | Revenue dependence, employment dependence, reaction of lenders and firms | A boundary inside which choices were made | Treating the boundary as a command |
| Four | How are the costs of reproducing labour power redistributed? | Service funding, household budgets, employer contributions | Costs shifted among state, employers, and households | Counting only the cash transfer |
| Five | Which contradiction does the measure manage? | Fiscal pressure, competing commitments, displaced costs | Accumulation and legitimation held together at a price | Calling every tension a crisis |
| Six | Whom does the measure organise, whom does it fragment? | Eligibility boundaries, collective provisions, individualised benefits | Capacities consolidated in one place, dissolved in another | Watching only the money |
| Seven | What does the justification naturalise? | Official language, necessity claims, excluded alternatives | Contestable choices presented as facts of nature | Mistaking the slogan for the policy |

A table, however, is a promise rather than a performance. Rows can be memorised in minutes and misapplied for years, because each cell compresses a judgement that only practice teaches. The sections devoted to the individual steps therefore slow down, unpack the evidence each step actually requires, exhibit the step succeeding and failing on generic material, and mark the points where honest analysts, applying the same step to the same measure, can still disagree. Disagreement inside a step is not a defect of the procedure. It is the procedure working as intended, locating the exact joint where judgement enters, instead of hiding that joint beneath a conclusion both parties were always going to reach for reasons neither examined.

One habit governs the use of the worksheet from the first row to the last. Every finding is provisional until the later steps have had their chance to qualify it, because a flow traced at step one can change meaning entirely when step four reveals who quietly absorbs the cost of the service the flow was funding, and a representation pattern that looks damning at step two can shrink to routine access once step three shows the constraint operating on governments of every stripe. The steps are a sequence of corrections as much as a sequence of discoveries. Analysts who treat the early rows as verdicts and the later rows as decoration will produce exactly the brittle, overconfident readings this procedure was built to replace, and they will produce them with a Marxist vocabulary that lends the brittleness an undeserved air of depth.

### How is the procedure different from asking who benefits?

Naming gainers opens the inquiry rather than closing it, because a list of beneficiaries cannot show how an outcome arose, whether anyone planned it, or whether circumstances permitted an alternative. Benefit is material demanding explanation. Six further inquiries supply the explanation, which is why the list occupies one row of the worksheet and not the whole of it.

The distinction matters because the shortest version of this tradition, the version quoted by its opponents and, regrettably, practised by some of its friends, stops at the beneficiary list and calls the stopping insight. A measure advantages employers, therefore employers authored it, therefore the state is their instrument: the syllogism has the rhythm of an argument and the substance of a pun. The procedure refuses the shortcut at two points. Step two replaces authorship with a graded account of access, agenda control, and anticipated reaction, each of which can be present without the others and none of which requires an instruction to have been issued. Step three then supplies a mechanism that can generate the beneficiary pattern with no authorship at all, which breaks the inference from outcome to intention at its root. What survives is slower, less quotable, and considerably harder to refute, because it claims a constraint where the shortcut claimed a conspiracy, and constraints can be checked against the behaviour of governments that manifestly wished to act otherwise.

There is a further difference, easy to miss, in what each approach does with measures that benefit the worse off. The shortcut has no honest place for them: gains won by workers or by the poor must be reclassified as disguised gains for capital, as legitimation purchases, as anything except what they appear to be, and the reclassification is performed by definition rather than by evidence. The procedure, by contrast, has a step built for exactly this case. Step five expects the state to purchase legitimacy, prices the purchase as a real transfer with real effects on real households, and asks what the purchase costs the accumulation process it simultaneously serves. A concession registered as a concession, with its fiscal weight and its organisational consequences examined, is a finding. A concession dissolved by definitional alchemy into its opposite is a ritual, and rituals are what this article is written against, in whichever camp they are performed.

### Why must the steps be run in their given order?

Sequence is a safeguard, since each stage hands material to the next and reversing the flow lets interpretation choose its own evidence. Map the money before naming interests and the map cannot be trimmed to suit the names; establish the constraint before criticising rhetoric and the criticism answers to something firmer than suspicion.

Consider the analyst who begins at step seven, with the language of justification, because the language is vivid and the quotations write themselves. By the time the flows are examined, if they are examined at all, they have been assigned their meaning in advance: every transfer reads as confirmation of the rhetoric's hypocrisy, every eligibility rule as the rhetoric's hidden clause. The reading may even be correct, in the way a stopped clock is correct, but its correctness is unearned and, worse, untestable, because no flow the analyst could have encountered would have altered the conclusion. Begin instead with payments and periods, and the rhetoric, when its turn arrives, is assessed against a map drawn independently of it. The gap between announcement and incidence, where it exists, then shows up as a measured distance between two established points rather than as an impression, and measured distances are what separate analysis from commentary in every discipline that has earned the name.

Order also protects the analyst against the most seductive error in this territory, which is the leap from step two to a theory of the state. Representation patterns are real, documented, and often ugly, and the temptation to rest the whole analysis on them, to write the article about access and influence and stop, is the temptation of the available evidence. Newspapers record meetings; nobody records a constraint. Yet the procedure insists that the unrecorded force may be the stronger one, and it places the constraint step immediately after the representation step so that the documented pattern must survive comparison with the undocumented mechanism before either is allowed into the conclusion. Analysts trained on other traditions will recognise the move as ordinary causal hygiene. Its presence here, inside a Marxist procedure, is the first sign that the procedure intends to compete as method rather than to circulate as doctrine.

## Step one: tracing the material flows of public policy

Every analysis under this procedure begins with accounting, and the accounting is more demanding than it sounds. A policy moves resources, and the movement has four dimensions that must each be pinned down before interpretation starts. There is the payer, who may be a taxpayer in general, a contributor under a specific levy, a service user charged at the point of use, or a future cohort inheriting a debt. There is the recipient, who may receive cash, a service, an exemption, a guarantee, or the removal of a cost previously borne. There is the form, because a transfer in kind disciplines its recipient in ways a transfer in cash does not, and an exemption delivers its value invisibly, to people who may never learn that a policy touched them. And there is the period, because a measure generous across one budget cycle and clawed back across the next three is a different measure from the one its announcement described, and only the full arc reveals which.

The distinction between stated and actual beneficiaries does most of the step's work, and it is drawn with documents rather than with suspicions. Stated beneficiaries live in preambles, speeches, and programme titles. Actual beneficiaries live in eligibility rules, income thresholds, contribution conditions, application procedures, and take-up patterns, and the two populations overlap imperfectly in almost every real measure. A benefit conditioned on a stable address, a bank account, a digital application, and a documented work history will flow, in practice, toward the already documented, whatever its preamble promises the undocumented. A tax relief claimed through a filing process will reach filers in proportion to their filing capacity, which correlates with the very resources the relief ostensibly supplements. None of this requires a designer with malign intent. Administrative form is a filter, filters have incidence, and incidence is a fact about the world that the first step exists to record before anyone argues about what the fact means.

Forms of payment deserve a slower pass, because the form is where policies hide their philosophy. Cash respects the recipient's judgement and exposes the transfer to political attack, since a visible payment can be counted, resented, and campaigned against. Provision in kind protects the transfer politically and overrides the recipient's judgement, specifying the good, the provider, and often the manner of consumption. Exemptions and allowances operate in silence: no cheque arrives, no queue forms, and the beneficiary population, frequently affluent, experiences the advantage as the natural absence of a burden rather than as a receipt from the state. Guarantees move risk rather than money, and their cost appears only when the guaranteed venture fails, at which point the payer discovers a commitment entered into years earlier, in language nobody read. An analyst tracing flows must therefore keep four ledgers at once, cash, kind, exemption, and risk, and must resist the convenience of the first ledger, which is always the easiest to fill and frequently the smallest of the four.

Time, the fourth dimension, is the one announcements most reliably falsify. Costs can be deferred past elections, past the tenure of the officials who incur them, into periods where they arrive as somebody else's emergency. Benefits can be front-loaded for visibility and then eroded by the simple device of freezing a threshold while prices and earnings move, so that a measure shrinks annually without any minister ever announcing a cut. Indexation rules, review clauses, and expiry dates are the small print where a policy's real trajectory is written, and the first step reads them with the attention usually reserved for headlines. A transfer whose value depends on a threshold is a transfer with a built-in future, and the analyst's job is to compute that future rather than to admire the opening position. Where the computation cannot be performed for want of data, the honest entry in the worksheet is a blank marked as a blank, because a procedure that permits invented precision will eventually be caught inventing it, and its genuine findings will be dismissed alongside the fabrications.

### Where does the pursuit of benefit begin, and where should it end?

Begin with the burden a rule assigns on paper, then chase that burden through prices, pay settlements, and rents until it settles. Stopping at the first recipient is commentary's commonest technical slip, because the hands a payment touches first are seldom the hands that keep its worth once markets finish adjusting.

The pursuit continues past the first recipient, because transfers rarely rest where they land. A subsidy paid to a landlord for repairs may settle, after rents adjust, as an advantage to tenants, to the landlord, or divided between them in proportions set by the tightness of the housing market rather than by the statute's intention. A levy placed on employers may settle partly on wages, partly on prices, partly on hiring at the margin, and the proportions are empirical questions with different answers in different labour markets. The analyst's obligation is to follow the transfer through at least one round of adjustment and to say, with evidence, where the trail goes cold. Cold trails are respectable findings. What is not respectable is halting the pursuit at the first pair of hands because those hands suit the argument, a manoeuvre the literature of every persuasion commits and which the worksheet's first row, with its blunt demand for period and form, is designed to embarrass.

Households complicate the tracing in a way the analyst must respect rather than simplify away. Resources entering a household are pooled, contested, and allocated by internal arrangements no budget document records, so a transfer addressed to one member may nourish another, and a service withdrawn from one member may be replaced by the unpaid labour of a second. The step does not require the analyst to model the interior of every household, an impossible demand, but it does require the worksheet to note where household allocation could reverse the apparent incidence, and to treat conclusions resting on individual receipt as provisional wherever care, cooking, and cleaning are the currencies in which policies are finally paid for. That note, made honestly at step one, becomes load-bearing at step four, where the reproduction of labour power brings the household from the margin of the analysis to its centre.

### What evidence separates a stated beneficiary from an actual one?

Qualifying rules separate them first, since written conditions shut out whoever cannot meet them, and claim records separate them again, since an entitlement nobody collects delivers nothing. Office practice redraws the line a third time through discretion and proof burdens, a redrawing the analyst recovers from refusal patterns and appeal outcomes rather than from the rulebook alone.

Each separation is a place where class enters the first step without any theorising at all, because the capacities that conditions reward are unequally distributed in ways that track income, security, and education with dreary reliability. Satisfying a documentation condition costs little to a household with a filing cabinet, a settled address, and an employer who answers letters. It costs dearly to a household moving between lodgings, paid in cash, caring for relatives across two addresses, and negotiating every official encounter in a second language. The policy need not mention class, need not intend class, need not have a single class-conscious sentence anywhere in its drafting history, and its incidence will still sort by class through the machinery of its conditions. Recording that sorting is not yet explaining it. Steps two and three compete to explain it, and the competition between them, access against constraint, is where this procedure earns its analytical wage. The first step's contribution is humbler and more fundamental: it makes the sorting undeniable, in figures of entitlement and receipt, so that later disagreement is disagreement about causes rather than about whether anything needs explaining.

A worked miniature may fix the step's discipline before the larger demonstration later in the article. Imagine a training grant announced for workers in a declining trade, payable to employers who release staff for instruction during working hours. The stated beneficiaries are the workers. The eligibility text requires employers of a certain size, with payroll systems capable of reclaiming the grant, and the take-up evidence shows claims concentrated among large firms whose training departments existed before the grant did. The actual first-round beneficiaries are those firms, reimbursed for activity substantially already planned. Whether the workers benefit depends on a second round, whether released time genuinely expands, whether instruction raises their capacities or merely certifies them, and that second round is where the analyst must go next, carrying the miniature's lesson: the announcement named the last hands in the chain, the money stopped at the first, and only the tracing noticed.

## Step two: representation, access, and the anticipation of reactions

With the flows mapped, the procedure turns to the making of the measure, and it turns there with a warning already issued: representation is to be understood through access, agenda-setting, and the anticipation of reactions, rather than through instruction. The warning restructures the inquiry. Instead of asking who ordered the policy, a question whose answer is almost always nobody in particular, the analyst asks whose representatives were in the room, whose submissions were solicited, whose evidence framed the options paper, whose objections were treated as obstacles to be designed around, and whose were treated as weather to be waited out. Influence in modern policy making rarely travels as a command. It travels as a calendar of meetings, a distribution of drafting invitations, a shared professional formation between regulators and regulated, and a thousand small anticipations by officials of how affected parties will respond to each draft that crosses their desks.

Access is the most visible mechanism and the easiest to document, which is why commentary overvalues it. Consultation lists, advisory committees, secondments, and the revolving movement of personnel between ministries and industries all leave records, and the records genuinely matter: a group never consulted will reliably find its interests mishandled in detail, in the thousand sub-decisions where a policy's real content hides. Yet access alone explains less than its visibility suggests. Groups with superb access lose arguments they care about, and groups with none occasionally win, and a procedure that cannot accommodate those outcomes will misread both. Access buys a hearing and an education in the possible. It does not buy outcomes, and the analyst who stops at the consultation list has documented the theatre of representation while missing its machinery.

Agenda-setting is the quieter mechanism, and it operates before any meeting convenes. Every policy process begins from a definition of the problem, and the definition selects the remedies that will count as serious before evidence is heard. Define a shortfall of skills and training schemes follow; define the same shortfall as a shortfall of wages and a different family of remedies becomes thinkable. Define a fiscal gap as a spending problem and one agenda assembles itself; define it as a revenue problem and another assembles, and the contest between the definitions, fought in reports, commissions, and the framing choices of official statistics, is frequently the real policy decision, taken early, in technical language, by people whose names the public record preserves without emphasising. The analyst's task at this joint is reconstructive: recover the definitions that lost, identify who carried them, and note the stage at which they were ruled out of order, because a remedy excluded at the framing stage never appears in the consultation record at all, and its absence there is evidence of the agenda mechanism rather than of its own implausibility.

Anticipation, the third mechanism, is the subtlest and, for this tradition, the most consequential, because it is the bridge to the structural argument of step three. Officials drafting a measure rehearse the reactions of those it will affect, and they rehearse some reactions with a vividness they grant to no others. The reaction of financial markets to a borrowing plan is imagined in detail, priced, and pre-emptively accommodated. The reaction of a dispersed population of claimants to a tightened condition is imagined vaguely, if at all, and accommodated late, after protest, if protest organises. This asymmetry of imagination is not corruption. It reflects the asymmetry of the reactions themselves: capital can exit, delay, or reprice within days, in full view of the treasury, while the costs borne by the dispersed arrive slowly, statistically, and deniably. Anticipation is where that asymmetry enters the drafting room without any capitalist entering it, and the analyst who learns to see anticipation at work has grasped the hinge on which this entire procedure swings.

### Why does a list of gainers not identify a decision maker?

Distributions and decisions rest on different records, so a map of gainers certifies nothing about authorship until process documents are added, and authorship then usually dissolves into access, agenda control, and foresight. Keeping the two apart restrains the accuser who treats gain as guilt and the defender who treats innocence as proof that nothing needs explaining.

Holding the separation is harder in practice than in statement, because beneficiary patterns are vivid and process evidence is tedious, and vividness wins arguments in print. The procedure therefore imposes a house rule: no sentence in a finished analysis may move from benefit to intention without passing through a named mechanism, and the mechanisms available are the three of this step plus the constraint of the next. A sentence that cannot name its mechanism is rewritten as a sentence about distribution, which is what its evidence supports, or deleted. The rule costs the analyst some satisfying paragraphs. It buys the analysis its defensibility, since every surviving claim about the making of a policy now rests on meetings held, definitions adopted, or reactions anticipated, each checkable, each falsifiable, none requiring the reader to believe in a room where the powerful instructed the obedient.

There is a mirror error on the other side, and the step guards against it with equal care. Because instruction is rare, the lazy defender concludes that influence is absent, that the process was a neutral arbitration among submissions, that the best argument won. The three mechanisms exist precisely to block that conclusion. A process can be thoroughly structured by unequal access, by an agenda fixed before consultation opened, and by anticipations weighted toward the mobile and the organised, while remaining, in every formal particular, impeccably open. Neutrality of form and neutrality of structure are different properties, and the second step's whole purpose is to grade the second after the first has been conceded. Analysts who grasp this stop asking whether a process was fair in form, a question with a bureaucratic answer, and start asking what the form was built to hear, a question with an analytical one.

Personnel deserve a paragraph of their own, because the movement of individuals between public office and private employment is the representation mechanism most often mistaken for the whole story. The movement is real and its effects are real: shared assumptions travel with the people who carry them, and a regulator formed inside an industry will anticipate that industry's reactions with an intimacy no briefing paper supplies. Yet the procedure treats personnel as one carrier of anticipation among several, not as a master key. Replace every official with a stranger to the industry and the structural facts remain: revenue still depends on investment, exit still outruns protest, and the drafting room still rehearses the reactions of the mobile before those of the settled. Personnel explain the accent in which a constraint is spoken. They do not explain why the constraint is spoken in every administration, by officials of every formation, including administrations staffed by the constraint's declared enemies. That persistence across personnel is the datum step three exists to explain, and step two's honesty consists in handing the datum over unsolved rather than pretending the consultation list solved it.

Evidence discipline for this step is documentary and comparative. The analyst gathers the consultation record where one exists, the membership of advisory bodies, the framing documents in which the problem was defined, and the sequence of drafts where drafts can be obtained, and then does the comparative work that turns documents into findings: comparing the treatment of submissions from different quarters, comparing the speed of response to different objections, comparing the present process with parallel processes in adjacent policy areas where the affected interests differ. A single process, studied alone, yields anecdotes. Patterns across processes yield the graded, mechanism-specific claims this step is licensed to make, and the licensing matters, because step two is where this tradition's popular reputation for overclaiming was earned, and where its scholarly reputation must be re-earned, one documented mechanism at a time.

## Step three: the structural dependence argument at the centre of public policy analysis

The third step carries the procedure's pivotal claim, and it deserves to be stated with a plainness that leaves nowhere for misunderstanding to hide. Governments, whatever their composition and whatever their intentions, depend for their revenue and for the employment of their populations on decisions to invest that are taken privately, by firms and holders of capital answering to their own calculations. A government that frightens those decisions away does not merely displease a constituency. It watches its tax base narrow, its borrowing costs climb, its unemployment rolls lengthen, and its capacity to fund every other commitment it has made, including its commitments to the poor, contract in consequence. Policies adverse to the conditions under which investment proceeds therefore carry costs that are systematic, foreseeable, and largely independent of the wishes of the government incurring them. Those costs constrain what gets chosen. They do not dictate a programme, they do not select the details of any measure, and they require no capitalist to telephone anybody. They operate the way a gradient operates on water: silently, continuously, and on saints and cynics alike.

This is the structural dependence argument, the namable claim at the heart of the article, and its history is part of its force. The argument was advanced by a Marxist, Fred Block, in an essay of 1977 titled The Ruling Class Does Not Rule, and it was advanced explicitly against the instrumentalist account in which a capitalist class directs the state through personnel, pressure, and instruction. Block's intervention turned the tradition's own evidence against its own weakest theory. The pattern to be explained, governments of every complexion converging on policies protective of investment, had been read by instrumentalists as proof of control, and control kept failing empirical inspection: the capitalists were divided, the officials were autonomous, the instructions could never be found. Block proposed that the pattern needed no controller at all, because the dependence of state revenue and employment on private investment decisions does the explanatory work unaided, through the rational self-preservation of state managers who need a thriving economy the way a mill needs a river. The strongest component of the Marxist framework for policy analysis is thus a component forged in internal criticism, against the framework's own vulgar version, and its strength has the same source as its origin: it explains more with less machinery, and machinery that cannot be found is machinery an explanation is better without.

The mechanism repays slow examination, because its plausibility lives in its parts. Revenue dependence is the first part. The modern state funds itself overwhelmingly from taxes on economic activity it does not itself perform: on incomes earned in employment it did not create, on profits realised in enterprises it does not own, on transactions it merely registers. A contraction of that activity is therefore a fiscal event before it is anything else, arriving in the treasury as a shortfall that must be met by borrowing, by cuts, or by tax rises on a shrinking base, each option carrying political costs the government can price in advance. Employment dependence is the second part. Livelihoods in such a society are supplied predominantly by private hiring, so the level of employment, and with it the incomes, the morale, and the electoral temper of the population, rests on investment and hiring decisions distributed across thousands of firms, none of which takes its instructions from the government whose fate their aggregate behaviour decides. Confidence, the third part, is the name for the forward-looking character of those decisions: investment responds not only to present conditions but to expected ones, so a government's reputation for unpredictability taxes it in advance of any measure it actually enacts, and the tax is collected by postponement, the quietest and least answerable sanction in economic life.

### What is the structural dependence argument?

That governments depend on private investment for revenue and employment, so policies adverse to investment conditions carry costs that constrain what is chosen, regardless of who holds office. It explains the pattern of policy without requiring any capitalist to control or instruct anyone.

The answer's economy is deliberate, and the sections around it exist to unpack each of its load-bearing phrases. Regardless of who holds office is the phrase critics test first, and it should be read precisely: the claim is not that office holders make no difference, which the record refutes daily, but that the difference they make is made inside a boundary they did not choose, and that the boundary's location is set by dependence rather than by preference. Governments of the left and right differ, genuinely and sometimes greatly, in distribution, in rhetoric, in the beneficiaries of their marginal decisions. What they converge on, across those differences, is the protection of the investment process itself, the refusal, in the end, to let revenue and employment collapse for the sake of any other commitment. Convergence at the boundary amid divergence within it is the pattern the argument predicts, and it is a subtler, more checkable pattern than uniformity would be. Uniformity would actually embarrass the argument, since dependence explains a floor and a gradient, not a script.

Constraint, likewise, must be read as a cost schedule rather than as a wall. A government can defy the gradient; governments do, and step three would be worthless if it denied the fact it most needs to interpret. Defiance is possible, sometimes durable, and occasionally vindicated, and the argument's content lies in its account of what defiance costs and who collects the payment. The costs arrive as capital flight or postponement, as a currency under pressure, as borrowing repriced by lenders who need no coordination to reach the same judgement, as employment softening in the sectors most exposed. Whether a government can bear those costs depends on reserves, on the composition of its debt, on the loyalty of its electorate, on the availability of alternative sources of investment and revenue, variables the analyst can examine case by case. The argument therefore grades governments not by their obedience but by their room for manoeuvre, and grading room for manoeuvre, estimating how much defiance a given state can afford and for how long, is the skilled work to which the third step apprentices its user.

A further clarification separates the argument from a neighbouring claim with which it is constantly confused. Structural dependence is not the assertion that business is politically powerful, though business often is, and step two handles that assertion with its own evidence. Nor is it the assertion that markets are wise, or that investment-friendly policy is good policy, judgements the argument neither makes nor implies. A constraint can be real and regrettable; a gradient can shape behaviour toward outcomes an analyst deplores, and recognising the gradient is the precondition of any strategy, reformist or revolutionary, that proposes to act against it with open eyes. The Marxist who states the argument and the finance minister who lives it are describing the same machine from different ends, and the description's truth is independent of the describer's hopes for the machine's future. That independence is why the argument travels, why it is taught in policy schools innocent of Marxist commitment, and why the misattribution examined later in this article, the assumption that the argument belongs to one camp, understates its standing in a way that flatters nobody.

The step also disciplines the analyst's treatment of business confidence, a phrase that attracts derision as though confidence were a mood that could be scolded away. Under the structural reading, confidence is a bookkeeping term for the expected return on commitments of capital across time, and its movements register real information about policy durability, however imperfectly and however entangled with herd behaviour and prejudice. Governments that treat confidence as mere bluff discover, in the postponement statistics, that the bluff calls itself. Governments that treat it as an oracle surrender their programme to the prejudices bundled inside the information. The analytical task is to separate the two, to ask which part of a confidence movement reflects calculable returns under stated policy and which part reflects the political preferences of asset holders wearing the costume of calculation. That separation is difficult, contestable, and indispensable, and it cannot even be attempted by an analyst whose theory requires confidence to be either omnipotent or fictitious. Step three, properly understood, requires it to be neither: a real constraint, unevenly informative, operating through decisions that are private in form and social in consequence.

## Step four: policy and the reproduction of labour power

The fourth step asks what a policy does to the reproduction of labour power, and the phrase, unpacked, names the most concrete process in the entire procedure. Labour power, the capacity to work, is not produced by the firms that purchase it. It is produced and renewed in households and communities, through feeding, housing, healing, resting, raising children, educating the young, caring for the old, and restoring the exhausted, and every element of that renewal has a cost. The cost is borne, in varying proportions, by three payers: by wages, which fund the privately purchased share of renewal; by employers directly, through contributions, provisions, and accommodations; and by the state, through services, transfers, and infrastructure supplied outside the market. A very large part of social policy, examined under this step, reveals itself as machinery for setting those proportions, for deciding how much of the cost of producing a workforce falls on the workforce's own households and how much is socialised, and the step's question is therefore precise: does this measure shift the cost, in which direction, between which payers, and with what consequences for the households absorbing the shift?

The lens reorganises familiar territory. A change in the funding of a public health service, to take a generic type, is usually debated as a question of efficiency or of fairness in access. Under step four it is also, and perhaps more consequentially, a decision about who pays for the maintenance of the workforce's bodies. Fund the service from general taxation and the cost of repair is socialised across all payers, including capital through its taxes. Shift the funding toward charges, insurance premiums, and employer schemes, and the cost migrates toward wages and household budgets, arriving there as a deduction, a premium, a debt, or as care forgone and capacity quietly diminished. The worker whose treatment is deferred does not present the analyst with a line in any budget. The cost surfaces later, as reduced capacity, as absence, as a household reorganised around an untreated condition, and the fourth step exists because the procedure refuses to call a cost abolished when it has merely been moved off the public ledger into private life, where accounting conventions stop following it.

Childhood is where the step's accounting bites deepest, because the reproduction in question is generational as well as daily. The raising of the next cohort of workers is labour, overwhelmingly unpaid, performed inside households under material conditions that policy sets: the availability and price of housing near work, the provision of care for the very young, the funding of schools, the security of income across the years when earning and caring collide. A measure that raises the private cost of raising children, by withdrawing a service, by tightening a transfer's conditions, by letting housing costs absorb a rising share of wages, is a measure that taxes the production of the future workforce and presents the tax as a thousand private difficulties. Households respond as they can: by working longer, by deferring children, by concentrating care on fewer shoulders, by drawing grandparents and kin into service. Each response is an entry in the true ledger of the policy, and the analyst who reads only the fiscal ledger will describe the measure as a saving while the society pays for it in exhaustion and in cohorts that arrive smaller, later, and raised under greater strain.

### How does a measure move the costs of renewing the workforce?

It shuffles those costs among household earnings, employer contributions, and collective provision, fixing how much of the price of food, shelter, healing, schooling, and care families carry alone. Withdrawn provision seldom cancels a cost. It relocates the bill into private life, where payment arrives as unpaid hours, postponed treatment, and quietly worn-down capacity.

The step also runs in the other direction, and its honesty requires the analyst to follow it there. Public provision can raise the quality and security of reproduction in ways that serve accumulation itself: a healthier, better educated, more securely housed workforce is more productive, more adaptable, and more employable, and employers as a class benefit from provision that employers as individual payers resist funding. This is one of the genuine tensions the fifth step will formalise, and step four is where the analyst gathers its evidence. The same service can be, simultaneously, a gain for households, a cost to the treasury, a benefit to capital in aggregate, and a burden resented by each particular firm asked to contribute toward it. Analyses that force such a measure into a single column, pro-capital or pro-labour, falsify the finding. The worksheet's fourth row is built to hold the four entries side by side, because the contradiction the next step examines is not an ornament on the analysis but a structure the evidence keeps reproducing.

Time use is the step's most underused evidence, and the analyst should cultivate it deliberately. When provision contracts, the work provision performed does not vanish; it reappears as hours, and the hours belong disproportionately to women, whose unpaid labour is the welfare state's silent partner in every settlement and its shock absorber in every retrenchment. Time-use surveys, where they exist, let the analyst price the absorption: hours of care transferred from services to households, valued even at modest rates, routinely rival the fiscal saving that justified the transfer. A policy can thus be shown, in the state's own arithmetic, to have saved money only by ceasing to count the currency in which most of its cost is paid. Step four counts that currency. It treats an hour of unpaid care as an economic quantity, not because the care is reducible to its price, but because a procedure that declines to count it will systematically bless every measure that shifts costs into the uncounted zone, and will call the blessing objectivity.

Education occupies a double position under this step, and the duality should be stated rather than resolved. Schooling reproduces labour power by forming capacities, and it also sorts, certifies, and accustoms, functions examined in the series article on [marxism and education theory](/2021/05/01/marxism-and-education-theory/), which treats the educational dimension in the depth it requires. For the policy analyst the relevant point is narrower: changes in educational funding and structure are changes in who pays for the formation of capacities that employers will later hire. A shift of formation costs onto students and their families, through fees and loans, relocates a share of the price of producing skilled labour power from the collectivity to the individual who embodies it, and the relocation follows that individual through decades of repayment. Whether the relocation is defensible is a political question the procedure does not answer. That it is a relocation, measurable in the distribution of costs across the three payers, is an analytical finding the procedure insists on recording before the political argument begins.

## Step five: the contradiction that public policy manages

The fifth step names the tension the previous steps keep encountering, and it names it as a contradiction rather than as a difficulty, because the two demands in tension are both structural and neither can be finally satisfied. The state must support accumulation: it must maintain conditions in which investment proceeds and profits are realised, and the economic activity that funds everything else continues. The state must also maintain legitimacy: it must secure the consent, or at least the acquiescence, of a population that experiences the accumulation process as insecurity, inequality, and periodic catastrophe, and that holds the state answerable for conditions the state does not fully control. James O'Connor, in The Fiscal Crisis of the State of 1973, gave the tension its canonical fiscal form: the state is required to socialise costs that accumulation generates while the surplus that might pay for them remains privately appropriated, so expenditure pressures grow from both directions at once, and the resulting fiscal strain is structural rather than a product of profligacy or error. Most policy, read through this step, is an attempt to satisfy both demands simultaneously, and the attempt's ingenuity, its displacements, and its eventual failures are the story the step teaches the analyst to tell.

The two demands generate two broad families of expenditure and regulation, and the families quarrel over the same revenue. Spending that supports accumulation includes infrastructure, the formation of skills, the underwriting of risk, the maintenance of demand, and the legal and monetary frameworks within which contracts and property hold their value, the legal dimension being treated in the series article on [marxist theory of law](/2021/04/01/marxist-theory-of-law/). Spending that supports legitimacy includes transfers to the unemployed and the old, services that cushion market outcomes, protections against the sharpest insecurities of wage dependence, and the visible fairness of provision open to all. A budget is the annual armistice between the families, and its politics are unintelligible until the analyst sees that each family can plausibly accuse the other of endangering the whole: accumulation spending starved too long undermines the revenue on which legitimacy spending floats, while legitimacy spending starved too long corrodes the consent on which accumulation's political conditions rest. Policy failure, under this step, is rarely simple incompetence. It is the armistice breaking down in one direction or the other, and the breakdown's direction tells the analyst which demand had been subordinated for too long.

Displacement is the step's central technique to watch for, because a contradiction that cannot be resolved can always be moved. Costs can be displaced across time, into debt whose service falls on later budgets. They can be displaced across space, onto other jurisdictions whose tax bases absorb the pollution, the care burden, or the unemployed that one jurisdiction's settlement exports. They can be displaced across the boundary between public and private, into households, as step four documented, or into charities and informal networks whose exhaustion registers nowhere official. They can be displaced into the future of the environment and the body, where they accumulate unpriced until they return as emergencies. A measure that appears to reconcile accumulation and legitimacy cheaply has usually located a displacement, and the analyst's question is forensic: where did the cost go, who holds it now, and under what conditions will it come back? Policies should be graded partly on the honesty of their displacements, since a settlement that moves a cost onto those least able to register a complaint has not solved the contradiction but hidden it, and hidden contradictions have a characteristic way of returning as crises that everyone agrees to call unexpected.

### Which two demands compete inside every settlement?

Keeping investment healthy competes with keeping the population's consent, since revenue and jobs hang on the first while acceptance of the whole arrangement hangs on the second, and both draw on a single purse. Settlements between the demands are truces rather than solutions, priced in budgets and sustained by pushing costs across time, borders, and households.

Fiscal pressure, on this reading, is the contradiction's thermometer, and the analyst learns to read it without the moralising that dominates budget commentary. Deficits are denounced as irresponsibility and celebrated as stimulus, in cycles that track the political calendar more faithfully than the economic one, while the structural account asks a different question: which expenditures grew because accumulation required them, which grew because legitimacy required them, and which revenue sources were left untapped because tapping them would breach the constraint of step three? A state that cannot tax capital at the rate its twin commitments require, and cannot borrow indefinitely against the difference, inhabits the fiscal crisis as a permanent condition with acute episodes, and its policy history becomes a sequence of improvisations at the boundary. The analyst who sees the improvisations as a sequence, each measure's displacement setting the problem the next measure inherits, has understood something that commentary treating each budget as a fresh moral drama cannot: the continuity underneath the theatre, the same unsolved equation being balanced, year after year, with different terms sacrificed.

The step has a discipline of restraint built into it, and the worksheet's fifth row carries the warning in its error column: not every tension is the contradiction, and not every fiscal difficulty is structural. Governments also make ordinary mistakes, waste resources on badly designed schemes, and inherit shocks that no framework caused. An analyst who reads every event as the accumulation and legitimation contradiction expressing itself has stopped analysing and started incanting, and the objection taken up in a later section, the charge of unfalsifiability, draws its real strength from exactly that habit among the framework's careless users. The step therefore requires a showing: the analyst must exhibit the two demands in the specific case, name the expenditure or rule in which each demand is embodied, and trace the displacement or the armistice line between them. Where the showing cannot be made, the honest worksheet records the step as not engaged, and the analysis proceeds on the strength of the other six. A framework that can record its own non-application is a framework that can, in principle, be wrong, and that possibility is the price of being taken seriously, paid gladly.

## Step six: whom a measure organises and whom it fragments

The sixth step turns from money to capacity, and it frequently identifies the most durable effect a policy leaves behind. Policies do not merely distribute resources among people who remain otherwise unchanged. They classify people, gather them into categories with shared interests and shared administrative experiences, equip some categories with meeting points, entitlements, and reasons to act together, and isolate others into individual cases, each negotiating alone with an office, a market, or a creditor. A measure can therefore consolidate collective capacity in one population while dissolving it in another, and the consolidation or dissolution outlasts the transfer itself, shaping what the affected populations can do, politically and organisationally, for a generation. The analyst's question is organisational before it is financial: whom does this measure bring into durable relation, whom does it leave facing the world singly, and what follows from that arrangement for the future balance of social forces?

Entitlements supplied universally organise differently from entitlements supplied selectively, and the difference is a staple finding of social policy research across traditions. A service or transfer that arrives as a right of citizenship gives its recipients a common status and a common interest in the provision's defence, and it gives them, in the queues, wards, and classrooms where they meet the provision, an experience of shared fate that political organisation can build on. A benefit hedged with tests, conditions, and individual assessments gives its recipients a different education: each case is singular, each failure is personal, each encounter with the administering office is a private negotiation in which the applicant's deservingness, not the provision's design, is the matter under examination. The first arrangement manufactures a constituency. The second manufactures clients. Both may transfer identical sums, and step one, unaided, would record them as equivalent, which is why the procedure does not stop at step one and why this step's findings so often reverse the evaluation the flow map suggested.

Fragmentation has techniques, and the analyst learns to recognise their signatures. Individualisation is the master technique: convert a collective provision into a personal account, a personal budget, a personal risk, and the shared interest in the provision's level dissolves into a million private optimisations. Categorisation fragments by subdivision, drawing eligibility boundaries through populations that might otherwise recognise a common situation, so that the just-eligible and the just-ineligible face each other across a line drawn by a schedule rather than standing together in front of it. Conditionality fragments by conduct, sorting recipients into the compliant and the sanctioned and recruiting the compliant as witnesses against the sanctioned. Competition fragments by design where provision is allocated by bidding, scoring, or league position, setting institutions that might have cooperated to lobbying against one another for shares of a fixed envelope. None of these techniques requires a fragmenting intention. Each has administrative rationales that can be honestly defended, tidiness, targeting, incentives, value for money, and the step's claim is about effects rather than motives: whatever the rationale, the organisational consequence is a population less able to act together than it was, and the consequence belongs in the analysis beside the transfer totals.

### What makes one provision a constituency and another a caseload?

Design decides the matter. An entitlement held as a citizen's right gives its holders a status in common and a joint reason to defend the provision, while a tested and conditional entitlement isolates each holder into a private file negotiated alone with an office. Equal sums can thus build collective strength under one design and dissolve it under another.

The organising direction deserves equal attention, because the step is an instrument and not a lament. Measures can deliberately build capacity: security of tenure that lets tenants associate without fear, legal recognition that lets workers combine, provision that frees time from the scramble for subsistence and leaves some of it for association, education that equips people to read a budget and challenge one. Employers, too, are organised by policy, a point the tradition's popular versions forget: incorporation law, competition rules, and the structure of public contracting all shape whether capital faces the state as a crowd of competing firms or as associations capable of a common line, and the state's own consultation machinery, examined at step two, often performs the organising for it, convening the representatives, circulating the papers, and teaching a sector to speak with one voice. Class capacity, in short, is not a substance classes simply have. It is an organisational condition that policy continuously manufactures and dismantles on every side of the class structure, and the sixth step's audit of that manufacture is what distinguishes a political analysis of policy from an accountancy of it.

The step also corrects a sentimental error to which this tradition is prone, the assumption that organisation is always the analyst's side that benefits. Fragmentation can protect: privacy in provision, individual entitlement against collective discipline, the right to be a case rather than a category, all have emancipatory uses, and some of the most resented policies in the record are resented precisely for their organising power, their enrolment of recipients into supervised collectivities. The worksheet asks what a measure does to capacity and leaves the valuation of the change to the analyst's stated politics, which must be stated rather than smuggled. That separation, finding first and valuation second, with the valuation owned in the open, is the procedure's recurring bargain with its reader, and step six, where the temptation to smuggle is strongest because the findings feel so plainly political, is where the bargain matters most.

## Step seven: the ideological work a justification performs

The last step examines language, and it comes last for the reason the ordering principle supplied: only an analyst who already knows the flows, the representation pattern, the constraint, the reproduction effects, the contradiction, and the organisational consequences can measure a justification against the policy it justifies. Ideological work, as the step defines it, is the labour a justification performs beyond describing its measure. It naturalises, presenting arrangements that were chosen as arrangements that could not have been otherwise. It necessitates, converting judgements about priorities into claims about requirements, budgets into weather, choices into physics. And it removes from contest, quietly relocating whole questions from the agenda of politics to the register of administration, where they are answered by procedure rather than argued in public. The analyst's task is to reverse each operation on paper: denaturalise by recovering the choice, test necessity by finding the alternative that was costed or could have been, and restore to contest the questions the justification retired, so that the reader can see the policy as one settlement among possible settlements rather than as the landscape itself.

Necessity is the hardest-working word in the vocabulary under examination, and it deserves forensic treatment. Some necessities are real: arithmetic binds, debts fall due, pathogens and demography impose facts no rhetoric created. The ideological operation begins where real necessity ends and borrowed necessity starts, where a genuine constraint at one point of a system is stretched to cover a whole programme, so that because something must be done, this thing, in this form, at this distribution of cost, must be done. The analyst separates the two by a simple exercise: state the binding fact precisely, then list the measures compatible with it, and measure the distance between the list and the chosen measure. That distance is the space of politics, and justifications that deny the space exists are performing the step's target operation in its purest form. Governments of every persuasion perform it, since necessity is the one argument that ends arguments, and the analyst trained by this step hears the operation whoever performs it, including governments the analyst supports, especially governments the analyst supports, because a method that only audits its opponents is a factional instrument wearing a procedure's clothes.

Naturalisation works more slowly and is caught by different evidence. Its signature is the disappearance of agents: costs arise, pressures build, adjustments become necessary, markets react, in sentences whose grammar has quietly dismissed every human subject who decided anything. Recovering the agents is the analyst's counter-move, and it is grammatical before it is political. Rewrite the justification's key sentences with subjects restored, naming the office that chose, the committee that recommended, the interest that was accommodated, and the prose that results is not yet an argument, but it is the precondition of one, because a reader cannot contest a decision that the language has dissolved into a process. The step pairs naturally here with step two: anticipation, which operated in the drafting room through imagined reactions, reappears in the justification as accomplished fact, the reaction presented as the decision's author. The analyst who has documented anticipation at work can show the join, and showing the join, the place where an imagined response was converted into a public necessity, is among the most clarifying single moves a policy analysis can make.

### What does a justification naturalise, and why does it matter?

It dresses embedded choices as necessities, and the dressing counts because a recognised choice can be argued with, priced against alternatives, and undone, while a necessity permits only obedience or irresponsibility. Recovering the alternatives a defence quietly retired returns the measure to the arena of argument, which is where the seventh inquiry wants it.

The step closes the procedure, and its position carries a warning the article has already issued and now repeats with the full sequence in view. Ideology critique performed without the prior steps is the cheapest writing in this entire territory: it requires no budgets, no consultation records, no incidence studies, only a suspicious ear and a fluent pen, and its products persuade exactly those who arrived persuaded. Performed after the prior steps, the same critique becomes the most expensive and the most valuable, because every claim about what a justification hides can now be checked against a map of what the policy does. The gap between the justification and the flows is a measured gap. The necessity claim has been tested against the constraint's actual tightness at step three. The naturalised choice can be exhibited beside the alternatives that step five's contradiction made visible and the settlement discarded. Ideological work, in a completed analysis, is not denounced. It is located, priced, and returned to its authors with the alternatives attached, which is a far more damaging service than denunciation ever performs, and a far more useful one for any reader who intends to argue about the policy rather than merely to feel correctly about it.

One more operation completes the step's catalogue, and it is the subtlest: the justification's distribution of sympathy. Measures are defended in the idiom of some population's deservingness and attacked in the idiom of another's, and the idioms do quiet classificatory work, dividing the affected into the prudent and the feckless, the contributor and the burden, the striver and the dependent, in patterns that step six has already taught the analyst to read as organisational interventions. A justification that sorts recipients into the deserving and the undeserving is not merely describing the measure's morality. It is recruiting one part of the affected population to police the other, and it is doing so in language whose categories will outlive the measure and pre-sort the public for the next one. Tracing those categories, where they came from, whose administrative convenience they serve, and which solidarities they pre-empt, is the final entry in the seventh row, and with it the worksheet is complete: seven passes over the same measure, each pass correcting the last, until the policy stands described in its money, its making, its constraints, its human costs, its tensions, its organisational wake, and its story about itself.

## A worked example: a subsidy for a category of employer, through all seven steps

A procedure without a demonstration is a promise, so this section runs the worksheet, in full, on a generic policy type described in the article's own words: a subsidy paid to a category of employer, defined by size and sector, toward the wages of workers taken on under specified conditions. No jurisdiction is named, no government, no party, no programme title, no date, because the type recurs across all of those and the analysis is of the type. The measure, as announced, exists to raise employment among workers whom employers judge risky to hire: the long-unemployed, the young without records, the older worker after a redundancy. The state offers to carry a share of the wage for a fixed period, after which the employer, having learned the worker, is expected to carry the whole. The announcement calls the workers the beneficiaries, the employers the partners, and the treasury the investor. The worksheet will now examine each of those assignments.

Step one traces the flows, and the tracing immediately complicates the announcement. The payment travels to the employer, not to the worker, and its first-round incidence is a reduction in the employer's cost of hiring. Whether the worker benefits depends on a counterfactual the flow map must state honestly: how many of the subsidised hires would have happened anyway? Every hire that would have occurred unsubsidised converts the payment into a windfall, a transfer to the firm with no employment effect, and the share of such hires is the measure's most contested and most important quantity. Beyond that share, the subsidy divides among three further destinations. Some hires are genuinely additional, and there the worker gains employment and the wage it carries. Some hires displace others, as subsidised candidates are preferred over equally qualified unsubsidised ones, and there the gain of one worker is the loss of another, invisible in the programme's own statistics. And some of the subsidy settles into prices, margins, or the wages of the subsidised workers themselves, in shares governed by how tight the labour market runs in the affected trades. The period matters throughout: the payment is temporary, the hiring decision is not, and the flow map closes with the question the announcement never asks, what happens to the subsidised worker in the month the subsidy expires, retained at full cost, released into a market where the next subsidised candidate is cheaper, or retained only where the learning the programme promised actually occurred.

Step two examines the making, and finds the three mechanisms in their usual proportions. Access was structured by the consultation that designed the category boundaries: employer associations were in the room, because the measure required their cooperation to function, and their evidence shaped the definitions of size and sector, the paperwork burden, and the payment's timing. Workers' organisations were consulted on the conditions attached to subsidised posts, and secured some, on training content and on protection against displacement of existing staff, protections whose enforcement machinery was left, in the final design, conspicuously underfunded. The agenda had been fixed earlier and deeper: the problem had been defined as a problem of hiring risk and employer hesitation, a definition under which a wage subsidy is the natural remedy, while the rival definition, a shortfall of aggregate demand for labour, under which the remedy would have been direct creation of employment, had been ruled out at the framing stage as belonging to another era's economics. Anticipation did the rest. The drafters rehearsed the reactions of employers in fine detail, calibrating the payment to the threshold at which hiring behaviour would actually move, while the reactions of the unsubsidised workers who would be displaced were nowhere rehearsed, because those workers are unorganised, unidentifiable in advance, and incapable of a reaction anyone must answer for. No instruction was issued by anyone. None was needed.

Step three applies the structural dependence argument, and it explains the measure's shape more economically than any account of the consultation can. A government facing weak employment confronts the dependence directly: hiring is private, revenue follows activity, and the menu of responses divides into those that work through the investment and hiring decisions of firms and those that bypass them. The subsidy works through them, and is therefore cheap to the constraint, indeed flattering to it, since the state pays firms to do what the state needs done and the payment itself testifies that employment is the firms' gift to bestow. The bypassing alternative, direct public employment, strains the constraint twice over, once by competing for labour and credit, and once by demonstrating, visibly, that the dependence is a choice of institutional arrangement rather than a law of nature. The analyst need not allege that any official reasoned in these terms. The constraint operates through the costing process itself, in which options working through private decisions attract favourable assessments of risk and deliverability while bypassing options attract warnings, and the warnings, accumulated across every appraisal the measure passed, are the gradient made legible. The subsidy's survival through appraisal, essentially intact, while its rival was redesigned into unrecognisability at each successive stage, is the pattern step three exists to record.

Step four asks what the measure does to the reproduction of labour power, and the answer is double. For the worker hired, employment restores the wage through which a household reproduces itself, and where the post is durable the restoration is real and the step records it without grudging. The measure also, however, acts on the terms of reproduction for the wider workforce. By attaching the state's contribution to the employer's judgement of risk, it certifies a category of workers as risky, as requiring a discount, and the certification can follow them past the subsidised post. By making a share of hiring dependent on temporary public money, it accustoms employers to regard part of the wage bill as the state's responsibility while leaving the state's leverage over the quality, security, and duration of the posts conspicuously weaker than its contribution. And by addressing unemployment one subsidised hire at a time, it leaves untouched every condition, housing, care, transport, health, that determines whether a worker can present for work reliably at all, conditions whose costs remain where step four always finds them, inside households, absorbed as strain. The measure's net entry in the fourth row is therefore genuinely mixed: reproduction supported at the point of the wage, reproduction's wider costs unaddressed, and a precedent set about who pays for the risk that a workforce's least secure members embody.

Step five locates the contradiction the subsidy manages, and the location is not difficult once the earlier steps have done their work. The accumulation demand requires employment to recover without wages rising so fast, or public commitments growing so large, that investment conditions deteriorate. The legitimation demand requires the unemployment to be seen to be addressed, visibly, compassionately, and in a form that credits the government with action. The subsidy satisfies both demands in a single instrument: it spends public money in a way that flows through employers rather than around them, it generates announcements, ceremonies, and individual success stories of exactly the kind legitimation consumes, and it does so at a fiscal cost far below that of the bypassing alternative while displacing the unresolved remainder, the workers displaced, the posts that expire with the payment, the reproduction costs unaddressed, into statistics that arrive slowly and blame no one. The measure is, in the strict sense of this step, an elegant settlement, and the analyst should be able to admire the elegance while recording the displacement, since elegance in managing a contradiction is not the same thing as resolving it, and the contradiction, unaddressed at its roots in the organisation of hiring itself, will present the next government with the same problem in a fresh costume.

Step six examines the organisational wake, and finds fragmentation wearing the dress of help. The subsidised workers are constituted as a category apart, their employment marked, their status in the workplace defined by the discount attached to them, and the marking divides them from the unsubsidised colleagues beside whom they work. The unsubsidised unemployed are divided from the subsidised by the eligibility line, and both populations are addressed by the measure as individuals, each case a file, each outcome a personal success or failure of job readiness. Employers, by contrast, are organised by the measure: the consultation convened them, the category definitions taught them their common interest, the administering machinery gives them a standing channel to the state and a shared grievance whenever payments slow. The asymmetry is the finding. A policy ostensibly aimed at the weakest participants in the labour market leaves the strongest more organised than it found them and the weakest more individuated, and that asymmetry, repeated across measures and decades, is one of the quiet mechanisms by which the balance of class capacity shifts without any single decision that a newspaper could report.

Step seven reads the justification, and the justification's key word is partnership. The state and employers are presented as partners in a common endeavour, the workers as the endeavour's beneficiaries, and the whole arrangement as the natural form of a modern employment policy, the only form, the justification implies, that a serious government could entertain. The analyst restores the agents and the alternatives. The partnership is between the two parties who were in the room; the beneficiaries were consulted through representatives whose protections were underfunded in the final design; the naturalness of the form conceals the framing decision, taken before consultation opened, that defined unemployment as employer hesitation rather than as a shortfall of demand; and the necessity claimed for the instrument dissolves, on inspection, into a preference for instruments that flatter the constraint of step three. None of this makes the subsidy a fraud. Workers are hired under it who would not otherwise be hired, and the analysis that cannot say so plainly has traded its credibility for its elegance. The worked example's conclusion is therefore a graded one, and it is stated as the brief for such demonstrations requires, in terms of the contradiction managed: the subsidy is a legitimation-bearing instrument of accumulation support, managing the contradiction between the need for employment recovery and the need for visible compassion by routing both through private hiring decisions, at the price of certifying a discounted category of worker, fragmenting the unemployed into cases, and leaving the costs of renewing the workforce where they lay. That sentence, with its credits and its charges in one construction, is what a completed seven-step analysis sounds like, and a reader who can produce its equivalent for a measure of the reader's own choosing has acquired the article's skill.

### What does the drafting room rehearse before it decides?

It rehearses reactions, above all the reactions of investors and employers, imagined vividly because they arrive fast, carry prices, and threaten revenue and jobs. Drafters design around those imagined responses long before lobbying begins, so foresight quietly performs the labour that popular accounts assign to orders, pressure, and backstage command.

The worked example also teaches the procedure's characteristic modesty, which should be made explicit before the article turns to objections. Every finding in the demonstration is revisable by better evidence. The windfall share can be estimated more precisely; the displacement effect can be measured rather than inferred; the consultation record can be opened and the framing history reconstructed from documents rather than from the logic of the outcome. The analysis invites those corrections, specifies where they would land, and would change its grading if they arrived with force. That openness is not a courtesy. It is the difference between the procedure and the doctrine it is so often mistaken for, and the next sections defend the difference at the points where critics have pressed it hardest: the charge that the framework explains everything and therefore nothing, the record of policies that damage capital's interests, and the standing of the central argument outside the tradition that produced it.

## The structural constraint is not only a Marxist argument

An honest article must now correct an error its own tradition encourages, because the correction strengthens the very argument it appears to dilute. The claim that markets impose a systematic constraint on democratic government, that the privileged position of business in a market society gives investment decisions a veto no election can override, was made in the same period by a political scientist who was not a Marxist at all. Charles Lindblom, in Politics and Markets of 1977, argued from within pluralist political science that business occupies a privileged position in market-oriented societies, that governments must induce rather than command investment performance, and that the need to induce it confines public policy within limits set by business confidence, limits that operate automatically, without conspiracy, coordination, or even political intention on business's part. Set Lindblom's formulation beside Block's of the same year and the resemblance is not a family likeness but a near identity: the same mechanism, the same rejection of instruction as the operative link, the same prediction that the constraint binds governments across the partisan spectrum.

### Who else reached the market constraint argument independently?

A pluralist scholar reached it in the same period and nearly the same words, holding that business is privileged because administrations must coax the investment their survival hangs on. Convergence from outside the tradition counts as evidence for the mechanism, and claiming the discovery as one camp's trophy misstates where it came from and how firmly it stands.

The correction cuts in two directions, and the article intends both cuts. Against the Marxist who claims the argument as a trophy of the tradition, the parallel establishes that the mechanism was visible from outside the tradition, to an analyst whose framework owed Marx nothing, and that its discovery therefore belongs to the analysis of market societies rather than to any school. Against the critic who dismisses the argument as Marxist doctrine, the parallel establishes that dismissing it requires dismissing a finding of mainstream political science as well, reached by its own methods and published as its own conclusion. What the parallel does not establish is identity of the surrounding theories. Lindblom's pluralism and Block's Marxism diverge sharply on what follows: on whether the constraint can be democratically loosened, on the nature of the interests the constraint serves, on the horizon within which reform is imagined. The mechanism is shared. The politics built on the mechanism are not, and the analyst who cites the convergence should cite the divergence in the same breath, because the shared mechanism with divergent conclusions is the precise configuration that makes the convergence evidentially valuable. Two witnesses who agree on the mechanism while disputing everything else have not colluded. They have triangulated.

Adjacent disciplines have absorbed the argument so thoroughly that its origin is now often invisible inside them, and the absorption is itself a finding about the argument's quality. Political science teaches the privileged position of business as a standard component of how market democracies work. Public policy research uses the accumulation and legitimation framework, O'Connor's inheritance, in the study of social provision, fiscal stress, and welfare reform, frequently without any Marxist commitment on the researcher's part and sometimes without awareness of the framework's provenance. Political economy treats the dependence of governments on private investment as a premise from which models are built rather than as a thesis to be argued. Students encounter both arguments in curricula designed by scholars who would decline the label this article's tradition claims, and the encounter does them no harm, because the arguments are tools, and tools are graded by the work they do. The series' own exam guidance, in the series guide to sociology examination answers, makes the pedagogical version of the point: a candidate who presents structural dependence as a sectarian slogan loses marks that the same candidate, presenting it as a mechanism with a pluralist parallel and a stated defeater, securely gains.

There is a lesson in the parallel for the procedure as a whole, and it generalises beyond this step. The seven steps were assembled inside a tradition, but their authority does not derive from the tradition, and the analyst serves them best by holding them to the standard the parallel illustrates: a component earns its place by surviving inspection from outside the camp, by being statable in terms an opponent can test, and by predicting patterns the opponent's own data confirm. Components that cannot meet that standard, and the tradition owns some, should be carried as hypotheses with their weakness labelled, not as heirlooms with their weakness concealed. The article's treatment of the legitimation concept, in the sections on objection and limits, applies exactly that discipline, conceding where the risk of vacuity concentrates. A procedure that grades its own components, keeping the triangulated mechanism at full strength and marking the elastic concept for sparing use, is practising on itself the honesty it recommends to governments' justifications at step seven, and the symmetry is not accidental.

## The unfalsifiability objection, stated at full strength

The strongest objection to this framework must now be stated as its critics state it, at full strength and without softening, because an article that answers a weakened objection has answered nothing. The objection runs as follows. A framework in which policies favouring capital demonstrate capital's structural power, and policies opposing capital demonstrate the state's need for legitimacy, has arranged matters so that no observation can count against it. Favourable policy confirms the accumulation function; adverse policy confirms the legitimation function; mixed policy confirms the contradiction between them; failed policy confirms the contradiction's depth. Every outcome is evidence, which is to say that no outcome is, and a scheme that is confirmed by everything is a doctrine wearing the costume of an analysis, unfalsifiable in the precise sense that separates empirical inquiry from faith. Critics of the framework, in the philosophy of social science and within political science itself, have pressed this charge against functionalist explanation in Marxist state theory for decades, and the charge lands hardest where the framework's users are laziest, which is often enough to make the charge worth answering as a description of practice and not merely as a logical puzzle.

The answer begins by relocating the framework's predictions, from the occurrence of policies to their pattern and durability, and the relocation is a genuine restriction of the framework's claims rather than a retreat dressed as one. The structural dependence argument does not predict that adverse policies never occur. It predicts that policies markedly hostile to investment conditions will encounter systematic costs, that those costs will register in revenue, employment, and borrowing, and that adverse policies will therefore tend to be reversed, diluted, compensated, or abandoned at higher rates than favourable ones, unless the state can bear or offset the costs from reserves, alternative revenue, or unusual political cohesion. That is a prediction about distributions and survival rates across many cases, and it can be wrong: a record in which adverse policies survived at the same rate as favourable ones, with no differential cost signature, would defeat it. The framework's centre thus makes a bet about the world that the world can refuse. What the centre does not do is license the analyst to explain any single case after the fact, and the article concedes, as a rule of method, that single-case explanations invoking the framework are illustrations of it rather than evidence for it, evidence being a property of patterns across cases the framework did not select.

The concession must then be aimed at the framework's own weak joint, because the objection is not wrong everywhere and pretending otherwise forfeits the answer's credibility. The legitimation component is where the unfalsifiability risk concentrates. Accumulation, at least, has a measurable signature: investment, revenue, employment, and the cost of borrowing all register whether the conditions for it are being sustained. Legitimation has no equivalent gauge. Consent, acquiescence, and their withdrawal are real, but they are inferred from behaviour that admits many explanations, and an analyst determined to find a legitimation purchase can find one in any popular measure, while an analyst determined to find legitimation failure can find it in any unpopular one. The concept, used without discipline, becomes a solvent that dissolves every counterexample to the accumulation thesis, and the solvent is what the critics are actually objecting to, correctly, in the work of the framework's careless users. The discipline this article prescribes is therefore specific: legitimation claims must name the population whose consent is being secured, the mechanism by which the measure secures it, and the observable behaviour, electoral, organisational, or industrial, that would show the securing had failed. A legitimation claim that cannot complete those three entries is struck from the worksheet, and step five is recorded, for that measure, as resting on its fiscal evidence alone.

### By what standard does the central argument judge an analysis?

By exactness about room. A persuasive reading reconstructs the penalties an administration faced, gauges the freedom those penalties left, and assesses the use made of that freedom. Readings that scold an outcome without pricing the alternatives, or credit an administration with liberty it never possessed, fail the standard regardless of the politics they serve.

With the objection answered at the centre and conceded at the joint, a residue remains, and honesty requires naming it. Frameworks of this breadth are not single hypotheses and do not die by single observations; they are research programmes, graded by whether their successive applications generate new findings or merely redistribute old vocabulary. The seven steps should be judged by that standard over their career in an analyst's hands. If the worksheet keeps producing surprises, measures whose flow maps contradict the analyst's expectations, constraints that prove looser than the rhetoric claimed, concessions that survive against prediction and force the survival conditions to be specified, then the programme is alive and the objection is being answered in the only coin that finally settles such disputes. If the worksheet produces only confirmations, each measure emerging with the profile the analyst brought to it, the programme has died in those hands whatever its logical status, and the analyst should suspect the hands before defending the framework. The article cannot run the reader's career. It can hand over the test by which the reader can audit it, and it has now done so twice, once for the central argument and once for the whole.

## The limits of the framework, without varnish

A procedure that has stated its own objection owes its reader a plain inventory of its limits, and the inventory begins with the limit the brief for this article fixes first: the framework predicts the direction of policy better than its content. Structural dependence explains why governments cluster inside a boundary; it does not explain why one government inside the boundary chooses a subsidy, another a tax allowance, a third a regulatory standard, and the differences among those instruments are not details. They distribute costs differently, organise populations differently, and age differently, and an analyst who needs to explain the choice among them must import other resources: the history of the institutions administering each instrument, the professional formation of the officials who favour it, the accidents of timing and scandal, the strength of the parties and movements pressing rival designs. The seven steps accommodate those resources at steps two and six without pretending to generate them. The framework is a skeleton. Skeletons are indispensable and insufficient, and the analyst who mistakes the skeleton for the animal will produce accounts that are structurally impeccable and concretely empty, correct about the boundary and silent about everything a citizen actually experiences.

The second limit concerns the policies the framework under-explains, and they are not rare exceptions to be footnoted but a standing category the analyst must expect: policies that damage capital's interests, enacted and sustained. Governments have imposed costly regulation on profitable industries, broken up dominant firms, taxed windfalls, mandated standards whose compliance consumed margins, and maintained welfare commitments through periods when capital's representatives demanded their retrenchment, and some of those measures endured for generations. The framework has partial resources for such cases. It can point to divisions within capital, where one fraction's loss is another's market; to the long-term interest of accumulation in conditions, health, skills, stability, that short-term interests resist; to legitimation purchases whose price capital is made to pay; to moments when organised labour or popular movements shifted the balance of forces far enough that the room for manoeuvre widened and governments used it. Each resource is genuine. Together they still leave a residue of cases where political conflict, ideology, and leadership did the decisive work and the structural variables merely set the stage, and the honest analyst records the residue as a residue, a measure of what the framework cannot digest, rather than stretching the concepts until the residue disappears and the unfalsifiability charge, so carefully answered, returns through the back door.

### Where does the framework's predictive power run out?

It fades at the level of content, since dependence explains clustering inside a boundary without explaining the choice among a grant, a tax break, or a rule. It also thins around measures that wound business and then endure, and wherever legitimation is permitted to absorb results instead of facing specified groups and tests that could return a negative.

A third limit is evidential, and it cuts against the procedure's centre rather than its periphery. Step two's mechanisms leave archives; step three's constraint mostly does not. Nobody minutes a postponement. The investment that never occurred because conditions were expected to deteriorate files no report, and the official who trimmed a proposal in anticipation of a reaction cannot afterwards prove the reaction would have come. The analyst of constraint therefore works from patterns: the convergence of unlike governments, the differential survival of policy types, the testimony of participants about options never formally proposed because they were known to be impossible, the revealing category of the idea that is dismissed in every capital with the same phrase about what the markets would accept. Pattern evidence is real evidence, but it is weaker per unit than documents, and it demands larger samples and more patience than commentary's deadlines encourage. The procedure accepts the weakness openly and compensates where it can, by requiring the constraint claim in any single case to be stated as an inference with its pattern basis named, so that a reader can see exactly how much weight the inference is being asked to bear.

A fourth limit is one of scope, and it should be stated to prevent a misuse the article can foresee. The procedure analyses policy within societies where investment is predominantly private and livelihoods predominantly wage-mediated, because its central mechanisms, dependence on private investment, reproduction of labour power through the wage, presuppose that arrangement. Where the arrangement differs, where subsistence production dominates, where the state itself is the overwhelming direct employer and investor, the steps do not transfer without reconstruction, and the analyst who carries them across unreconstructed will mistake the framework's presuppositions for the society's facts. Even within its home territory, the procedure is comparatively weak on policies whose main theatre is not material distribution at all: symbolic measures, recognitions, prohibitions whose costs and benefits run through status and identity rather than through budgets. Steps six and seven reach part of that territory, organisationally and ideologically, but the analyst should know that the worksheet's centre of gravity is material, and that a policy whose essence is symbolic will be under-read by a procedure that begins, rightly for its own domain, by following the money.

The final limit is temperamental, and it is the one the article's author most wants its readers to keep. The framework flatters its user with a feeling of depth, the sense of seeing the machinery beneath the theatre, and the feeling is addictive enough to become a substitute for the findings it should reward. An analyst can learn to say constraint, reproduction, legitimation, and ideological work with great fluency while ceasing to learn anything about particular policies, and the fluency will be mistaken, by audiences who share the vocabulary, for mastery. The procedure's defence against its own seduction is the worksheet's demand for entries: figures of incidence at step one, documents at step two, a cost schedule at step three, household evidence at step four, the two demands exhibited at step five, organisational consequences at step six, and alternatives recovered at step seven. Where the entries are thin, the analysis is thin, whatever its vocabulary, and the reader who grades analyses by their entries rather than their fluency will find the grading transfers to every other framework that reader ever meets.

## Verdict, defeater, and the grading of governments

The article's verdict can now be given in the form this series requires, as a judgement with its reversal condition attached. The verdict: the structural dependence argument is the most defensible component of the Marxist analysis of public policy, because it explains the convergence of governments on the protection of investment conditions without requiring control, instruction, or conspiracy, because it was formulated inside the tradition against the tradition's own weaker account, and because an analyst outside the tradition reached it independently at the same moment. The seven-step procedure built around it is serviceable as a general method for policy analysis, stronger on direction, distribution, and organisational consequence than on the specific content of measures, and honest use of it requires the disciplines this article has prescribed, particularly the sparing, evidenced use of the legitimation concept and the refusal to leap from benefit to intention without a named mechanism.

The defeater is stated with the same explicitness. The structural dependence argument would be defeated by a sustained record of policies substantially adverse to investment conditions surviving without the predicted fiscal and employment consequences, which is the empirical form the counter-claim would take. Note what the defeater demands of the critic and of the defender alike. The critic must produce a record, sustained rather than episodic, of adverse policies that endured and whose costs failed to arrive, not a single heroic government or a single defiant measure, since the argument predicts a gradient and gradients are refuted by distributions, not by anecdotes. The defender, symmetrically, is forbidden the two classic evasions: reclassifying every surviving adverse policy as secretly favourable to capital, which is the legitimation solvent in new dress, and retreating to the claim that the costs arrived in some unmeasurable currency, which abandons the argument's empirical content at the moment it is tested. Both parties are bound, by the defeater's terms, to the same ledgers: revenue, employment, borrowing costs, and the survival rates of policy types. A dispute conducted on those ledgers can be lost, which is the whole point of writing the defeater down.

### What price does a defiant government actually pay?

Payment arrives as dearer borrowing, projects shelved while firms await calmer signals, and hiring that softens where exposure bites, and the bill can be carried for a long while where reserves run deep or debts are patiently held. Defiance is therefore possible; what the argument denies is that defiance comes free of charge.

Grading governments follows from the defeater's logic, and it reverses the grading habits of commentary. Commentary grades governments by their intentions, their rhetoric, or their distance from the commentator's preferences, and the grades are worthless as analysis because they contain no information about the situation the government faced. The procedure grades by room and use: how much room for manoeuvre the cost schedule left, estimated from reserves, debt composition, revenue alternatives, and how mobile the capital was that the government confronted, and what the government did with the room it had, whether it spent its margin on durable gains for those it claimed to serve or dissipated it on gestures whose costs arrived before their benefits. Two governments with identical policies can earn opposite grades under this scheme, because one had room and used a fraction of it while the other had almost none and used all of it, and two governments with opposite policies can earn the same grade, each having read its schedule accurately and acted at its boundary. The grading is harder than commentary's, since it requires the schedule to be estimated before the verdict is reached, and it is fairer in the only sense analysis recognises: it judges decisions against the alternatives that were actually available, at the prices actually charged.

The verdict on the wider tradition follows, briefly. Marxist policy analysis, practised as this procedure prescribes, is neither the master key its enthusiasts advertise nor the conspiracy theory its detractors describe. It is a method with a strong centre, a weak joint, known limits, and a stated defeat condition, which is to say it is in the same epistemic condition as every other serious framework for understanding policy, and it should be granted the same terms: judged by its findings, corrected at its joints, and abandoned at its centre only when the defeater's record is produced. The article has tried to practise what it recommends throughout, crediting the framework's concessions as findings, refusing its solvents, and naming its parallel outside the camp. A reader who finishes the article less certain that the tradition owns the truth, and better equipped to analyse a policy by Thursday, has received exactly what the article was written to give.

## The misreadings, corrected in one place

Five misreadings of this analysis circulate so reliably that the article gathers their corrections into a single section, where a reader who arrived holding any of them can surrender it without hunting through forty pages. The first is that the analysis assumes a conspiracy. It assumes the opposite: its central mechanism was designed to explain the pattern that conspiracy theories were invented to explain, and to explain it better, with less machinery, in a form that survives the discovery that no conspirators can be found. The second misreading is that structural dependence means capitalists control government. Dependence is a relation between a government and a process, not between a government and a committee, and a sailor dependent on the wind is not controlled by the wind, nor by anyone who owns a sail. The third is that the framework holds every policy to serve capital. It holds that policies are made under a constraint favouring accumulation and a countervailing need for legitimacy, that the two demands produce settlements of every description, including settlements capital hates, and that the framework's credibility depends on registering those settlements as what they are. The fourth is that the framework is only Marxist, corrected by the parallel already examined. The fifth is that tracing beneficiaries is the whole method. Tracing is the first of seven steps, and the method's entire architecture exists to prevent the first step's findings from being mistaken for conclusions.

### Where does the conspiracy reading of this analysis fail?

It fails at the mechanism, because this account was assembled to explain governmental convergence on investor-friendly policy without hidden direction of any sort. Dependence can be inspected in budgets and borrowing costs by any reader, whereas secret intention can only be asserted, and explanations open to inspection outrank explanations that survive by repetition.

### Which two claims must a candidate keep visibly apart?

Keep apart the claim that outcomes are bounded, supported by dependence and by drafters' documented foresight, from the claim that outcomes were arranged by a group acting together, which needs communications nobody has produced. Examiners reward the first because it can be checked and found wrong; they discount the second because it can only be asserted again.

The misreadings share a common source worth naming, because naming it helps the reader retire all five at once. Each misreading substitutes the tradition's popular reputation for the tradition's best arguments, and the substitution is performed by friends and enemies with equal enthusiasm, by the supporter who wants a weapon and the opponent who wants a target. The best arguments are less satisfying than the reputation in both directions: less total in their claims, more conditional in their predictions, and dependent for their force on evidence that must be gathered case by case and pattern by pattern. An article in a series devoted to this tradition has a particular obligation at this point, which is to prefer the arguments to the reputation even when the reputation would recruit better. Recruitment built on misreading produces adherents who must eventually choose between the misreading and the evidence, and a tradition that keeps losing that choice, generation after generation, has only its recruiters to blame. The procedure taught here is offered as the alternative: slower to learn, harder to sloganise, and still standing when the evidence has been examined by hostile readers, which is the only standing that counts.

The corrections also have a practical use in argument that the student of this material should not miss. When the analysis is attacked in its misread form, the attack can be answered by quotation from the analysis itself, from the defeater, from the concession about legitimation, from the parallel with the pluralist, and the experience of watching an opponent discover that the position under attack is not the position on the page is instructive beyond this subject. It teaches the general lesson that frameworks should be engaged at their strongest formulation or not engaged at all, a lesson the article has tried to honour in reverse by stating the unfalsifiability objection more forcefully than its casual critics usually manage. Symmetry in argument is not politeness. It is the recognition that a method's credibility is a single fabric, and that an analyst who weakens objections for convenience will eventually weaken findings for the same reason, and be believed, in the end, about neither.

## Reading a budget through the seven steps

The budget is the document where the procedure's steps converge most densely, and a short exercise in reading one, generically, without figures for expenditure or revenue, which this article's discipline forbids it to quote, will show the worksheet operating on the material where policy is least deniable. A budget is an annual settlement rendered in numbers: it states what the state will raise, from whom, by what instruments, and what it will spend, on what, through which channels, and the analyst who reads it with the seven steps in hand reads a different document from the one the headlines summarise. The headlines report the settlement's theatre, the measures chosen for announcement, the losers selected for sympathy, the winners selected for credit. The worksheet reads the settlement's structure, and the structure is where the contradiction of step five signs its name.

Step one reads the budget as a flow map and immediately asks the questions the presentation suppresses. Which levies carry the raising, and on whom does their economic incidence finally settle after wages, prices, and rents adjust? Which expenditures are entitlements flowing by rule and which are discretionary flows that a future budget can quietly strangle? What has been moved off the visible ledger altogether, into guarantees, into tax expenditures whose cost appears as revenue forgone rather than as spending, into obligations deferred to later years whose totals the document mentions in annexes? The tax expenditure question is the budget reader's version of the stated and actual beneficiary problem, because a relief delivered through the tax code enjoys a political invisibility that an identical payment delivered as spending could never achieve, and budgets grow such reliefs the way gardens grow shade, without any single decision that can be debated. The analyst lists them beside the spending they resemble, and the budget's true shape, its real distribution among the three payers of step four and the two demands of step five, emerges from the listing.

### What fixes the sequence in which the steps are run?

Dependence among the kinds of evidence fixes it. Documentary stages, transfers and the politics of the making, precede the inferential stage of constraint, and the reinterpretive stages, household costs, competing demands, organisation, rhetoric, close the order. Judgement therefore arrives last, furnished with a map it must answer to, and readers can audit the working row by row.

Steps three and five read the budget together, as a pair, and their joint reading is the exercise's centre. The revenue side exhibits the constraint: the instruments a government dares to use, the bases it dares not touch, the rates that stop where the anticipated reaction begins, are the gradient rendered as a schedule of choices, and an analyst comparing successive budgets can watch the gradient hold steady across changes of government while everything else in the document changes. The expenditure side exhibits the contradiction: the two families of spending, accumulation support and legitimation, appear as line items whose relative fortunes across the years tell the story of the settlement's shifting balance, which demand is being fed, which is being starved, and which displacements, into debt, into households, into deferred maintenance whose bill has not yet arrived, are financing the appearance of balance. A budget read this way stops being an annual event and becomes a chapter in a continuous negotiation between a state and the conditions of its own survival, and the analyst who has learned to read chapters rather than events has acquired the temporal habit that separates this procedure from commentary most sharply. Commentary lives in the present tense of the announcement. The procedure lives in the sequence, and the sequence is where its explanations are.

The exercise closes with a caution the budget teaches better than any other document. Numbers confer an air of finality that the analyst must resist from inside the procedure's own disciplines, because a budget's figures are themselves settlements: classifications chosen, boundaries drawn, costs assigned to categories by conventions that step seven would recognise as ideological work in numerical dress. Whether a levy is classified as a contribution or a tax, whether an obligation appears as debt or as a footnote, whether a service's cost is counted gross or net of the charges it collects, are decisions that shape what the document can be used to prove. The analyst therefore reads the classifications as part of the evidence, asking of each boundary the worksheet's usual questions, who benefits from drawing it here, what it removes from contest, and the budget, interrogated in its categories as well as its totals, yields the last of its secrets: that even the state's account of itself is a policy, and falls under the same seven steps as every other.

## Failure modes: how analyses under this procedure go wrong

A method is known by its characteristic failures, and cataloguing them is a service to the analyst that no amount of positive instruction replaces. The first failure is the beneficiary shortcut, already prosecuted at step one, in which the flow map is mistaken for the explanation and the analysis closes at the moment it should open. The second is the access fallacy, step two's occupational disease, in which the consultation record is treated as the decision, and the analyst, having documented who was heard, believes the hearing has been explained, while the framing that preceded the hearing and the anticipation that surrounded it go unexamined. The third is constraint inflation, the mirror of the conspiracy error: the analyst, converted to structural explanation, begins to see the gradient everywhere at maximum steepness, until governments become ciphers and politics becomes the study of a wall, at which point the framework has lost the ability to explain the variation that fills the actual record, the governments that defied, the margins that were used, the settlements that differed. A constraint that explains everything a government does explains nothing about why this government did this, and the analyst must hold the gradient's estimated steepness as a finding to be established per case, not as a premise to be imported into all of them.

The fourth failure is legitimation drift, the solvent at work, in which step five's second demand expands until every popular measure is a purchase and every unpopular one a necessity of accumulation, and the contradiction, which began as a precise fiscal observation in O'Connor's hands, becomes a universal key turning in every lock. The fifth is organisational blindness, step six skipped, in which the analysis prices a measure to the last unit of currency while never asking what the measure did to the capacities of the people it touched, and is then surprised, years later, by political consequences that were legible in the eligibility rules all along. The sixth is rhetoric capture, step seven performed first and loudest, in which the analyst's ear for justification replaces the analyst's knowledge of the measure, and the resulting essay, fluent, indignant, and empty of incidence, is shared by readers who will never learn what the policy does. The seventh failure is the subtlest, and it is temporal: the analysis that treats a measure as an event rather than as a phase, grading the announcement instead of the arc, the first budget instead of the threshold's slow erosion, and thereby missing the dimension in which, as step one insisted, policies most reliably falsify their presentations.

### What separates a finding from a slogan in this procedure?

A finding points to a worksheet entry, a transfer chased, a record consulted, a schedule gauged, a group specified, while a slogan points only to itself and gathers force by repetition. Whenever the tradition's grand vocabulary surfaces in a draft, the writer must produce the entry that licensed it, and vocabulary without an entry is deleted.

Each failure mode, examined closely, is a step performed badly or a step performed alone, and that pattern is itself the deepest instruction the catalogue contains. The procedure's steps were designed as mutual corrections: flows correct rhetoric, representation corrects flows, constraint corrects representation, reproduction corrects the fiscal view, contradiction corrects the harmonious view, organisation corrects the financial view, and ideology critique, coming last, corrects and is corrected by everything before it. An analysis fails, characteristically, by breaking the circle, by letting one step's findings harden before the others have spoken. The practical rule follows directly. No step's entry in the worksheet is final until the seventh has been completed, and the analyst's draft should be revised backwards, from the ideology findings to the flow map, at least once, because the last steps routinely reveal that the first steps were read too quickly. Revision backwards is an unfamiliar discipline, and it is the closest thing this procedure has to a secret: the analysis is written forwards and earned backwards, and the difference shows in the grading of every examination, audit, and editorial review to which such analyses are submitted.

## Policy across time: feedback, inheritance, and the long arc

Policies do not act once and retire. They alter the conditions under which the next policies are made, and the analyst who grasps the feedback has grasped why the procedure insists on periods rather than snapshots. A measure creates constituencies that defend it, as step six predicts, and the constituencies become part of the representation landscape the next measure's step two must map. A measure creates administrative machinery, expertise, and data categories that make similar measures easier and dissimilar ones harder, biasing the state's own sense of the possible toward the instruments it already owns. A measure shifts the reproduction settlement of step four, and the shifted settlement changes the workforce, its health, its skills, its household structures, that the next round of accumulation must employ. And a measure spends or replenishes the fiscal and political reserves that step three prices, narrowing or widening the room in which its successors will be chosen. The seven steps, applied to a sequence rather than to a measure, become a dynamics, and the dynamics explain the phenomenon that static analysis finds most baffling: the persistence of settlements long after the forces that created them have dispersed, and their sudden collapse when the accumulated displacements return together.

Inheritance is the analyst's name for the past's presence inside a present decision, and every government inherits more than it chooses. It inherits commitments whose costs fall due on its watch and whose credit belongs to its predecessors, entitlements whose recipients are organised and vigilant, debts whose service pre-empts its discretion, and a reputation with lenders and investors that its own conduct only partly controls. The structural constraint, felt from inside an administration, is largely the experience of inheritance: the discovery that the budget's greater part is already spoken for, that the room for manoeuvre celebrated in opposition shrinks, in office, to a margin at the edge of commitments made by others, under other conditions, for reasons the inheritor may deplore and cannot unmake. The procedure grades governments by room and use partly in fairness to this discovery. It also explains, without excusing, the characteristic trajectory of governments elected on transformation: the early measures cluster at the margin where discretion lives, the rhetoric reaches for the centre where it does not, and the distance between the rhetoric and the margin becomes the opposition's evidence and the electorate's disappointment, in a sequence so regular that the analyst can set it out in advance and watch it run.

### How does an inherited settlement narrow a government's room?

Inheritance pre-commits revenue to entitlements, debt service, and expectations whose disturbance triggers priced reactions, leaving discretion at the budget's edge while argument addresses the whole. The narrowing can be gauged by the share of spending an administration can genuinely redirect within one term, a quantity rival analysts can dispute with evidence instead of adjectives.

The long arc also rehabilitates a question the procedure's critics press and its users should welcome: if the constraint is so steady, why does policy history show genuine transformations, settlements overturned, whole regimes of provision built and dismantled? The procedure's answer distinguishes the boundary from the settlement. Transformations occur within the boundary when the balance of forces shifts enough to move a settlement from one resting point to another: when organised labour is strong, the legitimation price rises and settlements grow generous; when it weakens, the price falls and settlements are renegotiated downward, each renegotiation described, in the language of the day, as modernisation or as necessity. Transformations of the boundary itself are rarer and more interesting, occurring when the dependence structure changes: when a state acquires revenue sources independent of private investment, when capital's exit options are curtailed by the organisation of the international economy, when a crisis discredits the anticipations through which the constraint had operated and a generation of officials learns new ones. The analyst's task at such moments is diagnostic precision about which level is moving, because confusing a settlement shift with a boundary shift, in either direction, produces the characteristic errors of political hope and political despair, each mistaking the other's timescale for its own.

Time, finally, is where the article's promise about dating itself is kept or broken, and the keeping deserves a sentence of methodological pride. Nothing in the procedure depends on the identity of any government, the fate of any programme, or the state of any market at the moment of writing, because the steps were built from mechanisms rather than from instances. A reader who takes the worksheet to a measure enacted long before this article, or long after, should find the questions intact and the evidence requirements unchanged, and if that reader finds a step that only the present moment could have suggested, the article will have failed its own structural test at the point of application. Durability is not a stylistic preference here. It is the procedure's claim about itself, submitted to the same grading the procedure applies to policies: judged by its arc, not by its announcement.

## The steps across policy families: three generic passes

The procedure claims generality, and generality should be tested rather than asserted, so this section runs abbreviated passes over three further policy types, chosen because their surfaces differ as much as policy surfaces can: a change in the funding of a public service, a shift in the tax treatment of a category of income, and a tightening of the conditions attached to an income transfer. The passes are compressed, a paragraph per family rather than a step per paragraph, because their purpose is to exhibit the worksheet's portability, the way the same seven questions find different centres of gravity in different material, and to leave the reader with templates that transfer to families the article has never touched.

Shifting how a public service is financed, away from general taxation toward charges, insurance contributions, or a blend of the three, announces itself as a technical reform of funding and reveals itself, under the steps, as a redistribution of the costs of reproduction. Step one follows the money from the consolidated fund to the point of use and discovers the payer changed at the moment of need: the charge falls on the sick, the student, the parent, at the point where the service is consumed, while the tax it replaces fell on the population at large in proportion to the tax schedule. Step four names the shift precisely, a movement of reproduction costs from the collectivity toward households, graded by their use of the service and therefore by their need. Step three explains the reform's attractiveness without recourse to anyone's ideology: funding streams tied to use or to contributions are partially insulated from the annual competition of the budget, and the insulation pleases treasuries confronting the fiscal pressure of step five, where the same reform appears as a displacement, the cost moved off the public ledger while the need it met remains exactly where it was. Step six notices the constituency dissolving as the universal service becomes a charged one, since a service everyone uses and everyone funds defends itself politically in a way a service some use and those users fund cannot. The funding change, in short, is never only a funding change, and the analyst who has run the steps can say, with entries in every row, what else it is.

Rewriting the tax rules for one class of receipts, an allowance widened, a rate differentiated, a base redefined, is the policy family where step one's distinction between stated and actual beneficiaries does its most devastating work, because tax measures are announced in the language of the category and received in the distribution of the category's holders. A relief for income from savings benefits holders of savings in proportion to their holdings; a relief for income from enterprise benefits those whose income can be characterised as enterprise income, a characterisation whose availability is itself unevenly distributed among accountants' clients; a threshold moved in a schedule benefits everyone above the old threshold by the same device that excludes everyone below it from the announcement's warmth. Representation, at the second step, shows itself in the technical consultations where categories get defined, a theatre of expertise in which the affected industries arrive with models and the affected publics arrive, if at all, with sentiments. Step seven finds the justification's master word, incentive, doing the naturalising work: the treatment is presented as the removal of a distortion rather than as the conferral of an advantage, and the analyst's reply is the oldest move on the worksheet, to list the relief among the expenditures, priced as revenue forgone, beside the spending programmes it would be compared with if it arrived by cheque. Few single moves in policy analysis clarify as much as that listing, and none is resisted more energetically by the beneficiaries of the invisibility it ends.

### What does a tightening of benefit conditions actually redistribute?

Before money moves, time, risk, and standing move: claimants surrender hours to demonstrating eligibility, households absorb the danger of interrupted support, and recipients are reclassified from citizens holding rights into applicants under scrutiny. Any saving is the smallest of these movements and the only one the announcement mentions, which is why grading waits for the later rows.

The third family completes the demonstration by showing the steps converging on a single schedule. Conditions attached to a transfer, work tests, contribution requirements, behavioural undertakings, are usually debated as questions of fairness between the deserving and the undeserving, and the debate's terms are the justification's gift to itself, since step seven shows the deservingness frame performing the sorting that step six then watches divide the recipient population against itself. Step four prices the conditions in the currency they actually consume, the claimant's time and the household's stability, and step five locates the contradiction the conditions manage with unusual nakedness: the transfer must be generous enough to legitimate and strict enough to reassure those who fund it that accumulation's disciplines, the obligation to seek work on the market's terms, are being enforced by the state on the market's behalf. The conditions are the seam where the two demands are stitched together, and the stitching is visible, in the sanctions statistics and the appeal records, to any analyst who reads the family's documents with the worksheet open. Three families, three centres of gravity, one worksheet: the generality the article claimed is the generality the passes exhibit, and a reader who doubts it is invited to run a fourth family, of the reader's choosing, and to report which step found nothing to say.

## Measuring room for manoeuvre: the third step as an estimation problem

Step three is the procedure's centre, and it is also the step whose findings are most often asserted where they should be estimated, so this section treats the estimation problem directly. Room for manoeuvre is not a metaphor to be gestured at but a quantity, rough, contestable, and composed of identifiable parts, and an analyst who learns to assemble the parts can convert the structural dependence argument from a commentary on politics into an instrument for grading particular governments at particular moments. The parts are these. The composition of the state's revenue determines how directly a contraction of private activity strikes the fisc: revenue drawn broadly from employment and consumption falls with the cycle but recovers with it, while revenue concentrated on a narrow base of profits, assets, or a single commodity transmits shocks at full force and adds volatility of its own. The composition and ownership of the state's debt determine the price of defiance: debt held domestically, denominated in the state's own currency, and spread across patient holders is a cushion; debt held externally, in foreign currency, by holders free to sell, is a tripwire. Reserves and public assets determine how long costs can be borne while a defiant policy waits for its benefits. The mobility of the capital the state confronts determines how quickly the cost schedule is presented: capital that can leave by wire presents it in days, capital sunk in plant and infrastructure presents it across years, and a state whose economy rests on the sunk kind buys time with every factory that cannot be moved.

Political variables enter the estimation on the same footing as the financial ones, because the cost schedule is paid in politics as much as in currency. A government's cohesion, its majority's discipline, its electorate's willingness to attribute hardship to the defiance's enemies rather than to the defiance, its opposition's credibility as an alternative custodian of confidence, all set the rate at which fiscal and employment costs convert into existential pressure on the government itself. Two states with identical financial positions can have very different rooms because one government's public will hold through a difficult year and the other's will not, and the analyst who prices only the finances will misgrade both. The estimation is therefore a joint product, part treasury exercise and part political judgement, and its honesty consists in showing the joint: stating the financial components from documents, the political components as judgements with their grounds, and the resulting room as a range rather than a point, because a range admits the uncertainty that a point conceals, and concealed uncertainty in this step propagates downward into verdicts that the evidence never licensed.

### How should an analyst estimate the cost schedule a government faces?

Assemble the parts separately: how broad the revenue base runs, the currency, holders, and maturities of the public debt, reserves available for a hard interval, and how mobile the affected capital is. Then adjust for political stamina, flag that adjustment as judgement, and publish the result as a range whose width is itself information about the case.

Estimation also disciplines the retrospective use of the argument, which is where it earns its keep in historical and journalistic work. The lazy retrospective reads a government's compliance with the constraint as proof of the constraint's tightness, and its defiance as proof of the constraint's absence, and both readings are worthless because they infer the schedule from the behaviour the schedule was supposed to explain. The disciplined retrospective estimates the schedule independently, from the financial and political components as they stood before the decisions under study, and only then compares the behaviour with the room: the government that complied despite a wide room has made a choice the analysis should name as a choice, and the government that defied inside a narrow room and paid the predicted price has furnished the argument with the kind of evidence, a cost arriving on schedule in a case selected independently, that patterns are built from. Analysts who hold themselves to the independent estimation will find their judgements of governments changing in a characteristic direction: less moralising about compliance, more precision about waste, since the scandal the estimation most reliably uncovers is not obedience to the constraint but room unspent, margins hoarded against risks that the schedule shows were never priced as the government claimed. The hoarded margin is this framework's equivalent of the unexamined beneficiary at step one: a finding that reorganises the politics of a case once it is stated, and one that only the estimation discipline, patiently applied, brings into view.

## The household as the state's silent partner

Step four's accounting deserves a section of its own at the level of the household, because the household is where the procedure's abstractions become biographies, and an analyst who cannot describe the mechanism at that level does not yet understand the step being applied. Every settlement between the state, employers, and wages rests on a foundation of unpaid work that appears in no budget: the cooking that makes the wage stretch, the care that makes the worker presentable to the working day, the kin networks that absorb the shocks, illness, unemployment, a child's crisis, that formal provision covers partially or not at all. The foundation is not a backdrop to policy. It is a variable inside every policy's true cost, because measures that strain the foundation consume it, and measures that support it replenish the capacity on which all the other payers silently rely. A state can run down its households the way it runs down its bridges, deferring the maintenance, booking the saving, and presenting the eventual collapse as an unforeseeable event in the private sphere.

The gendered distribution of the foundation's labour is the fact the analyst must state without flinching and without reducing the step to it. The unpaid work of reproduction falls predominantly on women, in every settlement and under every flag, and policy changes redistribute it among women before they redistribute it anywhere else: from the employed woman to the grandmother, from the mother to the older daughter, from the paid carer, whose job a cut abolishes, to the unpaid relative whose labour the cut conscripts. Step four's ledger, kept honestly, therefore records not only the movement of costs among state, employers, and households but the movement within households, along lines of sex and generation that the fiscal documents never mention. The analyst who keeps that inner ledger possesses an instrument of remarkable reach, because a striking share of social policy, interrogated about its inner-household incidence, turns out to be a negotiation about women's time conducted in a vocabulary of budgets, incentives, and modernisation, and the negotiation's terms improve markedly once one party to it, the analyst, insists on naming the currency.

Intergenerational transfers complete the household picture and connect it back to the long arc. The raising of children is a transfer from the working generations to the future; the support of the old is a transfer back; and the state's settlements mediate both, setting the terms on which the generations' obligations to each other are privately borne or publicly shared. A settlement that privatises both directions, leaving the young's formation to household resources and the old's maintenance to household care, loads the sandwich of obligations onto the same shoulders at the same years of life, and the loading shows up in the statistics of exhaustion, deferred fertility, and withdrawn labour that commentary treats as cultural mysteries. The procedure treats them as entries. Where a cost has been moved, the ledger shows the move; where the move's recipient is a household, the household's responses are data; and the analyst who follows the data from the budget to the kitchen table has performed the fourth step in its complete form, which is the form in which it justifies its place in a procedure that began, four steps earlier, with nothing more romantic than the instruction to follow the money. The money, followed far enough, always ends at somebody's unpaid hours, and the procedure's originality, such as it is, consists in refusing to stop following before it gets there.

The household lens also returns a dividend to the framework's centre, because it explains a regularity that purely fiscal readings of the constraint miss. Governments discovering the costs of defying investment conditions retreat, predictably, along the path of least organised resistance, and the path runs through the household: provision is trimmed where its users are dispersed, charges are raised where the payers cannot combine, conditions are tightened where the claimants negotiate singly, while the settlements protecting organised interests, including the interest of capital in its tax treatment, are disturbed last and least. Step six predicts the pattern from the organisational map, step four prices it in hours and strain, and step three explains why the retreat, when it comes, takes the shape it does. The three steps, converging on a single observable regularity from three directions, exhibit the procedure working as a system rather than as a list, and the convergence is the strongest internal evidence the article can offer that the steps were carved at the joints of the subject rather than at the joints of the tradition's vocabulary. A reader testing the article should test exactly there: find a retrenchment, run the three steps, and see whether the pattern they jointly predict is the pattern the record shows.

## What the procedure owes its critics, and what remains open

The framework assembled in this article did not develop by accumulating confirmations in a quiet study. It developed by being attacked, and its strongest components are the ones that were rebuilt under fire, a genealogy the analyst should know because it predicts where the framework is solid and where it is still scaffolding. Instrumentalism fell to the evidence its own researchers gathered, and structural dependence rose from the wreckage as the rebuild. Functionalism, the habit of explaining every institution by the need it serves, fell to the philosophy of science's demand for mechanisms, and the demand is honoured in this procedure's insistence that every step name its transmission belt, access, anticipation, cost schedule, household absorption, rather than intoning a function. The fiscal contradiction survived by being specified, in O'Connor's version, as an accounting tension with observable symptoms rather than as a destiny. Legitimation, as conceded, is still mid-rebuild, awaiting the operational discipline this article prescribed for it. A procedure with a genealogy of repairs is not thereby vindicated, but it is thereby locatable: the analyst can see which parts have been load-tested and which are newer than their confidence suggests, and can weight the worksheet's rows accordingly, trusting step three's mechanism as one trusts a bridge that has carried traffic for decades, and step five's second demand as one trusts a promising design still proving itself in weather.

Open questions remain, and the article closes its analytical business by leaving them open in an organised way, because a procedure's frontier is part of its teaching. The first concerns the constraint's operation where investment decisions are increasingly made by holders of mobile financial assets rather than by firms rooted in production, a shift that may steepen the gradient, shorten the state's warning time, and alter the politics of anticipation in ways the original formulation, built for an economy of plant and payrolls, only partly anticipates. The second concerns the reproduction settlement under demographic change, as ageing populations raise the legitimation family's claims on revenue while shrinking the working population whose activity funds them, tightening the fiscal contradiction from both sides in a configuration O'Connor's framework predicts in outline and history has yet to grade in detail. The third concerns organisation: whether the fragmentation techniques catalogued at step six meet their match in forms of association that new communications make possible, or whether the techniques adapt faster than the associations, is an empirical question on which the procedure's own concepts are, on the concepts alone, undecided, and the analyst should resist any version of the tradition that claims to know the answer in advance. The fourth concerns the boundary itself: the conditions under which states acquire room by reconstructing their dependence, pooling sovereignty, controlling capital movement, or building public investment capacity, are the conditions under which the framework's centre would need revision, and an analyst trained by this article should watch for them with genuine interest, since a framework whose defeater has been written down can afford to watch for its own defeat as a scientific event rather than as a bereavement.

The wider context in which this procedure sits should be located before the article turns to its audiences, because a method is partly defined by its neighbours. The account of the state that the procedure presupposes, and deliberately does not re-argue, is set out in the series guide devoted to the theory of the state. The welfare settlement that step five's contradiction most often manages is examined, in its social democratic form, at [marxism vs social democracy](/2019/11/15/marxism-vs-social-democracy/). The structural setting of the whole inquiry, the capitalism whose investment process does the constraining, is treated at [marxism and modern capitalism](/2020/11/01/marxism-and-modern-capitalism/). An analyst who has run the seven steps on a measure and wants to know what kind of state, what kind of settlement, and what kind of economy the findings imply will find those three companions waiting, and the procedure's modesty consists partly in knowing that its seven questions, however well answered, open onto those larger ones rather than closing them.

## Anticipation in the drafting room: the mechanism at closest range

The procedure's hinge, anticipation, has been invoked at several steps, and it merits extended treatment at close range, because it is the mechanism a working analyst will actually observe if the analyst ever gets near a drafting process, and because its micro-structure explains how a macro-constraint enters documents without leaving fingerprints. Anticipation begins as professional prudence. An official preparing options for a minister is expected to forecast consequences, and the forecast that matters most to the official's own standing is the forecast of trouble: which option will generate the headline, the market movement, the organised complaint, the resignation letter from an affected interest that a minister will have to answer. Options are therefore stress-tested asymmetrically from the first internal draft. The option that disturbs investment conditions is tested against a vivid, quantified, rapidly arriving reaction, modelled by the treasury, briefed by the financial press, and personified by identifiable firms whose postponement decisions can be telephoned and quoted. The option that disturbs a dispersed population is tested against a reaction that arrives as statistics, months later, attributable to many causes, personified by nobody who will be quoted, and the asymmetry of the testing is not a conspiracy against the dispersed. It is the rational allocation of an official's attention toward the reactions that can damage the official's minister first. Multiply the allocation across every drafting room, every appraisal, every spending review, and the gradient of step three has been manufactured, one prudent decision at a time, by people most of whom have never heard the argument this article is built on.

The close view also reveals anticipation's second layer, which is anticipation of anticipation. Officials do not merely forecast how investors will react to a measure; they predict how investors will respond to the forecast, how a signal of official nervousness will itself move the confidence it fears to disturb, and this recursion produces the characteristic public language of economic management, in which governments praise their own measures in the idiom of the audience whose reaction they are managing. Ministers announce that a budget is responsible, credible, and friendly to enterprise not only, and often not mainly, to persuade voters, but to perform the confidence whose absence they fear, in a theatre where the performance is part of the fact performed. The analyst reading such announcements at step seven should therefore grade them as moves inside the mechanism rather than as descriptions of it, and should listen, beneath the idiom of reassurance, for the tell-tale absences: the constituencies to whom no reassurance is performed, whose reactions require no management because they cannot be priced, and whose absence from the performance is the drafting room's asymmetry become audible.

A third layer completes the mechanism and returns it to step two. Anticipation is educated by access: the reactions that can be vividly imagined are the reactions of those the drafting room knows, meets, and can telephone, while the reactions of the unknown must be imagined from data, and data, however good, lacks the persuasive physiology of a voice. The revolving door and the consultation list thus matter to the structural argument itself, not as rival explanations of it, because they determine whose anticipated reaction arrives in the room with a face. The procedure's architecture, which places representation immediately before constraint, encodes exactly this relation: step two supplies the dramatis personae of step three's imagination, and an analyst who finds a reaction anticipated with unusual vividness should ask, as a matter of routine, what channel carried the vividness in. The question converts the structural argument from a claim about faceless forces into an investigative programme with leads, and leads are what separate an analysis that can be extended from a thesis that can only be repeated.

None of this requires the anticipated reactions to be accurate, and the analyst should mark the point carefully, because it is where the mechanism is most often misunderstood by its own defenders. Investors may misjudge a measure, may punish a government for costs the measure will never impose, may reward recklessness and penalise prudence for reasons of herd, prejudice, or fashion, and the constraint operates through the anticipation regardless, since it is the anticipated reaction, not the warranted one, that the drafting room designs around. The framework therefore predicts something subtle and checkable: policy will track the perceived interests of capital, as those interests are understood inside the state's own forecasting machinery, and will diverge from capital's actual interests wherever perception and reality part. That divergence is a gift to the analyst, because it explains a class of cases, measures that damage firms while genuflecting toward them, measures that serve firms while believing they defy them, that cruder versions of the framework must either deny or dissolve in legitimation talk. A mechanism that can be wrong about its object, and whose errors leave evidence, is a mechanism an empirical discipline can work with, and anticipation, specified at this closeness, is such a mechanism.

## Distribution beyond money: status, security, and voice as policy currencies

The first step taught the analyst to follow flows, and the flows were introduced in money's company, cash, kind, exemption, and risk, but a completed analysis must widen the ledger, because policies distribute at least three further currencies whose movements often matter more to the affected than the money does. The first is security, the degree to which a household can plan across time without the ground moving: a measure can leave incomes untouched and still impoverish by making them unpredictable, converting a steady entitlement into a reviewed one, a permanent post into a renewable contract, a known rent into a market-tested one, and the impoverishment is real though no flow map drawn in currency will show it. The second is status, the public standing a measure confers or withdraws: provision received as a citizen differs, in the life of the recipient, from the identical provision received as a supplicant, and policies trade in the difference deliberately, raising the standing of some recipients as contributors and veterans while lowering others into the examined and the suspected. The third is voice, the capacity to be heard in the processes that will decide the next measure: a policy that creates a consultative body, a legal standing, a recognised association, distributes voice, and a policy that dissolves them confiscates it, and the distribution of voice compounds across time in a way money does not, because voice spent in one round purchases influence over every later round's distribution of everything else.

Widening the ledger disciplines two opposite errors at once. Against the crude materialism that recognises only cash, the widened ledger insists that a reform can be a redistribution of the first importance while moving almost no money, and that some of the most contested measures in any policy landscape are contested precisely because their real transfers run in security, status, and voice while their budgets are trivial. Against the opposite error, the cultural reading that floats free of material flows altogether, the widened ledger insists that the three currencies are distributed by the same machinery as the money, through eligibility rules, administrative forms, and funding decisions, and that their movements can be traced with the same documentary patience step one taught. Security is withdrawn in clauses. Status is conferred in categories. Voice is allocated in the membership lists of bodies and the standing rules of courts and tribunals. An analyst who learns to read those documents for the three currencies possesses the completed form of the first step, and will find, returning to measures analysed years earlier in money alone, that the re-reading explains the political afterlife of measures whose financial grading never could: the reform that was cheap and is still hated, the programme that was expensive and is still defended, each carrying a distribution in the wider currencies that the money ledger missed.

The widened ledger also sharpens step five, because the two demands the state must satisfy are not denominated only in money either. Legitimation is largely a trade in status and security: a population consents, in the long run, less to a level of transfers than to a sense that its standing is recognised and its future is plannable, and governments that protect incomes while dissolving security discover that the legitimation they purchased does not arrive. Accumulation, likewise, trades in voice and status as well as in returns: the investor's confidence examined at step three is partly a judgement about whether the holders of capital will be heard, respected, and treated as partners in the national story, which is why governments perform partnership so assiduously and why the performance, catalogued at step seven, is functional rather than merely decorative. The contradiction, restated in the wider currencies, is the state's need to distribute recognition in two directions at once, honouring the contributor whose investment it needs and the citizen whose consent it needs, in a public language where honour shown to one is often heard as slight by the other. Much of the rhetorical difficulty of modern government, its contorted vocabularies of fairness, its simultaneous address to incompatible audiences, is the sound of that restated contradiction being managed in real time, and the analyst who can hear it has acquired the last refinement this procedure offers before its audiences are addressed directly.

## The analyst's stance: neutrality of method, honesty of valuation

This article has declined throughout to cheer or condemn any family of measures, and the decline should now be explained rather than merely practised, because readers reasonably ask whether a Marxist analysis can be neutral and what its neutrality could possibly mean. The procedure's neutrality is a neutrality of method: the seven steps are run in the same order, with the same evidence demands, on measures the analyst admires and on measures the analyst deplores, and the worksheet's entries are not permitted to vary with the analyst's wishes. A subsidy examined in this article emerged with credits and charges in a single sentence, and the same even-handedness is owed to a measure of the opposite family, whose flow map may embarrass its sponsors and whose organisational consequences may embarrass its critics. Method neutrality of this kind is not a claim to have no politics. It is a claim about the order of operations: findings first, gathered under rules that bind the gatherer, and valuation second, stated in the open as the analyst's own, where a reader who rejects the valuation can still use the findings. The analyst who reverses the order, valuing first and gathering under the valuation's direction, produces work that only the already convinced can use, and a procedure whose findings cannot travel across political disagreement has failed at the task this article set it in its opening paragraphs.

Honesty of valuation is the counterpart discipline, and it forbids the analyst a refuge that method neutrality can tempt: the pretence that the findings speak a politics by themselves, so that the analyst's own commitments need never be declared. Findings do not speak. The flow map does not announce whether the distribution it records is just; the cost schedule does not announce whether the room it measures should have been spent on defiance; the contradiction does not announce which demand deserves to win. An analyst who lets the worksheet appear to decide these questions has smuggled a politics into a procedure and denied the reader the chance to locate it, which is the precise offence step seven prosecutes in governments' justifications. The completed analysis therefore ends with its valuations labelled as valuations, argued for in the analyst's own voice, and separated from the findings by a visible seam. Readers who share the valuations gain an ally who can be trusted about evidence. Readers who reject them gain an instrument they can still use, and the instrument's usability by opponents is, as the pluralist parallel showed, the strongest evidence a framework can offer that it has found something real.

The stance has a final implication for how the tradition itself should be read, and with it the analytical sections of the article close. A tradition is not dishonoured by having its weakest components identified from inside, as Block identified instrumentalism, nor by having its strongest component claimed simultaneously by an outsider, as Lindblom claimed the constraint. It is dishonoured only by the refusal to grade its own inheritance, the treatment of every inherited claim as equally load-bearing, which forces its defenders to spend their credibility protecting the indefensible joints while the tested centre goes under-recognised. The seven steps, graded as this article has graded them, centre strong, legitimation joint weak, direction predicted better than content, evidence for constraint thinner per unit than evidence for access, are offered as the inheritance honestly graded. What the reader does with the grading, in the classroom, in the seminar, in the ministry, in the newsroom, is the subject of the three audience sections that follow, and of the questions and answers that close the article.

## Incidence in depth: how burdens travel through prices, wages, and rents

The first step's distinction between formal and economic incidence deserves a fuller treatment, because the travelling of burdens is where popular commentary makes its most confident and most avoidable errors. A levy is announced against a payer, and the announcement settles the public's understanding, while the levy's real journey begins at that moment and ends somewhere the announcement never mentions. Burdens travel along the channels of least resistance in a market: toward the party who can least easily withdraw from the transaction, toward the consumer whose demand is least elastic, toward the worker whose alternative employments are scarcest, toward the tenant whose need for shelter in a particular place binds hardest. Elasticity, the economist's word for the ability to walk away, is therefore a distributional variable of the first rank, and an analyst who learns to ask, of every charge, who in this chain can walk away and who cannot, possesses a portable instrument that works on levies, on mandates, on regulations whose compliance costs behave exactly like levies, and on benefits whose value is captured, in whole or part, by the party positioned to raise a price against them.

Consider the generic anatomy of a charge placed on employers in respect of each worker employed. The formal payer is the firm. The charge then divides itself, in proportions no statute controls, among lower money wages than would otherwise have been paid, higher prices than would otherwise have been charged, thinner margins than would otherwise have been earned, and fewer hires at the margin than would otherwise have been made. The proportions are set by the conditions of the particular market: where labour is scarce and organised, wages resist and the charge travels toward prices and margins; where labour is plentiful and unorganised, wages absorb; where the product faces foreign competition, prices resist and the charge travels toward hiring. An analyst pronouncing on such a charge from its formal incidence alone is not simplifying. The analyst is wrong, in a direction that can be specified, and the specification is the analysis. The same anatomy, reversed, governs benefits: a subsidy toward rents travels toward landlords where housing is scarce, toward tenants where it is plentiful, and the political argument about the subsidy is empty until the housing market's tightness has been established as a fact prior to the argument.

Rents, in the economist's older sense of payments above what a factor's supply requires, are the destination where travelling burdens and benefits most often come to rest, and the analyst should cultivate a nose for them. Wherever supply is fixed by nature, by licence, or by planning restriction, value injected into a market pools there: assistance toward purchase raises the price of the fixed stock, assistance toward fees raises the fees of the restricted providers, and the assistance's intended beneficiaries find themselves running to stand still while the owners of the fixed factor collect the programme. This is not an argument against assistance. It is an argument for reading assistance through the supply conditions of its market, a reading that sometimes recommends assisting the supply rather than the demand, sometimes recommends capturing the rent by taxation as the assistance flows, and always recommends against the innocence that treats a transfer's destination as settled by its address. The first step, completed at this depth, has quietly become a theory of markets in miniature, which is as it should be: a procedure for analysing policy that lacked an account of how markets move burdens would be a procedure for reading announcements, and announcements are the one text the analyst was warned, at the outset, never to mistake for the policy.

## Agenda-setting in depth: how problem definitions are manufactured

Step two named agenda-setting as the quiet mechanism, and its manufacture deserves inspection, because the definition of a problem is the most consequential and least examined artefact in the entire policy process. Definitions are manufactured in identifiable places: in the terms of reference of commissions, which decide in advance which questions a inquiry may ask; in the statistical series a state chooses to collect, which decide which conditions become visible as problems and which remain private troubles; in the professional consensus of the relevant experts, which decides which explanations are respectable; and in the media's standing frames, which decide which events count as instances of a problem and which count as accidents. A condition that lacks a series, a commission, a profession, and a frame does not exist politically, however many households it governs, and a condition that acquires all four becomes a problem with a budget attached. The analyst reconstructing an agenda should therefore begin before the beginning, asking not which remedies were debated but when the condition acquired its instruments of visibility, and whose work the acquisition was.

The manufacture has a characteristic sequence that recurs often enough to be taught. A condition is first endured as fate, attributed to nature, to providence, or to the character of those who suffer it. It is then counted, usually by an outsider, a reformer, a profession seeking jurisdiction, an opposition seeking a weapon, and the counting converts fate into a quantity with a trend. The quantity is then named, and the naming is the decisive political act, because the name selects the cause: a name that locates the cause in individual conduct summons remedies of correction and condition, a name blaming market structure summons remedies of regulation and provision, while a name that traces it to the organisation of production summons remedies that the agenda process will, under the constraint of step three, find reasons to defer. Finally the named problem is assigned to an administrative home, and the home's existing instruments become the problem's thinkable solutions, so that a problem housed with the police becomes a problem of order, housed with the treasury a problem of cost, housed with the educators a problem of formation. The analyst who can narrate a problem's passage through endurance, counting, naming, and housing has located the points where the agenda could have gone otherwise, and those points, not the later debates among remedies, are where the politics of the measure were most densely concentrated.

Two implications follow for practice. The first is that the most powerful actors in a policy process may be those who never appear in its consultation record at all: the statisticians who designed the series, the drafters of the commission's remit, the editors of the frame, none of whom decided anything about the measure and all of whom decided what the measure could be about. Step two's evidence base must therefore extend beyond the process's own documents to the instruments of visibility that preceded it, and the analyst should treat a problem's official history, the story the process tells about how the problem arose, as an artefact requiring explanation rather than as the explanation itself. The second implication concerns the analyst's own agenda, which is also manufactured, by training, by the literature's fashions, by the availability of data, and the honest analyst audits it with the same instruments: which conditions in the measure's territory lack series, lack names, lack administrative homes, and might therefore be invisible in the analysis for exactly the reasons they were invisible in the policy. An analysis that finds only the problems the process found has been captured by the agenda it set out to examine, and the capture is the more complete for feeling like common sense.

## Legitimation, operationalised: populations, mechanisms, and tests

The article conceded that legitimation is the framework's weak joint and prescribed a discipline for it; this section supplies the discipline in working form, because a prescription without a procedure is another slogan. A legitimation claim, to earn its place in a worksheet, must complete three entries. It must identify whose consent, acquiescence, or demobilisation is at stake, specified closely enough that the population's behaviour can be observed: not the public, not ordinary people, but a group with a location in the class structure, a relation to the measure, and channels through which its temper registers. It must name the mechanism, the route by which the measure is supposed to secure that temper: a transfer that relieves a grievance, a recognition that repairs a standing, a procedure that performs fairness, a punishment that dramatises protection. And it must name the test, the observable pattern that would betray a failed purchase: rising abstention in the population's districts, organisational growth among its associations, industrial action in its workplaces, migration of its allegiance to challengers who name the grievance the measure was meant to quiet. A claim completing all three entries is a hypothesis. A claim completing fewer is a mood, and moods are struck from the worksheet under the rule this article announced.

The operationalisation changes what counts as a legitimation success, and the change is bracing. Many measures celebrated as legitimation triumphs fail the test entry on inspection: the transfer arrived, the grievance persisted, the population's behaviour registered no gratitude, because the mechanism was misconceived, the population valued the standing the measure withheld more than the money it offered, or the grievance was never the one the designers imagined. Conversely, measures dismissed as merely symbolic sometimes pass, because the mechanism was recognition and the population's behaviour shows the recognition landing. The discipline thus protects the analyst from the framework's own temptation, which is to treat legitimation as the state's cunning and every outcome as its success. States misjudge consent constantly; they purchase the wrong populations, misprice the grievance, and discover the error in the behaviour the test entry watches. A framework that can register the state's legitimation failures as failures, rather than re-describing them as deeper successes, has taken the largest single step available toward answering the unfalsifiability charge in practice rather than in preface.

There is a further refinement the operationalisation makes possible, and it concerns time. Consent is not purchased once but rented, and the rent falls due repeatedly, at intervals set by the population's memory and by the arrival of fresh grievances. Measures differ in the durability of the consent they buy: a transfer buys until the transfer is absorbed into expectations, whereupon its withdrawal registers as theft while its continuation registers as nothing; a recognition buys longer, because standing, once conferred, becomes part of the population's self-understanding and defends itself; a procedural fairness buys longest of all where it is believed, and collapses fastest where the belief is punctured by a single vivid betrayal. The analyst grading a settlement should therefore record not only the legitimation purchased but the lease's term and the renewal price, because settlements that look equally stable in the year of their making differ enormously in what their maintenance will cost the budgets of the future, and step five's contradiction, which is a theory of exactly those future budgets, prices the difference.

## Fiscal strain without melodrama: reading the state's accounts as a settlement under pressure

Fiscal strain is the symptom through which the fifth step's contradiction most often announces itself, and the analyst needs a way of reading it that avoids the two melodramas between which public discussion oscillates: the melodrama of bankruptcy, in which every deficit is a nation living beyond its means, and the melodrama of denial, in which every shortfall is a bookkeeping illusion that only the timid fear. The structural reading replaces both with a set of questions about composition. What is the strain composed of: which expenditures grew because the accumulation process required support, which grew because consent required purchase, which grew because earlier displacements, debt taken on to postpone a settlement, have come home as service charges on the present? Which revenues were forgone, and at whose insistence, and under what anticipation of reaction? Strain read compositionally stops being a moral condition and becomes a map of the settlement's stresses, showing where the armistice between the two demands is thinnest and where the next improvisation is most likely to be attempted.

The compositional reading also explains the characteristic rhetoric of fiscal emergencies, which step seven can then process as evidence. In an emergency the two demands do not disappear; they are re-ranked, publicly, with legitimation expenditure reclassified as indulgence and accumulation expenditure reclassified as necessity, and the reclassification is performed in a vocabulary of national household management whose function is to make a political ranking sound like an arithmetic one. The analyst who has already mapped the settlement can watch the reclassification with precision, noting which items cross the line from necessity to indulgence and which are protected from crossing, and the pattern of protection is a finding about the balance of forces at that moment more reliable than any speech. Emergencies also reveal the hierarchy of the state's creditors and clients in an order that calm times conceal: whose payments are guaranteed first, whose are delayed, whose contracts are honoured and whose are renegotiated, whose incomes are indexed against the emergency and whose are left to absorb it. The hierarchy, once observed, should be recorded in the worksheet as a durable fact about the settlement, because it will govern the next emergency too, and the analyst who recorded it will read that emergency faster and more accurately than the commentary that treats each crisis as a premiere.

A final discipline governs the reading of strain over long periods. Settlements do not usually collapse; they erode, through the accumulation of small displacements whose individual costs were each judged bearable: maintenance deferred, provision thinned, eligibility tightened by increments, charges introduced as exceptions and normalised as rules. The analyst's long records should therefore track the settlement's quality dimensions and not only its expenditure dimensions, because a state can hold its spending roughly constant while the settlement it funds hollows out, the service scarcer, the queue longer, the entitlement narrower, the consent it purchases thinner with every cycle. Erosion is the fiscal contradiction's commonest modern form, and it is nearly invisible to commentary organised around annual totals. It is fully visible to a worksheet that asks, of each successive budget, the fourth step's question about where the costs went, and finds them, year after year, in the same place: inside households, absorbed as time and strain, by the payers of last resort whom the accounts never name.

## Organisation as a policy product: associations, unions, and employer bodies

Step six asserted that policy manufactures the organisational landscape it later confronts, and the assertion can be given a history-shaped form without naming any place or period. States teach interests to organise by rewarding organisation: a consultation machinery that receives delegations teaches every affected interest to form a delegation; a licensing regime that negotiates with a profession teaches the profession to maintain a negotiator; a funding stream allocated by competitive bidding teaches institutions to become bidders, with bid-writers, partnerships of convenience, and a shared interest in the bidding's continuation. The landscape of associations surrounding any mature policy field is therefore largely an artefact of the field's own procedures, and the analyst mapping step two's representation pattern for a new measure should read the existing landscape as the sediment of previous measures, asking of each association not only what it wants but what procedure called it into being and keeps it fed. Associations also die by procedure: withdraw the consultation, change the funding's form, individualise the benefit, and the corresponding organisation withers, not because its members' interests vanished but because the state's machinery stopped supplying the occasions and resources that held the organisation together.

The asymmetry that the worked example found, employers organised by the very machinery that individuates workers, has a general explanation the analyst should carry. Capital's organisational costs are subsidised by the firm itself: association is a business expense, its staff are employees, its coordination rides on the same structures that coordinate production and sale. Labour's organisational costs are carried by the organised themselves, out of wages and evenings, against the free-rider problem that every member benefits from the association's victories whether or not the member pays its costs. Policy can narrow or widen that asymmetry deliberately or by neglect: legal frameworks for recognition and dues, facilities for representatives, protections against retaliation, all shift the relative price of organising on the two sides, and shifts in that price are among the most consequential and least debated contents of the statute book. The analyst grading a measure at step six should therefore always ask the price question: what does this measure do to the cost of combining, for each affected group, and the answer, assembled across a government's whole output, describes a strategy of class capacity whether or not any strategist ever convened to design it.

Organisational findings also carry the procedure's most practical advice to the citizens who are its ultimate readers. A population that dislikes a settlement's terms and wishes to change them faces, before any argument about the terms, a question about its own capacity: does it possess the associations through which a grievance becomes a demand, the continuity through which a demand survives a defeat, the expertise through which a demand can be costed against the constraint of step three? Where the answer is no, the prior political task is organisational, and policies that build capacity, however modest their immediate transfers, may matter more to the population's future than policies that transfer generously while leaving the recipients as individuated as they were found. The tradition this article draws on has a name for the confusion between the two kinds of gain, and the name is less important than the distinction: a benefit consumed is gone, a capacity built compounds, and step six exists so that the analyst never grades the first as though it were the second.

## Numbers as ideology: classification, categories, and the politics of the count

Step seven examined justification in prose; its numerical counterpart deserves equal suspicion and equal precision, because the categories through which a state counts are choices with distributional consequences, and they are usually older, quieter, and harder to contest than any speech. Who counts as unemployed is a definition, and the definition decides whether discouragement counts, whether an hour of work in a reference week counts as employment, whether those on schemes vanish from the register they were counted into. Who counts as poor is a line, and the line's placement, absolute or relative, before or after housing costs, decides the size of the problem a government must be seen to solve. What counts as public spending excludes, by convention, the tax expenditures that step one taught the analyst to list as spending's twins, and the exclusion flatters every government's account of its own size. The analyst does not need to allege manipulation in any of this. Conventions have reasons, and most have histories of honest technical dispute. The analyst's point is prior to honesty: the conventions are settlements, they distribute visibility, and visibility is a resource in the politics the other steps describe, since an uncounted cost cannot be campaigned about and an uncounted population cannot be organised as a category at step six.

The politics of the count has a rhythm the analyst can learn to hear. Categories are contested most fiercely at the moments when they are about to move a headline, and the contest is conducted in the idiom of methodology, which is the idiom in which modern states prefer their politics to be conducted because it excludes the laity from the room. Methodological disputes deserve the analyst's respect and the analyst's translation: behind nearly every dispute about a definition stands a dispute about the size of a problem, the credit for a trend, or the blame for a level, and the translation, performed carefully and without imputing bad faith, restores the dispute to the public whose numbers are being defined. There is also a slower rhythm, the drift of categories behind the realities they were built to capture, as forms of work, household, and tenure change while the series go on counting the world of their design. Drift produces a distinctive analytical hazard: long runs of apparently consistent data describing a dissolving object, and policies aimed, with great precision, at populations that no longer exist in the counted form. The worksheet's defence is the habit of asking, of every series the analysis relies on, when its categories were fixed and what has moved since, a question that costs an afternoon and has saved more analyses than any other single habit this article teaches.

None of this licenses the lazy conclusion that numbers are merely politics and therefore worthless, a conclusion the analyst should refuse as firmly as the innocence it replaces. Counts constrain their makers: a series honestly maintained will embarrass its sponsors when the trend turns, a definition publicly fixed cannot be quietly moved when it becomes inconvenient, and the state's statistical machinery, for all its settled conventions, remains one of the few instruments through which the powerless can prove, in the state's own arithmetic, that a settlement is failing them. The procedure's stance toward official numbers is therefore double and should stay double: use them, gratefully and constantly, since steps one, three, and five cannot be worked without them, and audit them, permanently, since the categories are part of the policy under analysis and not a neutral window placed in front of it.

## Comparison as method: what contrasting settlements can and cannot show

The procedure's claims are about patterns, and patterns require comparison, so the analyst needs a disciplined account of what contrasting cases can establish. Comparison across political systems, conducted at the level of mechanisms rather than of national characters, is the strongest instrument available for testing step three, because systems differ in exactly the variables the estimation section assembled: revenue bases, debt structures, capital mobility, organisational landscapes. Where two systems with unlike governments and unlike political cultures converge on protecting the conditions for investment while diverging in distribution, services, and rhetoric, the convergence is the dependence argument's home ground, and the divergences map the room that dependence leaves open. Where a system departs from the convergence, the departure is the most valuable datum of all, provided the analyst interrogates it for the estimation variables before celebrating or deploring it: an unusual revenue source, an unusual debt position, an unusual closure of capital's exit, an unusual strength of organised counterweight. Departures explained by the variables confirm the framework by specifying it. Departures explained by none of them are the beginnings of the defeater's record, and the analyst committed to the framework should collect them with more care than its critics do, because a framework's own anomalies, honestly kept, are its research programme's agenda.

Comparison across time within one system is the second instrument, and it corrects the first's characteristic weakness, since systems differ in a thousand ways besides the variables of interest, while one system's history holds much constant and varies the settlement. The analyst reads a single polity's successive settlements as a series of experiments conducted by history: the same state, the same broad class structure, different balances of organisation, different fiscal positions, different governments, and watches the constraint's gradient steepen and slacken with the estimation variables while the settlement migrates within the boundary. The within-system series is also where step six earns its keep most visibly, because the organisational landscape changes slowly enough to be tracked as a variable rather than assumed as a backdrop, and the analyst can watch capacity built by one settlement being spent by its successor, or capacity dismantled by one settlement constraining what its successor's majorities can even attempt.

The comparative discipline has failure modes of its own, and they should be catalogued with the same candour as the framework's. The first is cherry-picking, the selection of cases by their conclusions, fatal to pattern claims and detectable by a simple rule: the analyst must state the case selection principle before stating the findings, and the principle must be independent of the outcomes. The second is the false parallel, the treatment of superficially similar measures in unlike systems as instances of one thing, when the measures' incidence, administration, and organisational meaning differ so deeply that the comparison compares labels. The third is the frozen contrast, the comparison of one system's present with another's past, or of a settlement's maturity with another's infancy, which manufactures differences out of timing. Guarded against the three, comparison converts the procedure from a way of reading single policies into a way of testing the framework that produced the readings, and the conversion matters because the article's central promise was never that the seven steps interpret well. It was that they can be wrong in ways the record can show, and comparison is where the record is largest.

## Policy in emergencies: when the settlement is suspended and revealed

Emergencies, wars of nature or of finance, epidemics, sudden collapses of demand, are the policy analyst's equivalent of the stress test, because a state under emergency conditions does in weeks what its peacetime procedures do in decades, and the speed exposes the settlement's load-bearing structures. Three regularities reward the analyst who watches emergencies with the worksheet open. The first is the sudden widening of the thinkable: measures that the constraint's anticipation had ruled impossible, direct provision, commandeered capacity, income support untied to conditionality, are enacted overnight when the alternative is the collapse of the order the constraint exists to protect, and their enactment proves something the framework always claimed, that the boundary is a cost schedule and not a physical law, since schedules can be paid when the price of refusal rises high enough. The second regularity is the emergency's incidence, which follows the old channels with brutal fidelity: the costs of the suspension fall along the lines of step four's ledger, into the households of those whose work cannot be done from safety, whose savings cannot bridge an interruption, whose care obligations cannot be paused, while the protections cluster where organisation and anticipation have prepared them. The third is the restoration, in which the emergency settlement is dismantled in an order that reveals the hierarchy the calm concealed: supports for the dispersed are withdrawn first and fastest, supports that have created organised constituencies survive longer, and the supports flowing to capital are normalised into the permanent settlement under new names, their emergency origins forgotten.

The emergency also tests the framework at a point where its critics expect it to fail, and the analyst should conduct the test honestly. If the state can suspend the constraint in an emergency, why is the constraint more than a peacetime habit? The framework's answer distinguishes the suspension's duration and finance from its existence: emergency measures are typically financed by the state's own borrowing on a scale that presupposes the future restoration of the conditions under which the debt can be carried, which is to say they borrow against the constraint's return, and the borrowing's terms, set by the same lenders whose anticipation governs peacetime, price the suspension from its first day. A suspension that attempted to become permanent, that proposed to govern indefinitely on emergency instruments, would meet the schedule in its full form, and the historical record of such attempts is, in the framework's reading, a record of the schedule being presented in currencies ranging from inflation to capital flight to the quiet strangulation of supply. The reading can be contested, and the contest belongs in the worksheet's margin, but the analyst should notice what the emergency evidence does to the crude version of the argument on both sides: it refutes the claim that governments cannot act against investment conditions, and it refutes, with the restoration's order of withdrawal, the claim that their peacetime reluctance to do so is a mere failure of will.

For the working analyst the emergency's chief lesson is archival. Emergency processes generate documents at a speed and candour that peacetime withholds: options papers written in days, reactions anticipated in writing, hierarchies of protection stated as operational priorities. The analyst who collects an emergency's paper trail acquires a map of the settlement's true wiring, drawn by the settlement's own engineers under pressure, and that map remains valid when calm returns and the wiring is re-concealed beneath procedure. Every mature policy field has its emergency archive, and the procedure's students should be sent to it early, because a single week inside an emergency's documents teaches the relation among the seven steps more vividly than a term of peacetime case studies, and the vividness, unlike the emergency, can be made permanent in the analyst's habits.

## Housing as a policy field: the worksheet on bricks and tenure

Housing is the field where the procedure's steps interlock most visibly for a lay reader, because everyone has watched a housing measure announced and then watched the housing remain unaffordable, and the gap between the announcement and the outcome is the worksheet's home territory. Step one follows assistance into a market whose supply is fixed by land, by permission, and by the construction industry's capacity, and finds the assistance pooling, as the incidence section predicted, in the price of the fixed stock and the rents of its owners. The making of housing policy shows a representation pattern in which the organised interests, landowners, developers, lenders, and the associations of existing owners, possess every advantage of anticipation and access, while the interest of the unhoused and the insecurely housed is dispersed among people whose defining condition is the absence of the stability that organisation requires. At the fourth step the field becomes reproduction in its most literal form: shelter is the first cost of renewing a workforce, its price sets the wage a household must command before any other need is addressed, and its insecurity consumes the time, health, and schooling continuity that the rest of the reproduction settlement presupposes.

The fifth step locates housing's characteristic contradiction with unusual clarity. Accumulation requires housing as an asset: a store of value, a collateral base for credit, a vehicle through which households are induced to save and banks to lend. Legitimation requires housing as a home: secure, affordable, available in the places where work is. The two requirements impose opposite price movements on the same stock, and policy in the field is the long management of that opposition, through instruments that subsidise entry without lowering prices, guarantee credit without expanding supply, and protect existing owners' values in the language of protecting communities. The sixth step finds the field's organisational signature along the tenure line: owners are constituted as a constituency with a shared interest in scarcity, renters are individuated by contract and mobility, and the line between them, policed by deposit requirements and credit assessments, is among the most consequential class boundaries a modern settlement draws, dividing populations whose incomes may be identical by their relation to an appreciating asset. The seventh step reads the field's master justification, the ladder, whose imagery converts a distributional structure into a biography of effort, and the analyst's reply is by now familiar: restore the agents, list the alternatives, and ask what the metaphor retires from argument, which is the value of a stock that cannot expand and the interests that value enriches.

The field also supplies the procedure with one of its cleanest demonstrations that direction is predicted better than content. Analysts working from the constraint and the contradiction can predict, of any housing settlement, that it will protect asset values while professing affordability, that its assistance will flow disproportionately toward demand rather than supply, and that its casualties will be recorded as personal failures of saving or timing. They cannot predict the instruments, the thresholds, the names, or the timing, and the honest housing analysis says so, grading governments by the room they had and the use they made of it within a field where the room is narrower, and the organised defence of the existing distribution stronger, than in almost any other the worksheet will meet.

## Health provision as a policy field: care, cost, and the body's ledger

Health provision tests the procedure differently, because the field's moral language is so strong that analysts of every camp resist the worksheet's first instruction, and the resistance itself is data. Step one insists on following the money through a field that prefers to speak of compassion, and the following repays the insistence: provision is funded from taxation, from contributions, from charges, and from the unpaid labour of households, in proportions that decide whose illness becomes whose cost. The funding mix is the field's constitution. A system funded generally and supplied freely at the point of need has socialised the risk of illness across the whole population; a system funded by employment-linked contributions has tied the body's security to the labour market's fortunes; a system funded substantially by charges and private payment has left the risk where illness itself falls, on the unlucky, graded by their capacity to pay. Step four then reads the same mix as the reproduction settlement in its most intimate register, since the maintenance of bodies is the literal core of reproducing labour power, and every queue, charge, and exclusion is a decision about how much bodily repair the society socialises and how much it returns to the household to finance, to perform, or to forgo.

The field's political economy has a distinctive feature the analyst should mark, because it complicates the framework in a productive way. The demand for health provision is nearly universal and intensely felt, the providers are organised and prestigious, and the costs are driven by technologies whose suppliers operate in global markets, so the legitimation pressure toward generous provision is stronger here than in almost any other field, while the fiscal pressure against it grows with every success, since provision that works extends lives into the ages where provision is most expensive. Settlements in the field therefore exhibit the fifth step's contradiction in a pure and permanent form, and their characteristic politics is the politics of the queue and the threshold: rationing conducted by waiting, by eligibility, by the quiet geography of where facilities stand, rather than by price, where the funding is public, or by price with unusual nakedness where it is not. The analyst grading a health settlement should resist both available sentimentalities, the one that reads every limit as betrayal and the one that reads each limit as necessity, and should instead map the rationing's incidence: whose waiting, whose travel, whose forgone treatment absorbs the gap between the demand the field generates and the revenue the constraint allows, because that incidence, and not the field's rhetoric of universality or of efficiency, is the settlement's true signature.

Health also returns the analyst to the household with particular force, since the boundary between formal provision and informal care runs through every treatment plan ever written. Discharge is a transfer of nursing to households. Prevention is a transfer of vigilance. The management of chronic conditions is a transfer of skilled, continuous, unpaid labour, predominantly women's, on a scale that dwarfs the formal system's own workforce, and settlements that economise on formal provision are, in the fourth step's ledger, expanding that conscription while recording the expansion as efficiency. The analyst who prices the conscription, even roughly, in hours, possesses the field's most subversive statistic, and the one most likely to reorganise a reader's understanding of what the settlement costs and who pays it.

## Old age as a policy field: pensions, time, and the intergenerational settlement

Provision for old age is the policy field where time, the first step's fourth dimension, dominates everything else, because a pension settlement is a promise stretched across half a century, made by one generation of workers and taxpayers to another, under conditions neither can foresee. Step one reads the field's flows as an intergenerational transfer wearing several costumes: the pay-as-you-go costume, in which current workers' contributions pay current pensions and the system's real asset is the future's willingness to continue the arrangement; the funded costume, in which contributions purchase assets whose value depends on the investment process the third step describes, so that the pensioner's security rests on the same accumulation the pensioner no longer participates in; and the household costume, in which the transfer travels as unpaid care and shared housing inside families, unrecorded in either of the other costumes' accounts. Every real settlement mixes the three, and the analyst's first task is to establish the mixture, because arguments about pension generosity that ignore the household costume are arguments about the visible fraction of the field.

Step five finds the contradiction in a distinctive tense. The accumulation demand presses toward pension arrangements that channel savings into investment and keep the retired population's claims on current output within the limits the revenue allows. The legitimation demand presses toward adequacy and security in old age, since the treatment of the old is a standing advertisement, read by every worker, of what the settlement is finally worth. Demographic change tightens both screws at once, raising the number of claimants relative to contributors while extending the years each claim runs, and the field's politics becomes a competition of displacements: the retirement age moved, which displaces the cost onto the bodies of older workers; the indexation formula adjusted, which displaces it onto the pensioners' future purchasing power in annual increments too small to organise against; the contribution base widened or the funded element expanded, each displacing toward different shoulders. Step six watches the displacements divide the field's populations, pensioners against workers, public schemes against private, cohorts just inside a reform's boundary against cohorts just outside it, and notes how reliably the divisions follow the lines the reforms themselves drew, fragmenting what might otherwise be the largest single constituency in the entire welfare settlement.

The field's deepest lesson for the procedure concerns the credibility of promises across time, which is the state's scarcest asset and the one the constraint governs most subtly. A pension promise is believed, and therefore functions, only while the population expects the settlement to survive, and the expectation rests on the same foundations as investor confidence, the anticipated continuity of the fiscal and political order. Governments therefore defend pension promises with a tenacity they grant to few other commitments, not from sentiment but because the promise's credibility is load-bearing for the whole intergenerational structure, and analysts should read reforms in the field as negotiations with that credibility: how much can be trimmed before the expectation cracks, and what happens to saving, to labour supply, and to consent if it does. The old, in this field, are not a residual category of policy. They are the settlement's memory and its prophecy, the one population every other population expects to join, which is why the field's politics are simultaneously the most conservative and the most explosive in the entire policy landscape.

## Industrial support as a policy field: picking winners and the discipline of the constraint

Support for industry, through subsidies, guarantees, protected procurement, and directed credit, is the field where the state's relation to accumulation is least disguised, and for that reason it is an unusually honest classroom for the procedure. Representation is at its most legible here: the recipients are few, organised, and expert, the costs are dispersed across taxpayers who will never identify their contribution, and the agenda is set by a definition of competitiveness whose terms the recipients themselves help to draft. Step three, however, complicates the picture in the framework's favour, because industrial support is where governments most visibly attempt to act on the constraint rather than merely obey it, building capacities whose absence made the economy dependent on decisions taken elsewhere, and the attempts' mixed record is the best available education in the difference between room and wish. Support succeeds, where it succeeds, when it is disciplined by conditions the recipient must meet, by horizons after which it ends, and by the state's capacity to withdraw it from failure, and it fails, characteristically, when the representation pattern captures the discipline, converting temporary support into a permanent entitlement of the organised, defended by the jobs it now employs as hostages.

The field therefore refines the article's central grading habit. A government supporting industry is neither simply serving capital nor simply defying it; it is bargaining inside the dependence, attempting to purchase future room with present revenue, and the analyst's questions are the bargain's questions. What capacity will exist when the support ends that did not exist when it began? What has the recipient surrendered, in equity, in obligation, in exposure to competition, in exchange for the public's money? What happens to the supported workers, the field's ostensible justification, if the bargain fails, and were they given any standing in its negotiation or only in its announcement? Step six adds the organisational entry: support that convenes an industry teaches it to act as one, and the convened industry's gratitude is less durable than its organisation, which will be turned, in due course, to the defence of the support itself. The seventh step takes the field's vocabulary of national champions and strategic necessity apart with the usual forensic care, restoring the agents and pricing the alternatives, while conceding what the vocabulary sometimes truly reports, that dependence on decisions taken elsewhere is a real vulnerability and that a state forbidden to address it would be a state forbidden to govern in one of the few registers where governing matters.

Industrial support also supplies a useful corrective to a lazy reading of the structural argument, the reading in which the state figures as capital's passive instrument and support as the proof. The record the analyst actually finds is of states bargaining hard, sometimes foolishly, sometimes shrewdly, with firms whose mobility is the state's problem and whose immobility is the state's opportunity, and of firms bargaining back with threats whose credibility the estimation variables decide. Instrumentalism cannot describe a bargain. The structural account can, because it locates the parties' unequal positions in the dependence structure rather than in anyone's instructions, and unequal bargaining inside a structure is a description that fits the documents, the memoirs, and the outcomes of the field better than either the conspiracy of the tradition's enemies or the neutrality of its liberal critics.

## Environmental measures as a policy field: costs that refuse to stay displaced

Environmental policy is the field where displacement, the fifth step's central technique, meets its limit, because the costs of production that settlements have historically exported into air, water, soil, and climate accumulate in media that return them, unpriced and uninvited, to the populations least responsible for their creation. Step one reads an environmental measure as an attempt to reassign a cost that was previously assigned by default: a standard assigns it to the producer, who passes what can be passed toward prices and wages; a charge assigns it by quantity; a prohibition assigns it entirely and immediately, which is why prohibitions are reserved for the costs whose return is most dangerous. Step three finds the constraint operating in a distinctive dialect, since the threat that disciplines governments here is not only the familiar postponement of investment but its relocation, the movement of production toward jurisdictions whose standards are lower, and the anticipation of that movement enters drafting rooms as an argument for harmonised weakness that no single government, acting alone, can easily answer. The field thus exhibits the dependence argument at the level of the system of states, where each government's room is narrowed by the others' anticipated concessions, and an analyst learns to read international coordination in this field not as idealism but as an attempt to widen every participant's room simultaneously by removing the exit that narrows it.

Step four extends the reproduction ledger to the conditions of life itself, since the environments in which labour power is renewed, the air of the district where workers can afford to live, the water of the towns built around the plant, are distributed by the same settlements that distribute income, and with the same gradient: the costs of production settle where resistance is least organised, and the bodies that absorb them present the bill, across decades, to the health field's budgets and the households' care. Step six notes the field's unusual organisational geometry, in which the affected population is partly unborn and wholly unrepresented at step two, so that the representation of the future falls to proxies, movements, scientists, litigants, whose access is contested precisely because their constituency cannot appear. The closing step reads this field's vocabulary of balance and sustainability as the armistice language of the fifth, and asks of every balance the worksheet's question: between which demands, at whose expense, with the cost displaced to which medium and which generation. The field's gift to the procedure is temporal depth: no other field forces the analyst to extend the first step's period across generations as a matter of routine, and the habit, once learned here, improves the analyst's reading of every other field whose costs are merely slower to return.

## Education funding as a policy field: formation, sorting, and the price of capacities

Education funding condenses several of the procedure's themes into a single field, because a school system is simultaneously a reproduction service, a sorting machine, a major employer, and a promise a state makes to families about the convertibility of effort into standing. Step one follows the funding to its real incidence, which is never only the pupil: resources per place, the geography of provision, the transport and materials and time that families must supply around the formal offer, all distribute the service's value unevenly among households nominally entitled to the same thing. The fourth step names the field's product precisely: capacities, embodied in the young, which employers will later hire and which the state therefore has an accumulation interest in forming well, alongside a legitimation interest in forming them fairly, since few experiences teach a family the settlement's real terms as durably as the schooling of its children. The two interests coincide often enough to fund the system and diverge often enough to structure its politics, because formation for accumulation prizes the capacities the labour market prices, while formation for legitimation prizes the equality of the starting line, and budgets must be divided between excellences that compete.

Step six reads the field's organisational effects across a lifetime. Schooling gathers children into cohorts and catchments whose boundaries become some of the most defended lines in the entire policy landscape, because a place in a well-regarded institution is a positional good whose value depends on others not having it, and parents, otherwise dispersed, organise with remarkable intensity around the boundary. The field thus manufactures constituencies of a peculiar kind, temporary, rotating as children age, yet fierce, and it fragments the wider population of families into competitors for positional advantage in a way that sits uneasily beside the system's legitimation story of common provision. Step seven examines that story, the language of opportunity and merit through which the settlement describes itself, and the analyst's task is the familiar one of measurement: set the language beside the flow map, the catchment map, and the distribution of outcomes, and record the distances. The distances do not abolish the language's partial truth, since effort does convert, sometimes, spectacularly, and the exceptions are the story's evidence. They locate the story's function, which is to generalise the exception into the rule's description, and a completed analysis of the field will hold both, the real mobility and the ideological work, in a single account without allowing either to dissolve the other.

Funding mechanisms in the field repay the same attention the article gave to tax expenditures, because money that follows the pupil, money that follows the institution's history, and money that follows assessed need are three different policies wearing one budget line, distributing advantage toward the mobile, the established, and the documented respectively. The analyst who asks which mechanism a settlement uses, and whose children each mechanism systematically advantages, has performed the first two steps of the worksheet in a single question, and will find the remaining steps falling into place around the answer.

## The worksheet in practice: an analyst's week with an unnamed measure

Abstraction has carried the article far enough that a narrative interlude is owed, showing the procedure at working speed on material resembling what an analyst actually receives. The measure arrives, as measures do, as a bundle: a press notice announcing a reform of support for a category of household expense, a set of regulations running to many pages, a costing annex, and a consultation summary in which the respondents are listed by organisation and their views compressed into a paragraph each. The analyst's first day belongs to step one and to nothing else. The regulations are read for eligibility, for the interaction with existing provisions, for the treatment of the boundary cases where entitlements overlap and claw back, and the costing annex is read against the regulations rather than beside them, since annexes cost the measure the announcement describes while regulations enact the measure that will exist. By the day's end the worksheet's first row holds a flow map with three entries flagged as uncertain, and the flags, not the certainties, are the day's real product, because they specify the evidence the rest of the week must find.

The second day belongs to step two and is spent in the consultation summary's silences. The respondents are sorted by the interests they represent, the absent are listed with the same care as the present, and the framing document that preceded the consultation, obtained after a request the analyst had learned to make as routine, shows the problem defined in a way that made the eventual measure the moderate option between two extremes the document itself constructed. The third day is step three's, and it is the week's most inferential: the analyst assembles the estimation variables from public accounts, reconstructs from the costing annex which alternatives were appraised and at what assumed reactions, and writes the constraint entry as a range with its grounds shown, resisting the temptation, which arrives punctually at midday, to write it as a verdict. The fourth day divides between steps four and six, in the company of time-use evidence and the advice sector's testimony about the claimant population's actual week, and produces the entry that will reorganise the analyst's own first impression: the measure's largest single effect is not its transfer but its reallocation of administrative labour onto the households least equipped to perform it, an effect invisible in the costing because the costing counts only the state's hours.

The fifth day runs step five and step seven together, the contradiction located in the measure's need to be seen relieving the expense while being seen not to reward it, and the justification read against the completed map until its key sentence, a claim of necessity, separates into the part that arithmetic supports and the part that the recovered alternatives refute. The analyst then writes backwards, as the failure catalogue prescribed, revising the flow map in the light of the household evidence, softening the constraint entry where the appraisal record showed genuine alternatives costed and rejected on stated grounds, and hardening the organisational entry where the consultation's absences proved, on inquiry, to be exclusions. The finished analysis is shorter than the week's notes and longer than the press notice by an order of magnitude, and its conclusions are graded in the article's manner, credits and charges in single sentences, with the defeater attached. Nothing in the week required genius. It required order, patience, and the refusal to let any step speak before its evidence had been heard, which is the article's whole method described as a working week, and the description is offered so that readers can see the procedure's cost as clearly as its yield: five days for one measure, honestly done, in a world that announces measures by the dozen and analyses almost none of them.

## Rival frameworks at the same table: what the procedure borrows and what it refuses

No procedure works a field alone, and the analyst's education is incomplete without a map of the rivals, drawn fairly, since each rival sees something the seven steps can miss. The pluralist account of policy, in its classic form, sees dispersed influence, shifting coalitions, and outcomes nobody intended, and it is the necessary corrective to any reading that finds too much pattern: the analyst should let the pluralist ask, of every structural finding, whether the messier story of competing groups and accidental timing explains the same evidence with less machinery, and should surrender the cases where it does. The public choice account sees officials and politicians as maximisers of their own budgets, votes, and careers, and it disciplines step two with an uncomfortable question the tradition prefers to skip: how much of the representation pattern is explained not by class at all but by the ordinary self-interest of the administering machinery, its preference for quiet, for growth, for the avoidance of blame. The institutionalist account sees rules, vetoes, and inherited procedures, and it explains the content the structural framework confessedly cannot, why this instrument, in this form, at this moment, by tracing the path a proposal had to travel and the gates at which its rivals died.

The procedure's relation to its rivals is neither absorption nor war. It borrows their findings at the steps where the findings belong, pluralist mess at step two, bureaucratic self-interest at steps two and five, institutional paths at the level of content, and it refuses only their occasional imperialism, the claim of each to be the whole story. What the seven steps add, and what none of the rivals supplies from its own resources, is the ordered attention to material incidence, to the reproduction of the workforce, and to the dependence that binds the whole process to the investment decisions of a class the rivals' vocabularies struggle to name. An analyst trained in the rivals and equipped with the worksheet will outperform an analyst equipped with either alone, and the article's ecumenism at this point is principled rather than polite: the framework's centre was itself produced by an internal critic borrowing an outsider's standards of evidence, and its parallel was produced by an outsider reaching its centre, so the borders of the tradition have been porous at exactly the points where the tradition advanced. The analyst should keep them porous.

One refusal should be recorded explicitly, because it defines the procedure against a temptation inside its own house. The worksheet refuses the services of any master variable offered as a substitute for the steps, whether the variable is class in the vulgar usage, or power, or discourse, or incentives in the economist's vulgar usage. Master variables fail in the same way wherever they are enthroned: they convert the analysis into a search for their own footprints, they dissolve the distinctions among mechanisms on which prediction depends, and they relieve the analyst of the evidence work that is the analysis. The seven steps are, in the end, a machine for preventing that relief. They are slower than any slogan and less satisfying than any master key, and their defence is the defence this article has offered from its first page: they can be run by an opponent, they can be wrong in the open, and they leave, on any measure to which they are honestly applied, a map that other analysts can check, correct, and build on.

## Taxation in depth: the revenue side as a portrait of the constraint

Taxation has appeared in this article mostly as the constraint's instrument panel, and it merits extended treatment as a policy field, because the structure of a revenue system is the most durable portrait a settlement ever paints of itself. Rates change with budgets and are forgotten; bases persist, and the base of a tax, what it reaches and what it is forbidden to reach, encodes the anticipations of generations. The analyst reading a revenue system should begin with its silences: the forms of wealth and income the system touches lightly or not at all, the transactions it exempts, the gains it declines to count as income, because the silences are where the third step's gradient has done its longest work, each exemption a fossil of an anticipated reaction that some past drafting room judged too expensive to provoke. Against the silences the analyst sets the system's loud places, the bases it taxes heavily and reliably, which are characteristically the bases that cannot move: labour, whose owner must live somewhere; consumption, whose payer is dispersed and unorganised at the point of payment; land and buildings, which cannot emigrate. The pattern is so regular across settlements that it can be stated as the revenue side's law of motion: taxation settles, like every other burden in this article, on the party least able to withdraw, and the fiscal sociology that reads tax systems as portraits of power begins exactly there.

The politics of taxation is correspondingly a politics of visibility, and step seven's tools apply to it with special force. Taxes differ enormously in how their payers experience them: the deduction that never reaches the wage packet is borne in near silence, the bill that must be actively paid is borne in resentment, the charge folded into a price is borne without attribution to any government at all. Settlements exploit the gradient of visibility as deliberately as they exploit the gradient of mobility, shifting the revenue burden toward the invisible end whenever the visible end becomes politically expensive, and the shift is a legitimation technique that costs the accumulation process nothing. The analyst should therefore grade a revenue change twice, once for its incidence and once for its visibility, and should expect the two gradings to diverge most sharply in the measures announced as simplifications, a word that in revenue language usually means that the burden has been moved somewhere the payer will notice it less, at a price in comprehension that the analyst's worksheet is designed to charge back.

Reform in this field is the hardest test of the room for manoeuvre doctrine, because the interests arrayed around any base are organised by the base itself, as step six predicts, and a government proposing to widen a base proposes simultaneously to create losers who know exactly who they are and winners, the general body of taxpayers, who will never know. The asymmetry of that exchange, concentrated loss against dispersed gain, is the field's standing obstacle, and governments surmount it rarely, usually in emergencies, in the aftermath of discrediting crises, or by compensations so elaborate that the reform's yield is spent purchasing its own passage. The analyst who understands the asymmetry will grade tax reforms with unusual charity and unusual precision: charity, because the obstacle is structural and not a failure of courage; precision, because the compensations are themselves policies, with incidences the worksheet can trace, and a reform's true content is often located in them rather than in the headline base.

## Regulation as a policy field: rules whose costs behave like taxes

Regulation is the policy family most often omitted from distributional analysis, because it spends no money and therefore leaves no flow for the first step to follow, and the omission is a standing invitation to error that the procedure must explicitly correct. A rule imposes costs as surely as a levy does: compliance costs, in equipment, in personnel, in forgone methods, and it confers benefits as surely as a subsidy does: protection from hazards, from fraud, from the competition of those who would cut the corners the rule forbids. The analyst's first task in the field is to construct the flow map the budget refuses to supply, estimating where the compliance costs settle after the travelling that the incidence section described, since a rule addressed to firms settles, in proportions set by their markets, on prices, on wages, on the viability of the smallest firms, and on the consumers of the protected good. The second task is to notice the rule's competitive incidence, because compliance costs are rarely proportional to scale, and a rule that a large firm absorbs as a department's routine can be an existential burden to a small one, so that regulation, whatever its protective intention, can operate as a machinery of concentration, raising the barriers behind which the largest producers shelter. Step two will find the largest producers, in consequence, among the rule's most sophisticated supporters, a finding that surprises only the analyst who expected interests to line up by slogan.

The field also exhibits, in an unusually pure form, the phenomenon the literature calls capture, and the procedure's three mechanisms give the analyst a finer vocabulary for it than the single word supplies. Capture by access is the regulated industry's standing presence in the rule's administration. Capture by agenda is the definition of the regulatory problem in the industry's own technical language, so that the questions the regulator asks are the questions the industry has already answered. Capture by anticipation is the regulator's rehearsal of the industry's reaction, its investment, its employment, its capacity to relocate, before any rule is proposed. The three captures can operate independently, and remedies differ by mechanism: access is answered by transparency and countervailing representation, agenda by independent expertise, anticipation only by changing the dependence that makes the anticipated reaction so expensive to defy. An analyst who diagnoses capture without specifying its mechanism prescribes without a diagnosis, and the procedure's contribution to this crowded field is precisely the specification.

Enforcement is where regulatory settlements are finally written, and it is the field's equivalent of the budget's small print. A rule's text states the settlement's ambition; its enforcement states the settlement, in inspection frequencies, in penalty levels, in the resources of the enforcing body, and in the discretion exercised at the point of contact between the inspector and the inspected. Settlements are renegotiated at the enforcement stage constantly and invisibly, as budgets for inspection are trimmed, as penalties fail to be indexed, as guidance softens the text's edges, and an analyst who reads only the rule will describe a country that does not exist. The worksheet's discipline, period, form, and the gap between stated and actual, applies here without alteration, and a regulatory analysis that ends with the enforcement entry completed has usually discovered that the field's real politics were located there all along, in the quiet arithmetic of how many inspectors a society is willing to pay for.

## Labour market rules as a policy field: the price of the capacity to work

Rules governing the labour market, minimum terms, hours, dismissal, combination, and the treatment of the unemployed, form the field where the procedure's subject matter is closest to the surface, because these rules set the terms on which labour power itself is sold, and every party to the field knows it. Step one reads a change in such rules as a redistribution of risk between the buyer and the seller of labour power: security of tenure moves the risk of slack demand from the worker's household to the firm's accounts, flexibility moves it back, and the argument between the vocabularies of protection and of rigidity is, in the worksheet's translation, an argument about where the risk of the business cycle shall be warehoused, in institutions built to bear it or in households built for other purposes. Step three prices the field's constraint with unusual honesty, because the employment effects of labour rules are the terrain on which the dependence argument's predictions are most directly testable, and the analyst should insist on the test rather than on the tradition's reflexes: the framework predicts that rules raising the cost of labour will be charged for somewhere in the system, in hiring, in hours, in the substitution of machinery or of informal arrangements, and the empirical question, genuinely open and varying by the tightness of the market, is where the charge lands and how large it is. Analysts who treat the charge as always zero and analysts who treat it as always total are both excused from the evidence, and the worksheet excuses neither.

The fourth step reads this field as the reproduction settlement's front line, since the wage and the hours together decide the material basis on which households renew themselves, and since the field's less visible rules, on scheduling, on the predictability of shifts, on the right to refuse additional hours, distribute the time in which reproduction happens with as much consequence as the wage distributes its money. At the sixth step the field's organisational stakes stand at their highest, because the rules of combination are themselves the subject matter: a settlement's treatment of unions, of collective bargaining's coverage, of the strike and its lawful forms, is a direct intervention in the class capacity of one side, answered, on the other side, by the legal and organisational immunities of the firm. No other field makes step six's question so literal, and the analyst should exploit the literalness, grading a labour settlement first by what it does to how costly combination has become for each side, and only then by its transfers, since the transfers will be renegotiated by capacities the rules have just reset.

The field's justification language deserves a final forensic note. Labour rules are defended and attacked in the vocabulary of freedom, the worker's freedom to sell on any terms, the employer's freedom to buy on any terms, and the analyst's counter-move is to ask, of each freedom, what it is a freedom from and for whom the alternative unfreedom is being arranged. A freedom to contract between parties of radically unequal reserves is a real freedom with a predictable incidence, and naming the incidence is not a denial of the freedom but its completion as a description. The completed description is what the seventh step owes every field, and in this field, where the parties' inequality is the explicit subject, the completion matters most.

## Care for children as a policy field: the generational ledger in miniature

Provision for the care of young children is a small field by expenditure and an enormous one by revelation, because it concentrates the fourth step's entire argument into a single service. The care of the very young is labour that somebody performs, paid or unpaid, and a settlement's arrangements decide who: the market, selling care to households at prices that consume a wage; the state, supplying or subsidising places as a public service; or the family, which in practice means its women, withdrawing from paid work in the years when careers and pensions are built. Step one follows a childcare subsidy into its markets and finds the familiar travelling, toward fees where places are scarce, toward parents where they are plentiful, toward the wages of the care workforce hardly at all, since that workforce's own labour is priced by the same settlement that prices the parents', cheaply, because the work is coded as an extension of the unpaid labour it replaces. The care workforce is the field's hidden centre: a settlement that expands provision by holding its workers' wages at the level of a vocation subsidises every other party, parents, employers, the treasury, out of the carers' pockets, and the analyst who names that subsidy has performed the first step in its complete form.

The fifth step locates this field's contradiction in the collision between the accumulation demand for parents' labour, which grows as economies organise themselves around two earners per household, and the fiscal reluctance to socialise the care that the demand presupposes. The collision is managed, characteristically, by a patchwork in which every party's contribution is partial and the seams are borne by the households at the joins: the parent who works the hours the provision does not cover, the grandparent whose retirement becomes a second shift, the carer whose own children are minded by arrangements the carer's wages cannot buy. Step six notes the field's organisational peculiarity, that its most affected population, parents of the very young, is the population least able to sustain organisation, since its members are exhausted, transient in the condition, and dispersed among workplaces and neighbourhoods, while the providers, where they are commercial, organise as businesses do. The representation pattern of step two follows from that geometry with depressing fidelity, and the analyst should record the mechanism plainly: the field's policies are made in rooms where the people who live the field's contradictions are represented, if at all, by those who employ them or sell to them.

The field's lesson for the whole procedure is about visibility and time. Care's costs are real whether or not they are counted, its withdrawal registers in labour supply, in fertility, in the exhaustion statistics of the health field, across lags that defeat annual accounting, and a state can therefore run a care deficit for a generation while congratulating itself on the saving. The fourth step exists to prevent that congratulation from surviving analysis, and childcare is where the step's student should practise first, because nowhere else is the movement of a cost from the public ledger into the household so rapid, so measurable, and so completely described by the single question with which this article's procedure began in earnest: not whether the cost was paid, but by whom, in what currency, and over what period of a life.

## Disability and incapacity as a policy field: assessment as the real policy

Provision for those who cannot work, or cannot work at the standard the market sets, is the field where the administrative form of a policy most completely becomes the policy itself, because the central problem the field must solve is a problem of knowledge: distinguishing, among claimants, conditions that are real, variable, and often invisible, under rules that must be applied by strangers at scale. The opening step follows the flows and finds them gated by assessment, so that the effective generosity of the provision is set less by its rates than by its tests, its evidence requirements, its review intervals, and its error rates in both directions, the awards made to those outside the intention and the refusals issued to those squarely within it. The analyst who reports the field's rates without its assessment regime has described the announcement, and the field is the standing refutation of every analysis that stops at announcements. The fourth step reads assessment as a cost imposed on the claimants least able to bear costs, since the labour of proving incapacity, gathering evidence, attending examinations, surviving interruption, is itself work, allocated to the sick, and the allocation should be entered in the ledger as plainly as any charge.

Step six finds in this field the fragmentation mechanism in its most refined form, because assessment individuates by design: each claimant's case is a file, each condition a category, each category's boundary a line across which claimants are set in implicit competition for a fixed envelope of sympathy and money. The categories also organise, however, in the other direction, since shared conditions create shared identities and, occasionally, movements, and the field's history includes the instructive spectacle of the assessed becoming assessors of the system, bringing to step two a constituency whose access was purchased by organisation against every obstacle the field could devise. Step seven examines the field's governing vocabulary of genuineness, in which the claimant population is sorted by a suspicion that the justification presents as prudence, and the analyst's measurement is comparative: set the resources devoted to detecting the false claim beside the resources devoted to correcting the false refusal, and the ratio states the settlement's real theory of error more honestly than any speech about compassion or about fraud. A settlement that prices one kind of error at a hundred times the other has made a distributional decision, and the worksheet's service is to make the decision visible as a decision, in a field where both kinds of error are paid for by the same population, in money, in health, and in standing.

## Infrastructure as a policy field: concrete, patience, and the geography of accumulation

Infrastructure, the roads, rails, ports, grids, and networks on which private production depends, is the accumulation family's most literal expenditure, and its analysis teaches the procedure's lessons about time and geography with unusual clarity. The opening step follows the investment and finds its benefits capitalised, characteristically, into the value of the land and assets it serves, so that a public work announced as a gift to a region arrives, in substantial part, as a windfall to the owners of what the work connects, unless the settlement has arranged to capture the windfall it creates. The question of capture, whether the state taxes the uplift its own spending generates or donates it, is the field's form of the gap between announced and actual beneficiaries, and it is decided, usually, by silence, in the original design, long before the work is opened with ceremony. Step three reads the field's finance: infrastructure is where the state's borrowing is most readily forgiven by the constraint, because the asset is visible, the return is narratable, and the lenders' anticipation is soothed by the collateral of future activity, so that governments discover in this field a room for manoeuvre they possess nowhere else, and the discovery explains the field's perennial attractiveness to governments whose every other ambition is being priced out of reach.

Step five watches the field's contradiction operate across decades. The work must be built where accumulation will use it, which concentrates it where activity already clusters, and it must be justified as serving the whole territory, which disperses its promise toward the places activity has left, and settlements oscillate between the poles, concentrating in one era under the language of efficiency and dispersing in another under the language of cohesion, each era's map recording the balance of the two demands at the moment of decision. Step six adds the field's distinctive organisational fact: infrastructure creates its own constituencies with unusual permanence, the industries of construction and operation, the regions connected and the regions bypassed, whose political weight long outlives the arguments that built the work, so that a settlement's map becomes a constitution, constraining its successors' geography as firmly as any law. The analyst's period discipline matters here above all: a work appraised at its opening is appraised at its most flattering moment, before maintenance's long bill arrives and before the traffic forecasts meet the population's actual movements, and the worksheet's insistence on the full arc, construction, operation, maintenance, renewal, is the difference between analysing infrastructure and attending its inauguration.

## Transfers and conditionality in depth: the seam where the two demands are stitched

Income transfers have been touched at several steps, and a consolidated treatment is owed, because the transfer is the welfare settlement's basic unit and conditionality is its most argued feature. An unconditional transfer, within its category, trusts the recipient with the money and spends nothing on verification beyond the category's boundary; a conditional transfer purchases, with the money, a set of behaviours, attendance, search, treatment, conduct, and spends a second sum on the machinery that verifies the purchase. Step one prices both sums and asks the field's founding question in its exact form: what is the conditionality for, and the possible answers are genuinely different policies. Conditionality can be a work incentive, pricing the transfer against the wage it might displace. It can be a legitimation device, reassuring the contributing population that the transfer is earned, and priced, in that case, against the consent it buys rather than the behaviour it changes. It can be a rationing device, reducing take-up by friction among the eligible, in which case its true function is expenditure control wearing a moral vocabulary, and the analyst identifies it by a signature the field cannot hide: the conditions whose breach is punished are enforced with a diligence that the conditions' stated purposes would never justify, while assistance to comply, the training, the childcare, the transport the behaviour requires, is funded as an afterthought.

Step four prices conditionality in the recipient's currencies: the hours of compliance, the risk of interruption that now hangs over the household's planning, the standing cost of being a person whose conduct is officially monitored. Step six records the sorting the conditions perform inside the recipient population, between the compliant whose cases proceed and the sanctioned whose cases become examples, and the recruitment of the first group as the second's judges, which the justification's vocabulary of fairness actively cultivates. Step five then locates the transfer in the settlement's economy with a precision the simpler accounts miss: the transfer is simultaneously the legitimation family's flagship expenditure and the accumulation family's instrument for maintaining a workforce available for work at the going wage, and conditionality is the seam joining the two functions, designed to keep the transfer generous enough to legitimate and disciplined enough to feed the labour market, with the stitching's tension absorbed, as usual, by the population at the seam. The analyst who can exhibit that stitching in a particular transfer, clause by clause, has understood the field, and the analyst who argues about transfers without locating the seam is arguing about the garment's colour.

A final comparative note disciplines the whole treatment. Transfers with identical rates and different conditions are different policies; transfers with identical conditions and different administrative tempers are different policies; and transfers identical in both can still differ by the company they keep, the services that surround them, the housing and health settlements into which their recipients step. The worksheet's seven entries, completed for a transfer in its actual company, are the minimum description from which argument about it should be allowed to start, and the article's insistence on the minimum is its answer to a public debate that habitually starts from the rate alone, as though a number could carry the weight of a settlement.

## The worksheet audited: turning the seven steps on the procedure itself

A procedure that teaches suspicion of justifications should submit to its own audit before closing, and the exercise is more than a gesture, because the seven steps, applied to the framework itself as though it were a policy, return findings the analyst should carry. Step one asks what the procedure distributes: it distributes attention, toward incidence, mechanisms, and households, and away from personalities, intentions, and the theatre of announcement, and that distribution has beneficiaries, the patient and the evidence-minded, and casualties, the fluent and the indignant, in every audience it reaches. Step two asks who was represented in the procedure's making, and the honest answer is a particular tradition's internal argument, instrumentalists and structuralists, fiscal theorists and their critics, with the pluralist arriving as an honoured outsider, and the analyst should know that other traditions, given the drafting, would have weighted the rows differently. Step three asks what constraint the procedure itself labours under, and the answer is the academic and publishing economy in which frameworks survive by being teachable, citable, and applicable by strangers, a constraint that explains the worksheet's very form, its preference for the portable over the profound.

Step four asks what the procedure does to the reproduction of its own practitioners, and the finding is double: it lowers the cost of producing an analysis, by supplying a sequence and a set of questions, and it raises the cost of producing a bad one, by specifying the evidence each claim requires, which is why its students experience it simultaneously as a support and as a discipline. The fifth step locates the procedure's own contradiction without difficulty: the procedure must be faithful to a tradition, which is its legitimation among the tradition's readers, and it must be usable by the tradition's opponents, which is its accumulation of credibility beyond them, and the two demands pull its every formulation in opposite directions, toward the vocabulary that signals belonging and toward the vocabulary that travels. This article has managed the contradiction by a stated settlement, mechanisms in plain language, tradition's terms retained where they earn their place, and the settlement, like all settlements, can be criticised from both sides, which the author accepts as evidence that the contradiction was real. Step six asks whom the procedure organises: it organises its users into a loose company of analysts who can recognise each other's working across political disagreement, and it fragments, deliberately, the audiences of slogan and of conspiracy, who find in the worksheet no comfort for their genres. Step seven reads its own justification, this article, and finds the ideological work in plain sight: the presentation of a tradition's method as a general instrument naturalises a claim about the tradition, that its best work was always method rather than doctrine, and the reader should hold that claim to the same standard as every other in these pages, asking what it retires from argument and what evidence, in the record of the tradition's actual career, would count against it. The audit closes the procedure's account of itself, and the article's remaining business is only to gather the whole into a usable memory.

## Public employment as a policy field: the state as employer and the boundary of the bypass

Direct public employment, the state hiring labour itself to supply services or to absorb unemployment, is the measure family that step three's logic identifies as the constraint's limiting case, because it bypasses the private hiring decision through which dependence operates. The analyst should therefore read every public employment settlement as a negotiation with the boundary itself. Step one follows the wages and finds them paid from taxation or borrowing rather than from the sale of the output, which makes the family's fiscal exposure total and immediate, and makes its legitimation value correspondingly high, since the employment is visible, attributable, and immune to the windfall problem that haunted the subsidy: a public post either exists or it does not, and its holder knows exactly which decision created it. Step three prices the bypass's costs without flinching: the state competes for labour and for credit with the private sector it also needs to flourish, its wage settlements become benchmarks the private labour market must answer, and its borrowing for employment competes, in the lenders' anticipation, with the borrowing of firms. None of this makes the bypass impossible. It makes its price legible, and the history of such settlements is a history of the price being paid in booms of public confidence and refused, through dismantlement, when the fiscal weather turns.

Step six records the family's most consequential organisational fact, which is that public employment organises its holders twice over, as workers with a common employer and as citizens with a common benefactor, and the double organisation makes the family the welfare settlement's most defensible and most attacked component, defended by constituencies that combine industrial and electoral weight, attacked in a vocabulary that recasts the double organisation as a vested interest. At the fifth step the family's place in the contradiction comes into equal clarity: it is the legitimation family's most credible instrument, because nothing legitimates like a job, and the accumulation family's most ambiguous one, because the employment it creates is real while the activity it funds stands partly outside the market's discipline, and settlements manage the ambiguity by confining the family to services whose public character is hard to dispute, or by subjecting it to market-mimicking disciplines whose costs the analyst should count as part of the settlement's price. The family's grading, in the article's manner, is by room and use: states with deep room have built durable public employment as a pillar of the reproduction settlement, states with narrow room have built it in emergencies and dismantled it in restorations, and the analyst who tracks the family's fortunes across a settlement's history possesses a sensitive instrument for measuring the room itself, since few gauges register the constraint's pressure as faithfully as the rise and fall of the bypass.

## Prices and incomes measures: the direct road and why it is rarely taken

Measures that act directly on prices and incomes, controls, guidelines, freezes, and their gentler descendants in the supervision of fees and charges, form the policy family whose rarity is itself a finding for the procedure to explain. Step one reads such a measure as an attempt to redistribute by decree, transferring real income between sellers and buyers, employers and workers, landlords and tenants, without passing through a budget, and the transfer's invisibility in the public accounts is the family's first attraction and its first analytical hazard. Step three supplies the explanation of the rarity: a direct measure confronts the investment process at its most sensitive point, the formation of prices and the expectation of returns, and the anticipated reactions are correspondingly vivid, evasion, postponement, the migration of activity into unmeasured channels, the deterioration of quality and supply that never appears in the controlled price itself. The cost schedule for this family is therefore steep in most conditions, and governments reach for it mainly in emergencies, when the legitimation price of uncontrolled prices exceeds the accumulation price of controlling them, and abandon it in the restoration with a regularity that the emergency section's pattern predicts.

The family's analytical value exceeds its frequency, because it isolates a variable the other families entangle: the state's willingness to govern distribution openly. A transfer hides its redistribution inside a budget of thousands of lines; a price measure signs its redistribution in a single clause, and the signature is why the family attracts a politics of unusual honesty and unusual ferocity at once. Step seven reads the family's justifications, which must argue necessity in its strongest form, since no government controls a price while claiming the market could have been trusted, and the analyst learns from the family's rhetoric what the state's necessity claims look like when they are meant, a standard against which the borrowed necessities of calmer measures can be measured. Step six notes the family's organisational asymmetry in a pure form: a controlled price benefits a dispersed population that will never organise to defend the control, and burdens organised producers who will never stop organising against it, so the family's settlements decay from the day of their enactment at a rate set by that asymmetry, whatever their initial popularity. The analyst grading such a measure should therefore grade its design for decay: whether it builds any constituency for its own continuation, whether its administration creates facts that outlast the emergency, and whether its authors understood, as the worksheet teaches, that a policy's organisational wake is the part of it that time cannot erode without a fight.

## Local and regional policy: the constraint at smaller scales

The procedure was described at the scale of the national state, and a section is owed to its behaviour at smaller scales, because a large share of the policy a citizen actually meets is made by authorities whose dependence is tighter and whose room is narrower than any national government's. A local authority cannot issue its own currency, cannot set the terms of its borrowing with anything like a state's freedom, confronts capital and residents who can exit across a boundary that costs an afternoon to cross, and funds itself from bases, property, local charges, and transfers from above, that the centre controls or caps. Step three's estimation, performed at this scale, returns a room so narrow that the analyst may wonder whether local policy is policy at all, and the wonder is instructive: much of what presents as local choice is the administration of settlements made elsewhere, and the honest local analysis begins by separating the genuinely local margin, the ordering of priorities within a fixed envelope, the quality and temper of administration, the local additions a determined authority can fund from its narrow bases, from the inherited structure it merely operates. The separation is not a counsel of despair. The margin is where citizens live, and a local authority's use of a narrow room can differ as dramatically as a national government's use of a wide one, and be graded by the same standard.

Step two at the local scale has a distinctive intimacy the analyst should exploit, because access is cheaper, the drafting rooms are nearer, and the anticipation of reactions can sometimes be observed in public, in the meetings where a development is debated and the mobile residents, the anchor employer, the prospective investor, are invoked by name as reasons. The local scale also exhibits step six in miniature with unusual clarity, since the constituencies a local settlement creates, the users of a facility, the residents of an estate, the traders of a street, are small enough to organise completely or to fail to, and an observer can watch capacity being built or dissolved within a single electoral cycle. Step five's contradiction operates locally as a competition between the authority's need to attract and retain the investment and the residents who fund it, and its need to be re-elected by the residents who experience the attraction's costs, in traffic, in prices, in the transformation of the place they chose, and the competition's local name is usually planning, under which modest vocabulary the entire argument of this article is conducted, in committee rooms, every week. The analyst trained on the national scale should therefore treat the local as the procedure's laboratory rather than as its poor relation: the mechanisms are the same, the evidence is closer, and the room, though narrower, is measured with a precision the national analyst can only envy.

## The procedure and the citizen: reading the news with a worksheet

The article has addressed students, teachers, researchers, and journalists in their professional capacities, and it owes a section to the reader who holds none of those offices and meets policy as a citizen, in headlines, in letters from administering offices, in the price of things and the wait for them. The citizen's version of the procedure is the same seven questions at walking pace, and their power at that pace should not be underestimated, because most public argument about policy fails at the first question and never recovers. A citizen who asks, of any announced measure, where the money comes from, where it lands, in what shape it travels, and across what stretch of years it falls due, and who refuses to proceed until the announcement has been made to answer, has already separated from the audience the announcement was designed for. A citizen who then asks whose reaction was priced into the design, and whose was not, has located the measure's politics more precisely than most commentary will. The remaining questions deepen the reading, the household costs, the contradiction, the organisational wake, the naturalised necessity, but the first two, honestly pursued, are enough to change a citizen's relation to the news from reception to interrogation, and the change, once made, does not reverse.

The citizen's worksheet has one advantage over the professional's, which is worth naming because it corrects a professional deformation. The citizen knows things the analyst must research: what the local office is like to deal with, how long the queue actually is, which conditions bite and which are dead letters, how the measure's categories fit or fail the street's actual households. Step one's actual beneficiaries, step four's household ledger, step six's organisational reality, are all, at the citizen's scale, matters of direct knowledge, and the professional literature's weakness, its dependence on the documents the process generates about itself, is the citizen's strength inverted. A public culture in which citizens bring that knowledge to the interrogation of announcements, in meetings, in consultations, in the questions put to representatives, raises the cost of every ideological operation the seventh step catalogues, because naturalisation works only on audiences who cannot supply the missing agents from their own experience. The procedure's final ambition is therefore not professional at all. It is to make the seven questions common property, as the pluralist parallel made the central argument common property, and to accept, as the price of that ambition, that the questions will be turned on settlements their author admires and on this article itself, which is not a price but the definition of the ambition achieved.

## A closing synthesis: the analysis in one sitting

The article's whole argument can now be gathered into the space a reader might give it in one sitting, and the gathering is a test as much as a summary, since a procedure that cannot be compressed without loss of its working parts was never a procedure but a commentary. A Marxist analysis of public policy traces who benefits materially, identifies the structural constraints under which the measure was made, assesses its effect on the reproduction of labour power, and identifies the contradiction it manages or displaces, and it performs those operations in an order that lets evidence discipline interpretation: flows first, then representation through access, agenda, and anticipation, then the dependence of the state on private investment, then the household ledger, then the competing demands of accumulation and legitimation, then the organisational wake, and last the ideological work of the justification. Its centre is the structural dependence argument, that governments face a systematic constraint favouring investment conditions because their revenue and their populations' employment rest on decisions they do not control, an argument built by a Marxist against the instrumentalist alternative, matched independently by a pluralist, and strong enough to survive both its friends' exaggerations and its critics' strongest objection, provided its users respect its stated limits and its written defeater.

What the synthesis cannot compress is the practice, and the article ends by returning the reader to it. Take a measure, any measure whose announcement has annoyed or pleased you into attention, and run the steps slowly, with documents, allowing a day to the flows and a day to the silences in the consultation, estimating the room before grading the government, pricing the household's hours before accepting the treasury's saving, and reading the justification last, against a map you drew without it. The first run will be slow and the worksheet will fight back at the joints this article marked as weak, and both experiences are the education the article promised, since a method is learned at its resistances and not at its slogans. The second run will be faster. By the fifth, the questions will have become habits, and the habits will have begun to reorganise the reader's attention across every field the article visited and several it did not, which is the point at which the procedure stops belonging to the article, or to the tradition that assembled it, and becomes what its centre always was: a finding about how policy gets made in a society organised as this one is, available to anyone willing to do the work, and answerable, as all findings should be, to the next measure the record produces.

## Money measures and the central bank: the constraint in its most technical dress

Monetary arrangements, the setting of interest rates and the management of the currency, form the field where dependence wears its most technical clothing and where the analyst's steps must be run with particular care, because the field's institutions are deliberately insulated from the politics the other steps describe. Step two, applied here, finds representation transposed into appointment and doctrine: the governors of such institutions are selected for soundness, a quality defined inside the profession they will regulate their distance from, and the agenda of monetary discussion is fixed by models in which some variables count as the institution's business and others, distribution among them, count as somebody else's. Step three finds the constraint doubled, since the monetary authority is itself one of the mechanisms through which the wider dependence operates, transmitting the anticipated reactions of lenders and currency holders into the price of credit for everyone, while remaining, in its own self-description, a purely technical body responding to indicators. The analyst's task is to hold both descriptions at once: the indicators are real, the transmission is real, and the selection of which indicators govern, whose pain registers as a signal and whose registers as background, is a settlement like any other, made by people, inside a structure, under anticipations the worksheet can name.

Step five reads the field's characteristic tension in a distinctive register, because monetary policy's two masters are price stability, which the accumulation process requires for calculation, and employment, which legitimation requires for consent, and the balance struck between them, encoded in mandates and interpreted in committees, is a distributional choice conducted in the vocabulary of technique. Debtors and creditors, wage earners and asset holders, experience each striking of the balance differently, and the analyst who translates the technical record back into those experiences has performed the field's essential act of analysis. Step seven completes the pass by examining the field's master justification, independence itself, which removes the balance from electoral contest in the name of credibility, and the analyst's question is the standing one, not whether the removal is defensible, since arguments for it are serious, but what the removal removes: which decisions, affecting which populations, at whose hands, have been relocated from the arena where they could be argued to the register where they can only be announced.

## Trade and the external account: dependence across borders

No settlement is made inside one territory alone, and the external account, the flows of goods, capital, and payments across a state's borders, is where the dependence argument acquires its international dimension and some of its sharpest evidence. A state that must earn foreign currency to pay for essential imports lives under a constraint its domestic politics cannot repeal: the investment and purchasing decisions of foreigners, the lending decisions of external creditors, and the commodity prices set in markets it does not govern all enter its drafting rooms as anticipated reactions of unusual vividness. Step three's estimation at this scale adds the external variables to the domestic ones, the reserves of foreign currency, the currency composition of external debts, the concentration of exports, the elasticity of import demand, and returns a room that for many states is narrower than any domestic consideration would suggest, which is why the policy convergence the framework predicts is often most visible, and most resented, in states whose external accounts are weakest. Their governments did not choose the convergence, their electorates did not vote for it, and their officials will describe it, in private, with a precision about mechanisms that this article's readers will find familiar.

The external dimension also disciplines a nationalist misreading of the framework that the analyst should explicitly refuse. Dependence across borders is not a story of virtuous insiders and conspiring outsiders; the same structure binds the strong states whose currencies others must earn, in subtler forms, since their room is widened precisely by the narrowness of others', and their settlements export adjustment to the periphery of the system as reliably as any domestic settlement exports costs to its households. Step five's contradiction, read internationally, becomes the tension between each state's need to run its external account in surplus or in credible balance and the arithmetic fact that surpluses require counterpart deficits somewhere, so that the system's settlements cannot all succeed at once, and the competition among them is a standing source of the friction that commentary attributes to national character or diplomatic failure. An analyst who has learned to see the domestic settlement's displacements will recognise the international ones by their shape: costs moved to where resistance is least organised, in this case to populations whose governments command the least room, and recorded, in the receiving countries' politics, as mysteries of instability that the sending countries' commentary finds inexplicable.

## Knowledge and expertise as a policy field: who may know, and what knowing costs

Every policy field rests on a knowledge settlement, an arrangement deciding which expertise counts, who produces it, who pays for its production, and whose experience is admitted as evidence, and the analyst who runs the steps on that settlement itself discovers a meta-policy beneath all the others. Step two finds the knowledge settlement's representation pattern in the funding of research, the staffing of advisory bodies, and the professional hierarchies that rank a model's output above a caseworker's testimony and a caseworker's testimony above a claimant's account of the claimant's own life. Step one follows the money through universities, institutes, consultancies, and official statistics, and finds knowledge produced, like everything else in this article, under anticipations: the funder's questions are answered more readily than the unfunded ones, the publishable finding travels further than the inconvenient one, and entire territories of policy-relevant fact remain unmapped because no payer has commissioned the map. The unmapped territories are not random. They cluster where the affected populations cannot commission research and the administering state prefers not to know, since knowledge creates obligations, and a state that has not counted a cost has not yet been asked to pay it.

Step seven reads the knowledge settlement's justification, the claim that expertise is neutral and its hierarchies merely technical, with the usual forensic respect: the claim is partly true, since methods do constrain their users and honest experts do report against their funders, and it is partly the naturalisation of a distribution, since the decision about which questions deserve experts is a political decision wearing a methodological coat. The analyst's stance toward expertise should mirror the stance toward official numbers prescribed earlier, double and permanently double: use it, gratefully, since the steps cannot be worked without it, and audit it, asking of every authoritative finding the worksheet's questions about its commissioning, its categories, and its silences. A policy analysis is only ever as good as the knowledge settlement beneath it, and the analyst who has examined that settlement has found the deepest layer at which a measure's politics were decided, earlier than the framing, earlier than the consultation, in the prior distribution of the capacity to know.

## Science, research, and the funding of inquiry as a policy field

Public support for research is a small field by money and a revealing one by structure, because it shows a state purchasing a good whose value it cannot specify in advance, from producers whose judgements it must largely trust, for benefits that arrive, if they arrive, on timescales no budget respects. The opening step follows the grants and finds a flow map dominated by institutions rather than individuals, with advantage accumulating where the capacity to apply, to administer, and to publish already concentrates, so that a funding system designed to reward excellence operates simultaneously as a machinery for widening the gap between the well-endowed centres of inquiry and the rest. Representation here takes an unusual form, since the field's constituency is its own producer class, organised in academies, associations, and peer networks that advise on the distribution of the funds they compete for, an arrangement defended as the only competent one and experienced, by those outside the charmed circle, as the definition of capture wearing academic dress. The analyst need not choose between the defence and the experience. Both describe the same machinery from different seats, and the worksheet's entry should record the machinery, its genuine epistemic rationale, and its distributional consequences, in adjacent sentences.

Step five locates the field's version of the standing tension. Accumulation wants inquiry that pays, in processes, products, and trained people whose skills the labour market can price, and it wants the paying inquiry soon. Legitimation wants inquiry that dignifies the settlement, that cures, that explores, that demonstrates the society's seriousness about truths beyond the market's horizon, and it is willing to wait. Funding settlements divide the money between the horizons under vocabularies of excellence and relevance whose meanings migrate with the balance of the demands, and the migration, tracked across successive settlements, tells the analyst which demand is ascendant in the wider settlement beyond the laboratory walls. Step four adds the field's reproduction entry, since a research system is also a machine for producing the next generation of knowers, and its funding decisions decide whose children get to become them, through the stipends, the unpaid internships, the years of insecurity that filter the aspirants by their households' capacity to subsidise a slow start. A science settlement can therefore be egalitarian in its rhetoric of open competition and sharply stratified in its actual intake, and the gap separating the rhetoric from the intake is measurable in the biographies of those who survive the filter, which is where the analyst should go looking for it.

The field closes the article's survey of policy families with a reflexive satisfaction, because the knowledge settlement examined in the previous section and the research settlement examined here are the same settlement seen from two sides, and the analyst who funds inquiry, or competes for its funding, is inside the object of study in a way the analyst of housing or pensions is not. That reflexivity is the procedure's final examination. Run the steps on the institutions that produced the analyst, record the entries without flinching, and the worksheet has done for its user what it promises to do for any measure: replaced a story about virtue with a map of flows, access, constraint, and consequence, in the one territory where the user had the strongest motive to prefer the story.

## A note on sources: what an analyst can consult, and in what order

A procedure is only as usable as its bibliography is honest, so the article finishes by arranging its own sources in the order an analyst should meet them. First come the primary formulations. Block's essay of 1977, The Ruling Class Does Not Rule, states the dependence argument in its original polemical setting, against the instrumentalism it replaced, and should be read before any summary of it, including this article's, because the essay's force lies partly in watching a tradition correct itself in public. Lindblom's Politics and Markets, from the same year, states the parallel from the pluralist side, and the pair should be read together, in either order, with attention to where the mechanisms coincide and where the conclusions part. O'Connor's The Fiscal Crisis of the State, from 1973, supplies the contradiction's fiscal anatomy, and repays reading as an account of budgets rather than as a prophecy, since its author built it from the expenditure categories of an actual state and its symptoms can be checked against any budget the reader cares to open.

Around the primary trio stands the wider literature the steps draw on, and the analyst should know its shape even where particular volumes go unread. The sociology of the welfare state supplies step four's household evidence and step six's findings about universal and selective provision. The public finance literature supplies step three's estimation variables and the incidence theory on which the opening step leans. The policy process literature, pluralist and institutionalist in the main, supplies the correctives the article borrowed at the second step and the account of content that the framework confessedly lacks. Official sources, budgets, consultation records, statistical series, appraisal documents, remain the analyst's daily bread, read with the double stance this article prescribed, used constantly and audited permanently. A reader who works through the trio, keeps the wider shape in view, and practises on the official record will find the worksheet becoming less necessary with every analysis, which is the fate good procedures intend for themselves: to be internalised, argued with, and finally carried in the analyst's questions rather than in the analyst's bag.

One habit should govern the consulting of every source named here, primary or official: read against the grain of the reader's own prior conclusion. The analyst who opens Block only to confirm the dependence argument will miss the passages where Block qualifies it, and the analyst who opens a budget only to confirm a settlement's character will miss the line that complicates it. Sources earn their authority by being allowed to disappoint the reader who cites them, and a bibliography arranged as carefully as this one deserves readers willing to be disappointed in public, since that willingness, practised on books, is the same willingness the worksheet demands toward evidence, toward governments, and toward the framework itself. The article's last instruction is therefore also its first, restated for the library rather than for the measure: follow the evidence before naming the interest, and let the naming answer, always, to what the following uncovered.

 The trio also models a virtue the analyst should copy into every worksheet entry: each author states the opponent's case before answering it, Block the instrumentalist evidence, Lindblom the pluralist faith in dispersed power, O'Connor the moralising account of deficits he intended to replace. Formulations that survive their opponents' best statement age better than formulations protected from it, and the procedure built from these three has inherited the habit as a design principle rather than as a courtesy.

## For teachers and seminar leaders

The teaching opportunity this article offers is a method that students can carry out of the room, and the session architecture that exploits it begins with a measure and a blank worksheet rather than with a lecture. Distribute a policy document, a budget extract, a programme's rules, stripped of commentary, and set the room to step one in pairs, flows only, no interpretation permitted, and let the pairs discover how much disagreement the supposedly factual step contains once incidence, form, and period are taken seriously. Then run the steps in plenary, one per interval, with the teacher's interventions reserved for the characteristic failures catalogued in this article: the beneficiary shortcut when the room wants to close at step one, constraint inflation when step three swallows the case, legitimation drift when step five begins to explain everything. The session's intellectual climax should be the parallel, Block beside Lindblom, set as a primary-source comparison, because students who watch two frameworks converge on a mechanism from opposite commitments learn more about what evidence is than any lecture on method delivers, and they learn it in the form this subject best teaches, as an experience of their own categories being rearranged by documents.

Assessment literacy completes the layer. ## For researchers, historians, and journalists

Researchers taking this procedure into larger projects should treat the article as a specification of evidence rather than as a set of conclusions, since each step corresponds to an archive and a method that a serious project will want to handle at first hand. The flow step belongs with budgets, incidence studies, and administrative data on take-up and refusal. The representation step belongs with consultation records, committee memberships, and the framing documents in which problems were defined before remedies were debated. The constraint step belongs with the comparative record across governments and with the financial components of room for manoeuvre, revenue bases, debt composition, reserves, and capital mobility, assembled independently of the behaviour they are used to explain. The reproduction step belongs with time-use evidence and the household literature. The contradiction step belongs with fiscal history read as a sequence of settlements. A project that works the archives in the worksheet's order will find its argument assembling itself with unusual clarity, because the order was designed as a research design before it was designed as an exposition, and the findings that emerge, including findings against the framework's expectations, will be publishable in the venues where policy is seriously studied, which is the test the tradition's work should be required to pass.

Journalists inherit policies at the announcement, under deadline economies that punish the worksheet's patience, and this layer's advice is therefore pitched at the level of the single story and the single question. The announcement is step seven's raw material and step one's target: before quoting the justification, ask who receives, in what form, over what period, and let the answers, even partial ones, set the story's frame. In the briefing room, the highest-value question this procedure supplies is the anticipation question, asked of any measure whose costs fall on the organised and whose benefits are claimed for the dispersed: what reaction was priced into this design, and whose reaction was not? Officials answer that question more revealingly than any question about intention, because it asks about their own craft rather than about their virtue. And when the story concerns a government accused of obedience to markets, or credited with defiance of them, the estimation discipline of this article is the journalist's protection against both camps' press offices: assemble the room for manoeuvre from the debt, the revenue base, and the reserves before writing the verdict, and the verdict, whichever way it falls, will be the journalist's own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do you do a Marxist analysis of public policy?

Work through seven inquiries in a fixed sequence. Begin by mapping where resources actually travel, separating the beneficiaries a measure announces from those its rules deliver. Then reconstruct how the measure was made, look for the constraint that investment conditions imposed, reckon what the measure does to the daily and generational renewal of the workforce, name the competing demands it reconciles, trace how it rearranges people's capacity to act together, and only at the end dissect the language in which it is defended. Findings stay provisional until the sequence completes.

### Q: What is the structural dependence argument?

Fred Block's 1977 formulation holds that a government's finances and its population's livelihoods both rest on investment choices taken by private firms, so measures hostile to the climate for those choices generate penalties, shrinking revenue, dearer borrowing, softer hiring, that narrow what any administration can select. Block aimed the claim against fellow Marxists who pictured a capitalist class directing the state. Dependence does the explanatory work that direction was supposed to do, and it does so without positing orders, meetings, or a coordinating will anywhere in the system.

### Q: How do you trace who benefits from a policy?

Distinguish the charge as a rulebook states it from the charge that survives contact with markets. Follow a payment or a levy past its first recipient, watching prices, pay settlements, rents, and hiring decisions absorb or transmit it, and compare the population a measure's preamble celebrates with the population its thresholds, documentation demands, and claim rates actually reach. Entitlement that goes unclaimed delivers nothing, however generous its schedule, and a subsidy can come to rest with landlords, providers, or employers rather than with the households named in the announcement.

### Q: What does policy do to the reproduction of labour power?

It allocates the expense of keeping a workforce alive, skilled, and available among three budgets: what households earn, what employers put in, and what is provided collectively. Schooling, medical care, housing, rest, and the raising of children all belong to that expense. When collective provision retreats, the expense seldom disappears; it migrates into kitchens and caring hours, surfacing as unpaid labour, postponed treatment, and worn-down capacity. Provision can also run the other way, lifting health and skill in ways the investment process itself profits from, which is why one measure can burden the treasury and benefit employers in aggregate simultaneously.

### Q: What contradiction does public policy manage?

It manages the pull between two requirements a market society's state cannot escape. Productive investment must be kept going, since the treasury and the nation's jobs both hang on it, yet the population living with insecurity and unequal outcomes must be given enough security and recognition to accept the arrangement. James O'Connor described the fiscal shape of the bind in 1973: costs thrown up by the economy are pushed onto public budgets while the gains remain in private hands. Individual measures are best read as truces between the requirements, financed partly by shifting costs elsewhere or into the future.

### Q: Does Marxist policy analysis assume conspiracy?

The version set out in this article assumes the reverse, having been built to explain governmental behaviour that conspiracy stories are usually invented to explain. Officials need no instructions to protect the conditions their budgets depend on; they need only do their jobs inside a structure where revenue and employment rise or fall with private investment. Influence, where the analysis looks for it, travels through consultation access, through the early fixing of what counts as the problem, and through drafters imagining powerful reactions in advance, all of which leave traces that hidden direction would not.

### Q: Can a government defy investor interests?

Defiance is possible because the constraint bills its victims rather than physically preventing them. An administration can enact measures investors dislike and absorb dearer credit, postponed projects, a pressured currency, and weakening payrolls, and it can keep absorbing them while reserves last, while its debts are held patiently at home, while substitute revenue can be raised, or while its supporters blame the pain on the right targets.
