Overdetermination is a term from the French Marxist philosophy of the 1960s, borrowed openly from Freud, and it names a single exact claim: in a social formation, no contradiction ever operates in a pure state, because every contradiction is always modified by the other contradictions that exist alongside it. A reader who met the word in a seminar, in a footnote, or in an argument about something else entirely has usually met it in a degraded form, as a fancy way of saying that an event had many causes. That degraded form is not a loose paraphrase of the concept. It is the concept with its point removed, and recovering the point is the whole work of this glossary entry. The point is historical before it is philosophical. The term was formulated to explain a specific puzzle, the success of revolution in the least industrially advanced of the major European powers, and it carries that explanation inside itself wherever it travels.

The plain contrast can be stated at once. A familiar picture of Marxist explanation holds that the economy determines everything else directly, so that politics, law, and culture are the economy wearing costumes, and history is the unfolding of one contradiction between the forces and relations of production. Overdetermination rejects that picture while refusing to abandon economic determination, and the tension between the rejection and the refusal is where the concept lives. What replaces the picture is a whole made of several real contradictions, unevenly developed, fused in shifting combinations, with one of them dominant at any given moment and with the economy determining, in a sense that needs careful unpacking, which one is dominant. The article proceeds by unpacking: first the definition word by word, then the Freudian source of the term, then the model of society the concept attacks, then the model it proposes, then the Russian case that motivated it, and finally the objections, including the strong objection that the whole construction is a truism dressed in technical vocabulary.

## The definition of overdetermination, taken word by word

Begin with the canonical formulation, which can be paraphrased without loss: overdetermination is the condition in which any contradiction within a social formation is always already affected by the other contradictions that constitute that formation, so that it never exists or acts in isolation. Four elements of this formulation carry weight, and each repays slow reading. The first is the word contradiction itself, which in this vocabulary does not mean a logical inconsistency or a mere disagreement. A contradiction is a structured tension between forces whose relation both constitutes a social order and presses against its limits, as the relation between wage labour and capital constitutes a factory's working day while generating disputes over its length, its intensity, and its price. Contradictions in this sense are not problems awaiting a solution. They are the moving parts of the formation itself.

The second element is the social formation, the concrete society under analysis, with its several levels or instances: an economic level, a political level, an ideological level, each with its own institutions, its own tempo, and its own contradictions. The third element is the claim of modification. Each contradiction bears the marks of the others. A wage dispute inside a factory is never only a wage dispute, because it is shaped by the state of the law, by the strength or weakness of unions, by the cultural expectations workers bring to the bargaining table, by the memory of previous defeats, and by the position of the national economy inside a world market. The fourth element is the negative claim, the one that does the real work: because modification is universal, no contradiction can be read off in a pure form, and no outcome can be explained by isolating one contradiction and treating the rest as noise. The purity that vulgar explanation assumes is precisely what the concept denies.

### What does it mean to say a contradiction is never pure?

A pure contradiction would act alone, on its own schedule, its effects traceable to it and nothing else. The denial of purity says every real contradiction arrives already bent by its neighbours: a wage struggle is shaped by law, custom, party organisation, and market conditions before the first demand is drafted, so its outcome belongs to the combination.

It follows that overdetermination is a claim about the form of causation in society rather than a claim about its quantity. The lazy reading counts causes and finds many. The concept instead describes how causes combine: by fusion, by displacement, by uneven development, in a structure where the combination itself, and not any ingredient, is what produces the event. This is why the concept cannot be replaced by the phrase multiple causation without remainder. A list of causes leaves each cause intact and adds them together. Overdetermination denies that the items on such a list survive their combination unchanged. The economic contradiction that enters a revolutionary crisis is not the economic contradiction of the textbook, because it has been worked over by war, by agrarian revolt, by national oppression, and by the collapse of a state, and it acts differently for having been worked over. Combination here is chemical in spirit rather than arithmetical: the compound has properties that the ingredients, inspected separately, do not display.

A last preliminary distinction protects everything that follows. The concept is a claim about how social formations work, offered by a philosopher, Louis Althusser, whose wider system is treated in the profile at [louis althusser intellectual biography](/2015/12/15/louis-althusser-intellectual-biography/). This glossary entry stays with the single term, its source, its target, its motivating case, and its critics. Readers who want the thinker's life, his other concepts, and his fate should go to that profile; readers who want the category of contradiction itself, on which this term leans at every step, should go to [contradiction in marxist method](/2012/08/01/contradiction-in-marxist-method/). What remains here is the narrow and profitable task of making one word exact.

## A word borrowed from the interpretation of dreams

Althusser never hid the loan. The term comes from psychoanalysis, from Freud's account of how dreams are formed, set out in the book on dream interpretation that Freud published in 1900. In that account, a dream is not a coded message with a single key. Each element of the manifest dream, the dream as remembered, is connected to several latent dream thoughts at once, and Freud's word for this many-sided determination, rendered in German as Überdeterminierung, is the word Althusser took. A dream image of a particular room, say, may be tied by association to a childhood scene, to a wish, to a fragment of the previous day, and to a pun, and the dream-work has knotted them together so thoroughly that no analysis can assign the image to one source alone. The image is overdetermined: determined, but determined several times over, by routes that intersect in it.

The borrowing was not decorative. Althusser took the term because the structure Freud described, a node where several lines of determination cross and modify one another, was exactly the structure he wanted for contradiction in a social formation. Just as the dream element cannot be explained by one latent thought, the social contradiction cannot be explained, or even identified, apart from the others crossing it. The loan also carried a methodological suggestion. Psychoanalysis reads the manifest product back to its intersecting determinants without ever expecting a single cause, and Althusser's analysis of a conjuncture, a concrete historical moment, aspires to the same patience. The conjuncture, like the dream, is a knot. Undoing it means following each thread far enough to see how it tightens the others.

Two of Freud's mechanisms travelled with the term and became working parts of the concept, and a later section of this article treats them in full: condensation, by which several dream thoughts are compressed into a single image, and displacement, by which emphasis shifts from the important element to an apparently trivial one. In the social translation, condensation becomes the fusion of distinct contradictions into a single ruptural moment, and displacement becomes the shifting of dominance from one contradiction to another. For the moment, what matters is the direction of the borrowing. A philosopher of Marxism reached outside his tradition, into a theory of the mind, for a word to describe how societies break. That reach is characteristic of the concept's family: it belongs to a series of borrowed terms in this tradition, each transformed by the borrowing, and the borrowing is not an embarrassment to be explained away but the mechanism by which the concept does its work.

### Why does the dream analogy matter?

The analogy matters because it supplies the concept's shape: a point where several independent lines of determination cross, each altering the others, with the product unintelligible from any single line. Freud's dream element is that point in the psyche; Althusser's contradiction in a crisis is that point in a society, which is why no cause decoding is offered or accepted.

There is a further reason the source matters, which is corrective. Because the term circulates widely without its origin, many readers assume Althusser coined it, and the assumption flatters the concept with an originality it does not claim while hiding the clue that makes it intelligible. Restoring the loan restores the clue. Whenever the social use of the word threatens to blur into the claim that things are complicated, the dream origin pulls it back to precision: a specific element, several specific determinants, and a knot that analysis can in principle untie thread by thread, naming each determinant and showing where it crosses the rest. A use of the term that cannot name the threads has not performed the analysis the word promises. That standard, set by the borrowing itself, becomes important when the critics arrive, because the strongest criticism of the concept is that its users routinely fail exactly this standard while keeping the word.

## The essay in which the concept appears, and the debt acknowledged in it

The concept was set out in an essay titled Contradiction and Overdetermination, written in 1962 and collected three years later in the volume For Marx, the book that announced Althusser's reconstruction of Marxist philosophy. The essay is a reading of two bodies of writing held together: Marx's own attempts to theorise the difference between his dialectic and Hegel's, and the practical reflections of revolutionaries, above all Lenin and Mao, on why revolutions happened where and when they did. Its manner is unusual for a theoretical text. Rather than building the concept abstractly and then applying it, Althusser extracts it from cases: from Lenin's analysis of the Russian situation, from Mao's writings on contradiction, from the embarrassing fact that the classical theory seemed to predict revolution in the advanced capitalist countries and history delivered it in a semi-agrarian empire instead. The concept arrives as the answer to a discrepancy between theory and record, which is why its defenders insist that it is not a piece of verbal machinery but a repair to a theory that the record had wounded.

The acknowledgment of the Freudian source sits inside the essay itself, in the course of a discussion of what it means for a contradiction to be specified by the structure of the whole in which it exists. Althusser flags the borrowing as a borrowing, remarking that he takes the term from psychoanalysis because no term in the Marxist vocabulary names the phenomenon he is after. The candour matters for interpretation. A thinker who conceals a loan invites the reader to treat the concept as a discovery about society. A thinker who declares the loan invites the reader to test the analogy: to ask whether the social formation really is knotted the way the dream is knotted, and to hold the concept to the standard the analogy sets. Much of the later debate about overdetermination is, on inspection, a debate about whether that test has been passed or merely announced.

The essay also fixes the concept's polemical address, which a glossary should record plainly. Its named enemies are two mirror-image simplifications. The first is economism, the doctrine that the economy is the sole motor of history and that every other level is its reflection, a doctrine Althusser associated with the orthodoxies of the Second International and with the codified dialectical materialism of the Soviet textbooks. The second is the Hegelian model of totality, described in the next section, in which a whole expresses a single spiritual essence and contradiction is the essence at war with itself. Against both, the essay poses a whole that is structured, uneven, and irreducible to one line of descent. The reader who keeps the two enemies in view will notice that overdetermination is always doing two things at once: complicating the causal picture, and defending a specific place for the economy inside the complication. Remove either task and the term collapses into the truism its critics describe.

### Where did Althusser first set out the concept?

The concept appears in Contradiction and Overdetermination, an essay written in 1962 and collected in For Marx in 1965, where Althusser extracts it from Lenin on the Russian conjuncture and Mao on contradiction. The essay acknowledges the Freudian borrowing on its face, presents the Russian case as its motivation, and names its two targets, economism and the expressive whole.

One structural feature of the essay deserves emphasis before the argument moves on, because it explains the concept's durability and its vagueness at a single stroke. Althusser presents overdetermination not as a special condition of crises but as the permanent condition of every contradiction, in quiet times as much as in revolutionary ones. A contradiction in a stable period is no less worked over by its neighbours; the working over simply produces reproduction rather than rupture, the continuation of the order rather than its break. This universality is what elevates the term from a remark about 1917 into a general claim about social causation. It is also what exposes the term to the deflationary objection, since a condition that obtains everywhere and always can start to look like a condition that explains nothing in particular. The objection is given its full hearing later in this article. First the target model needs its portrait, because the concept defines itself against a specific picture of wholeness, and without the picture the definition floats.

## The target: a whole that expresses one essence

The model under attack has a name in Althusser's vocabulary: the expressive totality, or expressive whole. In such a whole, every part is the expression of a single inner principle, an essence, which is present in each part and legible in each part if one knows how to read. The model descends, on Althusser's telling, from Leibniz and finds its philosophical summit in Hegel, for whom the whole of a historical epoch is the manifestation of one spiritual principle, so that the law, the art, the religion, and the economy of an age are all that principle wearing different clothes. Read a society's pottery and you read its god; read its god and you have read its pottery. Nothing in such a whole is genuinely other than anything else. Difference is appearance; identity is truth.

Transferred into Marxist dress, the expressive model yields the theory Althusser wanted to destroy. The essence becomes the economy, or more precisely the one contradiction between productive forces and production relations, and every other feature of the society becomes its phenomenon, its expression. Politics expresses the economy. Law expresses the economy. A strike, a war, a philosophical system, and a style of painting are all the one contradiction at different addresses. Explanation then consists in tracing the phenomenon back to the essence, and the essence, being simple, explains with a satisfying economy of means. The model also generates a prediction, quietly and without announcing itself as one: if history is the unfolding of one contradiction, revolution arrives where the central contradiction has ripened furthest, in the most advanced capitalist societies, where the forces of production strain hardest against their integument. The prediction failed. The revolutions of the European twentieth century arrived in Russia, in China, in the less developed peripheries, while the advanced centres absorbed their crises and endured. An expressive theory can describe that record only as an anomaly, a delay, a detour in the essence's timetable, and a theory reduced to pleading delay has confessed that its model of the whole is wrong.

### What makes a totality expressive rather than structured?

A totality is expressive when each part is treated as the manifestation of one inner essence, the parts differing only in costume, so the whole has a single contradiction between the essence and its appearances. It is structured when the parts are real, relatively autonomous instances with their own contradictions, held in a hierarchy of dominance that the economy sets.

The expressive model's deepest feature, for Althusser's purposes, is its treatment of time. In an expressive whole, history has one clock. Every level of the society lives in the same present, contemporaneous with the essence, because each level is the essence's own moment. A backward art and an advanced economy cannot truly coexist in such a model; one of them must be a survival, a leftover scheduled for disappearance. Althusser's counter-model denies the single clock. The levels of a structured whole develop unevenly, each at its own rhythm, some racing, some lagging, some carrying institutional survivals from older formations inside a newer one, and this non-contemporaneity is not an embarrassment to the theory but one of its central claims. Russia in 1917 held, inside one state, modern factories in Petrograd, a semi-feudal countryside, national movements at the empire's edges, and an autocracy of archaic form. The expressive model must call most of that unreal. The structured model calls it the explanation.

It is worth pausing on the fairness of the portrait, because a glossary that repeats a polemic as a description misleads its readers. Historians of philosophy dispute whether Hegel's whole is as simple as the portrait paints it, and Althusser's Hegel is avowedly a constructed opponent, an ideal type of expressive thinking rather than a census of Hegel scholarship. The concept of overdetermination does not stand or fall with the portrait's fairness to Hegel. It stands or falls as a claim about social causation: whether real formations behave as expressions of one contradiction or as structured combinations of several. The portrait's function is contrast, and the contrast is sharpest in the table that follows, which sets the two totalities side by side on the points where their difference becomes testable.

## The two totalities compared

The two totalities compared, side by side, give the concept its findable shape. Read across each row and the difference stops being a matter of philosophical taste and becomes a difference in what the analyst expects to find, where the analyst expects a break, and what the analyst would accept as refutation. The table is the article's instrument; the sections after it operate the instrument on the cases.

| Aspect | The expressive whole | The whole structured in dominance |
|---|---|---|
| What the parts express | Every part manifests one inner essence, as a phenomenon manifests what it is the appearance of | Each part is a relatively autonomous instance of a structure that has no single expressive centre |
| How many contradictions there are | One contradiction only, between the essence and its forms, simple and everywhere the same | Several real contradictions coexist, unevenly developed, with one of them dominant in a given conjuncture |
| What determines the outcome | The essence determines directly; in the economistic variant, the economy acts without mediation | The economy, in the last instance, appoints the dominant contradiction; the outcome itself is settled by the fusion of all of them |
| Where revolution is predicted | Where the single contradiction is most developed, in the most advanced economy | Where contradictions accumulate and fuse, which may be the least advanced link of a chain |
| What evidence counts against the model | A rupture where the essence was least developed, or indefinite survival where it was most developed | A rupture explained completely by one contradiction in isolation, with the others shown to be inert |

Two cells deserve prose emphasis. The first is the cell on the number of contradictions. The expressive model does not deny that a society contains many tensions; it denies that the tensions are several in the decisive sense, holding instead that each tension is the one essence refracted, so that the analyst who has grasped the essence has grasped every tension in advance. The structured model insists the contradictions are genuinely plural: the contradiction between capital and wage labour is not the contradiction between town and country wearing a mask, and neither is the national contradiction in an empire, and the proof of their plurality is that they develop at different speeds, mature in different decades, and can be combined by events into configurations none of them would have produced alone. The second cell needing emphasis is the last one, the refutation cell, because it is the row most summaries omit. A model that cannot state what would count against it has withdrawn from explanation into mood. Both totalities, as the table states their terms, can name their refuting evidence, and the concept's claim to seriousness rests heavily on the second column's ability to do so.

A reader pressed for time can take the table as the article's compressed content, and the persona layers at the end suggest ways of carrying it into study and teaching. A reader with time should notice what the table leaves out, because the omissions define the sections that follow: the mechanism by which dominance is assigned, the famous formula about the last instance, the Russian case in narrative form, and the Freudian mechanisms of condensation and displacement doing their social work. The table compares two static pictures. The concept, however, is about motion: about how dominance shifts, how contradictions fuse, and why the break comes at the joint rather than at the strongest point of the beam.

## Structured in dominance: several contradictions, one in command

The alternative model is named in the phrase structured in dominance, and each word earns its place. A whole is structured when its parts are not expressions but instances, arranged in relations that are real, durable, and unequal: an economic instance, a political instance, an ideological instance, each with its own effectivity, its own weight in the whole. The whole is in dominance when, among its several contradictions, one is dominant, organising the others around itself, setting the terms on which they can act, colouring them with its own character. Dominance is the concept's answer to the obvious question the plurality of contradictions raises: if there are several, why does a formation cohere at all, and why does it have a recognisable character in a given period rather than dissolving into a mere heap of tensions? It coheres because the contradictions are not equal partners. One of them commands, and the command gives the conjuncture its physiognomy.

The decisive subtlety is that the dominant contradiction is not fixed once and for all, and it is not automatically the economic one. In one conjuncture the political contradiction may dominate, as in a crisis of the state; in another the ideological contradiction may dominate, as in a struggle over the church or the schools; in another the economic contradiction takes command directly. The identities of the dominant and the dominated shift with circumstances, and Althusser credited Mao's essay on contradiction, with its distinction between the principal contradiction and its secondary companions, and between the principal and secondary aspects within a single contradiction, as a source for this mobility. What does not shift, in the theory, is the assignment mechanism: the economy determines in the last instance which contradiction occupies the dominant place. The economy is thus compared, in a figure Althusser's commentators often borrow, to the power that appoints the commander without fighting the battle itself. Battles are won and lost by the contradictions in the field. Which contradiction leads the army, and therefore what kind of battle it is, is settled one level back.

### How can the dominant contradiction change while the structure holds?

Dominance is a position inside a structure, not a substance, and positions can be reoccupied without the structure dissolving. The economy, determining in the last instance, can elevate the political contradiction in one conjuncture and the economic in another. What the structure guarantees is not which contradiction commands, but that one commands, and that the ranking has a ground.

Examples discipline the abstraction, and the concept's own favourite example is feudalism. In a feudal formation, on Althusser's reading, the political and ideological instances dominate: surplus is extracted not by a purely economic mechanism, the wage, but by direct political and legal coercion sanctified by religion, so the superstructure occupies the commanding heights while the economy, in the last instance, explains why it must: because the mode of extracting surplus from direct producers who possess their means of subsistence requires extra-economic force. Capitalism rearranges the hierarchy. Extraction proceeds through the wage contract, an economic mechanism, and the economy becomes dominant in its own person, pushing politics and ideology into relative subordination without abolishing their effectivity. One theory of the whole thereby explains two facts that the expressive model handles clumsily: why different kinds of society feel different in kind and not just in degree, and why the same theory can describe both without changing its basic vocabulary. Dominance is the variable; determination in the last instance is the constant; overdetermination is the name for the traffic between them.

This is also the point at which the model revises, rather than merely decorates, the older picture of base and superstructure. That picture, with its architectural metaphor of a foundation and what rests on it, is treated in full at [base and superstructure explained](/2010/02/01/base-and-superstructure-explained/), and the present glossary presupposes it rather than repeating it. What overdetermination adds is a refusal to let the architectural metaphor harden into a one-way street. The superstructure in the structured model is not a shadow cast by the base. It is a set of instances with real effects, relatively autonomous, capable of dominating a conjuncture, and yet not free-floating: their autonomy is relative precisely because the last instance assigns their rank. Readers who find the phrase relative autonomy slippery will find it examined on its own in a later section; the structural point to hold now is that autonomy and determination are not rivals in this model but two descriptions of one arrangement, the autonomy describing how instances operate day to day and the determination describing why the arrangement has the hierarchy it has.

## Determination in the last instance, and the hour that never comes

No phrase in the concept's neighbourhood has generated more commentary, or more suspicion, than determination in the last instance. The formula is Althusser's rescue of economic determination from the wreckage of economism. Economism had made the economy a first cause that acts directly, and its predictions had failed. Abandoning economic determination altogether was the other exit, and Althusser refused it, on the ground that a Marxism without the primacy of the economic in some form has dissolved into a general sociology of factors. The formula threads between the failures: the economy determines, but it determines in the last instance, and its mode of determination is to fix the hierarchy of the instances and the dominance among contradictions, not to script events. Engels had pointed this way in his late letters on historical materialism, insisting against impatient followers that the economic is the ultimately determining element in a field of interacting forces, and Althusser radicalised the concession into a doctrine.

The doctrine's most quoted sentence is also its most taunting. Having assigned the economy its last-instance role, Althusser adds that "the lonely hour of the last instance never comes". The sentence is not a retraction smuggled into the theory. It is the theory. The last instance never arrives in person, as an event where the economy acts alone and undisguised, because in every real conjuncture the economy acts through the structured whole it has organised: through the dominant contradiction it has appointed, through the fused and displaced forms the contradictions have taken. To wait for the economy to appear unmediated is to wait for the expressive model to be true. The economy's determination is like the determination a key gives to a piece of music: nothing in the performance is the key itself, no note is explained by pointing at the signature, and yet the signature governs which notes are consonant, which are strained, and how the whole can be heard. Remove the signature and the performance does not become free. It becomes a different performance, in a different key, and the analyst's task is to hear which key a conjuncture is in.

### What work does the last instance formula do?

The formula does three jobs at once. It preserves the Marxist claim that the economic is ultimately decisive, it denies that the economic ever acts alone or directly, and it assigns the economy a mechanism, the fixing of dominance among contradictions, which analysis can examine. Without it, the concept drifts toward pluralism, the economy demoted to one factor among others.

Suspicion of the formula is nevertheless rational, and the critics who call it a cop out are asking a real question, which the objection sections below take up in earnest: what observable difference does a determination make if its hour never comes? The defence available within the concept is that the difference is structural and comparative. Formations with different modes of production exhibit different standing hierarchies, as the feudal and capitalist examples show, and conjunctures within one formation exhibit dominance patterns that track the economy's requirements rather than varying at random. The claim is thus not that the economy will intervene at midnight. It is that the arrangement of the whole, at any hour, bears the economy's signature, and that the signature can be read by comparing arrangements across cases. Whether that defence is enough is the deepest question the concept faces, and this glossary does not pretend the question is closed.

## The puzzle the concept was built to solve: the Russian case

Every concept in this series has a motivating case, and overdetermination's case is the Russian Revolution. The puzzle can be posed as a schoolboy's objection to the classical theory. If revolution is the product of a mature contradiction between the forces and relations of production, revolution should have come first where capitalism was oldest and most developed, in Britain or Germany, and Russia, with its vast peasant majority, its thin industrial sector, its autocracy, and its economy entangled with foreign capital, should have come last. History ran the sequence backwards. The fullest narrative treatment of the case belongs to the series analysis at [october revolution marxist analysis](/2014/05/01/october-revolution-marxist-analysis/), and the problem it poses for theories of determination is treated at [economic determinism debate](/2010/03/15/economic-determinism-debate/); this glossary needs the case in the form Althusser used it, as the anomaly that forced a new concept of the whole.

Lenin had faced the anomaly in practice before any philosopher faced it in theory, and his response supplied the concept with its central image. Surveying the imperialist system as a chain stretched across the world, Lenin argued that the chain would break not at its strongest link, where industry was most advanced and the working class most organised, but at its weakest link, where the stresses of the whole system concentrated in a formation least able to bear them. Russia was that link. The metaphor is easy to quote and easy to misunderstand, so its logic wants stating slowly. A chain's breaking point is a property of the whole chain under tension, not of any link considered alone: the weakest link is weakest in relation to the strain the entire system transmits through it. Transferred to formations, the claim is that the site of rupture is determined by the accumulation of stresses in one place, stresses arising from the whole structure of imperialism and from the internal contradictions of the unlucky formation itself, rather than by the maturity of any single contradiction at that site.

### Why did Lenin call Russia the weakest link?

Lenin called Russia the weakest link because the stresses of the whole imperialist system, war above all, concentrated there on a formation whose own contradictions, agrarian, national, economic, and political, were simultaneously acute and mutually inflaming. The rupture is thus a property of the chain under tension rather than of the link in isolation.

Russia's accumulation was, on the analysis Althusser inherited, genuinely exceptional, and enumerating its strands in prose shows what fusion means concretely. The economic contradiction existed in a strange doubled form: islands of highly concentrated modern industry, some of the largest factories in Europe, set in an ocean of small peasant agriculture, so that the most advanced and the most archaic modes of production confronted one another inside one state. The agrarian contradiction, between a peasantry hungry for land and a landowning class protected by the autocracy, had been sharpened rather than settled by emancipation and by the reforms that followed the revolution of 1905. The national contradiction ran through an empire that held dozens of peoples in subordination, each with grievances the centre could neither satisfy nor suppress indefinitely. The political contradiction pitted an autocratic state, rigid and discrediting itself by incompetence, against every class in the society at once, landowners excepted, and even they despaired of it. Over all of these, from 1914, lay the war: a military contradiction between the empire's ambitions and its capacities, which armed the peasantry, ruined the currency, broke the transport system, and turned the state's own soldiers into a revolutionary constituency. No single strand on this list explains 1917. Germany had stronger industry and a stronger labour movement; France had sharper memories of revolution; Britain had a more developed capitalism by every index. What Russia had was the knot: all the strands pulled to their tightest in one place, each worsening the others, until the structure gave way not at its most developed point but at its most overdetermined one.

Althusser's essay dwells on the case because it converts an embarrassment into an instrument. Under the expressive model, 1917 is a scandal to be explained away, by pleading Russia's peculiarities or by promising that the advanced countries would follow in due course. Under the structured model, 1917 is exactly what the theory predicts once the theory is correctly stated: rupture occurs where contradictions fuse, and fusion is an empirical matter, discoverable by analysis of the conjuncture, not deducible from a single index of development. The case thereby does double duty in the concept's career. It is the evidence that motivated the concept and the standing illustration by which the concept is taught, and any adequate treatment must keep the two duties distinct, since an illustration chosen because it fits is weaker evidence than a prediction risked in advance. The sections on the weakest-link test take up that distinction directly, because the concept's claim to be more than a truism rests on whether the Russian reading can be generalised into a test that other cases could fail.

## The weakest-link test

The namable claim of this article can now be stated in its strong form. The weakest-link test holds that overdetermination earns its keep only when it issues in a determinate proposition about where ruptures occur: a break in a social formation happens at the point where contradictions have fused, and the analyst who invokes the concept must be able to name the contradictions, describe their fusion, and identify what would have to be different for the break to have occurred elsewhere or not at all. The claim is not that events have many causes. Any competent historian believes that, and a concept that merely repeats it adds vocabulary without adding knowledge. The claim is that the geography and timing of rupture are governed by accumulation and fusion rather than by the maturity of the single most advanced contradiction, and that this is a proposition with observable consequences, one that the historical record can embarrass.

Consider what the test excludes. It excludes the explanation that reaches a crisis and then lists the tensions present in the society, as if presence were explanation. Every society at every moment contains tensions; a list of them, however accurate, does not say why the break came in one year rather than a decade earlier, in one country rather than its neighbour, in one institution rather than another. The test demands the extra step: showing that the tensions were not merely present but knotted, that each had modified the others so that the combined configuration, and only that configuration, crossed the threshold of rupture. It thereby converts overdetermination from a description of complexity into a standard for explanations, a standard by which explanations can be graded. An explanation that names a dominant contradiction, traces its appointment to the structure of the whole, and shows the subordinate contradictions fusing around it passes. An explanation that inventories causes and stops fails, however impressive its inventory, and it fails on grounds the concept itself supplies.

### What would count as evidence against the weakest-link claim?

Evidence against the claim would be a documented rupture in which one contradiction demonstrably acted alone: a break whose timing and location are fully explained by the maturity of a single contradiction, with the formation's other contradictions shown to be quiet and disconnected. A repeated pattern of such cases, ruptures tracking one index of development, would restore the expressive prediction.

The test also clarifies what kind of thing the concept is, philosophically. It is not a law in the manner of a covering law in the physical sciences, from which the event can be deduced given the conditions. It is a constraint on adequate explanation, closer to a rule of method: it tells the analyst what a finished explanation must contain, and it forbids a class of finished explanations, the single-contradiction derivations, in advance. Methodological rules can be fruitful or sterile, and their worth is judged by the work they organise. The weakest-link rule has organised a recognizable body of work: analyses of conjunctures that proceed by inventorying a formation's contradictions, ranking them by dominance, and hunting for the fusion point, a procedure visible in the best Marxist historiography whether or not the word overdetermination is pronounced over it. The concept's defenders can fairly claim that the procedure predates the word, in Lenin's own practice, and that Althusser's contribution was to make the procedure self-conscious and to give it a theory of the whole to stand on. Its critics reply, also fairly, that a rule so hospitable can bless almost any narrative after the fact. Both claims are true, and the tension between them is the concept's permanent condition, examined head on in the objection sections below.

A concrete corollary is worth stating before the mechanisms are examined, because it is the corollary students most often miss. The weakest-link test reverses the analyst's instinct about strength. In an expressive reading, the analyst studies the strongest, most developed contradiction and waits for it to ripen. In a structured reading, the analyst studies the joints: the places where a formation's several arrangements abut and strain against one another, the colony inside the empire, the archaic countryside around the modern factory, the army drawn from a class the state is ruining. Ruptures belong to joints. The metaphor of the chain, properly heard, is about load distribution in a structure, and it directs attention away from the impressive and toward the strained, which is a genuinely non-obvious instruction and the strongest single reason the concept cannot be dismissed as a synonym for complexity. Complexity says the whole is hard to understand. The weakest-link test says where to look, and looking in the directed place is a falsifiable habit: formations may break at their most developed point after all, and if they reliably did, the instruction would be wrong and known to be wrong.

## Condensation and displacement, translated into social terms

The two Freudian mechanisms that travelled with the term do specific work in Althusser's hands, and distinguishing them prevents a common blur in which overdetermination becomes a general haze of mutual influence. Condensation, in the dream, is the compression of several latent thoughts into a single manifest element, so that the element is a knot in which many threads terminate. In the social translation, condensation names the process by which distinct contradictions, arising in different instances of a formation and obeying different rhythms, are fused into a single ruptural unity: a moment, a slogan, a crowd, a crisis in which the agrarian grievance, the national grievance, the war grievance, and the wage grievance cease to be separable complaints and become one movement against one order. The February days in Petrograd condensed bread queues, military mutiny, and political illegitimacy into a single event that none of the three, alone, would have composed. Condensation is thus the mechanism that produces the weakest link's snap: it is the fusion half of the fusion claim, the process by which accumulation becomes rupture rather than mere strain.

Displacement, in the dream, shifts the emphasis: the element that carries the charge is not the element that appears important, and the dream's apparent centre is a decoy for its real one. In the social translation, displacement names the shifting of dominance among contradictions, the process by which the contradiction that organises a conjuncture is replaced by another, or by which a conflict is lived and fought on a terrain adjacent to its ground. A struggle whose stakes are economic may be fought as a struggle over the church; a crisis of the economy may present itself as a crisis of the constitution; the dominant position changes hands, and with it the character of the whole conjuncture, while the displaced contradiction continues to work from its new seat. Analysts of ideology live on this mechanism, since a society's official account of its conflicts is displacement's natural product, and the analyst's craft consists partly in tracing the charge back from the terrain where the fight is visible to the contradiction where it is generated.

### How do condensation and displacement differ in Althusser's use?

Condensation fuses: it compresses several contradictions into one ruptural unity, as grievances over bread, land, war, and legitimacy fused in Petrograd in a single revolutionary moment. Displacement shifts: it moves dominance from one contradiction to another, so a conflict is fought on adjacent terrain, as an economic crisis is fought as a constitutional one.

The two mechanisms together give the concept its dynamics, and it is worth noticing how much of the concept's explanatory reputation rests on them rather than on the static picture of a structured whole. The table of two totalities compares structures at rest. Condensation and displacement describe the structure in motion: the fusing that precedes rupture and the sliding of dominance that precedes and follows it. A conjunctural analysis in this tradition is, in practice, the disciplined application of the two questions the mechanisms dictate. Where are the contradictions condensing, which separate grievances are being compressed into a single point of rupture, and where has dominance been displaced, which visible struggle is carrying a charge generated elsewhere? An analyst who can answer both questions about a formation has done most of what the concept asks. An analyst who invokes overdetermination without posing either question has used the word as an incantation, and the critics of the concept, when they are careful, are criticising the incantation rather than the instrument.

## Structural causality: a cause that exists only in its effects

Overdetermination presupposes a particular idea of what a social cause is, and Althusser developed that idea, largely in the companion volume Reading Capital, under the name structural causality. The idea is best approached through the two ideas of cause it rejects. The first is the mechanical or transitive cause of the billiard table: one thing strikes another, the effect is external to the cause, and the causal chain can be followed link by link with each link intact. The second is the expressive cause already met in the totality under attack: an essence that is present in each of its effects as their inner truth, prior to them and separable from them in thought. Structural causality is neither. A structure, on this account, is a cause that is immanent in its effects, present nowhere outside them, and knowable only through them. The structure of a formation does not hover above its instances pushing them around. It exists solely as the system of relations among those instances, and its causality is the way that system assigns places, weights, and licences to everything within it.

Spinoza is the acknowledged ancestor, and the borrowing parallels the Freudian one: another loan from outside the Marxist tradition, taken because the tradition's own stock of concepts lacked the needed shape. Spinoza's God is an immanent cause, not a craftsman standing outside the world but the world's own order, existing in and through its modes. Althusser secularised the shape. The mode of production is immanent in the formation it structures: it is not a thing behind the factory, the court, and the school, but the pattern of their articulation, the fact that the factory's labour process, the court's property law, and the school's credentialing fit together in a determinate way and reproduce one another's conditions. Structural causality therefore explains a peculiarity that transitive and expressive models both fumble, which is how a cause can be everywhere effective without ever being directly observed. One never sees a mode of production. One sees its effects, in the specific way a wage bargain presupposes a property regime, a property regime presupposes a coercive apparatus, and a coercive apparatus presupposes a fiscal base, each instance holding the others in place. The structure is that holding.

### Why did Althusser need Spinoza for the idea of a cause?

Althusser needed Spinoza because the models of causation on offer were the two his concept rejects: a mechanical cause striking from outside, and an expressive essence unfolding from inside. Spinoza's immanent cause offered a shape, a cause present in its effects, the shape of a structure. Without it, overdetermination slides toward billiard ball causation or the unfolding of an essence.

The connection to overdetermination is tight enough to state as an identity of problems. Overdetermination says every contradiction is modified by the whole's other contradictions. Structural causality says the whole that does the modifying is not an additional thing but the relations among the contradictions themselves. Put the two together and the picture completes: the structure is not a puppeteer standing behind the conjuncture, scheduling the fusion of its contradictions from outside. The structure is the conjuncture's internal arrangement, and its causality is exercised precisely in the way each contradiction's operation is specified by its place in that arrangement. This is why the concept's careful users resist the phrase the structure caused the crisis as firmly as they resist the phrase the economy caused the crisis. Both phrases smuggle in a transitive cause. The concept's own grammar is different: within the structure, given its arrangement, the contradictions fused at the weakest link. The grammar is fussier, and the fussiness is the price of the model's coherence.

A practical dividend follows for the working analyst, and glossaries exist to pay dividends. If the cause is immanent, then the evidence for the structure is the pattern of effects, and explanation proceeds by reconstruction: the analyst maps the instances, their contradictions, their ranks, and their articulations, and the map itself is the causal claim. There is no further hidden variable to be discovered behind the map, and no apology is owed for its absence. Disagreement then takes a productive form, as rival maps can be compared for what they explain: which map accounts for the timing, which for the location, which for the form the rupture took. Rivalry among maps is the normal science of this tradition, and it is a healthier spectacle than the rivalry among slogans that the concept, at its worst, also licenses.

## Relative autonomy, held within its limits

The phrase relative autonomy belongs to the same machinery and suffers the same fate in circulation, where it is often used as a permission slip: the superstructure, it is said, is relatively autonomous, so the analyst may discuss politics or culture without reference to the economy at all. The concept's actual doctrine is narrower and more interesting. Each instance of the formation, the economic, the political, the ideological, has its own specific effectivity and its own rhythm of development; none is a puppet of the others; each can, in a given conjuncture, occupy the dominant place. That is the autonomy. The qualifier carries the other half: the autonomy is relative because the instances form one structure, because their articulation is not optional, and because the economy, in the last instance, determines the hierarchy among them and therewith the limits within which each instance's freedom operates. A parliament may decide a great deal, and decide it by its own procedures, on its own timetable, swayed by its own traditions, and none of that is illusory. The claim is only that the range of what a parliament in a capitalist formation can decide, and durably enact, is set by conditions it did not choose, and that the setting of the range is the economy's quiet work.

### Why is relative autonomy not independence?

Independence would mean an instance whose effects answer to nothing outside itself, a level of society with a self contained logic and an uncaused history. Relative autonomy denies that while granting effectivity: the instance works by its own rhythms and can dominate a conjuncture, but inside a structure whose hierarchy the economy assigns, so its freedom is genuine and bounded.

The distinction matters because each half alone produces a familiar error. Autonomy without the qualifier produces pluralism, the picture of society as a parliament of independent spheres, each sovereign in its territory, with the economy reduced to one ministry among others and materialism quietly retired. The qualifier without autonomy produces economism in a new costume, a model in which the superstructure's rhythms are politely acknowledged and then ignored, so that the analysis proceeds as if the economy scripted the events after all. The concept holds both halves by assigning them to different questions. Autonomy answers the question of how instances behave: by their own logics, unevenly, sometimes dominantly. Determination answers the question of why the whole has the shape it has: because the mode of production sets the articulation and ranks the instances. A treatment that keeps the two questions separate can use the phrase without embarrassment. A treatment that fuses them into a vague gesture toward complexity has joined the incantation school, and the objection sections, which follow the remaining machinery, will deal with the school and its critics together.

## Materialism after the last instance

The question whether the concept abandons materialism deserves a section of its own in the body, separate from its short FAQ treatment, because it is the question on which the concept's identity inside its own tradition turns. The suspicion is easy to state. Classical materialism, in its Marxist form, asserts the primacy of material production in social explanation. Overdetermination appears to qualify that primacy past recognition: the economy never acts alone, its decisive hour never comes, other instances dominate conjunctures, and every economic contradiction arrives modified by political and ideological hands. A reader may fairly ask what remains of the primacy beyond a title of honour, awarded to the economy out of loyalty while the causal honours are distributed elsewhere. The question was pressed within Marxism from the start, by critics who suspected that the Parisian philosophy of the 1960s had kept the vocabulary and resigned the doctrine.

The answer available inside the concept runs through the distinction between primacy and monopoly. Materialism, on the Althusserian account, never required the economy to monopolise causation; that requirement belongs to economism, which is a deformation of materialism rather than its rigorous form. What materialism requires is that the order of explanation terminate, at the end of its regresses, in the material organisation of production and reproduction: that when the analyst asks why a formation's instances are articulated as they are, why its dominance hierarchy stands as it stands, why its contradictions take the forms they take, the final answers cite the way the society produces its means of life. Overdetermination distributes causal work across the whole while reserving that terminal position to the economy, and the reservation is not ceremonial. It has the comparative content described earlier: hierarchies differ systematically between feudal and capitalist formations, and the difference is explained from the mode of surplus extraction. A materialism that can be wrong about such comparisons, as this one can, has kept enough content to be a doctrine rather than a loyalty badge.

### Where does materialism live inside a theory of fused contradictions?

Materialism lives at the end of the regresses, in the answers given to the structural questions: why the instances are articulated in this pattern, why the dominance hierarchy stands, why the contradictions wear these forms. Those answers cite the organisation of production and reproduction. Day to day causation is distributed across the contradictions; the architecture that distributes it is material.

Honesty requires recording the other verdict, which many materialists have returned. On the hostile reading, the terminal position is too remote to govern anything: a primacy that never issues in a proximate difference, that is compatible with any conjunctural outcome provided the analyst narrates the regress skilfully, is a primacy in name only, and the doctrine has become a church with a distant god. The sections on the deflationary objection state this verdict at full strength, since the glossary's neutrality rules require the strongest form of the case against the concept to appear in the concept's own article. What can be said here, by way of boundary marking, is that the dispute is not about the word but about the standards for explanatory termination generally: every research programme has terminal commitments it does not test in each study, and the charge that a terminal commitment is empty must show that it excludes nothing, a showing the comparative hierarchy cases make difficult. The dispute is live, the concept's users are divided by it, and a reader who finishes this article unable to state both sides in their strongest forms has not finished the article.

## Not interaction, not pluralism, not a list of factors

A concept is partly defined by its neighbours, and overdetermination has three neighbours from which it must be separated with care, because in casual usage the four are endlessly confused. The first neighbour is interaction. In an interaction model, several distinct factors, each fully formed by its own history, meet and modify one another's effects, as two chemicals react or as two billiard balls, having arrived from separate trajectories, alter each other's paths. The factors are prior to their meeting, and their identities survive it. Overdetermination denies the priority. A contradiction inside a formation was never formed elsewhere and imported; it was constituted, from the start, by its place in the whole, and the whole was always inside it. The wage relation in a formation with a strong peasant sector and an autocratic state is not the pure wage relation plus external influences. It is a different object from the wage relation in a formation of smallholders and parliaments, different in its organisation, its legal dress, its cultural meaning, and its explosive charge. Interaction language, with its picture of self sufficient factors colliding, systematically understates this internality, and the student who substitutes interaction for overdetermination in an essay has chosen the weaker instrument while believing the choice was one of synonyms.

The second neighbour is pluralism, the doctrine that society has many centres of power and many lines of causation, none privileged, and that explanation consists in weighing them case by case. Pluralism and overdetermination agree in rejecting the single-cause picture, and the agreement ends there. Pluralism has no structure: its factors float in a flat space, and the question of why they are arranged as they are cannot be posed inside it. Overdetermination is a theory of arrangement. It insists that the factors are contradictions of one formation, articulated in a hierarchy, with dominance assigned and with the economy holding the last instance. Where pluralism sees a parliament without a constitution, overdetermination sees a constitution without a puppeteer: no instance scripts the others, and yet the whole is not an aggregate, because the ranks and relations are determinate and explicable. The third neighbour is the factor list, the working method of much journalism and some scholarship, in which an event is explained by enumerating the conditions that contributed to it: the war, the harvest, the succession crisis, the price of bread. The factor list is not false; it is unfinished. It tells the reader what was present and leaves untouched the questions the concept exists to answer: how the factors were combined, which of them dominated, what fused them into a single crisis, and why the combination crossed a threshold that none of its members approached alone.

### How does the concept differ from an interaction model?

Interaction begins with factors formed elsewhere and lets them collide, keeping their identity through the collision. Overdetermination begins with a whole whose contradictions were constituted by their places inside it, so the wage relation under an autocracy is a different object, in organisation and charge, from the wage relation under a parliament. The factors do not survive their combination unchanged.

The separations converge on a single discipline, which can be stated as a rule for reading and writing. Whenever the word overdetermined is used, the reader is entitled to ask three questions and to receive answers with content: which contradictions, fused how, under whose dominance? An interaction answer names factors and their collisions, and fails the second and third questions. A pluralist answer names centres and their weights, and fails the third. A factor list names conditions, and fails all three after the first. The concept's answer names contradictions of a formation, the fusion that condensation describes, and the dominance that the last instance assigns. The rule is severe, and much published usage fails it, which is not an argument against the rule but the explanation of why the rule is needed, and of why the deflationary critics, met next in their strongest form, have a target worth shooting at.

## The misreading that follows the term everywhere

Of the several misreadings this glossary is charged with correcting, one has escaped the seminar room and colonised the word's public meaning: overdetermined as a synonym for multiply caused, for having more causes than were needed, for being, in the loose journalistic phrase, over explained. Under this reading, a historian calls an outcome overdetermined when several sufficient explanations of it coexist, as if explanation were a budget that could be overspent. The reading has a surface plausibility. Freud's dream element does have several determinants; the Russian knot did have many strands; and a reader in a hurry can be forgiven for keeping the arithmetic and dropping the structure. But the arithmetic is the least of the concept, and dropping the structure drops the claim. Multiple causation says the causes are several and each is what it is. Overdetermination says the causes are contradictions of one whole, that each is modified by the rest, that their fusion and not their sum is what acts, and that the site and moment of rupture are determined by that fusion rather than by any member's maturity. The two claims differ the way a chord differs from a pile of notes.

### Is overdetermination just saying things have many causes?

No. The claim concerns not the number of causes but their mode of combination: no contradiction in a social formation ever operates in isolation, so the site of rupture is fixed by which contradictions fuse, not by which single one is most developed. Hence revolution in Russia, not in the most industrialised countries.

The misreading survives because it is comfortable. It allows the writer to gesture at complexity without performing an analysis, to concede that the world is tangled and proceed exactly as before, with a clear conscience and a longer word. Against this comfort the concept's own tradition supplies a rebuke in advance, since the founding essay is not a meditation on complexity but an intervention in a specific dispute about a specific revolution, written to rescue a theory from a falsified prediction. A term born in an argument about why the chain broke in Russia is ill used as a pillow for the thought that things are complicated. The glossary's corrective is therefore practical as much as philological: any use of the word that could be replaced, without loss, by the phrase for many reasons, is a misuse, and any use that survives the replacement test, that says something about fusion, dominance, and the location of rupture which the phrase cannot say, is the concept at work.

There is a reciprocal error worth naming while the misreading is on the table, committed not by the word's casual users but by its devotees. This is the use of overdetermination as a solvent for every definite claim: confronted with a prediction, a comparison, or a request for mechanism, the devotee murmurs that the matter is overdetermined and treats the murmur as an answer. The solvent use is worse than the arithmetic use, because the arithmetic use merely says little while the solvent use forbids saying more, converting a concept built to sharpen conjunctural analysis into a licence to decline it. The two errors meet in the middle, in prose where the word appears often and the named contradictions never do. The sections on the deflationary objection now take up the critics who built their case on exactly this meeting point.

## Four more misreadings, corrected in turn

The multiple causation gloss is the famous misreading, but the brief for this glossary lists four companions, and each deserves its paragraph of correction, since each fastens on a real feature of the concept and twists it by one degree. The first companion holds that overdetermination denies economic determination altogether, that with the last instance indefinitely postponed and dominance circulating among the instances, the economy has been politely shown the door. The correction has been built piece by piece in the sections above: determination was relocated, not repealed, moved from the scripting of events to the assignment of dominance and the architecture of the whole. A doctrine that explains why feudalism's commanding instance is political and capitalism's is economic, from the mode of extracting surplus in each case, has not abandoned economic determination. It has specified the only form of it that the historical record, with its Russian anomaly in plain view, permits an honest theory to hold.

The second companion is the assumption of coinage, that Althusser invented the word. He did not, and said so: the term is Freud's, naturalised in Marxist philosophy with its passport stamped, and the German original, Überdeterminierung, had a career in psychoanalytic writing before and beside its social career. The correction matters beyond scholarly tidiness. A coined term might mean whatever its coiner stipulates; a borrowed term imports the structure of its home discipline, and the import is the concept's explanatory engine, the knot of determinants crossing in a single element. Readers who know the loan can test the concept against its template. Readers who believe in the coinage can only take the concept on authority, and concepts taken on authority are the ones that decay into incantations. The third companion concerns dominance: the belief that structured in dominance means the economy is always the dominant instance, so that the model's mobility is a sham and economism returns by the back stairs. The correction is the feudal case and its like. In formations where surplus extraction requires direct coercion, the political and ideological instances dominate in person; the economy's determination consists in explaining why they must. Dominance describes which contradiction organises a conjuncture, determination describes why the organisation stands as it does, and confusing the two words collapses the model into the doctrine it was built to replace.

### Did Althusser invent the word from nothing?

No. The word is Freud's, taken from the theory of dreams and acknowledged as a loan in the essay that introduces the social use, and its German form had circulated in psychoanalytic writing for decades. The borrowing is substantive: the dream element determined by several intersecting thoughts supplies the template, a node of crossing determinations, that the social concept copies.

The fourth companion misreading treats the last instance formula as a concession, a polite retreat in which the theory, having announced economic determination, withdraws it under cover of a paradox. On this reading the sentence about the lonely hour is an apology, the sound of a doctrine paying its respects to a claim it no longer believes. The correction lies in the formula's function within the argument of the founding essay, where it is not a hedge appended to the doctrine but the doctrine itself, stated at the point of maximum tension. Althusser's polemic ran against economism, for which determination meant direct action, and against the humanist and pluralist alternatives, for which the economy's privilege dissolved. The formula is the narrow ridge between those opponents, and a ridge is not a retreat from either side but the only ground from which both can be fought. That the ridge is narrow, and that many who have tried to walk it have slid to one side or the other, is a fact about the concept's difficulty. It is not evidence that the formula was a concession, unless every difficult distinction is a concession in disguise.

### Does structured in dominance make the economy permanently dominant?

No. Dominance circulates: the political instance dominates where extraction needs direct coercion, the ideological can dominate a struggle over schools or churches, and the economic contradiction can dominate in its own person under capitalism. The economy's permanence lies in determination, in assigning the ranks, not in occupying the top rank, which would be economism restored.

## The deflationary objection, stated at full strength

The neutrality discipline of this series requires that the strongest objection to an article's subject be stated as its proponents would state it, and the objection to overdetermination is neither obscure nor weak. It can be assembled from the analytical criticism that greeted the concept in the decade after its publication and has never been satisfactorily answered, only outlasted. Stated at full strength, it runs as follows. A determination that is always modified by everything else is not a determination. If every contradiction is affected by every other, if the economy's decisive instance never arrives, if dominance can migrate to any instance as the analyst requires, then the theory is compatible with every possible course of events, and a theory compatible with everything explains nothing. Whatever happens, the analyst can point to a fusion; whatever fails to happen, the analyst can point to a displacement; the vocabulary redescribes outcomes in its own terms and calls the redescription an explanation. The concept is a machine for being right after the fact, and its apparent profundity is the profundity of a horoscope, which also has a term for every eventuality and a mechanism that can never be caught failing.

The objection sharpens when it turns from the concept to its materialist credentials, and this sharpening is the version recorded in the run's instructions as the one to answer. A determination whose decisive moment never comes, the critic says, is a determination in name only. The theory preserves the vocabulary of materialism, base and superstructure, determination, the last instance, while draining that vocabulary of the content that made it materialist, namely the assertion that identifiable material processes govern outcomes in ways that could, in principle, be checked and found wanting. What remains is an idealism of the structure: an invisible arrangement, known only through its effects, compatible with all of them, doing the work that Geist did for the Hegelians, with the difference that the Hegelians knew they were idealists. Writers in the Althusserian school's own aftermath, among them Barry Hindess and Paul Hirst in their successive self criticisms, pressed versions of this charge against the theoreticism of the whole enterprise; E. P. Thompson, from the opposing camp of a humanist Marxism, prosecuted a parallel case against the school's abstraction from experience and agency. The critics disagreed with each other about nearly everything else. Their convergence on this point is what gives the objection its standing.

A third strand of the objection is sociological, and it may be the most damaging because it is the most easily verified. Whatever the concept's founders intended, its career in the literature has been, in large part, a career of the misuse this article has been correcting: the word deployed as a synonym for complexity, as a solvent for definite claims, as a badge of theoretical sophistication pinned to analyses that name no contradictions, rank no dominance, and risk no prediction. A concept is partly responsible for its standard use. If the standard use of a precision instrument is as a ornament, the critic is entitled to wonder whether the instrument's precision was ever real, or whether the appearance of precision, the German noun, the Freudian pedigree, the paradoxical formula, was the product all along. The objection, fully stated, is therefore threefold: the concept is unfalsifiable in logic, evacuated in doctrine, and decorative in practice. Any answer that meets only one strand has not met the objection.

## The answer, and the concession that comes with it

The answer begins by accepting the objection's standard. A theory that excludes nothing explains nothing, and the concept must name its exclusions or stand condemned by the rule its own table of totalities announced. The exclusions are these. The concept excludes ruptures that track the maturity of a single contradiction; it excludes conjunctures in which the subordinate contradictions can be shown to have been inert; it excludes dominance hierarchies that vary randomly with respect to the mode of production; and it excludes, as a finished form of explanation, the single-cause derivation and the unweighted factor list. These are not trivial exclusions. The first two describe the actual predictions of the expressive and economistic theories the concept opposed, so the concept stands in genuine empirical rivalry with real alternatives, and the Russian case, with Germany as its control, is a case the rivals handle badly and it handles well: the most developed capitalism did not break, the least developed major power did, and the distribution of fusion across the two cases is an observable matter of agrarian crisis, national subordination, military collapse, and state rigidity, not a free choice of the narrator. The weakest-link test is the form in which the concept accepts the objection's challenge. It converts overdetermination from an atmosphere into a wager: breaks occur at fusion points, and the analyst must locate the fusion before knowing where the break will be, or admit, in cases already past, exactly which evidence about inert contradictions would have refuted the reading.

The doctrinal strand is answered by the comparative content of determination, already developed in the materialism section: the last instance is not an appointment endlessly deferred but a standing assignment whose effects are visible in the systematic differences between formations, differences a random hierarchy could not produce. The critic who calls this too remote owes an alternative account of why feudal extraction wears political dress and capitalist extraction wears economic dress, and the economistic account, that the economy simply acts, is precisely the one the record refutes. Remoteness, in any case, is a charge that cuts at every structural theory in the sciences, each of which posits arrangements known through effects; the question is never whether the posit is remote but whether it organises comparisons that its rivals organise worse.

Then the concession, which the brief for this article requires to be made without varnish. A great deal of writing that uses the term does exactly what the objection describes. It invokes overdetermination to avoid ranking causes, to avoid mechanisms, to avoid the risk of being wrong, and it treats the word as a conclusion when the word is at best a programme for reaching one. The concession does not damage the concept as this glossary has reconstructed it, since the reconstructed concept supplies the very standards, name the contradictions, show the fusion, assign the dominance, state the refutation, by which the degenerate usage is convicted. But the concession does fix the concept's honest price. Overdetermination is not a theory that can be applied by citation. It is a discipline that must be re-earned in every analysis, and where the discipline lapses the word is worse than useless, because it lends the prestige of rigour to its absence. The reader who takes one sentence from this article into the world should take that one: the word is a promissory note, and the analysis is the payment.

## Reading the founding essay closely

Glossaries usually summarise; this one pauses to read, because the founding essay's argument is frequently cited from its summary rather than from its pages, and the summary drops exactly the moves that protect the concept from the truism charge. The essay proceeds by taking a famous difficulty in Marx literally. Marx had insisted that his dialectic was the opposite of Hegel's, not its continuation, and the question the essay presses is what the opposite could consist in. If both dialectics speak of contradiction, negation, and totality, the difference cannot be a matter of vocabulary. It must be a difference in the structure of the whole that the dialectic describes. From this exegetical puzzle the essay extracts its thesis: Hegel's whole is expressive, its contradictions are one contradiction, the self alienation of a simple principle; Marx's whole, in the practice of his analyses and in the tradition of Lenin and Mao, is structured, its contradictions are several, uneven, and overdetermined. The concept is thus presented as a discovery about Marx rather than an invention imposed on him, a claim about what the mature Marxist analyses already do, which the essay undertakes to make explicit and thereby defensible.

The essay's handling of Hegel's contradiction deserves a reader's patience, because it contains the germ of everything distinctive in the concept. In the expressive whole, the contradiction is simple in a technical sense: it has one content, the opposition between the essence and its estranged form, and every concrete conflict in the society is that opposition in local dress. It follows that the contradiction develops uniformly, deepens everywhere at the same rate, and resolves in a single negation that restores the essence at a higher level. History, on this model, is a monograph written by one author. Against the monograph the essay sets the archive of revolutionary practice. Lenin's writings of 1917 do not read like the unfolding of one contradiction. They read like a dispatch from a knot: the war, the peasantry, the nationalities, the collapse of the state, the vacillation of the parties, each strand tracked separately and then weighed in combination. Mao's treatise on contradiction, for its part, builds plurality and unevenness into the category itself, distinguishing principal from secondary contradictions and insisting that the principal position changes hands as conditions change. The essay's claim is that practice had already revised the theory, and that philosophy's task was to write the revision down. Whether the claim about Marx's own texts survives scholarly scrutiny is debated; the claim about the practice, that Leninist and Maoist analysis works by fusion and dominance rather than by essence, is the essay's solid core, and it is what the weakest-link test generalises.

One further move in the essay is regularly missed, and it is the move that connects the concept to the problem of knowledge. Althusser argues that the concrete analysis of a conjuncture is not a preliminary to theory but theory's completion, since the general concepts, contradiction, structure, dominance, exist precisely to be specified in cases, and a specification is not an illustration pasted onto a finished doctrine. The essay calls the object of such analysis the concrete situation, and insists that its complexity is not the world's resistance to theory but theory's proper element. This is why the essay can be read, anachronistically but not unfairly, as a methodology text: it teaches a way of building explanations, from the inventory of contradictions through their ranking to the location of their fusion, and the concept of overdetermination is the name of the building code. Readers who approach the term through the essay's method, rather than through the secondary literature's atmosphere, reliably come away with the stronger version of it, the version that can be wrong.

## Unevenness inside the concept: contradictions do not keep the same time

Uneven development appears in the brief for this article as a property of the structured whole, and it deserves development in its own right because it is the feature of the model that working historians have found most usable, often without the surrounding philosophy. The claim is that the several contradictions of a formation, and the several instances themselves, develop at different rates and according to different rhythms, so that a formation is never contemporaneous with itself. Its economy may be organised on advanced lines while its political forms are archaic; its ideology may carry survivals from formations long dead, cults, kinship codes, and legal fictions that persist inside a social order whose economy has no use for them yet cannot dissolve them; its regions may live in different centuries of technique within one set of borders. The expressive model must treat such non-contemporaneity as illusion or lag, a defect the essence will iron out. The structured model treats it as a permanent and causally potent condition: the archaic inside the modern is not decoration but load bearing, and many ruptures are precisely the sound of an old element and a new one, forced to coexist, finally tearing at their joint.

### How do contradictions develop unevenly inside one formation?

Each contradiction obeys the rhythm of its own instance: economies transform at the speed of investment and technique, states at the speed of institutions and wars, ideologies at the speed of generations and schools. A formation therefore contains several clocks, none synchronised, and its crises begin where the clocks disagree most violently.

Russia remains the type case, but the generalisation matters, since a concept that fits one case is an annotation and a concept that fits the run of cases is an instrument. Consider what unevenness does to the idea of a revolutionary situation. In the ripeness model, a situation ripens as the central contradiction matures, and the analyst's clock is the economy's clock. In the uneven model, a situation forms when the disagreements among the clocks reach a configuration where the usual compensations fail: the state can no longer borrow the ideology's authority to cover the economy's failures, the army can no longer be counted on to settle the agrarian question by bayonet, the national movements can no longer be bought with reforms the treasury cannot fund. Nothing in the situation requires the economic contradiction to be at its most mature. It requires the compensations to be exhausted, and compensation is a relation among contradictions, not a property of any one. This is overdetermination in its temporal form: the when of a rupture is determined by the same fusion that determines its where, and a theory of timing falls out of the concept as a by-product, which is one more reason the concept cannot be equated with the bare observation that causes are many. Many causes have no theory of timing. Fusion under exhausted compensation does.

## The conjuncture: the concept's unit of analysis

Every theory has a characteristic unit, the object it is built to explain, and overdetermination's unit is the conjuncture: the specific configuration of a formation's contradictions at a specific moment, with its specific ranking, its specific fusions in progress, its specific displacements under way. The concept cannot be applied to an epoch in general or to a society in the abstract, because the determinations it names, dominance, fusion, unevenness, are properties of moments. This is a restriction with teeth. It means that the sentence capitalism is overdetermined says nothing, while the sentence the crisis of a particular state in a particular year was overdetermined by the fusion of its fiscal, military, and legitimacy contradictions under a dominant political one says something that can be examined, disputed, and improved. The conjuncture is where the concept lives, and treatments that lift it out of the conjuncture into a general philosophy of everything have performed the exact abstraction the concept forbids.

The conjuncture as a unit also explains the concept's curious affinity with political practice, an affinity its detractors hold against it and its defenders prize. A conjunctural analysis is, in form, a brief for action: it ranks the contradictions to find the dominant one, locates the weakest link to find the point of application, and reads the displacements to find the terrain on which the struggle is actually being fought, as distinct from the terrain on which it is officially described. Lenin's reputation as a reader of conjunctures, his insistence in 1917 on the specific configuration of that spring and autumn against comrades who reasoned from the general theory, is the affinity's founding example. The concept generalised the reputation into a method. It also thereby exposed itself to a standing temptation: the conjunctural brief can be written to order, its rankings chosen to ratify a strategy decided in advance, and the history of the concept's use inside political organisations contains enough such briefs to keep the critics supplied. The discipline the concept requires, and that the objection sections enforce, is that the ranking be argued from the structure and be vulnerable to the evidence, especially the evidence of the next conjuncture, which will punish a ranking chosen for convenience.

A last feature of the conjuncture unit deserves record, because it shapes how the concept can be taught. Conjunctures are comparable. Two formations in crisis, or one formation in two crises, can be laid side by side and their configurations compared: which contradiction dominated, where the fusion gathered, which compensation failed first. Comparison is the concept's laboratory, the nearest thing it has to an experiment, and the weakest-link test is a rule for conducting comparisons honestly, by requiring the analyst to state, for each case, the fusion and the dominance before drawing the moral. The sections below on the concept's predictions and on the discipline of its use are, in effect, instructions for that laboratory.

## What the concept predicts, and what it forbids

A concept that survived the objection sections must be able to say, in plain terms, what the world looks like if it is right and what it would look like if it were wrong. For overdetermination the predictions are of a distinctive kind: not forecasts of events, which no theory of society honestly issues, but expectations about patterns, about where explanation will succeed and where it will fail. If the concept is right, then across the run of modern revolutions and major political ruptures, the sites of rupture should correlate with the accumulation and fusion of contradictions rather than with indices of economic maturity alone; the advanced formations should display, in their survival, the compensating arrangements, the displacements and partial condensations, that absorbed their crises; and the timing of breaks should track the exhaustion of those compensations rather than the crossing of developmental thresholds. If the concept is wrong, the patterns should run the other way: ruptures should track maturity indices once other factors are held steady, and the elaborate vocabulary of fusion and dominance should add nothing to explanations built from the central contradiction with auxiliary conditions attached. These are empirical expectations about historiography, testable in the only laboratory the subject has, the comparative reading of cases, and the fact that the concept can state them is its charter of seriousness.

The prohibitions are the predictions' other face, and for the working writer they matter more. The concept forbids explaining an event by a contradiction the analyst has not shown to be connected to the event's other strands; it forbids treating a society's levels as a stack of reflections, so that a cultural phenomenon is read as the economy's symptom without the mediations being exhibited; it forbids the word ripeness and its cognates as explanations, since ripeness names the conclusion of an analysis that must instead be performed; and it forbids, most practically, the closing gesture in which the analyst, having listed tensions, declares the outcome overdetermined and stops. Each prohibition corresponds to a failure mode that the historical literature displays in abundance, which is the concept's pragmatic defence: it was written against real and common errors, and a rule that forbids real errors is not a truism even when careless speakers use it as one. Truism is a property of utterances, not of instruments, and the same sentence about fusion can be a truism in a book review and a research programme in an archive.

The pattern expectations also clarify the concept's relation to its parent theory's failed forecast, the expectation of revolution in the advanced West. The structured model does not merely excuse the failure after the fact; it converts the failure into evidence about the advanced formations themselves, directing analysis toward their compensations: the capacity of parliamentary displacement to move class conflict onto constitutional terrain, the capacity of rising consumption to condense and dissipate grievances that a poorer capitalism would have fused, the capacity of empire and then of global supply arrangements to displace contradictions outward onto weaker links. Whether those analyses are correct is arguable case by case. What they demonstrate is the concept working as designed, generating questions about the formations that did not break which are as specific as its questions about the one that did. A truism generates no questions. The concept, whatever its liabilities, generates them in bulk.

## A discipline for using the term well

From the machinery and the objections together, a discipline for the term's legitimate use can be extracted, and a glossary is the right place to codify it, since glossaries are consulted at the moment of writing. The discipline has the form of obligations. Whoever writes that a situation was overdetermined owes the reader, first, an inventory: the contradictions in play, named individually, with the instance each belongs to and the evidence that each was active rather than decorative. Second, a ranking: which contradiction dominated the conjuncture, and the structural reason for its dominance, referred in the last instance to the organisation of production. Third, a fusion account: the mechanism, condensation, by which the separate contradictions were compressed into the crisis at hand, or the displacement account if the claim is that dominance shifted rather than fused. Fourth, a refutation clause: a statement of what the record would have to contain for the reading to be withdrawn, such as a demonstration that one of the named contradictions was inert or that a single one suffices. A use that discharges the four obligations has earned the word. A use that discharges none has borrowed the word's authority, and the borrowing, multiplied across a literature, is what the deflationary critics are actually describing when they describe the concept's career.

### How should the term be used in an essay?

Use the term as a commitment, not a garnish: name the contradictions, rank them by dominance with a structural reason, show the fusion or the displacement that produced the moment under discussion, and state what evidence would make you withdraw the reading. An essay that does those four things may use the word freely.

The discipline has a stylistic corollary, which examiners and editors enforce even when they have never heard the term's theory. Overdetermination belongs at the end of an analysis, as its summary, not at the beginning as its substitute. Placed at the beginning, the word announces that complexity is coming and licenses the writer to wander; the reader braces for a survey and receives one. Placed at the end, the word is a verdict on work already done, compressing an inventory, a ranking, and a fusion into a single term whose content the preceding pages have supplied. The same logic governs the concept's spoken use in seminars, where the word functions best as a challenge, a request for the inventory, rather than as a full stop. Teachers who adopt the discipline, as the persona layer below suggests, find that the word's frequency in student writing drops sharply while its accuracy rises, which is the signature of a term restored to its trade.

## Agency inside an overdetermined world

One question trails the concept through every audience, and a complete glossary must meet it inside the body rather than abandoning it to the question pages: if outcomes are settled by fusions of contradictions within structures that assign dominance behind the actors' backs, what becomes of agency, of the parties, leaders, and crowds whose choices fill the narratives? The worry powered the humanist side of the famous quarrels around the school, where Thompson and others charged the whole apparatus with dissolving men and women into bearers of structures, and it retains force against careless versions of the concept. The careful version has an answer, and the answer is once again the conjuncture. Structures do not act; they arrange. The arrangement sets the menu of possible actions, their costs, and their likely collisions, but the acting is done by organisations and persons reading the conjuncture well or badly, and the difference between readings is historically enormous. The same Russian knot of 1917 was available to every party; the outcomes differed because one organisation had spent years building the capacity to act at the fusion point and its rivals had not. Overdetermination, properly used, raises the premium on political intelligence rather than abolishing its object: where the expressive model lets the actor wait for ripeness, the structured model rewards the actor who can read a configuration, and punishes, with irrelevance, the actor who cannot.

Here the concept's relationship to its parent thinker must be marked with care, since the philosopher of structural causality is often accused of a fatalism his political writings belie. The accusation and the record are both available in the profile of Althusser linked at the start of this article, and the glossary's task is only to keep the concept itself clean of the confusion. The concept says that actions succeed or fail inside configurations they do not choose. That is not fatalism; it is the premise of every serious strategy, military, electoral, or revolutionary, and its denial, the belief that will alone arranges the board, has a worse historical record than the concept it is used to scold. What the concept does deny is the expressive picture of agency, in which a class or a leader embodies the essence of the age and history unfolds through the embodiment. Classes, in an overdetermined world, are themselves knots: never pure, never unanimous, constituted by the same articulations that constitute the formation, which is why the question of which class or alliance can hold a conjuncture together is an empirical question about fusion and not a deduction from positions in production. The concept thus returns agency to the world at the price of returning it uninsured, which its critics experience as cold and its practitioners as the beginning of seriousness.

## The afterlife of the term, inside and outside the tradition

Terms with careers become evidence about the needs that recruited them, and overdetermination's career after its founding decade runs in two directions at once, toward technical refinement inside the tradition and toward dilution outside it. Inside, the most consequential development was the concept's migration into the theory of articulation, where Ernesto Laclau and others took the structured whole's plurality and asked how its elements are joined into unities, political blocs, and hegemonic formations that hold for a time without expressing any essence. The comparison with Gramsci's concept, and the traffic between the two vocabularies, is mapped at [ideology hegemony discourse compared](/2012/04/01/ideology-hegemony-discourse-compared/), which is also where the term's afterlife in cultural theory is traced: Stuart Hall and the Birmingham school took from the concept a licence, rigorously used in their best work, to analyse cultural and political formations as articulations without guarantees, where outcomes are real effects of real structures and yet no last instance arrives to underwrite a prediction. A separate line of refinement ran into economics, where Stephen Resnick and Richard Wolff rebuilt a class analysis on the term itself, treating every site in a formation as an overdetermined knot of processes and using the concept to refuse, on principle, any hierarchy of determination at all. That refusal marks the fork in the concept's career: keep the last instance, and overdetermination remains a Marxist instrument of the kind this article has described; drop it, and the term becomes the name of a general anti essentialism, fruitful in other hands, but no longer the concept the founding essay defined.

### Where does the concept travel after Althusser?

It travels along three roads. Into the theory of articulation and hegemony, where it underwrites unities without essences; into cultural studies, where it disciplines the analysis of formations whose outcomes are structured but not guaranteed; and into a heterodox economics that keeps the fusion while surrendering the last instance. Outside the tradition it degrades into a synonym for multiply caused.

The dilution deserves its own honest paragraph, since a glossary that polices a word must report the word's actual beat. In general academic and journalistic usage, overdetermined now commonly means determined by more than one sufficient cause, a usage with a respectable logic of its own, borrowed partly from the philosophy of science, where an event is overdetermined when each of several causes would have sufficed alone. That usage is not the concept of this article, and the difference is not pedantry. The philosophical usage concerns sufficiency and redundancy: causes lined up in parallel, any one enough. The Althusserian concept concerns fusion and necessity in combination: contradictions knotted together, no one of them sufficient, the knot itself the cause. The two usages share a word and a Freudian ancestor, since Freud's determinants, too, are sometimes described as each pressing toward the same element, but they answer different questions, and the reader who imports the redundancy picture into the Marxist text will misunderstand both the weakest link, which is about combination rather than backup, and the last instance, which is about hierarchy rather than spare causation. The FAQ pages below return to the public usage; the body's duty is discharged by keeping the two senses visibly apart wherever the word's travels are described.

What the afterlife shows, taken whole, is that the concept identified a real vacancy. Twentieth century social thought repeatedly needed a word for structured plurality, for wholes that are neither machines nor organisms nor spirits, and it reached for this word because the neighbouring vocabularies, interaction, pluralism, complexity, each lacked one of its components: the hierarchy, the fusion, or the materialist anchor. Where the borrowing disciplines kept the components, the concept did work that is still cited. Where the components were dropped one by one, the word subsided into the synonym for complicated that the critics describe, and the history of its dilution is, in that sense, the best confirmation of the definition this glossary has defended: the concept is exactly the sum of its components, and it survives only where they are kept together.

## The borrowed term pattern, and this word's place in it

The series pattern to which this article belongs can now be named, since the reader has watched it operate for many pages. Overdetermination is one of a family of concepts that entered Marxist theory from outside it and were transformed in the entry: hegemony came from the vocabulary of alliances and the labour movement's debates about leadership, the culture industry welded a word for manufacture to a word for cultivation, and overdetermination came from the theory of dreams. In each case the borrowing explains both halves of the concept's character, what it does and why it fits awkwardly. Hegemony does the work of describing consent because its home vocabulary was about leadership among allies, and it fits awkwardly because the alliance imagery keeps suggesting negotiation where the theory wants structure. Overdetermination does the work of describing fused causation because its home discipline had already solved, for the dream, the problem of an element with several intersecting determinants, and it fits awkwardly because the psyche's knots are tied by a dreamer while a formation's knots are tied by no one, a disanalogy the concept's users must hold in mind whenever the Freudian warmth of the metaphor threatens to smuggle an author into the structure.

Recovering the borrowing is therefore not an antiquarian pleasure but the glossary's central method, applied to this term as to its cousins. A reader who knows where the word was borrowed from knows what shape to expect its explanations to take, knows which questions it was built to answer, and knows the standard by which its uses should be audited, since a borrowed instrument carries its home discipline's quality controls in its baggage. For this term the quality controls are the analyst's obligations codified above: name the determinants, show their crossing, and never let the manifest product, the event, the crisis, the text, be assigned to a single latent source. Psychoanalysis calls that discipline the interpretation of dreams. The Marxist tradition, at its best, calls it the concrete analysis of the concrete situation. The glossary's claim is that they are, structurally, the same discipline, and that the identity, once seen, is the shortest route to using the borrowed term as its lender intended and its borrower promised.

## The German word and the passage between languages

A short philological section closes the body's machinery, because the term's travels between languages have left deposits that still confuse readers. Freud's word is Überdeterminierung, built from determinieren with the prefix über, over or beyond, and the Standard Edition of his works in English settled on overdetermination, with overdetermined for the adjective. The English carries an unfortunate suggestion, that a thing might be determined too much, as if determination were a quantity with a correct dose, and the suggestion feeds the redundancy misreading: an overdetermined outcome sounds like one with causes to spare. The German is less misleading to a German ear, since the prefix there marks the layering of determinants upon an element rather than an excess in the causal budget, but no translation could fully protect the term, because the redundancy picture is available in any language once the structural claim is dropped. Althusser wrote in French, where surdétermination performs the same layering sense, and the French word's subsequent career in literary theory, where it names the crowding of codes in a text, is a cousin usage that literary scholars and Marxists periodically mistake for one another at conferences, to mutual cost.

The translation history has one more lesson, about the adjective's grammar. Overdetermined properly describes elements and situations: a dream element is overdetermined, a conjuncture is overdetermined, a contradiction is overdetermined by the whole to which it belongs. The word is therefore incomplete without its company; it demands the inventory as a matter of syntax before it demands it as a matter of method, since to say what a thing is overdetermined by is to name the other contradictions, and a sentence that cannot complete that phrase has not used the concept but only its sound. Editors who enforce the completion, who pencil by what in the margin whenever the adjective floats free, are enforcing the concept itself. Few precision instruments carry their instructions in their grammar so plainly, and the fact is worth the student's gratitude: the word tells you, every time it is used correctly, what a correct use must contain.

## The control case: Germany, where the chain held

A test earns its name when it is run against the case that should embarrass it, and the weakest-link test's control case is Germany in the years of defeat and revolution that followed the same war that broke Russia. Germany satisfied the expressive model's conditions as no other country did: the most advanced industry in Europe, the largest and best organised working class, the strongest socialist party and unions, a Marxist tradition headquartered in its own language. If rupture tracked the maturity of the central contradiction, Germany was the designated site. The chain did not break there. The November revolution of 1918 toppled the monarchy and then stopped, the councils were absorbed or dissolved, the radical risings of the following years were defeated piecemeal, and the republic that emerged was governed in coalition with the old state's judiciary, bureaucracy, and officer corps substantially intact. An economistic theory experiences this record as a mystery or a betrayal, and the literature of betrayal, which explains the outcome by the treachery of leaders, is the expressive model's characteristic product: when the essence fails to unfold, someone must have prevented it.

The structured reading asks the inventory questions instead, and the inventory differs from Russia's at exactly the joints the concept directs attention to. The German agrarian contradiction was blunted by a Junker class fused with the state and by a peasantry less land hungry than Russia's; the national contradiction, so corrosive inside the Romanov empire, ran in Germany's favour as often as against it, since defeat could be displaced onto the peace settlement and the republic that signed it; the military contradiction, though real in the fleet mutinies and the soldiers' councils, was displaced with extraordinary skill onto the terrain of the stab in the back legend, which relocated the war's meaning from the system that waged it to the civilians accused of betraying it; and the political contradiction was condensed, but condensed into the wrong vessel, since the majority socialist leadership used its authority to fuse the workers' movement to the defence of order rather than to the rupture of it. None of this says Germany lacked contradictions. It says the fusion that occurred gathered around the preservation of the formation rather than its breaking, condensation in the service of the existing structure, a possibility the concept accommodates because fusion is a process with a direction and not a synonym for revolution. The comparison is the test working as advertised: same war, same decade, two formations, and the variable that separates the outcomes is the configuration of fusions, not the maturity indices on which Germany led Europe.

The German case also disciplines the concept against its own romantic misuse, the habit of treating every accumulation of grievances as a weakest link in waiting. Accumulation is not fusion, and grievances that run in parallel, each confined to its own instance, each compensated on its own terrain, may persist for generations without condensing. The analyst's task is to find the mechanism of condensation or to report its absence, and Germany teaches that the absence is as informative as the presence: a formation's strength lies precisely in its capacity to keep its contradictions from meeting, to settle the wage question in the factory, the constitutional question in the parliament, the national question in the myth, and to prevent the queues from forming at a single door. Statesmanship, in this vocabulary, is the art of preventing fusion, and counter revolution is that art practised under pressure. The concept thereby explains stability with the same instruments it explains rupture, which is a further mark against the truism charge: a truism about complexity has nothing particular to say about why some complex societies hold for a century and others snap in a season.

## Against the ladder: the concept and the philosophy of history

Beneath the concept's technical surface sits a refusal that reaches beyond method into the philosophy of history, and a complete glossary should bring it into the open. The expressive model carries a timetable: formations succeed one another as stages of the essence's unfolding, feudalism ripening into capitalism, capitalism into its negation, each society assigned its rung and its homework. Overdetermination breaks the ladder. If every formation is a structured combination, then formations can combine elements the ladder assigns to different rungs, advanced industry with archaic land relations, republican forms with imperial content, and the combination is not a transitional embarrassment but the normal case. Trotsky had named the phenomenon combined and uneven development a generation earlier, in the opening of his history of the Russian Revolution, arguing that backward countries, under the whip of external necessity, adopt the most advanced forms and telescope stages that the pioneers climbed one by one. Althusser's concept gives that observation a general theory of the whole to live in: combination is what structures do, unevenness is how their parts keep time, and the ladder survives, if at all, as a rough ordering of modes of production rather than as a schedule any particular society must keep.

The consequences for historical writing are large enough to state plainly. If no society is contemporaneous with itself, then the historian's period labels, feudal, bourgeois, modern, name dominances rather than essences, and the interesting questions shift from the label to the articulation: which survivals persist, what work they do, which contradictions they feed. If no contradiction is pure, then the biography of a single institution, a church, a code of law, a school system, cannot be written as the institution's self development but only as its career inside a structure that kept reassigning its function. And if rupture belongs to fusion, then the calendar of revolutions stops being a puzzle about laggards and prodigies and becomes a branch of comparative analysis, in which the German and Russian cases are data of the same kind. The concept's philosophical ambition is exactly this large, which is why its critics aim high when they attack it and why its defenders treat it as the centre of a reconstruction rather than as a term of art. A glossary must record the ambition while keeping the word's definition modest, since the definition is what the reader came for; but a glossary that hid the ambition would send the reader into the literature unprepared for the scale of the claims made in the word's name.

## Marx as the concept's first practitioner

The founding essay's boldest exegetical claim was that the structured model is not an import into Marx but a description of Marx's own analytical practice, and the claim is most plausible where Marx writes history rather than theory. The Eighteenth Brumaire of Louis Bonaparte, his analysis of how a mediocre adventurer came to rule France after the revolution of 1848, proceeds by exactly the operations this article has codified: an inventory of a formation's fractions and their separate grievances, the peasantry, the finance aristocracy, the industrial bourgeoisie, the lumpenproletariat of the cities; a ranking, in which the political contradiction dominates a conjuncture whose economic lines are comparatively quiet; a fusion account, in which the fractions' separate fears condense onto a single name and a single plebiscite; and a famous opening verdict that men make their own history under "circumstances existing already, given and transmitted", a sentence that could stand as the concept's motto a century before its christening. The Class Struggles in France and the drafts on the Commune work the same way, and even the great theoretical volumes pause, in their historical chapters, to let knots of circumstance do explanatory work that no essence is permitted to do alone.

The exegetical claim should be carried with the caution the essay's critics demand. Marx also wrote passages that the expressive reading quotes with justice, programmatic summaries in which the economic structure appears as the foundation of the real edifice of society, and the mature theory of capital is not itself a conjunctural analysis. The concept's careful defenders therefore state the claim in its defensible form: the structured model systematises one of Marx's two registers, the register of the concrete analyses, and gives it precedence over the programmatic summaries when the two conflict, on the ground that a theory is better judged by its worked explanations than by its manifestos. Readers who want the general question of Marx's method, and the category of contradiction that the present term presupposes, have the series treatment of [the category of contradiction in the method](/2012/08/01/contradiction-in-marxist-method/); what matters here is the moral for users of the word. Overdetermination was offered as a reading of a practice before it was a doctrine, and the fastest way to learn it is the way its inventor claimed to have found it: by watching a master analyst take a conjuncture apart and refusing, at every step, the simpler story the essence would have told.

## A worked miniature: the dress rehearsal

The discipline codified above can be shown in miniature on a case small enough to hold in view, and the Russian year 1905 serves, since it shares a formation with 1917 and ended differently, which makes it a natural experiment in the concept's variables. The inventory first. The economic contradiction stood between a rapidly industrialising economy, financed from abroad and concentrated in great plants, and a workforce without legal unions or political rights. The agrarian contradiction stood between peasant communes hungry for the gentry's land and a land system the autocracy defended. The national contradiction ran through the empire's borderlands, from Poland to the Caucasus, each with movements of its own. The political contradiction pitted the autocracy against the liberal professions and zemstvos as much as against the workers. The military contradiction arrived with the war against Japan, which the empire was losing at ruinous expense. By the inventory standard, 1905 was rich: five major contradictions, all active, several acute.

The ranking and the fusion account explain the outcome, which was defeat for the revolution and survival for the regime. The war's end, by the peace of the autumn, removed the military contradiction at the moment of maximum strain, a withdrawal no revolutionary body controlled. The agrarian rising and the urban general strike peaked on different calendars, the countryside burning brightest when the cities were already tiring, so the condensation that 1917 would achieve, the simultaneous fusion of soldier, worker, and peasant against the state, never quite formed; the regime could face its enemies in sequence rather than in combination. The political contradiction, dominant throughout, was displaced by the October Manifesto onto constitutional terrain, where the liberal opposition could be split from the workers by the promise of a parliament, and the split held long enough for loyal troops, recalled from the finished war, to settle the cities. Read with the test, 1905 is not a simpler 1917 that failed by being early. It is a different configuration: the same inventory, a weaker fusion, a successful displacement, and a regime that kept its contradictions from meeting at one door. The comparison of the two years, identical in structure and opposite in outcome, is the kind of evidence the concept trades in, and an analyst who can perform it fluently has learned the term in the only way it can be learned, as a practice rather than a definition.

The miniature also exhibits the refutation clause in action, since the reading can state its vulnerability. If the record showed that the agrarian and urban movements had in fact peaked together and been met together, and the regime had survived anyway by a margin attributable to a single factor, say the sheer quantity of loyal troops, the fusion reading of the difference between the two years would collapse and the analyst would owe the difference to a variable the concept treats as subordinate. That the reading can imagine its own refutation does not make it true. It makes it a reading, an inhabitant of the republic of evidence, rather than an incantation, and the distinction is the one this article has been building since its first section.

## The concept's limits, stated by its friends

A glossary owes its readers the limits of its term as well as its powers, and the fairest statement of the limits comes from the concept's sympathetic critics, those who use it and report where it chafes. The first limit is asymmetry of guidance: the concept instructs the analyst where ruptures occur but offers thin guidance on what follows them, since the post revolutionary conjuncture is a new structure whose analysis must begin again, and the word that explained the break does not predict the settlement. The second limit is granularity: the inventory of contradictions can be drawn at many levels of fineness, three or thirty, and the concept supplies no rule for the correct grain, so two analysts can honour every obligation in the discipline and still produce inventories that do not touch, a latitude that weakens comparisons and that the tradition has managed by convention rather than by principle. The third limit is the standing temptation of the dominance assignment, which is the concept's most powerful and least constrained move: since dominance is read from the conjuncture's character, and the character is what the analysis is trying to establish, the risk of circularity, dominance inferred from the outcome and then invoked to explain it, is permanent, and only the refutation clause and the comparative method hold it in check.

The fourth limit is acknowledged inside the founding school itself, in the self criticism that followed the first enthusiasm. Hindess and Hirst, who had built on the concept, came to argue that its theoretical objects, modes of production and their instances, were being treated as self sufficient entities whose relations could be deduced rather than investigated, and their recantation, whatever one thinks of its destination, identified a real degeneration to which structural vocabularies are prone: the map begins to be consulted instead of the territory. The late work of Althusser himself, the manuscripts on an underground current of aleatory materialism published after his death, pushed in the opposite direction from the founding essay's rigour, toward an ontology of the encounter, of atoms swerving and taking hold, in which the emphasis falls on contingency rather than structure. Readers meeting that late vocabulary should know they are reading the concept's author revisiting its foundations, loosening the structure into the rain of encounters from which structures precipitate. The glossary takes no side in that family quarrel. It records the limits because a term whose limits are known can be trusted inside them, and the limits, stated compactly, are these: the concept explains configuration, not sequence; it requires a grain it cannot fix; its central assignment courts circularity; its maps invite idolatry; and its own author ended by asking whether the structure had been drawn too firm. None of this retires the instrument. All of it instructs the hand that holds it.

## The ideological instance, briefly, and by reference

One member of the formation's trio has been used throughout this article without its own treatment, and the omission should be acknowledged in a short section that also discharges a duty of reference. The ideological instance, the level of lived relations to the world carried by institutions such as schools, churches, parties, and media, has its own contradictions and its own dominance stories, several of which have done work above under the heading of displacement. Althusser's theory of ideology, the essay on ideological state apparatuses with its doctrine of interpellation, is a separate construction with a separate article in this series, and the glossary deliberately leaves it there, since overdetermination is the wider and older concept and the ideology essay is one of its applications rather than its synonym. What the present article needs from the ideological instance is only its structural role: it is one of the levels whose contradictions fuse and shift within the whole, neither the whole's puppet nor its author, and its relative autonomy, examined above, is the space in which ideologies do their slow work on the other instances. Readers who conflate the two constructions, the term and the ideology theory, will find the term credited with claims about subjects and recognition that belong to the later essay, and the conflation is worth a paragraph of prevention because it is among the commonest in the secondary literature's index entries.

## The neighbourhood of categories: articulation, conjuncture, dominance, determination

The concept does not live alone, and a reader who can place it among its four nearest categorical neighbours owns a map on which most of the literature in its tradition can be located. Articulation names the joining itself: the way elements, instances, grievances, and organisations are linked into a working whole whose unity is real but never natural. Overdetermination presupposes articulation, since contradictions can only modify one another inside a whole whose parts are joined, and the quality of the joins, tight or loose, institutional or improvised, is part of what an inventory must record. Conjuncture names the moment configuration, the whole's arrangement at a time, as distinct from the structure that persists across moments; overdetermination is a property of conjunctures, and the same structure can generate conjunctures with opposite characters, as the two Russian years showed. Dominance names the ranking inside the configuration, the position from which one contradiction organises the others. Determination names the assignment of that ranking and, behind it, of the structure's articulation itself, referred in the last instance to the mode of production. The four neighbours answer four questions in sequence: how is the whole joined, how is it arranged now, which part commands, and why does the command stand where it stands? Overdetermination is the name for the condition that makes all four questions necessary, because in an expressive whole none of them can be asked: the joining is identity, the arrangement is unfolding, the command is the essence's, and the assignment is the timetable's.

The map also locates the concept's rivals with precision, which is a courtesy glossaries owe to contested terms. Economism can be defined on the map as the collapse of all four questions into the fourth, with determination answering directly and the other three struck from the record. Pluralism is the refusal of the fourth question, leaving articulation, arrangement, and command to float as matters of local fact. Voluntarism is the refusal of the first, treating the whole as unjoined and the will as its own articulation. Interactionism, the neighbour examined earlier, answers the first question with collisions. A reader equipped with the map can diagnose a text in a paragraph: find which questions it asks and which it suppresses, and the diagnosis will predict the text's characteristic blindnesses, the events it will explain well and the events it will explain away. That predictive power over texts is a modest but real test of the map itself, and seminar leaders who use the word, as the persona layer below proposes, generally use it at exactly this diagnostic level, where its yield is highest and its risks are lowest.

## The whole is not closed: the chain is a world chain

A formation, in the concept's usage, is not a sealed vessel, and the weakest link metaphor itself proves the point, since a link belongs to a chain that runs through other formations. The Russian knot was tied partly abroad: foreign loans bound the autocracy's finances to Paris, the war bound its strategy to its allies and its enemy, the grain trade bound its agrarian prices to world markets, and the international socialist movement bound its parties' vocabularies to debates in Berlin and Vienna. Overdetermination therefore operates across borders as well as within them, and an inventory confined to a national frame will omit contradictions whose other end lies outside: the contradiction between an empire and its colonies, between a debtor state and its creditors, between a national industry and the world market that sets its prices. The later literatures that took the point most seriously, the theories of dependency and unequal exchange treated elsewhere in this series, can be read as attempts to draw the inventory at the scale the metaphor always implied, the scale of the chain itself.

The openness of the whole has a methodological consequence that analysts ignore at their cost. Because a formation's contradictions are partly constituted from outside, the fusion that breaks it may be assembled abroad and imported: a war begun for reasons of alliance, a credit withdrawn for reasons of a foreign bank's balance sheet, a doctrine carried home by exiles. Conjunctural analysis must therefore be prepared to follow a strand out of the country and to admit, into the ranking of a national crisis, contradictions whose dominant instance sits in another formation's structure. This is not a complication added to the concept from outside. It follows from the concept's first principle, that no contradiction operates in its purity, since a national frame is one more purity that real contradictions decline to observe. The chain image, dwelt on for a final moment, captures the whole doctrine in a single picture: a world under tension, breaking where the accumulated strains concentrate, with the location of the break determined by the whole's configuration rather than by any link's private properties. The concept is that picture, theorised.

## Objections from the other flank: the humanist and the empiricist

The deflationary objection came from the side of analytical rigour. Two further objections arrive from other directions and deserve their hearing, because a concept beset from three sides is better mapped than a concept beset from one. The humanist objection, prosecuted most famously by Thompson against the whole Althusserian enterprise, holds that the structural vocabulary abolishes experience: men and women in the concept's world are positions in a structure, their moral choices, their cultures, their self understandings reduced to effects of instances, and history becomes a process without a subject, moved by arrangements no one inhabits. Against the concept specifically, the objection says that fusion and dominance are metaphors drawn over the places where lived agency should be, and that a historiography which cannot hear a crowd's reasons has lost the crowd. The concept's answer has been given above in the agency section: structures arrange and persons act, and the conjuncture is precisely the point where the arranging meets the acting. The honest residue of the objection is a warning about register. Conjunctural analysis written without the actors' own accounts of themselves is incomplete by the concept's own standard, since the ideological instance, which includes those accounts, is one of the contradictions in the inventory, and an inventory that omits it has failed the discipline from within.

The empiricist objection is quieter and, in the archive, more persistent. It grants the concept its internal coherence and asks for its cash value in the currency of research design: what would a historian do differently on Monday morning, which sources would be consulted that were not consulted before, which comparisons run, which questions put to the documents? The objection is fair in form and answerable in substance. The Monday morning differences are the discipline's four obligations operationalised: the historian draws the inventory before narrating, ranks dominance explicitly instead of letting the narrative's emphasis rank it silently, hunts the compensation mechanisms in stable periods with the same energy given to crises, and writes the refutation clause into the research design, specifying in advance which findings would demote a contradiction from the inventory or promote a subordinate one to dominance. Archival practice changes accordingly: the sources of the subordinate instances, the parish, the regiment, the provincial ministry, are read not as colour but as evidence about strands whose fusion is the hypothesis. Whether these differences justify the concept's theoretical superstructure is a judgement the reader is now equipped to make; that they are differences, specifiable and teachable, is the empiricist objection answered on its own ground.

A third, brief objection belongs to the concept's friends in the historical profession and concerns portability. The concept was forged on revolutions, its type cases are ruptures, and its vocabulary, weakest link, fusion, break, is a vocabulary of discontinuity. Applied to the long plateaus of social life, to the centuries in which formations reproduce themselves without drama, the instrument can seem to strain, forever hunting for a snap that is not scheduled. The tradition's reply is written into the founding essay's universality claim: overdetermination governs reproduction as much as rupture, since the same fusion of contradictions that breaks a formation in one configuration holds it together in another, the knots being the very mechanism of its stability. The reply is sound as far as it goes, and the glossary records the objection anyway, because the concept's users do in practice write better about breaks than about plateaus, and a reader forewarned will supply the deficiency by attending, in any conjunctural analysis, to the compensations with at least the care given to the strains.

## The principal aspect: Mao's contribution inside the concept

The founding essay's debt to Mao's treatise on contradiction has been noted at several points, and a dedicated section can now collect it, because the debt is specific and because students routinely underestimate how much of the concept's working machinery is Chinese in design. Mao's distinctions are three. Between the principal contradiction and the secondary ones: in any complex process there are many contradictions, and one of them, whose existence and development determine or influence the others, occupies the principal place for a stage. Within each contradiction, next, he separates its principal aspect from its secondary aspect: the two poles of one contradiction, as capital and labour are poles of theirs, change places in strength as conditions change, the dominated pole becoming dominant, which is Mao's way of explaining how a thing transforms into its opposite. And between antagonistic and non antagonistic contradictions: some contradictions are fought to a settlement by rupture and others are managed within a framework that survives them. Althusser took the first distinction as the seed of dominance, generalised the second into the mobility of the whole structure, and built the third, less explicitly, into the difference between conjunctures that fuse toward rupture and conjunctures that hold.

The borrowing was also an act of theoretical politics, and the glossary records it because it explains the founding essay's reception. To cite a Chinese revolutionary's philosophical treatise as a source for the reconstruction of Marxist philosophy, in Paris in the early 1960s, was to declare that theoretical innovation had occurred in the practice of revolutions outside Europe, and that the metropolitan tradition's task was to learn from it rather than to certify it. The declaration annoyed several audiences at once, which the essay's author appears to have counted as evidence of aim. For the concept itself, the debt has one practical consequence that readers should carry into the literature: where Mao's categories are doing the work, the analysis tends toward the conjunctural and the strategic, toward rankings and timings, because those categories were designed by a practitioner for use in a war and a revolution. The concept's centre of gravity, its pull toward the brief for action described earlier, comes from this parentage as much as from Lenin's, and accounts of overdetermination that present it as a purely Parisian seminar product have mislaid half its birth certificate.

## Why breaks surprise: the concept and the event

A pattern in the reception of revolutions is so regular that a theory of rupture should explain it, and the structured model explains it elegantly: contemporaries are almost always surprised. The crowd in the capital, the ministers in the cabinet room, the foreign embassies cabling home, the revolutionaries themselves in their memoirs, record astonishment at the speed with which an apparently durable order dissolved. The expressive model cannot be surprised in principle, since its essence ripens on a schedule the theory claims to know, and its adherents are reduced to claiming they were not surprised, a claim the documentary record rarely supports. The structured model predicts the surprise. Fusion is a threshold phenomenon: compensations hold, and hold, and hold, each absorbing strain invisibly, until the configuration crosses into a region where the compensations begin to feed the crisis instead of damping it, the mutinous regiment no longer restores order but advertises its absence, the manifesto no longer splits the opposition but certifies the regime's fear. Thresholds are crossed quickly and are invisible from inside the system that crosses them, because the indicators everyone watches, the maturity of the central contradiction, the size of the parties, the figures of production, move slowly while the configuration moves at the speed of its weakest compensation. Surprise, on this account, is not a failure of the observers' intelligence. It is the signature of an overdetermined event, the phenomenology of a knot tightening past the point where its strands can be followed one by one.

The point generalises beyond revolutions, to every domain where the concept's friends have carried it. Financial crises surprise their regulators for the same structural reason, the compensations, liquidity, confidence, guarantees, each sound in isolation, fusing into their opposite when the configuration turns. Institutional collapses, the sudden delegitimation of a church, a party, a curriculum, exhibit the same signature: long stability, invisible accumulation, and a break that everyone dates precisely and nobody forecast. The glossary raises these analogues only to mark the concept's explanatory shape, not to license verdicts on any present institution, a use the run's rules forbid and the discipline of the term independently rejects, since an inventory drawn in advance is the price of the analysis and no inventory has been drawn here. The shape is the lesson: where a system's stability is produced by compensations among its contradictions, its end will be sudden, its causes will be plural and interlocked, and its historians will need a word for the interlocking. The word exists, and this article is its glossary.

## What the word is worth

The body's long circuit can be closed by pricing the word plainly, as a bookseller might, by what a reader can do with it that could not be done before. Before the word, a reader of Marxist argument had two instruments for social causation: the essence unfolding, with its timetable and its anomalies, and the list of factors, with its honesty and its shapelessness. With the word, properly disciplined, the reader has a third: a structured whole whose contradictions fuse and shift under an assigned dominance, an instrument that explains why the chain broke in the east and held in the west, why the same formation survived one year and snapped in another, why the break surprised everyone watching the wrong indicators, and why the economy's government of the whole is real without ever appearing in person. The price of the instrument is the discipline: the inventory, the ranking, the fusion account, the refutation clause, owed on every use. Readers who pay the price will find the word buys them the best available map of a certain kind of problem, the problem of wholes that are one and many at once. Readers who decline the price should decline the word with it, and write lists, which have the virtue of not pretending. The glossary's own verdict, for what a glossary's verdict is worth, is that the word earns its place in the vocabulary of anyone who reads or writes about how societies hold together and how they come apart, provided it is spent only where the payment has been made.

## The political instance and the state as a condensation

Of the formation's instances, the political is where the concept's later development ran fastest, and a glossary that stopped at the founding essay would understate the term's estate. The state, in the classical instruments, was either the executive committee of the dominant class, an instrument in hands that could be identified, or the arena in which classes contend. The structured model made a third description possible, and Nicos Poulantzas, working directly from the concept's logic, gave it its canonical form: the state as the material condensation of a relationship of forces. The phrase is overdetermination's child. A condensation, in the Freudian mechanics the concept inherited, is a point where several lines cross and are compressed; a state, in Poulantzas's development, is exactly such a point in institutional form, the place where a formation's class relations, their compromises, their displacements, and their unstable equilibria are inscribed in apparatuses, laws, and procedures that then act back on the relations they condense. The state is therefore neither a tool lying ready for a hand nor a subject with a will of its own. It is a knot with an address, and its policies are the knot's tensions made legible.

The development matters to this glossary for two reasons. It shows the concept generating a research programme beyond its birthplace, since the condensation theory of the state reorganised a subfield and set its questions for a generation: which relations are condensed in this apparatus, which fractions are inscribed in that ministry, where the displacements are routed, which compromises the budget embodies. And it shows condensation escaping the revolutionary moment to become a general category of institutional analysis, applicable to any durable arrangement in which a balance of forces has been frozen into a form. A constitution, on this line, is a condensation; so is a party system, a collective bargaining regime, a university's curriculum settlement. Each is a point where contradictions were fused into a form that then distributes their future development, and each is therefore an overdetermined object in the strict sense this article has defined: incapable of being read off from a single essence, demanding an inventory, a ranking, and an account of the fusion that froze it. The reader who grasps the state example has grasped the concept's portability, and with it the reason the term survived its founding school.

## Fusion without rupture: how formations absorb their contradictions

The vocabulary of fusion has so far been illustrated by breaks, and the bias should be corrected explicitly, since most fusions in history do not break anything. A formation absorbs its contradictions continuously, and absorption is condensation working in the conservative direction: grievances are fused into a settlement, a reform, a ritual, a party, a career structure, and thereby converted from threats to the whole into components of it. The extension of the franchise in the nineteenth century fused the political contradiction of excluded classes into the constitutional order they had been expected to storm. Social insurance fused a portion of the wage contradiction into the fiscal state. Collective bargaining fused the factory's daily antagonism into a procedure with offices and calendars. Each settlement is overdetermined in the full sense: no single cause explains its timing or its shape, each was the point where several contradictions crossed, and each altered every contradiction it touched, so that the labour movement that emerged from legalisation, for instance, was a different object from the one that had demanded it. Analysts who reserve the concept for crises miss its main employment, which is the explanation of the ordinary miracle by which formations continue.

Absorption also has its mechanics of displacement, and the pair of mechanisms together constitute a rudimentary theory of reform. A demand that cannot be met on its own terrain is displaced onto an adjacent one: the demand for control over the labour process reappears as a demand for consultation; the demand for land reappears as a demand for credit; the demand for equality reappears as a demand for representation. Displacement is not necessarily a swindle, since the adjacent terrain may deliver real goods, and the concept forbids the analyst to grade displacements by their distance from an imagined pure demand, there being no pure demands in an overdetermined world. What the concept supplies is the map of the movement: which contradiction generated the charge, onto which terrain it was shifted, whose dominance the shift preserved, and what residue remained unabsorbed, to condense elsewhere later. Reform, read this way, is the management of fusion by those who fear rupture, and the great reforming statesmen of the record, the Bismarcks and the Roosevelts, were its intuitive masters, men who could read a configuration and buy its most dangerous strand at the moment before it knotted with the rest. The concept gives their intuition a grammar, which is what concepts are for.

## In debate with structuralism: a label its author refused

No account of the term is complete without its placement in the quarrel over structuralism, the label under which the concept was marketed, attacked, and misunderstood for two decades. Althusser's school was classified, by friends and enemies alike, as structuralist Marxism, and the classification had evidence: the decentred whole, the suspicion of the subject, the kinship with the linguistics and anthropology then in fashion in Paris. The author refused the label, on grounds that go to the concept's heart: structuralism, as he read it, tended to treat structures as formal combinatories, systems of differences indifferent to their content and to history, whereas the structured whole of the founding essay is a material articulation whose instances have specific weights and whose hierarchy is assigned by production. The refusal was not vanity. It marked the difference between a structure that explains by form and a structure that explains by articulation, between a chessboard and a chain. Overdetermination belongs to the second: its contradictions are not positions in a combinatory but real tensions between real forces, and its dominance is not a rule of the game but a fact about forces, established by analysis and revisable by events.

The quarrel matters to the reader practically, because the secondary literature shelved the concept under the refused label and generations of students met it there, pre judged as a specimen of a movement whose fortunes rose and fell on other grounds. When structuralism fell from fashion, the concept was buried in the same grave, and its revival in pockets of historiography and political theory since has proceeded largely without the label, under the protection of its working parts: conjuncture, articulation, dominance, the weakest link. A glossary cannot redistribute the honours of intellectual fashion, and should not try. It can register the moral, which is that the concept is best approached as it has been approached here, as an instrument with specifiable operations and specifiable limits, whose value is settled case by case in the archive and the comparison, and not as the emblem of a school whose collective fate it was made to share.

## A reader's itinerary: the primary texts in the order that teaches the term

Glossaries in this series close their bodies with itineraries, since a term is finally learned from its texts and not from its summaries. The efficient route to this concept runs through five stations, in an order chosen for pedagogy rather than chronology. Begin with Mao's treatise on contradiction, the shortest and most operational of the sources, where the principal contradiction and the shifting of aspects can be learned in an evening and where the vocabulary arrives attached to military and political cases. Proceed to the founding essay itself, Contradiction and Overdetermination, reading it against the Maoist station and noting where the French text generalises the Chinese distinctions into a theory of the whole. Then read the first chapter of Trotsky's history of the Russian Revolution, on the peculiarities of Russia's development, which supplies the combined and uneven material in narrative form and lets the reader watch the concept's machinery describe a country. Then turn to Lenin's writings of 1917, the letters and articles in which the weakest link stops being a metaphor and becomes a sequence of decisions. End with the opening sections of Reading Capital, where structural causality is developed, reading only as far as the idea of the immanent cause is secure and leaving the rest of that difficult volume for a second pass. A reader who completes the itinerary owns the concept in its strong form, and owns also the means of auditing the secondary literature, which can thereafter be read with the founder's texts open beside it and the discipline's four questions in hand.

Two warnings belong with the itinerary, both earned from the concept's classroom history. The first is against beginning with the polemics. The literature of attack and defence, Thompson's thunder, the analytical critics' scalpels, the school's replies, is vivid and quotable, and a reader who begins there learns the quarrel's positions before learning the thing the quarrel is about, and will spend years, as many did, defending or denouncing a concept that dissolves on inspection into its summary. The second warning is against the anthologies' excerpts, which tend to print the essay's programmatic paragraphs and omit its case work, thereby manufacturing the truism version at the source: read the essay whole, including its long engagements with Lenin's texts, since the cases are not illustrations of the concept but its birthplace, and a concept read without its birthplace will always look more like a slogan than it is.

## The concept applied to its own birth

A reflexive exercise, permitted to a glossary as a closing demonstration, applies the instrument to the moment of its own manufacture. Why did a concept of fused causation, borrowed from dream theory, appear in Paris in the early 1960s and find a large audience at once? The inventory writes itself in the concept's own terms. The contradiction inside the communist movement between the thaw after the secret speech of 1956 and the frozen structures of the parties had made the old economistic orthodoxy untenable for a generation of intellectuals who remained unwilling to leave the tradition. The Sino Soviet split had broken the monopoly of a single centre and put Mao's texts, with their heterodox categories, into circulation as licensed reading. The Algerian war had just ended, discrediting the metropolitan left's pieties and schooling it in the analysis of a colonial conjuncture where the national contradiction had dominated every other. The French university was expanding, producing the student audience that would carry the vocabulary into 1968 and beyond. And the structuralist moment in the human sciences had prepared the ear for decentred wholes. No single strand explains the concept's appearance or its welcome; the strands fused, in a configuration whose dominant contradiction, for a Parisian philosopher inside the communist movement, was the one between a theory that had to change and an institution that would not change it. The exercise is offered as a demonstration and not as a contribution to the author's biography, which lives in the profile linked throughout. Its point is the concept's self consistency: an instrument that cannot be turned on its own conjuncture without embarrassment is a poorer instrument than one that survives the turning, and this one, at least in miniature, survives.

## Crisis inside the economic instance itself

A last piece of machinery deserves statement, because readers often assume the concept demotes economic crisis to one strand among many and thereby misunderstands its treatment of the economy's own troubles. In the structured model, the economic instance contains its own contradictions, between forces and relations of production, between departments of production, between capital's fractions, and an economic crisis is a condensation within that instance: the point where its internal contradictions, each developed at its own rhythm, fuse into a general breakdown of reproduction. The crisis then propagates through the structure by the articulations, striking the political instance as a fiscal and legitimacy crisis, the ideological as a collapse of the common sense that governed expectations, and the propagation is itself overdetermined, since what a crisis becomes politically depends on the configuration it lands in. The depression of the nineteen thirties condensed, in one formation, into a New Deal and a realignment; in another, into fascism; in a third, into a war economy under a dictatorship of the old type. The economic strand was comparable. The outcomes were not, and the concept's entire raison d'être is compressed into that non comparability: the economy determines, the configuration decides, and the analyst who reports only the first clause has written half a sentence.

This treatment also clarifies the concept's distance from a vulgar crisis theory in which breakdown is automatic and politics is epilogue. Nothing in the structured model is automatic. A crisis is a conjuncture, and conjunctures are read, ranked, and acted in, with outcomes that include the formation's transformation, its authoritarian hardening, its reform, and its survival in damaged form. The economic contradiction's maturity, on which the older theory waited, turns out to be one variable in the configuration, and often not the dominant one at the moment of decision, since the dominant contradiction in a crisis is frequently the political one, the question of which force can impose a settlement. Critics who charge the concept with dissolving the economy mistake its architecture: the economy built the house, owns the ground, and sets the load limits, and the concept merely insists that houses fall at their weakest joints and not, as the expressive plumber's manual promised, at the point where the central beam is thickest.

## Survivals: the past inside the present

Among the structured model's quieter gifts is a theory of survivals, of the persistence inside a formation of elements whose originating mode of production has vanished. The expressive model cannot think survivals except as lag, remnants scheduled for the dustbin, and its historians repeatedly predict their disappearance on schedule and are repeatedly embarrassed by their vigour. The structured model expects them. Because the whole is an articulation rather than an expression, an element can be detached from the structure that produced it and rearticulated into a new one, acquiring new functions and new effects while keeping its old form: a monarchy presiding over a stock exchange, a peasant commune administering a collective farm's obligations, a established church blessing an industrial order its doctrines predate by a millennium and a half. The survival is not a fossil. It is a working part of the present machine, and its archaism is often precisely its function, since forms that arrive from the past carry authority that new forms must earn. Legitimacy, in formation after formation, is supplied by the oldest available furniture.

Survivals matter to the concept's central claim because they are standing proof against purity. A contradiction in a formation full of survivals is a contradiction among elements of different ages, and its behaviour cannot be deduced from the formation's dominant mode alone, since the survivals import the logics of their birthplaces into the present conjuncture. The Russian autocracy was itself a survival machine, an early modern state form stretched over a capitalist economy and a peasant society, and a portion of 1917's explosive charge came from the sheer number of centuries forced to share one state. The analyst's inventory, accordingly, must date its items as well as name them: each contradiction arrives with its own chronology, and the fusion of contradictions is also a fusion of times, the past detonating inside the present at the joint where their rhythms grate loudest. Historical writing that dates its elements this way acquires the peculiar density readers associate with the best conjunctural analysis, the sense that every institution in the narrative is carrying more years than the narrative's own timespan, which is simply the truth about institutions, stated at last in a vocabulary that can hold it.

## Contingency inside the structure

The concept is sometimes accused, from the side of narrative history, of abolishing contingency, of replacing the open moment with a configuration so complete that the event becomes its mere registration. The accusation misreads the model's logic in an instructive way. A structure of fused contradictions determines the configuration, the strains, the rankings, the points of weakness; it does not determine the spark, the assassination, the mutiny's first shot, the ministerial blunder, the week of rain that kept the crowd at home. The concept has always distinguished, in its careful uses, between the determination of a situation and the determination of an occurrence, and the distinction is built into the weakest link image itself, since a chain under tension is a situation whose breaking still awaits a pull. What the concept claims is that the pull's consequences are configured in advance: the same spark in a different configuration is a police report, and in this one a revolution. Contingency is therefore not the structure's rival but its trigger, and a history that narrates only the triggers is as incomplete as a theory that deduces only the tensions. The two genres need one another, and the concept's mature use is as the narrative's silent partner, supplying the account of why this spark, here, now, could not be contained.

There is a reciprocal lesson for the theorists, which the tradition learned slowly and which the glossary should pass on intact. Because the spark is not deducible, the analyst's predictions must stay at the level of configurations and vulnerabilities, and any writer who uses the concept to prophesy an event has exceeded the instrument's warrant and will discredit it when the prophecy fails. The concept tells where the load concentrates and which compensations are wearing thin. It does not tell the date, and its refusal to tell the date is not coyness but the exact boundary between a theory of structures and a fortune teller's licence. Readers who respect the boundary find the concept a permanent acquisition. Readers who cross it, in either direction, by demanding dates or by dismissing configuration as astrology, are repeating the two oldest errors about the word, and the article has now corrected both often enough to let the persona layers speak to the three audiences who will use the term in their different trades.

## The concept at the scale of a single coalfield

The worked miniature earlier used a whole formation across two revolutionary years. The instrument also works at small scale, and a small case reassures readers who suspect the vocabulary only fits empires. Take a British coalfield across the nineteen twenties, the kind of district where a single industry organised the whole of life. The inventory of such a district holds more than its wage bargain. The economic contradiction between owners facing falling export prices and miners facing wage cuts was sharpened by the geological contradiction, seams thinning at different rates in different districts, so that the national industry was itself an uneven structure whose districts could not be settled by one formula. The communal contradiction ran between the pit village's dense solidarities, its chapels, clubs, and unions grown into a single fabric, and the owners' need for a flexible labour force that the fabric existed to refuse. The national political contradiction placed the coalfield inside a state committed to the gold standard and to the price discipline that the coalfield's wages were required to fund. The generational contradiction divided miners who remembered the pre war world from sons who had fought in the war and returned with different expectations of authority. When the General Strike of 1926 came, called in support of the miners, the district's behaviour, its nine months of holding out after the general movement ended, is unintelligible from the wage question alone. It was the fusion that held: the wage cut arrived fused with an assault on the communal fabric, on the district's standing in the nation, on the returned soldiers' expectations, and the fusion made the lockout a defence of a world rather than a negotiation over a rate. The owners and the state, for their part, won by the opposite art, by keeping the national fusion from forming, isolating the coalfield's knot from the railways, the docks, and the other districts whose seams and prices differed. Every element of that story is an element of the concept, at the scale of a few valleys: the inventory, the unevenness inside a single industry, the fusion that explains endurance, the prevented fusion that explains defeat. A term that works at the scale of a coalfield and at the scale of an empire, with the same operations, has the portability of a real instrument, and the reader who has followed it at both scales has earned the summaries that the closing layers now provide.

## Three rankings: war, succession, and money

Dominance has been defined, defended, and illustrated at the scale of revolutions. It can also be watched at work in the three crises that visit formations most reliably, and watching it in the small crises sharpens the eye for the great ones. Consider first a wartime conjuncture in an agrarian empire of the old type. The inventory holds the usual strands, land hunger in the villages, a thin industrial sector in the ports and capitals, subject peoples at the borders, a treasury dependent on foreign credit. In peacetime the agrarian strand dominates, since the villages feed the state and the state's coercion is calibrated to the harvest. War reassigns the ranking without consulting anyone. The military strand, a subordinate matter of garrisons and budgets in the quiet years, takes command, because the army's appetite now sets the terms for every other strand: grain is requisitioned under its priorities, credit is raised for its contracts, the border peoples are armed or courted under its necessities, and the treasury's weakness becomes the army's weakness at one remove. Analysts who keep reading the conjuncture through the peacetime ranking misread everything that follows, blaming the villages for a crisis whose seat has moved to the front. The reassignment is the concept's bread and butter, and its lesson generalises: dominance is a wartime posting, revised by events, and the analyst's first duty in any crisis is to ask whether the ranking inherited from calmer times still holds.

A succession crisis teaches a complementary lesson, about the ideological instance in command. When a ruler dies without a settled heir, or a founding leader departs a young republic, the open question is not production but legitimacy: whose name can gather the formation's obedience while the arrangement of offices is redecided. For the length of the interregnum, the ideological contradiction dominates outright, and struggles that wear economic or military dress are in substance bids to define the legitimate succession, fought through funerals, oaths, genealogies, and the control of ceremony. The economy has not retired. Its assignment stands behind the episode, since the contenders' bids are priced by the interests they can protect and the revenues they can promise, and the settlement, once reached, reimposes a ranking in which legitimacy sinks back to its customary seat. But the analyst who insists on reading the interregnum through the economic strand will mistake the ceremony for decoration, and the ceremony is the battlefield. The concept's value here is disciplinary in the plainest sense: it forces the question of which strand is commanding this month, and it forbids answering from habit.

Money supplies the third schoolroom. A currency crisis in a trading state arrives dressed as a technical event, a matter of reserves and rates, and the dressing is itself a displacement worth noticing, since the monetary strand is where several deeper contradictions have agreed to meet. The fiscal strand, a state's chronic gap between its promises and its revenues; the external strand, its dependence on creditors and markets abroad; the distributional strand, the fight over whose income the defence of the currency will sacrifice; all three condense into the exchange rate, which becomes for a season the dominant contradiction's public face, the number in the newspaper that every other actor must watch. Governments fall over that number not because it is the deepest thing in the formation but because it is the point where the knot is visible, the weakest link of that particular chain at that particular hour. Read the three schoolrooms together and the pattern of the concept stands out in its working clothes: a standing inventory, a ranking that events revise, a point of condensation where the strands become legible together, and behind the ranking an assignment that the analyst must argue, not assume. War, succession, and money are not revolutions, and that is the point of choosing them. An instrument that only explains revolutions explains the rarest thing in history. This one claims the common crises too, and the claim is testable wherever a ranking can be argued and a condensation watched.

## The term in the wild: a field guide to its uses in print

A reader who has come this far owns the definition, the machinery, and the discipline. What remains is the fieldcraft: recognising the term's uses as they occur in print, in textbooks, reviews, lectures, and polemics, and pricing each use on the spot. The taxonomy offered earlier, weak, strong, and structural readings, becomes in the field a set of specimens that can be identified by their plumage. The weak use announces itself by position: the word appears in a preface, a peroration, or a chapter's final paragraph, after the argument has closed, and it governs no claim that the surrounding pages have not already made in simpler words. Delete it and the text loses a syllable of sophistication and nothing else. The weak use is not wicked, and in undergraduate essays it often marks the exact spot where a student has sensed, correctly, that a single cause account will not do, without yet possessing the machinery to build the alternative. The teacher's pencil should honour the instinct and tax the machinery: write the inventory in the margin, and the word may stay when the inventory exists.

The strong use has the opposite plumage. It appears in the middle of arguments, attached to a configuration: a named formation, a named moment, a ranking proposed and defended, a fusion located, a rival reading answered. Its tell is the presence of the comparative case, the neighbouring formation or the earlier crisis wheeled in as a control, because the strong use knows it is making a claim about why this configuration broke or held and reaches for the evidence that could embarrass it. In reviews, the strong use is the one that survives quotation: lift the sentence into another text and it still says something definite enough to dispute. The structural use is rarer and quieter. It appears as a method rather than as a thesis, visible less in any single sentence than in the architecture of a whole work, in the decision to organise a history by conjunctures, to rank a formation's contradictions before narrating its events, to treat a state or a church or a party as a condensation of forces rather than as an actor with a biography. Works in the structural use often never print the word at all, which is why the concept's influence is chronically underestimated by index counting: its deepest victory was to become a habit of analysis in writers who owe it nothing they would think to cite.

Three traps in the wild deserve named warnings, completing the field guide. The first is the honorific use, in which the word is bestowed on an admired analysis as a compliment, the scholarly equivalent of a medal, with no inspection of whether the analysis ranked or fused anything; the honorific use is how the term's prestige is spent without its discipline being exercised, and it should be read as a reviewer's applause rather than as a classification. The second is the polemical use, in which the word is denied to an opponent's analysis as a charge of simplicity, overdetermined functioning as the negative of crude; the polemical use tells the reader about the reviewer's allegiances and nothing about the text under review. The third trap is the translation use, in which the word crosses into a neighbouring discipline, literary studies most often, carrying its Freudian passport but not its Marxist one, and names the crowding of codes and determinants in a text; that use is legitimate in its home, and the trap is only for readers who import its results back into social analysis, where a poem's overdetermination and a revolution's are cousins by metaphor rather than by mechanism. With the specimens and the traps in hand, the reader can walk the literature as a naturalist walks a wood, naming what flies past, and the word, so often a fog in other people's prose, becomes in the reader's own a tool with a known weight.

## Before the formula: the late letters on historical materialism

The last instance has a birthplace older than the concept that christened it, and the glossary's account is incomplete until the birthplace is visited. In the final decade of his life, Engels found himself correcting, in private correspondence, the doctrine that younger followers were extracting from the texts he and Marx had left: a doctrine in which the economic element, as he complained in a letter of 1890 to Joseph Bloch, was being treated as the only determining one, a reading he called nonsense when applied by its holders and a distortion he partly blamed on the founders' own polemical emphases. The letters to Bloch, to Conrad Schmidt, and to others in those years construct, piece by piece, the position the concept would later formalise. Production and reproduction in real life are the ultimately determining element in history, Engels wrote, and no sooner had he written it than the qualifications assembled: the elements of the superstructure, political forms, legal traditions, the reflexes of struggles in the minds of their participants, also exercise their influence upon the course of the historical struggles and in many cases preponderate in determining their form. History makes itself, in his summary figure, as the resultant of a parallelogram of innumerable intersecting forces, in which what each individual wills is obstructed by every other, and the outcome is something no one willed in particular.

The letters matter to this concept for three reasons, and each can be stated without inflating the documents beyond their station. First, they establish that determination-without-monopoly was not a Parisian innovation of the 1960s but a correction issued from inside the founding partnership, against the same economism, four decades before the correction acquired its technical vocabulary. The concept's pedigree therefore runs through the tradition's own self criticism, and charges that it imports an alien pluralism into Marxism must reckon with the letters' existence. Second, the letters exhibit the precise weakness the concept was built to repair. The parallelogram of forces is a magnificent image and an incomplete theory, since it describes the resultant without specifying the structure that assigns the forces their weights, and Engels's own text acknowledges the gap by returning, each time the image threatens to dissolve determination altogether, to the ultimately determining element whose mechanism it does not pause to describe. The structured whole, with dominance assigned and the assignment argued, is that mechanism supplied at last: the letters state the problem, the essay of 1962 proposes the machinery. Third, the letters model the intellectual conduct the concept requires. Engels corrects his followers without surrendering the doctrine, distinguishes the doctrine from its catechism, and locates the error partly in the founders' own rhetoric, an admission that the glossary tradition represented by this article tries to honour by keeping its term answerable to the record rather than to the reverence of its users.

A caution belongs with the visit, since the letters are quoted in the literature with a selectivity that would embarrass their author. The same correspondence that qualifies economic determination also insists, against the opposite error, that to deny the economic element's ultimacy reduces the doctrine to a meaningless phrase, and Engels is found in the letters mocking the young men who used his qualifications as a licence to study everything except production. The concept holds both edges of the letters together, which is exactly the ridge described in this article's misreading sections: determination retained, monopoly refused, mechanism supplied. Readers who meet the letters only in anthologies of the qualification should read them whole, in their cranky, pedagogical entirety, where the old man fights on both flanks at once and thereby hands his inheritors not a compromise but a standard of argument, the standard this glossary has attempted, at its smaller scale, to keep.

## The classical summary and its rereading

Every reader of this tradition meets, usually early, the short summary Marx published in 1859 as the preface to a work of political economy, in which the relations of production are called the economic structure of society, the real foundation on which arises a legal and political superstructure, and in which the mode of production of material life is said to condition the social, political, and intellectual life process in general. Generations of textbooks treated those sentences as the theory itself, a complete account in a paragraph, and economism is in large part the preface read as a building code: foundation below, edifice above, causation running upward like damp. The concept under discussion in this glossary was designed, in considerable measure, as a way of rereading that summary without either worshipping it or discarding it, and the rereading repays attention because it shows the structured model at work on the tradition's most authoritative paragraph.

The rereading proceeds by asking what the summary compresses. A preface written for a book of economics, summarising the guiding thread of studies pursued elsewhere, is a map at small scale, and small scale maps omit the articulations. Read through the concept, the foundation metaphor is retained for the one claim it states well, that the shape of the whole is set at the level of production, and refused for the claims it states badly: that the levels sit one upon another in a spatial stack, that influence travels in one direction, that the superstructure's contents can be derived from the foundation's plan. In the structured rereading, the preface's own vocabulary quietly supports the richer model, since a foundation does not script the rooms built on it, a building's upper floors have their own engineering, and the metaphor's author, in his historical writings, never once performed the derivation the textbooks attributed to him. The point of the exercise is not to acquit or convict a paragraph. It is to demonstrate the concept's posture toward canonical texts generally: neither literalism, which makes a summary do the work of a science, nor dismissal, which wastes the summary's real content, but a reconstruction in which the compressed formula is unfolded into the articulated model its author's practice already used. Students who learn the gesture on the preface can apply it across the canon, and examiners reward it because it converts a quotation, which anyone can memorise, into an analysis, which only the prepared can perform.

There is a historical irony in the preface's career that the concept helps to see. The summary was written as a guide to a research programme, an announcement of work in progress, and its hardening into a catechism occurred inside organisations that needed teachable doctrine more than they needed research programmes. The structured model's complaint against economism is therefore not only philosophical but institutional: a theory of society that fits on a card will be carried on cards, and its simplifications will be enforced by the pedagogy that transmits it. Overdetermination, whose whole content is a refusal of the card, can be read as an attempt to return the tradition from its catechism to its programme, which is one reason the concept's fortunes have tracked the fortunes of intellectual freedom inside the movements that inherited it, flourishing where inquiry was licensed and withering where the card was law.

## Reflection refused: the concept and the theory of knowledge

A concept of society implies a concept of knowledge, and overdetermination's implication deserves a section because the implication explains a quarrel that otherwise looks merely sectarian. The older Marxist theory of knowledge leaned on the metaphor of reflection: consciousness reflects being, ideas reflect material relations, and the truth of a theory is its fidelity as a mirror. The metaphor harmonises with the expressive whole, since a reflection is an essence's appearance in another medium, and the school's critique of the one was always implicitly a critique of the other. In a structured whole, knowledge cannot be a reflection, because there is no single object, self identical and unfolding, for the mirror to face. What knowledge faces is an articulation, and knowing it means reconstructing the articulation, building a model whose parts and ranks correspond to the formation's parts and ranks, with correspondence itself judged by the model's yield in explanation and comparison rather than by a resemblance no one could inspect. Structural causality and the theory of knowledge are thus one doctrine seen from two sides: the cause is immanent and must be reconstructed, and the reconstruction is the knowledge.

The practical consequence falls on how theoretical work is evaluated, and it clarifies the concept's quarrels with empiricism from within. The empiricist model of knowledge takes its objects as given, lying open to inspection, and its method as the extraction of their common features. The structural model replies that the objects of social knowledge, a mode of production, a class fraction, a conjuncture, are not given but produced, carved out by concepts from a reality that does not present its own joints labelled, and that the carving is where the science happens or fails. This is why the founding school spoke of a break between ideology and science rather than of a gradual refinement of common sense, and why the concept of overdetermination sits uneasily beside any philosophy that treats theory as summarised experience. A conjuncture does not summarise itself. It must be constructed, and the construction is corrigible, by comparison, by the archive, by the next conjuncture, but never by a direct look, because there is no direct look at a structure to be had. Readers who find this epistemology forbidding should notice its modesty in practice: it asks the analyst to argue for the inventory rather than to announce it, which is only the discipline of the earlier sections stated in a philosophical register.

## The smallest complete example: one strike, four obligations

The discipline's four obligations can be discharged on a small object, and doing so in print may teach the concept faster than another theoretical section. Take a single strike in a single industry in a single year, the sort of event a local newspaper covers in a week and forgets. The inventory first. Beyond the wage contradiction in the plant itself, the analyst lists the legal contradiction, the framework that decides whether the strike is lawful, whether picketing is protected, whether replacement labour may be hired; the municipal contradiction, between the town's dependence on the plant's payroll and its resentment of the plant's owners; the household contradiction, between the strike's demand for solidarity and the family's need for income across the dispute's duration; the market contradiction, between the employer's order book and its competitors' capacity to steal its customers during a stoppage; and the generational contradiction inside the workforce, between those who remember a previous defeat and those too young to fear its repetition. None of these is decorative. Each will do work in the outcome.

The ranking second. In one town the legal contradiction dominates, where an injunction decides the strike in its first week and the wage question is never really joined. In another the household contradiction dominates, since the strike's endurance is a function of credit at the shops and of a community's food networks, and the settlement arrives when the kitchens do. The fusion account third. A strike becomes a crisis for the whole formation in miniature when its strands condense: when the injunction, the eviction of a picket, the mayor's speech, and the empty shop shelves arrive in one week, the separate grievances fuse into a single question about who governs the town, and the dispute stops being about wages in the hearing of everyone involved, including its participants. The refutation clause fourth. The reading falls if the record shows the legal and household strands were inert, if the settlement tracks the order book alone with a precision the configuration cannot improve on, and the analyst who has written the clause knows which documents would convict the reading and can go and look at them. Four obligations, one strike, and the word overdetermined, if it is used at the end, has been paid for in full. Multiplied by the scale of a formation, the same operations compose the analysis of 1917 with which this article began, and the continuity of scale, from the picket line to the empire, is the concept's final claim on the reader's credence.

## Strong, weak, and structural readings: a taxonomy of the literature

The literature that uses the term sorts itself, on inspection, into three readings, and a glossary can save its readers years by naming them. The weak reading treats overdetermination as a caution: explanations are warned against single causes, and the word is affixed to conclusions reached by ordinary pluralist means. The weak reading is harmless and nearly empty, and it is the reading the deflationary critics chiefly attack, since it is the reading most often met. The strong reading treats the term as a thesis about causation's form: contradictions are internally related, dominance is real and assigned, fusion is the mechanism of rupture, and the last instance anchors the whole in production. The strong reading is the founding essay's, and it is falsifiable in the pattern sense developed earlier, vulnerable to configurations that refuse it. The structural reading, developed in the companion texts, treats the term as one member of a system with structural causality and the conjuncture, a complete alternative account of how social wholes exist and change, and it stands or falls with the system's reconstructions across many cases. Confusion among the three readings explains a large share of the polemical literature's cross purposes: critics refute the weak reading and announce the concept dead, defenders exhibit the strong reading and announce it vindicated, and the structural reading proceeds in the monographs, largely unbothered by the obituaries.

The taxonomy also sorts the term's friends. The historian who invokes the word over a narrative of a crisis is usually working, whatever the vocabulary, in the strong reading, and can be held to its obligations. The theorist of the state or of ideology is usually working in the structural reading, and can be held to its reconstructions. The essayist who invokes the word in a peroration is usually working in the weak reading, and can be held, gently, to nothing, which is the reviewer's way of marking the passage as decoration. A reader armed with the taxonomy can price each use as it occurs, and pricing uses is the whole of the glossary method applied to a living word: not to fix the term in amber, which no glossary can do for a concept still at work, but to give the reader a scale on which its employments can be weighed as they are met, in the seminar, in the archive, and in the press of argument where the word continues to earn and to forfeit its keep.

## Progress without a timetable

One implication of the concept reaches beyond method into the moral furniture of the tradition, and a glossary that omitted it would leave the reader unprepared for the tone of the literature the term governs. A theory with an expressive whole can promise progress: the essence ripens, the timetable holds in outline even when dates slip, and history's sufferers can be told, with a straight face, that their place in the sequence guarantees the destination. A structured whole can promise no such thing. Configurations break toward settlements that no ranking foretold in detail; fusions that might have carried a formation forward can be prevented, bought, or dispersed; and the same machinery that explains a revolution explains, with no change of instruments, the century of stagnation on either side of it. What the concept offers in place of the promise is a discipline of attention: progress, where the word is still to be used, names a property of specific configurations laboriously built and defended, not a property of time itself. Movements formed inside this theory learn to speak of their victories as constructions rather than as instalments, and of their defeats as configurations rather than as delays, and the change of grammar marks a genuine change of doctrine, perhaps the deepest the concept carries. The older consolation, that history is on a side, does not survive the weakest link, since a chain has no side and a knot has no destination. What survives is the analysis, and the obligation, renewed at each conjuncture, to read the configuration honestly and to build the fusion one favours rather than awaiting the ripeness one was promised. Readers who find that austere have understood the concept correctly; it is the austerity, as much as the machinery, that its author intended to bequeath.

The austerity has a historiographical dividend that deserves recording before the article closes its body. A tradition armed with a timetable studies its defeats as accidents, searching for the traitor, the error, the missed appointment with an essence that would otherwise have kept its schedule. A tradition armed with configurations studies its defeats as data: the German record, the long plateaus of absorbed pressure, the settlements that bought a formation another half century, each examined for the compensations that held and the fusions that were prevented, with the same instruments used on the victories. The second historiography is sadder and better. It produces fewer martyrs and more knowledge, and it sends its readers back to the present configuration, their own, with the only question the concept permits to be asked in earnest: not when the ripeness will arrive, but where the strains are gathering, which compensations are wearing thin, and what would have to fuse, or be prevented from fusing, for the configuration to turn. A glossary can ask that question of its readers but cannot answer it for them, since the inventory of any living conjuncture belongs to those who inhabit it, and the discipline, unlike the catechism, refuses to bequeath conclusions in place of the work.

## For teachers and seminar leaders

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is overdetermination?

Overdetermination is the condition, named by Althusser with a word borrowed from Freud, in which every contradiction inside a society is shaped by the other contradictions around it, so none of them ever acts alone or in a pure form. An outcome, on this account, is produced by the way the contradictions are combined, ranked, and fused at a particular moment, rather than by any single one of them working by itself. The economy still governs the whole arrangement, though it governs by assigning the ranks among the contradictions instead of scripting events directly, which is why the concept is a revision of economic determinism rather than a surrender of it.

### Q: Did Althusser take the term from Freud?

Yes, and he acknowledged the debt openly in the very essay that launched the term's social career. Freud had used the word for the way a single element of a dream is fixed by several unconscious thoughts at once, none of which explains the element by itself. Althusser needed a name for the same knotted pattern inside a society, where one crisis or one struggle is fixed by several contradictions crossing in it, and he took Freud's word rather than inventing one, because the pattern he wanted to describe, a point where independent lines of determination intersect and alter one another, was already built into the borrowed term's home meaning.

### Q: What is an expressive totality?

An expressive totality is a model of society, which Althusser associated with Hegel, in which every part of the whole expresses one inner essence, the way each feature of an age might be read as the appearance of a single spirit. Under such a model there is really only one contradiction, between the essence and its forms, and every institution, artwork, or law is that contradiction in a different costume. Explanation then means tracing each appearance back to the essence, and history follows the essence's timetable, which is why the model expects revolution where the central contradiction is most advanced, an expectation the record of the European revolutions refused to meet.

### Q: What does structured in dominance mean?

It means that a society is a whole made of several real levels, economic, political, and ideological, each with its own contradictions and its own pace, held together in a ranking where one contradiction dominates and organises the rest. The ranking is not permanent: in one period the political contradiction may command, in another the economic, and the dominant place changes hands as circumstances change. What stays fixed is the assignment behind the ranking, since it is the economy that settles, at the end of the chain, which contradiction holds the commanding position. The phrase therefore describes a unity that has a shape and a hierarchy without having a single essence unfolding through it.

### Q: Why was Russia the weakest link?

Because the pressures bearing on the imperialist system as a whole settled most destructively on the formation least able to distribute them. Inside Russia, a backward agrarian order, a thin but concentrated modern industry, oppressed nationalities at the empire's edges, an autocratic state that had lost credit with every class, and a disastrous war all pressed at once, each aggravating the others, until the combination gave way. Germany's capitalism was far more developed, which the older theory treated as the qualification for revolution, but Germany's contradictions were absorbed separately for longer. The link that snapped was the one where the strains had fused, not the one where any single strain was greatest.

### Q: What is determination in the last instance?

It is Althusser's formula for the economy's role in a structured whole: the economy does not produce events directly, but it decides, ultimately, how the whole is arranged, which of its contradictions dominates a period, and within what limits the other levels enjoy their independence. The formula is paired with his celebrated remark that this last instance's lonely hour never actually arrives, meaning the economy never appears acting alone and undisguised; it works through the arrangement it has set. The doctrine this protects is materialism, the claim that how a society organises production explains the shape of everything else, restated in a form that the historical record does not embarrass.

### Q: Is overdetermination just saying things have many causes?

It is not, though the two claims are often confused because both reject single cause explanations. Saying that an event has many causes leaves each cause separate and simply adds them, like items on a bill. Overdetermination claims something stronger and stranger: that the causes in a society are contradictions of one structure, that each is modified by its contact with the others, and that what acts is their fusion rather than their sum. The test of the difference is predictive. The many causes view says nothing about where a break will occur; the overdetermination view stakes itself on the break occurring where the contradictions have fused, which is an expectation that could turn out to be wrong.

### Q: What are condensation and displacement?

They are the two mechanisms Freud described in the formation of dreams, carried over by Althusser into the analysis of societies. Condensation is compression: several separate thoughts are packed into one dream image, and in social terms several separate grievances are packed into one crisis, one movement, or one slogan, so that they act together. Displacement is a shifting of weight: the dream's emotional charge moves from its real object to a substitute, and in social terms the dominant place among contradictions changes hands, or a conflict is fought on a neighbouring ground, as an economic crisis is fought out as a constitutional one. Together they describe how a structured whole moves.

### Q: Does overdetermination abandon materialism?

Its author insisted it does the opposite, rescuing materialism from a version of itself that events had discredited. The version abandoned is economism, the claim that the economy directly produces every political and cultural fact. What is kept is the anchor: when the analysis asks why a society's levels are arranged as they are, why one contradiction rather than another dominates, the answers terminate in the way that society organises production and extracts its surplus. The strongest critics reply that an anchor so distant governs nothing, and the dispute is a live one inside the tradition. The glossary's position is that the anchor has observable consequences, visible in the differing hierarchies of feudal and capitalist societies, and is therefore a doctrine rather than a formality.

### Q: How is the term overdetermined used outside Marxism?

In wider academic and journalistic writing the word has drifted toward meaning that an outcome had more causes than it needed, each of them sufficient on its own, so that the explanation is redundant. That sense, which also appears in philosophy of science discussions of redundant causation, is a different claim from the one in this article. Althusser's term is about combination rather than redundancy: the contradictions involved are not spare causes waiting in reserve but strands knotted together, no one of which would have produced the outcome alone. Readers meeting the word in the wild should therefore ask which sense is in play, the knot or the surplus, before deciding what has been claimed.

### Q: How does the weakest-link test grade an explanation that lists causes?

It fails it, on grounds of incompleteness rather than inaccuracy. A list establishes that several tensions were present, and presence is cheap, since every society at every moment contains tensions of many kinds. The test demands the further steps that turn presence into explanation: which of the listed contradictions dominated the moment and why, how they were fused into the single crisis instead of running in parallel, and what evidence would persuade the analyst that one of them had been inert. An explanation that supplies those steps passes and may keep the word overdetermined as its summary. One that stops at the list has described the ingredients of an explanation and mistaken the description for the dish.

### Q: What should a student separate between overdetermination and interaction?

The student should separate where the factors come from. Interaction pictures independent factors, each complete in itself before the encounter, meeting and modifying one another's effects while remaining themselves. Overdetermination denies that the factors were ever independent: a contradiction inside a society was constituted from the beginning by its place in the whole, so a wage relation under an autocracy differs in kind, not merely in circumstance, from a wage relation under a parliamentary regime. The practical test in an essay is substitution. If the argument survives with the word interaction in place of overdetermination, it was an interaction argument wearing borrowed dress, and the weaker term should be restored to it.

### Q: Why did the concept need Freud's term to do its work?

Because the Marxist vocabulary available in the early nineteen sixties had no word for the pattern the concept names. Its terms for causation ran in two channels: the mechanical, in which one thing strikes another, and the expressive, in which an essence unfolds into its appearances. Neither channel could describe a point where several lines of determination cross and alter one another, which is what the analysis of Russia required. Freud's word had that structure already built, a single element held in place by multiple determinants, and borrowing it imported the structure in one move. Coining a fresh word would have hidden the template and invited readers to treat the concept as a free invention rather than as a disciplined analogy.

### Q: How does contradiction survive inside an overdetermined whole?

It survives by being pluralised rather than dissolved. Inside the whole there is not one contradiction but several, each real, each with its own opposed poles and its own rhythm of sharpening and easing, and the whole's motion is the resultant of their uneven careers. A single contradiction also has an internal life of the same kind: its two aspects change places in strength, the subordinate aspect becoming dominant as conditions shift, a point taken from Mao's treatise. Nothing in the plurality makes contradiction gentler. It makes it unsimple, since the analyst must always ask which contradiction, at which rank, in which phase of its own development, instead of invoking contradiction as a single engine under the floor of history.

### Q: What separates a structural cause from a first cause?

A first cause stands at the head of a chain, prior to its effects, separable from them, and in principle observable acting on the first link. A structural cause has none of those properties. It is the arrangement of a whole, present only in the pattern of its effects, never appearing as one event among others, and knowable solely by reconstructing how the parts are articulated and ranked. The economy in this doctrine is a structural cause of the whole's shape, which is why waiting for it to intervene as an event is a category mistake. The distinction, taken from Spinoza's idea of an immanent cause, is what allows the concept to claim determination without claiming a starting push.
