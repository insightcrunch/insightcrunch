Contemporary capitalism is the one topic in the syllabus family where the subject moves while the candidate writes about it, and that single fact reorganises everything an examiner can reward. A question on rent, on the falling share of income going to labour, on machine systems and the work they displace, or on whether the economy has changed its fundamental character is not really asking what has happened lately. It is asking whether the candidate can reason about a moving object without pretending the object has stopped. The candidate who answers with a catalogue of happenings has described the weather. The candidate who answers with mechanisms has described the climate, and climates are what the analysis of a mode of production is about. This guide exists to teach the second kind of answer, and it begins from a blunt observation: on this material, the level at which an answer argues matters more than the quantity of material the answer contains.

![An examination desk with a blank answer booklet and a wall clock, Insight Crunch](/assets/images/blog/blog-27.webp)

The correction comes first because it is worth more than any content that follows. Candidates preparing this topic almost always prepare it backwards. They gather instances, because instances feel like knowledge and because revision material aimed at this topic is stocked with them, and then they discover in the hall that the instances have quietly expired, that the examiner reading the script knows of developments the candidate has never heard of, and that every sentence built on an instance is a small wager against the marker's wider reading. The wager is unnecessary. Nothing in the assessment of this material requires a candidate to know what happened last, and a great deal in it rewards the candidate who declines to claim such knowledge. Structural argument outscores current example on contemporary topics, not as a stylistic preference but as a consequence of how marking works: a mechanism, once stated accurately, cannot be overtaken by events between the writing of the script and the reading of it, whereas an example begins ageing the moment the ink dries.

## Contemporary capitalism across the assessment landscape

The material examined in this guide surfaces in more settings than any other part of the Marxist tradition, because the present economy is the one object that every social science discipline claims as its own territory. Economics papers carrying a heterodox or political economy component set questions on distribution, on the behaviour of profit and wages as shares, and on whether market economies generate their own instability. Sociology papers on work, employment, and stratification set questions on the changing character of jobs, on insecurity, and on the organisation of labour under new forms of management. Politics papers, and the international political economy options beside them, set questions on the power of large firms, on the relation between states and markets, and on the global organisation of production. Economic geography examines the spatial pattern of investment and decline. Business and management studies with a critical component examine the firm itself. Development studies examine what incorporation into world markets does to economies at the periphery of the system. Civil service optional papers in economics and sociology, and national eligibility tests across the social sciences, return to the same territory from their own directions.

What unites these settings is more instructive than what divides them. Each discipline dresses the material in its own vocabulary, and each marking tradition phrases its criteria in its own idiom, yet the scripts that earn the highest bands in every one of these settings share a family resemblance that a candidate can learn to reproduce. They define their categories before using them. They distinguish quantities that loose writing confuses. They state the basis on which any comparison rests. They attach contested claims to the scholars who actually made them, and they close their judgements by naming the development that would overturn the judgement. None of these habits depends on the discipline. All of them can be practised in advance, on paper, under timed conditions, until they become reflexes rather than resolutions. That is the sense in which this guide treats a scattered set of papers as a single examination problem wearing several costumes.

The candidate preparing across more than one discipline faces a particular temptation, which is to prepare the material separately for each paper, as though the sociology version and the economics version were different subjects. They are not. The underlying analysis is one analysis, and the distinctions that organise it travel intact across the boundaries between subjects. What changes from paper to paper is the angle of approach, the vocabulary of the question, and the neighbouring theories against which the Marxist account is set. A candidate who grasps this economy of preparation can revise the core once and rehearse its several applications, which is a far better use of limited weeks than assembling parallel stockpiles of instances for each paper, every stockpile expiring at its own rate.

There is a further reason the topic rewards unified preparation. Questions on the present economy are disproportionately comparative and evaluative in form. They ask whether something has changed, whether a tendency has strengthened or weakened, whether a new form is genuinely new, whether an old prediction has been borne out. Such questions cannot be answered from a stock of facts, however fresh, because their verbs demand judgement about direction and character rather than recitation of states of affairs. A candidate can memorise a shelf of instances and still have nothing to say to a question phrased around the word assess, while a candidate armed with three distinctions and a habit of stating comparison bases can construct a serious answer to a question never seen before. Examiners know this, and the questions are designed to expose the difference. The design is not a trap for the unprepared so much as an invitation to the prepared, and this guide accepts the invitation on the candidate's behalf.

### What does an examiner reward in an opening paragraph?

an opening that defines the categories the answer will use, states the distinction on which the argument turns, and signals the judgement the answer will defend. Scripts that begin by restating the question in fresher words, or by announcing the importance of the topic, spend their most-read lines on ceremony and earn nothing for the expenditure.

The opening matters out of proportion to its length because it sets the terms in which everything after it will be read. A marker who encounters precise category work in the first lines reads the rest of the script with a presumption of competence, extending the benefit of ambiguity to later sentences that a weaker opening would have left exposed. A marker who encounters a throat-clearing survey of how significant and debated the topic is reads on in a posture of mild scepticism, waiting for the answer to begin. Candidates rarely believe the asymmetry is this large until they mark practice scripts themselves, which is one reason the teacher layer of this guide recommends exactly that exercise. The presumption of competence is not a trick played on the marker. It is the natural response of a reader who has been shown, early, that the writer knows what the words mean.

Defining categories early also protects the candidate against the commonest quiet failure on this material, which is drift. A script that uses the word rent in its everyday sense on the first page and in its technical sense on the third has not made an argument; it has made two unrelated noises with a shared spelling. The marker notices, because noticing category drift is precisely what markers of this material are trained to do. An opening that fixes the senses of the three or four load-bearing terms, rent and profit, share and rate, tendency and forecast, functions as a contract the rest of the script can be held to, and the discipline of writing to a contract is much easier than the discipline of improvising consistency across forty minutes of composition.

None of this requires the opening to be long. Two or three sentences of definition, one sentence naming the governing distinction, one sentence forecasting the shape of the judgement: that is a complete opening apparatus for a question on this material, and it can be drafted, in skeleton form, during the reading time before composition begins. The candidate who has rehearsed the apparatus on practice questions will find that the openings of unfamiliar questions assemble themselves with surprising speed, because the apparatus does not depend on the question's particular content. It depends only on the topic's standing distinctions, which are few, stable, and set out in the middle sections of this guide.

## The correction this guide is built upon

Every exam guide in this series opens by correcting the error that costs its candidates the most marks, and on this material the error is a confusion of levels. Candidates treat a question about contemporary capitalism as a request for news. It is a request for analysis. The distinction between the two requests is the whole subject of this guide, and everything in it, the three distinctions, the figures instruction, the treatment of contested categories, the matrix, the revision method, is machinery for keeping a script on the analytical side of that line under the pressure of the hall.

Consider what a marker actually does with an instance-heavy script. The marker reads a claim about some development the candidate presents as significant. The marker, who reads the journals of the field as a condition of employment, knows whether the development continued, reversed, or was quietly abandoned, and knows it with a confidence the candidate cannot match. If the development reversed, the candidate's paragraph is not merely unhelpful; it is evidence against the candidate, proof that the script's knowledge is a snapshot mistaken for an understanding. If the development continued, the paragraph earns the small credit due to accurate recall and no more, because recall was never the skill under assessment. Either way the instance has failed to do the work the candidate needed it to do. The paragraph that would have earned the credit states the mechanism of which the development was an instance, and mechanisms do not reverse in the same way, because a mechanism is a claim about how a system behaves under specified conditions, not a claim about what the system happened to do on a particular occasion.

The confusion of levels also explains why this material frightens candidates more than older material does. A question on the capitalism of Marx's own century can be answered from a closed body of scholarship; the texts are fixed, the debates are mapped, and diligence is reliably rewarded. A question on the present economy feels open-ended, as though the candidate were being examined on the world itself. The feeling is an illusion produced by the revision industry, which profits from the anxiety by selling freshness. The body of analysis relevant to the question is exactly as closed as the body relevant to any other topic: a small set of categories, a small set of distinctions, a small set of named positions, and a set of tendencies whose direction and duration can be described without a single date or magnitude. Once a candidate sees that the examinable core is closed, the topic loses its terror and becomes what it should always have been, the most predictable material on the paper, because its questions recur in a handful of shapes that the matrix in this guide enumerates.

There is a discipline-level version of the same correction. Teachers of this material, pressed for time, often teach it as a survey of the present economy with Marxist commentary attached, because the survey is easier to assemble than the analysis and because students ask for it. The survey then becomes the thing revised, and the commentary, which was the actual intellectual content, is remembered as decoration. Candidates emerging from such courses know a great deal about the present and very little about the analysis, and they meet questions that examine the analysis. The remedy is not to forbid knowledge of the present, which no serious student of political economy would want, but to demote it to its proper rank: background that informs judgement, never the substance of an answer. A candidate may know as much of the present as curiosity supplies. The script should show only what the analysis does with it, and the analysis works on mechanisms.

### Why does a mechanism outscore an example in the marking room?

because the mechanism states how the system behaves under given conditions, so later developments can illustrate it but cannot refute the sentence that states it, while an example asserts what happened on one occasion and stands or falls with the marker's knowledge of what happened next, which on this material is always wider than the candidate's.

The point deserves one more turn, because candidates often hear it as advice about style when it is in fact advice about logic. An example in an argument is evidence, and evidence has to be handled: its source weighed, its typicality defended, its date acknowledged. Under examination conditions none of that handling is possible, so the example enters the script as an unexamined assertion wearing the costume of proof. A mechanism, by contrast, is a piece of reasoning. It can be followed, checked against the categories, and credited even by a marker who disagrees with the conclusion it supports, because the credit attaches to the quality of the reasoning rather than to the fortune of the facts. Marking schemes across the disciplines represented here reward reasoning explicitly and recall only incidentally. The candidate who writes mechanisms is therefore not gaming the scheme; the candidate is doing the thing the scheme describes.

It follows that the preparation for this topic should invert the usual proportions. Most revision time goes to the analysis itself: the categories and their boundaries, the three distinctions, the named positions and the claims attached to them, the standard objections and the standard replies. A smaller share goes to rehearsing the application of that analysis to question shapes. Almost none goes to the accumulation of instances, beyond the generic and honestly dated illustrations discussed later in this guide for the minority of questions that demand application by name. Candidates who find this inversion uncomfortable should test it against a single practice question, writing one answer from instances and one from mechanisms, and then reading both a week later. The instance answer will embarrass its author. The mechanism answer will read as though written by someone who understood the topic, because it was.

## The structural answer scores higher: the claim of this guide

The structural answer scores higher. That sentence is the namable claim of this guide, and it is worth pausing over its exact meaning before the machinery that supports it is unpacked. A structural answer is one built from the enduring features of the capitalist mode of production: its categories, its mechanisms, its tendencies. It discusses the present economy entirely in terms of those features. It asks, of any phenomenon the question raises, which category the phenomenon belongs to, which mechanism produces it, which tendency it expresses or resists, and what evidence about direction and duration bears on the judgement. It does not ask what the phenomenon is called in the journalism of the moment, how large it has grown, or which named enterprise embodies it. The claim of this guide is that such an answer, on contemporary topics, beats an answer built on examples, and beats it for reasons a candidate can state: a mechanism cannot expire, cannot be ambushed by the wider reading of events that the marker brings to the script, and demonstrates that the candidate has understood the analysis rather than memorised illustrations of it.

Understanding and recall are the two currencies in which scripts are paid, and they are not interchangeable. Recall is cheap to display and expensive to verify; a marker cannot tell, from a fluent paragraph of instances, whether the candidate grasps the analysis the instances were meant to illustrate or has simply transcribed a revision summary. Understanding is harder to counterfeit. It shows itself in the handling of distinctions, in the refusal to infer across category boundaries, in the calm statement of what a body of evidence does not establish. Markers are trained to look for exactly these signs because the assessment exists to certify understanding, and on most topics the signs and the content coincide comfortably. On this topic they pull apart, because the content, the present economy, can be recited by someone who understands nothing, and understood by someone who recites nothing. The structural answer is the form in which understanding becomes visible on a topic where recall masquerades as knowledge. That is the whole of its advantage, and it is decisive.

A candidate might worry that an answer without instances will strike the marker as evasive, as though the candidate were hiding an empty cupboard behind a polished door. The worry inverts the actual psychology of marking. What strikes a marker as evasive is the script that gestures at significance, that calls a development important or transformative without saying in what the transformation consists. Significance asserted is evasion. Mechanism stated is the opposite of evasion; it is the most exposed form of writing there is, because every sentence can be checked. A script that calls income from access rights a claim on value produced elsewhere has committed to a proposition with definite truth conditions, and the marker can reward the commitment whether or not the marker shares it. The candidate who writes that a sector has grown enormously and changed everything has committed to nothing checkable and will be paid accordingly.

The claim also has a defensive dimension that candidates underrate. Scripts are read by markers whose knowledge of the present economy exceeds the candidate's by an order of magnitude, not through any superiority of intellect but through the division of labour: staying abreast of the field is the marker's occupation, while the candidate divides attention among many subjects. Any contest of freshness is therefore lost before it begins, and the only rational strategy is to decline the contest. Structural writing declines it completely. A script that cites no figure, names no enterprise, and dates no development offers the marker's superior knowledge nothing to strike against. The marker is returned, by the script's own discipline, to the only ground on which the candidate can win, which is the ground of the analysis itself. Candidates sometimes describe this as playing safe. It is better described as choosing the battlefield, which is what strategy means.

There is one more consideration, and it concerns the candidate's own knowledge rather than the marker's. Understanding of a mechanism is cumulative and portable; it survives the examination and serves in the next paper, the next discipline, the next stage of study. A stock of instances is none of these things. It decays, it does not transfer, and its possession can actively obstruct understanding by giving the candidate the sensation of knowing a topic whose analysis remains unlearned. The structural discipline is thus not merely an examination tactic. It is the form that genuine study of this material naturally takes, and the examination rewards it because, on this topic at least, the examination and the study point in the same direction. Candidates who internalise the claim of this guide will find that they have not learned a technique for writing about contemporary capitalism. They have learned how to think about it, and the writing follows.

### How does a structural answer differ from a chronicle of happenings?

a structural answer subordinates every phenomenon it mentions to a category, a mechanism, or a tendency, and can say which of the three each sentence is doing, whereas a summary of events arranges happenings in sequence and lets chronology impersonate explanation, so that the reader learns what followed what and never learns what produced what.

The distinction between the two kinds of script can be made operational with a simple test that candidates can apply to their own practice answers. Take any paragraph and ask what would have to change in the world for the paragraph to become false. If the answer is a particular event, a reversal of fortune, a discontinued product, a revised statistic, then the paragraph is a summary of events wearing analytical dress, and it carries all the fragility of its kind. If the answer is a change in the structure of the economy itself, in property relations, in the organisation of production, in the categories through which income flows, then the paragraph is structural, and it will survive the journey from the examination hall to the marking room intact. Candidates who apply the test honestly to their revision notes will find that whole pages fail it, and those pages are the ones to rewrite, not to memorise.

The test also clarifies what structural writing is not. It is not abstraction for its own sake, and it is not the avoidance of the concrete. A structural answer can be rich in phenomena; it simply refuses to let any phenomenon stand unexplained by the machinery that produced it. The growth of income from access rights, the lengthening of supply chains, the spread of insecure contracts, the concentration of markets: all of these belong in structural answers, described by direction and duration, attached to mechanisms, and weighed as evidence for or against tendencies. What does not belong is the name, the number, and the date, the three ornaments with which journalism dresses a phenomenon and with which candidates, imitating journalism, dress their scripts. Strip the ornaments and the phenomenon remains, now examinable. Keep them and the phenomenon is a clipping, already yellowing.

Teachers will recognise in this a familiar pedagogical pattern. In every discipline there is a stage at which the student must stop collecting and start organising, and the students who make the transition late are the ones whose files bulge and whose grades stall. This topic merely makes the transition urgent by attaching a penalty to its postponement, since the collected material expires. Whoever has organised the analysis owns something that cannot expire. Whoever has only collected owns a depreciating asset and an examination date. Between those two positions lies the entire practical content of this guide.

## How the guide is arranged and how to use it

The sections that follow observe a deliberate order. The three distinctions come first, because they are the load-bearing members of every answer on this material and because each of them disposes, in a sentence or two, of an error that markers see weekly. The figures instruction follows, since it is the practical rule candidates most resist and most need. Contested categories and the stating of comparison bases come next, as the two skills that convert a competent script into an evaluative one. The treatment of named theorists follows, because attribution is the cheapest evaluation a candidate can buy. The contemporary answer matrix then assembles the whole apparatus into a planning instrument for the common question types, and the sections on evaluation and on the thin-answer objection address the two anxieties that survive technical preparation: how to judge, and whether judgement without instances can possibly be enough. The persona layers and the revision method close the guide, and the questions at the end collect the queries candidates actually ask about this material.

Candidates short of time should read the distinctions and the matrix and rehearse those, leaving the rest for a second pass. Candidates with a full revision period ahead should work through the guide in order, writing a practice paragraph against each section rather than merely reading it, because the skills taught here are motor skills as much as intellectual ones. The hand must learn what the mind approves. Someone who can state the labour share distinction fluently in conversation but has never written it under time pressure does not yet possess it in the form the examination purchases. Possession, for examination purposes, means production: the sentence appearing, correctly formed, in the fourth hour of a demanding day, without the support of notes. Everything in the revision method section is organised around that definition.

One boundary should be marked before the content begins. This guide teaches the writing of answers about the contemporary economy; it does not re-teach the economy itself. The analyses of concentration, of financialisation, of platform income, of global chains, of automation, and of the rest belong to the companion articles of this cluster, and the candidate who lacks the substance should acquire it there and return here for the craft. The scorecard of tendencies against the evidence is set out in [marxism and modern capitalism](/2020/11/01/marxism-and-modern-capitalism/), which this guide treats as its content companion and does not reproduce. Craft without substance is empty, and this guide has no wish to produce elegant scripts about nothing. But substance without craft is the commoner tragedy on this material, and it is the one this guide exists to prevent: the candidate who knows the analysis and loses the marks to dating, to figures, to category drift, and to judgements left undefended against the question of what would change them.

## The first distinction: rent presupposes production

The first distinction is the one that earns its keep fastest, because a single sentence built on it dissolves the most ambitious claim made about the present economy. Rent, in the vocabulary of this tradition, is a claim on surplus value produced elsewhere. The owner of an asset, a plot of land, a mineral deposit, a spectrum licence, a catalogue of recordings, a position astride a channel through which others must pass, receives income not because value was created in the receiving but because value created in production somewhere in the system is transferred to the owner as the price of access. Rent presupposes production. It is a distribution of a fund that must exist before it can be divided, and the analytical consequence is immediate: an economy in which rent-like income grows is not thereby an economy that has escaped production, transcended the law of value, or entered a new mode of production. It is an economy in which the division of the produced fund has shifted toward owners of scarce access. The candidate who can write that sentence, and defend it, has answered the strongest version of the new-era argument before the argument has finished clearing its throat.

The distinction matters because the present economy presents rent in costumes that disguise it as profit. Profit, in the strict sense, is the form surplus value takes when it appears as the return on capital advanced in production; it is bound, however loosely in any individual case, to the organisation of a labour process. Rent is bound to ownership of a condition of production or of circulation that others need and cannot reproduce at will. The two incomes arrive in the same bank accounts, mingle in the same published accounts, and are summed in the same national statistics, which is precisely why the distinction has to be made analytically rather than read off the surface of the economy. A firm may earn profit on the production it organises and rent on the access it controls, in proportions no balance sheet discloses. The candidate's task is not to perform the impossible separation for any actual firm. The task is to show that the categories are distinct, that arguments about the economy's character turn on which is growing and why, and that an income's size says nothing about its category.

### What does rent presuppose before it can be collected?

rent presupposes that surplus value has been produced somewhere in the system, since the payment to an owner of land, rights, or access is a transfer out of a fund that production created, which is why the growth of rent-like income rearranges distribution without abolishing the productive foundation it draws upon.

Hold the distinction up against the question type it serves. Examiners ask, in many phrasings, whether some novel income form shows that capitalism has changed fundamentally. The weak script answers by describing the novel form at length, marvelling at its scale, and concluding that so much novelty must amount to a new system. The strong script answers by classification. It asks what the income is a claim on. If the income is payment for access to something scarce and owned, a channel, a standard, a body of protected content, a location in a network, then the income is rent in structure whatever its novelty in dress, and its growth demonstrates a shift in distribution within the mode of production, not an exit from the mode. The strongest version of the new-mode argument, the version that claims production itself has been superseded, collapses at that point, because rent cannot supersede the production it presupposes; a parasite's growth is evidence about the host's condition, not evidence that the host has been replaced. The candidate does not need to deny that anything has changed. The candidate needs to locate the change correctly, in distribution and in the balance among fractions of the propertied class, rather than in the foundation.

There is a subtlety here that separates good scripts from excellent ones. The classical treatment of rent concerned land, and its categories were built for an asset that nobody produces. Much of the rent-like income of the present economy attaches to assets that are produced: catalogues, brands, protected designs, assembled networks of users. Candidates sometimes conclude that produced assets cannot yield rent, and the conclusion is too quick. What makes an income rent is not the asset's origin but the income's basis: payment grounded in scarcity maintained by ownership and exclusion, rather than in the value the asset's production embodied. A produced asset can be held in a way that makes access to it a toll. The analysis in the companion treatment of the [rent versus profit debate](/2021/02/15/rent-versus-profit-debate/) works through the borderline cases at length, and the candidate should know its conclusion: classify by the basis of the payment, not by the biography of the asset.

A second subtlety concerns the direction of the argument. Showing that an income is rent does not, by itself, show that the income is illegitimate, parasitic in a moral sense, or doomed. The tradition's category is analytical before it is polemical, and scripts that slide from classification into denunciation lose the thread the marker is following. What classification establishes is a set of further questions. How is the scarcity maintained, by law, by network position, by control of a standard? Who bears the transfer, producers in the sectors that pay, workers in those sectors, purchasers of the final goods? Does the growth of the rent share alter the incentives facing productive capital, and with what consequences for investment and employment? A script that moves from the distinction into these questions is doing political economy. A script that stops at the label has done taxonomy, which earns less.

Candidates should also be ready for the mirror-image error, because examiners set it as a trap for the well-drilled. Not every payment connected with access is rent, and not every profitable novelty is a toll booth. Where an enterprise organises a labour process that produces a service others buy, the income contains profit in the strict sense, and a script that calls the whole of it rent has misclassified as surely as one that labels the whole of it profit. The disciplined answer separates the strands conceptually, acknowledges that the proportions cannot be read from published accounts, and rests its judgement on the structure of the claims rather than on an invented decomposition. That acknowledgment is not weakness. It is the marker's evidence that the candidate knows what the categories can and cannot do, which on this material is the difference between the top band and the one beneath it.

## The second distinction: the labour share is not the rate of exploitation

The second distinction guards the inference that examiners across several disciplines are watching for, because it is the inference that candidates most confidently make and most completely misunderstand. The labour share is the proportion of national income paid out as compensation to employees. The rate of exploitation, in the tradition's own terms, is the ratio of surplus labour to necessary labour, of the value workers produce beyond the value of their labour power to the value of that labour power itself. The two quantities live in different category systems. The labour share is a magnitude of distribution statistics, built from the categories of national accounting. The rate of exploitation is a magnitude of the theory of value, built from categories that national accounting does not contain. A movement in the first is not, without a chain of further argument that candidates almost never supply, a measurement of the second. Writing that a falling labour share shows a rising rate of exploitation commits a category error in a single sentence, and markers in economics, sociology, and politics are all trained to circle it.

Why the error tempts is easy to see. Both quantities concern the division of the product between those who work and those who own, and a loose intuition says that if labour's slice of the cake shrinks, exploitation must have intensified. The intuition fails on several grounds at once, and a strong script can name them. The labour share moves for reasons that have nothing to do with the labour process: shifts in the sectoral composition of the economy, changes in the boundary between employment and self-employment, the treatment of housing services and of the incomes of the self-employed in the accounts, movements in the prices of what workers consume relative to the prices of what they produce. The rate of exploitation, meanwhile, depends on the length and intensity of the working day, on the productivity of labour in the industries producing workers' consumption goods, and on the real wage, a configuration no single distributional statistic summarises. Two economies can display the same labour share with very different configurations of hours, intensity, and productivity, and the same economy can show a falling share while the value of labour power rises, if productivity in wage goods advances fast enough. The quantities are related the way a shadow is related to an object: informative under known lighting, treacherous under unknown.

### Why is the labour share not the rate of exploitation?

because the share is a ratio inside national accounting, dividing compensation by income as the accounts define both, while the rate compares surplus labour with necessary labour inside the theory of value, so the first can move through composition, classification, and relative prices while the second stands still, and equating them confuses two category systems.

What, then, may a candidate legitimately do with the labour share? A great deal, provided the claims are pitched at the level the evidence supports. The share is evidence about distribution between the broad income classes as the accounts record them, and a sustained movement in one direction, persisting across decades and across many economies, is a fact about the system's behaviour that any adequate theory must accommodate. The Marxist analysis can be brought to bear on it as an explanatory framework: the candidate can ask which mechanisms in the analysis, the reserve army's pressure on wages, the productivity race that cheapens wage goods while shedding labour, the weakening of workers' organised bargaining power, the global reorganisation of production, would lead one to expect pressure on the share, and can then judge the observed direction as consistent or inconsistent with the framework. That is the correct logical shape. The evidence tests the framework's expectations; it does not measure the framework's central ratio. The full treatment of what the distributional record does and does not establish is set out in [labour share evidence](/2021/03/01/labour-share-evidence/), and candidates should know its discipline as thoroughly as its content.

The distinction also protects candidates in the opposite direction, against a dismissal they will meet in hostile questions. If the labour share stabilises or rises in some economy over some period, a question may invite the conclusion that the exploitation thesis has been refuted. The invitation should be declined with the same distinction that blocks the friendly inference. A distributional statistic cannot refute a ratio it does not measure. What such a movement does is raise a question for the framework, about which counteracting mechanisms, organised labour's strength, welfare transfers within the wage relation, sectoral shifts toward labour-intensive services, were at work, and the framework has resources for that discussion. The candidate who sees that the distinction cuts both ways, blocking the overclaim and the over-refutation with the same stroke, has understood it as a piece of logic rather than memorised it as a slogan, and markers reward the difference visibly.

There is a writing discipline attached to this distinction, and it is worth stating as a rule. Whenever a script mentions the labour share, the sentence should be audited for its verb. Verbs of measurement, shows, proves, demonstrates, measures, are forbidden in connection with exploitation. Verbs of consistency, is consistent with, sits alongside, poses a question for, are permitted. The discipline sounds fussy and is in fact the whole distinction translated into grammar. Markers read verbs. A candidate whose verbs are disciplined has announced, without a wasted sentence of methodology, that the categories are under control, and the announcement registers before the marker has consciously formulated it. Candidates should practise the audit on their own prose during revision, hunting for the forbidden verbs wherever distributional evidence appears, because the error survives drafting precisely by hiding inside otherwise careful paragraphs.

## The third distinction: a tendency is not a forecast

The third distinction governs the candidate's whole posture toward Marx's claims about the direction of the system, and mishandling it produces the two symmetrical failures that markers on this material know best. A tendency, in the usage of this tradition, is a pressure generated by the structure of the mode of production, operating through the decisions of its agents, and always liable to be checked, deflected, or temporarily reversed by counteracting influences that the analysis itself names. A forecast is a statement that some state of affairs will obtain at some time. The two belong to different logical families. A tendency can be fully operative while its outcome is postponed indefinitely by counterweights; a forecast simply fails when its date passes. Candidates who treat tendencies as forecasts therefore put themselves in an impossible position. They must either defend predictions the analysis never made, straining to explain why the prophesied event has not arrived, or join the question in dismissing the analysis for failing an examination it never sat. Both scripts waste their substance on a confusion, and the confusion is visible to the marker from the first paragraph.

The correct handling is almost embarrassingly available once the distinction is seen. The candidate states the tendency and the mechanism that generates it. The candidate then states the counteracting influences, which the tradition itself supplies in generous quantity, since its founder devoted sustained attention to the forces that check the tendencies he identified. The candidate then weighs the record: the direction in which the pressure has told, the periods and places where counterweights prevailed, and the conditions under which each side of the contest strengthens. Judgement, on this handling, is a verdict about relative strength under specified conditions, and it can be defended, qualified, and, where the evidence runs the other way, surrendered, all without embarrassment. The analysis emerges neither as prophecy vindicated nor as prophecy exploded, but as what it always claimed to be: an account of the pressures a system generates and of the terrain on which those pressures contend.

### What separates a tendency from a forecast in an answer?

a tendency names a pressure with a mechanism, which counteracting influences may check for long periods without the pressure ceasing to operate, while a forecast names an outcome by a time and simply fails when the outcome misses the appointment, so confusing them forces a script to defend or bury claims the analysis never made.

The distinction transforms the commonest question on the paper. Examiners love to ask whether Marx has been proved wrong about the present economy, and the question is a gift to the candidate who owns this distinction, because the question's phrasing contains the error the candidate is equipped to expose. Proved wrong at what? At forecasting, the analysis is a poor defendant and a poorer prosecutor's target, since its dated expectations belong to its author's journalism and correspondence rather than to its theoretical core. At identifying tendencies, the question becomes empirical and interesting: have the pressures toward concentration, toward the socialisation of production within private appropriation, toward recurrent crisis, toward the expansion of wage dependence, operated as the analysis describes, and where counterweights have prevailed, were they the counterweights the analysis itself predicts, state intervention, organised labour, the opening of new fields for accumulation? A script that re-asks the question in this form has performed the single most valuable move available on this material, which is to relocate an examination of slogans onto ground where evidence can actually be brought to bear.

There is a companion discipline for the candidate's own sentences. Tendency language must be kept clean of forecast grammar. The analysis does not say that the middle strata will vanish by a date, that crises will arrive on a schedule, that the rate of profit will stand at a level in a decade. It says that competition compels accumulation, that accumulation concentrates capital, that the pressure operates wherever the relations hold, and that its pace and its interruptions are matters for evidence. Scripts should mirror that grammar exactly: pressures, directions, conditions, counterweights. A candidate who writes that the tendency toward concentration has been checked in some sector by deliberate policy has written a stronger sentence than one who writes that concentration failed to happen, because the first sentence keeps the mechanism in view and invites the analytical question of what the checking cost and how long it can be afforded, while the second treats a structural claim as a racing tip that lost.

The distinction also disciplines the use of the tradition's own dramatic vocabulary. Words like inevitable and collapse belong to the pamphlet literature that surrounds the analysis, and candidates who import them into examination scripts inherit obligations they cannot discharge. Nothing in a tendency is inevitable in the sense of guaranteed arrival; inevitability, where the tradition uses the notion carefully, attaches to the operation of pressures while the relations persist, not to the timetable of their victory. A candidate concluding that a tendency will prevail must therefore add the conditions under which the judgement holds and the developments that would revise it, which is the defeater discipline treated later in this guide. The mirror verdict, that a tendency has been defeated, owes the same apparatus in reverse. Either way, the distinction has done its work: the script is arguing about the world in a grammar the evidence can answer.

## Writing structurally rather than episodically

The sections so far have stated the principle; this one turns it into a craft. Structural writing about the present economy has a recognisable texture, and candidates can learn to produce it deliberately. Its sentences take mechanisms as their subjects. Competition compels, accumulation concentrates, the search for relative surplus value revolutionises technique, the reserve of labour disciplines wages, ownership of access extracts rent. Its evidence arrives as direction and duration: a share pressed downward over long spans in economy after economy, a form of contract spreading from sector to sector, a concentration of markets proceeding through successive waves of merger. Its comparisons announce their bases before drawing their conclusions. Its judgements carry their defeaters on their backs. A candidate reading a paragraph of such writing aloud should hear no sentence that a change in the news could falsify, and should hear, in every sentence, the machinery of the analysis at work.

The contrary texture is equally recognisable, and candidates should learn to hear it in their own drafts with distaste. Its sentences take events as their subjects. A firm launched, a market moved, a government announced, a technology arrived. Its evidence arrives as magnitude and instance: figures quoted without sources, cases named without typicality, superlatives doing the work of argument. Its comparisons float free of any stated basis, so that one decade is set against another with no word about what changed in measurement, in composition, in the boundary of the thing compared. Its judgements are moods: optimism or pessimism about the system's future, asserted in the cadence of a peroration. Markers do not hate such writing; they simply cannot reward it beyond the band reserved for competent description, because description is all it is.

### How do you write about contemporary capitalism without dating your answer?

argue from mechanisms rather than examples. A mechanism cannot be out of date, cannot be contradicted by more recent developments the examiner knows about, and demonstrates understanding rather than recall. Describe direction and duration of trends and cite no figures.

That answer, which the candidate should be able to reproduce from memory, compresses the craft into four instructions. Argue from mechanisms. Describe direction and duration. Cite no figures. Let the examiner's knowledge of events work for the script rather than against it, by confining events to the role of unnamed illustration where the question forces illustration at all. Each instruction expands into habits. Arguing from mechanisms means that every paragraph of substance can complete the sentence: this happens because the relations of the system reward, compel, or permit it, in the following way. Describing direction and duration means that every trend enters the script with its temporal shape attached: spreading, deepening, persisting across a long period, interrupted and resumed, confined to a sector or general across the economy. Citing no figures is the subject of the next section. Confining events means that where an illustration is unavoidable, it is drawn generically, an unnamed platform, a large retailer, a logistics network, so that the illustration's argumentative content survives the fate of any particular firm.

The craft has a planning corollary that candidates should adopt literally. When sketching an answer in the reading minutes, the plan should consist of mechanisms and distinctions, not of material. A plan that lists cases will produce a script that recites cases; a plan that lists the distinction to open with, the mechanism to develop, the counterweight to weigh, and the defeater to close with will produce a structural script almost regardless of the candidate's nerves. This is why the matrix later in this guide is organised by distinction and evaluation rather than by content: it trains the planning hand to reach for structure first. Candidates who practise with the matrix on past questions will find their plans changing shape within a few exercises, and the scripts follow the plans as reliably as water follows a channel.

One more habit completes the craft, and it is the habit of the second look. Structural scripts are written in the same hurried hand as any other, and current material creeps into them at the joints: a date slipped into a subordinate clause, a named enterprise doing duty for a category, a figure remembered from a textbook and deployed for ballast. The candidate who reserves the final minutes of an answer for a deliberate scan, hunting those three intruders and striking or generalising them, protects the whole investment of the preceding pages. The scan costs little, because the intruders are easy to see once named as a class, and it should be as fixed a part of the routine as the checking of the question's verb. Examiners cannot reward a discipline they cannot see, but they can and do penalise its lapses wherever a lapse hands them a fact they know to be stale.

## The figures instruction: cite no figure

This guide now gives the instruction that candidates resist most and need most, and it gives it directly, as an instruction, because hedging it would waste its force. Cite no figure for growth, for inequality, for employment, for market share, or for the labour share. Not a cautious figure, not a rounded figure, not a figure attributed to a body the candidate half remembers. None. The instruction rests on three grounds, any one of which would suffice. Figures move, and the figure a candidate memorised from a textbook or a revision summary describes a state of the economy that has already passed, so the script dates itself by its own hand. Figures cited without a source are penalised in every marking tradition represented here, because the marker cannot verify them and has been trained to suspect them. And figures earn nothing that a structural statement would not earn more safely, since the argumentative work a figure pretends to do, establishing that a trend is real and substantial, is done as well by direction and duration described accurately, and done without exposing the script to contradiction.

Consider the candidate's actual position with respect to any statistic. The number was produced by an agency, for a purpose, under definitions the candidate has not examined, for a period that has ended, and it has passed through at least two intermediaries, a textbook and a memory, before reaching the script. At each passage it shed its qualifications: its treatment of the self-employed, its handling of imputed income, its revision status, its margin of uncertainty. What arrives in the examination hall is the husk of a measurement, and the candidate proposes to rest an argument on it in front of a reader who may well have taught from the agency's own methodological notes. The recklessness of the proposal is disguised by the figure's air of solidity. Digits look like knowledge. On this material they are the opposite: they are the point at which the script's knowledge is thinnest, dressed in the costume of its greatest precision.

### Why does an unsourced figure cost more than it earns?

because the marker cannot verify it, knows the series it came from will have moved, and reads the willingness to quote it as evidence that the candidate cannot tell a measurement from an argument, while the directional statement the figure was decorating would have earned the same credit with none of the exposure.

The instruction has a positive half, and candidates should learn it as a replacement rather than a prohibition. Where a figure would have gone, put direction, duration, and mechanism. Direction: the share has fallen, concentration has risen, the insecure form has spread. Duration: across decades, through successive cycles, persistently rather than episodically. Mechanism: under pressure from the reserve of labour, through the cheapening of wage goods, by the deliberate reorganisation of production across borders. A sentence built from those three elements tells the marker everything a figure could tell, and tells it in a form that remains true as the series are revised, because direction and duration are robust to revision in a way that point values never are. Statistical agencies revise magnitudes constantly and directions rarely. The candidate who writes directions has, without knowing any agency's revision calendar, chosen the part of the evidence built to last.

There are two apparent exceptions, and both dissolve on inspection. The first is the question that seems to demand data, phrased around the extent or scale of some development. Extent questions are answered by characterising the development's reach: narrow in reach or economy-wide, shallow or deep in its reorganisation of the labour process, long established or still spreading. Scale, in an examination answer, is a quality to be argued, not a quantity to be quoted. The second apparent exception is the candidate's fear that a script without a single number will look unworldly. The fear is addressed at length in the section on the thin-answer objection, but its short answer belongs here: markers read for argument, and a number is not an argument. No marker in any discipline represented in this guide has ever raised a band because a script contained a statistic, while many have lowered one because the statistic was wrong, stale, or irrelevant to the sentence it adorned.

The instruction extends, candidates should note, to figures smuggled in verbal form. A script that avoids digits but writes that unemployment doubled, that a handful of firms control most of a market, that wages have stagnated for a generation of workers in a named economy, has cited figures in prose and inherits all their liabilities. The discipline is about claims of magnitude, not about typography. Directional language has its own precision, and part of the craft is learning its gradations: edged downward and fell sharply are different claims, and the candidate should choose the one the evidence, as the literature reports it, supports. Where even direction is contested in the literature, the contest itself is the examinable fact, and the script should report the disagreement and name its parties rather than pick a side by instinct. That handling converts a hazard into an evaluation point, which is the recurring alchemy of this guide: every place where the material resists a simple statement is a place where the marker is waiting to reward the candidate who notices the resistance.

## Contested categories: name the dispute, never borrow the label

The present economy arrives in the candidate's reading already labelled, and the labels are the second great hazard of this material. Platform capitalism, technofeudalism, cognitive capitalism, surveillance capitalism, and their kin circulate as though they were established periods or modes, with agreed boundaries and settled contents. They are nothing of the kind. Each is a contested proposal, advanced by particular scholars, carrying a particular thesis about what is essential in the present economy, and disputed by other scholars who locate the essence elsewhere. The candidate who uses such a label as established, who writes that under technofeudalism firms behave thus, has made two errors in one gesture: borrowed a conclusion without arguing it, and signalled to the marker that the borrowing was unconscious. The candidate who names the label as contested, states the dispute it sits inside, and declares the position the answer will take has converted the same material into a demonstration of command. On this material, the handling of labels is not a preliminary to the argument. It is a large part of the argument.

The dispute beneath the labels is genuine and worth stating in its strongest form, because a script that can state it has understood the contemporary debate better than most of the journalism that reports it. One family of positions holds that something in the present economy breaks with the capitalist mode of production itself: that income now flows from ownership of digital territory in a manner closer to feudal dues than to profit, or that production has become so centred on knowledge and communication that the labour theory of value loses its grip. The opposing family holds that the appearances deceive: that the novel incomes resolve, on inspection, into profit and rent of familiar kinds, that the labour process remains organised by capital for the extraction of surplus value, and that the novelty lies in technique and in distribution rather than in the mode. A candidate does not have to settle this dispute to score on it. The candidate has to show that the dispute exists, that the labels are its flags, and that the answer's own usage is chosen rather than inherited.

### How should a candidate treat a category that scholars dispute?

by naming the category as a proposal rather than a period, attaching it to the scholars who advance it, stating in one sentence what it claims is new and in another what its critics say is continuous, and then declaring the usage the answer will follow, so the marker sees a position taken instead of a label borrowed.

The practical technique is a formula, and candidates should drill it until it costs no time. The formula runs: label, proposer, thesis, objection, usage. In the hall it compresses into two or three sentences. A term such as platform capitalism is introduced with its scholarly provenance, glossed by the claim it encodes, that the organising form of the present economy is the privately owned digital intermediary extracting rent from the transactions it hosts, set against the objection that the same intermediaries organise production and logistics in ways continuous with earlier capital, and then either adopted with the gloss attached or set aside in favour of the underlying description. Two or three sentences of this kind, placed at the first use of any contested label, repay themselves across the whole script, because every later use of the term is then read as deliberate. The candidate who skips the formula and uses the label bare has spent the same words on description and bought no credit at all.

A related discipline governs labels the question itself supplies. Examiners sometimes build a contested term into the question's wording, and candidates occasionally suppose that the wording licenses the term's established use. It does not. A question that asks whether technofeudalism describes the present economy is asking for the dispute, and a question that asks candidates to discuss the platform economy expects the term to be handled as the literature handles it, which is argumentatively. The safe rule is symmetrical: whoever introduces the label, the script owes the dispute one clear statement. Where the question's own phrasing takes a side, the script may note the phrasing and proceed to examine it, which is itself an evaluative act of the kind markers prize. The candidate is never obliged to accept the question's categories as the price of answering it, and on this material the refusal, politely executed, is often where the top band begins.

Candidates should also keep a sense of proportion about the labels' shelf life. Fashion in terminology moves faster than fashion in anything else the candidate studies, and a label that dominates a textbook's chapter headings may be a period piece by the time the candidate sits the paper. This is the dating hazard in its purest form, since a script built around a fashionable label dates not by its facts but by its vocabulary. The structural defence is the same as elsewhere: the script's load-bearing terms should be the tradition's own durable categories, value, surplus value, rent, profit, accumulation, concentration, with the fashionable labels admitted only as exhibits in the dispute about how those categories apply. Exhibits can age without bringing the building down. Foundations cannot be swapped at that speed, and the candidate who builds on the durable categories has insured the script against the vocabulary market as thoroughly as against the statistical one.

## Stating the basis of a comparison

Comparative questions dominate this material. Candidates are asked whether the present economy differs from the economy of an earlier period, whether one economy's arrangements are more or less favourable to labour than another's, whether a tendency has strengthened across the decades that separate two states of the system. Such questions cannot be answered well without a stated basis of comparison, and they are answered badly, in script after script, precisely because the basis goes unstated. An unstated basis is not a neutral omission. It conceals the choices on which the comparison's validity depends: what is being compared, across what stretch of time, under whose definitions, and with what correction for shifts in the composition of the thing measured. The marker who cannot see the basis cannot credit the conclusion, and the candidate who has not chosen a basis does not, strictly, know what the conclusion asserts. Stating the basis is therefore not pedantry. It is the act that turns two descriptions placed side by side into a comparison.

The elements of a basis can be enumerated as habits. The candidate names the quantity or relation under comparison, in the categories the answer has already defined. The candidate fixes the span, by duration and by the character of the periods at each end, a boom against a slump being a different comparison from a decade against a decade. The candidate acknowledges composition: an economy whose employment has shifted between sectors will show movements in its averages that reflect the shift rather than any change within sectors, and a comparison that ignores composition attributes to the system's dynamics what belongs to its arithmetic. The candidate acknowledges definition: the boundary of employment, of unemployment, of the firm, of the sector, is drawn by conventions that differ between places and are revised between periods, and an honest comparison says so once, clearly, and proceeds. None of this requires statistical apparatus. It requires four or five sentences of methodological candour, placed where the comparison begins, and those sentences are among the highest-yielding in the entire repertoire this guide teaches.

### What makes a comparison between periods fair?

a comparison is fair when the script states what quantity is compared, over what span, under which definitions, and with what allowance for changes in composition, because each of those choices can reverse the apparent result, and a marker who cannot see the choices cannot credit the conclusion drawn from them.

The discipline pays an unexpected dividend, which is that it frequently dissolves the question's apparent difficulty. Many comparative questions are hard only because the honest answer is that the comparison runs differently on different bases: distribution looks one way measured by income shares and another way measured by the security of employment; concentration looks one way within national markets and another way across the world market; the working day looks one way in its contractual length and another in its intensity and its unpaid extensions into the hours around the shift. A candidate who states bases can give that differentiated answer, and the differentiated answer is nearly always the true one, and markers know it. The script that forces a single verdict across all bases reads as naive beside it. Basis-stating is thus not a defensive chore bolted onto an answer. It is a method for finding the answer the question was designed to elicit, since examiners construct comparative questions around exactly these reversals, and the candidate who walks into the construction with bases unstated has declined the question's actual content.

There is a cross-disciplinary dividend as well. The geographer's comparison between regions, the sociologist's comparison between cohorts of workers, the development scholar's comparison between economies at different levels of incorporation into the world market: all of them stand or fall by the same four choices, and the habit of stating them transfers without modification. Candidates preparing this material for several papers should notice that they are not learning several techniques but one technique with several costumes, and should practise it in each costume once, deliberately, so that the hand recognises the move under unfamiliar dress. The candidate who has stated a basis for a comparison of income shares in an economics answer will state one, unprompted, for a comparison of regional decline in a geography answer, and the second marker will reward a habit the first marker's paper trained. Few investments in this syllabus family pay interest in so many currencies.

A final warning belongs to this section, because the error is common and quiet. Candidates sometimes state a basis and then abandon it, drawing conclusions later in the script from comparisons built on different spans or definitions than the ones declared. The marker notices the slippage, because the declared basis has sensitised the marker to exactly that dimension of the writing. A stated basis is a commitment, and the script must be audited against it in the final minutes with the same care given to the hunt for dates and figures. Where the argument genuinely needs a second basis, the script should declare the change and justify it, which converts a lapse into a further display of control. The principle generalises across this whole guide: every discipline taught here is a promise the script makes to its reader, and the marks live in the keeping of the promises.

## Naming theorists: attribution as evaluation

The cheapest evaluation available to a candidate on this material is attribution, and it is chronically underused. A claim floating free in a script, the economy has become feudal, knowledge has displaced labour as the source of value, the platform is a new kind of firm, is an assertion, and assertions earn the credit due to opinions. The same claim attached to a named scholar and stated as that scholar's specific thesis becomes an object of analysis: it can be summarised accurately, located in a dispute, supported, qualified, and opposed. Attribution converts assertion into evaluated analysis by giving the marker something with edges. The rule for candidates is therefore simple to state and demanding to practise. Never let a position enter the script anonymously. Every significant claim about the present economy should arrive carrying the name of a scholar who holds it and a clause stating precisely what the scholar claims, no more and no less.

The material is kind to this discipline, because the contemporary debate has a small number of highly citable positions, and a candidate needs command of perhaps half a dozen. The founder's own positions come first, since questions about relevance are questions about them: the tendency toward concentration and centralisation of capital, the analysis of rent as a distribution of surplus value, the account of machinery as a weapon in the contest over the working day and as a means of producing relative surplus value, credit and the joint-stock form, where production is socialised inside private appropriation. Around those stand the positions of the tradition's later developers and its critics: scholars who argue that monopoly and finance have transformed the system's dynamics, scholars who argue that income from knowledge and access marks a new formation, scholars within economics who document the distributional record and dispute its causes, scholars who insist that the categories themselves need revision for an economy of services and intangibles. The candidate does not need the whole library. The candidate needs, for each position used, a name, a thesis stated in a sentence, and a sense of who disagrees and why.

### Why does naming a theorist strengthen an evaluation?

because a named claim can be weighed, while an anonymous one can only be echoed: attaching a position to the scholar who holds it, and stating the thesis exactly, gives the marker an object with edges, lets the script locate the claim inside a dispute, and turns the candidate from a repeater of conclusions into a judge of arguments.

Precision in attribution matters as much as attribution itself. A script that credits a scholar with a vaguer or stronger claim than the scholar made has performed the gesture of scholarship without its substance, and markers who know the literature, which on this material is most of them, discount the whole performance. The discipline is to state each thesis at its actual strength. A scholar who argues that rent-like income has grown in importance has not argued that production has ceased to matter, unless the scholar says so, and some do say so, which is why the distinction between the positions is itself examinable content. Candidates should prepare their attributions as they prepare their distinctions: as fixed, rehearsed sentences, each giving a name, a thesis, and the thesis's limit. Six such sentences, accurately held, will carry a candidate through any question this material generates, because the questions are built from the same small stock of positions, recombined.

Attribution has a second function that candidates rarely notice until a teacher points it out. It disciplines the candidate's own judgement by forcing the judgement to specify its target. A conclusion that Marx is outdated means nothing until the script says which claim, held by the founder or by which developer, the evidence has overtaken. A conclusion that the analysis remains indispensable means nothing until the script names the mechanism, and its formulator, that the present economy continues to exhibit. Conclusions in the top band are always conclusions about named claims, and they are graded accordingly, as acts of judgement performed on identified objects. The candidate who has attributed carefully throughout the script arrives at the conclusion with the work already done: the objects are on the table, the weighing has been shown, and the verdict can be pronounced in two sentences that the marker has been prepared to accept. The candidate who has asserted throughout arrives at the conclusion with nothing to judge but the script's own mood, and the mood, however fervent, is not a verdict.

One caution completes the section. Attribution is not name-dropping, and the difference is visible at a glance. A dropped name decorates a sentence the name does no work in; an attribution loads a claim onto a scholar and then works the claim. Scripts that sprinkle names across paragraphs without attaching theses read as anxious, and markers respond to the anxiety rather than to the names. The ration is therefore strict: fewer names, each carrying a thesis, each thesis used. A candidate who deploys four positions with precision will outscore a candidate who mentions a dozen, on this material, every time, because the marking criteria reward the evaluation of positions and mention is not evaluation. The revision method later in this guide builds the ration into the candidate's kit explicitly, with a card for each position: name, thesis in a sentence, limit in a clause, and the opposing name. Memorise the cards and the attribution discipline becomes a reflex the hall cannot shake.

## The contemporary answer matrix

Everything taught so far assembles into a single planning instrument, the contemporary answer matrix, which candidates should be able to reconstruct from memory and should practise reconstructing until the reconstruction costs less than a minute. The matrix has a row for each of the common question types on this material and four columns: the distinction to lead with, the evidence to reference, the evaluation that lifts the band, and the trap the question sets. Its purpose is not to supply content. Its purpose is to fix the order of operations in the candidate's mind, so that in the hall the first moves of any answer on this material are the right moves, made before nerves or the clock can interfere. Whoever has internalised the matrix does not face a blank page and a topic; the candidate faces a row, and the row tells the hand where to begin.

| Question type | Distinction to lead with | Evidence to reference | Evaluation that lifts the band | The trap |
| Assess Marx's relevance to contemporary capitalism | Tendency, never forecast | Direction and duration of the standing tendencies, weighed against their named counterweights | Judge named claims one by one, some borne out and some overtaken, rather than the analysis as a single block | Defending or dismissing predictions the theoretical core never made |
| Evaluate the claim that capitalism has changed fundamentally | Rent presupposes production | Rent-like income swelling alongside novel organisational forms, described structurally | Locate the change in distribution and technique, and state what would count as a change in the mode itself | Treating a contested label for the new formation as an established period |
| Analyse the causes of the falling labour share | Share against rate: two category systems | The share's direction across decades and economies, with its composition effects declared | Weigh mechanisms against each other and state the comparison basis before concluding | Reading the share as a direct measurement of exploitation |
| Discuss whether digital platforms represent a new form of exploitation | Rent presupposes production | The structure of the income, toll on access against organisation of a labour process | Separate the strands of the income conceptually and classify each by the basis of its payment | Calling the whole of a platform's income either rent or profit without separating the strands |
| Assess the impact of automation on work | Tendency against forecast | The displacement and reinstatement of tasks, described by direction and sector rather than by counts | Judge the mechanism, machinery as a means of relative surplus value, against the record of its operation | Citing figures for jobs destroyed or created, or forecasting a date for the end of work |

Read the matrix column by column and its logic shows itself. The first column is the guide's three distinctions, recycled: nearly every question on this material is governed by one of them, and the candidate's opening should say which, because that announcement organises everything the marker reads afterward. The second column is uniformly structural: directions, durations, structures of income, mechanisms at work, never a magnitude and never a named case. The third column is where the marks concentrate, since evaluation is the skill the higher bands exist to certify, and in every row the evaluation consists of a weighing performed on named objects rather than a sentiment expressed about the topic. The fourth column names the specific way the question is designed to catch the unwary, and in every row the trap is a version of the two costliest errors: dating the answer with figures or instances, or borrowing a contested category as though it were settled.

### In what sequence does the matrix arrange the movements of an answer?

the matrix fixes the sequence before composition begins: open with the governing distinction, develop the mechanism the question targets, reference evidence by direction and duration, weigh the named positions, and close with a judgement carrying its defeater, so the candidate spends the hour executing a plan instead of searching for one.

Candidates should practise the matrix actively rather than admire it. The exercise is to take a question of one of the five types, from a past paper or constructed by a teacher, and to spend five minutes producing the row entries in prose: the distinction stated in a sentence, the mechanism in two, the evidence characterised, the evaluation sketched, the trap named. Five minutes of planning against the matrix will outperform twenty minutes of unplanned writing on this material, because the failures the matrix prevents are planning failures, committed in the first minutes and paid for across the whole script. The exercise also exposes gaps in the candidate's kit with diagnostic precision. A row that cannot be filled shows exactly which distinction is unlearned or which position's attribution is missing, and revision can then be aimed rather than general. Teachers use the matrix the same way, as a marking lens: a script's distance from its row is a serviceable first estimate of its band.

Two rows deserve a word of expansion, since they draw candidates into characteristic trouble. The relevance row tempts candidates into a survey of everything the founder ever claimed, graded claim by claim in a breathless inventory. Resist it. The row's evaluation instructs the candidate to select: two or three named claims, each weighed properly, outweigh a dozen claims waved past, because the weighing is the skill under assessment and the inventory is its evasion. The automation row tempts candidates toward the literature of displacement counts and occupational prophecy, the most perishable material in the entire field. The row redirects attention to the mechanism the founder actually analysed, machinery introduced under the pressure to cheapen commodities and to discipline labour, producing relative surplus value, with displacement and reinstatement as its twin effects, a treatment developed in [automation ai and marxism](/2020/12/15/automation-ai-and-marxism/). A candidate who argues the mechanism can absorb whatever factual weather arrives with the marking. A candidate who argues the counts is hostage to the next revision of somebody else's model.

The matrix is deliberately incomplete, and its incompleteness is part of its design. Questions will arrive that match no row exactly, hybrids of the types, or questions angled from a discipline's particular concerns. The candidate's response is to decompose the hybrid into the rows it combines and to take the governing distinction from the row whose trap the question most clearly sets. What the matrix guarantees is not coverage but orientation: the candidate always knows which distinction is nearest, which error is likeliest, and which closing move is owed. Orientation, under examination pressure, is worth more than coverage, because the covered answer to the wrong question earns nothing while the oriented answer to the actual question earns from its first paragraph.

## Evaluation and the defeater: the candidate instruction

Evaluation is the skill that separates the bands on this material, and it has a final component that this guide teaches as a direct instruction to candidates, in the imperative, because it is the component most often omitted and the cheapest to add. After any judgement about contemporary conditions, state what development would change the judgement. That is the whole instruction. A candidate who concludes that a tendency remains operative should write the sentence that names the observation which would persuade the candidate otherwise: a sustained reversal of the direction, maintained across a full cycle and across the major economies, under conditions where the named counterweights were not strengthened. Where the verdict is that a category has been overtaken, the script should write the sentence naming what would restore it. The sentence costs a minute. Its effect on the marker is out of all proportion, because it demonstrates that the judgement is a judgement, held for reasons, responsive to evidence, rather than a loyalty or a mood dressed as a conclusion.

### What should follow any judgement about present conditions?

a statement of the development that would change the judgement, framed concretely enough that a reader could recognise the development if it occurred, because a verdict that names its own defeater shows the marker a mind weighing evidence rather than defending a position, and on a subject defined by movement that responsiveness is the quality the highest bands describe.

The instruction is unusually easy to obey on this material, and unusually effective, for a reason candidates should understand so that they obey it intelligently rather than mechanically. The subject of these questions is explicitly a moving object. The economy under discussion changes while the literature about it accumulates, and every serious position in the debate is, whether its holder admits it or not, a bet on the direction of that movement. A defeater sentence simply makes the bet's terms explicit. It tells the marker the conditions under which the candidate's side loses, which is the one piece of information that distinguishes a weighed conclusion from a recited one. Markers, reading their two-hundredth script on the same question, develop an ear for the difference the way musicians develop an ear for intonation, and the defeater sentence is the note they are listening for. Candidates who doubt this should consider the contrary case: a conclusion that nothing could change is, in an examination on a changing economy, a confession that the candidate has stopped thinking at the exact point where thinking was being assessed.

The defeater must be genuine to earn its credit, and genuineness has tests. A defeater that names an impossible development, the abolition of private property in the means of production by lunchtime, is a joke at the candidate's own expense. A defeater that names a trivial development, if the facts were different, is an evasion. The genuine defeater specifies a development within the field of the question: a direction reversing and holding, a mechanism ceasing to operate, a category's defining condition disappearing, a counterweight proving permanently stronger than the tendency it checks. Framing it takes the same knowledge as the judgement itself, which is why markers trust it as evidence: it cannot be faked by a candidate who does not understand the material, and it cannot be omitted by a candidate who does. The candidate should also place it correctly, immediately after the judgement it governs, not hoarded in a final paragraph where it reads as an afterthought, and should give each major judgement in a long answer its own, since a single defeater stretched across three verdicts covers none of them.

Evaluation more broadly, beyond the defeater, follows the pattern this guide has built piece by piece. Weigh named claims, not schools in the mass. Concede the strongest point of the side the answer opposes, and concede it at its full strength, since a concession extracted cheaply persuades nobody. Distinguish the parts of a position that the evidence supports from the parts it leaves open, and let the conclusion inherit that internal structure rather than flattening it into a verdict of wholly right or wholly wrong. The fuller treatment of evaluation technique across the Marxist topics lives in [critiques of marxism exam answers](/2019/12/15/critiques-of-marxism-exam-answers/), and candidates should read the two guides together, this one supplying the discipline specific to contemporary material and that one the general craft of judging a tradition. What this guide adds, and insists on, is that on a moving subject the defeater is not an ornament of evaluation but its completion: the judgement is not finished until the script has said what would finish it.

## The thin-answer objection: will a structural script read as empty?

Every candidate who absorbs this guide's teaching eventually raises the same objection, and it deserves a direct answer rather than reassurance. Will an answer without contemporary examples read as thin? The candidate pictures the marker turning the pages, finding no named case, no figure, no flash of the present tense, and concluding that the candidate simply did not know anything about the modern economy. The picture is vivid and it is wrong, and it is worth understanding exactly why it is wrong, because a candidate who merely obeys the guide while privately believing the objection will relapse in the hall at the first difficult question, reaching for an instance the way a swimmer reaches for a rope.

A mechanism stated precisely reads as command. Consider the difference in texture between two sentences a candidate might write about income from digital intermediaries. The first reports that such income has grown very large and names the largest recipient. The second states that the income is a toll levied on transactions the intermediary hosts, that its basis is ownership of a channel others must use, and that its growth therefore redistributes surplus value toward the owners of channels without regard to where the value was produced. The first sentence tells the marker what the candidate has read about. The second shows what its writer can do. Marking is the assessment of the second thing. A script composed of second sentences does not read as thin; it reads as dense, because every sentence carries an operation the marker can credit. Thinness is a property of scripts whose sentences carry no operations, and instance-stacked scripts are the thinnest of all once their instances are discounted, as markers are trained to discount them, to the small change of recall.

The objection also misunderstands what an example does in an answer. In scholarly writing an example is evidence, embedded in an apparatus of citation and qualification that lets a reader test it. In an examination script an example is an anecdote, untestable in the room, and markers treat it with the politeness due to anecdotes and the credit due to them, which is little. The candidate who feels an answer growing thin and reaches for an example is treating a symptom with its cause: the answer feels thin because its mechanisms are underdeveloped, and the cure is another turn of the mechanism, a counterweight weighed, a distinction applied to a harder case, a basis stated more carefully. Development deepens. Illustration merely decorates, and decoration on a thin structure advertises the thinness it was meant to hide.

### Can an answer that rejects Marx still earn full marks?

yes, and candidates should believe it without qualification: the assessment rewards the handling of the analysis, not allegiance to it, so a script that weighs named claims, concedes the strongest opposing points, and concludes that the categories have been overtaken by the economy's development earns exactly what an equally disciplined script concluding the opposite earns.

That answer needs its emphasis because a superstition circulates among candidates that scripts on this material are marked for loyalty, that the examiner is a partisan waiting to reward agreement. Examiners on these papers are scholars of a tradition, which is a different thing: their professional skill is the accurate statement of positions they do not hold, and their marking criteria, in every discipline represented here, are written in the vocabulary of analysis and evaluation rather than of conclusion. A conclusion that the founder's framework has been overtaken, reached by testing named claims against the record and stating what the record shows, is a conclusion the criteria are built to reward at the highest level. A verdict of vindication, reached by reciting the framework's claims while ignoring every counterweight, belongs by the same criteria in the middle bands. The superstition has the marking exactly backwards, and candidates freed from it write better scripts in both directions, since they stop performing allegiance and start performing judgement.

There remains the honest exception, and this guide states it as plainly as the rule. Some applied papers, in some disciplines, require named cases by the terms of the question itself: the question instructs the candidate to apply the analysis to a specified organisation, sector, or episode, or the marking scheme for a particular professional paper credits case knowledge directly. The tell is in the question's wording and in the paper's published conventions, which the candidate's teachers can identify in advance; this guide states no syllabus particulars, and candidates must take the identification from their own course documentation. Where the requirement is genuine, the guide's advice adjusts rather than collapses. One generic and clearly dated illustration is sufficient, and safer than several current ones. Generic, because an unnamed large retailer or logistics network carries the analytical point without wagering on a firm's fate. Clearly dated, because the candidate who writes that an arrangement is described as it stood in a named period has converted a liability into a display of the very discipline this guide teaches: the illustration is offered as a specimen, its vintage acknowledged, its argumentative weight placed on the mechanism it specimens. Several current illustrations add exposure without adding credit, since the second case rarely does analytical work the first has not done.

### When does a question genuinely require a named case?

only when the question itself orders an application to a particular firm, industry, or episode, or when the documentation of the candidate's own course confirms that the paper pays for case knowledge; every other demand for cases is one the candidate has imported, and imported demands earn nothing.

### How much illustration does an applied question need?

one illustration, generically drawn and honestly dated, placed where the mechanism it specimens has already been stated, because the illustration's function is to show the mechanism at work in a concrete setting, and a second illustration repeats that function while doubling the script's exposure to the marker's knowledge of what happened to the cases afterward.

Candidates should notice the shape of these two answers, because the shape is the guide's whole doctrine in miniature. The exception is admitted, bounded, and priced. Nothing in the structural discipline requires pretending that applied questions do not exist; it requires knowing exactly which questions they are, meeting them with the minimum exposure that satisfies their terms, and refusing to let their habits colonise the rest of the paper. The candidate who can make that discrimination in the hall has internalised the doctrine rather than memorised it, and internalisation, as every section of this guide has argued in its own vocabulary, is the thing being marked.

## The common mistakes, gathered in one place

The errors this guide exists to prevent recur with such regularity that they can be catalogued, and candidates revise well from catalogues, since a named error is an error half disarmed. The first is the news digest: a script organised as a survey of developments, each described and none analysed, which mistakes the topic for its raw material. The second is the figure reflex: magnitudes quoted from memory at every point where an argument should be, each one stale, unsourced, and inert. The third is the borrowed label: a contested category used as the script's own framework, so that the answer argues inside a thesis it never examined. The fourth is the share inference: the labour share read as a gauge of exploitation, committing the category error the second distinction exists to block. The fifth is the prophecy trial: tendencies arraigned as forecasts and acquitted or convicted on evidence that was never relevant to them. The sixth is the floating comparison: periods or economies set side by side with no basis stated, producing verdicts that reverse themselves unnoticed between paragraphs. The seventh is anonymous assertion: positions advanced without names, so that nothing in the script has edges enough to be evaluated. The eighth is the naked verdict: a conclusion with no defeater, asking the marker to trust a judgement the candidate has not shown to be corrigible.

### Which errors recur most often in scripts on this material?

the catalogue runs to eight: surveying developments instead of analysing mechanisms, quoting figures from memory, borrowing contested labels as settled, reading the labour share as exploitation, trying tendencies as forecasts, comparing without a stated basis, asserting positions without naming their holders, and delivering verdicts with no statement of what would change them.

Each error in the catalogue has the same underlying shape, which is why one guide can treat them all. Each substitutes something easier for the operation the question actually rewards: chronology for mechanism, magnitude for argument, vocabulary for analysis, intuition for category discipline, drama for logic, juxtaposition for comparison, opinion for attribution, confidence for judgement. Candidates rarely commit these errors from ignorance of the material. They commit them under pressure, when the easier substitute presents itself first and the clock argues against refusing it. The defence is therefore not more knowledge but earlier decision: the distinctions rehearsed until they arrive before the substitutes do, the matrix internalised until the plan forms around it, the scans routinised until the final minutes perform them unbidden. This is a hopeful doctrine, because decisions and routines are trainable in a way that brilliance is not, and the distance between the middle band and the top band on this material is mostly the distance between a candidate who knows the doctrine and a candidate whose hand has been trained in it.

A word should be said about the marker's side of the catalogue, since candidates imagine it wrongly. Markers do not read with a checklist of sins, waiting to pounce. They read with a rubric of achievements, looking for the operations the bands describe, and the catalogue's errors matter because each one occupies space where an achievement might have stood. The news digest is not punished for being a digest; it is simply not rewarded as analysis, because it contains none. The naked verdict is not penalised for its confidence; it earns the credit due to an opinion, which is small, because the operation that earns largely, the weighing, was never performed. This reframing is liberating for candidates who fear hostile marking. There is no hostility. There is a tariff of operations, publicly knowable, and this guide is a price list. Perform the operations and the marks follow with the reliability of a market the founder himself would have recognised.

## Topic by topic: the economics treatment

The guide now turns from general doctrine to the settings in which candidates actually meet this material, discipline by discipline, because each setting bends the doctrine slightly and candidates should know the bends in advance. In economics papers with heterodox or political economy components, the questions centre on distribution, accumulation, and instability, and the neighbouring theories are the mainstream accounts against which the Marxist analysis is set: marginal productivity accounts of distribution, efficient-market accounts of financial behaviour, growth models in which distribution falls out of technology and preferences. The candidate's structural discipline is unchanged, but its emphasis shifts. The comparison-basis habit matters most here, because economics questions are the most likely to be phrased around measured quantities, and the labour share distinction will be exercised in nearly every sitting. The candidate should be ready to state what the mainstream account takes distribution to be determined by, and what the Marxist account takes it to be determined by, as a contrast of mechanisms rather than of conclusions: in the one, factor prices settle at the value of marginal contributions under competition; in the other, the wage is the value of labour power, set by the cost of reproducing the worker and by the balance of class forces, and the surplus is the residual the system's categories define. Stated that way, the contrast survives any factual weather, because it is a contrast between two machines for explaining distribution, and the question of which machine fits the record can then be argued on direction and duration.

Economics markers also reward a particular move that candidates from other disciplines underuse: the internal critique, in which a position is tested against its own standards. The claim that financial markets allocate capital to its most productive uses can be weighed against the system's recurrent instability without importing any Marxist premise, and the Marxist analysis then enters as the framework that predicted the instability's recurrence as a tendency rather than deploring it as an accident. Candidates should notice that this structure, concession, internal tension, framework as resolution, is the steelman discipline applied to a whole theory, and that it scores for the same reason attribution scores: it gives the argument edges. The candidate who caricatures the mainstream position before opposing it earns the marker's quiet distrust, because the marker holds, or has held, the position being caricatured, and recognises the straw. The candidate who states the mainstream position at its strongest and then locates its difficulty has performed the operation the top band describes, whatever the conclusion.

A further economics-specific discipline concerns models. Questions may invite candidates to treat the tradition's schemata, of reproduction, of the falling rate of profit, of the circuit of capital, as though they were forecasting instruments. The tendency distinction is the defence, applied with an addition: a schema is an instrument for isolating a mechanism, and its artificial simplifications are the price of the isolation, not errors to be apologised for or predictions to be checked. The candidate who can say what a schema leaves out, and why leaving it out is the method rather than a flaw, has understood something about theoretical practice that markers in economics are especially placed to value, because their own discipline's models live or die by the same defence.

## Topic by topic: the sociology treatment

Sociology papers meet this material through work, employment, and stratification, and the questions centre on how the character of jobs is mutating, the spread of insecurity, the organisation of control in the labour process, and the shape of the class structure. The neighbouring theories here are the accounts of post-industrial society, of the knowledge economy, of individualisation and risk, and the candidate's task is to hold the Marxist categories steady while the vocabulary changes around them. The standing temptation in sociology scripts is the narrative of transformation: a world of secure, collective, lifelong employment dissolving into a world of fragmented, precarious, individualised work, told as a story of loss. The narrative is not false, but as an answer it is a summary of events at the level of a whole society, and it earns what summaries earn. The structural version asks the analytical questions underneath the narrative. Which mechanisms produce insecurity: the reserve of labour enlarged by the reorganisation of production, the deliberate reconstruction of contracts to shift risk from capital to labour, the weakening of the collective organisations that once policed the boundary? Which categories describe the result: a growth of wage dependence in less protected forms, a recomposition of the working class rather than its disappearance, a redistribution of the costs of the system's flexibility onto those least able to bear them?

### How does a candidate show command of a dispute?

by stating each side at its strongest, naming the evidence each side treats as decisive, identifying the point on which the sides genuinely divide rather than the points on which they talk past each other, and then judging that point, with the concession to the losing side recorded and the defeater attached to the verdict.

The class-structure questions deserve their own warning, because they attract a specific version of the prophecy error. Candidates are asked whether the working class has declined, vanished, or been replaced, and they answer by weighing imagery: factories against offices, overalls against laptops. The structural answer redefines the question before answering it. Class, in this tradition, is a relation to the means of production, not an occupation, a costume, or a level of income, and the spread of wage dependence across occupations that once stood outside it, the professions salaried, the managers employed, the technicians hired by the hour of their expertise, is on the analysis an extension of the working class's defining relation rather than its erosion. Whether that extension is the right description is arguable, and the candidate should argue it against the strongest opposing account, the claim that property in skills and credentials has created a genuinely intermediate position with its own interests. But the argument must be conducted at the level of the category, or it is not an argument about class at all. Markers in sociology are alert to the category's definition because their discipline's debates turn on it, and a script that opens by fixing the relational definition has announced its competence in the discipline's own terms.

Stratification questions add the dimension of the other hierarchies, of gender, of ethnicity, of citizenship status, and the candidate needs a disciplined way to bring them into a class analysis without either dissolving them into it or abandoning the analysis at their approach. The structural habit serves here too. State the mechanism by which each hierarchy operates inside the labour market: the segmentation of the reserve of labour, the assignment of the least protected work along lines drawn by the other hierarchies, the reproduction of labour power performed unpaid in households and distributed by gender. Then the hierarchies appear neither as rivals to the class analysis nor as decorations upon it, but as structures through which its mechanisms run, which is the position the strongest literature in the field actually defends, and which the candidate can attribute, weigh, and judge like any other.

## Topic by topic: politics, geography, and the applied fields

Politics papers and their international political economy neighbours meet the material through power: the power of large firms over the conditions of their own regulation, the relation between states and the accumulation process, the organisation of production across borders and the politics that organisation creates. The questions reward the candidate who can keep two claims in view at once, that the state in a capitalist society is neither a neutral arbiter standing above the economy nor a simple instrument in the hands of a unified class, and that its relative autonomy is itself a structural feature with mechanisms that can be described: dependence on the tax yield of accumulation it does not control, competition among states for mobile investment, the organisation of business as a political constituency with privileged access. The structural discipline expresses itself here as a refusal of both caricatures, and the attribution discipline matters greatly, because the debate about the capitalist state has named positions whose differences are the examinable content. To write that the state serves capital is to write a slogan. The candidate who names the mechanisms, and the scholars who dispute their weight, has written politics.

Economic geography examines the spatial expression of the same mechanisms: the concentration of investment in favoured regions, the abandonment of others, the lengthening of the chains along which production is organised, the exposure of places to decisions taken in distant boardrooms. The discipline's questions are comparative almost by definition, region against region, period against period, and the basis-stating habit therefore earns its highest yield here. The geographer's version of the dating error is the place study used as evidence without typicality: a single declining town or booming district asked to carry a general claim. The structural treatment generalises the specimen before using it, stating the mechanism of which the place is an instance, uneven development driven by the search for the conditions of accumulation, the spatial fix by which crises of overaccumulation are displaced outward into new built environments, and then letting the place illustrate a mechanism already established. Candidates in geography should also note that their discipline's markers are connoisseurs of scale: a claim true at the scale of the region may invert at the scale of the world market, and stating the scale of an argument is the geographer's form of stating a comparison basis.

The applied fields, business and management studies with critical components, and development studies, bend the doctrine in opposite directions, and the candidate should know both bends. Management questions are the likeliest in this whole landscape to carry the genuine case requirement discussed earlier, since the field's pedagogy is built on cases; the guide's bounded exception applies, one generic and dated illustration, with the mechanism stated first and the case made to serve it. Development questions are the likeliest to test the candidate's handling of the world market's hierarchy: the mechanisms by which value and income are distributed between the economies at the system's centre and those at its periphery, the debates over whether incorporation into the world market develops or deforms the incorporated economy. Here the attribution discipline and the comparison discipline meet, because the field's classic dispute, between the scholars of dependence and the scholars of modernisation, is a dispute about mechanisms conducted across comparisons whose bases were often left unstated by the disputants themselves. The candidate who states the bases the originals omitted performs an act of scholarship the marker will recognise, and performs it with tools this guide has supplied.

## The timed answer: architecture under the clock

Doctrine becomes marks only through an architecture that survives the clock, and this section sets out the architecture as plainly as the guide can make it. An answer on this material should be built in five movements. The opening fixes the categories and announces the governing distinction, in the manner described at the start of this guide. The second movement develops the mechanism the question targets, stated in the tradition's terms and attributed where a living scholar's formulation is used. The third movement brings the evidence, by direction and duration, with the comparison basis stated wherever the evidence is comparative. The fourth movement weighs: the strongest opposing position, stated fairly, the concessions owed to it, the points on which the answer's side prevails and the points it surrenders. The fifth movement concludes, with the verdict and its defeater in consecutive sentences. Five movements, each with a function the marker can identify, none of them padded. The candidate who writes to this architecture will find that length takes care of itself, because each movement has real work to do, and that the panic of the middle hour, when scripts traditionally wander, is disarmed by the knowledge of which movement comes next.

The planning minutes deserve a discipline of their own. Before writing, the candidate should produce, on the planning page, four things: the governing distinction in a phrase, the mechanism in a sentence, the opposing position in a sentence with its holder's name, and the defeater in a sentence. Four lines. A candidate who cannot produce the four lines is not ready to write and should spend another minute thinking rather than ten minutes writing in a circle. A candidate who can produce them owns the answer in embryo, and the writing becomes transcription with development, which is a far less frightening activity than composition from nothing. Teachers should drill the four lines as a separate exercise, against the clock, on questions the candidate has never seen, because the skill of the four lines is the skill of the whole examination in miniature: orientation before production.

### Why does direction and duration beat magnitude in an answer?

because magnitudes are revised, redefined, and overtaken, while the direction of a sustained movement and the length of its persistence are the features of the evidence that survive revision, and because an argument needs to know which way the system is moving and for how long, not the third decimal of a quantity the candidate cannot source.

Time allocation inside the answer follows the tariff of operations. The opening and the conclusion together should take a small share of the time and repay it out of proportion, because they frame everything between. The weighing movement should be protected against the common accident of its sacrifice: candidates who run long in the descriptive movements arrive at the evaluation with minutes left and compress the very operations the top band certifies into a breathless paragraph. The remedy is mechanical. Decide, when planning, the point by the clock at which the weighing must begin, and begin it then, even if the mechanism's development feels unfinished. An unfinished development costs a little credit. A missing evaluation costs the band. Candidates should also reserve the final minutes, as this guide has already urged, for the scan: figures out, names of enterprises generalised, dates struck, defeaters checked for presence after each verdict, verbs audited wherever the labour share appears. The scan is not proofreading. It is the enforcement, at the last possible moment, of every discipline the guide has taught, and it should be rehearsed as a checklist until the checklist runs itself.

There is a psychology to the timed answer that candidates should hear stated once. The hall rewards the candidate who trusts preparation over improvisation, and punishes, with a peculiar consistency, the candidate who treats each question as an invitation to think new thoughts in public. New thoughts are for the study. The hall is for the execution of rehearsed operations on fresh material, and the freshness of the material is precisely what the operations are designed to absorb. A candidate who has rehearsed the three distinctions, the attribution formula, the basis statement, and the defeater on a dozen practice questions possesses a machine that unfamiliar questions feed rather than break. The sense of calm that machine produces is not a side benefit. It is visible in the script's handwriting of the mind, its steady allocation, its unhurried openings, its conclusions that arrive on time, and markers, who are readers before they are judges, respond to it as readers do.

## The disciplines at work: a relevance answer in slow motion

Abstractions persuade less than demonstrations, so this section walks through the construction of an answer to the commonest question type, the relevance question, showing each discipline doing its work in sequence. The candidate reads the question, which asks, in whatever phrasing, whether the founder's analysis remains relevant to the economy of the present. The first move is decomposition. Relevance is not one property but a bundle, and the question is unanswerable until the bundle is opened: relevance of the categories, of the mechanisms, of the tendencies, of the specific expectations the founder expressed in his own polemical contexts. The candidate announces the decomposition in the opening, together with the governing distinction, tendency against forecast, which the question's phrasing has made necessary. Already, before any content, the script has performed two creditable operations, and the marker has been told how to read what follows.

The second move selects. From the bundle, the candidate takes a small number of claims for full treatment: the concentration tendency, the analysis of rent, the machinery mechanism. Each receives the full apparatus. The claim is stated in its strongest form and attributed. The mechanism is developed. The evidence is characterised by direction and duration: concentration proceeding through successive waves, checked in places by deliberate policy, resumed when the checking relaxes; rent-like income growing as a share of the whole, its basis in owned access plainly visible; machinery continuing to be introduced under the twin pressures the analysis names, cheapening and discipline, with displacement and reinstatement running together as the account predicts. The counterweights are weighed in the same paragraphs, not quarantined in a token paragraph of balance, because the tendency doctrine says the counterweights are part of the phenomenon, and the script that integrates them shows a grasp of the doctrine that the quarantining script does not.

The third move confronts the hardest opposing material at its strongest. The candidate states the claim that the economy's centre of gravity has shifted from production to access and knowledge, in the formulation of the scholars who argue it most forcefully, and answers it with the first distinction: the growth of access income is a fact about distribution, and a fact about distribution cannot by itself establish a change in the mode of production, because the access income is a claim on a fund production supplies. The candidate then concedes what the distinction does not dispose of: that the organisation of production has changed in ways the founder did not describe, that the forms of the firm and of employment have been rebuilt, that whole categories of the analysis, of the working day, of the factory, require translation for an economy of dispersed and monitored work. The concession is the making of the answer, because it converts the conclusion from a verdict on a slogan into a map: here the analysis stands, here it needs translation, here it has been overtaken. Maps are what markers reward, because maps are what understanding looks like on paper.

The fourth move concludes, and the conclusion is short because the work is done. The verdict is differentiated, claim by claim, in the proportions the weighing established. The defeater follows immediately: the verdict on concentration would change if the direction reversed and held across a full cycle without the strengthening of the counterweights that have historically checked it; the verdict on rent would change if access income were shown to arise without a produced fund behind it, which would be a change in the economy's foundations rather than in its distribution. The answer ends. Nothing in it can be dated by a marker, no figure can be contradicted, no label has been borrowed, no comparison floats free of its basis, and every judgement has declared the terms of its own revision. That is what the top of the tariff buys, and the candidate should notice that nothing in the demonstration required brilliance. It required the disciplines, executed in order, which is the most encouraging sentence in this guide.

### What distinguishes understanding from recall under time pressure?

understanding shows itself in operations performed on material the candidate has not rehearsed, distinctions applied to a fresh case, a basis stated for an unplanned comparison, a defeater framed for a verdict reached in the room, while recall can only reproduce material in the shape in which it was stored and falls silent when the question changes the shape.

The demonstration generalises, and candidates should perform the generalisation themselves as an exercise. Take the automation question and walk it through the same four moves: decomposition of the question into displacement, reinstatement, and the transformation of the labour process; selection of the machinery mechanism for full treatment; confrontation with the strongest opposing claim, that the present wave of technique differs in kind because it substitutes for cognitive rather than manual operations, answered by examining whether the mechanism, the production of relative surplus value under competitive pressure, cares about the manual or cognitive character of the tasks it reorganises; conclusion with defeater. Take the labour share question likewise: decomposition into the share as statistic and the mechanisms proposed to explain its movement; the distinction announced before any evidence; the mechanisms weighed, reserve army pressure, the cheapening of wage goods, the reconstruction of bargaining institutions, the relocation of production; the basis stated; the verdict delivered with its defeater. A candidate who has performed the walk three times owns the method. A candidate who has read about the walk owns a description of the method, which is a different and much less valuable property, and the difference will be visible in the hall.

## Rent in depth: the toll, the title, and the test

Because the first distinction carries so much weight in the matrix, candidates benefit from a second pass over its machinery, slower than the first, so that the sentence rent presupposes production becomes the visible tip of an understood structure rather than a charm recited against novelty. Begin with the classical case, which the tradition built the category on. Land used in production yields its owner a payment because the land is scarce in quality or position and because ownership lets the owner charge for access to a condition of production the owner did not create. The payment's size is set by the productivity differences among plots and by the pressure of demand, not by any labour the owner performs. Already in the classical case the category's marks are visible: income grounded in exclusion, scaled by scarcity, drawn from a fund produced by the labour of others. The candidate who can derive the marks from the classical case owns them; the candidate who has memorised the marks owns a list.

Now move the structure, unchanged, onto the assets of the present economy, and watch how much of the contemporary landscape it organises. A position astride a channel of distribution, through which producers must pass to reach purchasers, yields its owner a payment scaled by the traffic and grounded in the difficulty of building another channel. A body of protected content, recordings, texts, designs, yields payments for each use, scaled by demand and grounded in the legal fence around reproduction. A standard, a format, a network whose value grows with its membership, yields its controller payments and advantages grounded in the cost to any user of leaving. In each case the candidate should perform the same three questions. Is the payment grounded in exclusion from something scarce? Is its scale set by the traffic across the scarcity rather than by the labour of organising production? Does the fund it draws on originate in production elsewhere in the system? Three yeses make the income rent in structure, and the classification, not the novelty of the asset, decides what the income's growth means: a redistribution toward the owners of bottlenecks, with consequences for the producers who pay the tolls, for the prices purchasers face, and for the direction of investment, which is drawn toward the acquisition of bottlenecks and away from the organisation of production.

### How does a candidate test whether an income is rent?

by asking three questions of it in order: whether the payment rests on exclusion from something scarce and owned, whether its size follows the traffic across that scarcity rather than the labour of producing anything, and whether the fund it draws from was produced elsewhere, since three affirmative answers classify the income by its structure whatever costume the asset wears.

The test also shows its limits honestly, and candidates should display the limits rather than hide them, since the display is itself creditable. Real enterprises blend the strands. The owner of a channel also organises the logistics that run through it, employs labour, builds facilities, and the income of the whole is a blend of toll and profit in proportions the accounts do not publish. The candidate's correct move is the one this guide has already prescribed: separate the strands in thought, assign each to its category by asking what the payment is actually for, and rest the argument on that assignment rather than on a decomposition nobody can perform. A question that asks whether platforms are rent or profit machines is usually inviting exactly this answer, and the candidate who answers with a single word, either word, has failed the question's real test, which is whether the candidate can hold a blended object up to a distinction and report what the distinction shows: both, in unknown proportions, with the growth concentrated, on the structural evidence, in the toll strand. That last clause, the location of the growth, is where the analytical action is, because tendencies in the blend's composition are claims a script can defend structurally, by asking where investment flows and what acquirers pay for when they buy such enterprises: the channel and its traffic, or the labour process and its plant. The price paid for acquisitions is the market's own testimony about which strand the buyers value, and a candidate can cite that testimony in structural terms without quoting a single transaction.

One more refinement separates the strongest scripts. Rent in the classical treatment is connected to monopoly, and candidates should be ready to state the connection precisely. Where the scarce condition can be reproduced freely, competition among its owners drives the payment toward the ordinary return on whatever capital its provision required, and the rent element evaporates. Rent persists where reproduction is blocked, by nature, by law, or by the network structure itself. This gives the candidate a dynamic question to ask of any rent-like income: what blocks reproduction, and how durable is the block? A block grounded in statute lasts as long as the statute and the politics that sustain it. A block grounded in network membership lasts as long as the members stay, which is a question about the costs of leaving and the prospects of coordination among the dissatisfied. A block grounded in physical scarcity lasts longest of all. Scripts that grade the durability of blocks are doing the finest work this material offers, because they convert a static classification into an analysis of the income's future, which is what the relevance questions are asking for under another name.

## The labour share in depth: what the record can be asked

The second distinction likewise repays a slower pass, because candidates who grasp only its prohibitive half, the share is not the rate, are left with nothing to write where the evidence belongs, and the evidence sections of their scripts thin out into gestures. The positive doctrine is that the distributional record is a body of evidence about the system's behaviour, to be interrogated with questions the record can answer. It can answer questions about direction: whether the division of income between compensation and the property incomes has moved persistently one way across long spans and many economies. It can answer questions about variation: whether the movement is general or confined to particular sectors, economies, or institutional settings, and variation is often the more instructive answer, because a mechanism that operates everywhere predicts uniformity, while the record's variety points to the counterweights and conditions that modulate the mechanism. It can answer questions about timing at the coarse grain: whether movements align with the great institutional changes, the weakening or strengthening of organised labour, the opening of production to the world market, the reconstruction of welfare provision. What it cannot answer is the question candidates most want to put to it, the level or movement of the rate of exploitation, and the candidate who understands why, in terms of the categories each side of the comparison inhabits, can explain the refusal in a paragraph that markers remember.

Composition deserves a paragraph of its own in every candidate's preparation, because it is the point at which the record most often deceives the unwary. An economy's aggregate share is a weighted average of its sectors' shares, and the weights move. Employment shifts toward sectors where the compensation share is structurally high or low, and the aggregate follows the weights even if no sector's internal division changes at all. A candidate who reports an aggregate movement without asking the composition question has not yet begun to analyse the record, and a marker who knows the literature will wait, in vain, for the question to be asked. The structural habit is to decompose before interpreting: how much of the movement is the sectors changing their weights, how much is the divisions within sectors changing, and which mechanisms in the analysis speak to each part? The reserve army and bargaining power speak to the within-sector part. The relocation of production across the world market speaks to both. The candidate who performs the decomposition in the script, even qualitatively, has done more analysis in four sentences than a page of quoted aggregates performs, and has done it without a single figure.

### What can the distributional record legitimately be asked?

it can be asked about direction across long spans, about variation between sectors and economies, and about the coarse alignment of movements with institutional change, because those are questions its categories can bear; it cannot be asked for the level of exploitation, whose categories it does not contain, and knowing which questions to bring is understanding the evidence.

The candidate should also prepare the record's use in the opposing direction, since questions are set by examiners who enjoy symmetry. Suppose the movement in some economy, over some span, runs against the framework's expectation: the share rises, or holds, while the mechanisms of pressure operate unimpeded. The undisciplined script either hides the span or surrenders the framework. The disciplined script does the analytical work. It asks whether the span's basis is honestly stated, whether composition is flattering the aggregate, whether counterweights the framework itself names were strengthened in that span, whether the mechanisms were in fact unimpeded or were blunted by full employment's effect on the reserve of labour. Each of those questions is a place where the framework and the record genuinely engage, and the engagement, not the verdict, is the examinable event. A framework that survives interrogation with its mechanisms intact and its conditions clarified has been treated scientifically, and a candidate who treats it so, in either direction of the evidence, has written the kind of script that examiners describe to each other afterward. The candidate should aim to be describable in that way.

There is a final refinement, concerning the wage itself. Candidates often write as though the value of labour power and the compensation recorded in the accounts were the same quantity under two names. They are not, and the gap between them is instructive. The recorded compensation includes the pay of senior managers whose incomes behave like shares of surplus, includes payments in forms the accounts classify by convention, and is net of nothing the theory counts as deductions or gross of nothing the theory counts as the social wage. The theory's quantity, the value of labour power, is the value of the means of subsistence required to reproduce the worker at the historically given standard, a quantity no statistical office publishes. Holding the two apart in the script, with a sentence acknowledging the distance between the measured proxy and the theoretical quantity, is the labour share distinction at its most mature: not a refusal to use evidence, but a precise statement of the terms on which evidence and theory may meet. Candidates who can write that sentence have nothing to fear from any distributional question the papers can set.

## Tendencies in depth: the grammar of pressure and counterweight

The third distinction, too, has depths that the first statement of it only sketches, and candidates aiming at the highest band should work in them. The tradition's tendency claims share a grammar, and learning the grammar lets a candidate parse any tendency claim met in the wild, including claims from the tradition's critics, whose refutations often fail by misreading the grammar they attack. A tendency claim asserts that a mechanism, built into the relations of the system, generates a pressure in a direction. It asserts that the pressure operates continuously while the relations persist, not intermittently or by anyone's intention. It asserts that counteracting influences, some of them generated by the system itself, some imposed on it from outside by deliberate policy or organised resistance, contend with the pressure on the same terrain. And it asserts that the observable record is the resultant of the contention, so that the record can confirm the pressure's operation, in the places where the pressure tells, without the pressure ever appearing in the record in its pure form. A candidate who can unpack a tendency claim into those four assertions can handle any question built on one, because every such question is, underneath its phrasing, asking about one of the four: the mechanism, its continuity, the counterweights, or the reading of the resultant.

The counterweights deserve the candidate's closest study, for two reasons. First, they are where the analysis is most concrete: the tradition names them specifically, the cheapening of the elements of constant capital against the falling rate of profit, the expansion of foreign trade, the growth of the relative surplus population's effect on wages against the pressure of accumulation on the wage share, the state's interventions against the crisis tendency, organised labour against the lengthening and intensifying pressures on the working day. Pairing each tendency with its standing counterweights produces a texture of knowledge that no summary of events can imitate. Second, the counterweights are where evaluation lives. To judge a tendency claim is to judge the relative strength of pressure and counterweight under stated conditions, and that judgement, unlike a verdict on a forecast, can be refined indefinitely as the evidence is examined: stronger here, weaker there, prevailing in this period, checked in that one, with the conditions of the checking themselves analysed. The candidate's conclusions on tendency questions should therefore always be conditional in form, not from timidity but because the conditional is the logically correct shape of the claim, and markers read the shape as understanding.

### What makes a conclusion safe on a moving subject?

its conditional form: a verdict stated for named claims, under stated conditions, with the counterweights weighed and the development named that would revise it, because on a subject that moves while the candidate writes, the unconditional verdict is the one the next turn of the subject is most likely to embarrass.

Candidates should also learn to spot the tendency grammar when it is missing, because its absence diagnoses weak claims in the literature the questions draw on. A claim that some feature of the present economy spells the end of some feature of the system, offered without a mechanism, without counterweights, without conditions, is a forecast in tendency costume, and the candidate may say so, politely and precisely, as an evaluative act. The literature of the present economy is rich in such claims, at both poles of optimism about the system's transformation, and questions frequently smuggle one in as a premise. The candidate who tests the premise against the grammar, finds the mechanism unspecified or the counterweights unconsidered, and rebuilds the question on sounder terms has performed the highest operation this guide teaches: not answering the question as set, but showing what the question must become before evidence can answer it. Markers reserve their most generous readings for such scripts, because the operation is the one their own scholarship performs daily.

A closing caution on this distinction concerns the candidate's own side. The tendency grammar protects the analysis from refutation by non-arrival, and unscrupulous use of it can protect anything, since a pressure that never tells and whose counterweights always explain the record has been immunised against evidence altogether. Markers know this danger and watch for it. The candidate's protection against the charge of immunisation is the defeater, once again: the tendency claim in the script should come with the observation that would count against the pressure's operation, a direction reversed and held under conditions where the counterweights were not strengthened, a mechanism found not to operate where its conditions obtain. A tendency with a stated defeater is a scientific claim. A tendency without one is a faith, and the examination, whatever the candidate's private convictions, is an assessment of the first kind of writing only.

## Revision method: converting the doctrine into reflex

Revision for this material fails in a characteristic way, and the failure is worth describing before the method, because the method is designed against it. Candidates revise the contemporary topics by reading about the contemporary economy: surveys, summaries, journalism digested into notes, the notes re-read until their contents feel familiar. Familiarity is then mistaken for readiness. In the hall, the familiarity is present but unusable, because it was stored as narrative and the questions demand operations. The method this guide prescribes inverts the storage. Revise the operations, and store the material inside them. Every unit of revision should end with the candidate having performed, on paper, one of the guide's moves: stated a distinction against a fresh case, classified an income by the three questions, decomposed a distributional movement, parsed a tendency claim into its grammar, attributed a position in a sentence, stated a basis, framed a defeater. Reading may precede the performance, to supply the material, but the performance is the revision, and a session without one is a session that will not survive contact with the paper.

The kit the candidate builds is small, and its smallness is the point. A card for each of the three distinctions, carrying the distinction's statement, its test or questions, and the error it blocks. One for every citable position, carrying the scholar's name, one sentence for the thesis, one clause for its boundary, and the name of whoever disputes it. One for every row of the contemporary answer matrix. A card for the figures instruction, with the replacement formula on its reverse: direction, duration, mechanism. A card for the comparison basis, with its four choices listed as questions to answer. A card for the attribution formula and one for the defeater sentence, each with a worked instance in the candidate's own hand. The whole kit fits in a coat pocket, and a candidate who can reproduce every card unaided, in prose, against the clock, possesses the topic in the only form the examination purchases. The companion resources for building and drilling the kit are treated in the student layer below, and the sequence across a revision period is set out in the [marxism revision guide](/2022/01/15/marxism-revision-guide/), which this section follows rather than replaces.

### What does efficient revision of this material actually consist of?

by revising operations rather than narrative: one performance per session, a distinction applied, an income classified, a movement decomposed, a position attributed, a defeater framed, until the small kit of cards behind this guide can be reproduced from memory in prose under time pressure, since the hall purchases production and familiarity with summaries is not production.

The practice questions matter as much as the kit, and they should be chosen adversarially. Candidates naturally practise on the questions they can already answer, which rehearses their strengths and hides their gaps until the hall exposes them. The method prescribes the opposite selection: the question type whose matrix row the candidate fills least confidently, the distinction whose test the candidate performs least fluently, the position whose attribution the candidate states least precisely. Past papers supply the questions, and where a discipline's past papers are thin, the question types in the matrix can be rephrased by a teacher or a study partner into fresh wording, since the operations must survive rephrasing to be worth anything. Each practice answer should be marked, by the candidate or another, against the catalogue of errors rather than against a general impression: which of the eight errors appear, in which paragraphs, and what operation should have stood in their place. Error-counting is a crude instrument and an effective one, because the catalogue is finite and the candidate can watch the count fall across the revision period, which is the most honest encouragement the process offers.

Timing belongs to the method as well. The operations must be practised at examination speed, because several of them, the opening apparatus, the four planning lines, the closing scan, are worthless if they cost more time than the hall allows. The candidate should therefore schedule full timed answers among the shorter drills, at least enough to make the architecture of the five movements a habit of the hand, and should debrief each timed answer with the same error catalogue and one further question: where did the clock hurt, and which movement was compressed to feed another? The answer to that question directs the next drill. Revision conducted this way has a property that narrative revision lacks: it converges. Each session removes a named weakness, and the candidate approaches the paper with a kit that has been tested rather than a familiarity that has been felt. On a topic where the raw material moves daily, the tested kit is the only possession that does not depreciate, and building it is the whole of efficient revision for this material.

## The scorecard habit: weighing the standing tendencies one by one

Relevance questions fail most often not at the level of doctrine but at the level of bookkeeping. A script announces that the analysis will be tested against the record, and then tests nothing in particular, drifting among impressions of the economy's health until the conclusion arrives by atmosphere. The remedy is a habit this guide calls the scorecard: before writing, list the standing tendencies the tradition actually asserts, pair each with the counterweights its own literature names, and commit to rendering a separate verdict on each. The scorecard's virtue is that it makes vagueness physically difficult. A verdict owed on concentration cannot be discharged with a paragraph about insecurity. A verdict owed on crisis recurrence cannot be smuggled out under cover of a verdict on distribution. Markers read scorecard scripts with a sense of being answered, question by question, and the sense is worth marks before any single verdict is judged.

Take the tendencies in turn, as a candidate should be able to take them, each with its mechanism and its standing opposition. Concentration and centralisation rest on competition itself: capitals that cheapen commodities grow, capitals that cannot are absorbed, and the process feeds on crises, when assets pass cheaply into stronger hands. The counterweights are equally structural. Deliberate policy can police combinations and break them. New fields of accumulation open room for new entrants at the margin. Whole sectors arise whose early structure is fragmented, before the same pressure begins its work inside them. A verdict here is therefore never a single word. It specifies the terrain: proceeding in the established industries, restarted in the newer ones, checked where policy has chosen to pay the price of checking, resumed where the checking has tired. That sentence shape, varied across sectors and spans, is the concentration verdict in its mature form, and it can be written without one merger being named.

Crisis recurrence rests on the disproportionality and overaccumulation built into decentralised investment: production expands without a coordinating check against the market's capacity to absorb, credit stretches across the gap, and the correction arrives as a general event rather than a series of private disappointments. Its counterweights are the great institutional constructions of the system's self-defence: lender-of-last-resort facilities, deposit guarantees, countercyclical spending, the international coordination improvised under panic. Weighing this tendency teaches a subtle lesson that strong candidates display. A counterweight that works by socialising losses has not refuted the tendency; it has confirmed the diagnosis while changing the bill's recipient. The script that can distinguish between a tendency refuted and a tendency managed has reached a level of discrimination that examiners across the whole landscape surveyed in this guide are waiting to reward, because most of the public argument on the subject confuses the two.

Wage dependence, the tendency for livelihoods to be mediated by the sale of labour power, rests on the separation of producers from independent access to means of production, a separation the system continually renews as commons are enclosed, crafts are industrialised, and services once provided within households or by independent practitioners are reorganised as employments. Its counterweights include the persistence of small property at the margins, the growth of state employment on different terms, and the hybrid forms in which a worker sells labour while nominally contracting as a business. Verdicts here turn on the category discipline from the sociology treatment: the question is not how many people hold which job titles, but how many depend for their subsistence on a buyer of their capacity to work, and by that measure the tendency's long operation is difficult to dispute, while its forms have multiplied beyond anything its first formulator sketched. A verdict that separates the relation's spread from its costumes' variety has said the most important thing this tendency offers an examiner.

Production socialised under private appropriation is the subtlest entry on the scorecard and the one candidates handle worst, usually by ignoring it. The claim is that production becomes ever more collective in its actual organisation, interdependent chains, vast coordinated establishments, knowledge held in common and built cooperatively, while appropriation remains private and narrow, so that the system's own development sharpens the contradiction between how it produces and how it owns. Its counterweights are the genuine dispersal mechanisms of the newer economy: production fragmented across contractors, work dispersed into homes, coordination achieved through markets among small units rather than within great establishments. The weighing is genuinely difficult, which is precisely why examiners value it: the dispersal is real at the level of the workplace and questionable at the level of control, since the fragments are coordinated by owners of channels, standards, and finance whose grip the fragmentation may actually tighten. A candidate who can hold both levels in view, dispersal of execution alongside concentration of command, has found the verdict this tendency was waiting for, and it is a verdict no survey of developments could have produced.

Keeping the scorecard has a final use, which is defensive. Hostile questions often select the weakest tendency, or the one whose counterweights have lately prevailed, and invite the candidate to defend the whole analysis on that single field. The scorecard habit refuses the invitation's terms without refusing the question. The candidate renders the honest verdict on the field chosen, concedes what the counterweights have achieved there, and then notes, briefly and without pleading, that the analysis stands as a portfolio of claims whose fortunes differ, which is why the guide insists on judging them severally. The refusal reads as confidence because it is confidence: the candidate plainly knows the whole board and is not frightened of any square on it.

## Financial forms: credit, fictitious capital, and the candidate

No cluster of questions on the present economy is more treacherous for the unprepared than the cluster around finance, because the vocabulary is dramatic and the drama invites exactly the failures this guide was written to prevent. Candidates write of casinos and parasites, of money breeding money in a void, and the writing satisfies a feeling while forfeiting the marks. The tradition's own treatment is colder and more useful. Credit, in that treatment, is an accelerant built into accumulation: it mobilises idle balances, lets production expand ahead of realised demand, centralises capitals through the joint-stock form, and in the same motion builds the claims structure whose collapse is the crisis's financial face. Fictitious capital, the category candidates most need and least often command, names the capitalisation of expected revenue: a claim whose price is the discounted value of an anticipated income stream, tradable, accumulable, and capable of swelling far beyond the value of any underlying production, while remaining, in the end, a claim on revenue that production must actually supply. The candidate who owns that category can discuss booms in asset prices, the growth of financial incomes, and the periodic destruction of paper wealth in a single analytical frame, without once reaching for a figure or a named institution.

The examination value of the frame shows itself against the standard question types. Asked whether finance has become an autonomous economy detached from production, the undisciplined script answers with atmosphere, and the disciplined script answers with the category: the claims are fictitious in the technical sense, priced from expected revenue, and their autonomy is an appearance generated by their tradability, real enough in calm times, revoked in crises when the claims are marched back to the revenue that must validate them. Asked whether financial profit is a new kind of profit, the script applies the income classification learned for rent: interest is a share of surplus value, ceded by functioning capital to money capital, and the financial sector's earnings blend that share with fees that are claims on revenue and with gains from the appreciation of the claims themselves, each strand sorted by what its payment is actually for. Asked about instability, the script deploys the tendency grammar: credit stretches the system's expansion beyond its realised basis as a standing pressure, countered by the regulatory constructions erected after each collapse and eroded by competition during each long calm, an erosion that is itself a mechanism, since the institution that refuses the era's risks loses the era's business to rivals who accept them.

One discipline is specific to this cluster, and examiners watch for it. Candidates must resist moralising the categories. Fictitious is a technical term about the relation between a claim and its revenue basis, not a synonym for fraudulent, and the script that uses it as an insult announces that the category was borrowed rather than learned. The same restraint applies to speculation, which in the analysis performs functions inside accumulation, price discovery among them, however destructive its excesses, and to the figure of the financier, who is a functionary of a structure rather than a villain in a pageant. The tradition at its best analyses a machinery that produces its crises impersonally, through the rational conduct of every participant, and scripts that reproduce that impersonality of explanation outscore scripts that distribute blame, because the impersonality is the theory's signature claim about how the system works. A candidate whose finance paragraphs contain no villains has understood something deep, and markers trained in the tradition recognise the understanding instantly.

There is also a dating hazard peculiar to finance, worth naming for the candidate's protection. Financial questions tempt scripts toward the memory of collapses, and the collapses arrive in the candidate's mind wearing dates and the names of failed institutions. Every one of those names is an exposure. The structural script discusses collapse generically: the moment when claims are presented for validation and the revenue is not there, the chain of defaults through balance sheets bound together by mutual claims, the forced sale of assets into a market of only sellers, the state's arrival as validator of last resort. Nothing in that description needs a year, a bank, or a country, and everything in it is what the analysis actually claims. A marker reading it learns that the candidate possesses the mechanism. A marker reading a chronicle of failures learns that the candidate possesses a chronicle.

## Global chains: the world market as an examination topic

The reorganisation of production across borders is the contemporary topic on which the largest number of disciplines converge, and the candidate who prepares it structurally acquires an asset spendable in economics, geography, politics, and development papers alike. The analysis begins from a mechanism rather than from the spectacle of the chains themselves. Competition compels each capital to cheapen its commodities; labour is a cost whose price differs enormously across the world market; therefore production migrates, fragments, and reassembles itself across jurisdictions, with each fragment located where its particular cost and skill conditions are most favourable to the whole. The chain is not a conspiracy or a fashion. It is the spatial form the cheapening imperative takes once transport and communication costs fall low enough to permit it, and a script that opens with the mechanism has already outargued the scripts that open with the spectacle and never leave it.

Against that mechanism the questions arrange themselves. Examiners ask what the chains do to workers in the economies that receive production, and the disciplined answer refuses both sentimental poles. Incorporation into the chains brings wage employment where subsistence or informal labour stood before, which the workers themselves often experience as an improvement worth migrating for, and it brings that employment on terms set by the buyer's power to leave, which disciplines wages, conditions, and organisation with a threat no local employer could wield alone. The two facts are one fact viewed from two positions in the chain, and the script that holds them together, attributing the dispute between the scholars of development-through-incorporation and the scholars of dependence to its actual point of division, the counterfactual each side assumes about the alternative available to the receiving economy, performs the finest operation this topic offers. Turn the chain around, and the next question concerns workers in the economies production leaves, and its answer runs through the reserve mechanism at world scale: the credible threat of relocation enlarges the effective reserve pressing on the home workforce without a single factory needing to move, so that the chain disciplines labour in places it never touches. Examiners ask where the value goes, and the answer classifies the chain's incomes: the margins at the design, branding, and channel ends, where the scarce and ownable assets sit, against the margins at the assembly end, where competition among locations and contractors is fiercest, a distribution the rent analysis illuminates directly, since control of the chain's bottlenecks yields claims on value produced along its whole length.

The dating hazards on this topic are severe, because the chains are perpetually in the news: a disruption, a reshoring announcement, a trade conflict, and the candidate's notes fill with episodes. The structural script treats every episode as weather over a climate. Disruptions reveal the chain's fragility, which is a structural property, the consequence of inventories squeezed and single sources multiplied by the same cheapening pressure that built the chain, and a candidate can analyse the fragility, and the countervailing pressure toward redundancy and regional duplication, without narrating any particular blockage. Reshoring announcements invite the candidate to test a claim against its mechanism: production returns when the cost arithmetic changes, through wages converging, through automation cheapening the labour content until location matters less, through political risk repriced, and each of those is a tendency with counterweights, arguable in the grammar this guide has taught. The candidate who argues the arithmetic can absorb any headline. The candidate who collects headlines is absorbed by them.

One further concept belongs in the prepared kit for this topic, because questions reach for it constantly: the idea of a labour aristocracy, and its modern descendant, the claim that workers in the wealthy economies benefit from the chain's distribution and therefore stand in a different relation to the system than workers at its periphery. The candidate should handle the claim by its mechanism. If income at the chain's favoured ends is partly a claim on value produced at the other ends, then the wage levels of the favoured ends are partly supported by the arrangement, and the political consequences the tradition drew from that support, caution, sectionalism, attachment to the order that delivers the supported standard, become examinable propositions rather than insults. The script should state the claim at its strongest, name its holders, weigh the channels through which such a transfer could actually flow, and conclude conditionally. Handled so, a question designed to provoke a slogan receives an analysis, which is the exchange this guide exists to arrange.

## The working day reborn: intensity, monitoring, and unpaid margins

Questions about work in the present economy often arrive disguised as questions about technology, and the candidate who sees through the disguise to the older category underneath gains an immediate advantage. The older category is the working day: its length, its intensity, and the boundary between the hours labour is paid for and the hours it is not. The tradition's analysis of machinery was always, at bottom, an analysis of this boundary, since machinery's profitability lay as much in filling the day's pores, quickening the pace, and extending supervision as in cheapening the product. The present economy's celebrated innovations in the organisation of work repay reading in exactly those terms, and a script that reads them so demonstrates the deepest kind of relevance a theory can have: its categories organise phenomena its author never saw, without forcing.

Take the phenomena in their structural descriptions, as the candidate should take them. Work allocated by algorithm, summoned and dismissed by demand fluctuations measured in minutes, is the working day fragmented into its saleable pieces, with the waiting time between pieces transferred to the worker as unpaid availability. Monitoring by software, keystrokes counted, locations logged, pace displayed against a standard, is the overseer's function automated, the intensity of labour raised by a supervision that never tires and never looks away. The extension of work into the home and the evening through the devices every worker carries is the day's boundary dissolved at its edges, necessary labour and surplus labour interleaved in the same chair. The classification of the worker as an independent contractor is the wage relation in a legal costume that shifts the costs of reproduction, sickness, age, idle time, from the buyer of labour to its seller. None of these descriptions names a firm, a product, or a statute. All of them are immediately recognisable to any reader of the present economy, and all of them are arguments, because each assigns its phenomenon to a category with a mechanism attached. That is the structural method performing at its best, and examiners reward the performance because it is the thing their questions were built to detect.

The counterweights and complications must be given their due, or the script becomes a lament rather than an analysis. Intense monitoring also produces the data from which unreasonable standards can be challenged. Fragmented work is genuinely preferred by some workers for whom the standard job's rigidity is the greater burden, and the preference is evidence a serious script weighs rather than waves away. Legal systems periodically reclassify the costumes, restoring the wage relation's protections to workers the costume had stripped of them, and each reclassification is a counterweight in operation, the state redrawing the boundary the market eroded. The tradition's own framework expects exactly this contention, since the working day's length and intensity were, in its founding texts, the terrain of a struggle whose outcome no mechanism guarantees. A candidate who presents the boundary as contested ground, with pressure and counterpressure both in view, is faithful to the analysis and to the record at once, a combination markers find difficult to place below the top band.

A specific trap on this topic deserves its warning. Questions invite candidates to pronounce on whether the new forms of work are more or less exploitative than the factory forms they supplement, and the invitation conceals the second distinction. Exploitation's rate turns on the division of the day between necessary and surplus labour, and the new forms alter that division through several channels at once: unpaid availability lengthens the day's effective span, intensity raises the value produced within it, the shifting of reproduction costs lowers the wage's real content. A script can reason through the channels, conclude that the pressures run predominantly one way, and attach the defeater that a demonstrated shortening of effective hours or a rise in the real social wage would supply. What it cannot do is settle the comparison with an impression of harshness. Harshness is a quality of experience; the rate is a quantity of labour time, and the candidate who keeps the two apart on a question designed to blur them has passed the question's hidden examination.

## Services, intangibles, and the measurability questions

A family of questions presses the candidate on whether an economy dominated by services and intangible assets has outgrown the categories of an analysis built on material production. The questions sound technical and are usually marked generously for candidates who slow them down. Begin with the service question. Labour that produces a service rather than an object was never an embarrassment to the analysis, whose own texts treat transport, performance, and instruction as productive when exchanged against capital, the useful effect being the commodity even though it perishes in the act of delivery. The growth of services therefore does not, by itself, touch the categories. What it touches is measurement: output that consists in effects, in care, in attention, in experience, resists the productivity statistics built for countable objects, and a candidate can earn strong credit by separating the category question, which the analysis answers comfortably, from the measurement question, which is genuinely hard for every school of economics and not only for this one.

The intangibles question cuts deeper and deserves the slower treatment. Where the valuable asset is a design, a reputation, a body of data, a network's assembled membership, the candidate meets the claim that value now springs from knowledge rather than labour, and the claim should be met with the classification discipline rather than with indignation. Knowledge embodied in an asset yields income to the asset's owner through exclusion, since knowledge itself, once released, can be used by all at no cost, and must be fenced by law or secrecy before it will pay. Income from a fenced and costlessly reproducible asset is, by the three questions of the rent test, a claim on value produced elsewhere, scaled by the traffic across the fence. The labour that produced the knowledge was real labour, often vast in quantity, but the income's continuing flow is not payment for that labour repeated; it is a toll on the labour of everyone who must pass the fence to produce at all. Stated so, the intangibles economy does not refute the categories. It extends the territory of one of them, rent, to a scale the classical treatment could not have imagined, and the candidate who argues the extension, while conceding the measurement difficulties honestly, has written the answer the question was hoping for.

Measurement deserves its own paragraph in the candidate's preparation, because the honest concession is a scoring asset here. The theory's central quantities, value and surplus value, are not directly observable in any economy, material or otherwise; they are inferred through the forms in which they appear, prices, profits, wages, rents. Critics press this as a fatal defect, and the candidate should know the two available replies and their respective strengths. The first reply observes that every mature science traffics in quantities known through their effects, and that the demand for direct observation, applied consistently, would dissolve most of the critic's own economics as well. The second reply, stronger in the examination room, concedes the inference and relocates the test: the analysis is assessed by the tendencies it identifies and the mechanisms it exposes, which are observable in the record's directions, rather than by the direct audit of its magnitudes. That relocation is the tendency doctrine doing defensive work, and the candidate who performs it has connected two parts of this guide in a way that markers notice and reward, because the connection shows the doctrine functioning as a system rather than as a list of tips.

A neighbouring question asks whether the consumer has replaced the worker as the economy's central figure, since identities are formed in consumption and politics organised around prices and amenities rather than wages and conditions. The structural answer grants the phenomenon and disputes the inference. Consumption is the final act of the circuit, not a replacement for its productive acts, and the consumer's celebrated sovereignty is exercised with a budget whose size and security are settled in the labour market the consumer supposedly transcends. The same person is worker and consumer in sequence, and an analysis that begins from production is analysing the sequence at the point where its terms are set. Candidates can add the subtler point that the organisation of consumption itself has become a field of accumulation, with attention harvested and behaviour shaped as inputs to production processes elsewhere, so that the consumer's leisure is increasingly an annex of the working day rather than its opposite. The verdict, as ever, should be delivered claim by claim, with the defeater attached: evidence that living standards had decoupled durably from position in the labour market would genuinely trouble the analysis, and its absence is a fact the candidate may note without triumph.

## Reading the literature without inheriting its expiry dates

Candidates do not meet this material only in the examination hall. They meet it in textbooks, in revision summaries, in lectures, in the commentary of the press, and each of those sources has its own relation to time, which the candidate should understand in order to read safely. The textbook is the most respectable source and, on this topic, the most quietly dangerous, because its examples were selected for vividness at the moment of writing and have been ageing ever since, while its authority discourages the student from noticing the ageing. The candidate should read textbooks for their analysis, their definitions, their maps of the disputes, and should treat every example in them as a specimen in a museum: instructive about the kind of thing it is, silent about the state of the world outside the glass. The revision summary is worse, because compression strips the qualifications first, leaving the figures and the labels, the two most perishable elements, as the summary's entire content. A candidate who revises from summaries of this topic is memorising expiry dates.

Lectures occupy a middle position. A lecturer's examples are chosen for a room and a moment, and the lecturer, if competent, flags their status as illustrations. The candidate's notes, taken at speed, rarely preserve the flags, and the illustration hardens in the notebook into a fact of the course. The discipline is to annotate: when an example is given, the notebook should record the mechanism it illustrated in the margin, since the mechanism is what the examination can use and the example is what it cannot. Candidates who build this habit find their notebooks transformed from chronicles into kits, and the transformation is the revision method of this guide operating at the point of first contact with the material rather than at the point of last-minute repair.

The press and its digital successors are the richest source of phenomena and the poorest source of analysis, and the candidate should read them as a field naturalist reads a habitat: for specimens, not for conclusions. Every development reported can be converted, by the candidate's own operations, into examination capital: classified by the income test, parsed by the tendency grammar, decomposed by composition, located on the scorecard. The conversion is the skill, and practising it on the week's reading is the most enjoyable drill this guide prescribes, because it turns the flood of the present from a threat into a training ground. A hundred developments converted into structural notes give their converter a possession of the topic that no summary can confer, and owns it in the only tense the examination respects, the tense of mechanisms.

One source remains, and it is the one candidates underuse most: the questions themselves, gathered from past papers and from the specimen materials of their courses. Read as a body, the questions disclose the examiners' standing interests with a clarity no syllabus statement matches. The same five shapes recur, the same distinctions are probed, the same traps are laid, because the examiners are examining a defined body of analysis and their ingenuity operates within its borders. A candidate who has read five years of questions on this material knows, in outline, the sixth year's, and can say which matrix row each past question belongs to and which operation its marking rewarded. That knowledge is not a shortcut around understanding. It is understanding, of the assessment as an institution, and the candidate who possesses it walks into the hall with the peculiar calm of someone who has seen the exam before, because in every way that matters, the candidate has.

## Worked contrast: two openings on a single question

Doctrine is retained better from contrast than from precept, so this section sets two openings to the same question side by side in prose and reads them as a marker would. The question, of the kind set constantly, asks whether the growth of income from privately owned digital channels shows that the economy has entered a new phase beyond capitalism. The first opening begins by naming the largest channel owners, recites the scale of their revenues in round and unsourced magnitudes, calls the development unprecedented, and promises to show that production no longer matters. Read as a marker reads, that opening has spent its most valuable lines on three exposures and a borrowed conclusion. The revenues will have moved; the superlative invites the marker's private list of precedents; the promise announces a thesis the script must now defend at its maximum strength, including the part, production no longer mattering, that the evidence will not carry. Nothing in the opening can be credited as analysis, because no operation has been performed in it. It is a trailer for a film the marker has seen before, and the marker settles, with a sigh the candidate will never hear, into the posture appropriate to the middle bands.

The second opening begins by fixing the category. Income drawn from ownership of a channel that others have no choice but to use is, in the vocabulary of the analysis, a drawing right over value that production elsewhere supplied, since the channel's owner is paid for access rather than for organising the production the access serves. The opening then states the question that follows from the category: whether the growth of such claims marks a change of mode itself or a change in the distribution of its product, and it announces the answer the script will defend, that the growth is real, that its basis in exclusion makes it rent in structure, and that a redistribution toward bottleneck owners, however dramatic, leaves the foundation it draws upon exactly where it stood. Every sentence in that opening performs an operation. The category is fixed, the question is sharpened, the verdict is previewed at a defensible strength, and the marker has been given a contract against which every later page can be credited. The marker settles, with the opposite sigh, into the reading posture the top band deserves, and the posture has been earned before the argument has properly begun.

The contrast generalises beyond openings, and candidates should perform it on whole paragraphs until the difference becomes a sensation in the hand. The weak paragraph accumulates; the strong paragraph operates. The weak paragraph's sentences could be rearranged without loss, because their order is the order of the candidate's reading; the strong paragraph's sentences stand in an order of dependence, category before application, mechanism before evidence, concession before verdict, so that rearranging them would break the reasoning. A candidate can test any paragraph of practice writing with that single question, could these sentences change places without damage, and the answer diagnoses the paragraph more reliably than any impression of quality. Where the answer is yes, the paragraph is a summary wearing the dress of an argument, and the repair is not to polish it but to rebuild it around an operation.

There is a second contrast worth rehearsing, between two conclusions to the same answer, because conclusions are where the defeater discipline shows its value most nakedly. The weak conclusion gathers the script's themes into a warm peroration, declares the analysis more relevant than ever, and ends on a cadence. It has added no information and taken one risk, since its superlative invites disagreement it cannot answer. The strong conclusion renders its verdicts one claim at a time, in the proportions the argument earned, and then writes the sentence this guide has made the signature of the top band: the development whose arrival would overturn the verdict, specified concretely enough to be recognised when it came. The strong conclusion is shorter than the weak one and worth more, which is the pattern of this entire doctrine: the operations are economical, and their economy is part of their persuasiveness, because a marker can feel the difference between a script that spends words to fill a requirement and a script that spends them to perform work.

Candidates sometimes ask whether this manner of writing can be learned by someone whose natural style runs the other way, discursive, narrative, rich in instance. The answer is yes, and the route is the one this guide has laid out: the operations are learnable as operations, practised in drills, until they become the path of least resistance for the hand. Style is not the obstacle candidates imagine. The discursive writer who installs the distinctions writes discursively about mechanisms instead of about events, and the narrative gift, redirected, becomes an asset, since a mechanism narrated well, pressure building, counterweight engaging, resultant emerging, is the most readable form an analytical paragraph can take. What the doctrine forbids is not a style but a substitution, and every style can honour the prohibition.

## The last weeks and the final hour: a candidate's calendar

The guide closes its practical teaching with a calendar, because doctrine that is not scheduled is doctrine that evaporates. In the final weeks of preparation, the candidate's work divides into three streams that should run in parallel rather than in sequence. The first stream is the kit: the distinction cards, the position cards, the matrix rows, reproduced from memory at the start of every session, with any card that falters promoted to that session's drill. The second stream is the timed answer, written whole, under the clock, at a frequency the candidate's other subjects permit but never less than the frequency that keeps the five movements warm, and marked afterward against the error catalogue by the candidate's own hand before any teacher sees it, since self-marking against a catalogue is where the catalogue is learned. The third stream is the conversion drill: one development from the candidate's reading each day, classified, parsed, decomposed, and filed as a structural note of three sentences, a stream that costs minutes and keeps the operations exercised on fresh material, which is the precise condition the hall will impose.

The balance among the streams matters. A candidate who runs only the first stream arrives with a kit never tested under load. Running only the second stream practises errors at full length and calls the practice revision. Running only the third stream accumulates agility without architecture. The three together produce the candidate this guide has been describing throughout: equipped with a small and exact apparatus, practised in its execution at speed, and habituated to meeting the present economy as material for operations rather than as a spectacle to be memorised. Teachers constructing a revision programme for a cohort can adopt the three streams wholesale, assigning the kit as homework, the timed answers as classwork, and the conversions as the standing opening exercise of every session, five minutes in which the room turns the week's developments into structural notes aloud.

The final hour before the paper has its own small doctrine, and it is mostly a doctrine of refusals. Refuse the corridor summary, the classmate's compressed account of a topic, which arrives stripped of every qualification and pitched at the pitch of panic. Refuse the last-minute development, the development a fellow candidate announces as certain to appear, since the announcement is a specimen of the dating error in its social form. Refuse the re-reading of notes, which produces the sensation of knowledge at the exact moment when the sensation is least reliable. Accept instead the reproduction of the kit, once, slowly, in the candidate's own hand or under the candidate's own breath: the three distinctions, the matrix's five rows, the attribution formula, the defeater's shape. Then walk into the hall with the operations warm and the mind uncluttered, and spend the reading minutes on the four planning lines, and write the answer the preparation has built. The examination on this material is, in the end, a kind and predictable instrument. It asks whether the candidate can think about a moving economy with a steady apparatus. Everything in this guide has been an attempt to put that apparatus into the candidate's hand, and the hand, once practised, does not forget.

## Households, reproduction, and the social wage

A set of questions that candidates often meet unprepared concerns the work that makes work possible: the bearing and raising of children, the care of the sick and the old, the cooking, cleaning, and emotional maintenance through which labour power is daily and generationally renewed. The tradition's category for the whole field is the reproduction of labour power, and its founding insight is that the wage buys the worker's capacity without directly paying for most of the labour that produces and sustains that capacity. Households perform the renewal, overwhelmingly through the unpaid work of women, and the economy of paid production rests on that unpaid foundation as surely as a building rests on ground it did not purchase. A candidate who can state the relation cleanly possesses the key to an entire family of questions, about wages, about gender, about the welfare state, about demographic change, that examiners across sociology and politics set with increasing confidence that the material discriminates well between prepared and unprepared scripts.

The structural treatment runs through mechanisms, as this guide's doctrine requires. The value of labour power includes the means of subsistence for the worker and the worker's replacements, the children who are the next generation of sellers, so the wage's adequacy is always a question about a household and not about an individual. Where the household's unpaid labour is withdrawn or commodified, the costs reappear in the paid economy, as purchased childcare, prepared food, commercial care, and the wage must stretch or the state must provide. The social wage, the services and transfers workers receive outside the pay packet, is therefore not a gift appended to the wage relation but a contested component of the total terms on which labour power is reproduced, and its expansion and contraction across the decades is a distributional record to be read with the same discipline as the compensation share: direction, duration, composition, and the mechanisms, fiscal pressure, organised labour's strength, the reserve's size, that move it.

Questions on this terrain attract a characteristic error, which a prepared writer should be able to name and refuse. The error is to treat the household as a refuge outside the economy, a private sphere where the analysis stops at the door. On the analysis, the household is inside the circuit, performing a function the circuit cannot perform for itself, and the boundary between paid and unpaid labour is a frontier that capital and the state continually renegotiate, pushing tasks across it in whichever direction cheapens reproduction at the moment. The opposite error also circulates, the claim that unpaid household labour produces surplus value in the strict sense, and the candidate should handle it with the category discipline: the household's labour produces use-values and reproduces the commodity labour power, but it is not exchanged against capital in a production process, and stretching the category of productive labour to cover it dissolves the distinction the analysis needs elsewhere. The strongest literature in this field, which the candidate should attribute rather than summarise anonymously, holds both truths at once: the work is indispensable to accumulation, and its indispensability does not make it value-producing in the technical sense. Holding two truths that polemic prefers to separate is, once again, the performance the highest band exists to certify.

Demographic questions belong to this family and are increasingly set: what follows for the analysis when populations age, when birth rates fall below replacement, when the ratio of workers to dependants shifts across a whole society. The structural answer identifies the pressures without prophesying their resolution. A shrinking inflow of young sellers tightens the labour market and strengthens labour's hand, other things equal; a growing burden of dependants raises the share of the social product that reproduction claims, pressing on profits, on wages, or on the state's finances, with the distribution of the pressure settled politically; migration becomes the system's standing answer to local shortages, importing labour power whose reproduction costs were borne by other households in other economies, a transfer the world-market analysis can state precisely. Each pressure has its counterweights, automation against scarcity, later retirement against dependency, pronatal policy against decline, and the candidate's verdict should be a weighing, delivered with the observation that would change it, exactly as the defeater discipline prescribes for every other topic in this guide.

## Housing and the city: ground rent in modern dress

Urban questions give the candidate a chance to watch the oldest category in this guide operate in its original element. Ground rent was built for land, and the city is where land's scarcity is manufactured most intensely, by the concentration of employment, amenity, and connection in places that cannot be extended. The price of access to those places, paid as housing costs by workers and as site costs by businesses, is a claim on incomes produced elsewhere in the system, and its long rise relative to the wages of the workers who must live within reach of their employment is a distributional fact with mechanisms a candidate can state without quoting a single price. Agglomeration concentrates opportunity; opportunity bids up the ground beneath it; the owners of the ground collect a growing share of the product of everyone who needs to stand on it. The founder's category, differential rent arising from advantages of position, describes the process with an accuracy that startles candidates who meet it first in the urban context, and examiners know the startling effect and set questions to harvest it.

The examination value of the urban material lies in the disputes it stages. One position treats housing as a consumption good whose price follows incomes and planning scarcity, a matter for supply policy and nothing deeper. The structural position replies that the scarcity is not a policy accident but a product of the same concentration that drives accumulation, that the landowner's claim is a deduction from wages and profits alike, and that the politics of housing, the defence of asset values by those who hold them against the needs of those who must rent access, is a class politics conducted in the vocabulary of neighbourhood. A candidate should stage that dispute fairly, concede the supply mechanism's reality, and then ask the analytical question the dispute turns on: who captures the value that agglomeration creates, the producers who create it by gathering, or the owners of the ground they gather on? That question, posed cleanly, is worth more than any chronicle of prices, and it has the structural virtue of remaining the right question however prices move.

The city also supplies the clearest illustration in the whole topic of a counterweight's cost. Where policy has suppressed the landowner's claim, through public housing held outside the market, through rent regulation, through land held in common, the suppression shows up as a subsidy to wages, since workers can reproduce themselves near their work at lower cost, and as a deduction from the rent fund, which is why such policies are contested with a ferocity that purely technical accounts of housing cannot explain. The candidate who reads housing politics as a fight over a fund, between the owners of ground, the employers who benefit when reproduction is cheap, and the workers who pay the claim from their wages, possesses an interpretation that organises a century of urban conflict without naming a single estate, statute, or city administration. Examiners in geography and politics reward that organisation directly, because it is the form in which their disciplines' own best work on the city is written.

## Nature and the candidate: the metabolic questions

Environmental questions have moved from the margins of these papers toward their centre, and they test the candidate's structural discipline in a fresh way, because the material tempts scripts toward apocalypse, which is the prophecy error in a green costume. The tradition's own entry point is the concept of metabolism: production as an exchange of matter and energy between human labour and the rest of nature, organised under capitalism by the same imperative that organises everything else, the expansion of value. From that entry point the analysis generates its characteristic claims. The drive to cheapen commodities externalises costs onto air, water, soil, and climate wherever property and regulation permit, because a cost successfully externalised is a cost the individual capital does not bear. The spatial separation of production from its waste, and of extraction from consumption, breaks the cycles through which pre-capitalist metabolisms returned matter to its sources, a rift the founder himself described in the agriculture of his century. The system's time horizon, set by the turnover of capital and the competition of capitals, discounts consequences that arrive beyond the horizon, so that even perfectly informed capitals underinvest in averting harms they collectively foresee.

Candidates should notice what kind of claims these are. Each is a mechanism, each generates a tendency, each meets counterweights the candidate must weigh: regulation that forces costs back onto the balance sheet, technologies that cheapen the clean method below the dirty one, the political mobilisation of the populations who bear the externalised harms, the occasional alignment of a fraction of capital with the transition because its assets sit on the other side. The environmental question, handled structurally, is therefore not a referendum on the planet's future, which no candidate can deliver, but an assessment of pressures and counterpressures, identical in grammar to every other assessment in this guide. The candidate who writes it so demonstrates that the doctrine is a general instrument and not a set of topic-specific tricks, and examiners, who set environmental questions partly to discover whether a candidate's method travels, reward the demonstration accordingly.

Two traps guard this topic. The first is the dating trap in its most seductive contemporary form: the environmental script stuffed with the magnitudes of the crisis, concentrations, temperatures, tallies of disaster, every one of them a figure the candidate cannot source and the marker can correct. The discipline holds without exception. Direction and duration carry the argument: harms accumulating across the whole period of industrial expansion, costs displaced systematically toward those least able to resist them, counterweights erected after disasters and eroded in the calm that follows. The second trap is the mirror of the first, a script so abstract that the physical world disappears from it, leaving only the value form talking to itself. The corrective is to keep the material substrate in view: soils deplete, atmospheres accumulate, bodies sicken, in processes that obey natural laws indifferent to the categories, and the analysis earns its keep precisely by explaining why a social form systematically produces those physical outcomes. A paragraph that holds the value form and the depleted soil in a single sentence has achieved the topic's characteristic excellence, and candidates should practise writing such paragraphs until the conjunction feels natural.

## Definitions worth holding exactly

Underneath every operation this guide teaches sits a small stock of definitions, and the candidate's command of the whole apparatus depends on those definitions being exact rather than approximate. Approximation is invisible in conversation and fatal in scripts, because the distinctions are built from the definitions and inherit their slack. This section states the stock in the form candidates should be able to reproduce, not as a glossary to be copied into answers, definitions earn nothing recited, but as the fixed points from which the guide's moves are made.

Labour power is the capacity to work, sold by its owner for a definite period, and what the capacity is worth equals the worth of the subsistence goods needed to maintain its owner and to raise the owner's replacements, at the standard a given society at a given stage treats as necessary. The inclusion of the historical and moral element in that standard is not a vagueness to apologise for; it is the point at which class struggle enters the definition itself, since the standard is fought over. Surplus value is the value added by the worker beyond what that labour power is worth during the working day, appropriated by the buyer of the capacity. Constant capital is the portion of capital laid out on means of production, whose value reappears in the product unchanged in magnitude. Variable capital is the portion laid out on labour power, whose use creates more value than it costs, the difference being the surplus. The rate of exploitation compares the surplus with the variable capital; the organic composition compares the constant with the variable; the rate of profit compares the surplus with the whole capital advanced, which is why the same surplus yields different ratios in different mouths and why the candidate who confuses the ratios has confused the theory.

Two further definitions govern the contemporary topics specifically. The reserve army of labour is the population of workers surplus to capital's immediate needs, generated by accumulation itself as machinery and reorganisation expel labour faster than expansion absorbs it, and its function in the analysis is disciplinary: its existence presses on the wages and conditions of the employed, which is why its size relative to employment matters more than the count of its members at any moment. Fictitious capital, already treated in the finance section, is the capitalised claim on future revenue, and its definition should be held with the same precision: a title whose price is formed by capitalising an expected income at the going rate of interest, tradable independently of the revenue's source, and therefore capable of a career of its own until the revenue is called upon to validate it.

Candidates sometimes resist definitional drill as pedantry beneath the dignity of analysis. The resistance misjudges where analysis lives. Every distinction in this guide is a definition with its sleeves rolled up: the labour share distinction is the definition of two ratios refusing to be merged, the rent distinction is the definition of a payment's basis refusing to be read off its size, the tendency distinction is the definition of a law's logical form refusing to be graded as a prediction. A candidate whose definitions are exact finds the distinctions easy, because they are the definitions applied. A candidate whose definitions are approximate must memorise the distinctions as separate facts and will drop them, one by one, under the hall's pressure, in exactly the order the marking scheme punishes. The drill is therefore not preliminary to the real work. It is the real work, in its most concentrated form, and the final week's revision should return to it daily, briefly, the way musicians return to scales.

## The standing objections, answered at their strongest

Evaluation on this material frequently takes the form of objections, and the candidate's preparation should include the standing ones, each stated as its holders state it and answered without caricature. The first objection is the objection from marginalism: value, the critic says, is determined by subjective valuation and scarcity at the margin, prices form without any labour substance beneath them, and the theory's central category is a metaphysical survival. The candidate should give the objection its full force, including its genuine explanatory victories in the theory of price formation, before locating the division of territory. The labour theory was never primarily a device for predicting individual prices; it is an account of the social relations that price formation presupposes, of why the products of private labours confront one another as values at all, and of where the surplus that profits distribute originates. Whether that division of territory is a legitimate defence or a retreat is itself arguable, and the strongest scripts argue it, noting that a theory may be demoted from a claim about prices to a claim about relations and survive the demotion with its contemporary applications, rent, distribution, tendency, intact.

The second objection is the objection from entrepreneurship and risk: profit, the critic says, is the reward of innovation, judgement, and the bearing of uncertainty, earned by the capitalist's contribution rather than extracted from the worker's day. The analysis's reply distinguishes the functions the objection bundles. Superintendence and coordination are labour, and when the owner performs them the owner earns a wage of management, which the theory counts as such. Innovation's temporary advantage is real and is treated in the theory as a surplus profit, competed away as the innovation generalises, which is a prediction the record of every industry illustrates. Risk, finally, is borne with capital that must have come from somewhere, and the existence of a reward for bearing it does not settle the prior question of the fund's origin. A candidate who unbundles the objection has answered it more completely than a candidate who denies its phenomena, and the unbundling is another instance of the guide's master operation: separate the strands, classify each, and let the verdict inherit the separation.

The third objection is the objection from the consumer economy, that production no longer rules social life because wants are manufactured, identities are chosen in markets for symbols, and the worker as producer has been displaced by the shopper as citizen. Its strongest form adds that the theory's materialism cannot digest an economy whose commanding heights are meanings. The reply grants the manufacture of wants and asks who manufactures them, with what labour, in whose interest, and observes that the symbol industries are industries, employing wage labour to produce commodities whose use is signification, a fact the analysis accommodates without strain since a use-value was never required to be a lump of matter. The displacement claim then reduces to a question about where the terms of life are set, and the structural answer from the services section applies: the shopper's budget and time are fixed by the labour market the shopper supposedly transcends. Candidates should end the exchange with the concession the objection has earned, that the analysis of ideology and desire needs the supplements later thinkers built for it, since conceding the supplement's necessity costs nothing and demonstrates the weighing the bands reward.

The fourth objection is the objection from history's verdict, the claim that the twentieth century's experiments settle the contemporary questions in advance. On this material the objection is a phrasing trap as much as an argument, and the tendency doctrine supplies the handling. The analysis of capitalism is one object; the record of states that ruled in its author's name is another; conclusions about the first cannot be read off the second without an argument connecting them, and the candidate should ask for the argument rather than accept the transfer. Where the connection is argued seriously, through the theory of the state or the analysis of planning, the candidate engages it seriously, on the mechanisms. Where it is asserted as a verdict that ends discussion, the candidate notes, politely, that the examination rewards discussion, and proceeds to have it. That composure, neither defensive nor dismissive, is the tone the top band sounds like, and it can be practised like everything else in this guide.

## A note on tone: writing about opponents, allies, and the founder

Scripts have a tone, and markers read it as evidence. On this material three tonal failures recur, and writers should learn to catch them in their own prose during the final scan. The first is reverence: the founder treated as an authority whose statements settle questions, citations offered as verdicts. The tradition's own method forbids the posture, since its claims stand on mechanisms that any reader may inspect, and a script that argues from the founder's say-so has abandoned the method while praising it. Quote sparingly, argue constantly, and let every quotation be short enough to be examined rather than long enough to be leaned on. The second failure is contempt: opponents summarised in their weakest form, critics dismissed by motive, the mainstream treated as a conspiracy of the comfortable. Contempt is expensive because it forfeits the concession, and the concession is where evaluation lives; a script that has never granted an opponent a true sentence cannot be believed when it grants its own side one. The third failure is the jeremiad, the script as lament for a world going wrong, in which the analysis serves as a vocabulary for sorrow. Sorrow is not an operation. The marker's tariff pays for classification, mechanism, weighing, and verdict, and a script in any of the three failed tones is spending its words on something the tariff does not list.

The tone that earns is harder to describe than the failures, because it is mostly the absence of noise: level, exact, curious about the opposing case, willing to be persuaded in the open where the evidence persuades. Candidates can cultivate it by a simple revision practice. Take a paragraph of one's own that feels satisfying and interrogate the satisfaction: if the pleasure came from a verdict's force, rewrite the paragraph so the force comes from a mechanism's clarity; if it came from an opponent's discomfort, rewrite it granting the opponent the strongest sentence available. The rewritten paragraphs are less thrilling and worth more, and after a dozen such exercises the hand begins to produce the level tone unbidden, because it has learned where the marks actually sit. Teachers can accelerate the learning by reading two paragraphs aloud in class, one in each tone, on the same material, and letting the room feel which one it trusts. Trust is the marker's currency before it is the examiner's, and scripts that earn trust earn the generous reading in which ambiguities resolve in the candidate's favour and partial operations are credited as wholes.

## Migration and the world reserve

Few topics expose the difference between structural and journalistic writing as sharply as migration, because the journalistic treatment is organised entirely around episodes: a route opened or closed, a policy announced, a number crossing a border. The structural treatment begins from the labour market's geography. Labour power is sold where its price, adjusted for the costs and risks of moving, exceeds what it fetches where its owner stands, and the world market therefore generates standing flows from economies where the reserve is vast and wages low toward economies where accumulation is hungry for hands. Capital moves toward labour and labour moves toward capital in the same epoch, often along the same corridors in opposite directions at once, and grasping that the two movements are a single adjustment, the world market equalising the conditions of exploitation across its territory while never completing the equalisation, possesses the frame into which every migration question fits.

The frame generates the examinable disputes. Employers in receiving economies gain a workforce whose reproduction was paid for elsewhere, a subsidy from the sending households and states that no ledger records, and they gain it in a form that disciplines the resident workforce, since the newcomers' willingness to accept the going terms enlarges the effective reserve behind every wage bargain. Sending economies lose the educated fraction of a generation and receive back remittances that can exceed any aid programme, a flow that supports consumption while hollowing the productive base the consumption depends on. Resident workers experience the arrival as competition in the housing queue and the labour exchange simultaneously, and the politics of that experience, the attribution of structurally produced scarcity to the visible newcomer, is itself a mechanism the tradition analysed under the heading of the reserve's divisions: the employed against the unemployed, the settled against the arriving, each fragment's grievance real and each fragment's diagnosis purchasable cheaply from those who benefit by the fragments' quarrelling. A candidate who can run that whole circuit, subsidy, discipline, remittance, division, in four paragraphs has written the migration answer at the standard the topic deserves, and has done it without a route, a vessel, a border, or a count appearing anywhere in the script.

The counterweights belong in the answer as surely as the pressures. States ration entry, and the rationing creates the categories, legal and illegal, skilled and unskilled, whose boundaries are themselves objects of struggle. Migrants organise, settle, acquire rights, and cease to function as a reserve in the second generation, which is the system's own history repeating: every established working class was once the arriving fragment whose presence was said to dissolve the established one's standards. Sending economies sometimes convert the outflow into leverage, training for export as a deliberate strategy, or recall their diasporas when accumulation at home quickens. The verdict a candidate renders should therefore be about the persistence of the flows' generating conditions rather than about any epoch's volume: while the world market's wage gradients stand as they stand, the pressure to move stands, and policy decides the flows' legality and misery far more readily than their existence. That verdict, with its defeater attached, a durable narrowing of the gradients themselves, is the strongest sentence available on the topic, and it is a sentence no chronicle of crossings could have produced.

## Knowledge work and the professional strata

Questions about the growing weight of professional, technical, and managerial employment test the class category in its most contested modern application, and the candidate's handling should begin, as the sociology treatment prescribed, with the definition. A stratum defined by selling its labour power stands inside the working class by the relational test, whatever its income, its autonomy, or its self-image. A stratum that owns a scarce credential and rents the scarcity stands in a more ambiguous position, since part of its income behaves like a toll on exclusion, the guild's ancient rent in modern dress. A stratum that directs the labour of others on behalf of capital performs the function of capital while receiving a salary, and its income frequently includes a share of the surplus it helps extract, paid as the price of its loyalty and its vigilance. Most actual professional employment blends all three positions in proportions that vary by occupation and by epoch, and the candidate who can describe the blend, rather than assign the whole stratum to a single box, has answered the question that examiners are actually asking when they ask whether a new middle class has dissolved the old polarisation.

The dispute in the literature should be staged with its real stakes visible. One side argues that knowledge has become the decisive productive asset, that its owners form a class with interests distinct from both capital and labour, and that the politics of the coming order will be that class's politics. The other side replies that the credential is a wasting asset unless its scarcity is policed, that most knowledge work is being routinised and cheapened by the same machinery that routinised manual work before it, and that the professional's apparent independence lasts exactly as long as the employer's need. The candidate's weighing can draw on the tendency grammar: the pressure toward routinisation operates wherever the work can be codified, the counterweight operates wherever judgement, responsibility, and trust resist codification, and the map of the professions is being redrawn along precisely that frontier. A verdict that locates the frontier, and names the development that would move it decisively, the codification of judgement itself or its demonstrated impossibility, converts a speculative question into an analytical one.

There is a temptation on this topic that the guide's figures discipline exists to block, and it should be named for completeness. Candidates arrive with the shares of employment by sector memorised, the great migration from field to factory to office rendered as three numbers, and they deploy the numbers as though the numbers were the argument. The structural version keeps the migration and drops the magnitudes: employment's centre of gravity has moved across the sectors over the long run of the system's history, and the movement matters for the analysis not because of its size but because of its consequences, the recomposition of the working class's skills, organisations, and politics at each stage. The candidate who argues consequences from the movement, rather than exhibiting the movement as a trophy, has understood what evidence is for. That understanding, displayed on the topic where the trophy habit is most entrenched, is worth more than the same understanding displayed where it comes easily, and examiners set the topic partly to collect exactly that premium.

## Competition, monopoly, and the oldest contemporary question

Whether competition has been superseded by monopoly is the oldest question in this entire field, older than most of the syllabus, and it returns in every generation's dress: trusts, cartels, conglomerates, platforms. The candidate benefits from knowing that the question's longevity is itself a datum. Each era's observers announce that concentration has reached its final form, and each announcement is followed by a reorganisation nobody announced, because the process has no final form: concentration proceeds by absorbing capitals, competition reappears inside the concentrated structures as rivalry among giants, new fields open at the margin where small capitals swarm, and the giants of one settlement become the prey of the next. The tradition's mature position, which the candidate should be able to state, is that monopoly grows out of competition and does not abolish it but transforms its terrain, from price rivalry among many to a contest of scale, finance, and political influence among few, with competition's disciplinary pressure surviving the transformation. A script that states the transformation thesis, and tests it against the present landscape's structure rather than its headlines, answers the question in its strongest available form.

The test should be conducted with the guide's instruments. Ask of any concentrated market what the giants compete with, if not price: with capacity, with standards, with the ownership of channels and complements, with the capture of regulation, with the acquisition of the small firms whose innovations threaten the settlement. Ask where the small capitals persist and why: in the interstices the giants find unprofitable, in the supply chains' lower tiers where margins are thinnest and risk is dumped, in the new fields before consolidation arrives. Ask what the state does, since the modern form of the question is unanswerable without it: the state as referee of combinations, as purchaser whose contracts create giants, as the prize in the contest of influence, as the rescuer whose guarantee underwrites the largest concentrations of risk. Each of those questions is structural, each answerable from the analysis, and together they compose a treatment of monopoly that no list of the largest firms could rival for examinable content.

The verdict pattern for this question deserves explicit statement, because candidates habitually deliver the wrong shape. The wrong shape is a census: competition dead here, alive there, the economy scored like a league table. The right shape is a claim about the mechanism's mutation: the pressure that competition exerts, the compulsion to cheapen or be absorbed, operates undiminished, while its instruments have changed, and the proof of the pressure's survival is the behaviour of the giants themselves, who invest, cheapen, acquire, and lobby with an urgency that no secure monopolist would need. A monopolist genuinely beyond competition could rest. The observed giants cannot, and their inability is the tendency's fingerprint on the present economy. Candidates should attach the defeater in its proper form: the verdict would change if the largest capitals were observed to rest, earning without innovating across a whole cycle, their shares unchallenged and their returns unthreatened, since rest, and not size, is the true signature of competition's death.

## Small capitals and the persistence of the little firm

A related puzzle completes the concentration treatment, and examiners set it to catch candidates whose picture of the tendency is a simple enlargement of everything. Small firms do not disappear. They persist in enormous numbers, they are founded in waves, they employ a large share of the workforce in many economies, and the candidate must explain the persistence with the same analysis that asserts the concentration, or concede that the analysis and the record part company. The explanation is available and elegant. Concentration is a tendency of capital in each line of production, not a law that every line consolidates at the same speed, and whole territories of the economy resist consolidation because their production does not reward scale: services bound to a place and a person, crafts whose value lies in their singularity, the innumerable niches too small to interest a giant. More deeply, the giants themselves manufacture small capitals as a by-product of their organisation, outsourcing the risky, the labour-intensive, and the thin-margined fragments of their chains to nominally independent firms whose independence consists largely in bearing risks the giant declines. The little firm, in that arrangement, is not a survival from an earlier economy. It is a functional organ of the concentrated one, and the candidate who can say so, with the mechanism attached, has resolved the puzzle in the analysis's favour without denying a single fact the question was built on.

The political mythology of the small firm also deserves the candidate's attention, because questions smuggle it in. The little proprietor figures in public argument as the economy's independent citizen, beholden to nobody, and the figure does ideological work: it presents the wage relation as a way station on a road everyone might travel, and it recruits the proprietor's genuine anxieties, about taxation, regulation, and the giants' power, into alliances whose beneficiaries are rarely proprietors. The structural treatment is respectful toward the anxieties and exact about the position. The small owner's income blends a wage for the owner's own labour, often at hours no employee would accept, with a profit on a small capital and sometimes a rent on a location, and the blend's precariousness is the material basis of the politics. A candidate who decomposes the income with the guide's classification has done more for the question than a candidate who either celebrates or mocks the figure, and the decomposition is one more demonstration that the three distinctions are not examination furniture but instruments that cut wherever they are applied.

With that, the topic-by-topic preparation this guide offers is complete, and its shape should now be visible as a whole. Every topic yielded to the same few moves. The distinctions classified, the mechanisms explained, the tendencies were weighed against their counterweights, the evidence was admitted in the forms that survive, and the verdicts were delivered with the terms of their own revision attached. A candidate who has followed the guide across its topics has not learned a dozen topics. The candidate has learned one method twelve times, which is the only way methods are ever learned, and the examination, whatever it asks, will be the thirteenth occasion.

## The fiscal state: taxation, welfare, and the budget as a class document

Politics and economics papers both set questions on the state's finances, and scripts on them divide sharply between those that treat the budget as bookkeeping and those that treat it as a map of forces. The structural reading starts from dependence. A state that owns little of the productive apparatus must fund itself from the incomes accumulation generates, taxing wages, profits, and transactions it does not control, and borrowing against the expectation of their continuation. That dependence disciplines policy before any lobbyist arrives: measures that frighten accumulation shrink the revenue base itself, so the state's room for manoeuvre is set by the confidence of the owners of capital, a constraint operating impersonally through markets rather than through anyone's instructions. Scripts that grasp the impersonality write better politics than scripts that hunt for conspiracies, because the constraint explains outcomes across governments of every declared colour, which is precisely the pattern the questions present for explanation.

Welfare provision, on this reading, is a terrain rather than a gift or a theft. Its conquests register the organised strength of labour at the moments of their winning; its retrenchments register shifts in that strength and in the fiscal weather; its design, universal or means-tested, contributory or residual, shapes the solidarity or the stigma through which the next round of struggle will be fought. The candidate should resist the two sentimental treatments that dominate weaker scripts, welfare as the system's guilty conscience and welfare as a fraud practised on the taxpayer, and instead analyse provision as part of the total wage's social form, fought over because it alters the conditions under which labour power comes to market: a worker whose sickness, age, and unemployment are socially provided for bargains from a different position than a worker one pay packet from destitution. That difference in position is measurable in the only currency this guide permits, direction and consequence, and it connects the fiscal questions to the reserve mechanism, since provision that cushions unemployment softens the reserve's disciplinary edge, which is why the generosity of provision and the tightness of labour markets move through history in each other's company.

Public debt completes the fiscal triad and attracts the most melodramatic scripts, in which borrowing figures as a nation's credit card and default as its destiny. The colder treatment classifies the instruments. Debt is a claim on future revenue, held disproportionately by the propertied, serviced from taxation that falls across the classes, and its sustainability turns on the relation between the rate of interest and the growth of the revenue base, a relation governments manage, manipulate, and occasionally lose control of. Crises of public debt are therefore distributional crises wearing fiscal dress: the question is never whether the claims will be honoured in the abstract, but whose income will be conscripted to honour them, through taxation, through inflation's quiet revision of every nominal claim, or through the austerity that cuts the social wage to feed the coupon. A writer who frames a debt question as a contest over conscription has found its analytical centre, and can then weigh the historical settlements, which classes paid in which episodes of consolidation, with the basis of each comparison announced in the usual way. No figure for any debt, ratio, or deficit is needed at any point, and the absence will not be felt, because the argument was never about the size of the pile. It was about the direction of the transfers the pile organises.

## Arms, war, and the military economy

Questions on military expenditure and war appear in politics and history-adjacent papers, and they tempt scripts toward the simplest theory in the entire field, that wars happen because arms makers profit from them. The tradition's serious treatments are more demanding and more interesting. Military production is, in the categories, production of a peculiar kind: its products are consumed unproductively by the state, destroyed in use or in storage, and the labour they embody returns nothing to the circuit of reproduction. A school within the tradition built a large claim on that peculiarity, arguing that arms spending functions as a counterweight to the falling rate of profit by absorbing surplus capital in output that neither expands the means of production nor raises the workforce's consumption, and the claim, attributed properly and weighed against its critics, is excellent examination material precisely because it forces the candidate to operate the categories rather than recite them. Do the products of the arms sector re-enter reproduction as constant capital? They do not. Do they enter workers' consumption? They do not. Then the sector's expansion changes the composition of the total product in ways the reproduction schemes can be made to display, and the argument about its economic function can proceed on evidence of direction: the sector's growth in the epochs when the counterweight was needed, its sponsorship by the state as purchaser of last resort for the most advanced technologies, its research spillovers into the civilian economy, which complicate any simple verdict.

War itself requires the candidate to keep two levels distinct, and the failure to keep them distinct is the topic's standard error. The level of interest asks what configurations of accumulation, rivalry among capitals organised as states, pressure of surplus capital and surplus population, make periods war-prone. The level of occasion asks what incident ignited a particular conflict. Scripts that answer the second when the question asks the first produce chronicles of sparks in a room whose gas supply goes unexamined. The structural habit inverts the emphasis: the gas supply is the analysis, the spark is an illustration, and the strongest scripts say so in terms, noting that a system generating standing rivalry over markets, materials, and strategic position will find its occasions, while a system without the rivalry lets occasions pass as incidents. That formulation also shields the writer against the deterministic caricature, since pressures toward war, like every tendency in this guide, contend with counterweights, the interdependence that raises war's cost to the capitals themselves, the organised refusal of the populations conscripted to fight, the institutions built to arbitrate rivalry short of battle. A verdict on war's place in the system should therefore be conditional in the usual form, and its defeater is easily stated: a long epoch in which the rivalries intensified while the resort to arms durably declined would show the counterweights mastering the pressure, and the candidate should be willing to say that the record, read by direction, is the arbiter.

## Primary texts: citing the founder without leaning on him

Scripts on contemporary questions occasionally need the founding texts themselves, and the manner of the citation decides whether it earns or embarrasses. The rule of this guide is that a text is cited for a mechanism, never for authority: the passage is summoned because it states the machinery with a precision the candidate wishes to borrow, and the borrowing is acknowledged so the marker can see the candidate knows the difference between using a source and hiding behind one. Three bodies of writing do most of the work for this topic, and the prepared candidate knows what each contains and what each cannot be asked to bear.

The first volume supplies the working day, the machinery chapters, and the general law of accumulation with its reserve army, the load-bearing analyses for every question about work and technology. The second supplies circulation, turnover, and the reproduction schemes, the apparatus behind the fiscal and crisis questions. The third supplies profit's transformation into its surface forms, interest, merchant's profit, and rent, the apparatus behind the finance and urban questions, together with the celebrated chapter on the counteracting influences to the falling tendency of the rate of profit, which candidates should know as the tradition's own certificate that its laws were stated as tendencies from the beginning. Beyond the three volumes sits the unpublished and posthumous material, of which one piece matters supremely for contemporary questions: the fragment on machines in the notebooks of the late eighteen fifties, where the founder sketches a production process so automated that direct labour shrinks to supervision beside a self-moving apparatus, and knowledge, the general intellect embodied in the system of machinery, becomes the decisive productive force. Whole contemporary theories have been built on those pages, and whole refutations too, since the fragment's status, a notebook's exploratory sketch rather than a finished doctrine, is part of what a candidate citing it should acknowledge. The acknowledgement is not pedantry. It is the attribution discipline applied to a text: state what the source is, what it claims, and how much weight its form allows it to bear.

Citation technique in the hall is a small craft with strict economy. Paraphrase the mechanism in the script's own sentences and attach the source in a clause, in the second volume's treatment of circulation, in the fragment's sketch of the automatic workshop. Direct quotation should be rare, short enough to be weighed word by word, and reserved for formulations whose exact wording is itself the evidence, a definition, a distinction, the naming of a tendency as a tendency. A script that quotes at length signals that its author could not digest the passage, and digestion, the conversion of a text into the candidate's own operations, is the whole of what is being assessed. Teachers should enforce the economy in practice marking by a simple rule: every quotation must be followed by the candidate's own sentence doing work the quotation cannot do, or the quotation comes out in revision.

One refinement remains, concerning the founder's journalism and letters, where the dated expectations live. Candidates who know the theoretical core sometimes stumble when a question or a critic produces a confident prediction from the occasional writings and demands the analysis answer for it. The handling was built into the tendency section and needs only to be pointed at its target: occasional writings argue with their moment, under deadline, for effect, and the tradition's own scholars distinguish them from the systematic work without embarrassment. A candidate may make the distinction in one sentence and return to the claims the systematic work actually stakes. The sentence is worth writing because it demonstrates a scholarly discrimination markers prize, between the corpus's centre and its periphery, and because it refuses, once more, to defend what was never claimed in the form the attack requires.

## The shape of the whole: a closing inventory for the candidate

It may help, at the end of a long guide, to walk once around the apparatus and confirm that every part of it is portable. The candidate carries into the hall no library, no chronicle, no armoury of instances. What is carried is a way of meeting questions. Three distinctions, each a sentence long, each with a test attached. A prohibition on figures, with its replacement formula ready. A quarantine procedure for contested labels. A scaffolding routine for comparisons. An attribution habit that turns assertions into objects of judgement. A matrix that converts question types into sequences of moves. A closing discipline that attaches to every verdict the observation that would overturn it. None of these items weighs anything. All of them together decide, on the evidence of the marking criteria across every discipline this material touches, the band in which a script will be placed.

The inventory has a moral as well as a practical side, and the guide will end by stating it plainly. The structural discipline is a form of intellectual honesty adapted to examination conditions. It forbids the candidate to claim knowledge the candidate does not have, of magnitudes unsourced, of developments unread, of cases unexamined, and it replaces the forbidden claims with the knowledge the candidate genuinely can have: how the system works, what its categories distinguish, where its pressures point, what would count against a judgement. An examination is one of the few institutions that pays for honesty in this exact coin, and the candidate who discovers the exchange rate on this topic has learned something that transfers far beyond it. Every subject has its version of the dating error, its figures quoted from memory, its labels borrowed without their disputes. The candidate who has been trained, on the hardest topic, to refuse all three has been trained for the whole of a scholarly life, and the marks, when they come, are merely the receipt.

## Drills: exercises that build the apparatus

Reading about operations and performing them are different educations, and this section exists to close the gap. Each drill below isolates one move from the apparatus, strips it of every other demand, and repeats it until the move costs no deliberation. Work them in short sessions. A drill abandoned halfway through a long sitting teaches less than a drill completed in ten focused minutes, because the faculty being trained is not stamina but automaticity, the conversion of a decision into a reflex. Teachers can lift every drill here directly into class time; solitary candidates can run them against a clock with a notebook, which is all the equipment any of them requires.

The classification drill takes an income described in two or three sentences, drawn from any reading, and puts the three rent questions to it in writing. Where does the payment rest: on exclusion, or on the organisation of work? What sets its size: the traffic across a scarcity, or the labour performed? Whose production supplies the fund it draws from? Ten incomes classified in this way will do more for the first distinction than another reading of its statement, because the borderline cases, the blended enterprises, the tolls disguised as fees, teach the category's edges in a way its centre never can. Keep the classified specimens. A notebook of thirty worked classifications is a revision asset of the first rank, since each entry is a distinction already applied, waiting to be recognised when the hall presents its cousin.

For the distributional drill, take any movement described in a textbook or a lecture, a share rising or falling, a gap widening or narrowing, and write the decomposition before permitting any interpretation. How much of the movement could composition account for, the weights shifting among sectors, regions, contract types? How much survives as change within the parts? Which mechanisms from the analysis address each portion, and which recorded quantity is standing proxy for a theoretical quantity it does not equal? The drill's discipline is the pause it installs: interpretation is forbidden until decomposition is on paper. Writers who internalise the pause stop committing the share inference in the wild, because the inference requires skipping the pause, and the hand has learned to refuse the skip.

The tendency drill begins from a claim clipped out of commentary, any claim that something in the economy is ending, beginning, or becoming inevitable. Parse it into the grammar's four components on paper: the mechanism asserted, the continuity claimed for it, the counterweights acknowledged or conspicuously unacknowledged, the reading of the record proposed. Most clipped claims fail the parse at the second or third component, and writing the failure out, in sentences, is the evaluation the candidate will later perform in the hall at speed. A companion drill reverses the direction: take a tendency from the tradition, state its four components completely, and then write what observation would tell against the pressure's operation. Reversal is the harder exercise and the more valuable, because framing defeaters for one's own side is the skill the naked verdict lacks.

Attribution gets a drill of its own, conducted against a stopwatch. Choose a position from the contemporary dispute and write its card from memory: holder, thesis in one sentence at its true strength, limit in a clause, the opposing holder and the opposing thesis stated just as fairly. Then check the card against the sources and correct it in another colour. The corrections are the learning; a card that emerges unchanged was already known and the minute was spent confirming rather than building. Six cards corrected into accuracy constitute the citable cast this guide prescribes, and the stopwatch matters because the hall will not wait while a thesis is reconstructed from a general impression of a scholar's views.

Comparisons are drilled with scaffolding sprints. Take two periods or two economies named in any question from a past paper and write nothing but the basis: the quantity, the span, the definitions, the composition correction, the scale where the discipline demands it. Five minutes, no conclusions allowed. The sprint feels artificial the first three times and indispensable thereafter, because it separates the scaffolding from the verdict so cleanly that the writer begins to feel a verdict attempted without scaffolding as a physical discomfort, a step taken on a stair that is not there. That discomfort, installed before the examination, is worth more than any single piece of content in this guide.

Two drills address the script as a whole. The first is the rearrangement test, applied to practice paragraphs: copy a paragraph's sentences onto separate slips, shuffle them, and ask whether the paragraph survives. Where it survives, rewrite it until it does not, until each sentence stands where the reasoning needs it. The second is the closing drill: take any practice answer already written and compose, in isolation, the two sentences it should have ended with, verdict and defeater, proportioned to the weighing the answer actually performed. Most first attempts at the closing drill expose a gap between the answer's evidence and its conclusion that the writer had not noticed, and noticing that gap in practice, where it costs nothing, is the entire point.

The final drill is the scan, rehearsed until it runs itself in the last minutes of any timed writing. Its objects are the five intruders this guide has catalogued: a figure, a date, a proper name doing a category's work, a label worn without its dispute, a verdict standing without its defeater. Practise by seeding a page of one's own older prose with a dozen intruders, or better, by hunting them unseeded in a peer's page, and timing the hunt. Ninety seconds a page is the standard to reach. Writers who reach it stop fearing the final minutes of the examination, which become a verification they trust rather than a ceremony they perform, and the calm that replaces the fear is visible in the handwriting of the scripts themselves.

None of these drills requires talent, a library, or a teacher's presence, though a teacher's presence improves several of them. What they require is repetition distributed across the revision period, which is why this section sits near the guide's end rather than its beginning: the doctrine had to be stated before it could be practised, but the stating was never the education. The education is the repetitions. Running each drill a dozen times confers ownership of the apparatus in the only sense the examination recognises, as a set of things the hand does before the mind has finished deciding to do them, and on a topic where the material moves daily and the questions never repeat themselves exactly, that ownership is the whole of preparation.

## Study groups and peer marking: the social machinery of revision

Preparation for this material is usually imagined as a solitary activity, a desk, a lamp, a stack of summaries. The imagination undersells the social machinery available, because several of the apparatus's operations are learned faster in company than alone. Peer marking heads the list. Reading another writer's script against the error catalogue teaches the catalogue at a speed solitary self-marking cannot match, for a simple reason: the errors are visible in a stranger's prose in a way they refuse to be in one's own, where every sentence arrives with its author's intentions attached and the intentions masquerade as the text. Swap scripts weekly with one partner, mark against the eight failures and nothing else, and return the scripts with the failures numbered rather than discussed. Within a month both writers will feel the pen hesitate at the exact moments the catalogue describes, which is the hesitation the whole method exists to install.

Disputation is the second social instrument, and it suits this topic particularly, because the contemporary disputes are genuine and their sides are occupied by serious scholars. Assign the positions around a table, monopoly against competition, dependence against incorporation, the new formation against the old categories in new dress, and require each speaker to open by stating the assigned side's strongest evidence before advancing any of it. The requirement sounds ceremonial and works mechanically: nobody can state an opposing case at full strength weekly without acquiring the concession habit, and the concession habit, once acquired socially, transfers to the solitary script without further instruction. Groups should rotate the assignments without regard to private conviction. Conviction is not the group's business; fluency in both directions is.

A third instrument is the shared conversion file. Groups preparing together can keep a common notebook, physical or otherwise, in which each member files the week's structural notes, developments from the reading classified, parsed, and decomposed in three sentences apiece. The file grows into a collective kit richer than any member's private notes, and its entries, written for readers who were not present at the reading, are forced into the self-sufficiency that examination prose requires. Entries should be challenged as a matter of routine: a classification disputed, a basis demanded, a defeater requested and supplied. The challenges are not pedantry among friends. They are the examination's questions arriving early, in a room where they can still be answered by learning rather than by loss.

Solitary candidates are not excluded from the social machinery's benefits, because most of it can be simulated. Old scripts of one's own, marked after a fortnight's forgetting, read almost like a stranger's, the intentions having faded with the memory of writing. Published model answers, where courses supply them, can be marked against the catalogue with profit, including the uncomfortable discovery that some published answers commit the catalogue's errors in polished prose, a discovery that permanently cures the confusion of fluency with band. Even the disputation can be run alone, by the older method of writing the opposing case first, at full strength, before permitting one's own verdict a paragraph. The method is slower than a table of partners and nearly as effective, and it has the advantage of being available at midnight before the paper, when partners are asleep and the apparatus must run on one pair of hands.

## Plainness: the style the doctrine requires

A word about prose style belongs in this guide, because candidates sometimes suppose that analytical writing should sound elaborate, and elaborate prose is where several of the catalogue's errors hide. Latinity, subordination stacked three deep, nouns turned into processes and processes into destinies: the manner flatters the writer's sense of occasion and taxes the reader's working memory, and a marker reading at speed, late in a session, with a rubric to apply, experiences the tax as a doubt about the writer. Did that sentence classify something, or did it gesture at classification in a fog of abstraction? Where the marker cannot tell, the operation goes uncredited, and the script pays for its music with its marks. Plainness is therefore not a concession to simplicity. It is the style in which operations remain visible, and visibility is the currency this entire guide trades in.

Plainness has rules that can be practised like the rest of the apparatus. Prefer the verb to the noun it hides inside: write that competition forces capitals to cheapen, not that the imperatives of competitive cheapening exert force. Keep the subject of each sentence findable in its first half, since a marker who must excavate a subject will sometimes excavate wrongly and grade the excavation. Let one sentence carry one operation; the sentence that classifies and weighs and concedes in a single breath performs none of the three where a reader can see it. Shorten quotations until they are evidence rather than upholstery. Read finished paragraphs aloud in revision, not for elegance but for breath: a sentence the writer cannot finish in one breath is a sentence the marker cannot grade in one reading, and the two failures are the same failure wearing different clothes.

None of this forbids rhythm, emphasis, or the occasional sentence built to land. The verdict sentences of a strong script often carry a deliberate weight, and the defeater, placed after them, lands harder in a plain field than it would in an ornate one, the way a bell carries further in still air. What plainness forbids is decoration that competes with the operations for the reader's attention. On a topic whose raw material arrives pre-decorated by journalism, superlatives polished, metaphors minted, drama supplied free of charge, the plain script stands out by contrast as the one piece of writing in the pile that is doing its own work. Markers notice the contrast before they can name it, and the naming, when it comes, is the vocabulary of the top band: controlled, precise, assured. Assured is the word to dwell on. Assurance in prose is not loudness. It is the quiet produced when every sentence knows its function, and no writer whose sentences lack functions can counterfeit it.

## After the examination: keeping the apparatus in working order

Guides of this kind usually end at the examination door, as though the apparatus were a costume to be returned with the gown. That ending would betray the doctrine the guide has taught, because the operations were never examination property. They are instruments for thinking about a moving economy, and the examination was only the occasion that forced their acquisition under discipline. What happens to them afterward is worth a short section, since most candidates, left to drift, will let the instruments rust within a year, and the rusting is a waste of the one durable asset the whole preparation produced. The instances memorised for other topics will fade on schedule and nobody will mourn them. The apparatus is worth keeping oiled.

Keeping it oiled costs very little, which is the good news buried in this coda. The conversion habit is the whole maintenance regime: one development a week, taken from whatever reading the graduate is doing anyway, sorted by the income questions, read through the tendency grammar, placed on the scorecard, and filed in three sentences. Ten minutes. The habit preserves every operation at working temperature, because each conversion exercises classification, mechanism, counterweight, and verdict in miniature, and it compounds quietly: a file of fifty conversions is a private journal of the economy's movement written in a grammar that does not expire, readable years later with profit, unlike the diaries of impressions that graduates sometimes keep and cannot bear to reopen. Teachers who adopt the habit for themselves will find it changes their lessons, since the week's conversion is the freshest teaching material they own, already analysed, already safe from the dating that ruins the anecdote they were about to tell.

For those proceeding to research, the apparatus scales. The distinctions become the first questions put to any new literature: what does this school take rent to be, what does it do with the distributional record, are its laws tendencies or forecasts in disguise? The comparison scaffolding becomes the methods section's conscience. The defeater becomes the honest paragraph in the discussion, the one referees trust, where the author states what evidence would sink the argument and reports whether any of it is visible on the horizon. Researchers are sometimes surprised to discover how much of scholarly quality control is the examination apparatus wearing formal dress. They should not be. The examination was designed, at its best, as an apprenticeship for exactly that control, and the contemporary capitalism paper, with its moving subject and its traps for the unwary, is the apprenticeship's most honest exercise.

For those leaving study altogether, the apparatus leaves a residue of a different kind: an immunity. The graduate who has internalised the doctrine reads the public argument about the economy with a set of reflexes the argument's authors did not bargain for. Announcements of new eras trigger the classification question before the excitement. Statistics quoted in headlines trigger the scaffolding questions before the alarm. Prophecies of collapse or of perpetual plenty trigger the tendency parse before the fear or the euphoria. The immunity is not cynicism, which refuses all claims alike, but discrimination, which prices each claim by the operations it has performed. A citizenry harder to stampede is not the examination board's stated purpose, but it is among the better by-products of its work, and the candidate who carries the apparatus out of the hall has been paid for the preparation twice: once in marks, and once in a way of reading the world that the world cannot easily take back.

There is also a collective dimension worth recording before the guide closes. Cohorts that prepared together, swapping scripts and disputing positions across a table, tend to keep a version of the table in existence afterward: the argument continued in correspondence, the conversion file maintained by whoever cares most, the standing joke of the defeater demanded in ordinary conversation whenever a verdict is pronounced too confidently. Such survivals look like nostalgia and function as infrastructure. An apparatus practised socially decays more slowly than one practised alone, because the other members notice the rust first, a borrowed label slipping into an essay, a figure quoted without its scaffolding in a report, and the noticing is delivered as teasing rather than as a marking scheme, which makes it easier to accept and harder to forget. Graduates who can arrange even a vestige of the table, a yearly exchange of one structural note apiece, will find the apparatus still serviceable a decade on, when the syllabus that occasioned it has been revised beyond recognition.

That last observation returns the guide to its beginning, and the circle is worth closing deliberately. The subject of these pages moves; the papers change; the labels fashionable in one revision season are period pieces by the next; the figures in every textbook are already historical documents. Nothing in the candidate's possession survives except the operations, and the operations survive precisely because they were never about the state of the world at a moment. They were about how to think in the presence of a world that refuses to hold still. Whoever keeps them keeps the only part of the preparation that was ever really theirs, and the examination, for all its clocks and rubrics and numbered booklets, turns out to have been a surprisingly honest broker: it promised to certify understanding, it defined understanding as performance, and on this material, at least, it kept its word.

One further use of the apparatus deserves mention, because candidates discover it late and value it out of proportion to the space it can be given here. The operations transfer sideways, into the other subjects sharing the candidate's timetable, with almost no adaptation. The history essay has its own dating error, the vivid episode asked to carry a structural claim about a century. The literature essay has its own borrowed labels, the critical vocabularies worn as conclusions. The science report has its own figures quoted without their conditions, its own comparisons floating free of their controls. A student who has learned, on the contemporary economy, to ask what would overturn a paragraph, whose claim a sentence is, and what a comparison rests on, will ask those questions everywhere, at first deliberately and then by reflex, and teachers in the other subjects will notice a change in the scripts without knowing its source. This transfer is the deepest justification for the examination's existence that this guide can offer. A topic was set, a doctrine was drilled, a hall was sat in, and what walked out was not information about the topic, most of which would have expired anyway, but a set of questions that make their bearer harder to deceive in any library, any newsroom, any ministry, any boardroom where arguments are traded. The founder whose claims supplied the occasion for all this discipline had a phrase for the difference between knowing a thing and knowing how a thing is known, and the phrase need not be quoted for the difference to be honoured. The apparatus is the difference, carried in the hand.

Keep the kit itself, finally, as a physical reminder. The cards built during revision, distinctions, positions, matrix rows, scaffolding questions, defeater shapes, will yellow in a drawer, and their yellowing is no argument against them, since what they taught has moved into the hand that wrote them. But a candidate who glances at them years later will feel the operations stir in the order the cards impose, and the stirring is a small proof that the learning was real, stored in the only archive that matters. File them without sentimentality. They were tools, and tools are honoured by the work they made possible, not by the shelf they rest on.

And when the next fashionable vocabulary for the economy arrives, as it will, with its own coinages and its own obituaries for the categories, the graduate will know what to do with it: quarantine it, ask whose it is, what it asserts, what it says never changed, and whether the assertion survives the three distinctions. The vocabulary will pass. The questions will remain, and they are the inheritance this guide was written to deliver.

## For students and exam candidates

This layer gathers the guide into instructions a candidate can act on the day the guide is read, organised by the situations candidates actually face. The candidate with a single paper containing this material should treat the three distinctions and the matrix as the whole obligation, drill them to fluency, and let the rest of the guide serve as commentary. The candidate meeting the material in several papers should build the kit once and then spend the marginal effort on the bends: the comparison basis for the geographer, the category definition for the sociologist, the internal critique for the economist, the state debate for the politics candidate, the bounded case exception for the management candidate. The candidate in the civil service and eligibility streams, where the papers reward breadth across the whole tradition as well as depth in its parts, should place this material in the sequence the revision guide prescribes, neither first, where its disciplines lack the substance they organise, nor last, where its perishable reputation tempts neglect, but in the middle of the preparation, once the core analysis is secure and early enough for its operations to be drilled across many practice questions.

Begin with the economics-facing preparation, since it is the most technical. Drill the labour share distinction until its statement costs no thought: the quantities, the category systems, the verbs permitted and forbidden, the decomposition by composition, the distance between the recorded compensation and the value of labour power. Practise it against hostile phrasings, questions that invite the inference the distinction blocks, because the invitation is the examination. Add the reproduction schemata handled as instruments rather than oracles, and the internal critique of the mainstream distribution account, stated at its strongest before it is opposed. A candidate whose economics preparation consists of those three drills, performed repeatedly on fresh questions, is prepared in the only sense the paper recognises.

The sociology-facing preparation centres on the labour process and the class category. Drill the relational definition of class until it can be produced, applied to a fresh occupation, and defended against the imagery of costumes and incomes in a single paragraph. Practise the transformation narrative's conversion into mechanisms: given any story of work's changing character, produce the reserve, the contract, the organisation, the hierarchy through which the change runs. Prepare the segmentation material, the ways the other hierarchies structure the labour market's tiers, as mechanisms with attributions, since the field's literature is named and its names are citable. The candidate who can perform those conversions owns the sociology treatment of this material, because the questions in that setting are, without exception, invitations to perform one of them.

The politics-facing preparation centres on the state and the world market. Drill the two caricatures and their refusal: the neutral arbiter and the simple instrument, each stated, each shown its inadequacy, and the structural account put in their place with its mechanisms named, fiscal dependence, investment competition among states, organised business access. Prepare the named positions in the state debate as attribution cards, since that debate is the setting's citable core. For the international questions, prepare the mechanisms by which the world market ranks its economies, the channels through which value and bargaining power are distributed between centre and periphery, with the dependence dispute as the worked example of a comparison whose bases must be stated by the candidate because the originals left them loose.

The geography-facing preparation centres on scale and unevenness. Drill the basis statement in its geographical costume: quantity, span, definitions, composition, and scale, the fifth element this discipline adds. Practise the generalisation of the specimen place: given any town, district, or corridor, produce the mechanism of which it is an instance before permitting it into an answer. Prepare the spatial fix and the lengthening chain as mechanisms with their evidence characterised by direction, the spread of logistics landscapes, the hollowing of regions whose function in an older geography of production has been withdrawn. The applied preparations, for management and development candidates, follow the bends described earlier in this guide: the management candidate rehearses the bounded exception until its bounds are automatic, and the development candidate rehearses the centre and periphery mechanisms with the same attribution discipline the politics candidate applies to the state.

The kit itself should be assembled with the companion tools, and this is the place to name them plainly. The answer matrix and the distinction cards become revision cards, and the Marxist study notes tool at [VaultBook](https://vaultbook.net/tools/marxism-study-notes.html) is built for exactly that conversion, letting the candidate drill the cards in short sessions throughout the revision weeks, the rhythm at which cards outperform chapters. For candidates in the civil service stream, previous year questions are the honest test of the kit, and the UPSC previous year question explorer at [ReportMedic](https://reportmedic.org/tools/upsc-pyq-explorer.html) lets the candidate work from questions actually set rather than invented ones, running each through the matrix row it belongs to and marking the attempt against the error catalogue. The sequence across the whole revision period, for every stream, is laid out in the [revision guide](/2022/01/15/marxism-revision-guide/), and candidates should take their calendar from it and their doctrine from this guide.

One last instruction belongs to this layer, and it concerns the night before the paper rather than the weeks before it. Do not read about the economy. The temptation to spend the final evening absorbing one more survey of the present, one more digest of developments, is the dating error in its most seductive form, and everything it deposits will be either unusable or a liability. Spend the evening reproducing the kit: the distinctions from memory, the matrix from memory, the attribution cards, the four planning lines against one unseen question, answered in plan only. Then stop. Walking in with the drills still warm and the head clear is the last useful thing available, while walking in stuffed with the week's developments spends the night arming the opposition.

## For teachers and seminar leaders

The teacher's version of this guide's doctrine is a claim about instruction: the structural answer is teachable, and it is teachable faster than the content it organises, provided the teaching aims at operations from the first lesson. A course on this material should therefore be sequenced unnaturally, by the standards of survey teaching. Open with the distinctions, before the contemporary content they will be applied to, and let the students meet each phenomenon of the present economy for the first time through a distinction that classifies it. The student who learns about platform income by asking the three rent questions has learned the income and the method in one act. The student who learns about it from a survey and meets the distinction weeks later has two things to learn and an unlearning to perform between them. Teachers who have tried both sequences will recognise the difference in the scripts their students produce, and the difference is the whole argument of this guide restated as pedagogy.

Marking practice scripts is the teacher's most powerful instrument here, and it should be used in a specific way. Have the students mark each other's answers against the error catalogue, in class, with the catalogue projected and the scripts anonymised. The exercise teaches in an hour what lectures teach in a term, because the errors are far easier to see in another's prose than in one's own, and the student who has circled the share inference in a peer's paragraph will feel the pen hesitate over the same move in the student's own next script. Follow it with the reverse exercise: take a mid-band script and rewrite its three weakest paragraphs structurally, as a class, watching the band rise as the operations are installed. The structural-answer principle transfers, and teachers should say so explicitly to their students: every contemporary topic they will ever teach, in any of the disciplines gathered here, is subject to the same dating hazard and rewards the same discipline, so the student is not learning a trick for one topic but the house method for all of them.

Teachers also carry a responsibility the candidates cannot discharge for themselves, which is the identification of the genuine case requirement where it exists. No syllabus particulars are stated in these pages and no paper's conventions are named; the teacher knows the documentation of the teacher's own course, and should tell the students plainly, before the revision period begins, whether their papers are of the kind that credit named cases and where that credit stops. Ambiguity on this point is the single most expensive confusion a cohort can carry into the hall, because it sets the guide's doctrine and the course's practice apparently at war, and students resolve apparent wars by abandoning doctrine. A five-minute ruling at the start of term, this paper rewards structure and the case requirement does not arise, or this paper's applied question requires a case and here is its bound, aligns the two authorities and lets the students revise in peace.

Seminar leaders working with smaller groups can go further, into the live performance of the operations. Set a tendency claim from the literature and have the group parse its grammar aloud. Take a distributional movement described in a textbook and decompose it by composition before anyone is allowed to interpret it. Stage the state debate with the positions assigned and the attributions policed. These exercises have a vivacity that survey seminars lack, because the operations generate genuine disagreement, about classifications, about bases, about the strength of counterweights, and the disagreement is the subject matter thinking about itself. The seminar that argues for an hour about whether an income is rent has taught more political economy than the seminar that received an hour of findings, and it has taught it in the form the examination will demand.

## For researchers, historians, and journalists

The researcher layer of this guide is brief by design, because the researcher's needs differ from the candidate's mainly in one respect: the researcher must know where the guide's disciplines come from and where they stop. The three distinctions are not examination folklore. The rent distinction descends from the third volume's treatment of ground rent and its generalisation to monopoly incomes; the labour share distinction is an application of the elementary point that the categories of distribution statistics and the categories of the value theory are built for different questions; the tendency distinction is a reading of the founder's own method, in which laws are stated as tendencies precisely because their operation is mediated. A researcher using this material should follow each distinction into the primary texts rather than resting in the guide's formulations, which are compressed for the hall. The compression is honest, but it is compression, and the scholarly questions, about the coherence of the rent generalisation, about the measurability of the value categories, about the logical status of tendency laws, are live questions in the literature that the guide deliberately does not settle.

For the historian, the guide's central doctrine has a methodological echo worth stating. The structural answer is, in effect, a refusal to write the history of the present from its headlines, and the historian's craft has long known the same refusal under other names: the distinction between the event and the structure, between the conjuncture and the long duration. The candidate's discipline and the historian's meet at the point where both decline to let the most visible stratum of the evidence dictate the analysis. Researchers addressing the present economy historically may find the matrix's columns a serviceable checklist for their own drafts, since the trap column names, in examination language, the vices of journalistic historiography: the borrowed label, the floating comparison, the magnitude without a mechanism.

For the journalist, the guide is offered as an adversary worth reading. The news values that organise reporting, novelty, magnitude, personality, are precisely the values this guide instructs candidates to exclude from analysis, and the exclusion is not a judgement on journalism's own purposes. It is a reminder that analysis and reportage are different crafts with different evidences, and that the candidate's error, which the journalist's professional habits accidentally encourage, is to confuse them. A researcher or journalist who keeps the crafts distinct will find the guide's catalogue of errors a fair description of what happens when reportage is graded as analysis, which is a thing that happens in examination halls every year and in public argument every day.

## Question verbs and the traps they carry

A short section on the wording of questions will repay the candidate who reads it, because on this material the verb of the question is a reliable guide to the operation being purchased, and misreading the verb wastes the whole answer regardless of its content. Assess and evaluate purchase weighing: named claims, concessions, a verdict with a defeater, and a script that merely describes, however accurately, has not answered them. Analyse purchases decomposition: the bundle opened, the mechanisms separated, the parts named before their relations are traced. Discuss purchases the staged dispute: positions attributed, the genuine point of division found, a judgement delivered on that point. Compare purchases the stated basis, without which the verb has not been obeyed at all. Account for purchases mechanism: the phenomenon traced to the pressures that produce it, with chronology explicitly demoted. To what extent purchases the differentiated verdict, the map rather than the flag, with the proportions of agreement and disagreement argued rather than averaged. Candidates should perform the verb's translation during the planning minutes, writing beside the four planning lines the operation the verb demands, because the translation takes seconds and its omission is the commonest cause of the well-informed script that answers a question adjacent to the one set.

The traps carried by phrasing deserve the same attention. A question that begins by asserting a transformation, the rise of the knowledge economy, the death of the factory, the arrival of a new feudalism, has placed a contested label in the premise, and the candidate's first evaluative act is to examine the premise before building on it. A question that quotes a striking judgement from a named critic has told the candidate which attribution to begin with, and the script should take the hint literally, stating the critic's thesis exactly before weighing it. A question that asks whether the founder would recognise the present economy is a relevance question in fancy dress and should be decomposed like one. A question that asks for the causes of a distributional movement is a mechanisms question wearing the costume of a data question, and the candidate who answers it with mechanisms and a stated basis will outscore the candidate who answers it with the movement's chronology. In each case the phrasing is doing work, and the candidate who reads the work before writing has gained the first paragraph's advantage before the first paragraph is composed.

### How should a candidate handle a question worded around change?

by treating the wording as a claim to be examined rather than a premise to inherit: locate the change asserted, ask which category it belongs to and which mechanism would produce it, state what in the structure persists through it, and only then judge how fundamental the change is, since such questions reward scrutiny of their presuppositions.

There is a final phrasing the candidate will meet and should be armed against, the question that demands a side in the culture war around the founder's name: prophet or relic, vindicated or refuted, the framing in which the analysis is treated as a personality with a reputation. The framing is the prophecy error and the loyalty superstition combined, and the answer to it is the whole doctrine of this guide in one gesture. Decompose the reputation into claims. Weigh the claims. Conclude claim by claim, with defeaters. The candidate who performs that gesture on a question designed to elicit a cheer or a boo has demonstrated the exact independence the examination exists to certify, and markers, whatever their own position in the culture war, are professionals enough to pay for the demonstration. The founder's reputation is not examinable. The founder's claims are, and the candidate's business is with the claims.

## The guide obeying its own rule

It remains to note, because candidates notice and ask, that this guide has been written under the discipline it teaches, and that the fact is itself a piece of instruction. No statistic of growth, distribution, jobs, market dominance, or wage share appears anywhere in its pages. No enterprise is named, no technology is exhibited, no policy is cited, no development is dated. The theorists it invokes are invoked by position rather than paraded by name where the hall would need the name supplied from the candidate's own cards, because the guide's business is the form of attribution rather than the supplying of its content. A reader who knows the present economy intimately will find nothing in these pages to contradict that knowledge and nothing that depends on it, which is the condition the guide prescribes for scripts. A reader who returns to these pages after an interval of years should find them as usable then as now, for the same reason: the mechanisms have been the content, and mechanisms do not keep appointments with obsolescence.

That self-application is worth a moment's reflection, because it answers the deepest version of the thin-answer objection. If writing structurally about the present economy produced thin work, this guide would be thin, and the candidate can test the proposition directly: the guide has discussed rent, distribution, tendencies, the state, class, space, automation, and the organisation of work, at length and with discrimination, while citing no statistic and naming no firm. The discussion was not impoverished by the abstinence. It was made possible by it, since every sentence was free to address the structure rather than to maintain a crumbling scaffolding of instances. The candidate's scripts are offered the same freedom. What feels, in prospect, like a renunciation is, in the writing, a liberation of attention, and candidates discover this the first time they draft an answer under the discipline and find the paragraphs lengthening rather than shortening, because the mind, denied the easy paragraph of instances, does the harder and more rewarding work of analysis.

The doctrine can now be stated in its final compression, the form in which candidates should carry it into the hall. Argue from mechanisms. Classify incomes by the basis of their payment. Never read a share as a rate. Never try a tendency as a forecast. Cite no figure. Borrow no label without its dispute. State every basis. Name every position. Concede at full strength. Attach a defeater to every verdict. Ten instructions, each a sentence, each rehearsed, together constituting the structural answer. The candidate who executes them has not merely prepared for the questions on contemporary capitalism. The candidate has acquired the method by which the tradition itself instructs its students to think about any present, including the ones no syllabus has yet imagined, and the examination, at its best, is simply the occasion on which that acquisition is witnessed and certified.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do you write about contemporary capitalism without dating your answer?

Build every paragraph out of the system's workings instead of its headlines. Say what competition compels, what accumulation does to the size of capitals, how a toll on access is collected, and let happenings enter only as unnamed illustrations of those workings. Give each movement of the economy a direction and a length of run rather than a size. A script assembled this way cannot be ambushed by anything the marker has read since the candidate last opened a book, because nothing inside it wagers on the state of the world at a particular moment. Its claims concern relations and pressures, and relations change slowly enough for an answer to stay honest.

### Q: Which distinctions carry marks on modern capitalism?

Three, and they repay memorisation as exact sentences. First, income from owning something scarce that others must use is a distribution out of value already created in production, so its expansion rearranges who receives the product without replacing the productive order that generates it. Second, the share of national income recorded as employee compensation belongs to the statisticians' categories, while exploitation's rate belongs to the value theory's, and movement in one establishes nothing directly about the other. Third, the analysis asserts pressures, not appointments: a pressure can operate for generations while counterweights hold its outcome back, so its non-arrival at a destination refutes nothing. Scripts that open by deploying whichever of these a question touches tend to be graded as organised from the first paragraph onward.

### Q: How do you handle contested categories in an answer?

Quarantine them. When a term like platform capitalism or technofeudalism appears, in the question or in your own drafting, stop and give it a provenance before letting it do any work: whose coinage it is, what novelty it asserts, what its opponents say never changed, and whether your answer adopts it under that gloss or declines it. Two or three sentences of provenance convert a liability into evidence of command, because markers treat unexamined labels as borrowed conclusions. The same quarantine applies when the examiner's own wording smuggles the label into the question. Answering inside a premise you have not inspected is the one error on this topic that good knowledge cannot compensate for, since every later paragraph inherits the unexamined claim.

### Q: Should you cite figures on contemporary topics?

Leave them out entirely, for the five quantities candidates reach for most: growth, inequality, employment, market share, and the labour share. A remembered number describes the economy as some agency measured it for a period now closed, under definitions you have not inspected, and it will have been revised before your script is read. Markers cannot check it, are trained to distrust it, and award nothing for it that a well-described direction would not earn without risk. Verbal magnitudes are figures in disguise and carry the same penalty: doublings, halvings, and mosts and fews all assert sizes you cannot source. Where scale genuinely matters to an argument, argue it qualitatively, as reach across sectors or depth of reorganisation, and say that this is what you are doing.

### Q: Which theorists should you name on modern capitalism?

Fewer than instinct suggests, each carrying one thesis you can state exactly. Start with the founder's four load-bearing claims for this topic: capitals concentrate as competition proceeds; ground rent and its analogues divide a surplus they do not create; machinery serves the extraction of relative surplus value and the struggle over the day's length; credit and the joint-stock company socialise production while appropriation stays private. Add the living dispute in miniature: the monopoly and finance developers, the scholars of knowledge and access who announce a new formation, and the distributional economists who map the record and quarrel over its causes. Prepare each as a name, a thesis in a single sentence, and the strongest name on the other side. That small cast, precisely handled, covers every question shape this material generates.

### Q: Is it safe to conclude Marx is outdated?

Entirely safe, if the route to the verdict is the assessed one. Break the large reputation into separate claims, test each against the record's directions, give the opposing scholarship its strongest voice before answering it, and let the conclusion inherit the resulting proportions, some claims standing, some needing translation, some gone. Markers reward that procedure identically whichever way the final balance tips, because their criteria describe operations rather than loyalties. Danger lives only in the unexamined verdict: outdated pronounced as a mood, or vindicated pronounced as a tribal loyalty, both of which announce that the weighing was skipped. Finish by naming what would have to happen to move your balance the other way, and the conclusion becomes the safest paragraph in the script.

### Q: How do you state a comparison basis between periods?

Announce the scaffolding before hanging anything on it. Say which relation is under comparison, using the definitions your opening fixed. Say how long the span is and what sort of years stand at its ends, since a boom measured against a slump manufactures its own conclusion. Say what you have done about shifting composition, the sectors growing or shrinking inside the aggregate and dragging its averages with them. Say where the measurements' boundaries are conventional and may have been redrawn between the periods. Five honest sentences of scaffolding do more than preface the comparison; they often discover its answer, because many set questions reverse their verdict depending on the basis chosen, and examiners construct them to do exactly that.

### Q: What are the common mistakes on contemporary capitalism?

Markers meet the same eight failures in rotation. The survey that narrates developments and analyses none. The statistic quoted from memory, unsourced and already revised. The fashionable category worn as the script's own framework without a word about its disputed status. The compensation share treated as a reading of exploitation's rate. The tendency hauled into court as a failed prophecy. Two periods or two economies compared with the scaffolding never announced. Positions floated without holders, so nothing can be weighed. A verdict delivered bare, with no word about what evidence would overturn it. Notice that none of the eight is a failure of knowledge. Each is an operation skipped under time pressure, which is why drills, not further reading, are the cure this guide prescribes.

### Q: Do you need current examples to score well?

No. What scores is the visible performance of analysis, and an anecdote performs none. Inside a script, an instance cannot be checked, sourced, or defended for typicality, so markers file it as recollection and pay it at recollection's rate, when they do not discount it for staleness. A mechanism, by contrast, exposes itself to checking at every sentence, and that exposure is what the higher bands purchase. One qualification stands, and it is narrow. If the wording itself demands an application to a specified case, or your course's documentation confirms that case knowledge is credited on that paper, supply a single illustration, drawn generically, its period openly acknowledged, and set it down after the mechanism it instances has already been established. One is enough because illustration is demonstration, not evidence.

### Q: How do you revise contemporary capitalism efficiently?

Spend the hours on performances, not on re-reading. Assemble a pocket kit first: the three distinctions with the tests that operationalise them, the citable positions with their limits, the matrix's five rows, the replacement for figures, the scaffolding questions for comparisons, the defeater's sentence shape. Then drill against that kit in rotation, one operation per sitting performed on material you have not used before, an income classified cold, a movement decomposed, a verdict framed with its defeater. Choose practice questions by weakness, not comfort, and score each attempt by counting which of the eight standard failures appear in it. Mix in whole answers against the clock often enough that the five movements become handwriting rather than intention. Readiness on this topic is a set of reflexes, and reflexes are built by repetitions, not by familiarity.

### Q: How does the structural answer grade a response on contemporary capitalism?

It sorts scripts by what their sentences do. Sentences that fix a category, run a mechanism, weigh a named position, concede a point at full strength, or qualify a verdict with its overturning condition push the script upward, band by band, because each is an operation the criteria name. Sentences that narrate, decorate, superlativise, or assert without a holder leave the script where it lies, however accurate their contents, because accuracy of storage is the middle band's whole definition. A candidate self-marking with this instrument reads a draft asking one question per paragraph, what operation happened here, and the paragraphs that answer with silence are the ones to rebuild. Used this way the doctrine is a diagnostic before it is a style, and its predictions about a script's fate are uncomfortably reliable.

### Q: What should a student separate between demonstrating understanding and displaying recall?

Separate the verb from the material. Recall's verbs are reproductive: recite, list, recount, summarise. Understanding's verbs are operative: classify, decompose, distinguish, weigh, qualify, overturn. A student can audit preparation by watching which verbs a practice session exercised. An evening spent recounting the economy's features, however fluently, trained reproduction, and reproduction is what the hall least rewards on a topic whose features change. An evening spent classifying unfamiliar incomes, decomposing an unseen movement, or distinguishing two quantities a question has blurred trained the operations every band above the middle is paid for. The separation is not a counsel against knowing things. The operations need material to work on. It is a counsel about what the final form of the knowledge must be: not a store to be emptied but a set of moves to be executed.

### Q: Why do examples date an answer while mechanisms do not?

An instance is a claim with a timestamp hidden inside it. It reports the world as it stood when the candidate encountered the report, and the world declines to stand still for the convenience of scripts. Between the writing and the marking, the firm may falter, the trend may bend, the celebrated arrangement may be abandoned, and the marker reads with that sequel in hand. A mechanism carries no hidden timestamp because it claims something about relations rather than occasions: that competition presses capitals to cheapen, that ownership of a bottleneck collects a toll, that credit stretches expansion beyond its realised base. Sequels illustrate such claims or complicate them; they cannot expire them. Choosing mechanisms over instances is therefore not caution. It is selecting the only kind of assertion whose truth the calendar cannot vote on.

### Q: How does the contemporary answer matrix order a timed answer?

It turns the hour into a sequence decided in advance. First the governing distinction, deployed in the opening beside tight definitions of the terms that will bear weight. Then the mechanism the question is really about, developed in the tradition's vocabulary and credited to its living formulators where they sharpen it. Then whatever the record offers, admitted only as direction sustained over duration, with scaffolding announced wherever two periods or places are set against each other. Then the weighing, opposing positions given their best voice and the concessions recorded honestly. Last the verdict, proportioned to the weighing, with the development that would reverse it stated in the next breath. Candidates who rehearse that order on practice questions stop experiencing the paper as a search and start experiencing it as an execution.

### Q: What separates a structural answer from a summary of events?

Ask what each kind of writing does with a phenomenon. The summary places it in a sequence, before one happening and after another, and calls the sequence an account. The structural answer interrogates it: under which category does it fall, what pressure produced it, which standing tendency does it feed or resist, what would its reversal mean. Sequence is cheap because the world supplies it ready-made; interrogation is the assessed skill because only the candidate can supply it. A practical test while drafting: if a paragraph could be lifted into an answer to a different question without breaking, it is summary, since happenings are portable in a way analysis never is. Paragraphs that belong to exactly one question, because they perform that question's operations on that question's material, are the paragraphs markers reward.
