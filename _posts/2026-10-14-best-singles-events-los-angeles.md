## How to Make the Most of Singles Events in Los Angeles

Arrive early. The first twenty minutes of any mixer or speed dating night are when the room is loosest and introductions are easiest. Book ticketed dating events ahead: organizers balance numbers, and the side that books late is the side that gets waitlisted. For singles-friendly nights, give yourself a small job: join a trivia team that is short a player, take the beginner lesson before the social, or volunteer for the setup shift. A shared task turns small talk into a real conversation. And go twice. The second visit to the same run club, class or trivia night is when familiar faces become actual prospects.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Singles Events in Los Angeles

**What are the best singles events in Los Angeles this week?**
The strongest options in this window are the 10 labeled singles events above, led by speed dating nights and mixers built for introductions. The singles-friendly picks, from trivia to run clubs, suit anyone who prefers meeting people sideways, through an activity.

**What is the difference between a singles event and a singles-friendly event?**
A singles event is organized for dating: speed dating, singles mixers, lock and key parties and matchmaking nights. A singles-friendly event is a general social event, like a trivia night, class or festival, where lots of people attend solo and talking to strangers is part of the format.

**Are singles events in Los Angeles free?**
Some are. Many singles-friendly picks on this list are free, including run clubs, trivia nights and festivals, while ticketed dating events in Los Angeles typically run from about $10 to $50. Every listing shows its price as published by the organizer.

**Do I need to book speed dating in advance?**
Almost always, yes. Speed dating organizers cap and balance attendance, and popular nights sell out days ahead, often for one gender before the other. Use the event link in the listing to book.
