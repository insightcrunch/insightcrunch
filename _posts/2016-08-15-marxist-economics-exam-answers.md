Marxist economics is examined far more often than it is taught well, and the gap between those two facts is where marks are won and lost. What the assessment rewards on this topic is not loyalty and not hostility. It rewards accuracy about what is disputed: a candidate who can state what the first volume of Capital assumes, what the third volume shows, and precisely where the founder's own procedure gives out will outscore a candidate who has memorised a verdict, in whichever direction the verdict points. What it penalises is the overclaim. The answer that announces the labour theory of value as a doctrine of prices, or the transformation problem as either settled or fatal, tells the marker within two sentences that its author has read one source and believed it entire. Everything in this guide follows from that asymmetry. The topic is a discriminator not because it is obscure but because it is contested in print, by named economists, along lines a prepared candidate can reproduce exactly.

The single highest-return habit available to you is the plurality opening. Marxist economics is not one position but a research programme containing rival schools, united by the analysis of capitalism as a historically specific system in which profit originates in production, and divided over the status of value magnitudes, the transformation of values into prices, and the tendency of the rate of profit. An opening sentence that says so converts a recall answer into an analytical one before the marker has finished the first paragraph. The schools have names, and the names do the work: the simultaneist tradition descending from Bortkiewicz, the Sraffian critique associated with Steedman, the New Interpretation built by Duménil and Foley, the temporal single-system interpretation, the value-form readings, and the empirical school that measures embodied labour against market prices. A script that deploys three of those names accurately, and can say what divides them, is playing a different game from the script that writes that Marxists believe something and then proceeds as if belief were the topic.

This guide is about the writing rather than the theory. The theory itself, the systems and the disputes in full, is set out in the series article on the [marxist economics complete guide](/2016/04/01/marxist-economics-complete-guide/), and each topic in the cluster has its own treatment, linked at the points where a candidate needs depth instead of technique. What follows is organised around the five question types that examiners actually set on this material, gathered into the economics answer matrix, and around the small set of errors that cost the most marks. Two neighbouring territories are deliberately left to their own guides: the assessment of crisis theory belongs to the [marxist crisis theory exam answers](/2014/02/15/marxist-crisis-theory-exam-answers/), and general essay technique, the kind that transfers across every subject, belongs to the guide on [how to write a marxism essay](/2021/10/15/how-to-write-a-marxism-essay/). The territory here is narrower and more valuable: the economics questions, the formulations that survive a knowledgeable marker, and the discipline of stating a disputed thing exactly.

## What the assessment actually rewards on Marxist economics

Assessment on this topic operates under a constraint that candidates rarely notice: the marker almost certainly holds a view. The field's disputes are published, long-running, and attached to names, so the person reading your script has read Bortkiewicz or Samuelson or Kliman, has a settled sense of which reconstruction is respectable, and will grade a script that misstates their side as careless and a script that misstates the other side as ignorant. That is the first reason accuracy outranks verdict. The second is structural. Examination bands, whatever the institution, describe operations: the lower bands describe recall and narration, the middle bands describe explanation, and the upper bands describe evaluation conducted on material the candidate has stated correctly. A script cannot reach the evaluative operations on a foundation of misstated material, because the marker stops crediting the argument at the point where the statement of it fails. On most topics this is a mild handicap. On this topic, where the material itself is a century of argument, it is the whole game.

### What does the marker reward in the first paragraph?

Precision about the dispute. A first paragraph that names the question's contested point, signals which schools divide over it, and promises a verdict earned at the end tells the marker the script understands the field. Vague scene setting, biographical preamble, and a verdict announced before any evidence all cost ground.

The first paragraph is therefore a contract. It tells the marker which question is being answered, and on this topic the question is almost never the one printed on the paper in its naive form. A question asking whether the labour theory of value is defensible is asking, in the marker's hearing, whether the candidate knows that the theory exists in several versions with different claims, that some versions were abandoned by their own defenders, and that the respectable dispute concerns which version survives which objection. The opening that names that structure earns the marker's confidence, and confidence is a currency: a marker who trusts a script reads its later compressions charitably, as economy rather than omission. A marker who has been given a slogan in the first paragraph reads the rest of the script as a search for further slogans, and usually finds them.

There is a third reward, quieter than the other two, for the answer that carries one located textual reference. Locating a claim in its chapter, the first chapter of the first volume for the value form, the ninth chapter of the third volume for the transformation, the chapters on reproduction in the second volume for the schemas, does something no summary can do: it proves the candidate has handled the text rather than a digest of the text. Markers mention this in examiners' reports across many subjects, and on this topic it matters doubly because so much of the secondary literature is written at a distance from the volumes themselves. One accurate location, deployed once, outweighs a page of unattributed paraphrase.

The same logic explains what the assessment penalises, and the penalties cluster. It penalises the political answer to an economic question, the script that argues about whether capitalism is just when it was asked how prices of production are formed. It penalises the single-source answer, whose tells are examined in a later section. It penalises the unlocated generalisation, the claim about what Marx thought unattached to any text in which he thought it. And it penalises, most reliably of all, the figure quoted without its measurement convention, a habit this guide singles out for its own protocol, because the numbers in this literature are artefacts of accounting choices and a marker who knows the literature knows it.

## One subject, several schools: the plurality the answer must open with

The plurality is not a courtesy. It is the factual shape of the field since 1907, when Bortkiewicz published his correction of Marx's transformation procedure and thereby founded the genre of repair that has occupied the tradition ever since. From that point the subject forked. One line of work accepted the simultaneous-equation framing, corrected the arithmetic, and asked what of Marx survives the correction; that line runs through Sweezy's influential presentation to the modern simultaneist literature. A second line, descending from Sraffa's reconstruction of the classical surplus approach, pressed the harder question of whether value magnitudes are needed at all once prices and the profit rate can be determined from physical data and the wage. A third line refused the simultaneous framing itself, arguing that Marx valued inputs and outputs at different moments of a sequence and that the famous contradictions are artefacts of freezing that sequence into simultaneity. A fourth rebuilt the theory around a single monetary ratio linking aggregate labour time to aggregate money value added. A fifth returned to the first chapter of Capital and argued that value is a social form before it is a magnitude, so that the whole transformation debate had misconceived its object. A candidate does not need all five lines in every answer. A candidate needs to know that the lines exist, because the question's wording usually selects one of them, and answering along the wrong line is the classic route to a mediocre mark on a well-prepared topic.

### Why does naming the schools change the band of an answer?

Because it converts assertion into analysis. A script that attributes each claim to the school that holds it can then compare, test, and judge, which are the operations the higher bands describe. A script that treats the field as one voice can only repeat it, and repetition is what the lower bands describe.

Consider what each name buys you in practice. Bortkiewicz buys precision about the original problem: his three-department model showed that Marx's two aggregate equalities, total price equals total value and total profit equals total surplus value, cannot in general hold together once inputs are transformed as outputs are. Steedman buys the strongest form of the critique: working from Sraffa, he argued that prices of production and the profit rate can be derived from physical production conditions and the real wage alone, so that value magnitudes are a detour, and that under joint production the detour can even produce incoherent results. The New Interpretation buys a defence that concedes the relative-price field and holds the aggregates, redefining the value of labour power so that a monetary expression for labour time does the connecting work. The temporal single-system interpretation buys a different defence, holding both equalities by valuing each period's inputs at the prices actually paid for them. The value-form reading buys a reframing: the question stops being an accounting puzzle and becomes an inquiry into why social labour appears as the value of things at all. An answer that can spend these currencies in the right places looks, to a marker, like scholarship. An answer that cannot looks like a pamphlet.

Roemer's analytical Marxism deserves a sentence of its own because examiners in politics papers reach for it. Roemer reconstructed exploitation without the labour theory of value, defining it through unequal access to productive assets, and concluded that the moral charge Marxists care about survives the loss of the value theory. Whether that conclusion comforts or alarms the candidate, it is a named position with a named mechanism, and it belongs in any answer that evaluates what the tradition loses if the labour theory falls. The empirical school deserves its sentence too: researchers such as Shaikh, and Cottrell and Cockshott, have measured the labour embodied in commodities against their market prices across whole economies and reported close correspondence, a finding their critics answer by questioning what the correspondence proves. Both sentences are cheap to write and expensive to omit, because each signals that the candidate knows the dispute is alive and empirical as well as textual.

Dependency theory, which the development papers examine, is a branch of this plurality rather than a separate subject, and it has its own internal fork that markers listen for: Frank's development of underdevelopment, in which incorporation into the world market produces backwardness, against Cardoso and Faletto's associated dependent development, in which development and dependency coexist under specific political conditions. The fork matters because the standard evaluation of dependency theory attacks Frank's version and leaves Cardoso's standing, and an answer that knows the difference can make that point itself before the marker makes it against the answer.

## The definition that loses marks, and the one that carries them

Every examiner on this topic has read the losing definition hundreds of times. It says that Marxist economics is the theory that labour creates all value, that capitalists exploit workers by paying them less than they produce, and that this leads to crises and revolution. Each clause is a half-truth arranged to invite an objection the candidate cannot answer. The first clause invites Böhm-Bawerk. The second confuses surplus value with profit, an error examined below. The third substitutes a slogan for the several distinct crisis theories the tradition actually contains. A definition of that shape tells the marker that the candidate's knowledge is a poster, and the script is graded as a poster throughout, its later sophistication discounted in advance.

### What separates value from price in a defensible answer?

Function. Value, in the theory, is a magnitude of socially necessary labour time that regulates exchange behind the back of the producers; price of production is the form competition imposes once profit rates equalise across capitals of different composition. An answer loses marks when it treats the second as a failed copy of the first.

The definition that carries marks is the one this guide opened with, and it is worth learning as a form of words: Marxist economics names a family of rival schools rather than a single doctrine, bound by one shared conviction, that profit is made in production before it is realised in exchange, and split exactly where the examinations probe: over what value magnitudes are, over how values become prices of production, and over whether profitability carries a downward tendency within it. Notice what the form accomplishes. It makes a claim about unity, production as the site of profit's origin, that every school in the programme accepts, so the definition is accurate rather than diplomatic. It then locates the divisions exactly where the literature locates them, so the marker can see the map forming. And it describes capitalism as historically specific, which is the tradition's own founding gesture against an economics of eternal categories, a point comparative answers can later spend. A definition is not decoration. On this topic it is the first piece of evidence about whether the candidate has read the dispute or only its dust jacket.

Beneath the definition sits the distinction the whole cluster turns on, and a defensible answer states it early and keeps it clean. Value and price are not two estimates of the same quantity, one rough and one exact. They belong to different levels of the analysis. Value belongs to the level at which Marx asks what regulates exchange ratios in the aggregate and where the surplus that profit distributes is produced. Price of production belongs to the level at which competition among capitals is allowed to operate, redistributing that surplus so that capitals of equal size earn equal profit whatever their composition. The transformation problem, the subject of the next section, is the question of whether the passage between the levels is coherent. The candidate who grasps that the levels differ can state the problem without panic and evaluate the repairs without partisanship, which is precisely the posture the next sections build.

## The three-sentence transformation, the skill this whole topic examines

If you learn one thing from this guide, learn the namable claim at its centre: the three-sentence transformation. The highest-return preparation on this topic is the ability to state the transformation problem in three sentences, namely the opening volume's working assumption, the third volume's demonstration, and the point at which Marx's own procedure is incomplete, because that statement earns marks directly and immunises the candidate against the overclaiming trap in both directions. It is short enough to write under time pressure, exact enough to satisfy a specialist marker, and neutral enough to serve any verdict the candidate later reaches, since it describes the problem before judging any solution. Candidates treat it as a paragraph to memorise. It is better understood as a measuring instrument: everything else in an answer on this topic is either evidence for one of its clauses or evaluation of the attempts to repair the third.

### How do you explain the transformation problem in an exam?

in three sentences. The first volume assumes commodities exchange at their values. The third volume shows competition equalises profit rates, so prices of production diverge from values. Marx's own procedure transformed outputs but left inputs valued at value, which is incomplete.

Sentence by sentence, the construction is doing specific work. The first sentence establishes the baseline fairly: the opening volume's analysis proceeds as if commodities exchange in proportion to the labour socially necessary for their production, a simplification Marx flags as provisional rather than a prediction about observed prices. The second sentence introduces the complication from Marx's own text, not from a critic: competition among capitals equalises the rate of profit across industries whose organic compositions differ, so capitals of equal size must draw equal profit, and the prices that accomplish this, prices of production, diverge systematically from values. The third sentence locates the defect with surgical precision: in the worked procedure of the third volume's ninth chapter, what each industry sells is repriced in production prices while what it buys, the means of production and the labour power consumed in the making, still stands in the books at value, although those purchases are commodities traded on the very same market. A procedure that prices one side of the account and not the other is incomplete, and every subsequent dispute is a dispute about how to complete it.

### Where exactly does Marx's own procedure break down?

At the inputs. The outputs of each industry are converted into prices of production, but the constant and variable capital consumed in making them is left standing at value, although the same commodities, bought as inputs, must bear prices of production too. Correcting that asymmetry is the whole subsequent literature.

The fuller treatment, with the worked numerical example and the history of the corrections, is in the series article on the [transformation problem explained](/2016/04/15/transformation-problem-explained/), and a candidate who has time should read it before the examination period rather than during it. What the examination itself requires is narrower: the recognition that the two aggregate equalities Marx asserted, that the sum of all prices equals the sum of all values and that the sum of all profit equals the sum of all surplus value, cannot both be preserved under every consistent completion of his procedure. Bortkiewicz's correction preserved the equality of total price and total value by construction of his model's numéraire and let the profit equality go, or could be arranged the other way, but not both at once without further redefinition. That is why the reconstructions divide as they do. Each one chooses which of Marx's claims to hold fixed and rebuilds the surrounding definitions so the choice is coherent. The New Interpretation holds the aggregates by defining the value of labour power through the money wage and its monetary expression. The temporal interpretation holds them by refusing simultaneous valuation, pricing inputs at what was paid and outputs at what they fetch, period by period. The simultaneist tradition holds the price system and lets the value system stand as an intermediate computation. A candidate who can write that paragraph, the choice each school makes and its price, has the transformation question beaten at any level below a specialist paper.

There is a discipline point here that weaker guides miss. The three sentences must be statable without algebra, because in politics and development papers algebra is not expected and in economics papers it is the argument around the algebra that is marked. The candidate who can only reproduce a matrix has a party trick. The candidate who can say, in prose, what the opening volume takes for granted and where the third volume's arithmetic stops, has an understanding, and markers are paid to tell the difference.

## The economics answer matrix and how to read it

The economics answer matrix gathers the five question types into one instrument. Its rows are the questions as examiners set them, stripped of their local wording. Its columns are the decisions a candidate must make before writing: which schools the answer must name to be safe, which formulation to lead with so the statement of the topic is defensible, which evaluation moves the answer into the upper band, and which trap the question has been designed, over years of setting, to catch. Read the matrix horizontally when revising a topic and vertically when planning an answer: the formulation column is a set of opening moves, the trap column a set of epitaphs. The rows are treated at length in the sections that follow, and the matrix is best used as the map of those sections, reconstructed from memory at the start of revision until the five rows and their traps can be written out unaided.

| Question type | Schools to name | Formulation to lead with | Evaluation that lifts the band | The trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Explain the labour theory of value | Marx's own statement, then the classical inheritance from Smith and Ricardo | Value as socially necessary labour time, with the rate of surplus value, s/v, stated as unpaid over paid labour | Distinguish the versions: the embodied labour claim, the regulation claim, and the form claim do different work | Announcing that the theory predicts prices, which the third volume denies |
| Explain the transformation problem | Bortkiewicz for the correction; the New Interpretation and the temporal school for the repairs | The three sentences: the first volume's assumption, the third volume's equalisation, the inputs left at value | Grade each reconstruction by what it preserves and what it surrenders, in the schools' own terms | Declaring the problem solved, or declaring it fatal, in a single flat sentence |
| Assess whether the rate of profit tends to fall | Marx's tendency chapter; Okishio for the technical objection; the temporal school for the reply | The rate of profit as surplus value over the sum of constant and variable capital, s/(c+v), with the composition c/v rising | Separate the tendency claim from the measurement claim, and describe directions rather than citing figures | Quoting a profit rate for some economy and decade as if it settled a theoretical dispute |
| Compare Marxist and mainstream economics | The marginalist tradition stated fairly; Marx's programme stated as a rival, not a refutation | Different questions before different answers: allocation under given preferences against the origin of the surplus | Concede precisely what each programme explains well, then locate the genuine disagreement over profit's source | Caricaturing the mainstream as apologetics, or Marxism as envy, and marking neither |

Two features of the matrix deserve emphasis before the rows are unpacked. The first is that the formulation column never contains a verdict. Each lead formulation is a statement of the topic that a hostile marker and a sympathetic marker would both accept as accurate, because the band is earned in the evaluation column, not smuggled into the exposition. The second is that the trap column is not a list of insults. Each trap is a sentence a real candidate writes every year, usually because a textbook or a lecture slide wrote it first, and the sections below name the traps in their exact verbal form so that you can recognise them in your own drafts during revision, which is the only place they can still be removed cheaply.

## Matrix row one: explaining the labour theory of value

An explanation question on the labour theory of value requires the candidate to hold three claims apart that textbooks habitually fuse. The first claim is a definition of value's substance: the value of a commodity is the labour time socially necessary for its production, labour counted not as any individual happened to spend it but as the average under normal conditions with the prevailing level of skill and intensity. The second claim concerns magnitude and regulation: exchange ratios gravitate around values so defined, in the simplified conditions of the first volume. The third claim, the one the value-form school insists is the real theory, concerns form: in a society of private producers, social labour necessarily appears as the value of things, and the mystery of the commodity, its fetish character, is that a relation between people assumes the form of a relation between objects. An explanation that runs the three together produces the familiar undergraduate fog. An explanation that separates them, and says which one a given critic attacks, is already an upper-band performance, because most objections in the literature strike one claim and leave the others untouched.

### What exactly does the labour theory of value claim?

Three distinct things. That labour is value's substance, measured as socially necessary labour time. That exchange ratios are regulated by those magnitudes under the first volume's simplifying assumptions. And that social labour appears, in this kind of society, in the form of value at all.

Marx's own examples are the safest illustrations an answer can use, precisely because they cannot date. The coat and the linen from the first chapter, twenty yards of linen exchanging against one coat, let a candidate show the value relation being constructed between two commodities rather than asserted. The example of the spinner, the weaver, and the working day divided between the hours that replace the wage and the hours that do not, carries the theory of surplus value in miniature. Candidates reach instinctively for an illustration drawn from the headlines, and the instinct should be resisted for reasons a later section gives in full: an example drawn from the text is defensible in the viva of the marker's mind, while an example drawn from the news invites the marker to argue with the example instead of crediting the answer. The foundation treatment, with the classical inheritance from Smith's deer and beavers onward, stands as the series article on the [labour theory of value explained](/2010/07/01/labour-theory-of-value-explained/), and the explanation row of the matrix is best revised alongside it.

Two distinctions do quiet work in every good explanation and should be made explicitly. Labour power is not labour: what the worker sells is the capacity to work for a period, and what the capitalist consumes is the activity, and the gap between the value of the capacity and the value the activity creates is where surplus value lives. And concrete labour is not abstract labour: tailoring and weaving are unlike activities producing unlike use values, and the theory's claim is that exchange itself performs the abstraction, equating the labours by equating their products. A candidate who writes those two distinctions cleanly has pre-answered the two most common confusions the marker meets in weaker scripts, including the confusion of surplus value with profit that a later section takes up on its own.

## Evaluating the labour theory of value without overclaiming

Evaluation is where the bands separate, and the labour theory offers a fair fight, which is why examiners love it. The objections should be stated at the strength their authors gave them. Böhm-Bawerk's closing contradiction charged that the third volume's prices of production abandon the first volume's law rather than fulfil it, and that a theory cannot both found exchange on value and then distribute profit by a rule indifferent to value. The Sraffian objection, in Steedman's hands, is colder and harder: given the physical data of production and the real wage, prices of production and the profit rate are determined already, so the value system is an unnecessary detour, a set of magnitudes that can be computed but that do no work, and with joint production the detour can even generate negative values beside positive prices, which a magnitude that explains price should not do. The transformation objection, in Bortkiewicz's form, is narrower and purely internal: the procedure as Marx left it is inconsistent, whatever one thinks of the destination.

### Which objection must an answer on the labour theory take most seriously?

The redundancy objection. Internal inconsistency can be repaired by reconstruction, and several repairs exist; the charge that value magnitudes do no work, because prices and the profit rate are already fixed by physical data and the wage, survives every repair and must be met on its own ground.

The defences should be given the same courtesy, each attached to its school. The New Interpretation answers that the detour charge mistakes the theory's purpose: value analysis is not a rival price algorithm but the account of how aggregate living labour is expressed in money, and its central ratio, the monetary expression of labour time, connects the labour accounts to the money accounts by construction rather than by detour. The temporal school answers that the Sraffian determination works only in simultaneity, and that in a sequence, where inputs are bought at prices formed before outputs are sold, the redundancy result does not go through in its simple form. The empirical school changes the venue: embodied labour coefficients, computed from input and output tables, track market prices closely across whole economies, which the defenders read as the regulation claim operating statistically, and which critics answer by noting that any cost-based magnitude, including total costs themselves, correlates with prices for near-accounting reasons. The value-form school declines the venue altogether, holding that the theory was never a price theory and that judging it as one repeats the error the first chapter diagnoses.

A candidate's evaluation earns its band by adjudicating among these, not by summarising them. The strongest scripts identify which version of the claim survives which objection: the strong price-prediction version survives nothing and should be surrendered explicitly, since Marx's third volume surrenders it; the aggregate regulation version survives the internal objection under at least two reconstructions, at stated prices; the redundancy objection stands against every version that claims explanatory priority for values over prices, and the honest answer says so and then says what, if anything, the value analysis still explains that the price analysis does not, which is the distribution of the working day and the origin of the surplus. An evaluation that ends there, with the claim sorted into versions and the verdicts distributed among them, will satisfy a marker of any persuasion, because it has done the thing markers exist to reward: it has found out exactly how much of the theory is true.

## Matrix row two: the transformation problem and the price of each repair

The explanation of the transformation problem has its own section above because the three sentences are the cluster's master key. The evaluation of the problem is a separate skill, and examiners in economics papers test it directly: given that the procedure is incomplete, what follows? The candidate's task is to grade the reconstructions, and grading requires a currency. The currency this guide recommends is preservation and surrender: for each school, state what of Marx it keeps and what of Marx it gives up, in the school's own terms first and the critics' terms second. The simultaneist tradition keeps the corrected price system and surrenders the claim that the two aggregate equalities hold as substantive truths; on its reading they are accounting conventions or approximations, and value becomes an intermediate step that a complete price theory can bypass. The New Interpretation keeps both aggregate equalities and the claim that labour time is expressed in money, and surrenders the idea that the value of labour power is fixed by a bundle of wage goods valued independently, replacing it with the money wage read through the school's monetary ratio; its critics, the temporal school among them, reply that equalities secured by definition have been purchased by emptying them. The temporal single-system interpretation keeps both equalities and the first volume's claim that value and price form one system, and surrenders simultaneity, the assumption that inputs and outputs of the same period bear the same prices; its critics reply that a theory in which values chase prices period by period has made the tail wag the dog, and that its falling rate results depend on valuing stocks at historic prices.

### What do the reconstructions agree on, beneath their quarrels?

More than their polemics suggest. All accept that Marx's worked procedure, as printed, is incomplete at the inputs. All accept that competition equalises profit rates and that prices of production therefore diverge from values industry by industry. And all present themselves as completions of Marx rather than replacements of him.

Notice what this grading method refuses to do. It refuses to ask which school is right in the abstract, because the schools optimise different things: fidelity to the aggregate claims, fidelity to the text's monetary character, fidelity to the logic of simultaneous determination. The examiner who set the question knows this, and the script that announces a winner without stating the criterion reads as a script that has joined a faction rather than studied a dispute. The script that states the criterion, fidelity to text, or empirical usability, or logical economy, and then ranks the schools under it, has answered the question that was actually asked. That is the general form of evaluation on this whole topic, and the transformation row is where to practise it, because there the criteria are unusually visible and the schools unusually candid about their choices.

One more distinction belongs in this section because markers probe for it in the margin of otherwise strong scripts. A reconstruction is not a refutation, and a repair is not a vindication. Bortkiewicz's model is a repair undertaken by a critic; the New Interpretation is a defence that concedes the printed procedure fails; the temporal reading is a defence that concedes the standard simultaneist statement of the problem is fair against the printed procedure. Candidates who describe any of these as having won, or any critic as having been answered once and for all, are claiming a closure the literature itself has not reached in a century, and the marker, who reads the journals in which the argument continues, will mark the claim down as the surest sign of the single-source answer.

## Matrix row three: the falling rate of profit, and the figures you must not cite

The falling rate of profit is the cluster's second great set piece, and it differs from the transformation question in a way candidates should register: it is partly empirical. Marx's own statement, in the third volume's chapters on the tendency, is a mechanism, not a measurement. As accumulation proceeds, the composition of capital rises, more constant capital is set in motion by each worker, and since living labour alone produces surplus value, the mass of surplus measured against the total capital advanced tends downward, pressing the rate of profit down unless counteracting influences intervene. Marx himself listed the counterinfluences in the following chapter: the cheapening of the elements of constant capital, a rising rate of surplus value, foreign trade, the relative surplus population pressing on wages. The theory therefore predicts a tendency working against resistances, and any answer that reports it as a simple forecast of a falling number has already misdescribed its object.

### How should a candidate state the falling rate claim so it survives the Okishio objection?

As a claim about valuation and time. Okishio showed that cost-cutting innovations, simultaneously valued, raise the equilibrium rate unless real wages rise. The temporal reply holds that capital advanced at historic prices can see its rate fall on the same facts. State both fairly, and the claim survives as a dispute, not a slogan.

The Okishio theorem must be given its full force, because it is the most elegant objection in the whole cluster. Okishio proved that if capitalists adopt only techniques that cut costs at prevailing prices and wages, and the real wage is held constant, then the new equilibrium rate of profit cannot fall; it rises or stays level. Viable technical change, judged by the capitalist's own criterion, works against the tendency. The replies divide by school in the now familiar pattern. The temporal school argues that the theorem's simultaneity does the work: value the capital stock at the prices ruling when it was advanced, let product prices fall as productivity rises, and the rate computed on the historic advance can fall while the simultaneous rate rises, so the two camps have been measuring different objects. Other defenders relocate the mechanism, arguing that the tendency operates through the rising value composition only when the cheapening of constant capital is itself accounted, or that Marx's claim concerns the rate on new investment at the margin. Critics of all the replies answer that a tendency which requires a chosen valuation convention to appear has become a claim about accounting. The candidate's duty is not to settle this. It is to state the mechanism, state the theorem, state the valuation reply, and conclude with the accurate summary: the tendency is a theorem under some conventions and false under others, and the dispute is about which convention the theory is committed to.

That conclusion brings the section to the contested figures protocol, which this article treats as content rather than decoration. Instruct yourself as the brief for this topic instructs every candidate: do not cite profit-rate or exploitation figures as evidence. The reason is not squeamishness. A measured rate of profit depends on decisions that different researchers make differently: whether the capital stock is valued at historic cost or replacement cost, whether unproductive labour is counted in the surplus or against it, how the boundary between wages and salaries is drawn, which price index deflates the series. Under one package of decisions a long series for a major economy falls across the postwar decades; under another package the same economy's rate is flat or rising; and both packages are defended in print by competent specialists. A candidate who writes that the rate of profit in some country fell from one figure to another across some period has therefore not cited a fact but joined a measurement convention without knowing it, and a knowledgeable marker will treat the sentence as the tell of a script built on one partisan summary. The defensible formulation describes directions under stated conventions: measured on a replacement-cost basis, the series most often cited tends downward across the middle decades of the twentieth century, while on a historic-cost basis the same movement appears sharper, and the candidate who writes that sentence has converted a liability into a demonstration of method. Describe directions. Name conventions. Never let a number do the work of an argument.

## Matrix row four: comparing Marxist and mainstream economics in an answer

The comparison question is the one candidates most enjoy and most often ruin, because enjoyment licenses caricature. The ruin begins with a false description of one side, usually the mainstream, and a marker who works inside the mainstream tradition, as most economics markers do, will not forgive being told that their discipline exists to apologise for capitalism or that it assumes human beings are calculators. Mainstream economics, stated accurately, is a research programme organised around allocation: given scarce resources and given preferences, how are prices formed such that markets clear, and what properties do the resulting allocations have? Its core instruments are marginal analysis, the theory of choice under constraint, and the equilibrium concept, and its genuine triumphs are real: a rigorous account of price as a signalling device, of substitution at every margin, of the conditions under which decentralised exchange exhausts mutual gains, and a body of applied technique that governments and firms use because parts of it work. Any comparative answer that cannot write that paragraph, in the mainstream's own terms and without a sneer, is not ready to write the next one.

### What does mainstream economics explain well that an answer should concede?

Price formation in particular markets, and substitution. The marginal apparatus answers, with precision, how a price moves when a cost or a taste changes, and how agents reallocate at the margin. Marxist economics has no rival account at that level and gains nothing by pretending otherwise.

The Marxist programme, stated with the same fairness, asks different questions and should be compared at the level of questions before answers. Where the mainstream takes preferences, endowments, and technology as given and studies allocation among them, the Marxist tradition asks where the endowments came from, why labour power is a commodity at all, and what the surplus is and who appropriates it. Its theory of profit is a theory of origin: profit exists because the working day divides into paid and unpaid portions, and the various forms of revenue, profit of enterprise, interest, rent, are distributions of that unpaid portion. Its categories are historical: capital is not a thing but a relation that arose under specific conditions and can perish under others, which is why the tradition insists that its own categories would have puzzled a feudal economist. And its unit of analysis oscillates deliberately between the individual capital and the total social capital, a movement the mainstream, whose aggregation problems are notorious, should recognise as an honest attempt at a difficulty it shares. Stated that way, the comparison becomes precise: two programmes, overlapping in territory, divergent in what they treat as exogenous, in what they take profit to be, and in whether their categories claim universality or historical limits.

Evaluation then has somewhere to stand. The candidate can concede, accurately, that on the determination of a particular price next season the Marxist tradition offers little that a supply and demand analysis does not offer better; that its prediction record, where it has committed to magnitudes, is hostage to the measurement disputes described in the previous section; and that the mainstream's account of incentives and information is a permanent acquisition of economic science. The candidate can then press, accurately, the points where the Marxist questions remain unanswered by the rival programme rather than answered wrongly: the origin of profit as a systematic surplus rather than a reward for a factor, the treatment of unemployment as a functional reserve rather than a friction, the endogenous generation of the crises the mainstream models as external shocks. A comparison built as mutual concession followed by located disagreement will outscore a demolition in either direction, because it demonstrates the one thing the question exists to test: whether the candidate understands both programmes well enough to say what each is for. The series article on [marxist vs mainstream economics](/2016/07/15/marxist-vs-mainstream-economics/) carries the full two-programme treatment, and the comparison row of the matrix should be revised with that article open beside it.

## Matrix row five: dependency theory in development papers

Dependency theory arrives in the examination room by a different door, in development studies and international political economy papers, and it is examined as a theory of the world economy rather than of value. Its core claim, in the strong form given by Frank, is that development and underdevelopment are not stages through which economies pass but products of a single process: the same incorporation into the world market that developed the metropolis underdeveloped the satellite, through the chain of surplus extraction running from the satellite's interior to the metropolitan centre. The historical exhibits are the mining and plantation economies of Latin America, regions that were most tightly bound to the world market in earlier centuries and were left, on Frank's account, most deeply distorted when the binding loosened. Amin extended the claim to the scale of accumulation itself, arguing that accumulation on a world scale systematically blocks autocentric development at the periphery. Cardoso and Faletto, writing from inside the region the theory described, qualified it: dependency and development can coexist, associated dependent development is possible, and the outcomes depend on the political alliances through which incorporation is mediated.

### Which distinction inside dependency theory earns the evaluation marks?

The distinction between Frank's strong thesis and Cardoso's qualified one. The standard objections, stagnation at the periphery, the impossibility of local industrialisation, strike the strong thesis; the qualified thesis predicts dependent industrialisation and survives them. An answer that grades each version separately cannot be caught by either.

The standard evaluation, which candidates should be able to run in both directions, turns on the industrialisation of East Asia in the closing decades of the twentieth century. Economies of the periphery industrialised rapidly and deeply within the world market, which the strong thesis struggles to accommodate and which its defenders answered by pointing to the exceptional geopolitical conditions, the land reforms imposed under occupation or its shadow, and the unusually directive states involved, conditions they argue confirm rather than refute the claim that ordinary incorporation blocks development. The qualified thesis accommodates the cases more easily and predicts their character, industrialisation that is real, dependent on foreign capital and technology, and politically negotiated, which is close to what the record shows. Alongside the case evidence runs a methodological objection, pressed by Brenner among others, that the dependency tradition explains underdevelopment by circulation, by unequal exchange and surplus drain, where Marx's own explanation ran through production relations and the class structures that incorporation created; that objection, fairly stated, is the strongest internal criticism the candidate can deploy, because it comes from within the Marxist tradition the theory claims. The development treatment in full, Emmanuel's unequal exchange included, is given in the series article on [dependency theory explained](/2016/07/01/dependency-theory-explained/).

For the examination, the practical rules of this row are three. Name the version before evaluating the theory, since an unversioned verdict is the row's trap. Use the historical exhibits the literature itself uses, the Latin American mining economies and the East Asian industrialisations, rather than reaching for a present-day case whose facts the marker may know better than you and whose relevance is itself disputable. And keep the theory's claim at its proper level: it is a claim about incorporation and its consequences, not a claim that trade impoverishes or that every peripheral failure is externally caused, and the answers that state the claim at that level are the ones that can evaluate it without either worship or contempt.

## The reproduction schemas: the question candidates describe instead of use

The schemas of the second volume divide the whole social product into two great departments: a first department producing means of production, and a second producing means of consumption. Reproduction is simple when the whole surplus is consumed by its appropriators and each department's output matches the other's requirements exactly; it is expanded when part of the surplus is accumulated, which imposes stricter conditions, since the first department must then supply not only replacement means of production but the additional ones that accumulation demands, and the second must supply the additional consumption goods for the additional workers thereby set in motion. The exchange between the departments is the analytical heart: the portion of the second department's output equal to its constant capital must be exchanged against the portion of the first department's output that represents wages and surplus there, and the equality between those two magnitudes is the balance condition of simple reproduction. Candidates learn this as a diagram. Examiners reward it as an instrument.

### What do the two departments let a candidate show?

Proportionality as a problem. The schemas prove that a capitalist economy reproduces itself only when the departments stand in exact quantitative relations, relations no market signal guarantees in advance. That converts the schemas from a taxonomy into an argument about disproportionality and crisis.

The difference between describing the schemas and using them is the difference between the middle band and the top of this question type. Description recites the departments, the balance condition, and the distinction between simple and expanded reproduction, and stops, having demonstrated that the candidate once read the chapter. Use begins where description ends. A using answer deploys the schemas against a claim: against the doctrine, which Marx attacked through the schemas, that a general glut is impossible because production itself constitutes demand, the schemas show that aggregate demand and aggregate supply can be equal in value while mismatched in material composition, so that reproduction breaks down not for want of value but for want of proportion. A using answer deploys them forward into the tradition: Luxemburg's famous objection, that expanded reproduction leaves the demand for the surplus product unaccounted and must seek it in non-capitalist markets, is an argument conducted entirely inside the schemas, and Tugan-Baranovsky's counter-reading, that the schemas show capitalism capable of reproducing itself on the basis of production for production's sake, is its mirror image. A using answer can even deploy them sideways, noting that the two-department scheme is the ancestor of the input and output tables through which the empirical school measures embodied labour, a lineage that connects the most abstract chapter of the second volume to the most statistical section of the literature.

Planning such an answer under time pressure is a learnable routine. Open with the balance condition stated in words, because it is the one piece of machinery every later move needs. Move immediately to the claim the question is really testing, impossibility of glut, the source of demand for accumulation, or the possibility of crisis from disproportion, and run the schemas against it. Close by grading the schema itself: it abstracts from prices of production, from the credit system, and from foreign trade, and an answer that names its own instrument's limits has performed the evaluative operation the band descriptors ask for. The candidate who treats the schemas as a machine for generating conclusions, rather than a relic to be summarised, will find that the question type is among the most generous in the cluster, because so much of the competition is still describing.

## Surplus value is not profit, and other distinctions that carry marks

Some errors cost marks not because they are exotic but because they are load-bearing: everything built on them tilts. The confusion of surplus value with profit is the chief of these, and the brief for this topic lists it among the misreadings every candidate must unlearn. Surplus value is a magnitude of production: the new value created in the working day beyond the value of labour power, unpaid labour in value form. Profit is a form of appearance: that same surplus, or a share of it after the equalisation the transformation describes, measured against the total capital advanced and attributed, in the capitalist's own accounting and in everyday economic speech, to the capital as a whole rather than to the labour that produced it. The two magnitudes differ in level, in denominator, and in what they explain. An answer that says capitalists make profit by paying workers less than the value they create has gestured at the theory; an answer that distinguishes the creation of surplus value in production from its realisation in circulation and its distribution among industrial profit, interest, and rent has stated it.

### Why does confusing surplus value with profit cost marks out of proportion?

Because the confusion collapses the theory's levels. The transformation problem, the equalisation of profit rates, and the division of surplus among profit, interest, and rent all presuppose that surplus value and profit are different magnitudes. A script that merges them cannot state any of those topics, including the one it was asked about.

Around that central distinction cluster the smaller ones, each worth a sentence in revision and each a frequent casualty. Constant capital transfers its value to the product piecemeal or entire; it creates none, and the candidate who writes that machinery produces value has surrendered the theory's core claim in a subordinate clause. Variable capital is not the wage bill as a sum of money but the value of labour power, and the difference matters wherever the New Interpretation is discussed. The rate of surplus value and the rate of profit move differently because their denominators differ, which is why a rising exploitation rate and a falling profit rate are compatible, a conjunction weaker scripts treat as a contradiction and stronger ones use as an exhibit. Absolute and relative surplus value, the lengthening of the day against the cheapening of wage goods, belong to different parts of the argument and earn different examples. None of these distinctions is difficult. All of them are exactly the sort of thing a marker, reading the fortieth script of the day, uses as a sorting device, because they cannot be faked by fluency and they are either present or absent on the page.

There is a reason this guide gives the distinctions a section rather than a warning. In the economics of the examination hall, cheap accuracy beats expensive brilliance: a script that holds six distinctions cleanly across fifteen pages collects a steady toll of credit that a script with one dazzling paragraph and six confusions cannot match. The distinctions are also the connective tissue of the whole cluster. The transformation problem is a dispute about magnitudes at different levels; the falling rate is a claim about a ratio of those magnitudes; the schemas are a machinery for tracking them between departments. A candidate whose magnitudes are sorted can move among the topics as among rooms of one house, and that mobility, visible to the marker as ease, is what the upper band feels like from the inside.

## The three ratios, in words, and when the paper expects symbols

Candidates ask about formulas for this topic with an anxiety the topic does not justify. Marxist economics, as examined, contains three ratios that matter, and this guide states them in words, which is how they should be stated first in every paper and how they may be stated entirely in most. The rate of surplus value is surplus value divided by variable capital, the unpaid portion of the working day measured against the paid portion, and it is the theory's index of exploitation. The rate of profit is surplus value divided by the whole capital advanced, constant and variable together, the same surplus measured against the entire advance, and the difference between the two ratios is the difference between the theory of production and the theory of distribution in miniature. The organic composition of capital is constant capital divided by variable capital, the ratio of dead to living labour set in motion, and it is the variable whose rise drives the tendency argument of the previous section. Their symbolic forms are given once, inside the answer matrix above, where the falling rate row and the labour theory row carry them; that placement is deliberate, and this section explains the discipline behind it.

### When does an economics paper expect symbols rather than words?

When the question itself is technical. A paper that asks for a derivation, a proof, or a numerical transformation expects the notation of the models it examines. A politics or development paper asks what the magnitudes mean and who disputes them, and there a thicket of symbols reads as evasion rather than rigour.

The distinction by discipline is one of the highest-value pieces of practical knowledge in this guide. In an economics paper, and above all in a history of economic thought or political economy paper with a technical bent, the marker expects the candidate to handle the models: Bortkiewicz's departments, the simultaneous equations in words or symbols, the Okishio result. There the symbolic forms are the native language, and an answer that refuses them looks underprepared. In a politics paper, where this material appears inside questions about ideology, the state, or the critique of capitalism, the same symbols are a liability: the marker is examining whether the candidate understands what the dispute means for the theory's claims about society, and a page of algebra answers a question nobody asked. Development papers sit between the two and closer to the politics end: dependency theory is examined through its claims about incorporation and its historical exhibits, and the value theory enters as background that should be stated in a sentence of words, not derived. The candidate's task is to read the paper, not the topic: the discipline examining you sets the dialect in which your knowledge must be spoken, and the same knowledge, spoken in the wrong dialect, scores as a different and worse answer.

A corollary concerns the derivation questions themselves. Where a derivation is demanded, the marks attach to the steps and their justifications, not to the endpoint, and the candidate who writes the result and omits the reasoning has answered the one part of the question that carries no credit. State what is assumed at each step, say why the step is legitimate, and flag the step where the convention enters, simultaneous valuation, the choice of numéraire, the treatment of the wage, because examiners plant their marks exactly at the joints. That is also why the three-sentence transformation matters even in technical papers: it is the prose skeleton on which the derivation hangs, and the candidate who can state it before deriving has told the marker that the algebra to come is understood rather than transcribed.

## The two formulations that mark an answer as second-hand

Markers develop, over years, a small catalogue of sentences that identify a script's provenance instantly, and on this topic two formulations do most of the identifying. The first is the claim that Marx's theory of value exists to predict prices, usually followed by the objection that a diamond costs more than water or that an old bottle of wine outearns the labour that made it, presented as a discovery. The third volume of Capital is the refutation of the claim in advance: prices of production diverge from values systematically and by design, the divergence is the mechanism by which profit rates equalise, and Marx says so in the chapters his critics quote against him. A candidate who attributes the price-prediction claim to Marx and then defends or attacks it is shadowboxing with a position the text does not hold, and the marker, who knows the text, grades the shadowboxing.

The second formulation comes in twin forms: the transformation problem is fatal, or the transformation problem has been solved. Both are single-source sentences. The fatal version usually descends from a survey that presented Bortkiewicz and Samuelson as the end of the matter, omitting the half-century of reconstruction that followed. The solved version usually descends from a partisan summary that presented one reconstruction, temporal or monetary, as a restoration requiring no further comment. The literature itself supports neither closure. What it supports is the formulation this guide has used throughout: Marx's own procedure is inconsistent; several consistent reconstructions exist; each gives up something Marx appears to claim. That sentence is the password of the topic. It can be written by a candidate who will go on to defend Marx, by one who will go on to bury him, and by one who will suspend judgement, and it earns its writer the marker's trust in all three cases because it is the one statement about the problem that every serious party to the dispute can accept as the ground on which they disagree.

### Which two sentences should never appear in a script on this topic?

That the labour theory of value predicts prices, which the third volume denies in its own chapters. And that the transformation problem is either fatal or solved, in a flat sentence without a school attached. Each announces a single source; each costs more than the line it occupies.

Beneath the two famous formulations sits a family of lesser tells, worth a paragraph because they are so easily unlearned. The phrase that Marxists believe, applied to any proposition the schools dispute among themselves, tells the marker the candidate has not met the plurality. The biography paragraph, Marx's exile, his poverty, the British Museum reading room, offered as an opening, tells the marker the candidate is filling space the question did not allocate. The unattributed critic, the sentence reporting that critics argue or economists have shown, tells the marker the evaluation to come will gesture rather than engage, since a criticism without a critic's name cannot be weighed. And the contemporary illustration, a named company, a named government's policy, a named country's statistics, tells the marker the candidate has mistaken a newspaper for a source, for reasons the next section but one examines. None of these tells is a moral failing. Each is a habit acquired from the way the topic is usually taught, and the purpose of naming them in their verbal form is to make them visible in a draft while the draft can still be changed.

## Evaluation: naming a critic instead of gesturing at criticism

The content an answer must contain, on this topic, can be stated as a short inventory, and this section is the inventory's most important line: an evaluation naming a specific school or critic rather than gesturing at criticism. The gesture is the sentence that says the theory has been criticised, or that many economists reject it, or that it remains controversial. Such sentences are unfalsifiable filler, and markers are trained to read them as the absence of the thing they describe. The named evaluation does the opposite work at the same length. To write that Steedman's redundancy argument shows prices and the profit rate determined without values, and that the New Interpretation answers by denying that price determination was the value theory's office, is to compress a literature into two clauses that a marker can credit clause by clause. Evaluation, in this topic, is a directory: schools down one side, objections across the top, and the candidate's judgement entered in the cells where they meet.

### Why does a named critic earn more than a general gesture at criticism?

Because a name can be checked and a gesture cannot. The marker knows what Steedman argued and can credit a script that states it fairly; a script reporting that economists disagree offers nothing to credit. Named evaluation also disciplines the candidate, who must state the objection at the strength its author gave it.

The discipline cuts both ways, which is its virtue. A candidate who must name the critic must also state the critic's argument in a form the critic would recognise, and that requirement alone prevents the commonest evaluative fraud on this topic, the demolition of a position nobody holds. It also structures the reply: once the objection belongs to Steedman, the reply belongs to the schools that answered Steedman, and the answer acquires a skeleton of attribution that carries it from exposition into judgement without the candidate needing to invent transitions. In the margin of the examination, attribution is what analysis looks like.

Two refinements separate the very best scripts. The first is grading the objections themselves: not all criticisms strike the same version of the theory, and the script that observes that Böhm-Bawerk strikes the strong price version, Bortkiewicz the internal consistency, and Steedman the explanatory necessity has performed a second-order evaluation that markers reward with the band reserved for it. The second refinement is the located concession. Every strong answer on this topic concedes something specific, the price-prediction claim, the difficulty of joint production, the convention-dependence of the tendency, and concedes it at the point where the concession buys the right to hold the neighbouring ground. Concession scattered randomly reads as doubt; concession placed surgically reads as command. The candidate should decide, in revision, what their answer concedes on each row of the matrix, because in the hall the concessions will otherwise be extracted rather than deployed, and extracted concessions cost more.

## The strongest objection to this guide, answered

A candidate reading this guide will eventually ask the question the guide exists to answer, in its personal form: should my answer attack Marxist economics or defend it? Which posture scores? The question is natural and the answer is liberating. Neither posture scores. The band is set by whether the candidate can state what is disputed and why, and the strongest scripts do something more precise than balance: they identify which version of a claim survives which objection, and they distribute their verdict across the versions accordingly. A script that concludes the labour theory fails as a price theory, survives as an aggregate account under two named reconstructions at named costs, and stands unanswered as a theory of the working day's division has not sat on any fence. It has done the most aggressive thing an examinee can do, which is to be exactly right in public about a hard question. A script that cheers or jeers has done the easiest thing, and is graded accordingly, whichever side it took.

### What must be complete before a candidate's verdict earns credit?

The order of operations. Explanation must be complete and fair before allegiance is declared, because a verdict reached on a partial statement convinces nobody who knows the omitted part. The marker reads the explanation as the evidence of competence and the verdict as the evidence of judgement, and grades them in that sequence.

The counter-view deserves its full statement, because it is sometimes correct. Some economics papers, and some parts of some papers, want technical exposition without evaluation: the question instructs the candidate to derive a result, to show how a model works, to trace a mechanism, and an evaluative peroration bolted onto a derivation is not sophistication but noise, wasting the closing lines the marker wanted spent on the final step. The tell is in the command words and the question's construction. Derive, show that, calculate, and trace instruct exposition; assess, evaluate, discuss, and to what extent instruct judgement. A question that names a critic in its stem is asking for engagement with that critic; a question that quotes a formula is asking for its working. Reading the question word by word, which a later section treats as a skill in itself, is how the candidate learns which genre the next hour belongs to, and the guide's insistence on evaluation applies to the evaluative genre, where the band cannot be reached without it, and not as a universal solvent poured over derivations.

There is a deeper version of the same point, and it is the real answer to the candidate's question. Markers do not grade conclusions; they grade the relationship between conclusions and the material presented for them. A hostile conclusion drawn from a fair statement of the temporal school and the New Interpretation is a strong performance, because the hostility has been earned against the best available defence. A friendly conclusion drawn from a caricatured Steedman is a weak one, because the friendliness costs nothing. The candidate who internalises that asymmetry stops asking which side to take and starts asking the only question that matters in the hall: have I stated the other side at the strength its own authors would demand? Get that right, and the side you take care of itself.

## Answer architecture: openings, middles, and earned verdicts

An answer on this topic is built in three movements, and each movement has a craft of its own. The opening movement, treated already in the discussion of the plurality pattern, has one job: to restate the question as the dispute it conceals. The candidate reads the printed question, asks which of the matrix rows it belongs to, which schools contest it, and writes an opening that performs that translation in view of the marker. A question about whether Marx's economics has been refuted becomes, in the opening, a question about which claims, in which versions, have survived which named objections. A question about the falling rate becomes a question about mechanism, theorem, and valuation convention. The translation is not a trick played on the examiner; it is the answer's thesis in embryo, because the verdict at the end will be nothing but the translation, cashed out.

The middle movement is exposition under discipline. Its rule is that every paragraph must be attributable: a claim enters the answer attached to the text that made it or the school that holds it, and leaves the answer only through an objection that also has an owner. Paragraphs in the middle movement therefore have a characteristic shape on this topic. They open with the attributed claim, they state its grounds in the space the claim needs and no more, and they close by registering either the strongest reply or the point at which the claim's own defenders qualify it. A middle built that way cannot drift into the two characteristic failures of the topic's middles, the lecture transcript, which narrates the theory as if nobody had ever objected to it, and the debating society, which objects to claims it has not yet stated.

### Which textual reference earns its place in a short answer?

The transformation chapters of the third volume, cited by their location. Most questions in this cluster reduce, at one remove, to the passage between the first volume's values and the third volume's prices, and a candidate who can locate that passage precisely signals command of the whole architecture in one clause.

The closing movement is the verdict, and its craft is the craft of proportion. A verdict on this topic should be distributed, never global: it should say which version of the central claim survives, which objection remains unanswered, and what the candidate's own weighing of the two produces as a final position. The marker reads the verdict against the middle, checking that every element of it was purchased earlier in the script, and the candidate who has run the middle movement under the attribution discipline will find the verdict almost writes itself, because the weighing has been done incrementally and only the totals remain to be struck. What must never appear in the closing movement is new material, and especially not a new authority: a critic introduced in the final paragraph, unexamined, reads as a name dropped from a height, and forfeits exactly the trust the opening worked to build.

Length discipline belongs to architecture too. In a timed answer the movements should occupy rough proportions the candidate has decided in advance, something like a tenth for the opening translation, seven tenths for the attributed middle, and the remainder for the verdict and its qualifications, and the proportions matter more than their exact values. The commonest structural failure on this topic is the middle that eats the verdict: the candidate, knowing the literature is vast, keeps expounding until the clock ends the script mid-sentence, and the marker is left with a competent summary where an answer was required. The plurality opening protects against this in an unexpected way, because an opening that has named the schools and the criterion has already committed the script to its verdict's shape, and the middle can then be cut to fit a conclusion whose outline exists.

## Examples that work, and examples that date

The brief for this topic fixes a rule that candidates find counterintuitive, and this section exists to explain it: use no contemporary economic example. The rule is not prudery about politics. It is a judgement about how examples behave under examination conditions. An example drawn from the present economy arrives in the script carrying facts the candidate knows partially and the marker may know completely: the statistic that turns out to be revised, the company whose practices are more complicated than the paragraph allows, the policy whose effects are themselves the subject of a literature. The marker, meeting such an example, stops grading the answer and starts auditing the example, and the audit is never to the candidate's advantage. Worse, the example dates the script's knowledge to the news cycle and invites the suspicion, fatal on this topic, that the candidate's economics comes from journalism rather than from the texts the question examines.

### Why are Marx's own examples safer than illustrations drawn from the present economy?

Because they are part of the examined material. The linen and the coat, the spinner's working day, the corn model: the marker cannot dispute the example without disputing the text, and the example's relevance is guaranteed by the theory itself rather than asserted by the candidate.

The examples that work are therefore the literature's own. Marx's coat and linen carry the value relation. His division of the working day carries surplus value. Ricardo's cloth and wine, the classical illustration of comparative costs between England and Portugal, serve the comparison row when the candidate needs to show the classical tradition from which both Marx and the marginalists descended. The two departments of the schemas carry proportionality. The textile industry as Marx himself described it, the factory system of his chapters on machinery, carries the discussion of composition and the working day with the authority of the text. Each of these exhibits is checkable against the set material, each is immune to revision by statisticians, and each lets the candidate spend the examination's minutes on argument rather than on defending a fact. A candidate who needs a whiff of the concrete beyond the texts should take it from the history the literature itself treats as canonical, the Latin American mining economies for dependency, the East Asian industrialisations for its evaluation, because those cases arrive pre-argued, with the objections and replies already mapped by the disputants.

There is a converse point about abstraction. Some candidates, frightened of examples altogether, write answers of pure concept, and those answers plateau in the middle band for the opposite reason: the marker cannot tell whether the candidate's concepts touch ground. The textual example is the resolution of the dilemma, concreteness without exposure. One located exhibit per major claim is the working quota this guide recommends, chosen in revision and rehearsed until the example and the claim arrive together, because in the hall the candidate will not have time to invent exhibits, only to deploy them.

## Reading the question word by word

Examination questions on this topic are compressed, and their compression is information. Every qualifier in the wording is an instruction about scope, and the candidate who reads loosely answers a larger or smaller question than the one set. The discipline is to parse the question before planning the answer, marking the command word, the object, and the limits. Command words were treated in the discussion of the strongest objection: derive and trace demand exposition, assess and discuss demand judgement. The object repays equal attention. A question about the labour theory of value, taken by itself, is not a question about Marxist economics in general, and the candidate who answers it with a survey of the whole programme has spent the hour beside the point. A question about the profit rate's tendency is not a question about crisis, however adjacent the territories, and belongs to the profit rate row even if the candidate's revision was stronger on crisis theory; the crisis material has its own guide, and its absence from the answer is not a gap but a boundary correctly observed.

The limits are the qualifiers candidates most often skip: in the third volume, for a named school, as a theory of price, in the long run. Each limit excludes a body of material the undisciplined candidate will waste time including. A question that asks about the transformation problem as it appears in the third volume is not asking for Bortkiewicz until the evaluation stage, and the candidate who opens with the correction has skipped the exposition the question priced highest. A question that asks whether the value theory succeeds as a theory of price has already chosen the ground on which the redundancy objection is strongest, and the candidate who answers on the ground of the theory of exploitation has declined the set question while appearing to answer it.

Quotation stems demand a specific routine. Where the question prints a critic's sentence and invites comment, the sentence is the question: the answer should identify the sentence's target version, state the argument it compresses, and grade it, before ranging anywhere else. Where the question is two-part, the parts are weighted in the marking scheme in a way the candidate cannot see, and the only safe strategy is to give the second part, usually the evaluative one, at least the time of the first, because candidates systematically over-invest in the expository part they find easier and starve the part the bands reward. These are humble skills, and on this topic they are worth more than an extra school memorised, because the difference between the question set and the question answered is the purest waste in the examination hall.

## Timed conditions: the plan, the clock, and the rescue moves

Everything in this guide must survive contact with the clock, and the candidate's techniques should be chosen for the candidate who is tired, behind schedule, and writing the third answer of the sitting, not for the fresh reader of a quiet afternoon. The plan comes first. On this topic the plan is the matrix row, written in the margin in the time the examination allows for reading: the schools to name, the lead formulation, the evaluation, the trap to avoid, in that order, in shorthand. A candidate who has rehearsed the matrix can produce the plan in under two minutes, and those two minutes are the highest-yield expenditure in the paper, because they convert the answer from a journey into an itinerary. The plan also performs emotional work candidates underestimate: a script that knows where it is going can survive the discovery, midway, that a section has gone wrong, since the plan shows what remains and what the remainder is worth.

### What should a candidate write first when time runs short?

The three sentences. If the transformation is anywhere in the question's territory, the three-sentence statement is the paragraph whose absence costs most and whose presence anchors everything else; write it early, while the hand is steady, and let later sections refer back to it rather than rebuild it.

The rescue moves deserve rehearsal in their own right, because every candidate needs them eventually and improvisation under panic is poor. If the evaluation will not come, name the strongest critic and state their objection fairly; stated fairly, an objection is itself evaluative material and the marker will credit the fairness even where the reply is thin. If a school's details have fled, keep its name and its one-line position, which the revision method below is designed to preserve, and spend the recovered time on the schools that remain; a named position briefly held is worth more than a blank where the plurality was promised. If the conclusion will not form, distribute the verdict: state what survives and what does not, claim by claim, which is a conclusion in the only form this topic ultimately respects. And if the answer has gone genuinely wrong, if the wrong row was planned and half executed, finish the answer that exists rather than restarting: markers grade the script on the page, and a completed imperfect answer outscores a fragment of a better one in every marking scheme this topic is examined under.

Time allocation across the paper is the final timed skill. This cluster's questions are seductive; they reward knowledge richly and candidates linger. The discipline is to treat each question's minutes as a ration fixed before the sitting, to wear the ration visibly, and to leave the last minutes of each ration for the verdict, which is the portion of the answer whose absence the bands punish most. A candidate who finishes the paper with every verdict written and some middles compressed has played the marking scheme correctly. A candidate who leaves one answer verdictless and another encyclopaedic has donated marks to the field, and on a topic where the field is mostly unprepared, the donation is rarely reciprocated.

## The errors of the strong: how good candidates lose marks on this topic

Weak candidates lose marks on this topic in the ways already catalogued. Strong candidates lose them differently, and the catalogue of their errors is shorter and stranger. The first is over-engineering: the script that imports apparatus the question cannot price, the candidate who answers an undergraduate question on the labour theory with the full value-form literature, or a development question with the transformation debate, and discovers that sophistication deployed off-target earns the same mark as simplicity, minus the time. The question's level is a fact about the paper, not about the candidate's reading, and the strong candidate's task is to price their knowledge to the question, holding the surplus in reserve for the questions that ask for it.

The second error of the strong is the premature verdict. A candidate who knows the literature has usually formed a position, and the temptation is to announce it in the opening and spend the answer recruiting. The marker reads the announcement as a closure and the recruitment as advocacy, and the script is marked as a performance of opinion rather than of judgement, even when the opinion is the marker's own. The craft rule is severe: the verdict enters when its evidence is complete, and the opening promises only the criterion by which the verdict will be reached. The third error is the asymmetric statement, the scrupulous exposition of one's own school beside a summary execution of its rival. Markers are exquisitely sensitive to asymmetry because their own position is often the executed one, and because fairness to the opposition is the cheapest credibility a script can buy. The fourth error, subtlest of all, is answering the literature instead of the question: the script that reproduces a famous exchange, Kliman against his critics, the empirical school against its statisticians, in the form in which the journals printed it, when the question asked for something adjacent but distinct. Scholarship is the raw material; the question is the specification. Strong candidates, who possess the most raw material, are the most tempted to ship it unprocessed.

There is a fifth error, and it belongs to this topic alone: the candidate who, having learned the contested figures protocol, becomes so cautious that no magnitude is ever committed to, and the answer dissolves into a fog of conventions and caveats. The protocol forbids citing figures as facts. It does not forbid stating the theory's claims about magnitudes, the division of the day, the direction of the tendency, the structure of the ratios, and a script that will not say what the theory asserts cannot be credited with evaluating it. Caution is a seasoning on this topic, and the strong candidate's failure is to serve it as the meal.

## One topic, several examination rooms: how the expectations change

The assessment contexts this guide serves differ enough that a single revision product cannot serve them all, and the candidate should know which room they are sitting in. Undergraduate political economy papers examine the topic as a system among systems: the questions are broad, the marking rewards architecture, and the plurality opening is at its most valuable because the field answers as if the theory were a monument. History of economic thought papers examine it as a text among texts: location and lineage dominate, Ricardo must be present, the classical inheritance is assumed knowledge, and a candidate's handling of the first chapter's place in the argument matters more than anywhere else. Development papers examine chiefly the dependency row and the theory of imperialism behind it, and reward the version distinction and the historical exhibits. School-level specifications, where Marxist critique appears as a named perspective within a mainstream syllabus, examine recognition and fair statement: the candidate is asked to present the critique accurately against the orthodox account, and the skills are compression and neutrality rather than depth.

Civil service examinations with optional papers in economics and in political science form a distinct room with its own physics. The economics optional examines the topic at something near the undergraduate technical level, and derivations and models are priced as they are in university economics. The political science optional examines it inside political theory and ideology, where the value theory is background to questions about alienation, the state, and revolution, and where the candidate's economics must be worn lightly and stated in prose. The same body of knowledge is being priced by two different markets in the same examination system, and the candidate who prepares one product for both sells badly in each. National eligibility tests, which select for lectureships, examine by breadth under objective formats: there the skill inverts entirely, from constructing arguments to recognising positions, knowing which school holds which claim and which critic made which objection, at speed and without nuance. Revision for such a test should build the directory of attributions as a recognition instrument, names to claims and claims to names, both directions, and the matrix serves that purpose if it is learned as a table of who says what rather than as a set of essay plans.

Degree level shifts the expectations in a direction candidates consistently misjudge. At introductory level the bands reward accurate statement and forgive a thin evaluation; at advanced level the statement is assumed and the evaluation is the paper. The candidate moving between levels should therefore reallocate revision effort from the formulations, which are already secure, to the objections and the criteria, since those are what the advanced paper prices. The tell, as always, is available before the sitting: the reading list's centre of gravity. A list dominated by the primary texts signals a statement paper; a list dominated by the dispute's literature signals a judgement paper, and the candidate who notices has been told, in advance and for free, which of this guide's skills the examination will buy.

## The study workflow: cards, past questions, and timed rehearsal

The revision method this guide recommends is built from the matrix, and the workflow that sustains it deserves a statement of its own, since candidates commonly own methods and lack a routine. The matrix should be transferred to revision cards, one row per card, with the lead formulation on one face and the trap on the other, and the cards carried in the student's own notes system; the study notes tool at [VaultBook](https://vaultbook.net/tools/marxism-study-notes.html) is built for exactly this transfer, holding the matrix as a set of cards the candidate can annotate with specimen sentences and revisit across the revision period. Past questions are the second element, and they should be practised as questions rather than absorbed as information: the previous year papers gathered at [ReportMedic](https://reportmedic.org/tools/upsc-pyq-explorer.html) let a candidate collect every appearance of this topic across years and boards, sort the appearances by matrix row, and discover the consoling regularity that the five rows exhaust nearly everything examiners actually ask. Sorting old questions is the single most reassuring exercise available on this topic, because it converts an apparently boundless literature into a finite set of recurring demands, each with a rehearsed response.

Timed rehearsal is the third element, and it is not negotiable. An answer that has never been written against a clock is a hypothesis, and the sitting is a poor place to test hypotheses. The rehearsal protocol that works is plain: one row per session, the plan written in the first two minutes, the answer written in the time the real paper allows, and the script then marked by the candidate against the row's four columns, schools named, formulation clean, evaluation present, trap avoided. The self-marking matters as much as the writing, because it trains the internal marker whose absence in the hall is what the rescue moves exist to replace. Two full rehearsals per row is the minimum at which the techniques in this guide become reflexes; below that number the candidate owns the techniques the way one owns a phrase book, able to look things up and unable to speak.

Revision across the cluster should be organised as one topic rather than nine, for a reason the next section gives in full: the cluster's questions share their machinery. The transformation statement serves the labour theory row, the schemas serve the proportionality argument wherever it appears, the ratios serve three rows at once. A revision plan that allocates days to topics in isolation therefore wastes the overlap, while a plan that allocates days to instruments, the three sentences, the two departments, the three ratios, the directory of critics, and then days to rows, buys each instrument once and spends it everywhere. The final week is for consolidation of those instruments, the final day for the matrix reconstructed from memory and the three sentences written out by hand, and the final hour for nothing at all, since a candidate who is still acquiring material in the final hour has mistaken the examination for a library.

## The marking reality: a conclusion against the theory can score full marks

This guide now states, in its own voice and without qualification, the fact that organises everything else in it. A model answer concluding against Marxist economics can score full marks. Markers are not grading allegiance, and on this topic they cannot, because the examiners themselves are divided among the schools: the board that sets a transformation question contains, across its marking team, simultaneists and temporalists and Sraffians and historians who think the whole dispute a scholasticism, and the only currency that circulates among all of them is accuracy of statement joined to named evaluation. An answer that states the New Interpretation fairly, states Steedman's objection fairly, and concludes that the value theory should be abandoned as a theory of price while retained as a theory of the working day has given every one of those markers exactly what their band descriptors describe. An answer that defends the theory by misstating Steedman has given none of them anything, and its author's sympathy with the subject is not an extenuating circumstance but an aggravating one, since the defence was available honestly and was not taken.

### Can an answer that concludes against Marxist economics score full marks?

Yes, and this guide states it as marking fact. The bands reward accuracy of statement and named evaluation, neither of which has a direction. A conclusion against the theory, earned by stating its strongest defences fairly and answering them, satisfies every descriptor a sympathetic conclusion must satisfy, and markers divided among the schools can credit nothing else consistently.

The practical consequence is a freedom candidates should feel in their shoulders. You are not required, in any room where this topic is examined, to rescue Marx, and you are not required to bury him. You are required to know what he claimed, what his critics showed, what his defenders rebuilt, and what the rebuilding cost, and to arrange that knowledge under the question asked. Candidates who grasp this stop spending revision time rehearsing a posture and start spending it on the directory of attributions and the three sentences, which is to say they start preparing for the examination that exists. The specimen answers sketched in the next two sections are built to demonstrate the freedom concretely: one of them concludes, on the transformation question, that the printed procedure fails and that the best defence transforms the theory into something its author might not recognise; the other, on the comparison question, concludes that the mainstream programme has won the territory of price formation outright, and that the Marxist programme's surviving claims lie elsewhere. Both are constructed to full-marks standard, and neither could be mistaken for the work of a believer.

## Specimen answer one: the transformation question, walked through

Take the commonest form of the question: the transformation problem shows that Marx's value theory is inconsistent; discuss. The weak script opens with biography or with indignation, states the labour theory as a price doctrine, concedes or denies the inconsistency in a slogan, and closes with a verdict the middle never earned. The strong script, walked through here at the level of its moves, begins by translating the question. Its opening names the dispute precisely: an opening volume proceeding at values, a third volume equalising profit through prices of production, the unconverted inputs between them, and the schools divided over the repair, with the grading rule announced in advance, that every reconstruction will be weighed by what it keeps and what it pays. The marker has been told, inside a paragraph, that the script knows the problem's shape and will not be wasting the hour.

The middle then works the directory. Bortkiewicz states the inconsistency exactly and prices his own correction: the equalities cannot both hold under simultaneous transformation. Samuelson's famous eraser image, the claim that one may write the value system, erase it, and write the price system in its place, is given as the strongest rhetorical form of the internal critique, and answered at the level of its logic rather than its tone. The simultaneist settlement is stated, values as an intermediate computation, and its cost named: the conservation claims become conventions. The New Interpretation is stated in its architects' terms: labour time given a direct monetary expression, the value of labour power redefined through the money wage, both equalities restored, and its cost named by its critics: equalities held by construction testify to the construction. The temporal reading is stated, sequential valuation, historic prices for the advance, both equalities restored without redefinition, and its cost named: the value magnitudes now move with the prices they were meant to regulate, and its falling rate results are inseparable from that choice. The script closes by distributing its verdict across the three claims the opening separated: the printed procedure is inconsistent, as the question asserts; the inference that the value theory is therefore inconsistent does not follow, since consistent versions exist; and the price of every consistent version is a surrender the candidate names, concluding that the question's verb, shows, is stronger than its evidence. That is a discuss answer: every sentence of it was either statement or grading, and its conclusion against the printed Marx is exactly as creditable as a conclusion for him would have been.

## Specimen answer two: the comparison question, walked through

Take the comparison in its commonest form: Marxist and mainstream economics are rival explanations of the same economy; discuss. The weak script writes two summaries in sequence, Marx first and the mainstream second or the reverse, appends a paragraph saying the truth lies between them, and is graded as two half answers stapled together. The strong script begins, as the architecture section prescribes, by relocating the comparison from answers to questions. Its opening states that the programmes share a territory and divide over what is exogenous: the mainstream takes preferences, endowments, and technique as data and explains allocation; the Marxist programme treats the endowment distribution and the commodity status of labour power as the things to be explained, and locates profit's origin in production. The criterion is announced with the translation: each programme will be credited with what it demonstrably explains, and the disagreement will be placed where the explanations run out.

The middle then alternates at the level of problems rather than doctrines. On price formation in a particular market, the mainstream apparatus is stated at its genuine strength and the territory conceded outright; a script that grudges this concession loses the right to its later claims. On the origin of profit, the positions are reversed: the mainstream's accounts, profit as the return to a factor or as the reward of waiting and risk, are stated fairly and then located as descriptions of profit's distribution rather than its source, and the Marxist division of the working day is put as the rival claim, with the transformation literature acknowledged as the price the claim pays for its price theory. On unemployment and crisis, the Marxist categories, the reserve army, the endogenous cycle, are set against the mainstream's friction and shock vocabulary, and the candidate grades the exchange by asking which programme treats the phenomenon as a product of the system and which as an interruption of it, while conceding that the mainstream's formalisation of its answers exceeds anything the Marxist tradition has built. The close distributes the verdict by territory, as the marking reality section promised: price formation to the mainstream, the surplus's origin and division to a dispute the mainstream largely declines to enter, and the categories' historicity to the Marxist insistence that the economy has a history, an insistence the mainstream answer must either absorb or refuse. The script's sympathies, whichever they are, never appear as a premise. They arrive, if at all, as a residue the marker can weigh.

## The directory of critics: who argued what, and what answers them

Evaluation on this topic is a directory, the earlier section said, and directories should be printed. What follows is the working directory for the cluster, critic by critic, each entry giving the objection in the form its author pressed it and the reply the literature regards as canonical. It is not a substitute for the articles on the individual disputes. It is the revision instrument from which a candidate can reconstruct, in the hall, which name belongs in which sentence.

Böhm-Bawerk opens the directory because he opens the modern dispute. His charge, delivered a generation after Marx's death, was that the system contradicts itself between its first and third volumes: the first founds exchange on value, the third distributes profit by capital advanced, indifferent to value, and a theory may not found and abandon the same law. The canonical reply is the levels argument of this guide's third section: the first volume's law governs the aggregate and the third volume's prices are its redistributed form, a reply whose adequacy is precisely what the transformation dispute then tests. Bortkiewicz follows, the directory's most consequential name. His correction is technical rather than rhetorical: transform the inputs as the outputs were transformed, and the two aggregate equalities cannot in general be held together. Every later position is a response to that arithmetic, and the candidate who cites Bortkiewicz for anything else, a general hostility to Marx, a political verdict, has misfiled him.

Samuelson is the directory's rhetorician. His eraser image proposed that the whole value analysis could be deleted and the price analysis written directly, and his authority carried the redundancy charge into the textbooks. Steedman is the charge's rigorous form, and the two should never be fused in an answer: Samuelson asserted the detour, Steedman proved a version of it from Sraffa's premises, and the replies differ accordingly, the New Interpretation answering the proof by redefining the terrain, the temporal school by disputing the simultaneity the proof requires. Sweezy belongs in the directory as an editor as much as an author: his presentation naturalised Bortkiewicz's correction for the English-reading world and set the terms in which two generations learned the problem, which is why citing the correction through Sweezy is acceptable and citing it as Sweezy's invention is not.

Okishio holds the falling rate file. His theorem, that viable cost-cutting innovation cannot lower the equilibrium rate of profit at a constant real wage, is the strongest single result against a Marxist claim ever produced inside the theory's own formal language, and the replies, temporal valuation above all, concede its validity within simultaneity while denying that simultaneity is the theory's frame. Shaikh holds the empirical file from the defending side: his measurements of embodied labour against prices, and his insistence that the correspondence survives the controls its critics demand, make him the name to cite when an answer needs the regulation claim in its statistical form, with the critics' rejoinder, that cost magnitudes generally track prices, cited beside him. Roemer holds the reconstruction file: exploitation rebuilt on property relations, the labour theory discharged as unnecessary to the moral claim, a position politics papers examine and economics papers respect. Brenner holds the circulation file: his charge that dependency and much else in the tradition explains by exchange what Marx explained by production relations is the internal objection that development answers must meet. A candidate whose script can deploy six of these names in their correct files, with the replies attached, possesses the evaluation apparatus entire, and the remaining work is arrangement.

## The two equalities: what exactly is conserved, and why it matters

Beneath the transformation dispute sits a pair of claims so compact that candidates memorise them without grasping them, and a section on their content repays the space. The first equality asserts that total price of production, summed across the whole economy, equals total value: the transformation redistributes, it does not create or destroy. The second asserts that aggregate profit equals the aggregate of surplus value: the surplus distributed among capitals is the surplus produced, neither augmented by competition nor diminished by it. Together the equalities are the theory's conservation laws, the guarantee that the price system is the value system wearing the form competition imposes, and they are the precise point at which Bortkiewicz's correction bites, since once inputs are transformed the arithmetic sustains one equality or the other under a chosen numéraire, not both together as substantive truths.

An answer that understands the equalities can do three things weaker answers cannot. It can explain why the dispute is not pedantry: the conservation laws are the load-bearing claim that profit is unpaid labour in aggregate, and their failure or survival decides whether the price system conceals the value system or replaces it. It can grade the reconstructions in their own currencies: the temporal school and the New Interpretation are, at bottom, rival machines for keeping both conservations, and their ingenuity is unintelligible until the candidate sees what is being conserved. And it can state Samuelson's eraser at its true weight: the eraser is painless only if nothing is conserved, if the value accounts record nothing the price accounts lack, and the candidate who has grasped the equalities knows that the eraser deletes the conservation claim and must argue the deletion rather than perform it. This is the level at which the transformation question stops being a puzzle about arithmetic and becomes what examiners intend it to be, a check on whether the candidate understands what a theory claims when it claims that one system underlies another.

## The texts as evidence: citing the volumes like a scholar

The located reference, praised in this guide's opening section, deserves its working map. Marxist economics is unusual among examined topics in that its primary text is unfinished, posthumously edited, and stratified, and markers reward candidates who handle that stratigraphy knowingly. The first volume is the only one Marx saw through the press, and its first chapter carries the value form, its chapters on the working day and on machinery carry the production of absolute and relative surplus value in their most concrete form, and its final part carries primitive accumulation, the historical precondition the comparison row needs. The second volume, assembled by Engels from manuscripts, carries circulation: the turnover of capital, the distinction between fixed and circulating forms, and the reproduction schemas of its final part. The third volume, also Engels's construction, carries the forms of appearance entire: prices of production and the transformation in its early chapters, the profit rate's tendency and its counterinfluences in the chapters that follow, and the surplus split among profit, interest, and rent thereafter.

A candidate who can place a claim in that map gains two advantages. The first is precision under questioning by the page: asked where Marx qualifies the falling rate, the prepared candidate knows the counterinfluences have their own chapter beside the tendency, because the theory's author built the qualification into the architecture. The second advantage is the Engels caveat, deployed once and lightly: the second and third volumes are editorial constructions from drafts of differing dates, and the reconstructions' fidelity disputes are partly disputes about which stratum of draft represents the settled view. That caveat, stated in a sentence, signals a candidate who knows the text is an artefact with a history, and it inoculates the script against the charge of treating a posthumous compilation as a stenographer's record.

Beyond the three volumes, two further locations earn their keep. The Theories of Surplus Value, the manuscript history of the classical doctrines, is where Marx's reading of Ricardo lives, and the history of thought paper prices it accordingly. The Grundrisse, the rough draft of the whole project, is where several categories appear in their most speculative form, and the candidate citing it signals range, provided the citation is honest about the text's status as a draft its author did not publish. What none of these locations licenses is quotation at length. The pack discipline of this series, quotation kept short and earned, is also the examination's discipline: a clause of Marx, exactly placed, outweighs a paragraph of him, because the clause proves selection and the paragraph suggests transcription.

## What the top band looks like from the marker's chair

Markers describe the top band in a vocabulary candidates rarely hear, and it is worth translating. From the chair, the upper scripts share a feel before they share any technique: the feel of being addressed by someone who knows the field's map and is walking the marker across it at a pace the marker can follow. Ground is neither skipped nor re-trodden. Names arrive with their positions attached, as if the directory were printed on the script's inside cover. Concessions land where they purchase ground. The verdict, when it comes, has the quality of a sum being totalled rather than a flag being planted. Scripts with that feel are not necessarily the longest in the pile, and on this topic they are frequently shorter than the middle-band monuments, because their authors knew which materials the question priced and declined the rest.

The chair also sees the topic's specific pathologies in bulk, and the bulk is instructive. In any large sitting on this material, the majority of scripts will open with the losing definition or a biography paragraph; a large minority will declare the transformation problem settled in one direction or the other; a steady fraction will quote a profit rate with a country and a decade attached as if reading from a table of constants; and almost none will name Cardoso beside Frank, state Okishio beside the tendency, or locate the transformation in its chapter. The candidate cannot see the pile, but the pile is the competition, and the pile's habits are the reason the techniques in this guide return so much for so little. The plurality opening, the three sentences, the directory, the protocol: each is a small discipline, and each places the script that performs it ahead of a large and predictable fraction of the field. Examinations are relative instruments even when their bands are described absolutely, and on a topic where the field's errors are this regular, correctness is a form of speed.

## The value-form reading: the first chapter's revenge

A candidate can pass through this whole cluster treating the first chapter of Capital as a preliminary, the place where value is defined before the real machinery starts. The value-form school exists to say that this is backwards, and examiners in thought-oriented papers examine the claim seriously. On the value-form reading, descending from Rubin's essays and renewed by a later literature, the theory's centre is not a magnitude but a form: the question is why the social character of private labour appears as the value of its product, why relations between producers take the shape of relations between things. Marx's own sentence for the phenomenon is the one clause of quotation this guide licenses without anxiety: in the fetishism section the producers' social relation is said to assume, in their eyes, the fantastic form of a relation between things. Twelve words, exactly located, and worth more than the pages of paraphrase that usually surround it.

### What does the value-form reading change about how an answer should open?

The order of the theory. On this reading the magnitude questions, how much value, how transformed, are downstream of the form question, why labour appears as value at all. An answer examined under this school should state the form first, since a magnitude answer to a form question scores as a category error.

The examination significance is practical. A question phrased around appearance, fetishism, or the social form of labour belongs to this school whatever else it mentions, and the candidate who answers it with the transformation arithmetic has brought the wrong instrument, expensively. Conversely, a question about prices of production is not improved by a detour through fetishism, and the value-form candidate who insists on the form's priority in every answer commits the mirror error. The school's real gift to the prepared candidate is diagnostic: it explains why some questions in the cluster feel different in kind from others, and it supplies the formulation for the definition section of any answer that wants to signal depth, namely that the theory is a critique of the forms in which social labour appears, of which the magnitude claims are consequences rather than foundations. Deployed in the right room, that sentence is worth a band boundary. Deployed everywhere, it is a mannerism, and markers can tell the difference by the second page.

## Hard cases the examiners keep in reserve

Every topic has a shelf of hard cases that examiners use to separate the strongest scripts, and the candidate should know this cluster's shelf by name. Joint production is the first item. Where one process yields several products inseparably, the embodied labour arithmetic can misbehave: Steedman constructed cases in which aggregate surplus value comes out negative while aggregate profit is positive, a result he pressed as a reductio of the value framework. The replies are instructive in themselves: the temporal and monetary schools argue the result is an artefact of simultaneous valuation of joint products, and value-form writers argue that simple labour accounting was never the theory's claim. An answer that can state the case, the use Steedman made of it, and the two families of reply has reached the shelf's first level. Fixed capital is the second item: machines transfer value across many periods and are themselves revalued as productivity changes, the phenomenon Marx called moral depreciation, and any answer about the composition of capital that ignores the revaluation of the existing stock will be marked as a first approximation by a marker who knows the literature. The third item is the skilled labour reduction: complex labour counts as multiplied simple labour, and the sceptical question, what performs the multiplication if not the wage itself, threatens circularity; the honest answer states the problem, notes that Marx treated the reduction as accomplished behind the producers' backs by social process, and grades the reply as a promissory note the literature has never fully redeemed.

The fourth item is the oldest: the diamond and water paradox in its modern dress, the marginalist founding anecdote. Candidates meet it as a refutation of labour theories generally, and the prepared answer distinguishes the claims it touches. It touches any theory that reads price off embodied labour directly, which is the strong version this guide has already surrendered. It does not touch the aggregate claims, the form claims, or the theory of the working day, and the candidate who sorts the anecdote's targets performs the version discipline in miniature. Examiners keep these cases in reserve because each one tests sorting rather than storage: the knowledge required is modest, the discrimination required is total, and discrimination is what the final band boundary is made of.

## The wage question: what fixes the value of labour power

One fault line runs beneath nearly every dispute in the cluster, surfacing wherever an answer touches wages, and the candidate should learn to see it. What determines the value of labour power? The classical answer inside Marx's text is a bundle: the value of the means of subsistence required to maintain the worker and the worker's family, with a historical and moral element Marx explicitly acknowledged, varying by country and epoch. The bundle answer grounds the exploitation ratio in physical magnitudes, and it is the version most textbooks teach. The New Interpretation quietly replaces it: the value of labour power, on that reading, is the money wage divided by the monetary expression of labour time, a definition that makes the share of wages in the net product the operative magnitude and frees the aggregate equalities from the bundle's arithmetic. The temporal school keeps the bundle's spirit while disputing its simultaneous pricing, and the critics of the New Interpretation charge that its wage definition purchases consistency by making exploitation a matter of the wage share alone, so that the embodied labour of the wage goods drops out of the theory.

For the candidate, the fault line is a gift wherever it appears. A question about exploitation that ignores it can only recite; a question that engages it can evaluate, because the rate of surplus value itself changes meaning as the wage definition changes, and the marker knows it. The practical rule is to declare the definition in use. An answer that computes or describes exploitation should say, in a clause, whether labour power's value is being taken as the value of a subsistence bundle or as the wage share of value added, and should note that the schools divide at exactly that point. The clause costs a line and buys the script immunity from the most technical objection available against it. It also connects the cluster's abstractions to the one magnitude every candidate intuitively understands, the wage, and answers built around that connection tend to read, from the marker's chair, like economics rather than like catechism.

## Worked micro-plans for the five rows

A matrix is only as good as the plans it generates, and this section walks the five rows as a candidate would meet them in the first minutes of a sitting, plan in the margin, clock running. For the labour theory row, the plan is a sorting exercise: the question's phrasing is checked against the three claims, substance, regulation, and form, and the plan allocates the answer's weight to the claim the phrasing selects, with the other two held in a paragraph each so the marker sees the sorting happened. The opening writes the definition in its carrying form. The middle states the selected claim with its textual exhibit, then runs the objection that belongs to it and no other, Böhm-Bawerk for the price version, redundancy for regulation, and the close surrenders explicitly the version the objection kills. Five moves, each rehearsed, and the row is beaten before the writing starts.

For the transformation row, the plan is the specimen already walked through: translation opening, three sentences written early and in full, the directory in order of the dispute's history, each reconstruction priced in preservation and surrender, and a distributed verdict. The plan's single peculiarity is insurance: the three sentences are placed where a collapsing clock cannot reach them, because on this row the statement is worth more than any evaluation that might replace it. For the profit rate row, the plan separates three labours that weaker scripts fuse: the mechanism from the tendency chapters, stated as composition rising under accumulation; the theorem, Okishio stated at full strength with its assumptions visible, constant real wage, simultaneous valuation, viable innovation only; and the measurement paragraph, directions under named conventions, the protocol observed. The verdict then falls out as a sentence about conventions, which is the only form of verdict the row supports.

For the comparison row, the plan is an alternation schedule: three territories, price formation, the origin of profit, crisis and unemployment, with the concession direction reversing between the first and the others, and a closing paragraph that distributes the territories and names the residue question, the historical character of the categories, on which the programmes do not share a language. The plan's discipline is symmetry of statement: each side gets its strongest form before either gets its grade. For the dependency row, the plan is a version gate: the first decision is which dependency theory the question names or implies, Frank's strong thesis or the qualified tradition, and the plan refuses to proceed until the gate is passed, because every later move, the exhibits, the East Asian evaluation, the Brenner objection, prices differently on the two sides of it. Candidates who run the five plans as checklists in revision will find that the plans share a skeleton, translate, state, sort, evaluate, distribute, and that the skeleton, once internalised, answers questions the matrix has never seen, which is the definition of a technique as against a script.

## Crisis at the border: knowing where this cluster ends

The adjacency most likely to cost a prepared candidate marks is the border with crisis theory, and it deserves a section because the profit rate row sits directly on it. The falling rate is a tendency within the theory of accumulation; crisis theory is the account of how the system's contradictions erupt as breakdown, and the tradition contains several distinct crisis accounts, underconsumptionist readings that stress the restricted consumption of the masses, disproportionality readings that grow from the schemas, profit squeeze readings that locate the trigger in the wage share at full employment, and the falling rate itself promoted into a crisis mechanism by some of its defenders. Those accounts, their evidence, and their assessment belong to the crisis guide named in this article's opening, and the candidate whose question is about crisis should be writing there. The candidate whose question is about the tendency must resist importing the crisis apparatus wholesale, because the marker prices the boundary: a tendency answer that wanders into a general theory of breakdown has answered a larger question than was set, at a lower standard than either required.

The border is policed from the other side as well. Crisis answers are weakened by candidates who restate the falling rate as if it were the whole crisis theory, and the candidate who has revised the two territories together, tendency here, eruption there, holds a distinction that a noticeable fraction of the field fumbles. The border skill generalises, and it is worth naming as this guide's last technique: on a topic whose parts are as interlocking as this cluster's, marks flow to the candidate who knows which room each argument lives in, and the interlocking is a reason to learn the map more exactly, not a licence to ignore its internal walls.

## Short answers and objective formats: the recognition game

Not every assessment on this topic is an essay, and the candidate facing short answer sections or objective tests needs a different, colder skill set. The short answer, a paragraph or two priced for exact content, rewards the carrying definitions and the directory in their most compressed form: the three sentences shrink to their skeleton, each school becomes a name plus its one-line position, and the located reference becomes the single highest-density mark available, since a chapter cited correctly in a short answer is unambiguous evidence of knowledge. What short answers punish is throat clearing. Every sentence not carrying a claim, a name, or a location is waste at this length, and the candidate should practise by compressing each matrix row into a fixed small compass until the compression is clean.

Objective formats, the eligibility tests and the multiple choice sections of school and university papers, invert the skill from production to recognition, and they have their own traps. The distractors are built from this guide's catalogue of errors: the option stating that the labour theory predicts prices, the option attributing Bortkiewicz's correction to Sweezy or Steedman's proof to Samuelson, the option in which Cardoso holds Frank's thesis, the option assigning the monetary expression to the temporal school. A candidate who has revised the directory as an instrument of recognition, claims matched to names and names to claims, will find the distractors transparent, because each is a misfiling of a kind the revision has already catalogued. The technique for such papers is unglamorous and decisive: for every name learned, learn also the two misattributions examiners favour, since the test is built from them, and the candidate who knows what Kliman did not argue is armed against the commonest construction in the paper.

## What examiners' reports teach, and how to use them before the sitting

Across boards and levels, examiners publish reports on completed sittings, and the reports on this topic read, decade after decade, like a single document. The complaints recur with a regularity that should comfort the prepared candidate: scripts that retell the theory without engaging its disputes; verdicts announced in the first paragraph and repeated, unaltered, in the last; criticism attributed to nobody; figures quoted from textbooks as if they were constants of nature; answers to questions adjacent to the one set. The commendations recur too, and they are the mirror image: scripts that name the schools, locate their references, concede precisely, and distribute their verdicts. A candidate does not need to locate the reports for their own board to profit from this, although the past paper collections named in the workflow section usually sit beside them. The profit is in recognising that the marking of this topic is a stable technology. The same performances are rewarded because the bands describe the same operations, and the candidate is therefore revising against a specification that the reports have, in effect, published.

There is a way to convert that stability into a revision exercise of unusual efficiency. Take the five complaints and the five commendations as a checklist, and mark two of your own rehearsal scripts against nothing else. The exercise takes an hour and finds, in most candidates' work, the biography paragraph, the unnamed critic, and the premature verdict within the first two pages, each correctable by deletion or by a single inserted attribution. Candidates consistently report that self-marking against the reports' vocabulary changes their writing faster than additional reading does, which should not surprise: the reports are the only documents in the entire literature written by the people who will grade the script, about the scripts they have graded, and their testimony about what earns marks is not opinion but evidence.

## The conversion problem: owning the knowledge is not the skill being examined

The cruellest discovery candidates make on this topic is that they can know the cluster and still score in the middle band, and the explanation deserves a section because it reorganises revision. The knowledge and the performance are different assets. Knowledge is what the nine preceding articles build: the systems, the disputes, the history. Performance is what this guide builds: the translation of a question into its dispute, the attribution discipline, the timed plan, the distributed verdict. A candidate short of knowledge writes thin answers that no technique can save. A candidate short of performance writes knowledgeable answers in the wrong shape, surveys where evaluations were priced, treatises where the question asked for a sorting, and the marker, who can only grade the script on the page, records the shape and not the library behind it.

The conversion failure has recognisable symptoms in rehearsal scripts. The answer that could have been written before the question was read is the chief: if the script would serve equally for any phrasing on the topic, it serves this phrasing badly, because the bands pay for engagement with the set words. The answer whose paragraphs could be reordered without loss is the second: on this topic a real answer has a load-bearing order, statement before objection, objection before grading, grading before verdict, and a script whose middle is a heap rather than a sequence has not yet become an argument. The answer that ends by repeating its opening is the third, since a verdict identical to the introduction's promise means the middle did no work, and markers are paid to notice work. Candidates who learn to spot the three symptoms in their own drafts have acquired the internal marker, and the internal marker is the only coach who attends the sitting.

## Why examiners keep setting this topic

It is worth understanding, finally, why this material survives in examination after examination while other doctrines rotate out of syllabuses, because the reasons are the candidate's final reassurance. Examiners keep setting it because it discriminates. A topic on which everyone agrees produces scripts that differ only in fluency, and fluency is a weak signal of understanding. This topic's published, named, century-long disputes generate scripts that differ in knowledge itself, in whether the candidate knows the dispute exists, knows its parties, knows its current state, and a paper built on it sorts a cohort with unusual precision. Examiners keep setting it because it is fair in a specific way: the disputes are all in print, the texts are stable, the schools' positions are public, and no candidate needs a teacher's private line to discover what the positions are, which is why the single-source answer is graded so unsympathetically. And examiners keep setting it because the topic examines the exact virtue the disciplines claim to teach, the ability to hold a contested body of theory in view whole, to state positions one does not hold at the strength their holders give them, and to arrive at a judgement that the evidence presented can carry. The candidate who understands that this is the examination's real subject has understood the topic's last secret: the economics is the occasion, and the judgement is the thing examined.

## Reading a transformation table without fear

Sooner or later the candidate meets the transformation in its tabular form, a grid of industries with their capitals divided between constant and variable portions, their surplus values, their values, and their prices of production, and the grid frightens candidates out of proportion to its content. The table is a picture of the three sentences. Each row is an industry whose composition differs from the social average. The industries rich in constant capital, the heavy plant and the automated line, produce relatively little surplus for their size, because living labour is scarce in them, yet competition awards them the average rate of profit on the whole capital advanced, so they must sell above their value. The industries rich in living labour produce surplus abundantly and sell below their value, surrendering the excess. The columns total the transfers: what the first group gains the second group loses, and the table's whole argument is that the gains and losses are redistribution, the same surplus wearing different prices. A candidate who can narrate one row, any row, in that prose has understood the table, and a candidate who has only memorised the totals has understood the arithmetic of a table whose point is the transfers.

### What does a single row of the transformation table show?

Redistribution in miniature. An industry employing little living labour relative to its capital draws more profit than the surplus its own workers produce, and the difference is a transfer from labour-rich industries, effected by its price of production standing above its value. The row is the theory's mechanism in one line.

The examination use of the table is diagnostic rather than computational, except in the technical papers where the derivation itself is set. Elsewhere, the candidate deploys the table's logic in sentences: the equalisation transfers surplus toward capital-intensive production, the transfer is why individual prices cannot be read off individual values, and the aggregate conservations are what the transfer preserves. Candidates who grasp the narration can also handle the table's standard abuse in the textbooks, the presentation in which the divergence of price from value is offered as the theory's embarrassment, since the narration shows the divergence is the theory's prediction, derived before it was observed to be a problem. What the critics dispute was never the divergence. It was whether the conservation beneath the divergence survives consistent accounting, and the candidate who keeps that straight will not be moved by the textbook's raised eyebrow.

## What the first volume assumes, and what assuming means

The first sentence of the three carries a verb that examiners probe: assumes. Did Marx assume that commodities exchange at their values, or did he prove it, or did he merely fail to notice the difficulty? The accurate account matters because each reconstruction is, in part, a theory of that verb. Marx's method in the first volume is abstraction by stages. He analyses the capitalist mode of production under deliberately simplified conditions, exchange at values among them, in order to study how surplus value is produced apart from the complications of its distribution, and he announces the procedure as provisional: the departure of prices from values is no oversight waiting for his critics to discover it but a phenomenon his own third volume sets out to derive. The critics' charge, refined by Bortkiewicz, is therefore not that Marx forgot prices differ from values. It is that the derivation he eventually offered does not close, that the bridge between the abstraction and the concrete is structurally unsound at the inputs. Defenders who answer the crude charge, Marx never claimed prices equal values, are answering a claim no serious critic made, and markers downgrade the defence as a failure to meet the actual objection.

### Did Marx believe commodities actually exchange at their values?

No, and the answer matters. The first volume's exchange at values is a methodological bracket, adopted to isolate the production of surplus from its distribution, and the third volume exists to remove the bracket. The serious dispute is whether the removal succeeds, not whether the bracket was a blunder about the world.

This is why the three sentences are ordered as they are, and why the candidate should resist improving them. The sequence, assumption, complication, defect, mirrors the text's own architecture, abstraction, derivation, and the derivation's gap, and a marker reading the three sentences hears the architecture recognised. Candidates tempted to compress the problem into a single sentence about inconsistency lose the architecture and with it the means of explaining why the repairs differ: each school is a decision about where the architecture failed, at the definitions, at the timing, or at the very idea that two systems needed bridging. The assumption, properly understood, is the candidate's protection against the two formulations this guide forbids, since a bracket deliberately adopted cannot be refuted by the phenomenon it brackets, and a derivation that fails at the inputs cannot be rescued by pointing at the bracket's innocence. Both errors are failures to see what kind of claim the first volume is making, and the seeing is teachable, which is why this section exists.

## Terminology traps: the words that change meaning

This topic's vocabulary is a minefield of words that carry one meaning in ordinary speech, another in the classical economists, and a third in Marx, and examinations are lost in the gaps. Exploitation is the widest gap. In ordinary speech it is a moral term, and candidates write as if the theory were an accusation. In the theory it is a technical ratio, the division of the working day, and it is compatible, as a measurement, with rising real wages and shortening hours, a conjunction moralised scripts treat as impossible. Value is the second gap: in Smith it wavers between labour embodied and labour commanded, a wavering Marx diagnosed and candidates reproduce, and in ordinary speech it means usefulness or price indifferently, neither of which is the theory's magnitude. Capital is the third: a stock of things in ordinary and much professional usage, a social relation in the theory, and the candidate who writes about capital accumulating as if it were a pile of machines has lost the comparison row's central point before the row began.

The smaller traps are as costly for being less visible. Productive labour, in the theory, means labour productive of surplus value, not labour that is useful, and whole answers have foundered on the compliment the candidate thought the term was paying. Crisis and tendency are not synonyms, as the border section established. Rent, interest, and profit are forms of one surplus, not three independent incomes, and the candidate who explains each by a separate story has missed the architecture. Even the word theory shifts: the labour theory of value, as the comparison row showed, is no price forecast awaiting its test, and the candidate who asks only whether it is true in that sense will mishandle every version question in the cluster. The working rule is definitional hygiene: the first time a loaded term does serious work in an answer, the script should fix its technical meaning in a clause, and thereafter hold it fixed. Markers reward the hygiene disproportionately, because it is the visible sign that the candidate's knowledge is of the theory and not of the talk about the theory.

## Productive and unproductive labour: the boundary that moves the figures

The distinction between productive and unproductive labour sounds like a relic and functions in the examination as a precision instrument. In the theory's usage, labour is productive when it is exchanged against capital and produces surplus value, and unproductive when it is exchanged against revenue, however useful, skilled, or exhausting it may be. The commercial employee, the clerk, the teacher in a private school run for profit, the state functionary: the classification turns on the social form of the exchange, not on the dignity or the materiality of the work, and Marx's own examples are chosen to make the impersonality of the criterion unmistakable. Questions on the distinction appear in papers on services, on the changing structure of employment, and on the theory's applicability to economies where manual production is a minority pursuit, and they are set precisely because the distinction separates candidates who hold the technical meaning from candidates who moralise it.

### Why does the productive and unproductive distinction matter beyond its own question?

Because it moves the measurements. Whether commercial and financial labour counts as producing surplus or as a deduction from it changes every computed rate in the empirical literature, which is one root of the contested figures this guide forbids candidates to cite without their conventions.

The distinction also guards a flank of the comparison row. The candidate who meets the objection that a theory of industrial labour cannot speak to a service economy has the reply ready in its exact form: the theory's categories were never tied to material production, the singer employed by an impresario is productive in the technical sense and the same singer performing for a fee from revenue is not, and the boundary's mobility is a feature of a theory about social form rather than a defect discovered by the service sector. What the distinction will not do, and the honest answer concedes, is settle by itself the measurement disputes: two researchers can share the criterion and still draw the boundary differently around finance, advertising, and the state, and their computed magnitudes will diverge accordingly, which is the contested figures protocol arriving by another door. The boundary question is therefore best answered as the cluster answers everything, by naming the schools' placements of the boundary and grading the consequences, and the candidate who treats it as a definition to recite will score exactly what a recitation is worth.

## Money in the theory: the question behind the monetary expression

A family of questions in the cluster turns on money, and candidates underrate them because the word sounds elementary. The difficulty is real. Marx's value theory is stated in a world of commodity money, where the money commodity itself has a value and the expression of values in money is an expression in a produced thing. The modern economy's money is not a commodity, and the question whether the theory survives the change is a genuine one that examiners in monetary-minded papers will set. The New Interpretation's central device, the monetary expression of labour time, is best understood as the leading answer: it defines the link between labour time and money directly, as the ratio of money value added to the living labour expended, so that no money commodity is required and the aggregate conservations can be stated for a fiat money economy. The macro-monetary readings go further and hold that Marx's theory was monetary from its first page, that value exists only as expressed in money, and that the search for a pre-monetary magnitude was the simultaneist tradition's founding error.

For the examination, the candidate needs the dispute's shape and its exam deployment. The shape is a fork: either value magnitudes exist prior to and independently of their monetary expression, in which case the transformation is a real problem about two systems, or value is constituted in monetary expression, in which case much of the transformation problem dissolves and a different problem, the determination of the monetary expression itself, takes its place. The deployment is that money questions are criterion questions in disguise. An answer that announces its fork, states which side the named schools occupy, and grades the dissolution against the charge that it defines the problem away has done everything the band asks. An answer that treats money as a veil to be mentioned and dropped has missed the one point on which the modern literature has genuinely moved, and markers in the monetary papers, who set the question because their field moved, will record the miss.

## The candidate's library: whose summaries to trust, and for what

Revision time is finite, and the secondary literature on this cluster is a terrain of gateways and partisan guides in which the choice of door determines the answer's accent. A word about the doors is therefore examination advice and not bibliography. Sweezy's presentation remains the classical gateway to the transformation dispute, lucid and orderly, and its framing, Bortkiewicz's correction as the problem's settled form, is the framing the candidate will meet in most textbooks; read it for the problem's grammar and remember that its settlement is the simultaneist one, which later schools exist to contest. The large treatises, Mandel's above all, give the tradition's own systematic statement at length and are unmatched for the range of topics a broad paper might touch, at the price of a confidence in the doctrine that the candidate must learn to discount when converting treatise into answer. The scholarly histories of Marxian economics, of which Howard and King's remains the standard survey in English, supply the directory's connective tissue, who replied to whom, in which journal, with what effect, and are the fastest route to the attribution skill this guide prices highest.

Introductions written from inside a school should be read as what they are, the school's best case, and used accordingly. Heinrich's introduction carries the value-form reading with unusual clarity and will improve any candidate's first chapter. The monetary and temporal literatures each have their programmatic statements, and the candidate preparing an evaluative answer should read one from each side rather than two from one, since the examination's whole demand is the fair statement of both. What the library cannot supply is the one thing this guide exists to teach: the conversion of all that reading into answers shaped like the questions. Read the doors for knowledge, in the order the cluster's map suggests, and return to the matrix for the performance, and distrust any single volume, including the treatises of the tradition itself, that promises the dispute is simpler than the directory shows.

## Imperialism in the economics paper: the monopoly stage and the export of capital

Between the cluster's core and the dependency row sits a body of theory that economics and politics papers examine under the name of imperialism, and it has its own answer craft. The tradition's sequence is canonical and should be stated as a sequence. Hobson, a liberal critic writing outside the Marxist tradition, located the drive to expansion in underconsumption at home: maldistributed income left savings without domestic outlet, and the surplus sought foreign fields, dragging flags behind it. Hilferding systematised the domestic transformation, the fusion of bank and industrial capital into finance capital and the turn from competition toward monopoly and protection. Lenin's wartime pamphlet gathered the sequence into the doctrine candidates meet: imperialism as capitalism's monopoly stage, defined by concentration, finance capital, the export of capital as distinct from the export of commodities, the division of the world among combines and then among powers, and the completion of that territorial division as the trigger for its redivision by war. Luxemburg's contribution stands apart and should not be fused with Lenin's: her accumulation argument held that expanded reproduction requires buyers outside the capitalist system, making the non-capitalist milieu a structural necessity rather than a stage, an argument conducted, as the schemas section noted, inside the second volume's machinery and disputed there ever since.

### How does an imperialism answer avoid becoming a history essay?

By keeping the mechanisms in front of the events. The examinable content is the claimed machinery, surplus seeking outlet, capital exported, monopoly reorganising competition, and the events earn their place only as the machinery's exhibits. An answer that narrates the scramble for territory has described the stage; the marks are in the motors.

Evaluation on this row has its own canonical moves. Kautsky's ultra-imperialism, the conjecture that the great capitals might jointly exploit the world and suspend their rivalry, is the internal counter-thesis and should be stated in its own terms before it is graded against the intervening century. The empirical objections, that capital exports flowed mainly among developed economies rather than to the colonies, that the newest territories were rarely the most profitable, are the standard external critique and should be given their weight. The dependency tradition then appears in its correct relation, as the imperialism literature's second life, shifting the question from the exporter to the imported structure, and the candidate who draws that line cleanly has connected two rows of the wider syllabus in a way markers notice. As everywhere in this cluster, the versions must be separated before they are judged: Hobson's mechanism can fail while Hilferding's description stands, and Lenin's stage theory is a further claim built on both, not a synonym for either.

## Lineage questions: Smith, Ricardo, Marx, and the surplus tradition

History of economic thought papers examine this cluster backwards, as the end of a line, and the lineage answer has a shape of its own. The classical economists, Smith and Ricardo at their head, shared an approach the candidate should be able to name: the surplus approach, in which the economy produces more than the replacement of its inputs and the subsistence of its producers, and the theoretical problem is the surplus's size and distribution among the classes that claim it, wages, profit, and rent, with prices formed as the surplus is distributed. Ricardo's difficulty, the dependence of relative prices on distribution, is the lineage's internal wound, and Marx's transformation is best presented in this room as the wound's attempted surgery inside the classical frame. The marginal revolution then appears as the rival programme's founding: the surplus framework abandoned, value relocated to subjective valuation at the margin, distribution dissolved into the pricing of factor services. Sraffa's slim book of 1960 is the lineage's strange coda, a reconstruction of the classical approach from inside the marginal era, which is why the Sraffian critique of Marx carries the peculiar force this guide has recorded: it is the classical tradition judging its own most famous heir and finding the heir's detour unnecessary.

Answers in this room earn their marks by precision about inheritance. What Marx took from Ricardo is specific: the labour theory in its Ricardian form, the surplus framework, the falling rate's first sketch in the pressure of diminishing returns on the margin of cultivation, which Marx replaced with his own mechanism of rising composition, a replacement lineage answers should mark as the point where the pupil's system departs from the master's. What Marx added is equally specific: labour power as the commodity, the distinction between labour and its capacity, which dissolves Ricardo's confusion about what wages buy, and the claim that the categories themselves are historical formations. The candidate who can run the inheritance in both directions, debts and departures, and then place Sraffa as the tradition's return, possesses the lineage answer entire, and the questions in this room, which look like history, are revealed as the cluster's theory questions wearing their genealogy.

## A revision programme for the cluster, in prose

Candidates ask how to revise this efficiently, and the honest answer is a programme rather than a tip. The programme runs on the instruments, not the topics, and a fortnight is enough if the instruments are taken in the right order. The opening days belong to the statements: the carrying definition, the three distinctions of the labour theory row, the three ratios in words, until each can be written from memory without hesitation, because everything else in the programme is commentary on those statements and commentary cannot be rehearsed before the text exists. The middle days belong to the directory: one critic per sitting, stated at full strength, paired with the canonical reply and the school that owns it, worked as recognition drills in both directions until the misattributions the objective papers favour have lost their power. The three sentences are written daily throughout, by hand, because their function is reflexive and reflexes are built by repetition, not by understanding alone.

The later days belong to the rows as performances: one timed answer per day, rotating through the matrix, planned from the row and marked against the row, with the examiners' reports checklist applied to the finished script before the next day's reading begins. The final days belong to consolidation and to the borders: the distinction between this cluster and the crisis material rehearsed once, the dependency versions gated once, the contested figures protocol read aloud once, and then the matrix reconstructed entire from memory, which is the programme's graduation exercise. A candidate who completes it has not read everything, and should not try to: the programme's wager, which the structure of the examinations justifies, is that the instruments recur in every question while the topics merely host them, and that marks follow the instruments home.

## The countertendencies: why the tendency is a tendency

The third volume does not leave its own law undefended, and the chapters that follow the tendency's statement carry a catalogue of counteracting influences that every answer on the profit rate must be able to reproduce, because the catalogue is the difference between a tendency and a prophecy. Marx lists the rising intensity of exploitation, in which a faster and more supervised working day yields more surplus from the same variable capital. He lists the depression of wages below the value of labour power, which he flags as empirically widespread while excluding it from the theory's pure form. He lists the cheapening of constant capital's elements, the same productivity growth that raises the composition in use values lowering it in value terms, so that the numerator of the composition grows more slowly than its material bulk suggests. He lists relative overpopulation, which holds down wages and opens spheres where hand labour keeps composition low. He lists foreign trade, which cheapens both inputs and wage goods while raising the scale of production. And he lists the growth of share capital, whose dividends behave like interest and withdraw a portion of capital from the equalisation that presses on the industrial rate.

### Why does the catalogue protect the law from its own evidence?

Because a tendency with named counterforces makes claims about pressure rather than about a graph. A measured rate that stands still through mechanisation can be read, inside the theory, as the counterforces matching the tendency for a period. The candidate who grasps this states the law and its brakes in one breath and is never trapped by either number.

The catalogue also supplies the examination's favourite complication, since each counterforce can be developed into a question of its own, and the strongest profit-rate answers treat the section as a contest between the tendency and its catalogue rather than as a law with footnotes. The cheapening counterforce deserves particular care, because it is the tendency turning against itself: the same rise in productivity that expels living labour cheapens the dead labour that replaces it, and whether the value composition rises at all depends on the race between those two effects, which is why the careful statement in this guide's profit rate section defines the composition in value terms from the outset. An answer that can run the race in both directions, and locate the Okishio theorem as a claim about one season of it, has mastered the row.

## Breakdown and its critics: the Grossman question

Between the tendency literature and the crisis literature stands a sterner construction that examinations occasionally set and candidates consistently mishandle, the breakdown theory associated with Henryk Grossman's work of 1929. Grossman took the reproduction schemas in their expanded form and followed a numerical model of accumulation, built on Otto Bauer's figures, through successive cycles. As accumulation proceeds on his assumptions, the surplus pool, though growing absolutely, becomes insufficient at a calculable point to sustain both the enlarged constant capital's requirements and the capitalists' consumption at its customary level while accumulation continues at its customary pace. The system, on this construction, does not merely wobble: it runs out of the surplus its own expansion demands, and breakdown arrives as an arithmetic destination rather than a political hope. Grossman himself surrounded the result with qualifications, treating breakdown as a tendency modified by counteracting factors, among them the cheapening of constant capital, new spheres of investment, and the devalorisation crises accomplish, so that the theory's careful form is a claim about where accumulation's logic points, not a date.

The criticisms are canonical and should be marshalled in their order. The model's assumptions do the work: a fixed rate of accumulation, a fixed capitalist consumption norm, and no cheapening of constant capital are stipulated rather than derived, and relaxing any one postpones the destination indefinitely. Sweezy's influential treatment grants the construction its ingenuity while denying that the destination survives those relaxations. A second objection concerns the schemas themselves: a model built to display proportionality conditions cannot yield a breakdown date without importing the very rigidities the schemas abstract from. A third, from within the tradition, objects that breakdown theory replaces the political and class analysis of crisis with a mechanism that operates behind everyone's back, making collapse automatic and strategy ornamental, an objection the candidate should recognise as an argument about what kind of theory Marxism wants to be. In the examination room the topic's value is diagnostic: the candidate who can separate Grossman's construction from Marx's tendency, and both from the crisis theories altogether, has demonstrated the sorting skill this cluster exists to certify, and the answer that fuses them into a general prediction of collapse has demonstrated its absence.

## Monopoly capital as examined material

A family of questions examines the mid-twentieth-century recasting of the tradition around monopoly, and the candidate should be able to state its central substitution precisely. In the Baran and Sweezy construction, the competitive capitalism of the classical texts has been superseded by the giant corporation, and the theory's central category changes accordingly: where competitive capitals were driven to accumulate by rivalry, the monopoly firm generates an economic surplus, defined as the gap between what society produces and the costs of producing it, which tends to rise as a share of output because monopoly prices and falling costs widen the gap from both sides. The system's problem then inverts the schemas' problem. The difficulty is no longer producing the surplus but absorbing it, since capitalist consumption and investment outlets cannot grow as fast as the surplus itself, and the gap is filled by the sales effort, by civilian government spending, and above all in their account by military expenditure, with stagnation as the normal state that these absorptions merely interrupt.

Answers on this material earn their evaluation marks on three discriminations. The first is the category change itself: surplus in this construction is not surplus value, and the candidate must say what replaces what and what the replacement costs, since critics charge that the broader numerator loses the production relation that made the older concept an instrument of class analysis. The second is the stagnation thesis against its evidence, the long postwar boom standing as the obvious obstacle, which the theory meets by treating the boom as the absorption mechanisms' success rather than the tendency's absence, a defence whose price in falsifiability the strong answer names. The third is the connection back to the tendency literature: absorption failure is this school's form of the realisation problem, and the candidate who can trace the line from the second volume's departments through Luxemburg's outside market to the sales effort has unified three regions of the syllabus in one movement, which is exactly the species of connection the top band exists to reward.

## Unequal exchange: the transfer debate inside the theory

Development papers also examine a refinement that stays inside the value framework while redrawing its geography, the unequal exchange argument associated with Arghiri Emmanuel and developed by Samir Amin. Its premise is a pair of asymmetries. Capital moves across borders and equalises profit rates internationally, as competition does inside an economy, while labour does not move, and wages, set by each society's history and institutions, remain vastly unequal between centre and periphery for labour of comparable productivity. From those premises the conclusion follows with uncomfortable neatness: commodities exchange at international prices of production formed with a common profit rate but radically unequal wages, so the low wage economy systematically hands over more embodied labour than it receives, and trade that is equal in price is unequal in labour. Exploitation, on this argument, is joined by transfer, and the periphery's poverty is reproduced through the exchange relation itself rather than only through what happens inside its own workplaces.

The evaluation circuit is well mapped and should be travelled completely. The empirical premise can be challenged at both ends, since wage gaps partly reflect productivity gaps the argument assumes away. The theoretical premise invites a subtler objection: if wages are the independent variable, the argument explains trade's effects by distribution rather than by production, and some critics, Charles Bettelheim most prominently in the exchange of letters appended to Emmanuel's book, located the flaw exactly there, insisting that the transfer be derived from the development of the productive forces rather than posited through wage levels. The candidate should also place the argument against dependency proper: Emmanuel's mechanism requires no monopoly and no political domination to operate, which makes it at once more elegant and more disturbing than Frank's, and an answer that grades the two theories as rival explanations of the same impoverishment, with different machinery and different remedies, performs the plural statement this cluster's examinations are built to reward.

## Formal and real subsumption: the labour process in the examination

One further region of the first volume generates its own questions, particularly where sociology and political economy papers overlap, and it turns on a distinction candidates often meet only in passing. Marx describes capital's takeover of production in two phases. Under formal subsumption, capital takes over a labour process it finds already existing, the handicraft workshop and the domestic industry, and lengthens and disciplines it without yet transforming its technique; surplus is extracted chiefly in its absolute form, by extending the working day. Under real subsumption, capital rebuilds the process itself: cooperation, the division of labour in manufacture, and finally machinery and large scale industry revolutionise technique continuously, and surplus is extracted chiefly in its relative form, by cheapening the wage goods that define necessary labour. The distinction carries a periodisation, a theory of technology's motor, and a bridge to the sociology of work, where Braverman's study of the degradation of labour under monopoly capital extended the argument into the office and made the separation of conception from execution its central charge.

Examination answers on this region divide on prediction. The candidate who treats the distinction as a stage theory with dates will be caught by the evidence of mixture, formal arrangements persisting inside advanced economies and real subsumption reaching into agriculture and services at uneven pace. The candidate who treats it as a typology of extraction, two forms that coexist and combine, can use the distinction analytically: piece wages and the working day struggle belong to the first form's logic, automation and the productivity race to the second, and a single industry's history usually shows both forms at war. The strongest answers also mark the distinction's limit, which is that it describes capital's side of the process and must be completed by the resistance it provokes, the struggles over the day's length and the machine's pace standing in the text as the labour process's other author.

## Command words: the verb is the question

Candidates lose marks to verbs more quietly than to any other single cause, because the verbs look interchangeable and are not. Each command word in this cluster's papers specifies a different performance, and the strong candidate reads the verb as the question's true subject. Analyse asks for decomposition: the claim taken apart into its versions, its presuppositions, and its moving parts, with no verdict required and no credit for one offered early. Assess and evaluate ask for the weighing machinery this guide has described, named objections at full strength against named defences, ending in a distributed judgement. Critically evaluate adds an instruction candidates miss: the criteria of judgement themselves are in play, so the answer may ask by what standard, explanatory power, consistency, empirical reach, a school should be graded, and defend the standard it applies. To what extent is the most demanding of the common forms, because it forbids both the flat yes and the flat no and requires a calibrated position, the claim holding under these conditions, for these versions, against these objections, and failing beyond them.

Compare and contrast distribute their marks differently from all the above: the credit follows the axes of comparison, and the candidate who spends the answer describing each theory in turn, leaving the comparison to a final paragraph, has written two summaries and one gesture. Discuss is the loosest verb and the most treacherous, since it licenses the rambling tour; the strong candidate disciplines it by importing the structure of assess, sorting first and judging last, while keeping the conversational breadth the verb permits. A revision habit follows from all of this. Every practice answer in the final fortnight should be planned twice, once under its actual verb and once under a different one, because the pair of plans teaches the hands what the verbs demand faster than any taxonomy, and the candidate who has felt the same material reorganise itself under analyse and then under to what extent will never again treat the verb as decoration.

## The value-form scholarship: from Rubin onward

Behind the value-form school that this guide has listed among the five stands a scholarly lineage the researcher and the strong undergraduate should both know, because it explains why that school states the theory as it does. Isaak Rubin, writing in the Soviet Union in the 1920s, argued in his essays on the theory of value that Marx's subject was never labour as a physiological expenditure but labour in its social form, and that the theory's categories, value, money, abstract labour, describe the relations through which private labours are socially validated in a commodity society. His work was suppressed with its author and recovered decades later, arriving in Western debate as a rediscovery that reframed the older embodied labour reading as a physicalism Marx had already transcended. In Germany, a parallel recovery proceeded through close reading of the first chapter's revisions across editions, the current associated with Backhaus and Reichelt and often called the new reading of Marx, which treated the value-form analysis and the monetary theory as the theory's core and the transformation literature's preoccupations as a Ricardian inheritance to be sloughed off.

For the examination candidate, this scholarship matters in a practical form. It explains why two scripts can disagree about the most basic question, the content of the labour theory of value, while both citing the first chapter accurately: they are reading different strata of a text whose author revised its presentation repeatedly, and whose opening exists in materially different versions. It explains the money question's sharpness in this school, since on the form reading money is not a convenience added to barter but the necessary mode of appearance of value, so that a non-monetary statement of the theory is not a simplification but a different theory, the premise the monetary expression defence also builds on from the other side. And it gives the candidate a final sorting instrument: when a question's wording turns on form, appearance, or fetishism, the form school's apparatus is the relevant one, whatever the candidate's own allegiance, in the same way that a derivation question summons the simultaneist apparatus. Matching the school's tools to the question's register is the last refinement of the plural statement.

## The long essay: when the matrix becomes coursework

Not every assessment in this cluster is timed, and the coursework essay, the long seminar paper, the dissertation chapter, rewards the same instruments at a different tempo. The differences are worth stating because candidates regularly import timed habits into untimed work and are marked down for virtues in the wrong room. The long essay can and must show its working in the sources: where the timed answer cites the transformation chapters by location, the coursework version quotes sparingly under the discipline this guide's library section set out, engages the editions, and lets the reader watch a reconstruction being chosen on argued grounds rather than inherited from a lecture. The directory of critics expands into a literature review with its own architecture, the objections grouped by the premise they attack rather than by their authors' chronology. The verdict, which the timed answer compresses into a paragraph, becomes a defended position with its alternatives explicitly priced, the candidate stating what evidence or argument would move it.

What does not change is the order of operations. The plural statement still precedes the verdict, the versions are still sorted before they are graded, and the contested figures still travel with their conventions attached, indeed with more apparatus, since the coursework marker has the time to check the footnotes the examination marker must take on trust. The matrix serves the long essay as a planning instrument exactly as it serves the timed paper: the row supplies the schools, the formulation, the evaluation, and the trap, and the essay is the row unfolded at the length the assessment allows. Candidates who grasp that continuity stop experiencing coursework and examinations as different subjects and start experiencing them as the same subject at different speeds, which is what their teachers intended all along.

## Whole-paper strategy: choosing and sequencing questions

The final craft this guide teaches operates above the level of the single answer, on the paper as a whole. Where choice is offered, the selection is itself an examined act, and it should be made by inventory rather than by affection. The candidate's first pass through the paper sorts the questions by row, noting which instruments each demands, and chooses the set that maximises the overlap with rehearsed instruments, resisting the question on a beloved topic whose evaluation machinery was never practised. A transformation question attempted by a candidate whose three sentences are reflexes is worth more than a dependency question on material read once with enthusiasm, because the bands reward performed competence over remembered enthusiasm every time.

Sequencing follows from the same logic. The strongest row goes first, while the hand is fresh and the clock is generous, because a confident first answer settles the candidate and banks marks against later accidents. The weakest chosen question goes in the middle, where momentum carries it, and never last, where a faltering finish colours the marker's parting impression of the script. Time is allocated by the marks, not by the candidate's feelings about the topics, and the allocation is written on the question paper before the first answer begins, since mid-paper arithmetic is where allocations go to die. None of this is glamorous, and all of it is decisive at the margins where bands are separated, which is why this guide ends its teaching here: the candidate who has mastered the field and the paper together has nothing left to be taught by a guide, and should go and sit the examination.

## The measurement literature: what numbers questions actually test

A small but growing family of questions presents candidates with the empirical literature directly, the profit rate series, the labour value and price correspondence studies, the exploitation estimates, and asks for assessment, and these questions fail more candidates than any other in the cluster because they appear to ask about numbers. They ask about conventions. Every series in this literature is a construction, and the construction choices determine the result's direction before a single observation is consulted. Capital may be valued at historic cost, what was actually paid, or at current replacement cost, what the stock would cost to reproduce now, and the two measures can move in opposite directions across an inflationary period, carrying the computed profit rate with them. Depreciation may be charged geometrically, by book convention, or by the tax authorities' schedules. The boundary between productive and unproductive labour, this guide's earlier section, decides whether commercial, financial, and state employment enters the surplus's production or its consumption, and with it the size of every rate the series reports.

The correspondence studies pose the convention problem in its statistical form. The finding that market prices and labour values correlate closely across industries is the most quoted result in the empirical literature, and the assessment question is what the correlation proves. Critics observe that any two measures of industry size correlate, prices and values both scaling with output, so that a high correlation may certify size rather than regulation, and the literature's refinement, measuring deviations rather than levels, exists to meet that objection. The strong candidate travels this whole circuit: states the result, names the construction choices beneath it, states the size objection, states the refinement, and concludes that the empirical literature grades the schools rather than vindicating one, the temporal school claiming the historic cost series as its home ground and the simultaneist literature claiming the current cost ones. An answer of that shape can survive any figures the question supplies, which is precisely why the questions are set: they test whether the candidate reads a number as a claim about the world or as a claim about a measuring instrument, and the contested figures protocol generalises the lesson to the whole cluster.

## Exploitation without the labour theory: the analytical challenge in depth

Roemer's construction appeared earlier in this guide as a name in the directory; it deserves a fuller statement, because politics and philosophy papers examine it as a topic in its own right and because its shape teaches the cluster's deepest lesson about what a theory is for. The construction begins by discarding prices and values and rebuilding exploitation from assets. A group is exploited, in the general definition, when it would be better off withdrawing from the economy with its per capita share of society's alienable assets, and its complement worse off, a definition framed deliberately in the language of counterfactual coalitions that game theory supplies. From that austere starting point the familiar results are regenerated as theorems: exploitation tracks the unequal distribution of productive assets, labour markets and credit markets appear as alternative mechanisms for effecting the same transfers, and the moral charge conventionally carried by the word exploitation is located, explicitly, in the asset distribution rather than in any transaction at the point of production.

The debate that followed is the assessment material. G. A. Cohen, defending the classical intuition against the construction's own author, argued that the traditional claim rested on the labour theory not as a price theory but as a claim about who produces what the privileged consume, and that this claim survives the price theory's falsification intact. Roemer himself, in later work, followed his definition's logic toward a general theory of distributive justice, treating the Marxian construction as a station on a road that continues past it, which examiners use to test whether candidates can follow an argument that leaves its founding text behind. The examination point, for this guide's purposes, is the one the comparison row states abstractly: an answer on this material is an answer about what exploitation means and whether the meaning survives the machinery, and the candidate who can present the construction fairly, Cohen's rescue fairly, and the founder's departure fairly has performed the plural statement on the cluster's hardest case.

## The Kalecki border: effective demand and the profit equation

One neighbouring construction belongs in every complete map of this cluster, because examinations set it as the comparison that flatters neither camp. Michal Kalecki, working independently in the 1930s, built a macroeconomics of capitalism from Marxian materials, the reproduction schemas above all, and arrived at results the Keynesian literature reached by another road. His central relation can be stated without symbols: in a closed economy without a state sector, aggregate profits equal capitalists' investment plus capitalists' consumption, a sentence whose direction of causation is its whole point, since capitalists as a class earn what they spend, while workers spend what they earn. Pricing in his construction is by mark-up over prime costs, the degree of monopoly setting the mark-up and thereby the distribution of income between wages and profits, so that distribution is decided in the pricing decisions of firms rather than in any factor market.

The assessment circuit for this material runs on independence and priority. Kalecki derived his system from the schemas before the General Theory appeared, and the priority question is examinable precisely because it is documentary rather than evaluative. The comparison with the mainstream then differs from this guide's comparison row in an instructive way: here the rival is not marginal productivity but Keynesian effective demand, and the candidate must compare two theories of demand failure, one grounded in investment's psychology and liquidity preference, the other in distribution and the mark-up, before judging which better explains the phenomena each was built for. Steindl's maturity and stagnation extended the construction toward the monopoly capital school's territory, completing a triangle, schemas to Kalecki to Baran and Sweezy, that the strongest candidates draw explicitly. The border matters to this cluster because it marks where the Marxist economics tradition shades into a neighbouring research programme that shares its ancestry and disputes its conclusions, and examinations, which exist to test discrimination, will always hunt along borders.

## Interest, credit, and fictitious capital: the third volume's second mountain

Candidates prepare the third volume as though it ended at the transformation chapters, and papers punish the omission, because the volume's later parts carry topics with their own questions. The division of surplus value continues there: profit splits into interest, paid to the money capitalist who owns the capital, and profit of enterprise, retained by the functioning capitalist who operates it, and Marx's analysis insists that the split makes each part misdescribe itself, interest appearing as the natural fruit of ownership and the remainder as a wage of management. Interest bearing capital itself, money lent to return with an increment, is presented as the most fetishised form in the whole system, value apparently breeding value without the mediation of production, and the credit system built on it acquires, in the analysis, a double character: it accelerates accumulation, centralises capitals, and socialises the form of capital in the joint stock company, while building the claims structure whose periodic collapse is the crisis's financial face.

The term fictitious capital names the volume's most examinable construction in this region. Shares, bonds, and titles to future revenue are priced by capitalising the income they are expected to yield at the going rate of interest, and their prices therefore move with expectations and interest rates, not with the value of any capital they might be said to represent; the same real capital can be duplicated on paper, and the paper wealth can swell and vanish while the factories stand unchanged. Hilferding's finance capital grew from this soil, taking the banks' command over such paper as the system's commanding height, and the line from the third volume's fifth part to the imperialism literature runs through it. Examination answers on this region earn their marks by keeping the two evaluations distinct that candidates fuse: the analysis of the forms, what interest and fictitious capital are and why they misdescribe themselves, which belongs to the theory's permanent core, and the period claims about which institutions dominate an era, which belong to Hilferding's and Lenin's historical wager and must be graded as this guide's imperialism section grades them. A candidate who can say which of the two a given question is asking about has again performed the sorting operation, on the volume's second mountain rather than its first.

## Long waves: the tendency stretched across half centuries

A last family of questions stretches the cluster's mechanisms across long periods, and it should be handled with this guide's full apparatus of cautions. The long wave hypothesis, associated in its Marxist form with Ernest Mandel's elaboration of the waves Kondratieff had charted statistically, proposes that capitalist development moves in long phases of expansion and contraction, each lasting roughly a generation, driven by the profit rate's own long movements: phases of rising profitability powered by clusters of radical innovation and cheapened inputs, turning into phases of falling profitability as composition rises and markets saturate. Mandel's careful version makes the turning points asymmetric, the downturn following from the system's internal logic while the upturn requires extra economic shocks, wars, defeats of labour, new fields of investment, a qualification that saves the theory from mechanical periodicity at the price, its critics observe, of explaining each upturn by a different accident.

The assessment circuit is short and should be travelled briskly. The statistical base is thin, a handful of alleged waves across two centuries, each dated differently by different scholars, so that the hypothesis risks the fate of all pattern finding in short series. The mechanism, by contrast, is respectable, being nothing but the tendency and its countertendencies read at generational scale, and the strong answer says exactly that: the long wave is the profit rate literature in period costume, to be judged by the same standards, its directions arguable and its dates inadmissible as settled fact. Candidates should also mark the family's boundary with the crisis cluster, since the long wave's contraction phases host the crises that the neighbouring guide treats in their own right, and a sentence locating the border protects the answer from drifting into a neighbouring article's territory mid paragraph. Handled so, the topic becomes what it should be in the examination: not a prophecy to be believed or dismissed, but a trial of whether the candidate can hold a mechanism steady while its timescale changes.

## Rent: the third claimant on the surplus

The third volume's final distribution topic completes the trinity of revenues, and development and geography papers examine it where economics papers sometimes neglect it. Marx's rent theory begins where Ricardo's stopped. Differential rent arises, as in Ricardo, from unequal fertility and location: capitals applied to better land produce at lower individual cost while the market price is set by the worst land under cultivation, so the surplus profit on the better plots is captured by the landowner as rent, a transfer of surplus produced elsewhere in the system rather than a creation of the soil. Marx's extension runs in two directions. The second form of differential rent follows capital applied successively to the same land, with diminishing returns on the later doses, raising the question of who pockets the difference when the tenant's own investment improves the plot. And absolute rent answers the puzzle Ricardo could not: why the worst land also pays rent. Marx's construction rests on agriculture's lower composition of capital in his period, which made agricultural values stand above prices of production, with landed property acting as a barrier that prevents the excess from entering the general equalisation and diverts it to the landlord instead.

### Why does absolute rent test the transformation rather than merely repeat it?

Because its machinery presupposes the completed transformation. The argument needs values standing above prices of production in a low composition sector and a property barrier that arrests the flow between them, so a candidate who cannot run the third volume's price formation cannot state the rent theory's central claim at all.

The assessment circuit for rent is the cluster's circuit in miniature. The construction's empirical premise, agriculture's lower composition, has been overtaken in the developed economies by mechanisation, and the candidate should say so and then ask what survives: the differential machinery intact, the absolute form requiring either a new premise or honourable retirement, and the analytical point, that rent is a distribution of surplus governed by property rather than a reward for a factor's contribution, standing independent of both. Land and housing questions in development papers draw on exactly that point, and the candidate who arrives with it, able to separate the transfer analysis from the superseded premise, finds the rent row to be the profit rate row's gentler cousin: same sorting, lower stakes, steady marks.

## The metabolic rift and the ecological reading

One further literature has entered the examination perimeter, chiefly through sociology and political ecology papers, and candidates meeting it should know its textual anchor and its standing. The ecological reading fastens on a passage of the first volume in which Marx, drawing on the agricultural chemistry of Liebig, describes capitalist production as disturbing the metabolic interaction between human beings and the earth, exporting soil nutrients to distant cities as food and fibre and returning them as waste rather than as fertility, so that the soil's exhaustion and the town's pollution appear as two faces of one rupture. John Bellamy Foster's reconstruction built this fragment into a systematic ecological dimension of the theory, and a subsequent literature has disputed both the fragment's weight in Marx's own system and its bearing on the theory of value, since a reading that grounds crisis in material throughput sits in visible tension with readings that ground it in form and magnitude.

Examination treatment follows this cluster's standing rules. The textual anchor is genuine and should be cited by its location in the chapter on machinery and large scale industry, not inflated into a system its author never wrote. The school's claim should be stated as its holders state it, a dimension recovered, and the sceptical reply given as its holders give it, a dimension projected. And the candidate should resist the temptation, fatal in this region as in every other, to recruit the material as a verdict on present environmental policy, which is neither the literature's examinable content nor this guide's doctrine: the questions ask what the theory contains and what its interpreters have made of it, and the answers that keep to those questions are the answers the bands reward.

## Choosing a dissertation topic inside the cluster

Where a degree requires a dissertation, this cluster offers unusually tractable subjects, and choosing among them is a skill this guide can teach in a page. The tractable disputes share a property the candidate should test any proposal against: the literature disagrees about something that evidence or text can actually settle, at the scale a dissertation commands. The measurement disputes pass the test most easily. A candidate who recomputes a published profit rate series under the rival convention, historic cost against replacement cost, or redraws the productive boundary and reports how the direction of movement survives or fails, has a project whose conclusion is not known in advance and whose method the literature itself supplies. The textual disputes pass it too, at archival scale: the strata of the value-form chapter across the editions Marx revised, the editorial construction of the third volume's transformation chapters from the manuscripts Engels inherited, each supports a bounded project with primary material and a live scholarly audience.

The topics that fail the test share the opposite property, and supervisors in this field spend their autumns vetoing them. A dissertation that proposes to settle the transformation problem has mistaken the field's century for a semester. A dissertation that proposes to measure the rate of exploitation for an economy has adopted every convention dispute at once and will spend its length defending the instruments instead of reporting a finding. A dissertation that proposes to apply the theory to a present industry replicates, in slow motion, the error this guide corrects in the timed paper, staking the argument on facts the literature has not stabilised. The pattern to imitate is the matrix's own shape: choose a row, name its schools, find the joint where their claims diverge over something checkable, and build the project at that joint. The candidate who chooses so is examined, in the viva, on ground the candidate surveyed, and the defence of a bounded claim, fairly stated against its rivals, is the plural statement wearing academic dress, the final form of the skill this cluster's examinations exist to certify.

## Defending the answer aloud: seminars and the viva

Some candidates meet this cluster's material a final time in spoken examination, the tutorial defence, the seminar presentation, the dissertation viva, and the spoken forms punish different weaknesses from the written ones. The viva's characteristic trap is the invitation to collapse the plural statement under friendly pressure: the examiner who asks, with apparent sympathy, whether the candidate really believes the temporal defence works, is testing whether the sorted map survives the wish to please the room. The written discipline transfers exactly, hold the versions separate, answer for the evidence, and let the verdict arrive distributed, and the candidate who performs that transfer discovers that the examiners' follow up questions lose their menace, since each one lands on a joint the written preparation already surveyed. The seminar presentation inverts the test: the candidate chooses the ground, and the marks follow the choice's honesty, the reconstruction presented at its full strength including the part its own holders concede, the objection pressed as forcefully as its author pressed it, and the candidate's own position stated last and priced. In both rooms the failure mode is the same fusion this guide has corrected throughout, advocacy arriving before exposition, and the success mode is the same separation, the debate recognisable to its participants before it is judged by its presenter.

## A note on translations and editions in the examination

One small discipline protects candidates in every room, and it costs a single sentence to observe. The English Marx of the examination hall exists in more than one translation, and the translations diverge at points this cluster cares about: renderings of the terms for valorisation, for labour power's value, and for the forms of appearance differ between the older Moore and Aveling version, prepared under Engels's eye, and the later Fowkes translation that most courses assign, and a quotation carried from one into an answer framed on the other can wobble at exactly the clause the argument needs. The safe practice is consistency: cite the course's edition, quote only what has been checked in that edition, and where a term itself is under dispute, name the original concept in prose once, as this guide named the monetary expression, so that the marker can see the candidate knows a translation choice is in play. Markers, for their part, are instructed by their own experience to credit the argument across translations and to penalise only the candidate who appears unaware that a choice exists, which makes the single signalling sentence the cheapest insurance in the whole cluster.

## For students and exam candidates

Your preparation for this topic reduces to a small number of rehearsed instruments, and the instruments are few because the demands examiners make are stable. Learn the plurality opening until naming the schools is reflex rather than decision. Learn the three-sentence transformation until you can write it under any conditions, because no paragraph's absence is punished so reliably across the whole cluster. Learn the three ratios in words, the directory of critics with their replies attached, and the contested figures protocol until describing a direction under a named convention feels more natural than quoting a number. The full sequence of preparation, from first reading to the morning of the paper, is laid out in the series [marxism revision guide](/2022/01/15/marxism-revision-guide/), and this cluster should be revised inside that sequence rather than as a freestanding emergency.

**In economics papers,** expect the discipline's dialect: the models handled directly, the notation welcome where the question is technical, the derivation priced by its steps. Your performance there is built from the cluster's technical articles and from timed derivations rehearsed until the joints, the places where conventions enter, are places you can narrate.

**In politics and development papers,** the same knowledge must change clothes. State the theory's claims in prose, keep symbols out of sight, and spend your length on what the disputes mean: which version of dependency, which account of exploitation, which critic answered by which school. The marker in those rooms is buying judgement about a literature, and the candidate who delivers algebra has answered a question from a different building.

## For teachers and seminar leaders

Teach the versions before the verdicts. The single most effective change a teacher can make on this topic is to refuse, in class, the unversioned statement: every claim about the labour theory, the transformation, or the tendency should be returned to its sender with the question of which school holds it and which objection it survives. Students trained in that reflex produce the attribution discipline this guide describes without needing to be taught it as technique. The economics answer matrix works as a classroom instrument in its own right: set each row as a timed exercise, mark the scripts against the row's four columns, and let the class see that the trap column predicts their own sentences with uncomfortable accuracy, which teaches faster than any lecture on method.

In marking, reward the fair statement of the side the candidate rejects, and say so in feedback, since students calibrate their effort to what feedback praises. Penalise the unnamed critic and the unlocated figure explicitly and early; both habits extinguish quickly once they are priced. Where a syllabus permits choice, steer weaker candidates toward the comparison and dependency rows, where fair statement earns steadily, and stronger candidates toward the transformation and profit rate rows, where the sorting operations the top band describes are most densely rewarded.

## For researchers, historians, and journalists

The examination literature this guide teaches is a simplified shadow of a live research field, and the shadow's distortions are worth a researcher's notice. The disputes among the reconstructions continue in the journals with undiminished energy, increasingly organised around measurement: the monetary expression's behaviour across long series, the valuation of capital stocks, and the statistical standing of the embodied labour correspondence are active empirical fronts where the schools' claims meet data. The textual basis also continues to move, as the historical critical edition of Marx's and Engels's writings publishes further drafts and notebooks, and positions stated as settled fidelity claims should be checked against the strata the edition reveals. For the journalist, the caution is the contested figures protocol in professional dress: any profit rate or exploitation figure quoted from this literature carries a package of conventions, and a report that does not name the package has not finished its work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do you write an exam answer on Marxist economics?

Begin by translating the set question into the dispute it conceals, and let your opening paragraph name the schools that divide over it. Then build the middle out of attributed material: every position attached to the text or the school that holds it, every objection attached to its author. Hold your verdict back until the evidence for it is on the page, and when it arrives, distribute it across the versions of the claim rather than delivering it as a single cheer or condemnation. Keep one located reference from the volumes in reserve, cite figures only as directions under named conventions, and leave minutes at the end for the conclusion the band descriptors are waiting for.

### Q: Which schools of Marxist economics must you name?

The safe minimum is five, chosen for the dispute they map rather than for completeness. Bortkiewicz and the simultaneist tradition he founded, because they state the transformation problem in its corrected form. The Sraffian critique in the form Steedman gave it, because it presses the redundancy charge. The New Interpretation of Duménil and Foley and the temporal single-system interpretation, because they are the two great defences and they defend by opposite means. And the value-form reading, because a whole family of questions is about form rather than magnitude. Add Roemer where politics papers ask about exploitation without the value theory, and the Frank and Cardoso fork wherever development is examined.

### Q: How do you explain the transformation problem in an exam?

Give the marker a statement in three parts, each part doing one job. First, recall the simplifying device of the opening volume, where exchange is analysed as though goods traded in proportion to the labour their production requires. Next, bring in the equalisation of profit rates under competition, which forces the prices that capitals actually receive away from those proportions in a pattern set by their composition. Finally, pinpoint the gap in the founder's worked solution: he repriced what industries sell while leaving what they buy at the old measure. Stated that way, the problem is exact, neutral between the schools, and short enough to survive a timed paper intact.

### Q: Which formulas do you need for Marxist economics?

Three, and you need them as sentences before you need them as symbols. The rate of surplus value sets unpaid labour against paid labour and indexes exploitation. The rate of profit sets the same surplus against the entire capital advanced, which is why the two rates can move in opposite directions. The organic composition sets the means of production against the living labour employed, and its rise drives the tendency debate. In an economics paper you may write these in the notation your course uses, since the matrix in this guide shows the symbols once. In politics and development papers, leave the symbols out and let the words carry the magnitudes.

### Q: What are the common mistakes in Marxist economics answers?

The mistakes repeat so reliably that examiners list them. Presenting the labour theory as a device for predicting prices, which the third volume itself contradicts. Announcing the transformation problem as either demolished or disposed of, in a sentence with no school attached. Merging surplus value into profit, which collapses the levels the whole topic is built from. Quoting a rate of profit for some place and period as though it were a physical constant, when it is an artefact of measurement choices. Treating the field as a single doctrine with a single voice. And writing a political answer, about whether capitalism deserves to survive, to a question that asked how a mechanism works.

### Q: How do you evaluate the labour theory of value in an essay?

Sort the theory into its versions first, because each version has its own fate. The claim that individual prices are proportional to embodied labour fails, and the third volume is the first witness against it. The claim that labour magnitudes regulate exchange in the aggregate survives under named reconstructions, each at a stated price. The charge that the magnitudes are redundant, pressed from Sraffa's premises by Steedman, stands against every version that claims values explain prices rather than accompany them. Then say what the analysis still does that no price theory attempts: it divides the working day and locates the surplus. An evaluation built as that sorting, rather than as a verdict on the whole, is what the top band describes.

### Q: How do you compare Marxist and mainstream economics in an answer?

Start from what each programme is trying to explain. Mainstream economics, fairly stated, begins from given tastes, given endowments, and given techniques, and asks how decentralised exchange coordinates them through prices; credit it with its real victories, the analysis of price movements, of substitution, of allocation at the margin. The Marxist programme begins one step earlier and asks how the endowments were distributed, why most people must sell their capacity to work, and in whose hands the social surplus accumulates. Then judge by territory rather than by loyalty: the mechanics of particular prices belong largely to the first programme, the source and division of the surplus remain contested ground the first programme rarely visits, and the claim that economic categories are themselves historical belongs to the second. The answer that caricatures either side has failed before it has begun.

### Q: Which examples work for Marxist economics questions?

Choose exhibits whose credentials no marker can challenge, which means exhibits drawn from the set texts themselves. The exchange between linen and coat in the first chapter shows the value relation under construction. The spinner's day, split between the hours that replace the wage and the hours that do not, shows surplus value being produced. The two departments show reproduction balanced and unbalanced. For the development rows, the mining enclaves of Latin America and the industrial ascent of East Asia arrive with the literature's arguments already wrapped around them. What never works is the illustration fetched from the present economy, the named company or the named policy, whose facts the marker is free to dispute while your argument waits, unexamined, for the dispute to end.

### Q: Is it safe to say the transformation problem is unsolved?

It is safe in one precise form and dangerous in every other. Dangerous as a bare slogan, because a marker hears it as a verdict borrowed from a single critical source. Safe as a statement about the printed procedure and the literature's state: Marx's own worked solution is incomplete at the inputs, consistent completions exist in several schools, and no completion has commanded the field's assent, since each preserves a different part of the inheritance at a different price. Written that way, the claim is not a risk but a credential, because it is the one sentence about the problem that specialists on every side accept as the ground of their disagreement.

### Q: How do you revise Marxist economics efficiently?

Revise the instruments, not the topics, because the topics are only hosts and the instruments are what every question examines. First secure the statements that everything else comments on: the carrying definition, the three ratios stated in words, and the three-sentence transformation, written out daily until they are reflexes. Then work the directory of critics as a recognition drill, names to objections and objections to names, since evaluation and the objective papers both price attribution. Then rehearse whole answers, one matrix row at a time, against the clock, marking each against its row's four columns. Compress the schemas and the profit rate into their plans last, keep the final day for reconstructing the matrix from memory, and spend nothing on fresh reading in the final week that a rehearsal would not teach better.

### Q: How does the three-sentence transformation state the problem without algebra?

Its trick is to replace calculation with location. The first sentence tells the marker where the simplifying assumption lives, in the opening volume's method. The second identifies the force that breaks the assumption, competition equalising the return on capitals of unequal composition. The third names the exact seam where the founder's repair stops short, the inputs carried at the unconverted measure while the outputs are converted. Nothing in the three sentences needs a symbol because the problem is not, at this level, about quantities at all: it is about whether one account can be translated into another, and translation can be described completely in prose before anyone reaches for a matrix.

### Q: What should a candidate separate between explaining a debate and taking its side?

The two activities draw on different evidence and earn different credit, and mixing them corrupts both. Explanation is answerable to the texts and the schools: it succeeds when a holder of each position would accept the statement of it as fair. Allegiance is answerable only to the explanation that precedes it: it succeeds when every element of the verdict was purchased by material already on the page. A candidate who separates them writes the debate first as its participants would recognise it, and only then spends the accumulated trust on a judgement. A candidate who fuses them produces advocacy, which the marker grades as a claim about the candidate rather than about the subject.

### Q: Why does the examiner reward a named reconstruction over a verdict?

A verdict tells the examiner what the candidate concluded; a named reconstruction shows what the candidate knows. Anyone can write that the problem stands solved, or that it is fatal, and the sentence costs nothing and proves nothing. Naming the New Interpretation or the temporal school, and stating what the chosen repair preserves and what it gives up, demonstrates acquaintance with the literature's actual contents, and it gives the examiner something checkable to credit. There is a further reason: the marking team itself is divided among the schools, so a verdict can collide with a marker's own position, while an accurate account of the reconstructions is creditable from every chair in the room.

### Q: How do you plan a timed answer on the reproduction schemas?

Spend the first minutes on the machinery, stated in words: two departments, one turning out means of production and one consumption goods, and the balance condition that ties them, the requirement that the second department's constant capital match the first department's wages and surplus. Then decide, before writing, which claim the schemas will be used against, the impossibility of a general glut, the source of demand under accumulation, or the possibility of breakdown through disproportion, because a plan that names its target produces a using answer and a plan that does not produces a description. Reserve the closing minutes for the instrument's limits, the abstractions from prices, credit, and foreign trade, which is where the evaluative credit on this question type waits.

### Q: What separates an answer that describes the schemas from one that uses them?

A describing answer can be recognised by its shape: it recites the departments, states the balance condition, distinguishes simple from expanded reproduction, and stops, having proved acquaintance with a chapter. A using answer subordinates every one of those elements to a claim it is testing. It sets the schemas against the doctrine that supply constitutes its own demand, by showing that equality in value can coexist with mismatch in material form. It follows Luxemburg as she drives the machinery toward an outside market, and Tugan-Baranovsky as he drives it toward production for its own sake. Description is graded as knowledge of the schemas. Use is graded as knowledge of what they are for, and the bands price the difference.
