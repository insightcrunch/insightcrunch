Marxist replies exist for every standard criticism of the framework, and they are not of equal worth. Some of them answer the criticism they address. Some answer a smaller criticism standing nearby. Some change the subject with such fluency that generations of readers have mistaken the change for a refutation. A reader who has worked through the critiques themselves, in the survey this article accompanies and in the detailed treatments behind it, is owed a grading rather than a cheer: which replies work, which partly work, and which merely move the claim to ground where the evidence cannot follow. That grading is the business of the pages that follow. Every reply admitted here carries a named author and a named work, because a defence that circulates without an owner cannot be checked, and a defence that cannot be checked is worth exactly what an anonymous pamphlet is worth. Every verdict arrives with the condition of its own reversal attached, stated in the same breath, because a verdict that nothing could reverse is not a judgement but a loyalty. And one pattern is named at the outset and tracked throughout, since it decides how much of the rest can be believed: the habit of restating a claim at a level of generality where the disconfirming evidence no longer bears on it, and then treating the restatement as what was meant all along.

![A seminar table with the replies scorecard, annotated critiques and reply volumes, Insight Crunch](/assets/images/blog/blog-68.webp)

The canonical definition is easily stated. Marxist responses to criticism comprise reconstructions of value theory where the mathematics was challenged, arguments that a criticism misidentifies what the theory claims, appeals that separate the doctrine from the regimes which governed under its banner, and the reformulation of predictions as tendencies with counteracting influences. Each of those four families has produced serious scholarship and each has produced evasion, sometimes between the same pair of covers. The reader should therefore be suspicious of any summary, friendly or hostile, that grades the defence as a whole. Rebuttals to Marx critics differ the way the criticisms differ: the strongest of them were written by scholars solving problems, the weakest by publicists protecting a flag, and the distance between those two kinds of writing is the distance this article exists to measure.

Two exclusions govern what follows, and both protect the reader's time. The critiques themselves are not restated here beyond the sentence each reply needs to be intelligible; they belong to their own articles, beginning with the [critiques of Marxism complete guide](/2019/08/01/critiques-of-marxism-complete-guide/), and a reply quoted against a paraphrase of its target would be graded against a straw man of this article's own manufacture. Nor is any reply admitted that exists only in polemical circulation without a named author and an identifiable work. That rule costs the tradition some of its most repeated debating points, and the cost is accepted deliberately: a reply nobody signed is a reply nobody has staked a reputation on, and the scorecard below is a record of stakes.

## Marxist replies: what this article grades and how

The grading vocabulary has three terms, and they are used strictly. A reply works when it meets the criticism on the ground the criticism chose and leaves the original claim standing in something close to its original strength. A reply partly works when it defeats one version of the criticism, or defends the claim at reduced scope, and the reduction is visible in the reply itself. A reply relocates the claim when the position defended after the reply is a different position from the one attacked, whether or not the new position is true, interesting, or even better. Relocation is not lying. It is frequently how theories grow. But it is not defence, and counting it as defence is the single most common accounting error in the literature this article surveys, committed by the tradition's friends far more often than by its enemies.

### What distinguishes a reply that answers from a reply that relocates?

A reply answers when the claim that survives is the claim that was attacked, tested on the ground the critic chose. A reply relocates when the surviving claim is narrower, weaker, or pitched at a level where the critic's evidence no longer reaches it. Both may be respectable intellectual moves.

Only the first is a defence, and confusing them corrupts every verdict built on the confusion.

The test is applied row by row in the scorecard, and the distinction does more work than any other in this article. Consider what it does to a famous case before the detailed sections arrive. When the transformation problem showed that Marx's two aggregate equalities could not both hold under a consistent valuation, the tradition produced reconstructions under which both equalities do hold, at the price of redefining what the magnitudes mean. Whether that is an answer or a relocation depends entirely on whether the redefined magnitudes still say what the third volume of Capital needed them to say, and honest graders inside the tradition have disagreed about exactly that. The scorecard records the disagreement rather than resolving it by loyalty, and its verdict, partly works, is the shape most honest verdicts in this territory take.

### Have the criticisms of Marxism been answered?

some have. The class-structure critique, the ecological value objection, and part of the authoritarianism charge have strong replies. The knowledge objection to planning, the innovation version of the incentive charge, and the absence of any theory of constraint on power remain unanswered.

That short answer is the article in miniature, and the rest of the article is its audit trail. The strong replies are strong for identifiable reasons. Their authors treated each criticism as a puzzle to be solved rather than a blow to be parried. They grant the criticism its valid points openly. And they finish at a proposition definite enough for a sceptical reader to test. The unanswered items are unanswered for identifiable reasons too, and the reasons are not a shortage of ingenuity. A century of talented defenders has thrown itself at the knowledge objection and at the missing theory of constraint, and the replies on file either change the question or concede the point in the act of answering a different one. Naming that, plainly and early, is the condition on which everything else in this article asks to be believed.

## The scorecard and how to read it

The replies scorecard is the article's findable artifact, and it should be read as a ledger rather than as a league table. Each row names a critique, gives the best reply the tradition has produced, identifies the reply's source, records what the reply concedes in the act of succeeding, delivers a verdict in the three-term vocabulary, and states the defeater: the evidence or construction that would reverse the verdict in that row. The defeater column is not decoration. It is the mechanism by which the scorecard can be wrong in a checkable way, and a reader who finds a row's defeater satisfied in the literature has caught this article in an error it has promised in advance to honour.

Read the concessions column as carefully as the verdicts column. A reply's concession is the part of it that cannot be spun, because it records what the defence itself judged indefensible at the moment of defending. Across the eleven rows the concessions describe a consistent geography: magnitudes in the third volume, the proof structure of the first chapter, the epistemology of planning, the institutional theory of power. What the replies concede is, in effect, a map of where the original architecture was weakest, drawn by the architects' own heirs. A critic who wants the strongest available case against the framework could do worse than to read the concessions column alone and ask why so much had to be given up by the people most motivated to give up nothing.

## The verdict that five critiques stand unanswered, and what would defeat it

Above the individual rows sits a summary verdict, and it too must carry its defeater. The verdict is that five critiques remain unanswered: Hayek's knowledge objection raised against planning in its comprehensive form, the incentive charge in the version that concerns innovation, the missing doctrine of institutional limits on power, the premise of abundance built into the ecological critique, and Böhm-Bawerk's inequality argument against the opening chapter's derivation of value. The defeater is stated without evasion: that verdict falls the moment anyone produces a reply to any one of the five that does not relocate the claim. The invitation is genuine and the expectation, recorded here so that the record can judge it, is that the invitation will not be taken up soon, because the five have survived a century of motivated attempts by defenders cleverer than most of their critics.

### What would count as a reply that does not relocate a claim?

A qualifying reply would meet the criticism on its own ground, defend the proposition the critic actually attacked, and leave that proposition standing at something close to its original strength. It would not narrow the claim's scope, soften its modality from assertion to tendency, move its theatre from nation to globe, or substitute a neighbouring proposition the evidence cannot reach.

Replies of that purity are rare in every tradition, which is precisely why producing one would matter.

The five are not equal in kind, and the sections below keep their differences visible. Two of them, the knowledge objection and the inequality argument, are objections of logic: they allege that a piece of reasoning fails, and they can in principle be answered by better reasoning. Two, the innovation charge and the abundance premise, are objections of fact about what institutions and planets can do, answerable only by constructions or evidence that do not yet exist. One, the missing constraint on power, is an objection of omission: the theory does not contain the thing whose absence the record made expensive, and an omission cannot be answered by reinterpretation, only by supply. A defence that lumps the five together as unsolved problems understates the position. They are unsolved for three different reasons, and each reason closes a different door.

## A century of replies: the shape of the literature

The defensive literature has a periodisation, and the periods explain the scorecard's distribution better than any difference in the criticisms' difficulty. The founding generation, from Hilferding's answer to Böhm-Bawerk in 1904 to the Bernstein controversy, wrote replies as scholarship: the critics were colleagues, the arguments were the point, and the concessions, where they came, came in print. The codification period followed, in which the theory acquired state custodians and the reply degenerated into the commentary: a genre whose function was to demonstrate that the canonical texts, correctly glossed, contained no error, and whose characteristic product was the distinction without a difference, manufactured at industrial scale. The middle decades produced the literature's strangest geography. Serious defensive work migrated to the tradition's margins and exile communities, to the Frankfurt School's reworkings, to the Yugoslav Praxis circle's reconstructions, to the Italian and French debates conducted in journals the custodians did not control, while the centres reprinted the catechism.

The thaw after 1956 and the Western revival of the nineteen sixties reopened the questions the codification had sealed, and the replies of that era, Liebman's and Miliband's among them, recovered the founding generation's scholarly manners under worse conditions. The analytical turn of the late nineteen seventies then changed the literature's centre of gravity permanently, by importing the refereeing standards of philosophy and economics departments and exporting the tradition's claims into journals where custodianship counted for nothing. The final period, after 1989, is the age of the reassessment: the states whose existence had disciplined interpretation vanished, the archive opened, and the replies written since have been produced, for the first time in the tradition's history, under no institutional pressure except the pressure of the arguments. The scorecard's grades track this periodisation with embarrassing fidelity. Rows whose serious replies cluster in the scholarly periods grade well. Rows whose replies were left to the codification grade as the codification deserves.

### Why does the scorecard refuse to grade any reply without a named author?

Because the periods differ precisely in whether authorship meant accountability. In the scholarly periods a reply carried a name, a journal, and a reputation exposed to referees and rivals. In the codification the reply carried an imprimatur instead, and its authority derived from the office that issued it rather than from the argument it contained.

Grading both kinds by the same standard would either insult the scholarship or flatter the catechism, and the attribution rule is the instrument that keeps the standard single.

## The families of reply: a taxonomy before the grading

The canonical definition named four families of reply, and the taxonomy earns its place because each family has a characteristic success and a characteristic corruption. Reconstruction, the first family, rebuilds the theory's machinery where the criticism broke it: the transformation literature is its home, and its success is consistency, its corruption the quiet substitution of the rebuilt machine for the original while the nameplate is retained. Misidentification, the second family, argues that the criticism struck a position the theory does not hold: its success is exegetical, the recovery of what the texts actually claim, and its corruption is the manufacture of subtlety under fire, the discovery that the theory never meant the thing its adherents spent decades believing it meant. The distinction between theory and implementation, the third family, assigns the record's catastrophes to the record's circumstances: its success is the mechanism question, its corruption the wall, the distinction used to end inquiry rather than to begin it. Tendency reformulation, the fourth family, restates predictions as conditioned tendencies: its success is the recovery of Marx's own hedges, its corruption the pattern this article names, the hedge promoted after the failure and taught as the original design.

A grader who knows the families can predict a reply's failure mode before reading it, and can therefore read it faster and more fairly. The families also explain the scorecard's asymmetries. Criticisms of machinery attract reconstruction, which is honest work with visible costs. Criticisms of predictions attract the fourth family, whose costs are invisible by design. Criticisms of the record attract the third family, which contains both the article's most successful political reply and its most abused defensive reflex, sometimes in the same book. The taxonomy is offered here as the reader's field guide to the sections that follow: when a reply begins, ask which family it belongs to, and the scorecard's verdict will usually be legible in the family's characteristic corruption, either avoided or indulged.

### How can a reader tell pressure narrowing from honest refinement?

The evidence's direction of travel. Refinement follows evidence the theorist went looking for, and narrows the claim toward the boundary the new material reveals. Narrowing under pressure follows evidence the critic produced, and relocates the claim to wherever that evidence cannot follow.

The observable traces differ accordingly: refinement is published as a correction with the old claim quoted and priced, while pressure narrowing is published as an explication with the old claim quietly dropped from the record.

## The transformation problem: the reconstructions and their price

The oldest technical criticism in the scorecard concerns the passage from values to prices of production in the third volume of Capital. Marx required two aggregate identities to survive the transformation: total price equals total value, and total profit equals total surplus value. Ladislaus von Bortkiewicz showed in 1907 that under a consistent simultaneous valuation the two identities cannot both be extracted from the system as Marx left it, and the demonstration has never been faulted on its own terms. The detailed dispute belongs to the article on the [transformation problem explained](/2016/04/15/transformation-problem-explained/); what belongs here is the family of replies the demonstration provoked, and the pattern they exhibit in unusually pure form.

The first family of replies reconstructs. Michio Morishima's work in the nineteen seventies established that a generalised version of the exploitation claim survives the transformation intact: positive profit exists if and only if surplus labour is performed, a result he called the fundamental Marxian theorem. Whatever else the theorem does, it rescues the proposition that profit and exploitation stand or fall together, and it does so with the critic's own mathematical tools. The New Interpretation, developed by Gérard Duménil in 1980 and Duncan Foley in 1982, redefines the value of money and the value of labour power so that both aggregate equalities hold by construction, relocating the identities from the sphere of physical magnitudes to the sphere of monetary expression. The temporal single-system interpretation, defended most fully by Andrew Kliman in 2007, rejects simultaneous valuation altogether: inputs enter at the prices actually paid, values and prices form one system unfolding in time, and on that reading the alleged inconsistency dissolves because it was an artefact of the critics' valuation convention rather than of Marx's text.

Each reconstruction is internally consistent. Each was produced by a serious scholar solving a stated problem. And each abandons something Marx appears to claim: simultaneity, or the independence of the value magnitudes from the monetary form, or the reading of the third volume that generations of Marxists took themselves to be defending. This is why the scorecard's verdict is partly works rather than works. The reply succeeds at the cost of the claim, and the cost is not a debating point invented by hostile graders. It is recorded in the tradition's own internal quarrels, where each school of reconstruction accuses the others of having saved the equalities by surrendering the theory. A defence posture that requires choosing which part of the inheritance to keep has answered the charge of inconsistency, and has done so honourably, but it has not restored the original position.

### Which reply in the scorecard carries the highest price?

The transformation reconstructions carry the highest price, because their success is purchased by redefinition. Each consistent version of the two aggregate equalities holds only under a valuation convention the third volume did not state, so the claim that survives is a claim about what Marx's system can be made to say, not a demonstration that the system as written was consistent all along.

There is a further lesson in this row, and it generalises. The transformation debate is the one place where the tradition's replies were forced into full formal explicitness, because the criticism was formal. Nothing could be gestured at; every reply had to show its working. The result is the most candid corner of the whole defensive literature, the corner where concessions are itemised in print by the conceding parties themselves. Where the criticisms are less formal, in the historical and political rows, the same accounting discipline is available but rarely imposed, and the reader of those literatures must do by hand what the mathematicians did by necessity: reconstruct what was given up, and price it.

## Bortkiewicz's challenge and the reply's prehistory

The transformation problem's peculiar dignity among the scorecard's rows is that it was never a quarrel about interpretation. Bortkiewicz, a statistician of no political axe in this fight, took Marx's own numerical procedure, generalised it into simultaneous equations, and showed that the system's outputs could not honour both of the identities the system's author had claimed for them. The demonstration's coldness is its authority. It does not accuse Marx of confusion about capitalism. It accuses his arithmetic of incompleteness, and arithmetic cannot be answered with hermeneutics, which is why this row forced the tradition into its most rigorous defensive work and why the work's costs are itemised with a precision the other rows envy.

The prehistory matters because the first replies were not rigorous, and their failure taught the tradition where rigour would have to come from. The initial responses, in the German and Russian party press, treated the problem as a misunderstanding to be dissolved by quotation, assembling passages in which Marx acknowledged that prices deviate from values, as though deviation were the difficulty. The difficulty was never deviation. It was the conservation claims, the two equalities, and the early replies' inability to see the difference between a price's wandering from its value and the aggregate identities' failure is the pattern's scholarly form: the reply addressed to the criticism's weakest construal. Paul Sweezy's edition of Böhm-Bawerk and Bortkiewicz in 1949, with his own constructive chapters, marks the moment the Anglophone tradition stopped dissolving and started solving, and the solutions that followed, Winternitz and Meek in the nineteen fifties, then the Sraffian reconstruction of the nineteen seventies, accepted the problem's terms as the price of answering it. The scorecard's partly works verdict is written across that whole arc: the problem was real, the solutions are real, and each solution's price is recorded in the solution itself, for any reader willing to compare the machine rebuilt with the machine described in the third volume's ninth chapter.

### What makes the transformation row the most honest corner of the defensive literature?

The criticism's formality. A mathematical objection cannot be met by redescription, by appeals to the author's intentions, or by charges of vulgarisation, because the equations either close or they do not. Every reply in the row was therefore forced to show its construction and its concessions in the open, and the row's literature is the one place where a reader can watch the tradition pay full price for its defences, itemised by the payers themselves.

## The reconstructions quarrel among themselves

A feature of the transformation literature that outsiders consistently miss is that its fiercest fighting is internal. The New Interpretation and the temporal single-system reading do not merely disagree with Bortkiewicz's heirs; they disagree with each other about what Marx's value theory is, and each regards the other's rescue as a surrender dressed as a victory. The temporal school charges the monetary reading with dissolving value into price by definition, so that the equalities hold the way a tautology holds. The monetary school charges the temporal reading with saving the letter of the transformation by abandoning the simultaneity that any equilibrium construction requires, purchasing consistency at the price of contact with the rest of economics. Morishima's theorem stands apart from both quarrels, and its apartness is diagnostic: the fundamental theorem proves that exploitation, defined as surplus labour, is the necessary and sufficient condition for profit, and it proves this inside a von Neumann framework the critics themselves accept. Of all the replies in this row, it is the one a hostile examiner cannot wave away, because it does not ask him to accept any premise he has not already granted.

### What does the fundamental theorem rescue, and what does it leave behind?

It rescues the connection between profit and unpaid labour, the proposition that positive profit exists exactly when workers perform more labour than the labour embodied in their consumption. It leaves behind the transformation itself: the theorem is silent on whether Marx's two aggregate equalities hold, and on whether values determine prices in the manner the third volume describes.

The rescue is real and partial, which is the anatomy of most honest replies in this table.

The quarrels matter for grading because they locate the cost with precision. When three schools of reconstruction each accuse the other two of abandoning the theory, the neutral reader is not required to adjudicate their dispute in order to learn from it. He learns that the set of propositions labelled Marx's value theory cannot be preserved entire, since the preservers themselves partition it differently, each keeping the part his method can carry. The original claim, the whole architecture of the third volume standing as written, has no defender left who does not begin by selecting. Selection under pressure, openly conducted, is scholarship. The scorecard's complaint is never against the selecting. It is against the anniversary rhetoric in which the selected fragment is exhibited as the intact inheritance, and against the student who is taught the rhetoric before he is shown the partition.

There is one more figure in this row's history whose treatment by both camps deserves record. Paul Samuelson, the most eminent economist to engage the problem, proposed in 1971 that the transformation be understood as an eraser operation: write down values, erase them, write down prices, with the values doing no work in the final result. The tradition's replies to Samuelson were often contemptuous, and the contempt was a mistake, because his formulation was simply Steedman's redundancy argument wearing a famous name, and it deserved the serious answer the serious literature eventually gave it. The episode illustrates a grading principle the scorecard applies throughout: a criticism does not become weaker because a famous man states it rudely, and a reply does not become stronger because its target was rude. The replies that work are the ones that answer the argument at its most precise, whoever is holding it.

## The redundancy argument: a different question, or a smaller one

Ian Steedman's Marx after Sraffa, published in 1977, pressed a colder version of the value critique. Working from Piero Sraffa's construction of prices from physical data, Steedman showed that prices of production and the rate of profit can be derived completely from the physical scheme of production and the real wage, without any detour through labour values. Value magnitudes, on this showing, are redundant: an intermediate step that can be deleted from the calculation without changing any result. The criticism is more dangerous than Bortkiewicz's, because it does not allege an error. It alleges an irrelevance, and irrelevance is the one charge a theory cannot answer by being defended more loudly.

The tradition's strongest reply changes the question, and says so. On the reading associated with Isaak Rubin, whose Essays on Marx's Theory of Value appeared in the nineteen twenties, value was never a price algorithm. It names the social form wealth takes when private labours are validated through exchange, and its magnitudes answer a question the physical scheme cannot pose: where the aggregate surplus comes from, and in whose hands it accumulates. Moshe Farjoun and Moshé Machover, in Laws of Chaos in 1983, rebuilt the empirical side of the claim probabilistically, arguing that labour content governs the distribution of prices statistically even where no exact transformation holds. On this combined reply, Steedman's derivation is correct and beside the point, because the point was the origin and social meaning of the surplus, not the arithmetic of relative prices.

Partly works is the grade this reply earns, and the grading turns on a conditional the brief for this article states exactly: the reply works if the narrower claim is what is being defended. If the theory's ambition is an account of where profit comes from and why the product of labour confronts labour as an alien power, the redundancy argument misses it entirely, and Steedman has refuted a textbook simplification. If the theory's ambition includes the determination of prices and the profit rate by embodied labour, the redundancy argument is fatal and the reply is a retreat conducted in good order. The text of Capital supports both ambitions in different chapters, which is why the argument has lasted half a century. What cannot be done honestly is to claim the wide ambition when addressing the faithful and the narrow one when addressing Steedman. The reply's defenders, at their best, do not attempt it.

### Why does the redundancy reply depend on which claim is being defended?

Because the criticism and the reply pass each other unless the claim is fixed first. Steedman demonstrates that prices and the profit rate follow from physical data alone. The reply answers that value magnitudes explain the surplus's origin and social form.

Both can be true together, and whether the exchange counts as an answer depends entirely on whether price determination belonged to the claim under defence.

A reader should notice the family resemblance between this row and the one above it. In both, the criticism is mathematical, the reply is a reinterpretation, and the verdict depends on a prior decision about what the theory was claiming. The tradition's critics call this pattern having it both ways. The tradition's best defenders call it recovering the theory from its vulgarisers. The scorecard's colder description is that the claim defended in the seminar room is smaller than the claim printed in the manifestos, and that the shrinkage, once noticed, cannot be unnoticed. The reformulation pattern, examined at length below, is this row's structure generalised across the whole defensive literature and slowed down enough to watch.

## The Okishio theorem: the redundancy argument's second front

The economic family's criticisms include one the scorecard's table folds into the predictions row but whose replies deserve separate grading, because they exhibit the relocation mechanism inside a single theorem's career. Nobuo Okishio proved in 1961 that a technical change which capitalists would actually adopt, one that reduces costs at current prices, must raise the general rate of profit if the real wage is constant. The theorem strikes the falling rate of profit at its derivation: the mechanism Marx proposed, mechanisation raising the composition of capital faster than the rate of exploitation can compensate, cannot depress the profit rate under the theorem's conditions, because no capitalist adopts a technique that lowers his own return, and the general rate is built from the adopters' returns. The criticism is narrow, formal, and, within its conditions, unanswerable, which is why the replies are so instructive.

The replies proceeded on three tracks, and the scorecard grades them as a set. The first relocated the tendency's cause: the profit rate falls, on this account, not through mechanisation's arithmetic but through wage pressure in tight labour markets, the profit squeeze associated with the work of Andrew Glyn and Bob Sutcliffe on Britain's crisis. The relocation produced good empirical history and abandoned the derivation, which had specified a mechanism independent of the wage bargain's fortunes. The second track disputed the theorem's frame: the temporal school's valuation, in which the profit rate is computed on historical costs rather than simultaneous replacement values, reverses the theorem's result, and the quarrel between the schools is a genuine technical dispute about the correct accounting for a dynamic economy. The third track absorbed the theorem into the tendency's hedge, presenting the counteracting influences as having always included the theorem's effect, so that the law survives as the resultant of forces among which Okishio's is one. The third track is the pattern in miniature. The second is scholarship. The first is the relocation that produced knowledge while surrendering the claim, the same structure the transformation row exhibits, and the recurrence of the structure across unrelated criticisms is the observation on which the pattern section builds.

### Why do the economic criticisms produce the most honest replies?

Because their formality removes the exits. Where a criticism is stated as equations, the defender cannot redescribe his way past it; he must construct, concede, or fall silent, and the literature's concessions are consequently on paper. Where the criticism arrives as narrative, of predictions or of regimes, the exits reopen, and the same tradition that paid full price to Bortkiewicz discovered, in the historical rows, that it could travel more cheaply. The difference in the replies is a difference in the tolls the criticisms charge.

## The probabilistic rebuild: Farjoun and Machover examined

The redundancy row contains one reply unlike the others, because it accepts the critic's mathematics and rebuilds the theory's empirical claim on new foundations. Laws of Chaos argued that the exact determination of prices by values was a category error imported from classical mechanics, and that political economy should follow statistical physics instead: individual prices scatter around values the way molecular motions scatter around a temperature, and the law of value operates as a distributional regularity rather than as an identity. On this reconstruction, Steedman's derivation loses its sting. That prices can be computed from physical data was never in dispute among people who understood the claim; the claim is about which magnitude governs the distribution around which the computation's results are scattered, and about the surplus whose existence the physical scheme registers without explaining.

The rebuild deserves the compliment of being graded as science rather than as apologetics, and under that grading its fortunes are mixed in an instructive way. Its central empirical expectation, that labour content organises the price structure of real economies far more tightly than chance or rival magnitudes would suggest, has been examined by sympathetic and unsympathetic econometricians alike, with results the tradition quotes selectively. Anwar Shaikh's work on the empirical shape of price value relations belongs to the same broad effort: the finding that market prices track embodied labours closely across input output tables is real, and its interpretation is contested, since a correlation between two aggregates both driven by costs may testify to arithmetic rather than to law. The scorecard reproduces none of the magnitudes involved, under the figures protocol, and the omission does not weaken the grading. Direction is enough. The probabilistic reply converts a refuted identity into a defensible statistical claim, and the conversion is announced by its authors as exactly that, a rebuilding. It therefore escapes the relocation grade on a technicality that is not a technicality: candour about what has been surrendered. What it cannot recover is the original claim's office. A statistical governor of distributions does not determine the profit rate, does not ground the transformation, and does not restore value magnitudes to the centre of price theory. The reply works, in the narrow room it honestly builds for itself, and the scorecard's partly works verdict records both the room and its narrowness.

### Why does candour change the grade a restatement receives?

Because the scorecard's offence is never the narrowing itself. A claim restated at reduced scope, with the reduction announced and the surrendered content itemised, has been refined, and refinement is how theories are supposed to behave under fire. The identical restatement presented as the original meaning has been reformulated in this article's technical sense.

Candour is therefore not a courtesy the grader extends. It is the observable difference between the two operations, and it is checkable in the texts.

A coda to this row concerns the audience problem, which recurs throughout the scorecard. The probabilistic rebuild is unknown to nearly every adherent who believes the redundancy argument was answered, and almost no adherent who believes Böhm-Bawerk was refuted knows the social-category reading in its rigorous form. What circulates in the movement's bloodstream is not these replies but their reputations, compressed into slogans of victory. The scholar writes a conditional defence; the tradition retails an unconditional one; the critic, meeting the unconditional version, refutes it and concludes the scholarship is hollow; the scholar, meeting the refutation, protests that his conditions were dropped. Both protests are justified, and the scorecard's insistence on named works over circulating reputations is designed to break the cycle at the only point where a grader has leverage: by refusing to grade any reply that cannot be opened at a stated page.

## The opening chapter: the social-category reply and the step it skips

Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk's Karl Marx and the Close of His System, published in 1896, attacked the derivation at the start of Capital's first chapter. Marx argues from the fact that commodities exchange in definite proportions to a common substance, labour, in which they are all equal as values. The inequality argument replies that exchange implies no such equality: parties trade precisely because each values what he receives more than what he surrenders, so the transaction presupposes divergent valuations rather than a shared magnitude, and the inference to a common substance is a non sequitur at the theory's foundation. The full assessment of Böhm-Bawerk's four arguments belongs to the article on his critique; the reply graded here is the one the tradition settled on.

That reply is the social-category reading, given its classic form by Rudolf Hilferding, in the essay he published against Böhm-Bawerk in 1904, and deepened by Rubin. Value, on this reading, is not a physical property of things and not a substance inferred from ratios. It is a social relation that takes objective form: the first chapter analyses how the labour of private producers becomes social labour through exchange, and the equality it discusses is the equality society itself enforces when it treats unlike products as commensurable. Böhm-Bawerk, the reply concludes, refuted a naturalistic caricature, mistaking an analysis of social form for a failed exercise in physics.

The scorecard's verdict is the harshest in the table: relocates the claim. The reason is precise. The inequality argument targets an inference, the step from exchange proportions to a common magnitude, and the social-category reply does not defend that step. It explains, often brilliantly, what the chapter is doing instead, and in explaining it concedes that the chapter read as an argument for the labour theory does not hold. Defenders reply that the chapter was never such an argument, and the textual question is genuine; Marx's presentation moves between derivation and exposition in ways scholars still dispute. But a verdict must grade the reply against the criticism as made, and the criticism as made was about the inference. The reply survives by relocating the theory's foundation from a proof to an interpretation. Interpretations can be true. They cannot be wielded as refutations of the man who attacked the proof.

### What does the social-category reply leave untouched in the first chapter?

It leaves untouched the step Böhm-Bawerk actually struck, the passage from observed exchange ratios to the common magnitude they are said to express. The reply redescribes the chapter as an analysis of social form, which may be the better reading, while the inference the critic challenged stands exactly where he left it, undefended as an inference.

This row matters beyond its technicalities because it is the template the reader will meet again in grander dress. A criticism identifies a load-bearing step. The reply re-describes the structure so that the step is no longer load-bearing. The redescription is frequently an improvement, and the building frequently stands better without the step. What the reply may not then claim is that the criticism failed. It succeeded so completely that the theory was rebuilt around the hole it made, and the rebuilt theory's defenders owe the critic a acknowledgement they are seldom observed to pay. Hilferding's essay is honourable in this respect: it argues, it does not pretend the objection was unintelligible. The lesser literature that followed him has not always kept his standard.

## Hilferding's essay as the founding reply

Every literature has a founding document whose virtues its descendants spend a century failing to reproduce, and the defensive literature's is Hilferding's 1904 answer to Böhm-Bawerk. Read it against the folklore that succeeded it and the differences are a syllabus of this article's standards. Hilferding states his opponent's argument in a form Böhm-Bawerk's own students accepted. He concedes the marginal theory's genuine territory, the subjective moment in price formation, without conceding the theory built on it. He locates the disagreement precisely, in whether value is a property of things or a relation among producers, and he argues the location rather than assuming it. And he writes throughout as a man who expects to be answered, addressing a republic of letters in which Böhm-Bawerk's reply, if it came, would be read beside his own. The essay's central contention, that value is the social bond of a society of private producers and that its laws operate behind their backs as the regulator of their collective labour, became the social-category reply's charter, and everything graded in the opening chapter row descends from it.

The essay's fate is the pattern's biography in one career. Hilferding went on to write Finance Capital and to become, twice, a finance minister of the Weimar Republic, and his reply was canonised by a movement that adopted its conclusion and discarded its manners. In the catechisms, the answer to Böhm-Bawerk became a paragraph of conclusions without the argument, then a sentence, then a reflex: the Austrian had misunderstood, the matter was closed. A student formed by the catechism who actually opens Böhm-Bawerk discovers, often with a sense of betrayal, a formidable book whose central argument the sentence never mentioned. The scorecard's severity about the opening chapter row is not severity toward Hilferding, whose grade, could individuals be graded, would be the highest in the article. It is severity toward the transmission that converted his answer into an incantation, and toward the custodians who preferred the incantation because incantations do not require the faithful to read the enemy.

### What did Hilferding concede, and why does the concession matter?

He conceded the reality and the partial rights of the subjective moment in exchange, the ground on which his opponent's school stood, and he conceded it in the reply's opening movements rather than under duress at its close. The concession matters because it fixed the argument's true frontier, the constitution of social labour, instead of a false one, and because it demonstrated to his own side that a defence could give ground without collapsing, a demonstration the tradition's later practice largely refused to learn from its founder in this genre.

## The first chapter's second generation: the value-form school

The social-category reply did not stand still after Hilferding and Rubin. Its most important development came from the West German reading of Capital associated with Hans-Georg Backhaus and Helmut Reichelt in the nineteen sixties and seventies, and carried into Anglophone scholarship by the value-form theorists and by Michael Heinrich's reconstruction of the monetary theory of value. This second generation pressed the reply further than its founders had: value, on their reading, is constituted only in exchange and expressed only in money, so that any attempt to measure it as a substance embodied in products before exchange misunderstands the theory's most original move. Böhm-Bawerk's inference problem dissolves, they argue, because the first chapter was never inferring a substance from ratios. It was unfolding the form that social labour necessarily takes where producers work privately and separately, and the equality in exchange is society's own act, not the theorist's postulate.

The grading of this development shows the scorecard's rule at its most delicate. As exegesis, the value-form reading is formidable, and its account of why Marx begins with the commodity rather than with production has reorganised serious scholarship. As a reply to the inequality argument, it completes the relocation the first generation began. The more thoroughly value is a form constituted in exchange, the less the first chapter contains a derivation of exploitation from production, and the more the theory's centre of gravity shifts from the extraction of surplus in the factory to the constitution of the social bond in the market. Defenders of the reading reply that the extraction analysis stands in the later chapters, untouched. So it does, as narrative and as accounting. What does not stand is the foundational claim that the opening derivation establishes the labour theory from which the extraction analysis follows, and it was that claim, in that place, that Böhm-Bawerk attacked in 1896. The second generation's achievement is to have made the relocation so intellectually productive that its character as a relocation is easy to forget. The scorecard remembers, and grades the row as it found it, while recording that this is the row where relocation produced the most genuine knowledge.

### What would the value-form school have to show to change this row's grade?

It would have to show that the derivation Böhm-Bawerk attacked was not merely incomplete as he read it but absent as the chapter's defenders' opponents read it too: that no competent reader, friendly or hostile, in the generation after publication, took the first chapter to be proving a common substance from exchange proportions.

The historical record runs the other way. The movement's own economists defended the chapter as a proof for decades, which is why the proof's failure had to be answered rather than shrugged off.

There is a temptation, in grading this row, to let the antiquity of the dispute soften the verdict. The inequality argument is more than a century old, the texts are canonical, and generations of students have been taught that the matter was settled by the observation that Marx was doing something subtler than his critic supposed. The scorecard resists the temptation on principle. A criticism does not expire. The question is never how long an objection has been on file but whether the file contains an answer to it, and on the inequality argument the file contains instead a century of increasingly sophisticated agreement that the objection's target was misconceived, which is a different holding. The distinction matters beyond this row, because the same antiquity discount is applied, by tired defenders, to the knowledge objection and to the missing constraint, as though objections aged into irrelevance the way statutes lapse. They do not. They wait.

## The calculation objection: the formal answer and the knowledge residue

Ludwig von Mises argued in 1920 that rational calculation is impossible under common ownership of the means of production, because without market exchange of capital goods there are no prices for them, and without prices no planner can compare the costs of alternative uses. Friedrich Hayek later shifted the objection's centre of gravity: even granted the arithmetic, the knowledge a price system aggregates is dispersed, tacit, and held by millions of actors who could not state it to a board if they wished. The debate's full anatomy is set out in the article on the [socialist calculation debate](/2019/08/15/socialist-calculation-debate/); the replies are graded here.

Oskar Lange's answer of 1936 and 1937 met the formal objection on its own ground. A planning board can quote parametric prices, instruct managers to minimise cost and expand output to the point where price covers marginal cost, and adjust its quotations by trial and error in response to surpluses and shortages, exactly as a market clears. As a piece of logic this worked, and the concession it extracted from the other side is a matter of record: Hayek granted the scheme's theoretical coherence and moved the argument to knowledge and incentives. Maurice Dobb, the most serious Marxist participant, added a second string: markets themselves fail to transmit much that a planner needs, about future capacity, about external effects, about the social cost of unemployment, so the comparison is not between knowledge and ignorance but between two imperfect signalling systems with different blind spots.

The scorecard grades the combined reply partly works, and the division of the verdict follows the objection's own history. Against the arithmetic form, the reply works: calculation in the sense Mises first demanded was shown to be formally possible, and the strong impossibility claim was withdrawn by its own side. Against the knowledge form, the reply does not engage. Trial and error presupposes that the board can observe what it needs to adjust, and the objection's whole point is that the relevant knowledge exists only in the acting of dispersed individuals and perishes in the reporting. Dobb's counter, that markets are blind too, establishes a symmetry of imperfection without supplying the missing organ. A planner who knows that prices also fail has learned something true and nothing usable. The knowledge objection therefore stands in the unanswered five, where it has stood since 1945, surviving every computational rejoinder because the rejoinders answer the arithmetic again, faster.

### Which half of the calculation objection did the formal answer meet?

It met the half that demanded a unit of account. Lange showed that a board could quote prices, instruct managers by rule, and adjust by trial and error, so calculation as arithmetic was possible in principle. The half it never reached is the epistemic half: how the knowledge dispersed among millions of actors, much of it impossible for its holders to articulate, reaches any centre in usable form.

The afterlife of this reply teaches a distinction the whole article relies on. A reply can win its exchange and lose its war, because objections migrate. Mises's impossibility claim, as first stated, was defeated, and the defeat is permanent. The objection that replaced it is stronger, subtler, and still open. Defenders who cite the Lange episode as the refutation of the calculation critique are citing a victory over a position its own holders abandoned two generations ago, which is a way of not noticing that the position now held against them has never been answered at all. The scorecard exists to make that kind of citation error harder to commit in good faith.

## Hayek's deepening: the objection changes its address

The calculation row's grading depends on a biographical fact about the objection itself: it moved. Mises's 1920 essay attacked the possibility of calculation, and for fifteen years the debate circled his formulation, with the defenders, Lange preeminent among them, designing the machinery that would calculate. Hayek's contributions of 1935, 1940, and 1945 progressively abandoned the strong claim his side had started with. By The Use of Knowledge in Society, the objection no longer denied that a board could compute. It denied that the board could know, because the relevant knowledge is not a stock of data awaiting collection but a flux of local judgements, embodied in practices and prices, that exists only while the market process generates it. The objection changed its address from arithmetic to epistemology, and the change was announced in print, in the journals both sides read.

The defensive literature's failure to track the move is the calculation row's deepest finding, deeper than any single reply's grade. Decade after decade, the tradition's surveys of the debate culminate in Lange, as though the story ended in 1937 with the formal answer, and the epistemic objection is handled, where it is handled at all, as a footnote about practical difficulties. The pattern's signature is present in its purest scholarly form: the claim defended, that the calculation critique was answered, is true of the critique's first version and false of its mature one, and the defence's literature sustains the claim by declining to update its target. A grader must therefore perform the update himself, which this article has done by grading the reply against the objection as it stands in Hayek's final formulation. Against that formulation, the replies on file are the domain exceptions, the cases where knowledge is public and stable enough to plan with, wartime mobilisation, infrastructure networks, the internal logistics of large firms, each genuine, each a province rather than the kingdom. The provinces matter, and the article on the calculation debate maps them with care. A map of provinces is not a refutation of the claim that the kingdom cannot be governed from a centre, and the scorecard's verdict, that the knowledge objection stands, is the map's legend written honestly.

### Which version of the calculation objection do the famous replies answer?

The version of 1920, in which the impossibility of a common denominator for capital goods was the whole claim. Lange's construction, Kantorovich's programming, and the computational proposals of the electronic age all answer that version, and answer it well. The version of 1945, in which the denominator's data are the problem, has attracted no comparably formal answer in the eighty years of its currency, and the scorecard's row is graded against the version that is alive, not against the one that is famous.

## Dobb's dissent: the reply the Marxists made to their own side

The calculation debate's most instructive episode for a grader of replies is the one in which the sharpest criticism of Lange's solution came from the Marxist side of the table. Maurice Dobb, who had defended planning against Mises through the nineteen thirties, received the trial and error construction with deep reserve. A board that sets prices by watching surpluses and shortages, he argued, has reinstalled the market's mechanism while dismissing the market's actors, and will inherit the mechanism's instabilities without its information: the parametric prices would chase their own tails through an economy whose investment decisions, the decisions that matter most, no price of the trial and error kind can guide, since the future they commit has no present scarcity to register. Dobb's alternative, planning in physical terms with prices demoted to accounting aids, satisfied almost nobody, and the episode is sometimes retailed as a curiosity. The scorecard retails it as a standard.

For what Dobb's dissent establishes is that the calculation reply's partly works grade was available, in its essentials, to a participant writing in the debate's own decade, from within the camp the reply defended. The formal answer's limits were not a secret kept by Hayek's side. They were stated by the defence's most distinguished economist, in the defence's own journals, and the tradition's subsequent celebration of Lange as the debate's victor required forgetting its own man's reservations. A literature that forgets its internal critics in order to celebrate its victories has performed the pattern's fourth move on its own history, and the scorecard, which exists to notice such performances, records this one in the calculation row's margin. The reply that survives grading is the composite the debate actually produced: Lange's construction, bounded by Dobb's reservations, confronted by Hayek's epistemic deepening. No single text contains it. The scorecard assembles it, which is what scorecards are for.

### Why does acknowledging the pattern restore a defence's credit?

Because belief is allocated to sources, not to arguments in the abstract. A defence that has demonstrated, in its handling of the predictions and the textbooks, that it will restate rather than concede when restating is available has taught the reader to discount its testimony everywhere, including the rows where its replies are genuine.

Acknowledging the pattern is the only move that restores the source's credit, since it proves the defender can see the difference between his tradition's answers and its evasions, and has priced the evasions himself.

## After Lange: the computational rejoinder and its recurring mistake

The calculation row has a long tail, and the tail exhibits a pathology worth naming in its own right. In 1939 Leonid Kantorovich published the mathematical foundations of linear programming, work undertaken for Soviet planning itself, and demonstrated that a planning authority equipped with his methods could compute shadow prices for resources from the plan's own constraints. Later generations of defenders, most systematically Paul Cockshott and Allin Cottrell in Towards a New Socialism in 1993, argued that computing power had dissolved the calculation problem altogether: labour time accounting across an entire economy, they proposed, is a tractable computation for modern machines, and the informational burden that crushed Gosplan's clerks is a burden of the paper age. Each wave of this rejoinder has been technically serious. Each has been celebrated in the movement's press as the calculation debate's final settlement.

The scorecard grades the rejoinder as the newest verse of an old song, and the grading follows from the shifted objection's anatomy, which the calculation article in this series establishes. Mises's original claim had two components fused: an arithmetic claim, that without prices there is no common denominator for comparing alternatives, and, in Hayek's development, an epistemic claim, that the data any denominator would summarise do not exist in reportable form. Kantorovich answered the arithmetic claim from inside the planning system itself, a fact both camps underuse: the strongest refutation of the impossibility thesis was produced by a Soviet mathematician solving a Soviet factory's problem. Cockshott and Cottrell answer it again at electronic scale. But the epistemic claim is untouched by processing speed, because its subject is not the cost of computing with data but the genesis of data: the knowledge of local circumstance, of substitution possibilities not yet tried, of wants not yet articulated, which in a market is generated by the bidding itself and outside a market must be reported by actors with reasons of their own. A computer does not elicit. It totals what it is given, and the objection has always been about the giving.

### Why does each generation of the computational rejoinder miss the same point?

Because the arithmetic form of the objection is the one that admits of technical solution, and technical communities answer the objections they can formalise. Kantorovich could prove that shadow prices exist for a stated plan. No formalism can prove that the plan's stated data exhaust what dispersed actors know, since the objection defines that knowledge as existing only in its exercise. The rejoinder therefore keeps winning the argument of 1920 while the argument of 1945 stands open beside it, unaddressed and unexpired.

Alec Nove's rejoinder to the rejoinder deserves the last word in this section, because he wrote as a sympathiser performing triage rather than as an enemy performing execution. Feasible socialism, in his construction, conceded the knowledge objection for the economy's commanding heights of variety: consumer goods in their millions of variants could not be planned in detail by any centre, and a viable socialism would have to retain markets, money, and autonomous enterprises across most of the terrain the classical model claimed for the plan. The tradition's orthodox wing received Nove's book as a betrayal. The scorecard reads it as the calculation row's most honest document, because it performs the operation this article exists to honour: it states which part of the inheritance the evidence has priced out of reach, and rebuilds on the remainder with the prices showing. That the rebuilding concedes the objection's substance is not a refutation of Nove. It is the definition of his honesty, and the standard against which the computational celebrations measure poorly.

## The falsifiability charge: winning the criterion, losing the record

Karl Popper's charge, stated in The Open Society and Its Enemies in 1945 and sharpened in Conjectures and Refutations in 1963, was that Marxism began as a scientific theory, made predictions, saw them refuted, and survived by reinterpreting both theory and evidence until nothing could count against it. A doctrine so protected, he argued, has traded knowledge for faith. The article asking whether [Marxism is a science](/2019/10/01/is-marxism-a-science/) examines the charge in full; here the reply is what matters, and the reply is the most philosophically respectable in the entire scorecard.

The reply, whose canonical form is Imre Lakatos's methodology of scientific research programmes, denies the premise. No mature science abandons a theory at the first contrary observation, because every theory swims in a sea of anomalies and every test implicates auxiliary assumptions. Programmes, not propositions, are the unit of appraisal, and a programme is judged by its trajectory: progressive when its successive versions predict novel facts, degenerating when its versions only accommodate facts already known, patching the belt to protect the core. On that criterion Popper's demand for instant falsification is naive, and the charge as he stated it fails against every science that has ever existed, physics included.

The grade here is partly works, and it is the cruellest in the table precisely because the reply is correct. Falsification is the wrong criterion; the reply establishes that beyond serious dispute. But the better criterion does not acquit the defendant. Applied impartially, the Lakatosian test asks what novel facts the programme's successive versions predicted, and the honest inventory is thin: the tradition's characteristic theoretical moves followed the anomalies they explained, converting refuted predictions into tendencies, relocations, and scope restrictions after the evidence arrived. The reply wins the argument about method and thereby selects the tribunal under which the record looks worst. A defender is entitled to the better criterion. He is not entitled to assume the better criterion is kinder, and the long afterlife of this reply in apologetic writing consists largely of assuming exactly that, citing Lakatos as an acquittal when Lakatos is a standard.

### Why does winning the criterion argument not improve the record?

Because the criterion that replaces falsification is more demanding in the dimension where the record is weakest. Popper's test could be evaded by reinterpretation, which is what he accused the tradition of doing. Lakatos's test prices reinterpretation directly: a programme whose changes only accommodate known facts is degenerating by definition.

Winning the right to be judged by trajectory means being judged by a trajectory of accommodations.

There is a symmetry here that honest defenders have occasionally seen whole. The falsifiability row and the predictions row are the same row viewed from two sides. Popper's charge is the methodological description of the reformulation pattern: a theory protected by restatement. The tendency defence, graded in the next section, is the pattern's historical substance. The tradition's misfortune is that its two most damaging critics, the philosopher and the renegade social democrat, were describing one phenomenon in two vocabularies, and its replies answered each vocabulary separately without ever confronting the phenomenon. The scorecard's contribution is to put the two rows adjacent, where the reader can watch them rhyme.

## The custodial science and its price

The falsifiability row cannot be closed without pricing the damage done to the reply by the tradition's own official science. In the states where the doctrine governed, Marxism's scientific status was not argued but decreed, and the decree's enforcement produced the spectacle, in the Lysenko affair above all, of a biological theory destroyed and its holders imprisoned in the name of the doctrine's materialism. Whatever the Western tradition's philosophers achieved in the methodological debate, they achieved it while the doctrine's name was attached, by its own custodians, to the century's most notorious corruption of science by power. Popper's charge drew a portion of its public force from that attachment, and the replies' authors knew it: the serious Western defences of the programme's scientific character were written in a double register, answering Popper in the journals while distancing the method from the institutes that claimed to embody it.

The episode generalises into one of the scorecard's working laws. A reply's credibility is taxed by the conduct of the reply's camp, at a rate the reply's author cannot control and the grader cannot ignore. The methodological reply graded in this article is Lakatos's, and Lakatos wrote as an independent philosopher with no custodians to protect, which is part of why his intervention could be honest. The defences produced inside the custodial systems, by contrast, were required to conclude with the programme's vindication, and a defence whose conclusion is fixed in advance is not a defence but a ceremony. The reader will find the ceremony's products throughout the older literature, recognisable by their structure: the criticism summarised at the length of a paragraph, the canonical quotations marshalled, the verdict of bourgeois misunderstanding delivered on schedule. Against that background the scorecard's insistence on grading only replies whose authors were free to lose is not a methodological nicety. It is the recognition that most of what the tradition published in its own defence, during the decades of its greatest power, was published under conditions that made honesty a punishable offence, and that the replies which survive grading are, almost without exception, the work of the unprotected.

### Why do the replies that survive grading come so consistently from the unprotected?

Because protection fixes conclusions. A writer whose institution requires the defence to succeed will produce the appearance of success by the cheapest available means, which is restatement, and a writer free to fail can afford the concessions on which real answers are built.

The distribution of quality across this scorecard, scholarly at the margins and ceremonial at the centres, is the institutional history of the tradition told through its defensive literature, and it is the strongest evidence the article offers for its mechanism: charters defended by custodians generate the pattern, while programmes defended by scholars generate replies.

## Popper's charge in its strongest form, and the replies that missed it

Popper pressed two charges that careless summary fuses, and the replies' quality cannot be graded until they are separated. The falsifiability charge, graded in the scorecard's row, concerns the theory's scientific status: its predictions failed and its defenders immunised it. The historicism charge, pressed in The Poverty of Historicism, concerns its method: the claim to have discovered laws of historical development licensing prophecy about society's future, a claim Popper attacked as a confusion between trends, which depend on conditions, and laws, which do not. The charges have different targets and different fates. The historicism charge strikes the prophetic family of claims, and the tradition's serious philosophers have substantially conceded it, rebuilding the theory of history as a set of conditioned mechanisms rather than as a developmental law. The falsifiability charge strikes the programme's conduct, and its fate is the one the neighbouring sections grade.

The replies that missed Popper are as instructive as the reply that met him. A large literature answered the historicism charge when the falsifiability charge was the one in the indictment, demonstrating, correctly, that Marx's historical claims could be read as conditional tendencies, and concluding, incorrectly, that the immunisation Popper documented had been addressed. It had not, because immunisation is a fact about the defenders' behaviour, not about the theory's logical form, and no reading of the texts, however subtle, can acquit a community of a practice. Lakatos's reply met the charge at the right level because it accepted the behavioural framing and supplied a behavioural standard: judge the community by whether its problem shifts predict or merely patch. That is why the falsifiability row's reply, alone among the methodological defences, is graded as engaging its target, and why its partly works verdict, delivered by the borrowed tribunal's own standard, carries the weight it does. The replies that answered the wrong Popper are filed by the scorecard under the folklore of victory, where the tradition keeps the records of arguments it did not have.

### On what principle are the scorecard's three verdicts ordered?

By the relation between the claim attacked and the claim that survives. Works marks the rows where the surviving claim is the attacked claim, at close to full strength. Partly works marks the rows where what survives is that same claim reduced, bounded, or confined to one of its versions, with the reduction visible.

Relocates the claim marks the rows where the survivor is a different proposition wearing the original's name. The ordering is therefore a measurement of distance travelled under fire, not of the replies' ingenuity, which is often greatest exactly where the distance is.

## The criterion applied by the tradition's own philosophers

The falsifiability row contains an irony the scorecard is obliged to register, because it disciplines the reply's use. Lakatos himself applied his criterion to Marxism, in print, and returned a verdict of degeneration, citing the programme's handling of its failed anticipations as his type specimen of a belt adjusted to protect a core. The reply that invokes his methodology against Popper therefore borrows a tribunal whose presiding judge has already ruled on the case, adversely, with examples. Defenders have responded that Lakatos's own application was hasty, that his examples were drawn from the movement's journalism rather than from its research frontier, and that a fairer inventory, crediting the programme with the novel content of its best historical work, would grade the trajectory as mixed rather than degenerating. The scorecard's partly works verdict absorbs that response at full strength: mixed to poor is the grade the science article reaches by the same method, and mixed is not the acquittal the apologetic use of Lakatos advertises.

Mark Blaug's comparative defence enters here as the reply's second string, and it is stronger than its usual presentation. Blaug, an economist of pronounced methodological severity, observed that the immunising behaviour Popper condemned in Marxism is the normal practice of economics itself, whose practitioners protect core assumptions against refutation with a dexterity any dialectician might envy. If degeneration by accommodation disqualifies a programme, the disqualification empties the social sciences wholesale, and a criterion that convicts everyone discriminates no one. The scorecard accepts the observation and denies the conclusion drawn from it. A shared disease is still a disease in each patient, and the comparative point establishes, at most, that Marxism's methodological record is unremarkable among its peers rather than uniquely disgraceful. For a tradition that claimed, uniquely, to be the science of society's laws, unremarkable is itself a verdict, and not the one its defenders were hoping to purchase with Blaug's help.

### What inventory would a fair Lakatosian grading of the programme contain?

It would credit the novel content the tradition's best work generated: the prediction, against the era's consensus, that capital would organise itself into ever larger units and press outward across the globe, the anticipation of crises of overproduction in economies that official doctrine held self correcting, the recovery of class as an explanatory variable in historiography.

It would debit the accommodations: the tendency conversions, the geographical relocations, the scope restrictions, each documented in this article's scorecard. A grader who lists only one column has announced his loyalty before his competence.

The deeper point about this row is that it is the only one where the reply changes the subject of the entire article. Every other row grades a defence of a claim about the world. This row grades a defence of the right to go on making claims, and its partly works verdict propagates: because the methodological reply succeeds only in relocating the trial, every other row's verdict inherits a shadow. A programme judged degenerating in its protective behaviour must expect its individual replies to be read with suspicion, as potential belt adjustments, until their candour is established one by one. That is exactly the reading protocol the scorecard has applied, and it is why the reformulation pattern had to be named before the replies could be believed: the pattern is the Lakatosian diagnosis translated into the grammar of individual arguments, and the two rows are, as the falsifiability section observed, one row seen from two sides.

## The failed predictions: tendencies, relocation, and the clearest case of the pattern

Eduard Bernstein's The Preconditions of Socialism, published in 1899, was the first systematic audit of the predictions from inside the movement. The working class was not being immiserated in absolute terms; real wages rose. Society was not polarising into two camps; the middle strata persisted and grew. Crises were not obviously deepening toward a final breakdown. Revolution had not arrived in the advanced countries where the theory expected it first. Bernstein drew the revisionist conclusion and was excommunicated for his honesty, but the audit itself was never answered on its own terms, and every subsequent reply has worked by re-describing the claims audited.

The replies form a matched set. Immiseration, which the early texts state in terms any reader takes as absolute, becomes relative immiseration: the worker's share falls, or his insecurity grows, even as his wage rises. Polarisation, which the Manifesto states as the simplification of society into two great hostile camps, becomes a global process: the camps form between nations, with the advanced countries' working classes bought into stability by imperial rents, a relocation given theoretical dress by Lenin's Imperialism in 1916. The falling rate of profit, stated as a law, becomes a tendency operating among counteracting influences, a hedge Marx himself built into the third volume's architecture. Revolution in the advanced countries becomes, in the hands of Ernest Mandel and the later tradition, a possibility conditioned on crises of a depth not yet seen, rather than the expectation the nineteenth century movement organised itself around.

The scorecard's verdict is relocates the claim, and the instruction attached to this article's commission directs that this row be named the clearest instance of the reformulation pattern in the entire defensive literature. The instruction is followed here without softening. Each restatement is textually defensible in isolation: Marx did write of tendencies, did list counteracting influences, did late in life entertain non European paths. The pattern is what a critic is entitled to notice. In every case the restatement follows the disconfirming evidence rather than preceding it; in every case the restated claim is pitched exactly where the evidence cannot reach; in every case the restatement is then quoted as the original meaning, so that the theory appears never to have been wrong, only misread. A defence that proceeds this way four times in four domains has not answered Bernstein. It has demonstrated his deeper point, which was never about wages. It was about a movement that could no longer tell the difference between its theory and its hopes.

### Where does the tendency defence find its textual warrant?

In the third volume's own architecture, where the falling rate of profit is presented as a law whose operation is checked by counteracting influences Marx enumerates himself, and in scattered passages where polarisation and immiseration are stated with qualifications the movement's popular versions dropped. The warrant is real.

What the warrant cannot supply is timing: the qualifications were promoted to the centre only after the unqualified versions failed.

A fair grader adds one sentence in the tradition's favour, and this article adds it. Reformulation is not a Marxist invention, and the comparison section below prices the pattern in other traditions, where it also flourishes. The charge against the replies in this row is not that they reformulated. It is that they reformulated while denying that anything had been reformulated, teaching two generations of adherents that the tendency language had always carried the weight, when the movement's own nineteenth century conduct, its timetables, its splits, its expectations, proves the weight was carried originally by the strong versions. The denial, not the adjustment, is the dishonesty the scorecard records.

## The revisionism that governed while orthodoxy preached

The predictions row has a political dimension that the textual grading alone would miss, and it concerns the strangest fact in the movement's history: the party that excommunicated Bernstein governed by his conclusions. German Social Democracy, which condemned revisionism at congress after congress, conducted itself in practice as a reform party of the working class within the existing order, accumulating votes, administering cities, and postponing the revolutionary conclusion to a future its leaders privately ceased to schedule. The orthodox defence of the strong claims and the practical abandonment of them proceeded together for decades, and the conjunction is the reformulation pattern's institutional form: the claims were retained at full strength in the programme, where they cost nothing, and retired in practice, where they would have cost everything. Kautsky's role in the arrangement earned him the title his enemies used, the pope of Marxism, and the title was just in a sense he would not have welcomed: his orthodoxy functioned as the custodianship of claims the custodians no longer acted on, which is the precise definition of a charter detached from a programme.

The replies produced inside that arrangement bear its mark. When the strong claims were challenged, the defence could neither surrender them, since the charter's prestige was the party's cohesion, nor enact them, since the party's practice had priced them out of its own strategy. Restatement was the only remaining move, and the movement's theorists, Kautsky honourably among the first rank, performed it with increasing ingenuity as the century's evidence accumulated. The scorecard's finding about the predictions row is therefore not that its replies were written by dishonest men. It is that they were written inside an institution that had made honesty about the predictions organisationally impossible, since an honest sentence about immiseration's failure was a sentence about the charter, and the charter was the party. The analytical Marxists who finally wrote the honest sentences did so from universities, where a sentence about a theory costs its author a referee's report rather than a movement. The migration of the audit from the party school to the university is the predictions row's real history, and it is the same migration the scorecard has recorded in every row it grades.

### What would a successful reply to Bernstein have required when the audit appeared?

It would have required what Luxemburg attempted and what the party could not afford: a defence of the strong claims that staked them against the future in public, with the conditions of their surrender stated, so that the century's evidence could have vindicated or retired them cleanly.

Failing that, it would have required Bernstein's own move performed from the other side, an orthodox revision that repriced the claims openly. The party chose the third option, retention in the programme and retirement in practice, and the scorecard's relocation grade for the row is that choice's long shadow.

## Mandel's long waves: the most serious rescue of the predictions

Ernest Mandel's Late Capitalism, published in 1972, deserves a section of its own in the predictions row because it is the strongest reply that row contains, and grading the row without grading its best exhibit would repeat the offence this article exists to correct. Mandel rebuilt the theory of capitalist development around long waves of expansion and contraction, driven by the profit rate's movement and punctuated by exogenous shocks, wars, defeats of labour, and technological revolutions, that reset the system's conditions. Within that architecture the failed predictions change their aspect. Immiseration, polarisation, and breakdown were never, on Mandel's reading, calendar claims; they were claims about the system's tendencies across a wave structure whose phases the nineteenth century mistook for the whole cycle. The advanced countries' postwar prosperity becomes a long expansion's predictable content rather than an anomaly, and the theory's apparent refutations become its periodisation, discovered late.

The scorecard's assessment of Mandel is the most divided in this article, and both halves must be stated at strength. In his favour: the long wave construction generated genuine content, a periodisation of the postwar boom and a prediction, issued while the boom still ran, of its exhaustion and the return of open crisis, which the nineteen seventies supplied on schedule. A reply that predicts the turn it was built to explain has earned credit no tendency conversion earns, and Mandel's credit is real. Against him: the wave architecture's shock absorbers are exogenous by design, so that any sequence of events can be assigned to phases after the fact, and the construction's predictive successes and its accommodations are difficult to separate even for a sympathetic grader. The verdict the scorecard records is that Mandel's reply raises the predictions row from folklore to scholarship without reversing its grade. The claims as Bernstein audited them, national, absolute, and imminent, remain unrescued. What Mandel rescued was a descendant theory, stated with a scholar's candour about its machinery, and the rescue's quality is precisely what exposes the rest of the row: this is what answering looks like, and the tendency conversions taught in the textbooks are not it.

### Under what conditions does the tendency reformulation hold up?

In Mandel's construction, partially, because he paid for the reformulation with a mechanism, a periodisation, and a prediction that exposed the construction to the future. In its textbook form, no, because the tendency language there costs nothing and risks nothing: a tendency with indefinitely extensible countertendencies, asserted without the wave machinery that gave Mandel's version its content, accommodates every outcome and therefore explains none. The reformulation holds up exactly where its user accepts exposure, and collapses into the pattern where he declines it.

## The founding scene: the Bernstein controversy and the lesson the custodians learned

The reformulation pattern has a founding scene, and it predates the predictions it would come to manage. When Bernstein published his audit in 1899, the movement possessed a living mechanism for handling him: debate. Kautsky answered with orthodoxy's full artillery, Luxemburg answered with Reform or Revolution, a reply worth pausing over because it violates the pattern this article tracks. Luxemburg did not restate the claims Bernstein audited. She defended the strong versions, breakdown, polarisation, the revolutionary conclusion, and argued that Bernstein's evidence described a phase whose contradictions his own method could not see maturing. Her reply was, in the scorecard's terms, an answer rather than a relocation: it met the audit on its ground and staked the original claims. That it staked them on a future that did not arrive is a separate matter, belonging to the predictions row's verdict on the claims themselves. The reply's form was honest, and its form is what the tradition would shortly cease to produce.

What the controversy taught the movement's custodians was not Luxemburg's lesson. The organisational sequel to the debate was the condemnation of revisionism by resolution, the anathematising of the auditor while his statistics circulated unanswered, and eventually the construction of parties in which the audit function itself was treason. The pattern's preconditions were assembled in those years: a doctrine fused with an organisation's charter, an auditor reclassified as an enemy, and a repertoire of restatements that allowed the charter to survive any congress. Lenin's contribution to the repertoire was double edged in a way the authoritarianism section will meet again. His Imperialism performed the century's most consequential relocation, moving polarisation's theatre to the globe, and performed it as theory construction rather than as retreat, which it also was. The same writer built the organisational form that made retreats unnecessary to acknowledge, since a party that monopolises interpretation can promote a restatement to orthodoxy overnight and record the promotion as clarification.

### Why does the Bernstein controversy matter to a grading of replies written decades later?

Because it fixed the institutional template within which later replies would be produced and judged. After 1899 the movement knew, at the level of organisation rather than of doctrine, that audits were to be survived rather than answered, and that survival had techniques: anathema for the auditor, restatement for the claim, and the charge of vulgarisation for the record.

The replies graded in this scorecard were written inside institutions formed by that template, and their characteristic failures are its fingerprints.

The counterfactual is worth one paragraph, because it isolates the pattern's cause. Had the movement metabolised Bernstein as Luxemburg's form suggested, by defending the strong claims openly and letting them stand or fall, the twentieth century would have delivered its verdicts cleanly and the tradition would have been forced into either vindication or honest reconstruction by the nineteen thirties. The analytical Marxism of the century's end, which did exactly the reconstructive work under conditions of candour, shows what the earlier forcing might have produced sooner. Instead the custodians chose survival, and the choice compounded: each unacknowledged restatement raised the cost of the next acknowledgment, since to concede the pattern in 1960 was to confess the episodes of 1910 and 1930 as well. Sunk dishonesty behaves like sunk cost. The scorecard's coldest finding is that the pattern, once installed, is self financing, and that breaking it required writers, Kołakowski from the exit door and the September Group from the seminar room, who no longer needed the custodians' permission to publish.

## The authoritarianism charge: mechanism, counter-examples, and the residue

The charge, stated by Karl Kautsky and Rosa Luxemburg in 1918 from inside the movement and repeated by every generation since, is that the authoritarian outcome was not an accident of Russian backwardness but a destination the doctrine itself selected: that wherever parties of this tradition took power, the suppression of opposition, the fusion of party and state, and the conversion of the dictatorship of the proletariat into a dictatorship over it followed with a regularity that circumstance cannot explain. The article on [Marxism and authoritarianism](/2019/07/01/marxism-and-authoritarianism/) grades the charge itself; the replies are the subject here, and they are the strongest in the political family.

The first reply specifies a mechanism. The outcome, it argues, was produced not by the doctrine of class and value but by a datable organisational invention: the Leninist party form, with its democratic centralism, its ban on factions, its substitution of the party for the class and then of the leadership for the party. Marcel Liebman's Leninism Under Lenin, published in 1975, documented the form's construction under the pressures of illegality and civil war, and the reply built on his work has the great virtue of specificity. It names a mechanism, dates it, and generates a testable implication: where the form is absent, the outcome should differ. The second reply supplies the differing cases. Parties of the tradition have contested elections, lost them, and handed over power: the Communist Party of India in Kerala accepted dismissal and defeat and returned by the ballot; the Eurocommunist parties, whose programme Santiago Carrillo stated in 1977, committed themselves in writing to pluralism and alternation; the Chilean road attempted the transition under a constitution its enemies controlled. Ralph Miliband's Marxism and Politics, also 1977, assembled the tradition's own democratic resources, its council forms and its suspicion of the state, into the case that the doctrine contains more than one political future.

The scorecard grades this combined reply partly works, the highest grade any political reply earns here, and the boundary of the grade is exact. The mechanism reply succeeds in bounding the charge: it converts an allegation about a doctrine's essence into a claim about an organisational technology, and organisational technologies can be refused, as the counter-examples show parties refusing them. What it does not supply, and what no reply on file supplies, is the missing term in the doctrine itself. There is no Marxist theory of institutional constraint on power: no account of what, inside the theory, prevents a victorious party from doing what victorious parties do, no doctrine of rights against the revolutionary state, no design for the separation of powers in a society that has abolished the social basis on which the classical designs rested. The counter-examples borrowed their constraints from the liberal constitutions around them. The residue is therefore not a debating point but a gap in the architecture, and it stands in the unanswered five.

### How does the mechanism reply change what the authoritarianism charge must prove?

It changes the charge from a claim about essence into a claim about transmission. Once the Leninist party form is named as the mechanism, the critic must show either that the form is unavoidable for any party holding this doctrine, or that the doctrine generates the form reliably across unlike conditions.

That is a harder and more honest argument than the original slogan, and forcing the critic to make it is a genuine defensive achievement.

The pattern-conscious reader will ask whether the mechanism reply is itself a relocation, and the question deserves its answer. It is a narrowing: the doctrine defended after the reply is the doctrine minus its most successful organisational expression, which is a considerable subtraction. The scorecard nonetheless refuses the relocates grade here, for a reason that states the grading rule in its sharpest form. A narrowing counts as relocation when the narrower claim is substituted silently and the original denied. The mechanism reply, in Liebman and Miliband, is argued openly as a discrimination within the inheritance, with the costs on the table. Candour is the difference between a research programme correcting itself and a publicist changing the subject, and the scorecard prices candour wherever it finds it, including in the enemy's camp and including here.

## The charge's founding documents: Luxemburg's warning and Kautsky's pamphlet

The authoritarianism charge was born inside the tradition, in 1918, in two documents whose authority no subsequent reply has matched, and the replies' history begins with the failure to answer them at the time. Luxemburg's manuscript on the Russian Revolution, written in prison and published after her murder, granted the Bolsheviks their revolution and indicted their method in the tradition's own vocabulary: freedom reserved for the supporters of the government is no freedom, and a dictatorship exercised by a clique in the class's name would corrupt the class's own political life until the bureaucracy remained as the only active element. Kautsky's The Dictatorship of the Proletariat, published the same year, argued from the doctrine's democratic premises that a minority regime resting on the suppression of universal suffrage had abandoned the theory it invoked. Between them, the two documents contain the charge's entire subsequent development in embryo: the mechanism, the substitution sequence, the prediction that the emergency instruments would become the constitution.

The replies of the moment were polemics, and they are graded here as the genre deserves. Lenin's answer to Kautsky, The Proletarian Revolution and the Renegade Kautsky, is a masterpiece of invective that engages its target's weakest formulations and leaves the argument about suffrage and substitution untouched beneath the abuse. Luxemburg received no answer at all from the victors; her manuscript waited decades for full publication in the state her warning concerned, a suppression that constitutes its own verdict on the warning's accuracy. The episode fixed a precedent the political rows never escaped: the charge's most authoritative statements came from the tradition's own saints, and the tradition's custodians handled them by the two techniques the pattern would refine, anathema for the living critic and the archive's delay for the dead one. The scholarly replies graded in this article, Liebman's mechanism and Miliband's reconstruction, were written two generations later, by men working outside the custodianship, and their first task was archaeological: to recover the founding documents as arguments after their long service as embarrassments. That the recovery was possible at all is the tradition's credit. That it was necessary is the charge's.

### Why does the charge's internal origin matter to its grading?

Because it forecloses the defence of misunderstanding. A criticism pressed by the movement's own founders in the movement's own vocabulary cannot be dismissed as a hostile caricature, and every reply must therefore meet it as an internal audit rather than as an external attack.

The internal origin is also the scorecard's recurring datum: the strongest criticisms in nearly every row, from Bernstein's statistics to Benton's ecology, were first stated by adherents, which is the tradition's distinction and its indictment in a single fact.

## The theory and its implementations: the distinction and its limits

Behind the authoritarianism row stands the most frequently deployed reply in the entire defensive repertoire, the distinction between the theory and the states that governed in its name, and the scorecard owes it a general grading before the particular one. The distinction is legitimate in principle. Every doctrine is distinct from its enactments, and a theory of capitalism's dynamics is not refuted by the crimes of a government that nationalised the banks, any more than liberalism stands refuted by the empires its adherents administered. The reply's abuse begins where the distinction is used to assign all outcomes to circumstance while reserving all credit to doctrine, an accounting convention under which the theory's ledger can never show a loss. The scorecard's test for the distinction's honest use is the convergence question. Where enactments of a doctrine, across unlike countries, unlike cultures, and unlike starting conditions, converge on a common outcome, the outcome is evidence about the doctrine or about its organisational instruments, and the distinction's user must say which, and why. The mechanism reply is what the distinction becomes when the convergence question is answered honestly: the convergence is assigned to the party form, a specifiable component of the inheritance, and the doctrine is thereby indicted in one of its parts rather than absolved in its entirety.

The distinction's dishonest use has a tell that readers can apply without any of this article's machinery. It is deployed asymmetrically in time. The distinction is invoked for the failures, which belong to implementation, and suspended for the successes, industrialisation, literacy, the defeat of fascism's armies, which are credited to the doctrine's inspiration. A distinction that sorts evidence by its favourability is not a distinction but a filter, and the replies built on it are not replies but bookkeeping. The scorecard has graded the authoritarianism row's serious literature, Liebman and Miliband and the historians of the party form, as the distinction's honest employment, and the grade should not be transferred, by association, to the distinction's catechetical use, in which the theory is defined as whatever the record cannot embarrass. That use is the pattern in its political uniform, and its prevalence in the tradition's popular defence is the main reason the authoritarianism charge, alone among the political criticisms, retains its popular force despite the quality of the scholarly reply: the public meets the wall, not the mechanism, and concludes correctly that the wall is an evasion, and incorrectly that no better reply exists.

### How honest are the replies that circulate as the settled answers?

The scholarly ones, mostly, and the scorecard's grades rest on their concessions, which are in print. The standard ones, the formulations that circulate as the tradition's settled answers, are honest in proportion to their distance from the scholarship they summarise, which is usually considerable. A reader who wants the honest defence must go to the named works.

A reader who wants to know what the tradition tells its own will find the standard replies, and should grade the gap between the two literatures as the measure of the custodians' work.

## The counter-examples examined closely

The mechanism reply's empirical weight rests on its cases, and the cases repay the close reading the scorecard's method requires, because they are not equally strong and their inequality is informative. Kerala is the cleanest. A communist ministry won a state election in 1957, governed within a federal constitution it did not control, was dismissed by the centre, contested again, lost, won, and alternated across the decades as one party among others in a poor and literate electorate. Whatever constrained that party, the doctrine it carried did not prevent the constraint from operating, and the constraint's sources are identifiable: a constitution written by others, courts it did not appoint, an electorate it had educated to judge it. The case proves that the doctrine and alternation can coexist when the surrounding architecture supplies the binding. It does not prove the doctrine supplies the binding itself, which is the residue's whole point.

Eurocommunism is the ambiguous case, and its ambiguity is the finding. Carrillo's 1977 programme committed the Spanish party, in writing and against its own history, to pluralism, alternation, and the rejection of the single party state, and the Italian party under Berlinguer travelled a parallel road under the name of the historic compromise. The commitments were sincere in the only sense a grader can test: they were made at cost, against the protests of the movement's own custodians in Moscow, and they were kept in opposition, where keeping them was cheap. None of these parties held national power under its new dispensation, so the case establishes that the tradition could revise its political theory under democratic pressure, and cannot establish what the revised theory would have survived. The Chilean road is the tragic case, and the scorecard treats it with the care its dead are owed. The Unidad Popular government attempted the transition inside a constitution, respected the courts and the congress that obstructed it, and was destroyed by a coup its enemies organised and its own side's maximalists helped to provoke. Chile shows a Marxist government declining to seize the authoritarian instruments within its reach, at the price of its existence. As evidence about the doctrine's political possibilities it is powerful and unbearable in equal measure: the counter-example held, and holding cost everything, which is a datum about the environment's tolerance as much as about the doctrine's resources.

### What do the counter-examples, taken together, actually establish?

They establish that parties formed by this tradition have repeatedly chosen constitutional constraint over the seizure of undivided power, and have accepted the consequences, dismissal, defeat, and in the Chilean case destruction. They do not establish that the doctrine generates the constraint from its own resources, since in every case the binding architecture was inherited from the surrounding order.

The mechanism reply is thereby confirmed in its bounded form and the residue is confirmed with it, by the same evidence.

A fourth case belongs in the record because it tests the mechanism from the other direction. The parties that built the classical outcome were not, in the main, parties that had passed through a democratic phase and discarded it. They were parties constructed from the first on the Leninist template, in conditions of illegality that made the template's disciplines feel like survival, and they carried the template into power as their constitution in waiting. The historiography Liebman began made this sequence legible as a sequence: substitution practised in the underground became substitution enthroned in the state, and each stage's emergency supplied the next stage's precedent. A mechanism that explains both the outcomes and the exceptions, by the presence or absence of one organisational variable, has earned the scorecard's respect in the only currency the scorecard issues: it has survived contact with the cases its own side did not choose.

## The incentive charge: effort answered, innovation open

The charge, given its systematic form by János Kornai in his studies of the socialist economy and by Alec Nove in The Economics of Feasible Socialism in 1983, holds that social ownership dissolves the link between effort, enterprise, and reward. Under soft budget constraints the failing firm is rescued, the successful manager's surplus is confiscated by the plan, and the worker's diligence buys nothing he can keep; the predictable harvest is shortage, hoarding, and the quiet sabotage of the indifferent. The article on the [incentive problem in socialism](/2019/11/01/incentive-problem-socialism/) separates the charge's versions; the reply graded here addresses the version about work itself.

The reply rests on the cooperative evidence, and its theoretical charter is Jaroslav Vanek's The General Theory of Labor-Managed Market Economies, published in 1970. If the effort problem were a property of social ownership as such, firms owned by their workers should underperform conventional firms systematically. The empirical literature, surveyed by John Bonin, Derek Jones, and Louis Putterman in 1993, finds something more interesting: labour-managed and cooperative firms match or exceed the productivity of comparable conventional firms where the workers' stake is real, participation is genuine, and the firm's survival depends on its results. The Mondragón cooperatives in the Basque country operated at industrial scale for decades under exactly those conditions. Where the stake is fictitious, as in the state firm whose workers owned everything in general and nothing in particular, the incentive failure the critics describe appears on schedule. The variable that predicts effort, the reply concludes, is not the legal form of ownership but the reality of the connection between a worker's decisions and a worker's returns.

Partly works is again the verdict, and the boundary is the one the brief fixes. Against the effort version of the charge, the reply works: the claim that social ownership as such destroys diligence is empirically false, and the cooperative record is the refutation. Against the innovation version, the reply is silent, and the silence is structural. Innovation requires the staking of resources on uncertain futures by actors who capture enough of the upside to justify the risk, and who are permitted to fail at scale without political rescue or political punishment. No cooperative sector has yet operated as the innovation engine of a whole economy, and the reply's evidence, drawn from firms surviving inside market economies that supply the surrounding risk machinery, cannot show what a socialised economy would generate at its frontier. The innovation version therefore keeps its place among the unanswered five, not because defenders have been lazy, but because the experiment that would answer it has never been run under conditions a grader could accept.

### What does the cooperative evidence establish, and what does it leave open?

It establishes that effort and care respond to real stakes rather than to the legal form of ownership, since worker-owned firms with genuine stakes match conventional productivity while state firms with fictitious stakes reproduce the predicted indifference. It leaves open the question the evidence cannot reach: whether an economy of such firms generates frontier innovation, where the returns are distant, uncertain, and mostly captured by people other than the innovator.

One further discipline applies to this row, and it is the figures protocol this cluster observes throughout. The empirical dispute between the charge and the reply is real, and it is quantified in the literatures both sides cite; none of that quantification is reproduced here, because the verdicts in this article turn on what the evidence shows in direction, not on magnitudes this page has not verified. The reader who wants the numbers will find the dispute's evidence assembled in the articles this one links, which is where numbers in this series are kept: next to the methods that produced them, where they can be cross-examined.

## The market socialist reply to the incentive charge

Between Kornai's anatomy and the cooperative evidence stands a reply that belongs to neither, the market socialist construction descending from Lange, in which publicly owned firms compete under hard budgets and their managers are rewarded on results. The construction's defenders, in the feasible socialism debates of the nineteen eighties and in John Roemer's later designs for coupon socialism, argued that the incentive charge confuses ownership with governance: what disciplines a manager is the budget's hardness and the labour market for managers, both of which a market socialist order can supply while the capital stock remains social. Roemer's design pressed the argument to ingenuity, distributing the capital stock as coupons to citizens, forbidding the coupons' conversion into cash, and thereby attempting to socialise the returns to capital while keeping every incentive that depends on enterprise intact.

The scorecard grades this family as the incentive row's most intellectually serious reply and notes the characteristic price it pays. Each design feature that restores an incentive does so by recreating, inside the socialised frame, the institution whose abolition was the frame's purpose: the hard budget recreates the threat of failure, the managerial labour market recreates differential reward, the coupon recreates a claim on the social surplus distributed by a rule indistinguishable in its effects from a property right. The designs may be admirable; several of them are. What they defend, however, is a political economy the classical programme would not have recognised as its destination, and their authors, Roemer honourably among them, generally say so. The row's grading therefore repeats the transformation row's structure in political dress: the reply succeeds by surrendering part of what it defends, and its best authors' candour about the surrender is what separates their work from the folklore, in which market socialism is retailed as the classical destination with the rough edges filed, rather than as the successor doctrine it actually is.

### How do Marxists answer the criticisms, in the shortest honest form?

Family by family, and at two levels of quality that barely resemble each other. The scholars reconstruct, concede, and rebuild, and their replies fill this scorecard with its works and partly works grades. The custodians restate, deny, and anathematise, and their products fill the folklore.

Any shorter answer, whether it credits the tradition with a complete defence or dismisses it with a complete failure, has graded the defence as a whole, which is the one operation the evidence forbids.

## Kornai's anatomy: the charge in its mature form

The incentive charge reached its mature form in János Kornai's Economics of Shortage, published in 1980, and the reply literature cannot be graded without the maturity in view. Kornai, writing from inside a socialist economy and inside its reform debates, replaced the crude motivational slogan, people will not work without profit, with a systems anatomy. The soft budget constraint was his master concept: the state enterprise that cannot be allowed to fail faces prices, taxes, and credits as negotiable courtesies rather than as limits, and from that single softness the system's characteristic behaviours follow without any assumption about human laziness. Investment hunger, hoarding of materials and labour, the seller's market that makes quality a favour, the queue as an institution: the shortage economy is deduced from the constraint's softness, and the deduction's elegance is that it indicts the institutional wiring while acquitting the workers, whose behaviour inside the wiring is shown to be rational throughout.

The replies to Kornai divide by their relation to his central concept. The reform economists of Hungary and Poland, Kornai's own milieu, largely accepted the anatomy and disputed the remedy's reach: harden the constraint, they argued, by bankruptcy law and price reform, and the behaviours would follow the wiring into health. That reply concedes the charge's mechanism and contests its necessity, locating the softness in policy rather than in social ownership as such. The scorecard grades this the strongest form of the incentive reply, stronger than the cooperative evidence against the charge's general form, because it engages the charge's best version. Its limit is the one the Yugoslav and Hungarian records together impose: hardening was attempted, repeatedly, by governments committed to it, and the constraint re-softened under political pressure each time, since a state that owns the firms cannot credibly promise to let them die. The reply therefore survives as a conditional: if the softness is policy, the charge fails; the record suggests the softness is structural, and the conditional's antecedent is the very thing in dispute. Nove's feasible socialism, which accepted the anatomy and redrew the frontier between plan and market accordingly, remains the row's most honest settlement, and its honesty consists, once again, in publishing the price.

### Which single reply passes the most of the scorecard's tests?

By the scorecard's combined tests, the relational reconstruction of class, because it alone among the major replies defends the founding claim at full strength, absorbs the critic's evidence as its own illustration, and issues in a research programme that continues to lose arguments in public and therefore to win them. The ecological recovery stands beside it on textual grounds.

Between them they mark the standard the remaining rows are graded against, and the distance of the other rows from that standard is the article's quantitative finding in qualitative dress.

## The evidence base of the incentive reply: self-management and the cooperatives

The cooperative evidence graded in the scorecard rests on two great bodies of experience, and a grader owes the reader their complications as well as their direction. Yugoslav self-management, installed from 1950 and elaborated for four decades, was the largest experiment in worker governed production ever conducted, and both camps mined it. The reply's side cites its longevity and its enterprises' survival through market reforms as proof that workers can govern production without the predicted collapse of discipline. The charge's side cites the system's pathologies, its unemployment exported as migrant labour, its investment hunger and soft credit, its eventual dissolution along the federation's fractures, as the incentive problem photographed at national scale. The scorecard's reading is narrower than either. Yugoslavia demonstrates that the charge's effort version is false at scale, since its factories were not emptied by indifference. It also demonstrates the innovation version's resilience, since the system's technical progress rode substantially on imported licences and foreign technology, the frontier always arriving from economies organised otherwise. An experiment can answer one version of a charge and corroborate the other, and the Yugoslav file does exactly that, which is why honest graders quote it in halves and partisans quote it whole.

Mondragón supplies the second body, and its complication is the more instructive. The Basque cooperatives built, over decades, a federation with its own bank, its own university, and industrial firms competing in European markets, a record no serious grader dismisses. The complication is the federation's growing reliance, in its expansion, on wage labour outside the cooperative membership, in subsidiaries at home and abroad whose workers hold no stake of the kind the theory celebrates. Critics read this as the system's confession: success purchased by recreating the wage relation at the perimeter. Defenders read it as a transitional accommodation to hostile markets. The scorecard takes it as the reply's boundary made visible. The cooperative form solves the effort problem inside the membership by aligning stake and return. At the boundary, where the form meets people it has not enfranchised, the old relation reappears, and the question the innovation version asks, whether the form can organise an entire economy's frontier, reappears with it. Evidence that answers a charge at one scale and reproduces it at another has not been refuted. It has been located, and locating it is what grading is for.

### How should a reader use a verdict of partly works?

As a map reference rather than as a mark. Partly works means the reply succeeds against a specified version of the criticism and fails, or falls silent, against another specified version, and the verdict is only as useful as the specification. A reader who carries away the grade without the boundary has acquired a slogan.

A reader who carries the boundary away knows exactly which argument remains open, which is the knowledge the scorecard exists to transmit.

The kibbutz movement's long record belongs in this section as the limiting case that tests the reply's outer edge. Here were communities that abolished not merely private ownership of production but wages, differential reward, and in their strictest period the family economy itself, sustaining the abolition across generations by ideological selection and social sanction. Their economic performance was respectable and their eventual trajectory, toward differential wages and hired labour in community after community, is the single most instructive dataset the incentive debate possesses, because it isolates the variable the charge cares about. Where reward was severed from contribution entirely, the severance held only while the founding generation's convictions policed it, and eroded as the communities' own children priced their labour against the surrounding economy. The cooperative reply survives the kibbutz evidence, since its claim was always about real stakes rather than about abolished ones. The stronger claim sometimes made in the reply's name, that material incentives are a dispensable scaffolding of capitalism, does not survive it, and the scorecard strikes that claim from the reply's credit wherever the literature attempts to bank it.

## The class-structure critique: the reply that works outright

The critique came from several directions at once. Bernstein noted the persistence of the middle strata in 1899. Ralf Dahrendorf argued in 1959 that authority in institutions, not ownership of capital, had become the operative line of conflict. The affluent worker studies conducted by John Goldthorpe and his colleagues in the nineteen sixties reported a prosperous, privatised working class whose orientations embarrassed the embourgeoisement thesis and the polarisation thesis simultaneously. Assembled, the critique held that the class map at the centre of the theory, two great camps simplifying out of a dissolving middle, described a nineteenth century tendency that the twentieth century had reversed.

The reply begins by refusing the critique's unit of analysis. Class, in the theory's serious version, was never an income band or a consumption style. It names a position within the relations of production: whether a person must sell labour power to live, whether he commands the labour of others, whether he owns assets that free him from the wage relation altogether. Measured by that definition, the persistence of intermediate strata does not refute the map; it populates it. Erik Olin Wright's work from 1978 onward rebuilt the map on relational foundations, locating managers, supervisors, and credentialed experts in contradictory class locations, torn between the poles their incomes concealed. Harry Braverman's Labor and Monopoly Capital, published in 1974, supplied the dynamic the static critique lacked: the great clerical, technical, and service occupations of the twentieth century were shown being proletarianised in their conditions, routinised, monitored, and stripped of autonomy, whatever their salaries did. The professional who cannot choose his tasks, set his pace, or refuse the metric by which he is graded occupies, on the relational reading, a position his payslip misdescribes.

This reply earns the grade of works, one of only two unqualified grades in the table, and the grade is earned by the reply's structure. It does not narrow the claim after the fact; the relational definition is the definition in the theory's founding texts, recoverable from Capital and the Manifesto alike, and the income-band reading was always the vulgarisation. It does not deny the critic's evidence; Goldthorpe's affluent workers are exactly what the relational map predicts, prosperous wage labourers whose prosperity changes their politics without changing their position. And it generates the further research programme, Wright's comparative class analysis, that a merely defensive manoeuvre never generates. The concession recorded in the scorecard is real: the two-camp rhetoric of the Manifesto fits no advanced society, and the tradition's propagandists did the theory lasting damage by defending the rhetoric instead of the relation. But a reply that discards the poster and keeps the argument has answered, not relocated.

### Why is the class reply counted among the strongest?

Because it satisfies the three tests the scorecard applies everywhere and most replies fail somewhere. The definition it defends is the definition in the founding texts, not a substitute coined under pressure. The critic's evidence is absorbed rather than denied, since prosperous wage labour is a prediction of the relational map.

And the reply issues in new research, Wright's comparative programme, instead of closing the question down.

The contrast with the predictions row is instructive, and the reader should hold the two side by side. In the predictions row, the defended claim was restated after the evidence arrived, in a vocabulary its first audience would scarcely have known for its own. In this row, the defended claim is the original claim, stated more carefully than the movement's pamphlets stated it, and the evidence that was supposed to bury it turns out to be one of its illustrations. The difference between those two structures is the difference between a tradition correcting its expositors and a tradition correcting its theory while pretending otherwise. Only the second deserves the name this article gives the pattern.

## Dahrendorf's alternative: authority as the line of conflict

The class critique's most theoretical form came from Ralf Dahrendorf, whose Class and Class Conflict in an Industrial Society of 1959 proposed that the operative divisions of advanced societies run not through property but through authority: in every institution, from the firm to the ministry, a minority commands and a majority obeys, and conflict organises along that line wherever it is drawn. The proposal was dangerous to the relational map in a way the income literature never was, because it accepted the map's method, structural positions generating opposed interests, and contested only the positions' identity. If authority rather than ownership is the generative relation, then the abolition of private capital leaves the conflict structure untouched, and the socialist firm reproduces the classes it was built to dissolve, a prediction the Yugoslav and Soviet records were available to illustrate.

The reply, developed across Wright's work and the power resources literature it influenced, refuses the either or. Authority inside the firm, on this reading, is not an independent substance but a delegation: managers command because the capital relation installs them to command, their authority is bounded by the owner's purposes, revocable by the owner's decision, and exercised over a labour process whose design answers to accumulation rather than to the commanders' whims. Where authority appears autonomous, in the state bureaucracy or the managerial stratum of the nationalised economy, the reply treats the appearance as the problem the authoritarianism row already owns, the emergence of a stratum whose power rests on position rather than property, and concedes that the classical map describes that stratum badly. The concession is significant and the scorecard records it in the class row's margin: Dahrendorf's alternative fails as a replacement for the relational map in capitalist societies, where command tracks ownership with a fidelity his theory cannot explain, and succeeds as a diagnosis of the societies where ownership was socialised and command remained, which is to say it succeeds exactly where the missing constraint also lives. The two rows corroborate each other from opposite directions, and a reader who notices the corroboration has understood more about the framework's true frontier than either row states alone.

### Which Marxist replies actually work, stated without the scorecard's machinery?

The ones a hostile examiner would have to plan around. The relational account of class, which reorganised a field. The recovery of the metabolic rift, which reversed an objection's polarity from the texts. The mechanism specification in the authoritarianism debate, which converted a slogan into a researchable claim.

The fundamental theorem in the value controversy, which proved the exploitation connection inside the critics' own framework. Each of these changed what the other side must argue. The rest changed, at most, what the defence could say.

## The embourgeoisement debate: what the affluent worker studies settled

The class row's evidence has a history that repays one more pass, because the affluent worker studies were designed as a test and their result was subtler than either camp's headline. Goldthorpe and Lockwood's Luton research of the nineteen sixties examined prosperous manual workers in a boom town, the population on which the embourgeoisement thesis staked everything: if affluence dissolved class, it would dissolve there. The findings refused both theses on offer. The workers were not becoming middle class in orientation or association; their sociability remained privatised and familial, their unionism instrumental, their image of society a pecuniary hierarchy rather than a ladder of prestige they hoped to climb. Nor were they the classical proletarians of the movement's imagination, solidaristic and class conscious in the approved forms. They were, the researchers concluded, a new kind of working class, affluent, instrumental, and privatised, whose existence required both camps to revise.

The relational reply's absorption of this result is the episode the scorecard's works grade commemorates. An income reading of class had to choose between the theses and lose either way. The relational reading predicted the finding's shape in advance of its interpretation: workers whose position in production was unchanged, whose prosperity was real, whose politics would therefore be a politics of the wage bargain rather than of either embourgeoisement or insurrection. Lockwood's own typology of working class images of society supplied the mediating machinery, distinguishing the worker's picture of the social order from his position in it, and the mature class programme built the distinction into its instruments. The episode deserves its place in this article because it is the cleanest case in the entire scorecard of the sequence the pattern inverts. Here the evidence arrived hostile, the claim under test was the founding claim, and the reply consisted of showing that the claim, correctly understood, had expected the evidence all along, with the correctness checkable against texts written before the evidence existed. That is what an answer is. The predictions row shows what its counterfeit is, and the reader who holds the two episodes side by side has the article.

### In which parts has the framework been progressively narrowed?

In its prophetic and programmatic parts, demonstrably, and the relocation grades in the table are the measurement. In its analytical parts, no: the class programme's instruments are more precise than the founding texts, the metabolism analysis richer than its nineteenth century sketch, the social form critique deeper than its first statement.

The framework as a whole has therefore not so much narrowed as changed its centre of gravity, shedding the parts that made predictions it could not keep and retaining the parts that make analyses its rivals still borrow. Whether the remainder deserves the ancestral name is the question the objection section refuses to settle cheaply.

## Wright's programme under its own evidence

The class reply's grade of works is the scorecard's most exposed verdict, and the exposure is deliberate: this is the row where the article's method can be checked against a living research programme rather than against settled history. Wright's comparative project, built across the nineteen eighties and nineties with collaborators in a dozen countries, operationalised the relational map and submitted it to survey evidence on a scale no previous Marxist class analysis had attempted. The findings were not a coronation. Class locations structured income, attitudes, and political allegiance measurably but modestly; the contradictory locations behaved, in several tests, more like the poles they were torn between than like a coherent third force; and the programme's own later volumes revised the map's geometry under the pressure of its data. Wright himself, in his final work, migrated from the analysis of class structure toward the theory of real utopias, a migration his critics inside the programme read as concession and his defenders as development.

The scorecard's grade survives this record because the grade was never a verdict on the map's every contour. It is a verdict on the reply's structure against the critique that provoked it. The class-structure critique alleged that the theory's central category dissolved under twentieth century evidence. The reply rebuilt the category on its founding definition, generated two generations of empirical work, absorbed corrections in public, and left class analysis as a going concern in a discipline, sociology, that does not grade on loyalty. A reply whose research programme can lose arguments is a reply that has answered in the only sense that finally matters: it has kept its claim in the world where claims are tested. Set against the predictions row, where the claims were insulated from every test the century offered, the difference is the article's whole argument in miniature, and it is not an accident that the two rows were produced by the same tradition in the same decades. The difference was never the tradition. It was the institutional setting of the writing: a university programme answerable to referees, against a movement answerable to its charter.

### What did the critics get right about the replies themselves?

The sharpest of them noticed that the defence's quality tracked the defender's distance from custodianship. Kołakowski observed that the tradition's serious thinking migrated steadily outward, to exiles, heretics, and eventually to scholars with no organisation to protect, while the official centres produced commentary on commentary.

The observation is borne out by this scorecard's own sources: its strongest replies were written by men the custodians expelled, ignored, or posthumously forgave, and its weakest by the apparatus's retained voices.

E. P. Thompson's intervention belongs in this section because it tested the relational reply from the historical side and complicated it permanently. Class, in The Making of the English Working Class, is not a location but an event, something that happens in human relationships when shared experience is articulated against an opposing interest. The Thompsonian correction embarrassed the structural versions of the map, Wright's included, by showing class formation as cultural and temporal rather than positional. The mature relational programme absorbed the correction as it had absorbed Goldthorpe: class position structures the probabilities, class formation is the historical process that sometimes converts position into agency, and the two claims require each other. A critique absorbed as a distinction inside the reply is the strongest form an answer can take, and the class row's grade rests finally on this absorptive capacity, demonstrated across forty years of hostile and friendly fire. No other row in the table can show its like, which is why the row's verdict is the standard the objection section will use when it asks what the framework's survival actually consists in.

## The ecological objection: exclusion as diagnosis

Ted Benton's critique of 1989, the most searching the ecological family produced, charged that the labour theory of value structurally blinds the tradition to nature: by counting labour as the sole source of value, the theory licenses the treatment of the natural world as a free gift, inexhaustible and unpriced, and the productivism of actually existing socialism was the predictable fruit. The objection appeared to strike at the value theory's root, and for a decade the tradition had no organised answer to it.

The reply, built by Paul Burkett in Marx and Nature in 1999 and by John Bellamy Foster in Marx's Ecology in 2000, inverts the objection's polarity. Nature's exclusion from value, on this reading, is not the theory's oversight but the theory's diagnosis: it is capital that treats nature as valueless, because value in the system measures labour alone, and the theory's point is to expose that accounting as the system's own, with the metabolic rift between town and country, soil and eater, as the material wound the accounting conceals. Marx's analysis of soil exhaustion under capitalist agriculture, recovered from the texts by Foster, becomes the reply's Exhibit A: a nineteenth century critique of ecological plunder written from inside the value theory Benton accused of licensing it. The objection, the reply concludes, mistook the defendant's testimony for the defendant's crime.

The scorecard grades this reply works, the second unqualified grade, and again the structure explains the grade. The reply is textual before it is convenient: the rift analysis and the soil discussion stand in the published works, not in notebooks mined under pressure, which separates this row decisively from the abundance row below. It concedes what it must: the early writings carry a productivist strain, the movement's later practice carried it further, and neither is defended here. And it reverses the burden with evidence rather than with indignation. The ecological objection remains live against the tradition's practice and against its productivist texts. Against the value theory itself, as the reply reconstructs it, the objection has been answered.

### How does the ecological reply turn an objection into a diagnosis?

By relocating the exclusion of nature from the theory's premises to the theory's object. The critic charged that a theory measuring wealth by labour alone must be blind to nature. The reply shows the exclusion belongs to capital's own accounting, which the theory exists to expose, and that the same texts analyse the resulting rift between human production and natural cycles as a wound the system inflicts.

## The productivist texts: what the ecological reply does not defend

The ecological reply's concession deserves its own section, because the texts it declines to defend are neither few nor marginal, and a grader who understates them has repeated the defence's own selectivity. The Manifesto's celebration of the bourgeoisie's subjection of nature's forces is unambiguous in its admiration. The German Ideology treats the development of the productive forces as the absolute practical premise of emancipation, and the Critique of the Gotha Programme makes the higher phase wait upon abundance's arrival as a matter of definition. Across the nineteenth century corpus the mastery of nature figures as the species' coming of age, and scarcity as the realm of necessity whose overcoming is history's plot. A reader who assembled only these texts would conclude that Benton's second barrel struck the tradition's heart, and that the productivism of the socialist states was the corpus faithfully enacted by men who had read nothing else.

The reply's strength is that it does not need to deny this corpus. It needs only to establish that the corpus contains a second analysis, of capital's specific metabolism with nature, which the productivist passages do not cancel and which the tradition's practice ignored at the cost Benton described. Marx can hold, and the texts show him holding, both that the productive forces' development is emancipation's premise and that capital's form of that development tears the metabolic fabric on which all production rests. The tension between the two holdings is real, and the honest literature, Burkett's especially, treats it as a tension to be theorised rather than a contradiction to be dissolved by quotation warfare. What the reply establishes is that the value theory belongs to the second analysis and is innocent of the first's excesses. What it cannot establish, and what the abundance row grades, is that the destination survives the tension, since the destination was specified in the first corpus's vocabulary of abundance. The family's two rows, taken together, are the scorecard's clearest demonstration that a tradition can be acquitted on its analysis and convicted on its promise by the same evidence, read carefully.

### What is the reformulation pattern, in one paragraph a student can carry away?

It is the habit of answering disconfirmation by restating the claim at a level where the evidence cannot reach it, and then teaching the restatement as the claim's original meaning. Immiseration becomes a falling share, polarisation a global process, the profit law a tendency among countertendencies, revolution a possibility without a date. Each restatement may be defensible.

The pattern, the sequence of restatement, denial, and the charge that critics attacked a misunderstanding, is what convicts the defence of bad faith, and naming it is the precondition of believing any individual reply the tradition offers.

## Benton's challenge in full: the two barrels separated

Ted Benton's 1989 essay on Marxism and natural limits is the ecological family's founding document, and the replies cannot be graded fairly until its two arguments are held apart, since their fates diverged completely. The first argument concerned the value theory's structure: a theory that counts labour as value's sole substance, Benton argued, cannot register natural limits as anything but external accidents, and its concept of the labour process, borrowed from the manufacturing of its century, treats nature as the passive material of human intention. The second argument concerned the destination: the emancipated society of the tradition's promise presupposed the transcendence of natural necessity through expanding production, an abundance premise that a finite biosphere refuses. The first argument is about how the theory sees. The second is about what the theory wants. A reply could answer either without touching the other, and the subsequent literature did exactly that, which is why the scorecard keeps the ecological family in two rows and grades them in opposite directions.

Benton himself is a figure the scorecard treats with the respect due to the internal critic at his best. He wrote as a Marxist ecologist attempting the tradition's reconstruction, not as an enemy collecting scalps, and his essay's structure, immanent, textual, addressed to the theory's own standards, is the structure of the criticisms this tradition has historically been unable to answer by anathema. The fate of his two arguments illustrates the asymmetry the whole article tracks. The argument about seeing was answered, by the recovery of the rift, because the texts contained the answer and scholarship could retrieve it. The argument about wanting was not answered, because the texts contain the premise he attacked, and the only reply on file retrieves a different destination from the notebooks. A tradition's replies are strongest where its archive is deepest and weakest where its commitments are clearest, and Benton, by aiming one barrel at each, mapped the asymmetry in a single essay. His later work, which moved toward a reconstructed eco socialism drawing on the rift literature his challenge provoked, completes the episode's symmetry: the critic absorbed by the reply he forced, in the row where the reply worked, and unanswered still in the row where it did not.

### Which five items remain unanswered after the audit?

Five items, by this article's audit. First, the planning objection from dispersed knowledge, which no eliciting procedure has met. Second, the incentive charge where it concerns innovation, unanswered by any institutional design yet operated. Third, the want of any doctrine for binding revolutionary power, which reinterpretation cannot manufacture.

Fourth, the published destination's presupposition of plenty, which the notebook reply moves rather than meets. Fifth, the argument from inequality against the opening derivation, which the defence walked away from instead of mending.

## The rift literature after the recovery

The ecological reply's afterlife confirms its grade in the way the scorecard trusts most: it generated a literature that argues with evidence. Foster's recovery was extended by Brett Clark and Richard York into a general account of the rift's widening under the world economy's metabolism, applied to the oceans and the nitrogen cycle as Foster had applied it to the soil, and the research programme acquired the normal furniture of a science, measurements, disputes about them, and findings its founders did not anticipate. A reply that was a mere relocation could not have done this, because relocations are constructed to be untestable in the new location. The rift reply exposed itself: if the texts had not contained the analysis, the recovery would have collapsed at the first hostile seminar, and hostile seminars were not lacking.

The reply has also attracted the internal criticism that healthy programmes attract, and the scorecard records it because it sharpens the grade's boundary. Andreas Malm's work on fossil capital argues, against the rift literature's grain, that the ecological catastrophe's driver is not a generic metabolic relation between humanity and nature but a specific historical choice, capital's adoption of fossil energy for the discipline it imposed on labour, made at a datable moment against available alternatives. Malm's correction matters to this row because it relocates the ecological problem's centre from metabolism to power, and some of Foster's readers received it as a rival. The scorecard reads the quarrel as the programme's vitality: two Marxist accounts of the ecological crisis, disagreeing about mechanism, both unintelligible without the value theory's analysis of why nature enters production unpriced. The objection Benton pressed in 1989 alleged that the theory structurally blinded its holders to nature. A generation later the theory's holders are producing the period's most cited accounts of the crisis and quarrelling over which of them is right. Whatever blindness afflicted the tradition's productivist decades, the reply's demonstration that it was not structural has been ratified by the only ratification that counts, the subsequent literature's existence.

### Why is attribution to named scholars a condition of inclusion here?

Because an unattributed reply cannot be graded. Grading requires a text whose claims can be quoted, whose concessions can be located, and whose author has staked a reputation that a failed defence would damage. The tradition's folklore replies fail all three requirements, and their exclusion is not pedantry.

That refusal is how the scorecard keeps the defence's reputation, which is excellent, from being credited to its scholarship, which is uneven.

One boundary of this row must be drawn with care, since the ecological family's two rows are easily confused and the confusion flatters the defence. The works grade attaches to the value objection: the charge that the labour theory's structure blinds the theory to nature. It does not attach to the tradition's ecological record, which includes the productivism of its states and the strain in its early texts that the scorecard's concession column records, and it does not extend to the abundance premise, which is graded separately below and fails. A reader who merges the rows will believe the ecological critique has been answered. It has been answered at the level of the value theory and not at the level of the destination, and the distance between those levels is precisely the distance between an analysis of capitalism and a promise about what follows it. The tradition's ecological defenders are at their best when they keep the rows separate themselves. The scorecard follows them in it.

## The abundance premise: the reply that moves to the notebooks

Benton's second barrel, reinforced by André Gorz's farewell volume of 1980, targets the destination rather than the analysis. The society the theory promises, in which distribution by need replaces distribution by labour, presupposes a material abundance in which scarcity's discipline can be retired; and the Gotha Critique ties the higher phase to springs of wealth flowing more abundantly. A finite planet, the critique concludes, cannot underwrite the promise, and a theory whose destination is physically unavailable has been refuted at the level of its hope rather than its arguments.

The reply on file is Kohei Saito's, developed in Karl Marx's Ecosocialism in 2017 and in his subsequent work on degrowth communism. Saito argues from the late Marx's notebooks, the natural science studies and the excerpts of the final decade, that Marx himself broke with the productivism of his earlier programme, arriving at a vision of stationary material throughput, shortened labour, and wealth redefined as free time and communal richness rather than as accumulated goods. On this reading the abundance premise belongs to the young system the mature Marx was dismantling, and the destination survives the ecological critique in the revised form its own author was preparing.

The grade for this reply is relocates the claim, with respect for the scholarship and none for the manoeuvre's bookkeeping. The reply's evidence is real, and Saito's recovery of the late notebooks is a genuine scholarly event. But the claim defended after the reply is not the claim the critique attacked. The critique addressed the published programme, the Gotha Critique's flowing springs, the destination as the movement proclaimed it for a century and a half. The reply answers from unpublished materials the movement never read, which means the destination the reply saves is one nobody was promised. That is relocation in its most respectable form, relocation by archive, and it leaves the abundance premise standing in the unanswered five, where the scorecard keeps it until a reply engages the published texts on the ground the critique chose.

### What separates this ecological row from the one above it?

The evidence's address. The value objection was answered from the published works, from analyses of soil and rift that any reader of Capital could have found before the objection was raised. The abundance reply is drawn from notebooks unpublished in the author's lifetime and unknown to the tradition that carried his name.

A reply the tradition could not have given during the century of its ascendancy is a discovery about Marx, not a defence of Marxism as it was actually held.

## Gorz's farewell and the Budapest answer: the abundance premise from inside

André Gorz's Farewell to the Working Class, published in 1980, pressed the abundance premise from a direction the ecological critics had not used, and the replies it drew complete this row's file. Gorz argued that the classical destination had become unavailable for internal reasons: the productive forces' development had dissolved the class whose needs and capacities the destination presupposed, replacing the producer of the classical texts with a fragmented stratum for whom work could no longer be life's prime want. The premise under attack was not the planet's capacity but the subject's existence, and the attack's force came from its author's credentials as a theorist of the tradition he was farewelling. The replies divided. The orthodox dismissed the farewell as a journalist's capitulation to a bad decade. The serious answer came from the Budapest School's theory of needs, from Ágnes Heller and her colleagues, whose work on the dictatorship over needs had already indicted the socialist states for administering the population's wants from above. On the Budapest reading, the destination was never a cornucopia. It was a society in which radical needs, for free time, for unalienated activity, for relations unmediated by money, could be articulated and met without an administering authority, and scarcity of goods was compatible with the destination so long as the needs that mattered were not goods intensive.

The scorecard grades the Budapest answer as the abundance row's most interesting near miss. It engages the premise at the level of the destination's content rather than fleeing to the notebooks, and its account of radical needs is a genuine theoretical construction with roots in the early Marx. Its limit is textual and biographical at once: the needs reading reconstructs the destination from the 1844 manuscripts and from the tradition's humanist margin, while the canonical destination, the one the movement promised and the Gotha Critique specified, runs on the springs of wealth. The reply therefore relocates by canon rather than by archive, substituting the young Marx's destination for the mature programme's, and the substitution, however attractive the substitute, leaves the attacked claim standing where Gorz and Benton left it. The row's verdict is unchanged, and its file now shows the full range of the defence's options exhausted: archive, canon, and redefinition have each been tried on the abundance premise, by serious people, and the premise remains, because the text that states it is canonical, published, and unambiguous. Some claims cannot be replied to. They can only be withdrawn, and withdrawal is the one operation the custodial structure exists to prevent.

### What must a successful reply to the abundance premise concede first?

That the canonical destination was specified under abundance's condition, in so many words, in the tradition's one programmatic text on the future society. Every reply that begins elsewhere, in the notebooks, in the manuscripts, in the theory of needs, has declined the concession and thereby announced its genre.

A reply that began with the concession could then argue, as refinement rather than as rescue, that the destination's core survives the premise's withdrawal. That argument has never been made by a custodian, because its first sentence is the one the structure forbids.

## The notebooks and their readers: the abundance reply's second generation

Saito's reading has itself become an object of scholarship, and the second generation's quarrel over it is the best available evidence for the scorecard's grade. Sympathetic critics have pressed two difficulties. The first is textual proportion: the late notebooks are excerpting notebooks, thousands of pages in which Marx copied and annotated the natural science of his day, and building a revised destination on excerpts requires inferring a settled intention from a scholar's reading notes, an inference the same scholars would resist if the destination inferred were one they disliked. The second is the canon problem. The Gotha Critique's abundance condition is not an obscure text. It is among the most canonical documents the tradition possesses, the one programmatic statement about the future society its author allowed to circulate, and a reply that subordinates it to unpublished notebooks must explain why the notebooks outrank the canon, beyond the fact that the canon has become inconvenient. Saito's answer, that the notebooks are later and therefore represent the mature view, applies a principle, last thoughts win, that the tradition applies nowhere else in its canon, where earliness and lateness are invoked selectively according to the needs of the argument being defended.

The degrowth movement's embrace of the reading adds a sociological datum the grader should not ignore. A reply that arrives precisely when a new constituency needs it, supplying a founding father for a programme the founder never named, bears a burden of suspicion that has nothing to do with its author's integrity, which is not in question. Programmes adopt ancestors opportunistically in every tradition, and the adoption is evidence about the programme's needs rather than about the ancestor's views. The scorecard's grade of relocates the claim is therefore doubly secured: the reply moves the destination from the published programme to the notebooks, and the movement that celebrates the move does so because the published programme had become indefensible in the constituency the movement addresses. None of this diminishes the notebooks' scholarly interest. Marx's late reading was real, his dissatisfaction with his earlier productivism is plausibly documented, and a history of his development should say so at length. But a defence of the tradition's promise must answer for the promise as it was made and believed, and the promise as believed ran through Gotha, not through the excerpts. The abundance premise stands.

### What would reverse the finding that five critiques stand unanswered?

The production, for any one of the five, of a reply meeting the specification this article sets out: a defence on the critic's chosen ground, of the proposition actually attacked, at close to its original strength, conceding what the criticism gets right, and terminating in a checkable claim with its defeater attached. The invitation is standing and specific.

A single qualifying reply reverses the summary verdict for its item and demotes the five to four, and the scorecard's author would record the demotion with more pleasure than any other correction the article could receive.

There is a final refinement to enter about this row, because it generalises to the pattern section that follows. Relocation by archive differs from relocation by restatement in one respect: it is honest about its evidence. Saito does not pretend the Gotha text says what he needs. He argues that another text, later and unknown, outweighs it. The manoeuvre is relocation nonetheless, because the claim under defence has changed identity in transit, from the programme the movement proclaimed to a programme its founder privately approached. But the candour about the evidence places the reply nearer the refinement boundary than the tendency conversions ever came, and a future grader, presented with a tradition that had openly adopted the notebook destination as a correction, would be obliged to grade the adoption as refinement and the premise as answered at the price of the original promise. The price is the point. It has not been paid, because paying it requires the custodians to say the promise was withdrawn, and the pattern's fourth move, the denial, forbids exactly that sentence.

## The reformulation pattern named

Everything so far has been preparation for the article's centre, which is not a reply but a diagnosis of the replies. The reformulation pattern is the tradition's characteristic response to disconfirmation: shift the claim upward into a generality the offending facts cannot touch, then present the shifted version as the doctrine's perennial meaning. The pattern has four moves, and the reader who learns to spot them owns the master key to a century of defensive literature. First, the strong claim meets evidence it cannot survive. Second, a weaker relative of the claim is located in the texts, or constructed from their materials. Third, the weaker relative is presented, with genuine citations, as what the theory held all along. Fourth, the critic is charged with having attacked a vulgarisation, and the episode closes with the theory's prestige intact and its content reduced. Each move is defensible in isolation. The sequence is the offence.

The pattern must be distinguished from two neighbours it resembles, because the tradition's defenders are entitled to the distinctions and this article grants them in advance. It is not refinement. Refinement narrows a claim because new evidence shows where the truth's boundary runs, announces the narrowing as a correction, and pays for the correction with predictive content surrendered in public. Nor is it the ordinary auxiliary adjustment every research programme performs, the Duhemian repair of instruments and assumptions that Lakatos priced into his methodology. The reformulation pattern is defined by the conjunction of the other two features: the restatement is pitched precisely where the evidence cannot follow, and the restatement is denied. A programme may adjust. A programme may even retreat. What it may not do, and remain an honest programme, is retreat while teaching its students that no ground was given.

### Why must the pattern be named before the replies can be believed?

Because the pattern, once seen, re-prices every individual reply. A defence operating inside the pattern asks the reader to accept each restatement as the original meaning, which trains the reader to stop checking originals. Naming the pattern restores the checking: every verdict in the scorecard was reached by comparing the claim defended with the claim attacked, and a reader equipped with the pattern can repeat the comparison independently.

The pattern's evidentiary status deserves a paragraph of its own, because the tradition's ablest defenders will ask, reasonably, whether the pattern is a finding or a rhetorical frame imposed on miscellaneous episodes. The answer is that the pattern is a claim about timing and presentation, and both are checkable. Timing: in the canonical cases, the weak version's promotion follows the strong version's failure, and the documentary record of the movement, its congresses, its controversies, its popular literature, establishes which version carried the movement's expectations before the failure. Presentation: the weak version is standardly introduced not as a correction but as an explication, accompanied by the charge that critics attacked a misunderstanding. Both features are matters of record. A defender who wishes to defeat the pattern diagnosis in a given case has a clear path: produce the weak version carrying the movement's expectations before the disconfirming evidence arrived. The scorecard's defeater for the predictions row is written in exactly those terms, and it has not been met.

There is also a mechanism to name, because patterns without mechanisms are astrology. The reformulation pattern is what a theory produces when it is simultaneously a research programme and the charter of a movement. A pure research programme can afford corrections; its currency is reputation among peers, and peers reward the man who prices his own errors. A movement's charter cannot be corrected by its custodians without cost, because the charter is also a promise to the faithful and a title deed to institutions. The parties, publishing houses, and later the states that held the theory in trust had constituencies for whom the strong claims were not hypotheses but inheritances. Under that pressure, restatement without acknowledgment is not a mystery of intellectual character. It is the predictable output of an institutional arrangement, and it would afflict any doctrine, however truthful its core, that was held the same way. This is why the comparison with other traditions, below, is a matter of degree and structure rather than of unique Marxist vice.

## Four restatements examined one by one

Immiseration first, because it is the pattern's type specimen. The strong claim, as the movement's first generations received it, held that the worker's condition deteriorates absolutely under capital accumulation: the more wealth the worker produces, the poorer he becomes, in a formulation the young Marx gave its most quotable form. Bernstein's statistics showed real wages rising across the advanced economies, and the strong claim died of them. The restatement proceeded on two tracks. Relative immiseration shifted the variable from the wage to the share: the worker's portion of the social product falls even as its absolute size grows, a claim about distribution rather than destitution. Insecurity immiseration shifted the variable again, from income to exposure: the worker's dependence deepens, his employment grows precarious, his old age unprovided. Both restatements capture something real, and the second in particular has worn well. Neither is the claim Bernstein audited. And the standard presentation of both, in the textbooks of the tradition, introduces them as the theory's meaning from the start, with the absolute version dismissed as a youthful exaggeration its author supposedly outgrew on a schedule the publication dates do not support.

Polarisation second. The Manifesto's architecture requires society to simplify into two great hostile camps, and the nineteenth century movement read the sentence as a forecast with a date implied. The twentieth century produced instead a proliferating structure of intermediate positions, salaried professionals, small proprietors, state employees, whose existence the strong claim could not digest. The restatement relocated the process from the nation to the world: polarisation proceeds globally, between a core whose workers are stabilised by imperial rents and a periphery whose super exploitation supplies them. Lenin's Imperialism gave the relocation its theory, and the theory explained real things about the world economy's structure. It also converted a claim about every capitalist society into a claim about the system as a whole, at which altitude no national evidence could embarrass it. The class row of the scorecard shows the honest alternative that was available: a relational restatement argued from the founding texts and priced openly. The polarisation restatement, by contrast, was standardly presented as the unfolding of the original insight, and the camps of the Manifesto continued to be quoted in the indicative mood by writers who no longer believed them in it.

The falling rate of profit third, because here the pattern's textual warrant is strongest and the case therefore hardest. Marx himself stated the law as a tendency and himself catalogued the counteracting influences, in the third volume's most careful chapters. A defender is entitled to every word of that. What the pattern adds is the observation of use: in the movement's strong periods the tendency was preached as a mechanism of inevitable breakdown, with the counteracting influences treated as delays; after the breakdown failed to arrive on any schedule, the counteracting influences were promoted to co stars, and the law's content thinned toward the observation that profitability faces pressures. A tendency whose countertendencies are indefinitely extensible predicts everything and forbids nothing, and the distance between the third volume's hedged law and the movement's preached certainty is the distance the pattern measures. Henryk Grossman's breakdown reconstruction of 1929 stands as the honourable exception that proves the grading rule: he stated the strong version, drew its conclusion, and let the conclusion's failure count against the version he had stated.

Revolution in the advanced countries last. The expectation structured the movement's strategy for two generations: the most developed capitalism would break first, because its contradictions were ripest. The breaks came at the periphery instead, in the least developed empires, and the advanced countries produced reform. The restatements multiplied. Revolution became a possibility rather than an expectation, conditioned on crises of unspecified depth. The advanced countries' failure was reassigned to their labour aristocracies, their welfare states, their consumer integrations, each explanation plausible, each post hoc, each converting a falsified sequence into a deferred one. Mandel's Late Capitalism attempted the most serious rescue, embedding the deferral in a theory of long waves, and even Mandel's construction illustrates the pattern's gravity: the more powerful the rescue apparatus, the less the original expectation constrains it. A claim that survives every outcome by redescribing the outcome's place in a longer rhythm has not been defended. It has been pensioned.

### How is the reformulation pattern different from ordinary refinement?

Refinement announces itself. It states the narrower claim as a correction, identifies the evidence that forced the narrowing, and accepts the loss of predictive content as the price of accuracy. The reformulation pattern performs the same narrowing while denying that any narrowing occurred, presenting the weaker claim as the original meaning and the critic as a man who attacked a misunderstanding. The observable difference is therefore presentational and documentary, not metaphysical, and it can be checked in any library.

## The pattern's fourth move: the charge of vulgarisation

One component of the reformulation pattern deserves isolation, because it does the pattern's dirtiest work and is the component a reader is most likely to meet unaccompanied. When a strong claim fails, the restatement requires an account of why the strong claim was ever believed, and the account the pattern supplies is the charge of vulgarisation: the strong claim, it is said, was the property of popularisers, journalists, and the movement's enemies, while the theory itself always held the subtle version. The charge is sometimes true. Marx complained about his expositors in his lifetime, and every complex doctrine accumulates a simplified double that circulates more widely than the original. The pattern's use of the charge is distinguished by its timing and its direction. It is produced after the failure, never before it, although the vulgarisation, if it existed, was available for correction all along. And it is directed outward, at the critic who took the strong claim seriously, rather than inward, at the movement that taught the strong claim to its own members for generations and corrected them only when the correction became unavoidable.

The documentary test dissolves most instances. A vulgarisation that fills the movement's own catechisms, congress resolutions, and canonical textbooks, endorsed by its highest authorities across decades, is not a vulgarisation of the doctrine. It is the doctrine as the doctrine's custodians held and taught it, and its later reclassification as a misunderstanding is a falsification of the tradition's own record, performed to protect the record's custodians. The scorecard therefore applies a rule of custody: a claim counts as the tradition's claim if the tradition's authorised teaching carried it, whatever the founding texts' subtleties, because a doctrine exists in its transmission and not only in its sources. The rule is severe, and it is the same rule the tradition applies to its enemies, whose popular versions it has never scrupled to hold against their scholars. Symmetry in grading is the whole of the scorecard's ethics, and the fourth move fails it absolutely.

### When do the goalposts move, and when do they stay?

The scholarly tradition, at its best, does not: its strong replies defend the original claims or publish their surrenders. The custodial tradition does, as a matter of documented routine, and the reformulation pattern is this article's name for the routine. The honest answer to the question is therefore institutional rather than tribal.

Goalposts move where a charter must be protected and cannot be corrected in public. They stay where referees, rivals, and the author's own reputation make movement more expensive than concession.

## Two rows the table could not hold: the national question and the feminist audit

The brief's scorecard holds eleven rows, and two further criticisms, graded in the companion survey, deserve a prose verdict here because their replies illuminate families the table underrepresents. The national question first. The criticism, pressed by Tom Nairn with a Scot's authority, held that national allegiance had outlasted and outweighed class allegiance at every test the century set, and that the theory had no account of the fact, only a recurring surprise at it. The tradition's replies were of two kinds. The Leninist reply converted the criticism into a strategy: national movements were to be supported as solvents of empire, their energy borrowed for a transition they would not control. As an answer to the criticism's explanatory charge, this was a change of subject so complete that the scorecard's vocabulary strains; the criticism asked why nations command the loyalty classes were predicted to command, and the reply explained how to use the loyalty, not why it exists. The Austro Marxist reply, Otto Bauer's construction of national cultural autonomy, was the serious one: an institutional design for detaching nation from territory and administering culture as a personal rather than a territorial right. It answered a question adjacent to the criticism, the constitutional one, with genuine ingenuity, and left the explanatory question, the depth of the attachment itself, where Nairn had put it. The row that would result, could the table hold it, grades relocates the claim with an honourable mention, and its absence from the table is a function of the brief's scope rather than of the verdict's doubt.

The feminist audit cuts the other way, and its reply file is better than the tradition's reputation in the area suggests. Heidi Hartmann's 1979 diagnosis of the unhappy marriage between Marxism and feminism charged the theory with sex blindness: its categories, she argued, are empty of the relations between men and women, and the marriage's terms required feminism to supply what the theory lacked. The strongest reply came from Lise Vogel's Marxism and the Oppression of Women, published in 1983, which refused the dual systems settlement Hartmann's essay implied and rebuilt the account of women's oppression from the theory's own centre, the reproduction of labour power, arguing that the material root of the oppression lies in capital's need for the daily and generational renewal of the workforce and in the privatised form that renewal takes. The reply's structure will by now be familiar in its virtues: it concedes the blindness of the received categories, it works from the founding texts' own logic rather than from a protective gloss, and it generates a research programme, social reproduction theory, whose descendants argue with evidence in journals the custodians never read. The row would grade partly works, the residue being the tradition's long practical record, in its parties and its states, of postponing the question to the revolution's morning after. Two rows beyond the table thus confirm the table's law from outside it: the replies grade by their candour and their institutional address, in feminism's case as in value theory's, and the frontier of the defence is wherever a scholar was free to concede.

### Why do the rows beyond the table confirm the table's law?

Because they were not selected to. The national question's replies and the feminist audit's were graded here after the scorecard's method was fixed, against criticisms the brief did not assign, and they distribute exactly as the method predicts: strategy and ingenuity in place of explanation on the one side, concession and reconstruction toward a working programme on the other.

A method that predicts the grades of cases it was not built on has earned more confidence than one that merely summarises its own sample.

## A positive control: the transition debate and the tradition arguing well

The pattern diagnosis needs a control case, a controversy in which the tradition argued at its best, so that the reader can verify that the scorecard's standards are attainable inside the tradition and are not a rigged instrument. The debate over the transition from feudalism to capitalism, opened by Maurice Dobb's Studies in the Development of Capitalism in 1946 and joined by Paul Sweezy in the exchange that followed, supplies the control. Here were two Marxists disputing the mechanism of a world historical transformation, the dissolution of feudal relations against the solvent of long distance trade, in the pages of a journal, with the evidence of the manorial records and the price series marshalled on both sides. The exchange exhibits every virtue the scorecard rewards. Claims were stated in forms the evidence could reach. Concessions were made in print, by Dobb on the trade mechanism's local force and by Sweezy on the internal relations' primacy in the English case. Later participants, Rodney Hilton's generation and Robert Brenner's after them, inherited a question sharpened by the exchange rather than a catechism defended by it, and the debate's descendants are still producing findings.

Set the control beside the predictions row and the diagnosis sharpens from accusation into measurement. One and the same tradition argued the transition question, in those very decades, like a science and the immiseration question like a charter. The difference was not talent, since Dobb appears in both literatures, on the calculation question as a scholar and in the prophetic controversies as a custodian's ally. The difference was the question's relation to the charter. The transition debate threatened no promise the movement had made to its members; its outcome, whichever way it fell, cost the organisation nothing. The predictions were the promises. Where a question's answer could embarrass the charter, the pattern governed the arguing. Where it could not, the tradition's scholars were as good as any in the century, and the control case proves the capability existed all along, waiting for questions the custodians did not need to fear.

### What does the control case establish about the pattern's cause?

That the cause is not the doctrine's content but the doctrine's employment. A theory whose historical claims are argued under charter pressure produces the pattern; the same theory's claims, argued where the charter has no stake, produce scholarship indistinguishable in manners from any healthy field's.

The diagnosis therefore indicts an institutional arrangement rather than a cast of mind, and it implies a remedy the tradition's remnants have in fact applied: remove the charter, keep the questions, and the replies improve.

## The pattern measured: how a sceptic could test this article's central claim

The reformulation pattern is this article's namable claim, and the article's own discipline requires that the claim be stated in a form a sceptic could test, with the evidence that would defeat it named in advance. The test is documentary and laborious, which is the usual price of testing claims about literatures. Take a strong claim and fix its received form at a date when the movement's expectations ran on it, using the movement's own teaching and agitational materials as the record of reception. Then trace the claim's form in the same tradition's teaching materials at intervals after the disconfirming evidence, and record two variables: whether the form narrowed, and whether the narrowing was announced as a correction or presented as the perennial meaning. The pattern predicts a specific signature across the sample: narrowing concentrated after disconfirmation, announcement rare, and the charge of vulgarisation appearing at the moments of transition. A sample that showed narrowing announced as correction at the rate ordinary sciences announce theirs would defeat the pattern diagnosis for that claim, and a sample that showed it across the canonical claims would defeat the diagnosis altogether.

The article's author records, in the spirit of the exercise, that the test has been performed informally against the four restatements examined above and against the textbook tradition in three languages, and that the signature appeared in each. The informality is confessed because the scorecard's standard applies to the scorecard: a claim about a literature, offered without a replicable method, is a folklore claim with footnotes. The method is therefore printed here in full, the sample is described, and the invitation to replicate is standing. Should a replication find the announcements this article found missing, the pattern section's verdict falls, and the fall would be recorded by the same instrument that recorded the verdicts on the tradition: publicly, in the row where it belongs.

### What evidence would defeat the reformulation pattern diagnosis itself?

A documentary sample showing that the tradition's teaching literature, at the moments when its strong claims met disconfirming evidence, standardly announced the resulting narrowings as corrections, quoted the surrendered forms, and priced the losses. One claim so handled would dent the diagnosis. The canonical claims so handled would destroy it.

The diagnosis is offered in the confidence, checkable in any good library, that the sample runs the other way.

## Reformulation elsewhere: the comparison with other traditions

The brief for this article imposes a comparison discipline, and the discipline is honoured here in both directions. The reformulation pattern is not unique to Marxism, and pretending otherwise would be this article's own act of bad faith. Psychoanalysis reformulated its claims about memory, seduction, and therapeutic efficacy under evidential pressure, standardly presenting each restatement as the master's deeper meaning. Neoclassical economics absorbed the Cambridge capital controversies by quietly ceasing to assert the aggregate production function in its strong form while continuing to teach its parables. Modernisation theory converted its failed sequence predictions into a vocabulary of dimensions and prerequisites. The pattern is a general pathology of doctrines that organise communities, and the mechanism named above, charter plus programme in one text, predicts its appearance wherever that conjunction holds.

What can be assessed is degree, and the scorecard's implicit finding is that the Marxist case sits at the severe end of the distribution, for two structural reasons. The first is the custodianship of states. For much of the twentieth century the theory's official interpreters commanded police powers over interpretation itself, which removed the peer correction that disciplines other traditions: a Soviet economist could no more announce a correction to the doctrine than a court theologian could announce one to the creed, and the pattern's fourth move, the denial, was enforced institutionally rather than merely practised rhetorically. The second is the theory's own comprehensiveness. A doctrine that speaks to value, history, politics, and the future of the species offers its defenders an unmatched surface of adjacent claims across which a threatened proposition can be relocated, from economics to philosophy, from prediction to tendency, from nation to globe. Traditions with narrower charters run out of room to retreat sooner, and are corrected sooner by the simple exhaustion of alternatives.

### How does the comparison with other traditions discipline the pattern's use?

It forbids two opposite errors. Against the hostile reader, it establishes that reformulation is a pathology of chartered doctrines generally, so its presence in Marxism cannot by itself convict this theory of unique fraudulence. Against the friendly reader, it establishes that degree is a real and assessable quantity, and that the Marxist degree was worsened by identifiable structures, state custodianship and doctrinal comprehensiveness, which the tradition chose and maintained. The comparison thus converts a slogan into a measurement problem.

## The pattern's economics: why restatement is cheaper than correction

The mechanism section named the pattern's institutional cause, and a short analysis of its price structure completes the explanation. Correction is expensive for a chartered doctrine in four currencies. It costs authority, since the corrector announces that the charter's custodians taught error. It costs cohesion, since the corrected claim was often the one the faithful loved best, the promise rather than the analysis. It costs territory in argument, since every conceded criticism arms the critics' next formulation. And it costs the corrector personally, in movements where the correction of doctrine has historically been a career ending and occasionally a life ending act. Restatement, by contrast, is cheap in all four currencies. It preserves the formulae in which authority and cohesion are stored, concedes no territory that a headline can register, and exposes its author to no charge worse than subtlety. A rational custodian, pricing the options in the currencies his institution actually uses, chooses restatement every time, and the pattern's century of dominance is the aggregate of those rational choices.

The universities invert the price structure, which is why the scorecard's geography of quality follows the institutional map so faithfully. In a refereed literature, correction is the currency of advancement: the scholar who prices his predecessor's error, or his own, gains exactly the reputation the movement's corrector loses. Restatement, correspondingly, is expensive, since referees are paid in the coin of detected evasions and a restated claim presented as an original meaning is the kind of evasion detection is easiest against a literature with footnotes. The same writer, moved between the two institutions, will produce the pattern in one and replies in the other, and the tradition's history supplies the experiment repeatedly: scholars whose party writings are catechetical and whose journal writings are candid, sometimes in the same year, on the same question. The pattern is therefore not a measure of the tradition's intelligence or of its honesty as a collection of persons. It is a measure of which institution held the pen, and the scorecard's practical advice to readers follows directly. Ask of any reply not only who wrote it but where, and under what price structure, and the grade will usually be legible before the argument is.

### Why does the same tradition produce both the strongest replies and the folklore?

Because it was never one institution. It was a movement with a charter, which generated custodians and their catechisms, and a diaspora of scholars in universities, exile journals, and prison cells, who generated the replies. The two literatures share a canon and a vocabulary and nothing else: not their incentives, not their audiences, not their standards of success.

Grading the tradition as a whole averages the two literatures into a fiction, which is why this article grades replies one by one and lets the distribution speak.

## The pattern in the textbooks: how restatements are taught

The reformulation pattern would be a curiosity of polemics if it did not reproduce itself pedagogically, and its reproductive organ is the textbook. A student meeting the tradition through its teaching literature encounters the claims in their restated forms from the first page: immiseration introduced as the doctrine of the falling share, polarisation introduced as a feature of the world system, the profit law introduced with its counteracting influences already promoted to equal billing, the constraint problem absent altogether, since a syllabus cannot examine an omission its own tradition never registered. The student who later meets Bernstein's audit, or Böhm-Bawerk, or Hayek's epistemic argument, experiences the critic as a man attacking positions nobody holds, because the positions the critic attacks were retired from the syllabus before the student arrived. The pattern thereby acquires its most effective defence: a readership for whom the strong claims are literally unknown, and who experience the charge of goalpost moving as a slander against teachers who only ever taught the posts in their present positions.

This is why the scorecard insists on the documentary test, setting what was defended beside what the first audiences heard. Reception is evidence. The movement's nineteenth and early twentieth century conduct, its timetables for revolution, its splits over Bernstein, its popular catechisms with their confident sequences, establishes what the strong claims were and that they were believed in their strong forms by the people whose belief constituted the tradition. Against that record, the textbook presentation of the weak forms as the perennial meaning is not a pedagogical simplification. It is the pattern's fourth move performed on the tradition's own children, and it explains a phenomenon every teacher of this material has met: the student who has studied Marxism for years, in good faith and with good teachers, and has never once encountered the proposition that Bernstein refuted, because the proposition was withdrawn from circulation before his education began.

### Why does the pattern's pedagogical form matter more than its polemical form?

Because polemics end and syllabuses compound. A restatement performed in a controversy persuades the controversy's audience once. A restatement installed in the teaching literature manufactures, every year, a cohort for whom the original claim is inconceivable, and who will therefore grade every future critic as a man tilting at a misunderstanding.

The pattern's long half life is a curricular fact before it is an intellectual one, and breaking it requires the unglamorous work of restoring the strong claims to the syllabus alongside their fates.

The remedy the scorecard implies is worth stating, since diagnosis without remedy is this article's own version of the failing it tracks. Teach the claims in their strong forms, with their dates and their audiences, and teach the restatements as events in the theory's history, with their dates and their occasions. Immiseration absolute, then Bernstein, then the relative restatement, presented as a restatement. Polarisation national, then the century, then the global relocation, presented as a relocation. A student taught that way loses nothing of the tradition's genuine contents, since the weak forms remain available with their merits argued, and gains the one thing the pattern exists to deny him: the knowledge that the theory has a history of being wrong in specific places, which is the precondition of believing it where it is right. The traditions that teach their own refutations produce the scholars who write the strong replies. The traditions that teach only the restatements produce the custodians who write the folklore. The scorecard's sources divide along exactly that line, and the division is the article's most practical finding.

## The replies that work best

Three replies stand out from the scorecard's eleven rows, and the brief for this article identifies them in advance: the relational definition of class against the class-structure critique, the value-theoretic reply to the ecological objection, and the identification of the Leninist party form as the mechanism inside the authoritarianism charge. What unites them is worth extracting, because it amounts to a user's manual for telling a real defence from a performance of one. Each of the three was produced by a scholar working on the criticism as a problem in its own right, not as an attack to be repelled. Each concedes, in print, the part of the criticism that is correct. And each terminates in a proposition that can be checked by a reader who owes the tradition nothing. Replies with those three properties have a perfect record in the scorecard. Replies lacking them account for every relocation grade in the table.

The class reply rewards a second visit, because its strength is easy to understate. Wright's reconstruction did not merely rebut the embourgeoisement literature; it rebuilt class analysis as an empirical programme with instruments, surveys, and comparative designs, so that the relational claim could lose arguments in public and occasionally did. A reply that generates a research programme with its own internal critics, its own measurement quarrels, its own findings unwelcome to the tradition, has passed the severest test available to a defensive manoeuvre: it has stopped being defensive. The proletarianisation thesis inside it, Braverman's account of the degradation of clerical and technical labour, was stated strongly enough to be partly refuted, by evidence about skill's recomposition rather than its simple decline, and the programme absorbed the correction without relocating the claim, because the claim had been stated in a form that allowed correction to reach it. That is what the healthy version of the tradition looks like, and the scorecard's two unqualified grades both come from its neighbourhood.

The ecological reply's strength is of a different metal. Burkett and Foster did not reconstruct; they recovered. The metabolic rift analysis stands in the published corpus, in the discussion of large scale industry and agriculture in the first volume and in the third volume's treatment of ground rent, and its recovery required no redefinition of any term the objection attacked. The reply therefore enjoys a property no other strong reply in the table possesses: it could have been given, word for word, in the decade the objection was first framed, by a defender who had simply read the whole of the work he was defending. The lesson cuts against the tradition as much as it flatters the reply. For a century the custodians of the inheritance read Capital as a quarry for the strong claims, the laws and the timetables, and walked past the analyses, the rift among them, that would have answered the next century's questions. The replies that work best were mostly written by readers rather than by custodians, and the distinction explains more of the scorecard's distribution than any difference in talent.

The mechanism reply to the authoritarianism charge is the most consequential of the three, because its subject is the charge with bodies in it. Its strength is the strength of specificity. By naming the party form as the transmission mechanism, Liebman and the historians who followed him converted a metaphysical quarrel, does the doctrine contain tyranny in its essence, into an empirical one with cases on both sides: movements holding the doctrine without the form, forms operating under other doctrines, outcomes tracking the form's presence more closely than the doctrine's. The counter-examples then do real work, because a mechanism claim predicts variation and the variation is observed. What the reply cannot do, and its best authors have not claimed to do, is fill the doctrinal gap the charge's residue identifies. Bounding a charge is not answering it, and the scorecard's partly works grade for this row is the table's most important single verdict, because it marks the exact frontier where the tradition's best defensive work stops.

### What separates the strongest replies from the weakest ones?

Three properties, all visible from outside the tradition. The strong replies were written by scholars treating the criticism as a problem rather than as an attack. They concede, explicitly and in print, whatever the criticism gets right. And they end in propositions a neutral reader can check, propositions stated in a form that lets evidence reach them.

The weak replies invert all three properties: they defend a flag, concede nothing, and conclude in restatements no evidence could disturb.

There is a corollary for readers rather than for partisans. The strength of these three replies is evidence about the framework's surviving contents, and the evidence is specific. A relational theory of class that generates empirical research, a value theory whose analysis of capital's metabolism answers objections its author never heard, a historiography capable of isolating the mechanism of its own worst outcome: these are not the assets of an emptied programme. They are the assets of a programme whose centre of gravity has moved from prophecy to analysis, which is a transformation the tradition's custodians resisted and its scholars performed. The strongest objection, confronted below, argues that the narrowing emptied the framework. The strongest replies are the standing evidence that something else happened alongside the narrowing: a distillation, in which the parts of the inheritance that could survive cross-examination did, and the parts that could not were carried for decades by the pattern this article has named.

## The geography of candour: exile, translation, and the reply industry abroad

The defensive literature's map is as instructive as its chronology. The replies that survive grading cluster in improbable places: in the journals of exile communities, in the seminars of countries where the tradition was a minority taste, in translations produced by scholars working between languages the custodians did not read. Rubin's value theory was written in Moscow and suppressed there, surviving through a German edition and a delayed English one. The Praxis school's reconstructions were produced in Yugoslavia, in the one socialist state whose heresies had a university to live in, until the heresies were closed down there too. The value-form reading was a West German seminar product, carried into English by translators a generation later. Liebman wrote in French, from Geneva. The pattern in the geography is the institutional law stated earlier, drawn on a map: candour flourished at the exact distance from custodianship at which a writer could still command the tradition's texts and no longer needed its permission.

Translation played a role in the defence's quality that intellectual histories understate. A reply available only in Russian or German or French was, for the Anglophone movement, a reply that did not exist, and the movement's English speaking custodians retailed the folklore in the assurance that no better existed in any language they were asked about. The great translation projects of the postwar decades, which carried Rubin, the Grundrisse, the early Lukács, and eventually the notebooks into English, therefore functioned as defensive reinforcements arriving decades late: each one armed the scholars against the folklore and embarrassed the catechisms that had filled the silence. The scorecard's grades are, in part, a report on that logistics. Rows whose key replies crossed languages early, the calculation debate above all, were argued at a high level in public. Rows whose key replies waited forty years for a translator were argued, in the interval, by the catechism, and the interval is where the pattern did its deepest work.

### Why did the tradition's best defensive work so often require exile or a foreign language?

Because the defence's quality is set by the price of concession where the writing occurs. At the custodial centres concession was treason and the replies are ceremonies. At the margins, in exile journals and foreign seminars, concession cost a writer an argument with his peers and bought him their respect, and the replies are scholarship.

The geography is not a mystery of national character. It is the institutional law with place names attached, and it predicts, correctly, where the next strong reply will come from if one comes.

## Silent absorptions: the criticisms that were simply accepted

Between the answered criticisms and the relocated ones lies a third category the scorecard's table cannot show, because it consists of claims that vanished. The tradition once held, and no longer holds, a series of positions that criticism killed without ceremony: the reduction of skilled to simple labour as an operation anyone could perform, the expectation that the peasantry would dissolve on schedule, the Asiatic mode of production as a category of the official schema, from which it was struck in the nineteen thirties for reasons of state rather than of scholarship, and the imminent withering of the state as a prospect its own statesmen were expecting. None of these positions received a famous reply. None was formally withdrawn. They were absorbed, silently, by a literature that simply stopped asserting them, and their absence from the scorecard is a gap in its method that honesty requires be registered.

Silent absorption is, in one light, the healthiest response a tradition can make to a fatal criticism: the claim dies, the literature moves on, no face is saved and no energy is wasted on the funeral. In another light it is the pattern's invisible twin, since a tradition that drops its dead claims without record teaches its students a history in which the claims were never held, and the student's resulting confidence in the surviving claims is built on a censored sample. The scorecard cannot grade absorptions, having no replies to grade, but it can perform the record keeping the tradition omitted, which this section does in miniature. A complete register of the absorbed claims would be a book, and a melancholy one, because its entries share a property the scorecard's rows mostly lack: they were, many of them, the claims in whose name the movement's members organised their lives. The tradition owes its dead claims a register for the same reason it owes its critics the concessions. The people who believed the claims are the creditors, and they are mostly beyond collecting.

### Why does the scorecard register claims that received no reply?

Because their absence would otherwise flatter the distribution. A table of replies alone suggests that every criticism met some response on a spectrum from answer to relocation. The absorbed claims met none, and their fate, silent deletion, is the response the custodial structure prefers where it is available, since it costs neither the concession of an answer's defeat nor the visibility of a relocation. Registering them keeps the article's account of the defence's repertoire complete: answer, relocation, and the memory hole, in ascending order of the custodians' preference.

## The weakest replies and their shared structure

Against the three strong replies stand the defensive performances the scorecard grades lowest, and their shared structure is more instructive than any of their contents. The weakest stratum of the literature consists of replies that exist without owners: formulations repeated across textbooks and pamphlets, attributed to the tradition in general, traceable to no work a grader can open. The attribution rule excluded them from the scorecard, and the exclusion itself is a finding. A substantial fraction of what adherents experience as the tradition's answers to criticism is a folklore of answers, whose function is to let the adherent feel the criticism has been met without the inconvenience of meeting it. Folklore replies share three marks. They are always shorter than the criticism. They are always more confident than the scholarship behind them. And they always describe the criticism in the form most convenient to the reply, so that the exchange, if it can be called one, takes place between a caricature and a slogan.

The second weak stratum does have owners, and its structure is the relocation examined throughout: the reply that redescribes until the criticism misses. Its purest specimens cluster around the predictions, where the tendency vocabulary performs the redescription, and around the opening chapter, where the social-category reading, honourable in Hilferding, degenerates in lesser hands into a spell for making Böhm-Bawerk vanish without the labour of reading him. The third weak stratum is the appeal to implementation: the theory is distinguished from every state that enacted it, correctly, and then the distinction is used not as the beginning of an analysis, of why the enactments converged, but as its replacement. A distinction that explains nothing about the pattern of outcomes is a wall, not an argument, and the authoritarianism row shows what the same distinction becomes in serious hands: the start of the mechanism question, not a substitute for asking it.

### Why do the weakest replies share a single structure?

Because they are produced by the same institutional pressure the pattern section identified: the defence of a charter by custodians who cannot afford corrections. A reply written to protect an inheritance must be short enough to memorise, confident enough to reassure, and addressed to a version of the criticism the faithful have already met in caricature.

Scholarship written to solve a problem has the opposite properties. The structure of the weakest replies is therefore not a failure of intelligence but a signature of their function.

## What remains unanswered

The unanswered five were stated at the scorecard and are collected here with their doors closed one by one, so that a reader finishing the article holds the tradition's true balance sheet. Hayek's objection stands because the knowledge it concerns is generated in the act of exchange itself and has never been shown to exist in collectible form; the computational rejoinders answer the arithmetic objection, which died in 1937, with ever greater speed. The incentive charge, in its innovation form, stands because no institutional design has yet combined social ownership with the permission to stake, fail, and capture at the scale innovation requires, across the decades the demonstration would need. The missing theory of constraint on power stands because it is an absence, and absences are answered by supply or not at all; a century's production contains brilliant analyses of power's class basis and no architecture for binding a revolutionary government, no rights that hold against it, no separated institutions with the standing to refuse it.

The abundance premise stands because the published destination presupposes the retirement of scarcity, and the single reply in the record answers from notebooks the tradition never possessed. The inequality argument stands because the derivation it attacks has been abandoned by the defence rather than repaired: the social-category reading concedes the inference's failure in the act of explaining why the inference was never the point. Five doors, three kinds of lock. Two would open to arguments, and the arguments have had a century. Two would open to constructions or evidence, and neither exists. One would open to an act of theoretical creation, a Marxist constitutionalism, that the tradition's suspicion of formal rights has so far made psychologically impossible for its own thinkers to attempt.

### Which unanswered item is the oldest, and what does its age show?

The inequality argument is the oldest, pressed in 1896 against a derivation published in 1867, and its age shows that longevity in this list is not a function of a criticism's fashion. The argument survives because the defence chose, early and permanently, to change the subject to social form rather than to repair the inference, and a subject once changed is seldom changed back. Unanswered items do not fade with time. They harden into the landscape, and later generations cease to see them as questions at all.

This article's figures protocol applies to the balance sheet as to everything else in it. Several of the disputes behind the five are quantified in their home literatures, and none of those quantities is reproduced here, because each belongs with the methods that produced it. The reader is routed, as the cluster's rule requires, to the articles holding the evidence: the calculation debate for the knowledge objection's documentary record, the incentive article for the cooperative and comparative data, the authoritarianism article for the record of the party form, the science article for the programme's predictive inventory. A verdict about evidence is given here. The evidence itself lives where it can be cross-examined.

## The frontier since the archive opened

The scorecard's grades are indexed to the literature's present state, and the present state has a dateable peculiarity: the evidence base shifted under several rows within living memory, and the grades moved with it. The opening of the Soviet archives after 1991 did not settle the authoritarianism row, but it changed the row's evidentiary manners, replacing the duel of estimates with documentary sequences in which the mechanism's operation, the substitution of party for class and centre for party, can be traced in the custodians' own paperwork. The mechanism reply gained what mechanisms need, a record of the transmission working, and the circumstantial defence lost its favourite resource, the fog in which every outcome could be assigned to encirclement. In the value rows, the historical critical edition's progress through Marx's manuscripts has sharpened the textual questions both sides argue from, showing drafts in which positions the canon states flatly were tried, qualified, and sometimes abandoned in the workshop, a record that assists the exegetical replies in some passages and the critics in others, impartially, as evidence does.

The frontier's movement carries a lesson for the scorecard's own shelf life, and the article states it against itself. Grades indexed to a frontier expire as the frontier moves. The transformation row's grade presupposes the reconstruction literature's present state; a new construction satisfying the row's defeater would reverse it tomorrow, and the defeater is printed precisely so that the reversal can be recognised when it happens rather than argued about after. The abundance row's grade presupposes the notebook scholarship's present reception. The class row's grade presupposes the comparative programme's findings as they stand, revisions included. A reader consulting this article in a later decade should therefore treat its verdicts as dated instruments, recalibrate them against the frontier sections, and distrust any summary of them, including the tradition's and the enemies', that presents them as timeless. The demand for dated, defeasible, frontier indexed judgement is the article's whole method, and it applies to the article first.

### Why are this scorecard's verdicts offered with an expiry condition?

Because the alternative is the catechism's method, in which a verdict, once delivered by an authorised voice, circulates forever at its original valuation. The replies graded here suffered exactly that treatment at their custodians' hands, their decade's frontier frozen into a permanent triumph.

A grader who has documented the damage of frozen verdicts cannot issue his own in the same form, and the expiry condition is the form's correction: every grade in the table is a claim about a literature at a date, staked against the literature's future.

## The replies to the replies: how the critics answered back

A scorecard that graded replies without noting that the critics replied in turn would freeze a moving argument at the moment most flattering to the defence. The record, taken whole, is a dialectic in the unglamorous sense: claim, criticism, reply, counter reply, with the frontier moving at each exchange and the scorecard's verdicts describing the frontier's present position rather than a final settlement. Steedman answered the value form defence by granting the social form its interest and denying its relevance to the magnitudes, a counter that forced the defence onto the aggregate surplus ground where the scorecard found it. The Austrian school's later writers answered Lange's construction by absorbing it, granting the formal solution and relocating their own objection to knowledge and incentives, a relocation on the critics' side that this article has graded by the same standard it applies to the defence: Hayek's move was announced, argued, and staked, and it therefore counts as the objection's refinement rather than its evasion, the mirror image of the standard the defence so often failed.

Popper's heirs answered Lakatos by disputing his application while accepting his framework, which is why the falsifiability row's verdict is stated in the framework's own terms and cannot be appealed to a friendlier court. Kornai answered the reform economists from inside their own camp, conceding their sincerity and doubting their premise that a state owner can bind itself. In each case the counter reply raised the standard the next defence must meet, and the scorecard's verdicts are indexed to the frontier as it stands after the counters, not as it stood when the famous replies were new. This indexing is the difference between a scorecard and a commemoration. Several replies graded partly works here were, in their decade, the strongest available move, and their authors are not diminished by the frontier's advance. But a defence that continues to cite them at their original valuation, as though the counters had not been filed, is trading on a stale price, and the reader is entitled to the current one.

### How should a reader date a reply's grade?

To the frontier, not to the reply. A defence is worth what it can still do against the criticism in its present form, and the criticism's present form includes every counter the reply provoked. Grades therefore decay, and the scorecard's are offered with that decay assumed: a verdict of works asserts that the reply still functions against the strongest current statement of its criticism, which is a claim about the literature's present state and is falsifiable against it, as every claim in this article is designed to be.

## The five doors revisited: what each would cost to open

Before the objection is faced, the unanswered five deserve a second pass at the level of construction: not why they stand, which the earlier section established, but what an answer would have to look like, priced honestly. The exercise matters because unanswered is a verdict about the record, not about possibility, and a grader who cannot describe the missing answer has not understood the question. Take the missing constraint first, since it is the most nearly constructible. The tradition's own archive contains the embryo of an answer in the council tradition, in Pannekoek's workers' councils and in the Commune model Marx praised: power dispersed to revocable delegates, administration rotated, the standing army and the separate bureaucracy abolished together. What the embryo never grew is the part the liberal tradition, for all its hypocrisies, actually built: rights that bind the revolutionary power itself, courts with standing against it, an opposition whose suppression the theory itself names as crime rather than as necessity. A Marxist constitutionalism that supplied those parts, argued from the tradition's own premises about power's material roots, would answer the residue. Its absence is not an oversight of detail. It follows from the theory's deepest suspicion, that formal rights are the mask of material domination, a suspicion true enough about the rights' abuse to have blinded the tradition to their use. The door is openable. The key is a concession the tradition has found psychologically indigestible for a hundred and fifty years, and the indigestibility, not any logical barrier, is what the unanswered verdict records.

The knowledge objection's missing answer is the hardest to price, because describing it reveals its shape as a challenge in epistemology rather than in economics. An answer would have to exhibit a procedure that generates, continuously and at national scale, the knowledge that exchange generates as a by-product of actors pursuing their own ends with their own resources at stake. Every candidate procedure founders on the same rock: elicitation changes the elicited, since actors reporting to a centre report strategically, while actors bidding in a market reveal by paying. The one development that might alter the grading is not computational but institutional, a planning form in which the reporting units are themselves the residual claimants of their reports' accuracy, and the scorecard's defeater for the calculation row is written to admit exactly that construction if it is ever operated. Until then the objection stands, and its standing is the single most consequential fact in this article for anyone whose interest in the tradition is practical rather than antiquarian.

The innovation door and the abundance door are priced in the world rather than in argument. An institutional design that socialises ownership while preserving the ecology of risk, failure, and capture within which frontier innovation has always occurred would answer the first, and the design problem is genuine engineering, not ideology: the components are known, their combination under social ownership is the unsolved part. The abundance door would be opened by a demonstration, from the published corpus, that the destination was specified for sufficiency rather than for scarcity's abolition, and the demonstration's difficulty is that the canonical texts really do say what Benton said they say. The inequality door, the oldest, would be opened by a derivation, and derivations are the cheapest answers to produce and the longest awaited, which is its own commentary on whether the derivation exists.

### What should a student separate between a reply that answers and the specification of a missing one?

Between the record and the possibility. The scorecard grades the record: what replies exist, signed and checkable, and what they achieve. The specification of a missing answer grades nothing; it describes the shape a future reply would need, so that the reader can recognise one if it arrives and can refuse the counterfeits, which arrive regularly, announced as breakthroughs and constructed, on inspection, as relocations wearing new vocabulary. Confusing the specification with an answer is the final form of the pattern: the claim that the door could be opened, retailed as though it had been.

## The strongest objection: a tradition in retreat

Every grading this article has performed can now be turned against the enterprise itself, and the objection that does so must be stated at the strength its best proponents gave it. Leszek Kołakowski, who knew the tradition from inside and wrote its most formidable critical history in Main Currents of Marxism, delivered the verdict toward which his three volumes march: a doctrine that began by promising a science of society's transformation ended by protecting itself against every finding the transformation produced. The objection, in the form this article's brief requires it to be faced, runs as follows. A tradition that requires a permanent industry of replies to decisive arguments is a tradition in retreat. The scorecard itself is the evidence, compiled by a sympathetic grader: of eleven rows, two replies work, six partly work, three relocate the claim, and five critiques stand unanswered after a century of motivated effort. Read without loyalty, that distribution is not a defence with blemishes. It is the documented contraction of a framework, narrowing claim by claim under fire, until what survives is a set of propositions no longer distinctive enough to be worth the name they are defended under. The honest conclusion from a scorecard of this kind is that the framework has been progressively emptied, and that the replies industry exists to prevent the emptying from being announced.

The objection is the strongest in the article because it concedes everything the article concedes and draws the conclusion the article has so far declined to draw. It does not depend on any single criticism surviving. It depends on the pattern of survivals, and it prices the reformulation pattern not as a presentational failing but as the framework's biography: a long retreat conducted in good prose. A defender who answers the eleven rows one by one and never faces the distribution has answered nothing that matters, because the objection is the distribution.

The answer begins by separating the framework's components, since the objection's force depends on treating the narrowing as uniform. It was not uniform. Three families of claims must be distinguished. The prophetic family, the timetables and terminal expectations, immiseration in its absolute form, polarisation as a national simplification, breakdown as a scheduled event, revolution arriving first where industry was ripest: this family narrowed to nothing, and the scorecard's relocation grades are its death certificates, honestly issued. The analytical family behaved differently. The relational theory of class did not narrow; it ramified, into Wright's comparative programme and a literature that argues with data. The analysis of capital's metabolism did not narrow; it was recovered at full strength a century late. The critique of political economy's categories, the social form analysis that Rubin restored, did not narrow; it deepened, and its descendants work in departments that have forgotten the pamphlet wars entirely. The programmatic family, the theory of what is to be built, narrowed worst of all and in a different direction: not into weaker versions of its claims but into silence at exactly the load-bearing points, the epistemology of planning and the constitution of power.

### Which components of the framework narrowed, and which did not?

The prophetic components narrowed to extinction: the absolute immiseration, the two-camp simplification, the breakdown schedule, the expected geography of revolution. The programmatic components narrowed into silence at the points where the record pressed hardest, planning's knowledge problem and power's constraint. The analytical components did not narrow.

Relational class analysis ramified into an empirical programme, the metabolism analysis was recovered intact, and the critique of social form deepened. The framework's centre of gravity shifted from prophecy toward analysis, a change that is a transformation and not only a loss.

The concession the objection is owed must then be made without varnish, because this article's credibility is its only asset. The narrowing is real. It is not a hostile invention, it is not a misreading of the scorecard, and it is not answered by pointing at the analytical family's health, because the framework as its founders held it was the union of the three families: the analysis existed to underwrite the prophecy and the programme, and a Marxism reduced to its analytical family is a different and smaller thing than the doctrine whose replies this article has graded. Kołakowski's conclusion, that the remainder is too indistinct to be worth the name, is wrong in its final step and right in everything before it. It is wrong because distinctiveness is not measured by the survival of the whole: a relational account of class, a theory of capital's metabolic relation to nature, and a method for reading social forms remain distinctive possessions, held by no rival framework in the same combination, and the proof is that rival frameworks keep importing them. It is right that the import trade is the tell. The components survive increasingly as contributions to other people's programmes, in sociology, in ecology, in the historiography of capitalism, detached from the union that made them a doctrine. Whether a framework whose parts flourish in exile while its whole contracts has been emptied or has been inherited is a question this article leaves genuinely open, because the evidence supports both descriptions and the choice between them is a judgement, not a finding. What is not open is the burden the objection fixes. After a century of narrowing, the burden of statement lies on the tradition: to say what its remaining distinctive claims are, in their strong forms, with their defeaters attached. A tradition that cannot produce that statement has conceded the objection in the only way that matters, by silence.

## The testimony of the friends: the tradition's internal auditors

The objection gains its final weight from a source no defender can discount: the testimony of the tradition's own most gifted adherents, delivered at the ends of careers spent inside the defence. G. A. Cohen, whose Karl Marx's Theory of History of 1978 was the most rigorous reconstruction the theory of history ever received, spent his later work conceding, piece by piece, the ground his masterpiece had held: the functional explanations required mechanisms he could not supply, the productive forces' primacy survived as a tendency he could no longer state strongly, and his intellectual energy migrated to egalitarian ethics, where the questions that had defeated the theory of history could be asked in a form that admitted of answers. Cohen never renounced the tradition and never performed the pattern. His trajectory is the objection's best evidence precisely because it is honourable: if the framework's ablest living defender concluded, by continuing to work, that its centre could not be held in its classical form, the conclusion deserves more weight than any enemy's pamphlet.

Jon Elster's Making Sense of Marx performed the audit from a position of declared sympathy in 1985 and reached the distribution this scorecard reaches, by an independent route: the dialectical method discharged as obscurantist, the value theory discharged in its classical form, the theory of history retained in fragments under mechanism discipline, the analysis of exploitation and ideology retained at strength. The September Group of analytical Marxists, Roemer and Wright among them, institutionalised the audit as a method, accepting microfoundations, rational choice instruments, and the obligation to state claims in forms that could fail. What the analytical school produced is, by the scorecard's lights, the tradition's most successful defensive operation of the late century, and it succeeded by the exact inversion of the pattern: it surrendered territory in public, itemised the surrenders, and rebuilt the remainder to a standard its critics were invited to test. Roemer's reconstruction of exploitation without the labour theory of value is the type specimen: a reply that begins by conceding the redundancy argument's substance and then rebuilds the moral core of the theory on property relations, where it stands more securely than it ever stood on embodied labour.

### How does the pattern grade the defensive literature taken as a whole?

It grades them as a distribution rather than as a verdict on any single argument: a minority of genuine answers, concentrated where scholars worked outside custodianship, a large centre of partial defences whose boundaries the scorecard maps, and a systematic drift, wherever the charter's prestige was at stake, toward restatement presented as explication.

The pattern's grade for the whole is therefore not failure. It is the finding that the defence's quality is predictable from its institutional setting, which is a finding about the tradition's organisation of knowledge before it is a finding about its doctrines.

The internal auditors matter to the objection's answer as much as to the objection, and the answer section that follows leans on them for its central distinction. If the framework had simply been emptied, its best minds would have exited into silence or into other doctrines entire, as the ex Marxists of an earlier generation exited into liberalism and conservatism. The analytical school's trajectory is different: its members stayed with the problems, class, exploitation, ideology, the dynamics of capitalism, while abandoning the charter's letter claim by claim. A framework whose problems remain compulsory for its critics' grandchildren, long after its prophecies have lapsed, has not been emptied in the manner of a spent fad. It has undergone the fate of every great research tradition that overclaimed: reduction to the parts that work, under new management, with the name retained by courtesy and by the parts' family resemblance. Whether that fate is survival or death is the judgement the objection section now takes up, and it takes it up knowing that the tradition's own auditors have already voted, with their careers, for the description that keeps the problems and drops the charter.

## The frontier in motion: platform work, logistics planning, and the newest replies

A frontier indexed scorecard owes its reader a closing survey of where the frontier is actually moving, and three movements deserve record. The class reply is being extended, by a generation of labour scholars, to platform work, where the relational map faces its most interesting test since the affluent worker: workers classified as independent contractors, managed by algorithm, owning in some cases the instruments of their labour, a car, a phone, while the platform owns the market access without which the instruments are inert. The relational reply's descendants argue that the case is the contradictory location in digital dress, the worker's formal independence masking a dependence more complete than the factory's, since the platform can dismiss by disconnection and discipline by rating. The argument is being conducted with the programme's characteristic manners, empirical, concessive, and refereed, and its outcome will move the class row's grade in one direction or the other within the decade, which is exactly how a living row should behave.

The calculation row is moving too, from an unexpected direction. The logistics literature, popularised by Leigh Phillips and Michal Rozworski's The People's Republic of Walmart in 2019, revived the argument from the large firm: the great retail and logistics corporations operate internal planning systems at the scale of national economies, allocating without internal markets, and their existence is said to refute the calculation objection's practical form. The scorecard's assessment of the revival follows the row's established anatomy. The firms plan means toward ends given from outside, prices and profit targets supplied by the surrounding market, and their internal knowledge problem is solved by a hierarchy whose errors are priced by the firm's own possible death in competition. The revival therefore answers, once again, the arithmetic and scale questions, and leaves the epistemic question, who sets the ends and with what knowledge of wants, exactly where Hayek left it. It is nonetheless the liveliest development in the row for thirty years, because it has forced the objection's defenders to state their claim's boundaries precisely, and a criticism whose boundaries are being sharpened by its opponents is a criticism in good health.

The third movement concerns the pattern itself. The newest generation of the tradition's scholars works under conditions its predecessors never enjoyed: no charter to protect, no state to flatter, no movement whose cohesion prices concession out of reach, and a scholarly commons in which the tradition's claims compete at the same referees' desks as every rival's. The early returns, in the social reproduction literature, in the class programme's digital extension, in the archival historiography, display the manners this article has rewarded throughout, because the price structure finally rewards them. If the pattern was an institutional product, its conditions have been abolished, and the scorecard's final prediction is offered in the falsifiable form the article has demanded of everyone else: the replies produced under the new conditions will grade, on average, a full step above the replies produced under the old, and a future scorecard that finds otherwise will have refuted this article's mechanism along with its optimism.

### Where is the defence's frontier most likely to move first?

In the class row, because its programme is alive, instrumented, and pointed at a labour market whose forms are changing faster than its categories. The platform extension will either confirm the relational map's reach, by showing dependence beneath formal independence, or force the map's next published correction.

Either outcome is the programme working as programmes should, and either will be recorded by the scholars themselves, which is the deepest change the new conditions have made.

## What the scorecard means for the critiques themselves

A grading of replies is also, read in the mirror, a grading of the criticisms, and the mirror shows several famous critiques in a worse light than their reputations suggest. A criticism that met only folklore in reply may have been unanswerable, or it may have been so badly stated that the scholars declined the invitation and the catechism answered in their place. The vulgar version of the immiseration critique, which read the claim as a prediction of falling money wages and refuted it with wage series, was of this kind: it struck the catechism's claim, not the corpus's subtler ones, and the tradition's failure to say so cleanly at the time, preferring the tendency conversion to the exegetical correction, cost it the exegetical high ground it actually held. Conversely, a criticism that provoked a generation of first rate reconstruction, as Bortkiewicz's did, certifies itself by the company it forced: the transformation problem is a great criticism because the replies it necessitated rebuilt a field. The scorecard's rows therefore carry a double information, and the reader who wants the critiques' own grades will find the companion survey's assessments converging with the mirror's testimony in most rows and diverging instructively in a few.

The divergence cases deserve a sentence each. The falsifiability charge grades higher as a criticism than its row's reply might suggest, because Popper's behavioural observation, whatever its methodological frame, identified the pattern before the pattern had a name, and the tradition's inability to hear a true observation from an enemy's mouth is part of the record the objection section prices. The calculation objection grades lower in its famous form than in its mature one, and critics who continue to cite Mises's 1920 formulation as the objection are committing the mirror offence of the defenders who cite Lange: both are trading on the version of the argument their side won. Symmetry, again, is the scorecard's whole ethics, and it is offered to the critics' camp as freely as to the defence's, in the expectation of being refused by both and used by the readers who matter.

### Which criticisms were improved by the replies they provoked?

The ones whose authors lived to answer back. Hayek's objection deepened under Lange's pressure from an impossibility claim into an epistemology. The class critique sharpened under Wright's, shedding its income form for a contest about relations and authority. The ecological critique, in Benton's own later work, absorbed the rift recovery and re-aimed at the destination.

A criticism that forces its author to improve it has been answered in the only way that advances knowledge, whatever the scorecard's row grade says, and the frontier sections of this article record the improvements as carefully as the verdicts.

## Reading the replies: a short shelf with warnings

The article closes its argument with the shelf a reader should actually work through, since a scorecard that cannot be checked against its sources is a catechism with better manners. Begin with the founding pair: Böhm-Bawerk's great critique of 1896 and Hilferding's reply, read together, in that order, so that the criticism's strength is felt before the defence's quality is judged. Continue with the debate's own documents rather than its summaries: Lange's essay on the economic theory of socialism beside Hayek's essay of 1945 on knowledge in society, and Dobb's reservations beside both, so that the calculation row's composite reply can be assembled by the reader's own hand. For the predictions, Bernstein's Preconditions must be read whole and not in the extracts its enemies circulate; its statistics are dated and its honesty is not, and Luxemburg's Reform or Revolution should follow it immediately, as the strongest form of the answer Bernstein provoked.

The modern shelf is shorter. Wright's Classes for the relational programme in its mature form, with the affluent worker studies' findings available in the secondary literature the programme itself cites. Burkett's Marx and Nature for the ecological reply at full strength, read against Benton's essay rather than against the tradition's summary of it. Liebman's Leninism Under Lenin for the mechanism in its documentary depth, and Miliband's Marxism and Politics for the reconstruction it enabled. Kołakowski's Main Currents, finally, for the objection at the length it deserves, read with the scorecard open beside it, since the two documents agree on most of the evidence and differ, instructively, on what the evidence sums to. Two warnings govern the whole shelf. Read no reply in an edition prepared by the tradition's custodians without checking the translation against the controversy's record, for reasons the geography section gave. And read every summary, this article emphatically included, as a claim about the shelf rather than as a substitute for it: the scorecard's grades are wagers on what the reader will find there, and the wagers are meant to be collected.

### Which single exchange teaches the most about how the replies work?

The Lange and Hayek exchange, because it contains the whole repertoire in forty pages: a formal answer that genuinely answers, an objection that deepens under the pressure into a stronger form, a reservation from the answerer's own camp that history vindicated, and a subsequent literature that froze the exchange at the moment of its own side's victory.

A reader who understands why the scorecard grades that exchange as it does understands the grading of every other row, and can proceed to the shelf equipped.

One further item belongs on the shelf's edge, as a reminder of the standard's antiquity. The founding generation's exchanges were conducted in journals that printed criticisms and replies in adjacent issues, often in adjacent columns, on the assumption that readers would hold both texts at once. The scorecard's method is nothing more than that assumption restored to a literature that spent decades arranging for its readers to hold only one text at a time, the reply without the criticism, the restatement without the original. Every instrument this article has used, the named author, the printed concession, the defeater in the same row, the comparison of the claim attacked with the claim surviving, is a device for putting the two texts back on the same desk. The tradition's best defenders never needed the restoration, because they wrote with the criticism open beside them. The scorecard exists for the rest of the shelf, and for the rest of us.

## Grading the graders: this scorecard's own exposure

An article that has spent its length demanding defeaters owes the reader its own, collected in one place. The scorecard's verdicts fall, row by row, to the defeaters printed in the table, and the reader who satisfies one has reversed that row. The summary verdict of five standing unanswered falls to a single qualifying reply to any of the five, as the article has twice recorded. The pattern diagnosis falls to the documentary sample described in the method section, should the sample show corrections announced at the ordinary rate. The objection section's judgement, that the framework's analytical family survives its prophetic family's collapse, falls to a demonstration that the analytical survivals are not distinctive, that rival frameworks hold equivalents of the relational class analysis, the metabolic account, and the social form critique in the same combination, a demonstration no critic has yet attempted in full and several have attempted in part.

One exposure remains that no defeater can retire, and it is registered here because the article's standard requires the registration of unanswerable weaknesses as well as answerable ones. Every grade in the scorecard rests on an antecedent judgement about what the original claims were, and that decision, however documentary its basis, is a judgement on which competent scholars differ. The transformation row's grade depends on what the third volume claims. The predictions row's grade depends on how the movement's expectations are weighed against the texts' hedges. A grader who fixed the original claims more generously would raise several verdicts, and a grader who fixed them by the catechisms alone would lower others. The scorecard has stated its rule of custody, that a claim counts as the tradition's when its authorised teaching carried it, and has applied the rule uniformly, to the defence's cost in some rows and to its benefit in others. Uniformity is the most a grader can offer. The reader who applies a different rule will reach a different table, and the article's final claim is only that the argument about which rule is right is the argument worth having, since it is the argument the tradition's custodians spent a century preventing.

### How do Marxist responses to criticism look from the grader's chair at the end?

Better than their reputation among enemies and worse than their reputation among friends, which is where an honest grading of a century's work usually lands. The defence contains genuine triumphs of scholarship that hostile summary erases, and a systematic evasion at its centre that friendly summary conceals. Both summaries are available in the bookshops.

This article exists because the truth is a distribution, and distributions cannot be cheered or booed, only read.

## What a successful reply would require

The scorecard's discipline can be inverted into a specification, and the specification is the article's constructive residue. A successful reply to any of the standing critiques would have to satisfy five conditions, each abstracted from the failures catalogued above. It would meet the criticism on the ground the critic chose, not on adjacent ground where the footing is friendlier. It would defend the proposition actually attacked, at something near its original strength, rather than a descendant proposition wearing the family name. It would concede, in the reply itself, whatever the criticism gets right, since a reply that concedes nothing has announced its genre. It would terminate in a checkable proposition, one a neutral reader could in principle find false. And it would state its own defeater, the evidence that would reverse it, in the same document, so that the reply enters the record as a stake rather than as a performance.

No reply in the scorecard satisfies all five, and the strongest, the class and ecological replies, satisfy four, which is why they grade as they do. The specification's use is prospective. The tradition continues to produce replies, in journals and in the analytical schools that are its healthiest descendants, and a reader equipped with the five conditions can grade new arrivals in an afternoon without waiting for a scorecard. The conditions are also portable beyond this doctrine, which is the comparison discipline's final dividend: they state what any chartered theory owes its critics, and the traditions examined in the comparison section would grade no better against them than this one does. The difference the scorecard records is not that Marxism has defenders who fail these tests. It is that Marxism's defenders were for so long forbidden, by the custodianship structure, from being seen to pass the third condition, the concession, which is the cheapest of the five and the one whose absence poisons the rest.

### What burden does the scorecard place on the tradition's defenders?

The burden of statement. After a century in which the framework's strong claims were narrowed, relocated, or pensioned, the defender who wishes the remaining doctrine to be taken seriously must state its surviving distinctive claims in strong form, name the evidence each rests on, and attach to each the condition that would reverse it.

Defenders who continue to cite the replies industry as proof of vitality, without producing that statement, are asking for credit the scorecard does not issue.

One misreading remains to be corrected before the personas, because it inverts the article's whole argument. The scorecard is not a verdict that the framework is finished, and it is not a certificate of the framework's vindication. It is a finding that the defence of the framework, as an activity, has been carried on at two levels so unlike that they amount to different activities: a scholarly one, at which real problems were solved and real concessions made, and a custodial one, at which the pattern did its work. Readers who take the article as ammunition for either loyalty have misread it in the way the article most carefully warned against. They have done the single thing the opening section ruled out, which is to judge the defence in bulk.

## Why the replies matter beyond the tradition

The article's subject is one tradition's defensive literature, but its findings are addressed to anyone who reads intellectual history, because the configuration that produced them is general. A doctrine of unusual comprehensiveness, claiming to explain the economy, the past, the state, and what comes next in one frame, was adopted as the charter of organisations whose survival depended on the charter's prestige. The configuration guaranteed a century of defensive writing at two levels, and the levels' products are distinguishable in any archive by the tests this article has used. Readers of other traditions, of churches, of schools of economics, of therapeutic and political movements with founding texts and authorised interpreters, will recognise the configuration and can apply the tests without modification: fix the claim as its first audiences received it, trace its form after the disconfirming evidence, and read the transitions for announcements or for denials.

The transferable finding is not that defenders cheat. It is that the price structure of the defending institution predicts the defence's manners more reliably than the doctrine's content predicts anything, and that the exceptions, the Hilferdings and Cohens and Liebmans, are explained by their distance from the structure rather than by their exemption from human nature. A reader who absorbs that finding reads all defensive literature differently afterward, including the literatures of positions he holds. That is the article's ambition for its own side effects, and it is why the scorecard was built as an instrument rather than as a verdict sheet: verdicts expire with their frontier, as the frontier section confessed, but an instrument goes on grading new arrivals, in this tradition and in every other that keeps a charter, long after the particular verdicts of 2019 have been superseded by the literature they were staked against.

### What is the single habit this article most wants its readers to acquire?

The habit of setting what was defended beside what was attacked before evaluating the argument between them. Most defensive literature, in every tradition, depends for its effect on the reader's failure to make that comparison, since the argument connecting a strong original to a weak survivor can be made to look like a refutation of the survivor's critics only while the two claims are blurred. Fix both claims in writing, with their dates and their audiences, and the blur becomes impossible. The rest of grading is commentary on that single act of attention.

## A row restored: functional explanation and the demand for mechanisms

One criticism graded in the companion survey was omitted from this article's table by the brief's design, and its reply file is too instructive to leave unrecorded. The criticism, pressed by Jon Elster from 1985, targeted the theory of history's explanatory form. Historical materialism, in G. A. Cohen's rigorous reconstruction of 1978, explains institutions and ideologies by their function for the growth of the productive forces or the stability of the relations of production: the superstructure is as it is because of what it does for the base. Elster's objection was that functional explanation without a mechanism is a promissory note drawn on an empty account. In biology the note is redeemed by natural selection, which supplies the feedback loop that makes consequence explanations respectable. In social theory, Cohen's explanations named no loop, and until one is supplied, the claim that a legal system exists because it stabilises production relations is a pattern in search of a process.

Cohen's reply is the episode's glory, and it belongs in this article because it demonstrates the anti pattern in its purest available form. He did not restate the claim. He conceded the demand's legitimacy, distinguished the kinds of elaboration a functional explanation requires, supplied the forms the feedback might take, through the deliberate action of ruling classes, through selection among competing institutional variants, through the unintended stabilisation that rewards the arrangements a system stumbles into, and he marked, in print, which parts of his original construction the concession had weakened. Elster, for his part, conceded the legitimacy of consequence explanation where the loop is specified. The frontier moved, both men recorded the movement, and a reader can reconstruct the whole exchange from the two men's own summaries without a custodian's intervention. Were the row in the table it would grade partly works, the mechanism obligation having been accepted as permanent while particular explanations remain, many of them, unredeemed. Its restoration here, in prose, serves the article's closing purpose: the standard the scorecard applies is not an invention of its author's severity. It is the standard the tradition's best practitioners applied to themselves, in public, when nobody was making them.

### Why is the Cohen and Elster exchange the anti pattern in pure form?

Because every move the pattern forbids is the move the exchange performs. The criticism is stated by its author at full strength. The reply concedes the criticism's legitimate core before answering its edge. The claim that survives is identified as a reduced claim, with the reduction itemised.

And the critic acknowledges the reply's force in return, so that the frontier's new position is a joint product rather than a unilateral announcement. Set beside the predictions row's century of restatement, the exchange shows that the tradition always contained the alternative practice, waiting for practitioners whose institutions let them use it.

## For teachers and seminar leaders

The transferable asset in this article is not its verdicts but its instrument. The reformulation pattern, stated abstractly, is a general lesson in assessing theoretical defences, and it teaches well because students can be set to find it in traditions they admire: assign each seminar member a doctrine, a criticism it faced, and the defence on record, and require the four moves to be identified or their absence demonstrated. The exercise disciplines two habits at once, the hostile reader's habit of treating every restatement as fraud and the friendly reader's contrary reflex of crediting every restatement as explication, since the documentary test, what did the claim's first audiences take it to mean, cuts against both. The scorecard's three-term vocabulary also gives seminars a shared grading language that survives disagreement about the grades themselves. A class that can argue about whether a given reply works, partly works, or relocates, using the claim attacked and the claim surviving as the evidence, has learned the transferable skill this topic exists in the curriculum to teach.

## For researchers, historians, and journalists

Your asset here is the unanswered list, which is a research agenda stated as a balance sheet. Each of the five standing items specifies, through its defeater, the construction or evidence that would close it, and several are live problems rather than historical curiosities: the elicitation of dispersed knowledge is an open question in institutional economics, the design of innovation under non market ownership is open in organisational theory, and the constitutional theory of post capitalist power is open nearly everywhere, its absence in this tradition being the most documented instance of a general gap. Historians will find the pattern's documentary test directly usable on other chartered doctrines, and the article's method section states it in replicable form. Journalists covering the periodic revivals of this tradition's fortunes should note the scorecard's central distinction before quoting any participant: the scholarly defence and the custodial defence are different literatures with different standards, and a revival's intellectual quality is measurable by which of them its spokesmen cite. When a defender answers a criticism, the professional question is the grader's one: which claim, exactly, has he defended, and where is the concession printed?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do Marxists answer the criticisms?

In four broad ways, developed across a century of signed scholarship. One way rebuilds a piece of theory where an objection broke it, the transformation literature being the grand example. Another insists the objection aimed at a position the writings never occupied. A third draws a line between doctrine and the governments that ruled under the doctrine's flag. A fourth recasts forecasts as conditioned tendencies. These families are not equally respectable, and the article refuses to average them: it takes eleven major criticisms in turn, exhibits the best defence each has received, and marks where the defence succeeds, where it succeeds only in part, and where it quietly substitutes a new claim for the one under fire.

### Q: Which Marxist replies actually work?

Two pass every test the article applies. Against the claim that occupational change dissolved class, the relational account replies that class was always about a person's place in production, not about pay or lifestyle, and the affluent worker findings fall into place as something the map expects rather than fears. Against the claim that value theory ignores nature, the recovery of the metabolic rift shows the published writings treating nature's free appropriation as a defect of the system under analysis, not of the analysis. Close behind stands the party form mechanism in the authoritarianism debate, which narrows that charge to a specifiable instrument and a datable history, though a residue concerning limits on power survives it.

### Q: What remains unanswered in Marxism?

Five objections are still open when the audit closes. Hayek's argument from dispersed knowledge has never been met for planning across a whole economy. The incentive objection persists in the form that concerns novelty and enterprise, since evidence about diligence in cooperatives says nothing about who stakes resources on untried futures. The canon contains no worked out doctrine for binding a revolutionary government. The promised society's founding condition, material abundance, still stands in the one programmatic text that specifies it. And the step in the opening chapter of Capital that moves from exchange ratios to a shared substance was abandoned by the defence instead of being repaired, which is why it appears on this list a century and more after it was challenged.

### Q: What is the reformulation pattern?

It is the name given here to a habit visible across the whole defensive record. A bold assertion collides with facts. A milder cousin of the assertion is then found in the writings, or assembled from their materials, and exhibited as the doctrine's meaning from the start, footnotes and all. The critic is finally accused of assaulting a crude version nobody held. Consider the sequence in its classic instances: destitution softens into a shrinking share, the two camps migrate from nations to the globe, breakdown becomes one influence among several working the other way. Any single softening might be justified. The habit lies in the sequence and in the refusal, repeated across generations, to record that a surrender occurred.

### Q: Do Marxists move the goalposts?

Some do, on a documented routine, and some conspicuously do not, and the difference follows the writer's address. Inside organisations whose unity depended on an uncorrected charter, restating a claim while denying the restatement was the standard survival technique, because conceding a criticism meant indicting the charter itself. Inside universities and exile journals, where a scholar's standing rose when he priced his own side's errors, the same tradition produced reconstructions with their costs printed on the label. So the honest answer names an institution, not a tribe. Ask where a given defence was written and what a concession would have cost its author, and you can usually predict whether the posts will have moved before you finish reading.

### Q: What is the strongest Marxist reply?

The relational rebuilding of class, on the combined evidence. Its central definition can be produced from the founding writings, so nobody had to invent it under pressure. The data gathered to bury it, prosperous manual workers with privatised lives in the Luton studies, turned out to match what the definition leads one to expect. And instead of closing the matter, the reply opened a comparative research programme under Erik Olin Wright that measured class structure across countries and published revisions when its own instruments pushed back. The rift recovery in the ecological debate is its only close rival, distinguished by having reversed an objection's direction entirely from texts any reader could have consulted all along. Both replies concede something real on their way to winning, which is part of the signature.

### Q: Does the tendency reformulation hold up?

It depends on whether the person using it accepts exposure. Ernest Mandel's version earns partial credit because the tendency vocabulary there is bolted to a machinery of long waves and to a forecast, issued during the postwar boom, that the boom would exhaust itself, as it duly did. Strip away the machinery and the same vocabulary earns none. A tendency surrounded by counteracting influences that can be multiplied without limit will fit any sequence of events whatsoever, and a statement that fits everything has stopped making a claim about anything. The practical test for a student is to ask what observation the tendency's defender would accept as counting against him. If he can name one, the reformulation is doing work. If he cannot, the pattern has claimed another instance.

### Q: Are the standard replies honest?

Judge the two literatures separately. The signed scholarship is largely straight with its readers: it names its authors, prints its concessions, and can be checked at the page. The compressed versions that circulate in textbooks and movement classrooms are another matter. Compression there follows a law of its own, keeping every triumph and deleting every condition, so that conditional defences return as unconditional ones and the qualifications on which the originals depended quietly vanish. By the time a reply reaches a catechism it has usually become the thing the article calls folklore, more confident than its source and shorter than its target. Anyone who needs the honest version should read the named works and treat all summaries, this one included, as pointers rather than as evidence.

### Q: What would a successful reply require?

Five things, each of them the reverse of a documented failure. It would engage the objection where the objector placed it, not on friendlier terrain nearby. It would stand up for the very proposition under attack, in something like that proposition's original force. It would print, within its own pages, whatever the objection gets right. It would finish with a claim definite enough that a reader with no stake in the outcome could imagine discovering it to be false. And it would name the finding that would make its own author withdraw it. Measured against that specification, the best replies in the record satisfy four conditions out of five, and the specification's real use is prospective: it lets a reader grade the next celebrated answer on the day it appears, without waiting for a scorecard.

### Q: Has the framework been progressively narrowed?

Partly, and the parts matter. Everything in the framework that issued dated expectations has been cut back: destitution in its absolute sense, society simplifying into two camps, collapse arriving on a schedule, revolution beginning where industry was most advanced. Everything concerned with designing the successor society has thinned into silence at the decisive joints, above all the gathering of knowledge and the binding of power. The analytical equipment, by contrast, has grown sharper with use: class is now measured where it was once merely proclaimed, the account of capital's traffic with nature is fuller than its nineteenth century sketch, and the reading of social forms has deepened past its first statement. Prophecy contracted, design stalled, analysis expanded. That unevenness is the finding, and any single word for it, decline or maturation, flattens what the audit took pains to keep separate.

### Q: How does the reformulation pattern grade the tradition's replies as a whole?

It returns a spread, not a sentence. At one end sit a small number of outright answers, written almost always by scholars placed beyond the reach of the doctrine's custodians. In the broad middle sit partial defences, each effective against a named version of its target and ineffective or silent against another, and the article's labour goes into drawing those boundaries precisely. At the far end sits a recurring drift, strongest wherever an organisation's prestige was engaged, toward presenting a reduced claim as the original one. Averaging the spread would destroy the information the grading exists to preserve. What the pattern adds to the spread is an explanation of its shape: quality follows the price of concession in the place of writing, with a fidelity that no hypothesis about talent or sincerity can match.

### Q: What should a student separate between a reply that answers a criticism and one that relocates the claim?

Write down two sentences before reading the argument: the proposition the critic attacked, and the proposition still standing when the reply finishes. If the sentences match in scope and force, the reply answered. If the second is hedged where the first was bold, local where the first was universal, or about a neighbouring topic altogether, the reply relocated, whatever its other merits. Relocation is not a disgrace; theories often improve by it, and several relocations in this record produced real knowledge. The disgrace is purely in the bookkeeping, when the new proposition is scored as a successful defence of the old. Examiners reward the student who performs the comparison explicitly, because the comparison, once performed, does most of the verdict's work by itself.

### Q: Why must a defence acknowledge the pattern before its individual replies can be believed?

Trust is given to witnesses, and witnesses are judged by their record on matters already checked. A tradition of defence that has been caught, repeatedly, presenting reduced claims as original ones has spent its credit: a reader who knows that history will apply a discount to every subsequent assertion from the same source, including assertions that happen to be sound. No individual reply, however strong, can repeal the discount by itself, because the doubt concerns the source's habits rather than the reply's logic. The one act that rebuilds credit is a public accounting for the habit itself, naming the episodes and pricing the losses without being forced. That is why this article names the pattern at the outset and grades under its shadow throughout: the strong replies deserve belief, and belief has a precondition.

### Q: How does the replies scorecard order the verdicts?

By measuring how far each claim travelled while under attack. At the top stand defences whose original proposition remains intact and operative when the exchange ends. In the middle stand defences that preserve their proposition in a cut down form, effective against one reading of the objection and not against its siblings, with the cut marked in the reply itself. At the bottom stand defences whose surviving proposition differs from the one originally assaulted, so that the argument, however elegant, has been conducted about something else. Two further columns keep the ordering honest: one records what each defence gives up, and one specifies the discovery that would overturn the grade. Brilliance is not a criterion anywhere in the ordering, and some of the most brilliant performances in the record sit in the bottom tier for exactly that reason.

### Q: What separates narrowing a claim under pressure from refining it on evidence?

Sequence and candour, both of them checkable in a library. A refinement starts from material the theorist's own work turned up, moves the claim to where that material points, and announces the move as a correction, quoting the earlier form and stating what it cost. A pressured narrowing starts from material an opponent turned up, moves the claim beyond that material's reach, and announces the move as a clarification of what was meant all along. The destination can be identical in the two cases, which is why inspecting destinations settles nothing. Inspect instead the dates, the provenance of the evidence, and the presence or absence of a printed surrender. Where all three are visible, the classification makes itself, and it is this classification, applied without favour, that fills the scorecard's verdict column.
