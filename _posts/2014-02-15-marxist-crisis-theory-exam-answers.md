Crisis theory is the topic on which examination candidates lose marks in the most preventable way in the whole Marxist syllabus. The standard revision resources present one mechanism, the falling rate of profit, as though it were Marx's theory of crisis, complete and settled. A candidate who trusts those resources walks into the examination with an answer that is incomplete before the first paragraph ends, because the scholarship recognises several rival accounts inside Marx's own writings and several more built from them by later hands. The answer is also misleading in a second direction, since it attributes to Marx a finished doctrine he never assembled. Markers who know the field see both faults at once. This guide repairs the damage at its root, by teaching the one opening move that converts recall into analysis, and then by working through each family of explanation with the mechanism, the evidence, the objection, and the traps a candidate needs in order to score at the level the material deserves.

The organising claim deserves its name immediately, because named moves are the moves candidates actually perform under time pressure. The plurality opening is the practice of stating, in the first paragraph of a crisis answer, that Marx left no single crisis theory, and of naming at least two rival mechanisms before expounding any of them. The sentence costs a handful of words and buys three distinct advantages. It demonstrates textual command, since the claim is simply true about the state of Marx's manuscripts. It licenses comparison, which is the operation mark schemes reward most heavily on this topic. And it protects the evaluation that follows, because criticism can then be aimed at one family at a time, specified rather than sweeping, and a marker can credit each act of specification separately. Everything else in this guide is scaffolding around that opening: the families stated accurately, the formula handled without fear, the counteracting influences restored to their proper place, the strongest objections faced first rather than last, and the conclusion taught to match its strength to the evidence the answer has actually marshalled.

Scope needs one honest paragraph. The doctrines themselves, expounded at full length, live in the companion treatment of the [marxist crisis theory guide](/2013/10/01/marxist-crisis-theory-guide/), and a reader unsure of the underlying theory should work there first. What follows assumes the material can be found and addresses the narrower, more neglected question of what examiners reward when several hundred scripts compete. The assessment settings served here are durable types rather than named papers: undergraduate political economy and heterodox economics courses, development studies programmes where Marxist accounts of instability appear as a perspective, civil service optional papers in political science and economics where Marxist economics is a named topic, national eligibility tests in economics, political science, and sociology, and school specifications in economics and sociology that include Marxist analysis of capitalism. No syllabus code, paper number, or mark allocation is stated anywhere in this guide, because those change and the craft does not. The craft is the subject.

## Why crisis theory rewards the plurality opening

The textual situation explains why the plurality opening is available at all, and a candidate who understands the situation can defend the opening instead of merely performing it. Marx announced a large architecture for his critique of political economy in the late eighteen fifties, a sequence of books moving from capital through landed property, wage labour, the state, foreign trade, and the world market. Crisis belonged to the later reaches of that design, where the concrete movements of the world market would finally be treated. The design was never completed. What survives on crisis is material distributed across works written for other purposes: the notebooks of eighteen fifty seven and fifty eight, where crisis erupts inside an argument about money; the long manuscript on the history of economic thought, where rival explanations are dismantled one by one; the second volume's schemes of reproduction, which show the proportions a growing economy must maintain; and the third volume's chapters on the profit rate, assembled by Engels from drafts Marx left in varying states of finish. No chapter anywhere gathers these strands into a single doctrine and names it the theory of crisis.

Scholars have drawn the obvious conclusion in different registers. Some treat the plurality as an editorial accident, a theory that would have been unified had Marx lived to write the world market book. Others, Simon Clarke prominent among them, argue that crisis in Marx is not a detachable mechanism at all but the form in which the contradictions of the whole system periodically surface, so that hunting for one privileged passage misunderstands the project. A third line, associated with Michael Heinrich's reading of the manuscripts, stresses how much of the famous third volume material is provisional draft, reordered and smoothed by its editor, which makes any single-mechanism orthodoxy built on it doubly fragile. A candidate need not adjudicate among these scholarly positions. The examinable point is smaller and firmer: the texts license several mechanisms, the tradition developed them into rival families, and an answer that says so in its opening has aligned itself with the state of knowledge rather than with the revision-guide simplification.

### Which crisis theories must you name in an exam?

Name at least the falling rate of profit mechanism and one demand-side or realisation account, and state that Marx left no unified theory. Adding disproportionality or profit squeeze strengthens it further, as does noting that these mechanisms make opposing predictions about wages, demand, and profitability across the cycle.

That answer is the floor, not the ceiling, and the sections below build each element of it into working knowledge. Notice what the floor already contains: two mechanisms rather than one, a claim about the texts rather than about the economy, and a comparative hook, the opposing predictions, that an examiner can follow into the body of the script. An opening built on those three elements has performed selection, accuracy, and analysis before the exposition proper begins. Markers form their expectations early, an unfairness of human grading that candidates cannot abolish but can exploit honestly, by front-loading the evidence of command.

### Why does a two-mechanism opening change the band?

Two mechanisms change the band because comparison becomes possible, and comparison is the operation that separates analytical scripts from narrative ones. With one mechanism an answer can only assert and defend; with two it can weigh, specify, and discriminate, crediting each family where its evidence runs and refusing each where its evidence stops.

There is a further, quieter reason the opening pays. Crisis questions are set by examiners who know the plurality perfectly well, and the questions are frequently worded to invite it, with phrases about accounts, explanations in the plural, or tendencies rather than a theory. A candidate answering in the singular has declined an invitation printed on the paper. Reading the wording of questions for exactly that signal is a learnable habit, and the worked answers later in this guide model it repeatedly: the question's grammar is the first piece of evidence about what the marker hopes to read.

## The families at a glance, without flattening them

Five families organise the field for examination purposes, and each deserves a paragraph of honest summary before any is worked in depth. Compression at this stage is a map, not a substitute: every family named here returns later with its mechanism exposed, its best evidence presented, and its hardest objection stated at full strength.

The falling rate of profit family locates crisis in production itself. As capital accumulates, the mass of machinery, plant, and materials worked up by each labourer grows faster than the living labour employed, and since living labour alone produces surplus value on Marx's account, the ratio of profit to total capital advanced comes under downward pressure. Crises then appear as the violent episodes in which devalued capital, bankruptcies, and idle capacity restore the conditions of profitability by destruction. The family's textual home is the third volume's part on the law of the tendency, and its great strength is that it grounds crisis in the accumulation process rather than in mistakes, moods, or policy errors.

The realisation family, often taught under the label underconsumption, locates the blockage on the side of demand. Capitalism separates production from the consumption of the producers, wages being both a cost each firm tries to compress and the source of the demand that must purchase the product. On this account the system chronically tends to produce beyond the consumption its wage structure can support, and crises break out when the gap can no longer be bridged by investment spending, exports, credit, or luxury outlay. Sismondi saw the shape of the argument before Marx, Rodbertus gave it a distributionist form, and Rosa Luxemburg built her theory of accumulation on the claim that surplus value cannot be realised within a closed capitalist economy at all.

The disproportionality family locates crisis in coordination. A decentralised economy must continually guess the proportions in which steel, grain, machinery, housing, and a thousand other lines should expand relative to one another, and it discovers its errors only after capital has been sunk in the wrong proportions. Marx's reproduction schemes in the second volume display the required proportions with algebraic clarity, and Mikhail Tugan-Baranovsky turned the insight against the underconsumptionists: crises arise, he argued, not because consumption lags but because investment lurches between departments, disproportions accumulating behind the appearance of growth until the correction arrives. Hilferding absorbed much of this into his account of finance capital, and the family survives wherever crises are read as failures of proportion between sectors.

The profit squeeze family reverses the demand-side story. Where underconsumption blames wages that are too low, squeeze blames wages that have grown too strong: as an expansion lengthens and unemployment drains away, workers' bargaining power rises, money wages outpace productivity, and the profit share is compressed until investment falters and the downturn begins. Andrew Glyn and Bob Sutcliffe built the canonical version for postwar Britain, adding international competition that prevented firms from simply passing costs into prices, and Robert Boddy and James Crotty carried a variant into the American literature. The family's examinable gift is its contradiction of the underconsumption account on the wage question, which is precisely the kind of internal opposition the plurality opening exists to exploit.

The monetary and financial family works at the surface where crises actually announce themselves. Marx's writings on money and credit insist that a monetary economy carries the formal possibility of crisis from the moment sale and purchase can separate in time, and that the credit system, which papers over realisation difficulties by lending against future production, eventually concentrates the reckoning into the money market, where everyone demands payment at once. Later writers in this line, and readers who approach it through Hyman Minsky's financial instability hypothesis as a neighbouring framework, treat the migration of finance from hedging toward speculation as the mechanism that times the crash. For examination purposes the family's role is usually to explain the form and the trigger, while the other families compete to explain the underlying vulnerability the trigger exposes.

Hold the five together and the pattern the examiners are testing comes into focus. The families disagree about where the blockage sits: in profitability, in demand, in proportion, in distribution, in finance. They disagree about wages, which one account wants higher and another blames for rising. They disagree about what a crisis does, whether it restores profitability by destruction, enforces proportions by bankruptcy, or resets distribution by unemployment. An answer that can stage those disagreements has material for analysis in every paragraph. An answer armed with only one family has a hammer and must pretend the whole topic is a nail.

## The crisis answer matrix and how to read it

The crisis answer matrix compresses the guide's method into a single findable object. Each row takes a question type that recurs across papers and names the mechanisms the answer must deploy, the anchor a candidate can cite, the evaluative move that lifts the band, and the trap that the row exists to warn against. It is a revision instrument and a planning instrument at once: in the examination room its columns become the skeleton of a plan, mechanisms first, anchor second, evaluation third, trap held in mind as a negative instruction throughout.

| Question type | Mechanisms to name | Anchor to cite | Evaluation that lifts the band | Trap |
| Explain the Marxist theory of crisis | The falling rate of profit with a realisation account alongside | The third volume chapters on the law of the tendency | State that the tradition disputes which mechanism is fundamental | Presenting one mechanism as the settled doctrine |
| Explain the falling rate of profit | Rising organic composition pressing on the profit rate, with the counteracting influences | The formula p equals s over c plus v, worked with numbers | Concede the Okishio objection before weighing the replies | Omitting the countertendencies and predicting a smooth collapse |
| Assess whether capitalism is prone to crisis | At least two families, plus a rival framework for contrast | The reproduction schemes and the proportions they require | Match the verdict to the family whose evidence survives scrutiny | Treating proneness as a prophecy of terminal breakdown |
| Compare Marx and Keynes on crisis | Effective demand set against the profitability and realisation families | Keynes on the marginal efficiency of capital collapsing | Note that both reject automatic equilibration while locating failure differently | Merging the two into a single demand story |
| Evaluate crisis theory through a historical episode | The family the episode best fits, tested against one that fits it badly | The financial crash treated as a case with a date and a sequence | Grade the episode as evidence, asking what it cannot show | Narrating the episode at length while the mechanism goes unexamined |

Reading the matrix well means resisting two misuses. The first misuse treats it as a script to memorise, producing answers whose paragraphs arrive in matrix order regardless of the question asked; markers detect that rigidity within a page, and the guide returns to the template danger in a later section. The second misuse treats each row as sealed, when the strongest answers borrow across rows: an explain question gains credit from a sentence of the evaluation column, and an assess question collapses without the mechanisms column fully stocked. Use the matrix to check coverage, not to dictate sequence. The sequence belongs to the question.

One further habit makes the matrix earn its keep. When revising, cover the evaluation column and reconstruct it from the mechanism column unaided; the evaluative moves are the part candidates most often intend to include and most often drop under pressure. When planning in the room, reverse the exercise: glance at the trap column last, as a final instruction to the self about which error this particular answer is most likely to commit, because each question type has a characteristic failure and the candidate who has named it in the plan rarely commits it in the prose.

## The falling rate of profit, in examinable form

Begin with the formula, because the formula disciplines everything said afterwards. The rate of profit, written p, equals the surplus value produced, s, divided by the total capital advanced, which is constant capital c plus variable capital v. Divide top and bottom by v and the same rate appears as the ratio of the rate of exploitation, s over v, to the organic composition of capital plus one, where the composition is c over v. The algebra carries the whole theory in miniature. If exploitation stands still while the composition rises, meaning each worker sets in motion an ever larger mass of machinery and material, the rate of profit must fall. Accumulation itself drives the composition upward, because competition forces every firm to mechanise, and mechanisation is nothing but the substitution of dead labour embodied in equipment for the living labour that alone, on this theory, generates surplus.

Worked numbers make the mechanism stick in a way prose cannot. Imagine a capital of one hundred units split evenly, fifty constant and fifty variable, with exploitation at one hundred percent so that the fifty of variable capital yields fifty of surplus value. The rate of profit is fifty over one hundred, fifty percent. Let accumulation proceed until the same hundred is split eighty constant and twenty variable, exploitation unchanged; the twenty of variable capital yields twenty of surplus, and the rate is twenty over one hundred, twenty percent. Nothing has gone wrong. No manager erred, no market glutted, no banker panicked. Profitability halved because the source of surplus shrank relative to the mass it must set in motion. That is the austere core of the family's claim, and a candidate who can reproduce the arithmetic in two lines has an anchor no marker can mistake for bluff. The dedicated treatment of the mechanism sits in the article on the [tendency rate of profit to fall](/2013/10/15/tendency-rate-of-profit-to-fall/), which works the textual chapters at length.

### What does the profit rate formula actually claim?

The formula claims a ratio, not a destiny: profit measured against all capital advanced, so that anything enlarging the denominator faster than the surplus in the numerator presses the rate downward. It asserts a tendency generated by accumulation itself, whose operation the counteracting influences may mask for long periods without abolishing.

Three precisions separate strong treatments of the law from weak ones. First, the law is stated by Marx as a tendency, a pressure whose effects can be offset, delayed, and locally reversed, and answers that upgrade it into a timetable of collapse misquote the text they claim to expound. Second, the mass of profit can rise while its rate falls, since a growing total capital at a lower percentage may yield a larger absolute surplus, and confusing rate with mass is a marked error because the theory's dynamics, the desperation of capitals competing over a relatively shrinking pool, turn on the rate. Third, the composition that matters is the value composition, the ratio in value terms, and technical change that cheapens machinery can hold the value ratio below the physical one, a subtlety the counteracting influences section develops. A candidate who volunteers these three precisions has signalled scholarship; a candidate who omits all three has summarised a summary.

Why does the tendency issue in crises rather than in a gentle glide of profitability toward zero? The vol 3 material answers with the violence of the adjustment. As the rate sags, capitals fight harder over the available surplus; speculation and credit stretch to sustain returns the productive base no longer delivers; and eventually the structure is corrected by the destruction of capital values themselves, bankrupt stock, written-down plant, and idle capacity that cheapen the denominator until profitability revives. Crisis, on this reading, is not the tendency's failure but its enforcement mechanism, the periodic purge by which the system restores the conditions for another round of accumulation. That formulation, crisis as cure within the family's own logic, is the sentence that lifts an exposition into an explanation, and it should appear in any answer that gives the family more than a paragraph.

## The counteracting influences, the commonest omission

Marx follows the statement of the law with a chapter on counteracting influences, and omitting that chapter is, by broad marker consensus, the commonest content error in crisis answers. The influences are not footnotes. They are the reason the law operates as a tendency rather than a schedule, and an answer that lacks them attributes to Marx a crude breakdown prediction he explicitly refused to make. Learn the set as a working kit of six. The intensity of exploitation can rise, through longer hours, faster work, or the compression of necessary labour, lifting the numerator against the composition's drag. Wages can be depressed below the value of labour power for stretches of time, again feeding the numerator at labour's expense. The elements of constant capital can cheapen, because the same productivity growth that multiplies machinery also reduces the value of each machine, so the value composition climbs more slowly than the physical mass of equipment suggests. A relative surplus population can hold wages down and supply cheap labour to new lines where composition starts low. Foreign trade can cheapen both inputs and wage goods while opening fields of higher profit abroad. And the growth of interest-bearing capital can redistribute the surplus in ways that sustain the rate measured on functioning capital, though at the cost of the financial fragilities the monetary family studies.

### Why do the counteracting influences deserve a paragraph of their own?

Because they convert the law from a prediction into an analysis, and markers read their absence as proof the candidate learned the theory from a summary. Naming two or three influences, with the cheapening of constant capital foremost, shows the tendency and its resistance grasped together, which is the theory Marx actually wrote.

Each influence also carries an examinable sting, and the strongest answers mention at least one. The cheapening of constant capital is driven by the very productivity growth that raises the technical composition, so the law contains its own brake, and whether brake or engine dominates in a given epoch is an empirical question the theory alone cannot settle. Foreign trade displaces the pressure geographically, which is one root of the tradition's interest in imperialism and unequal exchange. Depression of wages collides with the realisation family's warning that compressed wages undermine demand, so two of Marx's own countertendencies stand in tension with two of his own crisis mechanisms, a fact candidates can deploy to show that plurality is not an examiner's invention but a feature of the material itself. Handled this way, the counteracting influences stop being a list to recite and become the hinge on which the whole evaluation swings.

There is a presentational point as well. Answers typically introduce the countertendencies apologetically, as qualifications weakening the theory, when their correct presentation is the reverse: a law stated together with its counteracting factors is a stronger scientific object than a bare trend, because it specifies the conditions under which the trend will show itself and the conditions under which it will hide. Candidates who frame the chapter that way, as Marx's own methodological care, earn credit for understanding the architecture rather than the slogans. The framing also prepares the empirical discussion, since the measurement disputes examined later in this guide are, at bottom, disputes about which influences dominated the recorded decades.

## Realisation and underconsumption, the demand-side family

The second great family begins from a different question: not whether surplus value can be produced, but whether it can be realised, sold for money at its value. Production and realisation are separate acts in a monetary economy, and the gap between them is where this family lives. Its simplest form reasons from wages. The working class receives back in wages only part of the value it produces, so workers cannot buy back the whole product; capitalists' own consumption covers a further slice; the remainder must be absorbed by investment, by the purchase of new means of production and additional labour. While investment booms, the circuit holds. When the inducement to invest falters, the unsold remainder appears as a glut, and the crisis announces itself as overproduction relative to solvent demand. Sismondi had argued a version of this against the optimism of Say and Ricardo in the eighteen twenties, Rodbertus later grounded it in the falling wage share of a growing product, and Marx inherited the problem while criticising the crude versions of the solution, a double relation candidates should state because examiners love it: Marx both uses the realisation problem and attacks particular underconsumption theories as shallow.

Luxemburg radicalised the family into a theory of the system's outer limit. In her account, the surplus destined for accumulation cannot be realised among capitalists and workers alone, since each round of expanded reproduction requires buyers for an ever larger surplus product; capitalism therefore survives by selling into, and drawing demand from, non-capitalist strata and territories, and imperialism is the competitive struggle over those remaining outlets. The argument was battered on publication, by critics who showed the reproduction schemes could balance without outside buyers as a matter of arithmetic, and its defence migrated to historical ground: whatever the schemes permit, actual accumulation leaned heavily on colonial markets, state spending, and militarism. For examination purposes the Luxemburg episode is best used as a demonstration that a family can fail as logic and flourish as history, and that examiners reward candidates who can say which of those verdicts they are delivering.

### How does underconsumption differ from overproduction?

Overproduction names the symptom, output that cannot be sold at prices covering its value; underconsumption names one proposed cause, the chronic insufficiency of mass purchasing power. The terms are not synonyms, because overproduction can equally be charged to disproportion between sectors or to a profitability collapse that halts investment before any glut of consumer goods appears.

The precise Marxist usage rewards care. When the third volume remarks that the conditions of production and the conditions of realisation are not identical, and that the narrow basis of working-class consumption underlies the whole contradiction, underconsumptionist readers claim a textual warrant, while their opponents reply that the same text subordinates that point to the profitability dynamic and mocks the idea that crises would vanish if wages rose. A strong answer stages exactly that tug of war over the passages instead of pretending the texts speak with one voice. It also distinguishes the strong underconsumption claim, that deficient consumption is the chronic and sufficient cause, from the weak claim, that constrained consumption is one standing condition among several, because the weak claim is nearly undeniable and the strong claim is where the argument lives.

Paul Sweezy's treatment is the bridge examiners most respect. Sweezy judged the falling rate of profit exposition incomplete as Marx left it, and rebuilt the demand-side case with modern apparatus, later extending it with Paul Baran into the theory of monopoly capital, where the problem becomes absorbing a rising surplus through waste, advertising, and state expenditure. Citing that lineage shows a candidate that the family has a living scholarly form, not merely a nineteenth-century ancestry. It also supplies a graceful evaluation: the family's weakness is timing, since chronic demand deficiency explains a permanent condition rather than the cycle's turning points, while its strength is the directness with which it connects distribution to instability, a connection the profit rate family reaches only by a longer road.

## Disproportionality, the coordination family

Open the second volume and the problem changes shape. Marx constructs schemes in which the economy is divided into two great departments, one producing means of production and one producing articles of consumption, and derives the exchange proportions between them that expanded reproduction requires. The schemes are the most formal object in the whole crisis literature of the tradition, and their lesson is fragility of a special kind. Balance is possible; the arithmetic closes. But nothing in a decentralised economy guarantees the proportions in advance, since investment decisions are taken privately, on expectations, with capital sunk into plant that cannot be costlessly moved when the proportions turn out wrong. Crisis is the discovered error, generalised: too much capacity in one line, too little in another, credit having financed both mistakes impartially until the accounts are called.

Tugan-Baranovsky wielded the schemes against the underconsumptionists with genuine brilliance. If Department One grows by selling machinery to itself, he argued, production can in principle expand with consumption stagnant, so deficient demand cannot be the necessary cause; what breaks is proportion, when one department's expansion outruns the complementary growth the schemes demand. The argument permanently damaged crude underconsumption, and any answer on the demand family that ignores it will be marked as one-sided by a reader who knows the literature. Hilferding then absorbed disproportionality into a wider account in which cartels and banks partially plan production within sectors while intensifying the anarchy between them, so that crises become less frequent in some tellings and more violent in others, a nuance worth a sentence.

The family's evaluative profile is distinctive. Its strength is modesty: it explains how crises can arise without any secular trend in profitability or demand, purely from the coordination problem of an unplanned economy, and it fits episodes where sectoral imbalance, a construction boom or an inventory spiral, visibly leads the collapse. Its weakness is the mirror of that modesty. If disproportion is the whole story, crises look accidental, correctable by better information or wiser banking, and the family's critics, Marxists and others, press the question of why the errors recur with such regularity and in such synchrony if nothing deeper drives them. The sophisticated answer therefore often demotes disproportionality from cause to transmission: the channel through which a profitability or demand disturbance expresses itself sector by sector. That demotion, argued rather than asserted, is an analytical move of exactly the kind the higher bands exist to reward.

## Profit squeeze, the wage-side family

Every family considered so far treats wages mainly as a constraint on demand or as a deduction from surplus. Profit squeeze turns the variable around and treats the wage as an active force that expansions themselves strengthen. The mechanism runs through the labour market. As a boom matures, the reserve of unemployed labour drains; workers who were cautious under the threat of the sack grow willing to press claims; unions rebuild strength; and money wages climb faster than productivity, particularly where shop floor organisation is dense. Firms facing strong product demand may concede, expecting to pass costs on, until international competition or a tightening of credit closes that escape. The profit share narrows, investment plans built on the earlier share are shelved, and the economy tips into the downturn that restores discipline by rebuilding the reserve. Crisis, on this account, is the political economy of full employment working itself out.

The canonical construction is Glyn and Sutcliffe's study of Britain in the late nineteen sixties and early nineteen seventies, which documented a profit share squeezed between militant wage pressure at home and competitors abroad who blocked the pass-through into prices. Boddy and Crotty gave the American case a political form, arguing that the postwar accord between capital and organised labour held while growth funded both sides and broke when slowing productivity made the claims incompatible. The family's great examinable virtue, already flagged, is contradiction: it predicts crisis from wages rising, where underconsumption predicts crisis from wages stagnating, so a candidate who stages the two against each other over the same decade has manufactured analysis out of the syllabus itself. Examiners do not need to agree with either account to reward the staging.

Evaluation should be equally specific. The squeeze mechanism struggles where labour is weak: it cannot directly explain downturns that arrive amid falling wage shares and quiescent workforces, and critics note that the seventies, its showcase decade, also featured oil shocks and a productivity slowdown that confound the attribution. Defenders reply that squeeze is a theory of a phase rather than of every cycle, and that its real legacy is methodological, the insistence that distribution is fought over rather than technically given, which permanently changed how the other families are read. A candidate who grants the family a phase and denies it the whole cycle has delivered a calibrated verdict, and calibration, as this guide repeats in several registers, is the currency in which top marks are paid.

## Money, credit, and the financial form of crisis

However deep a candidate digs with the other families, the event itself always surfaces in money. Marx's analysis of the commodity already contains the seed: once sale and purchase separate, once goods are sold against promises and the promises chain together, the possibility of a general refusal of payment exists inside the form of exchange itself. The credit system then builds an inverted pyramid on that possibility. Credit stretches realisation across time, finances proportions nobody has verified, and lets profitability appear sustained after its base has thinned; and because credit is a web of mutual obligations, its failure is not local but systemic, the demand for cash becoming universal at the moment cash is least available. The third volume's chapters on interest-bearing capital and the money market of eighteen fifty seven describe the sequence with a journalist's concreteness: speculation, overtrading, the scramble for liquidity, the Bank rate driven upward, and solid houses falling alongside the fraudulent because the distinction cannot be made in a panic.

For examination strategy, the monetary family's function is usually twofold. It explains the trigger and the form, why the break comes when it comes and why it arrives as a financial event with queues, rates, and collapses, while the deeper families compete to explain the vulnerability that made the trigger sufficient. And it disciplines the use of financial episodes as examples, because a candidate formed by this family will describe a crash as the moment hidden imbalances were priced, not as a self-contained story about greed. Minsky's hypothesis, that stability itself breeds the shift from hedge to speculative to Ponzi finance, sits naturally beside this material as a neighbouring framework from outside the tradition, and answers may use it as such, provided the border is marked: Minsky supplies a financial dynamic, not a theory of value, and conflating the two forfeits the precision this topic exists to test.

## Worked answer one: explain the Marxist theory of crisis

Take the commonest question in its commonest wording, an invitation to explain the Marxist theory of crisis, and watch what the plurality opening does to it. The weak script begins by equating the theory with one mechanism, usually the falling rate of profit, expounds that mechanism for the whole answer, and ends having explained a fraction of what the wording requested. The strong script begins by noting that the singular in the question is a convenience of phrasing, that the tradition contains rival accounts of where the blockage in accumulation sits, and that the answer will expound the two best anchored and then indicate the others. Two sentences have done the decisive work: the marker now reads the exposition as a selection from a known field rather than as the candidate's entire stock.

The exposition itself should give the falling rate of profit its full form, formula included, because it remains the most textually elaborated mechanism and the one the question's author most likely has in view. Composition rising under competitive pressure, surplus tied to living labour, the rate pressed downward, crises as the destructive restoration of profitability: four moves, each earning its sentence. Then the realisation family receives the same respect in shorter compass, production outrunning the consumption the wage structure supports, investment bridging the gap until it falters. The pivot sentence follows, and it is the sentence the whole structure exists to make possible: the two accounts locate the failure at opposite ends of the circuit, one in the production of surplus and one in its sale, and they consequently read the same crisis differently, one stressing profitability restored by destruction, the other stressing demand restored by redistribution or waste. A paragraph each on disproportionality and profit squeeze completes the field, not as padding but as demonstrated range, with one clause apiece on where each locates its blockage.

Close such an answer by returning to the question's grammar. An explain command does not ask for a verdict, and the candidate who saves the adjudication for a final measured paragraph, observing that the families are rivals in the scholarship and that their rivalry is itself the most accurate summary of the theory's state, has answered the question that was set instead of the question that was feared. Notice what the model demonstrates about proportion: the opening cost two sentences, the two main families took the bulk, the secondary families were handled by compression rather than omission, and the conclusion added a claim about the literature rather than a repetition of the introduction. That proportion, generous to mechanisms and stingy with throat-clearing, is transferable to every explain question on the topic.

## Worked answer two: explaining the falling rate of profit under pressure

A question naming the law itself changes the assignment. Here the plurality opening shrinks to a framing sentence, this mechanism is one among several the tradition contains and the one this question singles out, and the marks concentrate in the exposition and in the candidate's handling of the machinery around the law. The worked substance runs as follows. State the law precisely: accumulation raises the organic composition of capital, and with the rate of exploitation given, the rate of profit tends to fall. Unpack each term once, constant capital as the value of means of production set in motion, variable capital as the wage bill that purchases the living labour, surplus value as the unpaid portion of the working day. Present the formula in its two dresses, s over the sum of c and v, and the exploitation rate over the composition plus one, because the second dress shows at a glance which forces move the rate and in which direction.

Then work a numerical illustration, the one this guide built earlier or a variant with different figures, and state what the arithmetic proves and what it does not. It proves the direction of the pressure when composition rises faster than exploitation; it does not prove the pressure wins, because the same chapter sequence supplies the counteracting influences, and the model answer gives those influences their own paragraph rather than a dismissive clause. Cheapening of constant capital belongs first, as the influence internal to the mechanism itself. Rising exploitation belongs second, with the remark that it cannot outrun physical and social limits forever. Foreign trade and the surplus population follow in a sentence each. Only after tendency and countertendency both stand on the page may the answer turn to crisis, presenting the downturn as the form in which the suppressed pressure discharges: overaccumulation relative to profitable outlets, capital lying idle beside labour lying idle, and the purge of devaluation that resets the denominator.

Evaluation within an explain question should be brief but real. One paragraph suffices if it chooses the strongest objection rather than the most convenient, and on this law the strongest objection is the Okishio result examined later in this guide: that cost-reducing technical change, adopted because it raises the innovator's profit, raises the general rate of profit too at a constant real wage, so the mechanism's microfoundations point the other way. The model answer states that result fairly, notes the replies mounted by temporal and single-system readings of Marx's value theory, and declines to settle the dispute inside an explain answer, recording instead that the law's status is contested at the level of its logic and not merely its statistics. That refusal to overclaim, performed explicitly, is worth more than a paragraph of loyalist rhetoric, because it shows the marker a candidate who knows where exposition ends and open scholarship begins.

## Worked answer three: assessing whether capitalism is prone to crisis

Assess and evaluate questions change the centre of gravity again: exposition becomes the platform and judgement becomes the building. The plurality opening now does triple duty, since it supplies not only the mechanisms to be tested but also the structure of the test, family by family, with the verdict assembled from the parts. The strong script announces that proneness means different things inside different families: a secular profitability pressure in one, a chronic demand shortfall in another, a recurring coordination failure in a third, and that the answer will ask which of those claims the evidence and the logic sustain. That sentence alone has converted a slogan question into a research programme small enough for an examination script.

The body then runs the families through a consistent gauntlet. For each, the candidate states the mechanism in two or three sentences, names the strongest supporting consideration, names the hardest objection, and records a provisional grade. The falling rate of profit draws support from the long-run pressure of mechanisation and from the theory's ability to explain why crises restore conditions rather than merely interrupt them, and it draws the Okishio objection and the measurement quarrels as its debits. Realisation draws support from the evident narrowness of mass consumption relative to output and debits on timing, since a chronic condition explains a climate rather than the storm's date. Disproportionality draws support from the visible sectoral anatomy of actual downturns and debits on the recurrence problem, why mere guessing errors should synchronise across an economy. Squeeze draws support from the distributional record of its showcase decade and debits on the many downturns that arrive with labour already subdued.

The verdict, when it comes, can therefore be genuinely differentiated, which is the kind markers trust. A model conclusion might hold that proneness is sustained in the weak and important sense: the system generates recurrent crises from its ordinary functioning rather than from external accident, with the coordination and profitability families carrying the strongest joint case; while proneness in the strong sense, a tendency toward a final breakdown from which no recovery is possible, is supported by none of the families as Marx stated them and should be explicitly refused. That two-level verdict, yes to recurrence and no to terminus, is the signature of a candidate who has read the texts against the slogans. It also demonstrates the guide's central lesson about conclusions: their strength is set by the strongest surviving element of the body, never by the temperature of the candidate's sympathies.

## Worked answer four: Marx and Keynes in the same script

Comparison questions on Marx and Keynes fail in a predictable way, by dissolving both thinkers into a shared puddle of demand pessimism. The repair begins with the differences of object. Keynes theorised the short period of a monetary economy in which investment, driven by volatile expectations about an unknowable future, collapses against a floor of liquidity preference, and output and employment settle below full capacity without any tendency to self-correct. Marx's families theorise the long movement of accumulation, in which the production and realisation of surplus value encounter barriers generated by the process itself. One thinker asks why an economy can rest in unemployment equilibrium; the others ask why prosperity periodically breaks. A candidate who opens with that contrast of questions has already outperformed the amalgam, because every subsequent similarity can now be stated as a similarity between different projects, which is a more valuable observation. The fuller side-by-side treatment lives in the article on [marx vs keynes on crisis](/2014/01/15/marx-vs-keynes-on-crisis/), and this section confines itself to the answer craft.

The similarities, handled second, are real and creditable. Both reject the doctrine that supply creates its own demand in a monetary economy; both make the investment decision the unstable hinge of the system; both treat the crisis not as an external shock but as an endogenous product of normal functioning; and both were read, after the great crash of the nineteen thirties, as prophets of an instability the orthodoxy of their respective moments had defined away. A strong answer adds the asymmetry inside the resemblance: Keynes locates the failure in the sphere of circulation and expectation, where psychology and convention move the marginal efficiency of capital, while the Marxist families locate it in production and distribution, in profitability, proportions, and the wage relation, with expectation as the messenger rather than the author. That asymmetry explains the divergent remedies each tradition reaches for, demand management and the socialisation of investment in one case, transformation of the production relations themselves in the other, and remedies are examinable gold because they reveal what each theory thinks the disease is.

Close the comparison with the scholarly caution markers listen for. The two bodies of theory were built with almost no direct engagement between their authors, Keynes's remarks on Marx being few, dismissive, and based on limited reading, so the comparison is an examiner's construction rather than a historical debate, and saying so shows a candidate alert to the difference. Then deliver the comparative verdict in one disciplined sentence, perhaps that Keynes explains the depth and persistence of slumps better while the Marxist families explain the recurrence and the pre-crash anatomy of booms better, and that a complete account of capitalist instability arguably needs a theory of why investment is at once indispensable, profitable-dependent, and prone to collective miscalculation, a sentence whose hedged final clause models exactly the calibration this guide teaches.

## Worked answer five: the historical episode as evidence

The episode question, evaluate a theory through a crash or a slump the candidate knows, inverts the usual temptation: the episode is the test, not the topic, and the marks follow the testing. Choose the episode the syllabus and the series both equip best, the financial crash, whose anatomy this series examines in the article on [marxism and the financial crash](/2013/12/15/marxism-and-the-financial-crash/), and handle it under a strict ration of narrative. Two or three sentences of sequence suffice: a long credit-fed expansion in housing and construction, the layering of claims on that expansion through securitised finance, the moment when the underlying assets were repriced and the chain of promises seized, and the state's rescue of the payments system. Everything beyond that ration is colour the marker cannot credit, and every sentence of colour has displaced a sentence of analysis.

With the episode rationed, the examination begins. Run the families across the case and grade their fit openly. The monetary and financial family fits the surface almost perfectly, since the crash arrived in the money market, through a liquidity panic among institutions holding claims on claims, exactly where that family says crises announce themselves. The disproportionality family fits the sectoral anatomy, a construction and housing complex grotesquely out of proportion to the rest of the economy, financed as though the proportion could never be called. Realisation fits the prelude in part, stagnant mass incomes bridged by household borrowing, so that debt temporarily performed the demand that wages did not, a genuinely illuminating reading candidates should present as the family's strongest modern hour. The falling rate of profit fits least directly on the usual tellings, and the honest answer says so, noting the attempts to connect the crash to a prior profitability sag and grading them as contested rather than established. Squeeze fits hardly at all, arriving as it did amid weak labour organisation and a falling wage share, and naming that misfit is as creditable as naming the fits, because evaluation that only confirms is advertising.

The conclusion of an episode answer should generalise about evidence itself. One episode can falsify a family's claim to universality, embarrass its timing, or vindicate its anatomy of the surface, but it cannot by itself establish a mechanism that operates, if it operates at all, across many cycles and under institutions that change between them. Candidates who write that sentence have understood what historical evidence can do in a theory examination, and they have protected themselves against the trap the matrix names for this row: narration swallowing examination. Episodes are witnesses, and witnesses are cross-examined, not applauded.

## The strongest objection to the plurality opening, stated fairly

Candidates resist the plurality opening for an intelligible reason, and the guide does the objection the courtesy of full strength before answering it. The objection runs: examination answers are judged on conviction and command, a candidate who begins by announcing that the tradition cannot agree with itself sounds like a candidate who cannot decide, and markers under time pressure reward the script that takes a position over the script that surveys a field. There is a harder version underneath. Some questions genuinely want one mechanism expounded, and a candidate who spends the opening on plurality there has spent words the command did not purchase, diluting the exposition that carries the marks. Both versions deserve respect, because both identify real failure modes: listing without comparing is indecision dressed as learning, and plurality pasted onto a single-mechanism question is decoration.

The reply turns on a distinction between plurality as an opening and plurality as an evasion. The opening this guide teaches is two sentences long and is immediately followed by commitment: the answer selects the families the question engages, expounds them in order of strength, and adjudicates among them by the final paragraph. Indecision is not the presence of several mechanisms; indecision is the absence of a verdict about them. A script that names two families and then grades both has taken more positions than a script that recites one, not fewer. As for the single-mechanism question, the tell is in the wording. When the question itself names the law, the thinker, or the text, when it asks for the tendency of the rate of profit or for Luxemburg's argument, the assignment is exposition of that object, and plurality correctly contracts to one orienting sentence that places the object inside its family quarrel before the exposition begins. When the question speaks of theories, accounts, or explanations, or asks whether capitalism is prone to crisis without naming a mechanism, the field is the assignment, and plurality is the answer's spine. Reading the question for that tell is a five-second discipline that decides the architecture, and candidates should practise it on past questions until the discrimination is reflexive.

### When does a single-mechanism answer still make sense?

When the question names the mechanism, the thinker, or the text itself, exposition of that object is the assignment, and plurality contracts to one orienting sentence. It also suits the very short answer form, where the marks purchase a definition and a mechanism rather than a survey of the field.

There remains the marker-psychology worry, and it deserves a direct answer rather than a dismissive one. Markers are trained against mark schemes, and mark schemes on this topic, across the assessment types this guide serves, are written to credit range, specification, and adjudication, because the examiners who draft them hold the plurality themselves. The candidate's fear projects onto the marker a simplicity the marker was hired not to have. What genuinely reads as indecision is the unranked survey, family after family in textbook order with no comparative sentence anywhere, and the cure for that failure is not fewer families but the comparative and adjudicative sentences this guide keeps modelling. Plurality with a spine is the most decisive-looking structure available on this topic, because it shows the candidate deciding repeatedly, at every junction, rather than once by inheritance.

## Evaluation that lifts the band

Evaluation is where crisis answers gain or surrender their top marks, and its first law is selection: one strong objection, accurately stated and pressed home, outweighs four gestures at controversy. The arsenal below is stocked in order of examinable value, and the candidate's task is to choose from it according to the family under judgement, not to discharge the whole magazine at every target.

### What makes an objection strong enough to lead an evaluation?

An objection leads when it strikes the mechanism rather than the conclusion, comes from a named source whose argument the candidate can state in two sentences, and survives the family's standard reply in a form the candidate can specify. Objections that merely report disagreement, or that attack a prediction the theory never made, decorate an answer without evaluating it.

For the falling rate of profit, the leading objection is the Okishio theorem, and it must be stated correctly or not at all. Nobuo Okishio showed in nineteen sixty one that if capitalists adopt only technical changes that reduce their costs at prevailing prices, changes they adopt precisely because the change raises their own profit rate, then the new equilibrium general rate of profit, at an unchanged real wage, is higher than the old, not lower. The result appears to cut the law's engine out: the individual rationality of mechanisation produces the opposite of the collective result Marx's mechanism requires. Replies exist and the model answer names their shape: that the theorem is proved within a simultaneous valuation framework in which inputs and outputs are priced at the same moment, while Marx's values unfold in time, so that on a temporal reading the innovator's advantage and the subsequent devaluation of existing capital restore the downward pressure; and that the theorem holds the real wage fixed, building into its premises the distributional peace the cycle itself disturbs. A candidate who can state the objection and the contour of the replies has evaluated at the level the scholarship sets, whatever verdict follows.

### How should a candidate handle the Okishio theorem in an answer?

Introduce it as the strongest logical objection to the falling rate of profit, state its premise, that only cost-reducing techniques are adopted, and its result, a higher equilibrium rate at a constant real wage. Then name the temporal valuation reply fairly, and withhold final settlement, since the dispute turns on contested readings of the value theory itself.

The second tier of objections targets the theory of value beneath the law. From Bortkiewicz onward, critics argued that Marx's transformation of values into prices of production is internally inconsistent, and Steedman's restatement concluded that price and profit can be computed from physical data without the value detour, making the whole apparatus redundant. Whether or not a given paper expects that depth, one accurate sentence acknowledging the critique and its temporal single-system answer signals a candidate reading beyond the textbook. A third line of evaluation comes from inside the house: Robert Brenner's account of modern downturns relocates the mechanism to overcapacity generated by uneven international competition, in which established producers cannot exit and new entrants will not stop, so profitability sags through rivalry rather than through the composition of capital. Brenner matters examinably because he accepts the phenomenon, a long profitability downturn, while replacing the mechanism, which lets a candidate compare two Marxist explanations of the same episode instead of rehearsing Marx against a straw orthodoxy.

Rival frameworks from outside the tradition complete the arsenal, and the brief for this guide is explicit that mainstream business-cycle explanations are legitimate evaluation material, to be written as serious rivals rather than as errors awaiting correction. Keynesian effective demand theory has already served in the comparison section. Austrian accounts, in which artificially cheap credit distorts the structure of production toward lengths the savings of the public cannot sustain, offer a genuinely contrary anatomy of the boom, blaming the credit system for falsifying proportions, and a candidate may set that beside disproportionality to show two theories agreeing on the symptom and dividing on the cause. Real business cycle theory stands furthest away, treating fluctuations as optimal responses to real shocks, principally in technology, and its examinable use is as the limiting case: the framework for which endogenous crisis proneness is not a finding but an impossibility, against which the Marxist claim can be stated in its sharpest form. Schumpeter's creative destruction deserves a sentence as the near neighbour, a theory from inside the celebration of capitalism that nonetheless makes crisis the mechanism of its renewal, uncomfortably close to the purge logic of the profit rate family.

## Concluding against a family, and still scoring full marks

The neutrality of this guide is not a posture; it is a description of how marking works, and this section exists to prove it with worked demonstrations rather than assurances. A candidate may conclude that a Marxist crisis family fails, may conclude that several fail, may conclude that the entire research programme misfires, and still take full marks, provided the route to the verdict runs through accurate exposition, the strongest objections, fairly stated replies, and a conclusion whose strength matches the surviving evidence. Markers are not employed to police conclusions. They are employed to discriminate among qualities of argument, and an examiner who penalised a well-built hostile verdict would be unable to defend the mark against the scheme, which on this topic credits the operations rather than the destination.

Watch the demonstration run against the falling rate of profit, the family candidates most fear to oppose. The script expounds the law with its formula and its worked numbers, presents the counteracting influences honestly, and then builds the hostile case in order: the Okishio result against the mechanism's logic; the simultaneist critique against the value framework beneath it; the measurement record, in which the long-run behaviour of measured profit rates depends so heavily on valuation choices that the law's defenders and critics read opposite trends off the same century; and the historical awkwardness that the theory's showcase collapses, the eighteen seventies and the nineteen thirties and two thousand eight, each admit powerful rival anatomies. The replies are then given their paragraph: temporal valuation against Okishio, the falling measured rate on historical-cost accounting in Kliman's series, the point that rival anatomies explain triggers better than vulnerabilities. The verdict follows at the strength the exchange supports: that the law as a universal tendency is not sustained, that its logic requires a contested theory of value at every joint, and that its durable contribution is the research question it bequeathed, why accumulation should recurrently undermine its own profitability. Every operation in that script is creditable, the exposition accurate, the objections the strongest available, the replies neither hidden nor caricatured, and the conclusion, though wholly negative toward the family, is worth every mark the question carries.

Run the demonstration in the opposite direction against underconsumption, for candidates whose sympathies run the other way. The script expounds the realisation problem and Luxemburg's radicalisation, then presses the objections: the reproduction schemes balance without external buyers, so the necessity claim fails as arithmetic; Marx's own text mocks the notion that higher wages would abolish crises, since rising wages squeeze the surplus the system exists to produce; and the family's chronic condition cannot time the cycle's turns. Replies are heard: the schemes show possibility, not actuality, and actual accumulation leaned on credit, arms, and empire; the wage mockery targets a panacea, not the structural narrowness of consumption Marx himself names as the ultimate ground of crisis. The verdict, that underconsumption survives as a standing condition and a component of crises but fails as their sufficient theory, is a hostile verdict against the family's strong form, and it is likewise worth full marks. A third demonstration, against the plurality habit itself, belongs here in miniature: a candidate may argue that the families share so much machinery that the rivalry is overstated, and that one underlying account of overaccumulation wears several costumes. That too can score in full, if the shared machinery is demonstrated rather than asserted. The destination is free. The road is marked.

## The measurement quarrel and the empirical record

Every family eventually faces the same tribunal: the numbers. Profit rates can be estimated for the long run in the advanced economies, and they have been, by scholars who disagree about nearly every construction choice the estimation requires. Should capital be valued at historical cost, what it was bought for, or at current replacement cost, which flatters the denominator in inflationary times and can turn a falling series into a flat one? Should the labour counted as productive be confined to the sectors Marx's categories suggest, or spread across the whole economy? How should the growing weight of finance, whose profits are real enough in the accounts, be handled inside a theory built for industrial capital? The quarrels are not pedantry. On one influential construction, associated with Andrew Kliman, the American rate of profit falls across the postwar period with only partial recoveries; on another, associated with Gérard Duménil and Dominique Lévy, the rate sags into the nineteen seventies and then recovers a large part of its height under the distributional counter-revolution that followed. Both series are competently built. Their divergence is the lesson, and the dedicated examination of it sits in the article on the [rate of profit empirical evidence](/2013/12/01/rate-of-profit-empirical-evidence/), which candidates should treat as the evidence caveat attached to every empirical sentence they write.

The examinable discipline follows directly. Never assert, in an answer, that profit rates have fallen, full stop, as though reporting a thermometer. Assert instead that measured rates behave in a stated way on a stated construction, or that scholars dispute the trend and the dispute turns on valuation, and the sentence becomes unassailable. Examiners set traps precisely here, because the flat assertion is the signature of the candidate who has read conclusions rather than arguments. The same discipline extends to the other families: the wage share's behaviour across a chosen decade, the consumption share's long stability in the national accounts against crude underconsumption, the sectoral data behind disproportion. In each case the creditable sentence names the evidence, names its construction or its source tradition, and states the conclusion at the strength the construction can bear. Evidence literacy of that order is rare enough at undergraduate level to be worth explicit rehearsal, and the candidates who practise it convert the empirical sections of their answers from liabilities into some of their highest-scoring paragraphs.

## The small mathematics kit

Candidates ask, in consultations and forums, whether the mathematics of this topic can hurt them, and the honest answer is that the kit is tiny and entirely friendly. Three ratios exhaust the formal core. The rate of profit divides surplus value by the sum of constant and variable capital. The rate of exploitation divides surplus value by variable capital alone, expressing the split of the working day between paid and unpaid labour. The organic composition divides constant by variable capital, expressing how much dead labour each unit of living labour must animate. Every proposition in the falling rate family is a claim about how these three move against one another under accumulation, and a candidate who can restate the law as a sentence about the ratios, if the third rises while the second is bounded, the first is pressed down, possesses the theory in its most portable form.

### How much mathematics does a crisis answer need?

Very little, and all of it friendly: the three ratios, one worked numerical illustration of the profit rate under a rising composition, and the ability to say what changes when exploitation moves instead. Derivations, calculus, and simultaneous equation systems belong to the scholarly disputes behind the topic, not to its examination answers.

Two elaborations reward the candidate who adds them. The mass of profit moves with total capital as well as with the rate, so a falling rate is compatible with rising total profit and rising total employment of capital, which is why the theory predicts strain and competition rather than immediate paralysis; an answer that confuses the two magnitudes will be corrected by any competent marker. And turnover matters at the margin: capital that completes its circuit faster realises its surplus more often within the year, raising the annual rate above the rate per circuit, a refinement worth one clause in a strong answer and no more. Beyond those points, mathematical display in a crisis answer has diminishing returns, and candidates who fill pages with notation while the evaluation waits unwritten have mistaken the topic's costume for its body. The examiners of this subject, across its assessment types, are grading economic reasoning conducted in prose; the ratios are there to keep the prose honest.

## The trap catalogue, in prose

Examiners' reports across assessment types name the same failures with a weariness that should instruct every candidate, and this section walks the catalogue slowly, one trap per paragraph, because naming a failure in advance is the cheapest insurance against committing it. Each trap below is a habit, not an ignorance: candidates fall into them while knowing better, under pressure, which is why the insurance has to be taken out during revision rather than during the examination.

The first trap is the single-theory presentation itself, the answer that treats one family as the doctrine. Its root is the revision literature, which simplifies for portability, and its cost has been the subject of this guide from the first paragraph. The second trap confuses overproduction with underconsumption, sliding between output that cannot be sold and demand that cannot grow as though the words were interchangeable. They are not: one names the glut, the other names a theory of the glut's origin, and disproportionality and profitability accounts also predict gluts, so the slide silently abandons the plurality the answer worked to build. The third trap asserts that profit rates have fallen, as a settled measurement, when the record is a construction-dependent quarrel; the repair, stated in the empirical section, is to name the construction or name the dispute. The fourth trap reaches for a contemporary economic condition as evidence, a habit that dates the answer, invites the marker's own views into the grading, and violates the discipline of this entire series, which uses historical episodes as examples precisely because their records are closed and documented. The fifth trap writes that Marx predicted collapse, converting tendencies into a terminus the texts refuse; Grossmann built the nearest thing to a breakdown theory a generation after Marx, and attributing his construction to the founder is an anachronism markers notice. The sixth trap narrates the financial crash, or whatever episode the candidate knows best, for pages, while the mechanism the episode was meant to test waits offstage; its repair is the narrative ration imposed in the fifth worked answer.

Two quieter traps complete the catalogue. The seventh is the vanishing countertendency, the exposition of the falling rate of profit from which Marx's own counteracting influences have evaporated, leaving a law cruder than its author wrote; it has its own section above because it is the commonest single content error on the topic. The eighth is asymmetric charity, in which the candidate's favoured family receives its strongest form and every rival receives a caricature, or in which mainstream frameworks are introduced only to be dismissed as apologetics. Markers read asymmetry as a confession that the candidate cannot afford fairness, and they mark accordingly. The whole catalogue reduces to one sentence worth memorising: every trap is a way of writing less than one knows, and the defence against all of them is the discipline of stating each position, including one's own conclusion, at exactly its defensible strength.

## Decoding the command words on crisis questions

Command words are the contract printed at the head of the question, and crisis papers use a small set whose meanings candidates chronically blur. Explain purchases exposition with mechanism: the marker wants the causal chain laid out so that a reader can see the parts move, and neither verdict nor rebuttal is owed beyond a sentence of orientation. Explain the falling rate of profit is therefore the second worked answer's assignment, and the candidate who spends its second half attacking the law has answered a different question at the cost of the one set. Assess and evaluate purchase judgement with grounds: exposition shrinks to what the judgement needs, the objections and replies take the stage, and a verdict of some kind is owed by the end, since an assess answer that never assesses has defaulted on the contract. Compare purchases structured juxtaposition: the two objects handled under shared headings, mechanism, evidence, remedy, so that similarities and differences emerge from the architecture rather than from a concluding paragraph that asserts them. Discuss, the loosest command, purchases an organised exploration with a position arrived at, and it forgives more range than the others while punishing mere summary exactly as they do.

Short commands deserve a sentence of their own, because candidates overbuild them. Distinguish purchases the difference and nothing else: underconsumption from overproduction, tendency from law, rate from mass, each in a handful of sentences whose entire virtue is precision. Outline purchases the skeleton without the flesh, and the candidate who writes the full essay under an outline command has starved the paper's later questions of time. To what extent, finally, is not a command but a calibration device built into the question, and it purchases a verdict expressed as a degree: largely, in the weak sense but not the strong, for one family and against another. The graded verdicts modelled in this guide are the native answer form for that phrasing, and the candidate who has rehearsed two-level verdicts, yes to recurrence and no to terminus, owns the form already.

## A grading clinic on openings

Abstract advice about openings convinces nobody, so consider three openings to the same assess question, graded in the margin as a marker would grade them. The first announces that Marx believed capitalism would collapse because profits fall as machines replace workers, and that the essay will show he was right. A marker reads a misstatement in the first clause, since the law is a tendency with countertendencies and no collapse timetable; a borrowed verdict in the second, since the conclusion precedes the evidence; and a single family where the question invited the field. The script can recover, but it begins two bands down and must climb. The second opening announces that Marxist crisis theory contains several accounts, of which the essay will describe the falling rate of profit and underconsumption before concluding that both have strengths and weaknesses. The plurality is present and the recovery is under way, yet the marker notes the survey promised without adjudication, strengths and weaknesses being the vocabulary of the undecided, and predicts a tour. The script will score respectably and stop there.

The third opening announces that Marx left no unified theory of crisis; that the answer will test the two families with the strongest textual anchorage, the profitability account of the third volume and the realisation account descending from Sismondi, against the evidence each claims and the objections each must survive; and that it will argue the coordination failure visible in every crisis is best explained as the form those deeper pressures take. Plurality, selection, method, and a thesis, in three sentences, each of which the body can now cash. The marker's expectation has been set at the top band, and, what matters more, the candidate has been set a task list: the opening is a plan the candidate wrote for an audience of one marker and one self. Practise by rewriting weak openings rather than by writing whole essays, since the opening carries the architecture and the architecture carries the mark; twenty openings rewritten teach more than four essays completed, and the exercise fits the margins of a revision timetable where essays do not.

## Planning under the clock

Time pressure converts good candidates into summarists, and the conversion happens in the first five minutes, when the plan is skipped. The discipline this guide recommends is a seven-minute architecture, scaled to the paper's length, in which the candidate writes, in telegraphic shorthand, the opening's plurality sentence, the families in the order the question rewards, the anchor and the worked element for each, the strongest objection per family, and the shape of the verdict, two-level if the evidence will split that way. Seven minutes feels expensive and is the cheapest purchase in the room, because every subsequent paragraph is then written toward a known destination, and the conclusion, which unplanned scripts compose in a panic, has existed since the plan. Candidates who fear the clock should rehearse the architecture on past questions with a timer visible, discovering empirically that planned scripts finish faster than unplanned ones of the same length, since the unplanned script spends its time in hesitations the plan has already absorbed.

Proportion inside the plan follows the command word. For explain questions, give the mechanisms three quarters of the script and evaluation one quarter, staged as orientation rather than warfare. For assess questions, invert toward balance, exposition compressed to what the gauntlet needs. For comparisons, reserve the final fifth for the comparative verdict, without which the juxtaposition remains two essays stapled together. Episode questions cap narrative at a tenth. These ratios are heuristics, not law, and the candidate who treats them as starting allocations to be adjusted by the question's wording will outperform both the candidate who ignores proportion and the candidate who worships it. The deeper principle is that a marker should be able to predict, from any paragraph, what the next paragraph is for; when that predictability holds, the script reads as an argument, and arguments are what the bands describe.

## Short answers and the objective forms

Not every encounter with this topic is an essay. Eligibility tests and school papers carry short and objective forms, and they punish a different weakness: the candidate who knows the essay but cannot compress. The two-mark distinction, the five-mark explanation, the multiple-choice item that offers four mechanisms and asks which a named thinker held, all reward the same underlying asset, the family map held precisely enough to survive miniaturisation. Revise for these forms by building one-sentence mechanisms, a discipline stricter than it sounds. The falling rate of profit in one sentence: accumulation raises the mass of means of production per worker faster than the living labour that produces surplus, pressing the ratio of profit to total capital downward. Realisation in one sentence: the wage structure that minimises costs also restricts the consumption that must validate production, leaving investment to bridge a gap it periodically refuses to bridge. Disproportionality in one sentence: uncoordinated investment discovers its required proportions only after capital is sunk, and corrects the error through generalised glut. Squeeze in one sentence: mature expansions drain the unemployment reserve, wages outrun productivity, and the profit share is compressed until investment breaks. A candidate who can produce those four sentences cold owns the objective paper outright.

Short forms also carry their own traps, mirror images of the essay traps. The definition that smuggles in a verdict wastes its marks; the distinction that explains both terms at length and never states the difference wastes more. Objective items exploit the confusions this guide has catalogued: overproduction offered as a synonym for underconsumption, Grossmann's breakdown offered as Marx's own prediction, the countertendencies offered as critics' objections rather than as Marx's chapter. Each confusion is a designed distractor, and the candidate who has met the traps in prose recognises the design. There is a final consolation for the essay-trained candidate facing objective forms: the plurality habit, so valuable in the essay, is equally valuable here, since the items are built by examiners who hold the plurality and who construct their distractors out of its denial.

## Revising the families as one system

Revision for this topic fails when the families are learned as five separate essays and succeeds when they are learned as one system of disagreements. The efficient instrument is the family card, a single page per family carrying five entries in the candidate's own shorthand: the mechanism in one sentence, the anchor text or formula, the best evidence, the hardest objection, and the standard reply with its weakness. Five cards, reviewed in rotating order, build the comparative reflex the essays reward, because the mind begins to hold the families simultaneously rather than serially. Add a sixth card for the counteracting influences and a seventh for the formula kit, and the topic's entire examinable core fits in a folder that can be reviewed in an hour. The wider sequence into which these cards slot, across the whole Marxism syllabus, is mapped in the [marxism revision guide](/2022/01/15/marxism-revision-guide/), whose architecture this topic's cards are designed to join.

Method matters as much as materials. Retrieval practice, the attempt to reproduce a card's contents before checking them, builds the availability that the examination room actually tests, while rereading builds only familiarity, which evaporates under pressure. Interleaving matters too: questions attempted in mixed order, a squeeze item following a realisation item, force the discrimination the examiners are probing, whereas blocked practice, ten questions on one family, trains recognition of the block rather than of the question. Timed skeletons are the third instrument: the seven-minute architecture of the earlier section, executed on a fresh question in plan form only, twice a week, until the plan's five elements assemble themselves in the order the command word requires. Candidates routinely report that the topic felt enormous in October of their course and small by the examination, and the contraction is not the material shrinking but the system forming: five families, six countertendencies, three ratios, four question types, one opening. That inventory, held whole, is what mastery means at this level, and it is genuinely attainable, which candidates in the grip of the single-theory resources, facing a topic with no edges, cannot be blamed for doubting.

## Anchors and where the texts actually live

An anchor is a located passage a candidate can cite with confidence, and crisis answers need few of them, well chosen. The primary anchor is the third volume's sequence on the law of the tendency: the chapter stating the law, the chapter of counteracting influences, and the chapter on the internal contradictions, where the argument opens out into credit, overproduction, and the famous compression of the whole theory into the observation that the real barrier of capitalist production is capital itself. That sentence, quoted exactly, is the one quotation this topic needs, because it states the family's deepest claim, that the barrier is internal and self-produced, in the author's own words. Candidates should know it belongs to the chapter on internal contradictions and should quote it once, in the profitability context, rather than scattering it as an epigraph. One quotation per source and per answer remains the discipline; a script studded with quotations reads as a scrapbook, and markers credit the candidate's prose, not the anthology.

The secondary anchors distribute across the families. The second volume's reproduction chapters anchor disproportionality, and the candidate need cite only their existence and their departmental construction, since no examiner expects the schemes reproduced entire. The manuscript on the history of theories anchors the realisation debates, where Marx works through the crisis arguments of Ricardo's school and its critics and clarifies, against both, why the possibility of crisis in simple circulation is only a possibility until the conditions of capitalist production are added. The notebooks of the Grundrisse anchor the monetary family's pedigree, the crisis of eighteen fifty seven having provoked the writing itself. Engels's editorial role in the third volume deserves its sentence wherever the anchor is load-bearing, because acknowledging that the law's chapters were assembled from drafts by another hand is both true and, in an examination, a mark of the textual command this guide keeps identifying as the topic's real currency. Anchors are not ornaments. Each one certifies that a mechanism has an address, and an answer with addresses can be trusted in a way an answer of floating summaries cannot.

## Symmetry, or writing the rivals as carefully as the theory

Part of what the higher bands certify is fairness under disagreement, and fairness is a technique before it is a virtue. The technique is to state every rival in the form its own defenders would accept before any criticism is attempted, a discipline sometimes called steelmanning, and its examination value is brutally practical: a criticism aimed at the strong form of a position earns credit even when the marker shares the position, while a criticism aimed at a caricature earns none even when the marker despises the position. Apply the discipline across the whole field. Keynes is not the thinker who said governments should spend; he is the theorist of investment collapsing against liquidity preference in a monetary economy where the future cannot be calculated. The Austrian is not the thinker who blames meddling; the Austrian holds that credit expansion falsifies the price signals by which the structure of production is coordinated, generating the malinvestment the bust then liquidates. The real business cycle theorist is not naive about instability; the claim is that observed fluctuations largely reflect efficient adjustment to real shocks, a claim with econometric machinery behind it. Each of those statements can be written by a candidate who rejects every one of the positions, and writing them is what licences the rejection that follows.

Symmetry also governs the treatment inside the tradition. The underconsumptionist is not the soft-hearted cousin of the profitability theorist; the realisation problem is a genuine theorem-shaped difficulty about who buys the surplus, and Luxemburg's version of it drove a research programme in economic history. The squeeze theorist is not a trade union pamphleteer; the Glyn and Sutcliffe construction was an econometric argument about the profit share with international constraints specified. Even Grossmann, the tradition's breakdown maximalist, built his model as a rigorous extension of a reproduction scheme, and his failure, where it fails, is instructive precisely because the construction was serious. Answers that distribute this respect evenly read as scholarship; answers that reserve it for the candidate's own side read as advocacy, and the bands know the difference even when the candidate does not.

## The crash example, under discipline

Because the worked episode answer imposed a narrative ration, this section supplies the complementary discipline: how to choose and prepare the example before the examination, so that the ration in the room costs no thought. Prepare one episode deeply rather than three shallowly, and prepare it as a test-bed rather than as a story. The candidate's notes on the episode should be organised by the families, not by chronology: what the profitability record showed in the preceding expansion, how the wage share and household demand behaved, which sectors visibly outgrew their proportions, how the credit structure layered claims, and where the break first appeared. Organised that way, the episode arrives in the examination pre-analysed, and the answer's work is selection among ready judgements rather than improvisation among half-remembered headlines. The financial crash rewards this preparation best, because its documentation is vast and its sectoral and monetary anatomy is unusually legible, which is why the series gives it an article of its own and why this guide keeps returning to it.

Alternative episodes insure against a question wording the favourite fits badly. The crisis of the eighteen seventies suits questions about the profitability family's pedigree, arriving as it did in the decades when the law's chapters were drafted and revised. The slump of the nineteen thirties suits the Keynes comparison and the realisation family, since mass unemployment beside idle capacity and unsold goods is the demand failure made visible, and since every framework of the period was reforged in its heat. The downturns of the nineteen seventies suit squeeze and its rivals, the decade of the profit share's compression, distributional conflict, and supply shocks that let a candidate practise disentangling mechanisms that coincide. A candidate holding one deep episode and two serviceable ones can meet any wording the paper offers. What no wording licenses is the contemporary case, assembled from journalism and argued from conditions still unfolding; the record is not closed, the documentation is not settled, and the examiners' own framing of this series forbids it, a prohibition candidates should experience as liberation, since the historical cases are richer in every examinable respect.

## Conclusions matched to evidence

The conclusion is the paragraph candidates most often write at the wrong strength, inflating a body's worth of mixed findings into a trumpet blast or deflating a strong body into a shrug. The rule that governs it can be stated in one line: the conclusion may claim exactly what the strongest surviving element of the body established, in the vocabulary of that element, and nothing beyond. If the body's gauntlet left the coordination family standing and the others dented, the conclusion is a sentence about coordination, with the others' partial survivals acknowledged in subordinate clauses. If the body showed that one objection, Okishio's, survives its replies in part, the conclusion is partial in the same place. This mirroring is not timidity; markers experience it as the answer keeping its accounts, and they trust every preceding paragraph more because the last one refuses to steal.

Vocabulary does the fine work. Verbs and hedges form a ladder the candidate should climb deliberately: a mechanism is consistent with an episode, supports an interpretation of it, discriminates between two readings, or establishes a claim, and those are four different strengths, not synonyms. The phrases that signal calibration to a marker are unglamorous and learnable: on the construction measured, for the decades examined, as a theory of timing rather than of tendency, in its weak form. Practise by taking conclusions from one's own drafts and demoting every claim by one rung, then checking whether the body still supports the demoted version; where it does not, the body has a hole the examination would have found. The exercise feels like loss and is actually the conversion of rhetoric into credit, since an overstated conclusion taxes the whole script, while a conclusion priced exactly at the evidence reads as the signature of a mind the marker can trust with the topic's hardest questions.

## Combining families without incoherence

Strong candidates eventually ask whether the families can be combined, since actual crises seem to exhibit all their symptoms at once: profitability sagging, sectors disproportionate, demand faltering, finance collapsing. The question is legitimate and dangerous in equal measure. Combination done by accumulation, a paragraph of each with a concluding gesture at complexity, produces the survey failure in a new costume. Combination done by assignment, in which each family is given a level or a phase and the relations among them are argued, produces the most sophisticated structure available on this topic. Assign levels: profitability as the slow variable that conditions the cycle's vulnerability, proportion as the anatomy through which the disturbance spreads, credit as the amplifier and timer, realisation as the form the collapse of demand takes once investment breaks, distribution as the contested terrain on which the recovery's terms are fought. Each family now has a job, the jobs do not collide, and the answer can defend the assignment itself as its thesis.

The defence matters, because the eclecticism objection is real: the families were built as rivals, several of their authors explicitly against one another, and a synthesis that ignores their quarrels will be read as evasion. The answer that earns the synthesis names the quarrels it is overriding and prices the override, noting for instance that squeeze and underconsumption cannot both be the cycle's prime mover on the wage question, so the synthesis demotes at least one of them to a phase or a condition, and says which, and why. Grossmann stands at the edge of this section as its cautionary monument: his attempt to derive breakdown from a single scheme, extended until the accumulation fund mathematically failed, shows both the ambition of unification and its price, since the price was a rigidity no defender could maintain against the countertendencies. The lesson for candidates is not to fear combination but to invoice it. Every act of synthesis must declare what it subordinates, and the declaration is where the marks are.

## A paragraph clinic

Abstract faults are hard to expel; visible ones are easier, so this section puts three common paragraph diseases on the table in miniature and describes the repair of each. The first disease is assertion without mechanism, whose symptom sentence runs that capitalism inevitably produces crises because of its contradictions. The sentence is not false within the tradition; it is empty, since contradictions names the topic rather than explaining it, and the repair is substitution of machinery for motto: which contradiction, between what and what, operating through which variable, with what observable consequence. Two sentences of machinery convert the motto into the start of an answer. The second disease is summary without location, the paragraph that recounts a thinker's view in fine detail and never says where the view sits in the field, what it denies, or which family it extends. The repair is a single comparative clause, and candidates underestimate how completely one clause, noting that the position relocates the blockage from production to circulation, redeems a page of summary by giving the marker the map reference the summary lacked.

The third disease is the verdict without a receipt, the conclusion that announces a family's failure while the body recorded only its exposition and a rumour of controversy. The repair is structural and must happen in the plan: every verdict noun in the conclusion must be traceable to an objection actually stated and a reply actually weighed, and candidates can audit their drafts with a pencil, drawing the line from conclusion back to evidence, deleting any claim the line cannot reach. That audit, performed twice on practice scripts, installs the habit the examiners call substantiation. Notice that all three repairs are cheap: a substitution, a clause, an audit. The distance between the middle bands and the top ones on this topic is rarely knowledge, which the middle bands often possess in bulk. It is these small disciplines of attachment, every claim tied down to a mechanism, a location, or a piece of evidence, that the top scripts perform paragraph by paragraph until the marker stops checking.

## Coursework, dissertations, and the long form

Everything taught so far scales, and the candidate facing coursework or a dissertation chapter on crisis theory should know how the proportions change when length is generous. The plurality opening becomes a literature architecture: the families are now literatures, each with founders, refinements, and internal schisms, and the long form is marked on the quality of the map before it is marked on any verdict. The worked numbers become engagement with the empirical literature at first hand, where the measurement quarrels of the earlier section must be handled with actual series and their constructors named, Duménil and Lévy against Kliman against the national accounts traditions, rather than gestured at. The evaluation becomes a dialogue in which replies receive the same space as objections, and in which the candidate's own contribution is typically a discrimination: a demonstration that two families, believed rivals, are answering different questions, or that one celebrated objection targets a version of the law its best defenders never held.

Two warnings belong to the long form specifically. Length licenses depth but also invites the indefinite postponement of the verdict, and markers of long work penalise the survey that never lands as firmly as examiners penalise its timed cousin; the adjudication should be announced in the introduction's final paragraph and then earned, not discovered by the writer in the final chapter. And the long form tempts quotation: pages of the founder's prose, deployed as authority. Resist it with the same discipline as the timed script, one quotation per source per argument, chosen at the joints of the reasoning. A dissertation that quotes the barrier sentence once, in the right place, and builds a chapter on its implications will outscore the anthology every time, because the credit attaches to the building.

## The scholarship behind the simplification

Researchers and advanced candidates sometimes ask what the examinable families leave out, and the answer is a scholarly landscape stranger and richer than the syllabus suggests. Henryk Grossmann's nineteen twenty nine construction, built by extending Otto Bauer's reproduction scheme until the surplus available for accumulation mathematically failed, remains the most rigorous breakdown model ever produced inside the tradition, and its subsequent fate, admired, attacked, and quietly abandoned by most of the tradition itself, is a case study in the difference between a tendency and a terminus. Paul Mattick carried a version of the profitability account through the middle of the century almost single-handed, insisting against Keynesian and underconsumptionist fashions that the law of value set limits no demand management could repeal. Roman Rosdolsky's reconstruction of the Grundrisse's place in Marx's plans unsettled every simple story about which texts the crisis theory should be read from, and Clarke's work pressed the unsettling further, arguing that crisis is where the whole theory of capital proves itself or fails, not a department within it.

The editorial scholarship adds a final layer. Work on the manuscripts behind the third volume, pursued in the historical-critical edition of the complete writings, has shown how much construction separates Marx's drafts from the chapters generations of readers memorised: passages reordered, transitions supplied, a provisional exploration hardened by print into a doctrine. Heinrich's conclusion from that evidence is the most unsettling available to a candidate, that the law chapters in their published form overstate the systematic confidence of the manuscripts beneath them. A candidate need not import that scholarship into a timed answer wholesale. Its examinable yield is a single, powerful sentence of perspective: that the plurality of crisis theory is not a failure of Marx's followers to agree but a faithful reflection of material that was itself exploratory, contested by its author across decades, and left open. Sentences of that quality, placed at the end of an evaluation, are remembered by markers after the script is closed.

## Synthesis, the answer the whole guide has been building

Draw the threads together into the stance a candidate should carry into the room. Crisis theory, as the texts left it and the tradition developed it, is a field of rival mechanisms with addresses: profitability in the third volume, proportion in the second, realisation in the debates the history-of-theory manuscript preserves, distribution in the literature of the postwar decades, finance in the money chapters and their successors. The candidate's craft is a sequence of operations on that field: open by naming its plurality, deploy each family the question engages with its mechanism and its anchor, work one numerical illustration where the profit rate is concerned, restore the counteracting influences wherever the law appears, lead the evaluation with the strongest objection rather than the nearest, write rivals inside and outside the tradition in their strongest forms, ration narrative when episodes serve as evidence, and set the conclusion's strength by the strongest thing the body survived. None of those operations requires brilliance. All of them together are what brilliance looks like from the marker's side of the desk.

There is a last reassurance to give, and it is earned rather than sentimental. Candidates fear this topic because the single-theory resources leave them nowhere to go when a question turns: the memorised mechanism either fits or the script stalls. The plurality method reverses the fear's basis. Every wording the examiners can devise now arrives as a selection problem the candidate knows how to solve, which families, which anchor, which objection, which verdict strength, and a selection problem is a friendlier thing than a memory test because it can be practised, planned, and improved by the disciplines this guide has drilled. The field is plural. The method is singular. That asymmetry, held firmly, is the whole secret the revision industry never told its customers.

It is worth recording, finally, what this article has deliberately refused to do, since the refusals are part of its method and candidates should be able to name them. It has stated no syllabus code, no paper number, and no mark allocation, because those change and would date the guidance. It has used no contemporary economic condition as evidence, holding its episodes to documented and closed records. And it has delivered no verdict of its own on the families' truth, beyond the verdicts its worked answers modelled for instruction, because an exam guide that smuggled in a party line would betray the neutrality it teaches. The candidate is the one who must conclude, in the room, at the strength the evidence allows.

## Reading a question for its family signals

Questions telegraph their expected families through vocabulary, and the candidate who learns the telegraphy gains marks before writing a sentence of substance. A question speaking of barriers, limits, or the self-obstruction of accumulation is angled toward the profitability family and its barrier sentence, whatever the candidate's preferences. A question speaking of markets, outlets, purchasing power, or the fate of the product is angled toward realisation, and the reproduction schemes will be wanted as the counterweight. Proportions, balance, sectors, and bottlenecks summon disproportionality with the same reliability. Distribution, the wage share, militancy, and full employment summon squeeze. Liquidity, credit, speculation, and panic summon the monetary family and ask what vulnerability the panic priced. A question that names none of these vocabularies, asking baldly whether the system is prone to crisis, is the assess form in its pure state, and it purchases the gauntlet of the third worked answer, all families tested in turn.

The telegraphy has a second layer: the verbs smuggle in the expected strength of claim. A question asking why crises recur purchases mechanism; a question asking whether Marx explained crises purchases the evaluation of mechanisms; a question asking whether a named episode confirms a theory purchases the evidence discipline of the fifth worked answer. Candidates should underline the operative nouns and verbs during the reading minutes and let the underlining choose the architecture, because most disappointing scripts on this topic are good answers to adjacent questions, the candidate's favourite family expounded while the paper's signals pointed elsewhere. There is a humility in this discipline worth naming: the examination is a conversation the examiner started, and the plurality method's flexibility exists precisely so the candidate can join the conversation that was actually opened rather than delivering the lecture that was prepared at home.

## The two circuits as an answer scaffold

Underneath the family quarrels sits a piece of architecture every candidate can use as scaffolding, the distinction between the production of surplus value and its realisation in circulation. Marx's circuit of capital moves through purchase, production, and sale, and the families sort themselves, at a first approximation, by the stage at which they locate the characteristic failure. The profitability family locates it in production, where the composition of capital governs how much surplus a given advance can extract. The realisation and disproportionality families locate it in circulation, the one in the aggregate demand that must validate the product, the other in the proportions among branches that the exchanges between departments must satisfy. Squeeze locates it at the boundary, in the wage bargain that precedes production and distributes its proceeds in advance. The monetary family locates it in the form that binds the stages, money and credit, whose breakdown halts every stage at once.

The scaffold earns its place in an answer because it converts the plurality from a list into a map, and maps are what markers mean by structure. An opening that names the plurality can immediately offer the map in one sentence, the families disagreeing about where in the circuit the failure originates, and every subsequent paragraph then has coordinates the reader already holds. The scaffold also generates comparisons unaided: production-side and circulation-side accounts differ in what they predict about the boom's final phase, in whether the pre-crash economy should show strained profitability or swollen inventories, and a candidate can adjudicate between families by asking which signature the historical record of a given episode displays. Teach the scaffold as the answer's skeleton and the families stop being five essays competing for memory; they become five positions on one diagram, and diagrams are what the mind retains under pressure.

## Breakdown and crisis are different words

No distinction protects a candidate more reliably than the one between crisis and breakdown, because conflating them is the error on which hostile caricatures of the whole tradition are built. Crisis, in every family this guide treats, is a recurrent event internal to the system's functioning, violent, destructive, and followed, through the destruction itself, by restored conditions for accumulation. Breakdown is a terminal event, the point at which the contradictions become unmanageable in principle and the system cannot resume. Marx's texts are saturated with the first concept and strikingly reticent about the second; the law of the tendency names counteracting influences in the same breath as the tendency, the crisis chapters describe restoration through devaluation, and the founder's journalism treats each successive crash as an episode in a continuing history rather than as the overture to a finale. The breakdown construction belongs to the tradition's later systematists, Grossmann above all, whose model drove a reproduction scheme forward until the arithmetic of the accumulation fund gave out, and whose work the tradition itself largely declined to canonise.

Examiners probe the distinction because it separates candidates who have read the texts from candidates who have read the polemics. A question may invite the conflation directly, asking whether Marx predicted the collapse of capitalism, and the creditable answer is a small masterpiece of discrimination: Marx predicted crises, recurrent and necessary on his account; he expected the system's supersession as a political and historical judgement woven through his work; and he nowhere derived a mechanical terminus from the crisis theory, the nearest derivations being later constructions whose fate inside the tradition was rejection as often as adoption. Each clause of that answer is a mark. The same discrimination disciplines the candidate's own conclusions everywhere else, since an assess answer that ends by granting proneness to crisis while refusing the terminus has applied the distinction to itself, and markers recognise the self-application as the surest sign the distinction was understood rather than recited.

## The seventies as a laboratory

One decade serves this topic the way a laboratory serves a science, because within it the rival families can be watched competing over the same events with the evidence unusually rich. The long postwar expansion ended in a cluster of disturbances: profitability sagging in the advanced economies from the middle nineteen sixties on several constructions, wage militancy rising as unemployment stayed low, the international monetary order built at Bretton Woods collapsing, and then the oil price shocks that hostile and friendly accounts alike must somehow digest. Profit squeeze was practically invented in this laboratory, Glyn and Sutcliffe reading the British profit share's compression as the squeeze of organised labour between its own strength and foreign competition. Brenner's overcapacity reading was also forged here and in the decades after, locating the sag in manufacturing rivalry among the United States, Germany, and Japan, where sunk capacity could not exit and new capacity would not stop entering. Profitability scholars of the Kliman school read the same decades as the law of the tendency working through an incomplete purge, the destruction of capital values having been repeatedly softened by state rescue, leaving the underlying rate unrestored.

For the candidate, the laboratory's value is that it defeats lazy adjudication. The oil shocks supply every family with its hardest question, since an external blow to costs sits awkwardly inside theories of endogenous failure, and the creditable answer does not pretend otherwise: it asks what the shock struck, a system whose profitability and distribution were already strained, and distinguishes the trigger from the vulnerability in exactly the manner the monetary family teaches. An assess answer that runs its gauntlet through the seventies, grading squeeze strong on the distributional record and weak on the subsequent decades of labour weakness, disproportionality partial, the profitability families divided by their measurement quarrels, has done something better than reach a verdict. It has shown the marker a mind using history the way the discipline uses it, as the contested ground on which mechanisms are weighed, and that display is portable to any episode the paper might name instead.

## The wage question, where the families collide

If one variable deserves a section of its own, it is the wage, because the wage is where the families' contradictions become impossible to paper over and therefore where examinations are won. Consider the collisions in sequence. Underconsumption wants wages higher, since mass purchasing power is its deficient variable, and its policy shadow is redistribution toward consumption. Squeeze wants the wage's growth named as the problem, since the profit share is its deficient variable, and its shadow is the restoration of the reserve army's discipline. The profitability family treats the wage ambivalently in a way candidates find slippery until it is stated plainly: a lower wage raises the rate of exploitation and so resists the tendency, which is why depression of wages appears among Marx's own counteracting influences, while the same lower wage tightens the realisation constraint the demand family watches. The wage is thus a lever that moves two mechanisms in opposite directions at once, and any answer that pulls it in one direction without acknowledging the other has simplified the field past the point of accuracy.

That collision is a gift to the prepared candidate, because it manufactures evaluation on demand. Stage the two demand and distribution families against each other over any concrete episode and the analysis writes its middle paragraphs: the squeeze reading of the seventies against the underconsumptionist reading of the decades that followed, when wage shares fell, household debt replaced wage growth as the engine of demand, and the realisation problem returned wearing financial dress. The candidate can then adjudicate with the discipline this guide teaches, asking which family's deficient variable the episode actually displays, and noting that the two accounts, enemies on the wage question, agree on the deeper premise that distribution is not a technical residue but a fought-over determinant of stability. Agreement at the level of premise between theories that collide at the level of mechanism is a sophisticated observation, the kind examiners quote to one another, and it is available only to the candidate who learned the families as a system of disagreements rather than as a shelf of separate doctrines.

## The matrix under stress, three misfires and their repairs

Instruments fail in characteristic ways, and a candid guide names its own instrument's failures before the examination does. The first misfire is row capture: the candidate meets a question that resembles a matrix row, reproduces the row's contents in order, and produces an answer whose competence is real but whose ceiling is set by the resemblance, since assess questions wearing an explain row's clothing are common. The repair is the command-word check of the earlier section, run after the row is chosen and before the plan is written, asking what the verb purchases that the row does not already contain. The second misfire is anchor drift, in which the candidate cites the formula for a realisation answer or the reproduction schemes for a squeeze answer, anchors wandering from their families under the pressure of recall. The repair is the family card discipline, anchor bound to mechanism on a single page during revision, so that in the room the pair is recalled as one object. The third misfire is trap inversion: the candidate, warned against narrating the episode, omits the episode's evidence altogether and evaluates in a vacuum, or, warned against the single theory, refuses to expound any family at working depth. Every trap in the matrix names an excess, and the failure mode of the warned candidate is the opposite excess; the repair in both directions is proportion, the ratios of the planning section, which exist precisely to hold the middle the traps flank.

There is a fourth, quieter misfire that deserves naming because it afflicts the strongest candidates: the matrix, thoroughly learned, can make its owner impatient with questions that reward slowness, the unusual wording that conceals a simple exposition task, or the cross-topic question in which crisis theory is one guest among several, beside the state or the party or imperialism. The instrument is a servant of the question, and the candidate who re-reads the question once after planning, asking what in it the matrix does not see, has installed the final safeguard. Examination craft at the highest level is largely this: a repertoire of strong instruments, each fitted with the habit of checking itself against the particular case.

## From description to explanation, the verbs of mechanism

Markers distinguish description from explanation with a consistency candidates underestimate, and the difference is carried disproportionately by verbs. Descriptive prose reports that profits fell, that unemployment rose, that banks failed, in the order events occurred. Explanatory prose subordinates those reports to mechanism verbs: the rising composition pressed the rate downward; compressed wages starved the demand that realisation required; credit concealed the disproportion until the money market priced it; the purge of devalued capital restored the denominator's relation to the surplus. A paragraph can be audited for its verbs alone, and candidates should perform that audit on practice work, underlining every main verb and asking whether the paragraph's claims would survive if the verbs were the only evidence of thought. Where the verbs are all occurrences, was, rose, fell, followed, the paragraph is a chronicle. Where the verbs carry causation, pressed, starved, concealed, restored, the same facts have become an argument.

The audit scales into a revision technique. Take a descriptive draft paragraph and rewrite it under the constraint that every sentence must contain a mechanism verb and an explicit variable, composition, wage share, proportion, liquidity, and the paragraph's band rises without a single new fact being added, because the facts were never the deficiency. Examiners' language betrays the same standard from the other side: their reports praise answers that trace, locate, and discriminate, and fault answers that recount, list, and narrate, and the two vocabularies are verb lists before they are anything else. Candidates who internalise the standard stop asking whether they know enough and start asking whether what they know has been put into causal form, which is the question the top band exists to answer.

## The global dimension in an answer

Crisis theory's families were built inside national frames, and the examination candidate who notices the frame has found another seam of credit. Marx's own counteracting influences already break the frame: foreign trade cheapens the elements of constant capital and the wage goods, exports the pressure, and imports fields of higher profitability, so the law's operation in one country is entangled with its trading partners from the start. Luxemburg made the entanglement the whole theory, accumulation feeding on non-capitalist milieux until the planet's conversion closed the frontier. The disproportionality family globalises as supply chains, in which the proportions that must hold now span jurisdictions, currencies, and legal systems, and the discovery of error arrives through exchange rates and shipping costs as well as through gluts. Even squeeze has its international register, since the competition that blocked British firms from passing wage costs into prices in the canonical study was foreign, and the reserve army whose discipline the mechanism requires is now recruited, in part, across borders.

Handled with restraint, the global dimension strengthens answers in two places. In exposition, one sentence acknowledging that each family's variables have cross-border forms signals a candidate aware of the literature's development toward world-market analysis, the very stage of Marx's own unfinished design where crisis was to receive its concrete treatment. In evaluation, the global register supplies a discriminating test: families differ in how naturally they travel, realisation and disproportionality extending almost without friction, squeeze requiring a world labour market its original construction did not assume, and the profitability law raising the formidable question of whether a world rate of profit is even a coherent object. A candidate who raises that question, without pretending to settle it, has found the examination's ceiling and touched it, which is all that any answer can be asked to do.

## A timed rehearsal, from first reading to final sentence

Walk through one examination performance entire, because the method's pieces interlock in time and are best learned in sequence. The question, of the assess family, asks how far Marxist theory explains why capitalist economies suffer recurrent crises. Reading time is spent as the signal section taught: recurrent underlined, because it selects proneness rather than terminus; explains underlined, because it purchases mechanism plus evaluation; Marxist theory left ununderlined as the field the plurality opening will organise. The plan assembles in its seven minutes: the plurality sentence; profitability first among families, as the most anchored, with formula, countertendencies, Okishio, and the measurement caveat in shorthand; realisation second, with the timing debit; disproportion as the form of appearance; squeeze confined to a paragraph and a decade; the monetary family as trigger; rivals in reserve, Keynes for the slump's depth and the Austrian for the credit boom's anatomy; verdict sketched as two levels, recurrence sustained, terminus refused.

The writing itself then proceeds at the pace the plan purchased. The opening lands its three sentences and the exposition runs, each family introduced by its location in the circuit, each closed by its comparative clause. Midway, the candidate performs the discipline that separates rehearsed scripts from hopeful ones: a glance back at the question's exact wording, confirming that recurrence, not collapse, remains the subject, and a glance at the clock against the plan's ratios, finding the evaluation its protected share. The evaluation leads with Okishio rather than warming up on weaker game, hears the replies, turns to the measurement quarrel for the empirical verdict, and admits the rivals for the aspects the families handle worst, the depth of slumps and the timing of turns. The conclusion executes the two-level verdict the plan promised, its strength visibly set by the gauntlet's survivors. Nothing in the performance was improvised except the sentences, and that is the point: on this topic, preparation does not compete with thought in the room; it clears the space in which thought can happen at speed.

## The policy-error rival, stated at full strength

Every evaluation arsenal needs the rival that denies the premise, and for crisis theory that rival is the family of accounts for which instability is imported into the system from outside its logic: by policy error, by monetary mismanagement, by regulatory blunder, by shocks no economy could be expected to absorb. Its strongest modern form is not foolish. It observes that the postwar decades of active demand management coincided with shallower cycles in several economies; that the worst modern contractions followed identifiable decisions, the defence of a parity, the tightening into a fragile system, the toleration of a leveraged bubble by authorities charged with watching it; and that a theory of endogenous crisis struggles to explain why the timing and severity of downturns correlate so visibly with the quality of stewardship. On this account, capitalism is a fundamentally self-adjusting order whose crises are accidents of governance, and the Marxist families mistake the ambulance's route for the disease.

The candidate's handling of this rival decides whether the evaluation reads as scholarship or as apologetics with the signs reversed. Grant the correlations; they are real, and denying them costs credibility the answer needs elsewhere. Then discriminate. The policy-error account explains the variance among episodes better than it explains the existence of the series: it must treat each downturn's endogenous anatomy, the pre-crash leverage, the swollen sector, the sagging profitability visible in case after case, as coincidence arranged by blunder, and it struggles with the recurrence of those anatomies under stewards of every doctrine and competence level. The Marxist families, read at their defensible strength, explain the recurrence and the anatomy while conceding the variance, which is why the two-level verdict keeps reappearing in this guide: proneness belongs to the system, severity to the stewards. That sentence structure, conceding the rival its domain while refusing it the whole field, is the most valuable single move in the evaluation repertoire on any topic, and crisis theory is simply the topic where candidates get the most practice at it.

## Combination questions, when crisis meets the other topics

Papers delight in joining topics, and crisis arrives in scripts beside the state, beside imperialism, beside the party and the falling rate's quieter neighbours. The joined question tempts the candidate to write two half-answers, and the plurality discipline supplies the alternative: find the junction and build there. Where crisis meets the state, the junction is the management of the purge: the profitability family says devaluation restores the conditions of accumulation, and the state's modern role, lender of last resort, rescuer of payments systems, guarantor of deposits, is precisely the organised interruption of that purge, which lets a candidate argue that state intervention does not refute the family but displaces its mechanism, socialising the devaluation into public debt and inflation, a thesis with a literature behind it. Where crisis meets imperialism, the junction is displacement through the countertendencies: foreign trade and the export of capital as the system's spatial answer to a profitability problem it cannot solve at home, Luxemburg and Lenin waiting at the junction with their rival versions of the same insight. Where crisis meets the theory of the state in its exam-guide form, the candidate who met the plurality pattern in that topic will recognise the shape at once, since the method transfers entire: name the rival accounts, specify which version each criticism reaches, and refuse the monolith.

The transferability deserves emphasis because it converts this guide from a topic treatment into an instrument. The state guide in this series teaches a plural-theories opening; the class guides teach the same habit for class theory; the pattern is the series' thesis about how Marxist topics are examined, that they are all, in the scholarship, fields of rival mechanisms wearing a single textbook label. A candidate who learns the crisis version deeply has therefore revised for questions far beyond the crisis paper, and teachers who grasp the pattern can build a course around it, each topic introduced by its quarrel rather than by its caricature. That is the deepest sense in which the plurality opening is the highest-return sentence available: it is not a trick for one answer but the correct description of the intellectual terrain, and examinations, at their best, are audits of whether the candidate has noticed the terrain.

## What the marginalia mean

Candidates who receive marked practice scripts often misread the comments, treating them as verdicts on knowledge when they are usually directions about operations. A margin that asks where the second theory is, is not complaining of ignorance; it is reporting that the script performed exposition where the scheme pays for comparison. A margin that writes assertion beside a paragraph is reporting the missing mechanism, the disease of the paragraph clinic's first case. A margin that writes and so, or develop, beside a conclusion is reporting the verdict without a receipt. Even the dreaded one-sided is an operational note: the marker met the favoured family in its strong form and its rivals in fancy dress, and the repair is symmetry, an afternoon's practice at stating opponents fairly. Reading marginalia this way converts feedback into a training programme, each comment naming the drill that answers it, and the candidate who keeps a list of personal marginalia, ranked by frequency, owns a revision plan more precise than any published guide can supply.

Teachers can exploit the same translation from the other side. Comments written as operations rather than as judgements, name the countertendency you omitted, state Okishio before you answer him, give the squeeze paragraph its decade, teach the candidate the craft while marking the script, and they have the incidental virtue of being defensible comments, tied to the scheme's operations rather than to the marker's sympathies. The assessment types this guide serves all publish some form of examiner commentary, and mining those documents for their operational vocabulary, the verbs of the bands, is among the highest-yield revision activities available to a class in its final term. The examiners have, in effect, published the marking scheme's spirit; the plurality method is what that spirit looks like when a candidate obeys it.

## A final self-test, administered honestly

Before the examination, the prepared candidate can audit readiness with questions sterner than any paper will ask, and this section poses them as a prose examination of the self. Can the three ratios be produced, with their meanings, in under a minute, and the law restated as a sentence about their motion? Can the six counteracting influences be named without prompting, with the cheapening of constant capital explained rather than merely listed? Can each family be given its one-sentence mechanism, its anchor, its best evidence, and its hardest objection, in rotation, without notes, in under ten minutes for the set? Can Okishio be stated in two sentences his defenders would accept, and the temporal reply in two more? Can a two-level verdict be delivered on proneness, on the law, and on underconsumption, each at a strength the supporting gauntlet was actually run to establish? Can the barrier sentence be quoted exactly, once, with its chapter named?

Where any answer fails, the failure is local and repairable, which is the self-test's kindness: it converts the fog of general anxiety into a list of named repairs, each an hour's work at most. Where every answer holds, the candidate possesses something the single-theory resources cannot sell at any price, a map of the whole field with the quarrels marked, the anchors placed, the traps catalogued, and a method for converting that map into scripts under time pressure. Walk into the room with the map. Open with the plurality. The rest of the performance is the one this guide has rehearsed from its first paragraph, and it belongs to the candidate now, not to the guide.

## Ernest Mandel and the long waves, a synthesis examined

One construction deserves separate examination because examiners with a scholarly bent reach for it and because it tests the synthesis discipline of the earlier sections. Ernest Mandel proposed that the history of capitalism since the industrial revolution falls into long waves of roughly a generation, expansions and stagnations alternating, with the downturns explained by the falling rate of profit's slow pressure and the upturns explained by extra-economic shocks, wars, defeats of labour, sudden enlargements of the world market, that reset profitability from outside the cycle's own logic. The construction is candidly asymmetric: the mechanism explains why expansions exhaust themselves, while the renewal is entrusted to history. Critics inside the tradition charged that the asymmetry surrenders the theory's claim to endogeneity at exactly the point where it matters most; defenders replied that the asymmetry is honesty, since no serious reader of Marx believes the system's recoveries are automatic, the purge requiring political and social conditions that economics alone cannot schedule.

The examinable value of the long waves lies less in their truth, which remains contested among economic historians who doubt the waves' statistical robustness, than in what the construction reveals about the families' division of labour. Mandel's synthesis assigns the profitability family the long run and the distributional and political families the turning points, an assignment by timescale that mirrors this guide's assignment by level, and a candidate can use the parallel to show how syntheses are built: not by stirring the families together but by giving each a domain, a timescale, a level, a phase, and defending the gift. The candidate can equally attack the construction at its joint, the exogenous rescue, and the attack and defence together form a complete evaluation paragraph on any question touching periodisation. Long wave questions are not the commonest on this topic, but they are disproportionately set on the papers aimed at the strongest candidates, and the candidate who has rehearsed the joint owns them.

## When the service economy objects

A family of objections arrives from the changed shape of the economies the theory must now describe, and candidates meet it in seminars before they meet it in papers: what becomes of a crisis theory built on industrial capital when services dominate employment, when the largest firms sell platforms and advertising rather than steel, when intangible assets outweigh plant? The objection deserves its strongest form. The organic composition was framed for production in which machinery visibly displaces labour; the productive and unproductive labour distinction, on which some measurements of the profit rate depend, becomes treacherous where output is a stream of services; and the spectacle of enormous profits at firms with modest tangible capital seems, to the seminar sceptic, to refute the composition mechanism by inspection.

The replies available to the candidate are instructive about the theory's real structure. The composition is a value relation, not a photograph of factories: what matters is the ratio between the value advanced in means of production, broadly construed to include the software, networks, and data infrastructure through which modern services are delivered, and the value advanced in wages, and the apparent lightness of platform capital dissolves somewhat when its infrastructure is honestly inventoried. The measurement quarrel returns in a new costume, since whether the enormous incomes of the platform sector count as profit on productive capital, as rent on monopoly positions, or as redistributed surplus determines what the series show, and the candidate can present the dispute as the empirical section's lesson in a fresh setting. And the families divide over the objection in the usual instructive way: the realisation and disproportionality accounts travel to a service economy with barely a limp, squeeze translates into the politics of scarce credentialed labour, while the profitability family must do the hardest work, which is exactly the distribution of burdens the objection was designed to reveal. An answer that stages the objection and its uneven incidence across the families has evaluated at a level no summary reaches.

## Why examiners keep setting this question

It is worth asking, once, why this topic survives every syllabus revision while neighbouring topics are pensioned off, because the answer tells the candidate what the question is really for. Crisis theory compresses the whole discipline's demands into one script. It requires textual knowledge with addresses, formal reasoning with ratios, historical judgement with episodes, distributional politics with the wage, and philosophical care with words like tendency and law. A candidate cannot fake any of those for long, and cannot display any of them without the others becoming visible, which makes the topic a nearly perfect instrument for discriminating among scripts at scale. It also sits at the crossing of the examination's constituencies: economists meet it as business cycle theory, politics candidates as the theory of the system's stability, sociologists as the political economy of modernity, and each constituency's examiners can set it in confidence that the others' candidates will find it fair.

There is a further, institutional reason. The topic audits teaching as much as learning. A cohort taught the single-theory version produces scripts with a recognisable shape, and examiners' reports across the decades complain of that shape in the weary vocabulary this guide's trap catalogue borrowed. The question persists, in part, as a standing invitation to teachers to repair the teaching, and the candidates who benefit first are those whose teachers accepted the invitation, or who accepted it on their own behalf by reading past the revision summary into the field itself. The plurality method is, in that sense, simply the topic taught as its examiners understand it, and the candidate who adopts the method has stopped preparing for an examination and started, in miniature, practising the discipline.

## The reading architecture for this topic

Candidates ask what to read, and the honest architecture for examination purposes is shallow at the centre and deep at one chosen point. At the centre stand the primary passages themselves, in the order of the anchors section: the law chapters of the third volume with their counteracting influences attached, never detached; the reproduction chapters of the second, read for their construction rather than memorised; and the crisis discussions preserved in the history-of-theory manuscript, where the founder's own discriminations among his predecessors model the evaluative manners this guide teaches. Around that centre, one scholarly companion per family suffices at this level: Sweezy for the demand side and the mid-century state of the argument, Glyn for squeeze and its evidence, a modern statement of the profitability case with its measurement quarrels owned rather than hidden, and Clarke or a comparable guide for the claim that the whole architecture is the theory. The candidate who reads one point deeply, one family quarrel followed into its replies and counter-replies, gains more than the candidate who reads everything once, because the deep point becomes the template the mind applies to every other quarrel it meets.

Two reading warnings close the architecture. Beware the anthology excerpt that prints the law without its countertendencies, an editorial habit responsible for more bad examination answers than any candidate error, since it manufactures the crude version at the source; when a set text presents the tendency, check what the edition has placed around it before trusting the impression it leaves. And beware the commentary that resolves the plurality by decree, announcing which family the founder really held; the scholarly debate exists because the texts sustain the debate, and a commentary that cannot tolerate the tension will leave its reader defenceless before an examiner who can. Keep the working library for the topic, the family cards, the worked numbers, the episode file, in a form that survives revision's end: the study notes companion at [VaultBook](https://vaultbook.net/tools/marxism-study-notes.html) exists for exactly that keeping, letting a reader store the cards and anchors beside the passages they summarise, while the question practice that keeps the method warm is served by the previous year question explorer at [ReportMedic](https://reportmedic.org/tools/upsc-pyq-explorer.html), where the wording of real papers can be mined for the family signals this guide teaches its readers to read.

## What would change your mind, the falsifiability paragraph

Sophisticated evaluation ends by specifying the evidence that would reverse the verdict, and candidates almost never write that paragraph, which is why it gleams when it appears. Each family implies observations that would embarrass it, and naming them converts an opinion into a position. The profitability family would be embarrassed by a long era in which composition rose, exploitation stagnated, and measured rates on every honest construction climbed regardless; the candidate who writes that sentence has told the marker the theory is being treated as a claim about the world rather than as a loyalty. Realisation would be embarrassed by sustained expansions in which mass consumption lagged, investment faltered, and no compensating demand from credit, exports, or the state appeared, yet no crisis followed. Squeeze would be embarrassed by downturn after downturn arriving on falling wage shares with quiescent labour, a pattern its defenders must either absorb by demoting the family to a phase theory or refuse at the cost of candour. Disproportionality would be embarrassed by crises whose sectoral anatomy showed no leading disproportion, contraction arriving uniformly like a tide going out.

Writing the falsifiability paragraph disciplines the candidate as much as it impresses the marker, because composing it requires asking of one's own favoured family the question its enemies ask, and the habit of asking generalises across the whole syllabus. It also completes the guide's account of conclusion strength. A verdict accompanied by its reversal conditions is calibrated twice over: it claims what the evidence supports and it declares the price at which the claim would be withdrawn. Examiners who meet that paragraph recognise a candidate who has understood what theories are for, namely to be held against the world in a form the world can answer, and on a topic where slogans have done so much damage, that recognition is the highest currency the examination mints.

## The journalism as a hidden asset

Candidates rarely exploit the fact that Marx watched crises arrive in something close to real time and wrote about them for newspapers, yet the journalism is a legitimate and underused asset. Retained to report European affairs for an American paper through the eighteen fifties, he covered the commercial crisis of eighteen fifty seven as it broke, tracing the failure chains from American banks to Liverpool merchants to Hamburg houses, and reading the Bank of England's rate movements the way later journalists would read central bank signals. The notebooks that became the Grundrisse were begun in that crisis's immediate shadow, the author writing at night, by his own account in correspondence, in the fear that the flood would arrive before the outline was drained. The episode gives a candidate three examinable assets in one: a biographical anchor that makes the monetary family's pedigree vivid; a demonstration that the founder's crisis thinking began from the money market's surface and dug toward production, which complicates any claim that the tradition despises the financial level; and a model of the episode discipline this guide teaches, since the journalism itself moves constantly between the event and the mechanism it might reveal.

Deploy the asset sparingly. One sentence in an exposition, locating the Grundrisse's composition in the eighteen fifty seven crisis, earns its place wherever the monetary family or the texts' history is in play. One further sentence, noting that the dispatches treat the panic as a revelation of imbalances accumulated in the calm, can carry the whole trigger and vulnerability distinction in historical dress. Beyond those sentences the journalism becomes colour, and colour is what the narrative ration exists to restrain. The restraint itself is the lesson the asset teaches best: the founder had more colour available than any candidate will ever have, the telegraph wires of a collapsing Atlantic economy, and the mature work subordinates every dispatch of it to the machinery of the circuit.

## The oral examination and the interview form

Some assessment types defend the written script with speech, and the plurality method adapts to the viva with unusual grace, because the viva's questions are the evaluation section conducted aloud. The examiner who asks what the candidate makes of Okishio, or whether the candidate's essay has not simply listed theories, is running the gauntlet in person, and the prepared candidate recognises the stations. Answer in the order the guide teaches: state the objection's premise before its result, concede what must be conceded without theatre, name the reply and its cost, and close with the verdict at its calibrated strength. The oral form punishes two habits the written form tolerates: the memorised paragraph, which collapses at the first interruption, and the bluff of range, since a viva examiner follows any named family one question deeper. The defence against both is the same depth the family cards were built to hold, one family known to the bottom, its quarrel followed through the replies, while the others are held honestly at the level the candidate can defend, with the boundary stated rather than disguised. Examiners forgive a declared boundary; they do not forgive discovering one.

There is a consolation specific to the oral form worth passing on. The plurality opening, which on paper must justify itself against the fear of seeming indecisive, in speech reads immediately as command, because the examiner hears the map before the territory and can steer the conversation across it, which examiners enjoy and reward. Candidates who have practised the self-test aloud, mechanisms and objections spoken in rotation, discover that the topic shrinks to conversational size, five families and their quarrels being, after all, an evening's argument among positions the candidate can introduce like guests. That social image is not a bad final preparation: the examination, written or oral, is the candidate hosting the quarrel fairly, and the host's virtues, accuracy about each guest, fairness among them, and a closing judgement the evening has earned, are precisely the virtues the bands describe.

## The reserve army, the mechanism inside the mechanisms

One piece of machinery recurs so persistently across the families that a candidate who isolates it gains a private organising device: the reserve army of labour, the pool of the unemployed and underemployed that accumulation itself produces and maintains. Watch it work in each family's engine room. In the profitability account, the reserve holds wages in check and so protects the rate of exploitation against the composition's drag, appearing in the founder's own text among the conditions that keep the tendency a tendency. In squeeze, the reserve is the variable whose depletion starts the mechanism, the boom draining the pool until the wage bargain turns. In realisation, the reserve's existence is the standing proof that the system's demand for labour is intermittent while its need to sell is constant, and the unemployed worker is the realisation problem wearing a human face. In the monetary family the reserve matters less directly, yet the crisis that rebuilds it, through bankruptcies and layoffs, is the event by which the other families' corrections are socially enforced.

The device pays in answers because it lets the candidate compare families at the level of a shared component rather than of rival slogans. A comparative paragraph can ask what each family predicts about the reserve's behaviour across the cycle: profitability expects it replenished by the very mechanisation that raises composition; squeeze expects it drained by the boom and restored by the slump; realisation reads its size as a measure of demand forgone. Those predictions can then be held against an episode's labour market record, which is documented with a fullness the profit rate's record never achieves, so the reserve becomes the empirically tractable proxy through which partially testable families can be compared. Examiners notice the move because it demonstrates the thing the plurality method exists to produce: mechanisms held simultaneously, in working relation, rather than recited in sequence. A candidate who builds one such cross-family paragraph, on the reserve or on credit or on the wage, has manufactured the analytical texture the top band describes out of materials every candidate possesses.

## Risk management for the non-specialist marker

A prudent word about the residual risk in the plurality method, because candidates deserve honesty about the whole board. Most scripts on this topic are marked by specialists or against schemes specialists wrote, and the method is built for that world. Occasionally, however, a script meets a hurried or generalist reader for whom the single famous mechanism is the expected centre of any answer, and a plurality opening executed too briefly can be misread as evasion before the exposition arrives. The insurance is cheap and worth buying universally: within the first paragraph, immediately after the plurality sentence, name the falling rate of profit as the tradition's most elaborated mechanism and promise it the answer's fullest treatment, which the answer then delivers. The specialist reads the sentence as correct prioritisation inside the field; the generalist reads it as the expected centre arriving on schedule. No reader is deceived, since the promise is kept, and the plurality stands protected in both worlds.

The same insurance logic applies at smaller joints. Technical terms should be glossed at first use in a clause, organic composition as the ratio of dead to living labour in value terms, so that no reader needs the glossary the candidate cannot supply. The formula should be written in words once as well as in symbols, the rate of profit as surplus value divided by the sum of constant and variable capital, because symbolic fluency varies among readers while verbal fluency is the examination's common tongue. Historical episodes should carry their dates and their one-line identification on first mention, since the reader's memory is not the candidate's. None of these courtesies costs the specialist anything, and together they express a principle worth learning early: an answer is an act of communication under adversarial conditions, and its architecture should assume a reader who is competent, hurried, and not obliged to be generous. Write so that generosity is never required.

## The word tendency, a seminar in miniature

One word carries more of this topic's examinable weight than any other, and a short seminar on it closes the guide's technical instruction. Marx called the falling rate of profit a tendency and titled the relevant part of the third volume with the law of that tendency, and the double naming is precise rather than indecisive: a law, in his usage, states the pressure a mechanism exerts; a tendency states that the pressure operates through a field of counteracting forces whose resultant is an empirical matter. Candidates who grasp the grammar stop making the two characteristic errors the word exists to prevent. They stop upgrading, writing as though a tendency were a schedule, since a schedule claims the resultant in advance and the whole point of the counteracting chapter is that no one is entitled to it. And they stop downgrading, treating tendency as a synonym for weakness or vagueness, since a tendency in this sense is a stronger scientific object than a bare correlation: it names the mechanism, the direction of its pressure, and the catalogue of its resistances, which is more information than most established laws in the social sciences provide. An answer that spends two sentences on the grammar, placed where the law is first expounded, has quietly educated its marker about the candidate's schooling, and the education shows in every subsequent paragraph's marks. The word is small. The seminar is the topic.

The grammar radiates outward through the other families, and the candidate who has learned it here will find it working everywhere. Realisation is properly the name of a standing condition that becomes acute under stated circumstances, not a permanent paralysis; disproportionality names a tendency of uncoordinated investment to discover its errors in clusters, not a guarantee of perpetual imbalance; even squeeze is a tendency of mature expansions, suspended wherever productivity growth funds both claims or political settlements restrain them. Examiners listen for the grammar the way musicians listen for tuning. A script whose claims are all stated at tendency strength, with the counteracting forces acknowledged inside the same sentences, sounds like the discipline itself; a script of absolutes sounds like the pamphlets the discipline defines itself against. Tune the prose once, at this word, and the whole performance settles into the key the markers reward.

## A worked paragraph, taken apart

Guides that preach operations owe their readers one full demonstration at paragraph scale, so consider a single evaluation paragraph of the kind this guide exists to produce, presented whole and then taken apart move by move. The paragraph runs as follows in the candidate's script. The strongest objection to the law is not that profit rates have failed to fall on some favoured measure, since measures can be quarrelled over indefinitely, but that the mechanism producing the fall appears to refute itself: Okishio showed that techniques adopted because they cut costs at prevailing prices raise the general rate of profit once prices adjust, provided the real wage holds, so the competitive pressure Marx trusted to mechanise production should, on this result, lift the rate it was meant to press. The reply developed by temporal readings of the value theory denies the simultaneity on which the result rests, valuing inputs at the prices of their purchase rather than of their replacement, and on that valuation the innovator's temporary gain and the devaluation of the capital stock already in place restore the downward pressure the theorem appeared to abolish. Which reading of the founder's categories is correct remains a live scholarly dispute, and the honest verdict inside an examination answer is that the law's logic is contested at its foundation rather than merely unverified in its statistics, a stronger and more precise charge than the vague assertion that the evidence is mixed.

Count the moves, because each is separately creditable and the set is learnable. The paragraph opens by ranking the objections and discarding the weaker one explicitly, which performs selection. It states the chosen objection in its strong form, with its premise, cost-reducing adoption, and its condition, the constant real wage, both visible, which performs accuracy about an opponent. It identifies the exact joint at which the reply engages, the simultaneity of valuation, rather than gesturing at disagreement, which performs specification. It names the reply's mechanism, purchase prices against replacement prices and the devaluation of existing stock, so the defence is machinery rather than loyalty. And it closes by pricing the whole exchange, foundation contested rather than statistics mixed, a conclusion whose strength is visibly set by the exchange it summarises. Five operations in nine sentences, none of them requiring knowledge beyond one theorem and one reply, and the paragraph would sit comfortably inside any of the worked answers this guide has built. Candidates should construct one such paragraph for each family during revision and keep them with the family cards, not to be reproduced verbatim, since questions differ and verbatim reproduction is the template failure, but to be cannibalised for their moves under the pressure of a wording no one predicted. The moves transfer; the sentences should not.

## The reproduction schemes, worked slowly

The schemes of the second volume intimidate candidates out of proportion to their difficulty, and since they anchor an entire family, the intimidation is worth curing with one slow walk through the arithmetic. Divide the whole economy into two departments. Department One produces means of production, machines, raw materials, plant. Department Two produces articles of consumption, everything workers and capitalists buy to live. Each department's output divides in value into the constant capital consumed in making it, the variable capital paid out in wages, and the surplus value remaining. For the economy simply to reproduce itself at the same scale, a famous balance condition must hold: the part of Department One's output that its own workers and capitalists need as consumption goods must exactly equal Department Two's need for replacement means of production. In the founder's own illustrative figures, Department One produces six thousand in value, four thousand constant, one thousand variable, one thousand surplus; Department Two produces three thousand, two thousand constant, five hundred variable, five hundred surplus. Department One's wage earners and capitalists together require two thousand of consumption goods; Department Two requires two thousand of means of production to replace what it used up. The exchange closes, the proportions hold, and the economy repeats its year.

Now disturb the picture in the two ways the text invites, and the crisis families enter through the arithmetic itself. First, let Department One's capitalists accumulate, converting part of their surplus into additional constant and variable capital rather than consuming it. Their demand for consumption goods falls below the old figure while their demand for means of production rises, and Department Two must correspondingly shrink or redirect its output, its own accumulation adjusting in a precise ratio the schemes can compute. Expanded reproduction is possible; the numbers close again at a higher level. But the adjustment has to happen through thousands of uncoordinated decisions, each taken on expectations about the others, and the schemes' true lesson stands revealed: balance is a razor's edge the system must continually find without a finder, and every error is discovered only after labour and materials have been embodied in the wrong proportions. Second, let the proportions hold in value while the material composition mismatches, too much steel of one grade, too little transport capacity, and the same conclusion follows in physical dress. Disproportionality, which sounded abstract as a family name, is here a property of arithmetic plus decentralisation, and a candidate who has walked the numbers once can state the family with an authority no paraphrase supplies.

The walk also purchases the evaluation that damaged crude underconsumption, because the schemes demonstrate, in figures anyone can check, that accumulation can proceed with consumption's share stagnant, Department One selling machinery to an expanding Department One, provided the proportions are found. Tugan-Baranovsky's triumph was to insist on exactly this demonstration against contemporaries who held deficient consumption to be a logical bar. The sophisticated candidate therefore learns to wield the schemes twice: once as the disproportionality family's anchor, and once as the standing refutation of the strong underconsumption claim, whose defender must retreat from arithmetic to history, from what the schemes permit to what actual economies did. That double use is economical in the examination room and impressive on the page, since it shows a single object mastered from two directions. Candidates intimidated by the algebra should note that nothing in the walk exceeded addition and proportion; the schemes' reputation for difficulty is a rumour the textbook industry never bothered to correct, and dispelling it in one's own preparation is among the cheapest advantages available anywhere in this topic.

## One topic, four assessment cultures

The assessment types named in this guide's scope reward the same method at different depths, and the candidate who knows which culture is examining can tune the performance without changing its spine. School specifications treat crisis theory as a perspective within a wider paper on economic systems or sociological theory: the questions are shorter, the expected mechanisms are the famous two, and the plurality opening earns its credit in a single paragraph of comparison before the answer moves on to the specification's other demands. The trap in that culture is disproportion, the candidate unloading the full scholarly apparatus on a question that purchased a perspective and a contrast. Undergraduate papers invert the proportions: the topic may carry an entire question to itself, the scholarship is expected to show, and the depth discriminations of this guide, Okishio stated correctly, the measurement quarrel named, the editorial status of the third volume acknowledged, become the difference between the respectable script and the distinguished one. Eligibility tests compress in the opposite direction, objective and short forms dominating, and there the family map's precision under miniaturisation, the self-test's one-sentence mechanisms, is the whole game.

Civil service optional papers form the fourth culture and in some ways the most demanding, because their questions are set for candidates who will administer economies, and the examiners' taste runs to balance performed under genuine uncertainty. The plurality method is native to that culture: the answer that names rival mechanisms, weighs the record, concedes the policy-error rival its domain, and delivers a two-level verdict reads to those examiners as the temperament of a future administrator rather than as examination technique. Candidates in that culture should also note its preference for the historical episode handled as precedent, reasoning from a closed case toward a general discipline, which is the fifth worked answer's form exactly. Across all four cultures the invariants hold: the opening, the anchors, the countertendencies, the strongest objection first, the calibrated conclusion. What changes is only the dosage, and dosage is a judgement the prepared candidate makes in the reading minutes, from the question's length, its command word, and the company it keeps on the paper.

## Provisional knowledge, held properly

Underneath every technique in this guide sits an intellectual posture that the topic rewards more reliably than any other in the syllabus, and a closing section should name it plainly: the willingness to hold a theory as a claim whose credentials can be inspected, rather than as a possession to be defended or a relic to be mocked. Crisis theory makes the posture unavoidable, because its texts are unfinished, its measurements are constructions, its mechanisms collide, and its history is still being argued by serious people. The candidate who needs the topic to be simpler than it is will write the single-theory answer whatever the revision materials say, since the simplification is a psychological requirement before it is an error. The candidate who can tolerate the field as it stands, plural, contested, and partially undecided, finds that the tolerance converts directly into marks, because every operation the schemes reward, comparison, specification, adjudication, calibration, is simply that tolerance expressed as prose.

The posture has a name in the philosophy of science the candidates may meet elsewhere in their courses, but the examination needs no name for it, only its fruits. Its fruits are sentences of a recognisable kind: the acknowledgement that a chapter was assembled by an editor from drafts; the concession that an objection survives its standard reply in part; the verdict that divides by domain and decade; the falsifiability paragraph that prices the writer's own conclusion. Sentences like those are not hedges. They are the most expensive sentences in any script, costing the writer the comfort of certainty, and markers pay for them accordingly. A candidate who leaves this guide with one durable possession should leave with that exchange rate understood: on this topic, honesty about the state of knowledge is not the alternative to scoring well. It is the mechanism by which scoring well is achieved, as surely as composition is the mechanism by which the profit rate is pressed, and considerably more reliably.

## For students and exam candidates

Your situation is better than the revision industry has led you to believe, because the repair this topic needs is small, named, and entirely within your power: stop presenting one mechanism as the theory, and open every crisis answer by stating that Marx left no unified theory and naming at least two rival families. Build the rest of your preparation around that opening in the order this guide has laid out. Learn the three ratios and one worked numerical illustration until they are reflexes. Learn the counteracting influences as a set, with the cheapening of constant capital explained, since their omission is the commonest content error markers report. Build a card for each family carrying its mechanism in one sentence, its anchor, its best evidence, its hardest objection, and the standard reply with its weakness, and revise the cards in rotation so the families stay simultaneously present rather than serially filed. Practise the seven-minute plan on past questions until the architecture assembles itself, and rehearse two-level verdicts, yes to recurrence and no to terminus, until calibration feels natural rather than timid. Above all, hold the reassurance this guide proved rather than asserted: you may conclude against any family, or against all of them, and still take full marks, provided the exposition was accurate, the objections were the strongest available, the replies were heard fairly, and the conclusion claimed only what survived. The examiners are grading the quality of your argument, and argument is a skill with drills, which means it is a skill you can finish learning before the examination arrives.

## For teachers and seminar leaders

The uncomfortable fact this guide rests on is that the single-theory presentation is transmitted in classrooms before it is ever read in revision books, because it is teachable in one lesson and the plurality takes three. Accept the three. Introduce the topic by its quarrel: put the wage collision on the board in the first session, underconsumption and squeeze demanding opposite movements of the same variable, and let the class discover that the tradition argues with itself before any outsider criticises it. Teach the formula and the counteracting influences in the same lesson, never in successive weeks, since the separation of the law from its chapter of resistances is the editorial accident behind the commonest error your students will commit. Set the Okishio theorem as a seminar problem rather than a lecture conclusion, and let students who defend the law discover the temporal reply themselves, because an objection a student has survived in argument is retained in a way no summary achieves. Mark practice scripts with operational comments, name the omitted countertendency, state the objection before answering it, give the squeeze paragraph its decade, and your marginalia become the training programme the assessment reports keep requesting. Finally, audit your own materials for the traps in this guide's catalogue, especially the anthology excerpt that prints the tendency without its counteracting chapter; the repair of one set text can lift a whole cohort's ceiling on this topic.

## For researchers, historians, and journalists

The gap this guide exists to close, between the examinable simplification and the scholarship, is widest on this topic and worth a researcher's attention in its own right. The plurality the examinations reward is not a pedagogical convenience; it tracks a genuine condition of the sources, manuscripts left in draft, a third volume assembled by an editor's hand, a theory of crisis distributed across genres from journalism to reproduction algebra and never gathered by its author. The historical-critical edition's ongoing publication of the drafts continues to move the ground, and the distance between the crisis theory of the textbooks and the crisis thinking recoverable from the manuscripts is now itself a research object. Historians will find the examinable families useful as a periodisation device for the tradition's own development, each family dominating the decades whose events it best anatomised. Journalists covering the teaching of contested doctrines might note the rarer phenomenon this series documents: an examination culture that has quietly corrected its textbooks, rewarding candidates for reporting a scholarly dispute their revision guides still deny exists.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do you write an exam answer on Marxist crisis theory?

Open with the plurality: state that Marx left no single crisis theory and name at least two rival mechanisms, the falling rate of profit and one realisation account at minimum, before expounding either. Expound each mechanism with its anchor and, for the profit rate, its formula. Evaluate by specifying which family each objection reaches, leading with the strongest, and close with a verdict whose strength matches what survived. The architecture matters more than any single content item, because markers read the opening as evidence of command and grade the adjudication as the answer's proof.

### Q: Which crisis theories must you name in an exam?

Name the falling rate of profit first, since it carries the fullest textual elaboration in the third volume, and at least one demand-side account beside it, underconsumption or the wider realisation problem, so that comparison becomes possible. Add disproportionality for the coordination mechanism and profit squeeze for the distributional one as the question's wording invites. The minimum is two, accurately mechanised rather than merely listed. What examiners are counting is not names but located blockages: profitability, demand, proportion, and distribution are four different places a circuit can fail, and an answer that locates two has begun to analyse.

### Q: How do you explain the falling rate of profit in an exam?

State the formula first: the rate of profit equals surplus value divided by constant plus variable capital, which restates as the rate of exploitation divided by the organic composition plus one. Then give the mechanism: accumulation raises the composition as machinery displaces living labour, the sole source of surplus value, so the rate is pressed downward at a given rate of exploitation. Work one numerical illustration with a composition shift. Finally, restore the counteracting influences, cheapening of constant capital foremost, and present the law throughout as a tendency rather than a timetable of collapse.

### Q: Which evaluation points work on crisis theory?

The points that work strike mechanisms rather than conclusions and come with names attached. Against the falling rate of profit, lead with the Okishio theorem, that cost-reducing innovation raises the equilibrium rate at a constant real wage, then note the temporal valuation reply. Against underconsumption, press the reproduction schemes, which balance without external buyers, and the timing problem of a chronic condition explaining a cyclical event. For every family, the measurement disputes supply a point, since rival constructions of the profit rate read different trends from the same decades. Close by naming what evidence would change the verdict.

### Q: What are the common mistakes in crisis theory answers?

The costliest is presenting one mechanism, usually the falling rate of profit, as the whole theory. Behind it come confusing overproduction, the symptom, with underconsumption, one proposed cause; omitting the counteracting influences so the law becomes a collapse prediction Marx never made; asserting that profit rates have fallen without naming the measurement construction; reaching for a contemporary condition as evidence instead of a documented historical episode; and narrating a financial crash at length while the mechanism it was meant to test goes unexamined. Each mistake is a habit rather than an ignorance, and each has a named repair in this guide.

### Q: How do you use the financial crash as an exam example?

Ration the narrative to a few sentences: the credit-fed expansion, the layering of financial claims upon it, the repricing, and the seizure in the money market. Then cross-examine the episode with the families, grading each one's fit openly. The monetary family fits the surface, disproportionality fits the swollen construction sector, realisation fits the debt-bridged stagnation of mass incomes, and profit squeeze fits hardly at all, which is itself a creditable finding. Close by stating what one episode can and cannot establish about mechanisms that claim to operate across cycles, since that reflection on evidence is where the marks concentrate.

### Q: How do you compare Marx and Keynes in an answer?

Begin with the difference of object: Keynes theorised the short period, investment collapsing against liquidity preference and an economy settling into unemployment equilibrium, while the Marxist families theorise accumulation's long movement and the barriers it generates internally. Then credit the genuine similarities, both rejecting automatic equilibration in a monetary economy and both making investment the unstable hinge. Draw the asymmetry inside the resemblance: Keynes locates failure in circulation and expectation, the Marxist accounts in production and distribution. Note that the two authors barely engaged, making the comparison an examiner's construction, and finish with a verdict assigning each framework the phenomena it explains best.

### Q: Which formulas do you need to know for crisis theory?

Three ratios carry the whole formal core. The rate of profit divides surplus value by the sum of constant and variable capital. The rate of exploitation divides surplus value by variable capital alone. The organic composition divides constant by variable capital. Restate the law through them: if the composition rises while exploitation is bounded, the profit rate is pressed downward. Add the distinction between the rate of profit and its mass, which can rise together while the rate falls, and one worked numerical example, and the mathematical equipment of the topic is complete for examination purposes.

### Q: Is it safe to argue Marx was wrong about crisis?

Yes, and this guide demonstrates the safety twice over, against the falling rate of profit and against underconsumption, because markers grade the operations of an answer rather than its destination. The conditions are strict: the exposition must be accurate enough that a defender would accept it, the objections must be the strongest available rather than the most convenient, the standard replies must be heard and weighed, and the conclusion must claim only the strength the exchange supports. A hostile verdict built that way can take full marks. A hostile verdict built on caricature takes none, exactly as a loyal verdict built on recitation does.

### Q: How do you revise crisis theory efficiently?

Revise the system of disagreements rather than five separate essays. Build one card per family with its one-sentence mechanism, anchor, best evidence, hardest objection, and the standard reply, plus a card for the counteracting influences and one for the three ratios. Review the cards in rotating order using retrieval, reproducing each before checking it, and interleave practice questions across families so discrimination is trained rather than recognition. Add timed skeletons, the seven-minute plan executed on fresh questions, twice a week. The whole topic compresses to five families, six countertendencies, three ratios, four question types, and one opening, and that inventory can be reviewed in an hour.

### Q: How do you structure an essay comparing two crisis families?

Choose the axis of comparison before the families' details: the location of the blockage in the circuit of capital works for almost any pairing, with the wage as a second axis whenever the pair collides over distribution. Open with the plurality sentence and the axis, expound each family under the same headings, mechanism, anchor, evidence, so the juxtaposition is architectural rather than appended, and reserve a full section for the direct collision, the point where the two accounts predict opposite behaviour of the same variable. Close with an adjudication that may divide the field by domain or decade rather than crown a winner.

### Q: What should a teacher mark first in a crisis theory essay?

Mark the opening first, because it predicts the script's ceiling: a plurality opening with named families signals the comparative architecture the scheme rewards, while a single-mechanism opening announces the tour that follows. Next, check the exposition of the falling rate of profit for the counteracting influences, whose omission is the commonest content error and the easiest to teach away. Then mark the evaluation for specification, whether each objection is aimed at the family it actually reaches, and for the strength of the leading objection. Comment operationally throughout, naming the repair rather than the fault, so the marginalia become the student's next revision plan.

### Q: How do you use the falling rate of profit without overstating it?

Keep the word tendency in every statement of the law and let it do real work: the rate is pressed downward by rising composition, and the pressure can be offset, delayed, and locally reversed by the counteracting influences, which must be named in the same paragraph. Separate the rate from the mass of profit, which may rise as the rate falls. When evidence enters, name the construction behind any measured series or name the dispute between constructions, never asserting a settled fall. And refuse the terminus: crises inside this family are restorations through devaluation, not the system's scheduled end, so the answer's conclusions should speak of recurrence and strain rather than collapse.

### Q: Why does the plurality opening earn marks in an evaluation paragraph?

Because evaluation is specification, and the plurality opening pre-builds the targets specification needs. Once two or more families stand named and located, every criticism can be assigned to the version it actually reaches: the Okishio result strikes the profitability mechanism and leaves realisation untouched, the wage evidence of one decade embarrasses squeeze and assists underconsumption. A single-theory answer cannot perform that assignment, so its evaluation degenerates into attack and defence of everything at once. The opening also licenses the divided verdict, sustained for one family and refused for another, which is the form markers describe when they praise judgement in their reports.

### Q: How do you revise the counter-tendencies efficiently before an exam?

Learn them as three pairs with a logic rather than as six items. The first pair works on the numerator: rising intensity of exploitation and the depression of wages below the value of labour power both enlarge the surplus relative to the wage bill. The second pair works on the denominator: the cheapening of the elements of constant capital and the opening of foreign trade both restrain the value composition's climb. The third pair works through the system's margins: the relative surplus population holds wages down from outside the immediate bargain, and the growth of interest-bearing capital redistributes the surplus among its claimants. One card, three pairs, reviewed by retrieval, and the set is available whole under pressure.
